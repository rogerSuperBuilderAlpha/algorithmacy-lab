**Title:** Algorithmacy: A Construct for Coordinating Through an Evaluating System

## Abstract
IS research has several constructs for how people work with algorithms. These are AI literacy, algorithm literacy, algorithmic competency and algorithm sensemaking, and each places the system in one position. It is a partner to one user, a channel between two users, or a manager over one worker. Many platforms hold a fourth position: they stand between two interdependent people, evaluate both, and bind both to their verdicts. This short paper defines algorithmacy as the competence to coordinate with another person through such a system. It has three facets. The first is interpreting an ambiguous result, the second is specifying intent so the counterpart can read it through the system, and the third is tracking rule changes that go unannounced. I set out a research model that separates algorithmacy from its rivals, and state the scale development and validation work that remains.

**Keywords:** algorithmacy; algorithmic management; AI literacy; coordination; construct development
