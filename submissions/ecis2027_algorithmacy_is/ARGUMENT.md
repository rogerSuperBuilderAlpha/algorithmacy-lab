# Argument: Algorithmacy as an IS construct

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026. For the author's ruling. Sources are card paths in this repo.]

**P1.** Each IS-adjacent construct for working with algorithms fixes one structural position for the system: a partner to one user, a channel between two users, or a manager over one worker. In none of them is the system an evaluator of two interdependent people.
- *Warrant:* the constructs' own definitions and items place the system that way.
- *Sources:*
  - AI literacy: `lima_pdw/literature/cards/long2020.md` (full text)
  - algorithm literacy: `lima_pdw/literature/cards/dogruel2022.md` (abstract) and `lima_pdw/literature/cards/gagrcin2026.md` (full text)
  - algorithmic competency: `lima_pdw/literature/cards/jarrahisutherland2019.md` (abstract) and `lima_pdw/literature/cards/zhou2025apjhr.md` (full text)
  - algorithm sensemaking: `lima_pdw/literature/cards/mohlmann2023.md` (abstract)
  - AI-mediated communication: `dxd_foundation/literature/library/hancock2020ai.md` (full text)

**P2.** On platforms and in AI-mediated work, the system often holds the third position. It evaluates both parties and binds both to its verdict, so each party's outcome depends on the counterpart and on the rule at once.
- *Warrant:* published studies of marketplaces and platforms describe a three-sided structure (Stark & Pais) in which one side evaluates the other through the operator (Curchod et al.).
- *Sources:* `dxd_foundation/literature/library/stark2020algorithmic.md` (p. 53); `dxd_foundation/literature/library/curchod2020working.md`; `lima_pdw/literature/cards/mohlmann2021.md` (matching and control).

**C.** IS needs a construct defined by that third position. Algorithmacy, the competence to coordinate with another person through an evaluating intermediary, predicts outcomes that the existing constructs cannot. In particular, it predicts discriminant effects where the counterpart's behaviour and the rule's behaviour must be told apart.
- *Warrant for the step:* a construct is warranted when its defining structure differs from its rivals' and that difference implies testable relations they cannot produce.
- *Testable form:* the research model in OUTLINE §5. It has three facets: interpreting results, specifying intent and tracking rule change. It sets out discriminant relations with AI literacy and algorithmic competency, and its boundary conditions are task interdependence and perceived system authority.
