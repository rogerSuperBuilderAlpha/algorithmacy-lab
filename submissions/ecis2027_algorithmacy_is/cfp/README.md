## Dates (verbatim)
- "Paper submissions: 30 October 2026". No timezone is stated.
- "Paper acceptance: 1 February 2027". The conference is in May 2027, in Luxembourg.

## Format (verbatim)
- "Short Research Papers must not exceed eight (8) single-spaced pages ... The page limit is all inclusive, encompassing the title, body of the paper, figures and tables, references, and appendices."
- Short papers "can be theoretical (presenting promising frameworks)". They "should include a paragraph describing what work remains to complete the paper".
- Submit through PCS using the ECIS template. "All papers must submitted to one – and only one – conference track." An author may appear on at most three submissions.
- Generative AI use must be declared in PCS. The AI reviewer is on by default, and authors can opt out.

## Originality (verbatim)
- "A submission, or a similar version, must not have been published or accepted in a journal or another conference proceedings. Further, submissions must not be concurrently under consideration for publication or presentation elsewhere."
- "Accepted short papers ... will appear in the ECIS 2027 Proceedings."
