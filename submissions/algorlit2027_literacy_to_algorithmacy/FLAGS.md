# Flags: AlgorLit 2027

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026. Cautions kept out of the prose.]

1. **The template asks for "Results".** For a conceptual paper, the results are the reading of the definitions. That reading is the author's: "none describes a second person" is a claim about the definitions examined, and it must be checked against each one before submission.
2. **Oeldorf-Hirsch & Neubaum** focus on social media, so the absence of a counterpart there is partly a matter of scope (card caution).
3. **Gagrčin et al.'s 169 studies** include 5 dissertations and 78 found by citation chasing, so do not call them "169 peer-reviewed".
4. **Graff's pages** are from the 1991 reissue. Graff also calls the myth "partly true".
5. **Kamperman & Graff's own remedy** is person-side (curriculum and disclosure, p. 37). Do not present them as allies of the structural argument.
6. **Wilkinson (1965), Olson (1994) and Goody & Watt (1963)** are not read. They are not cited in the abstract.
7. **Double-blind review:** the abstract does not name the author's prior work.
8. **Confirm the date.** The page's calendar link says 15 October.
