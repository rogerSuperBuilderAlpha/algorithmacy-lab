# Thinkers on the triad — the Substack companion

Public posts, one per paper in [`../../org_frontier/thinkers/`](../../org_frontier/thinkers/). Each post
takes a finished thinker paper and tells its result for a general reader: the thinker's claims in his own
words, what the instrument returned, and which claims held. The paper carries the method and the numbers;
the post carries the story and links back.

Same arrangement as the Hegel series ([`../hegel_coordination/`](../hegel_coordination/)): the lab drafts,
the author revises, nothing posts anywhere without the author sending it himself. Posts do not re-run Φ;
every number in a post appears verbatim in the paper's `results/` and in `ci/reproduce.json`.

## Ground rules

1. A post follows its paper; no post before the paper's probes pass `ci/reproduce.py`.
2. Quote the thinker from the primary text with the edition the paper used. No paraphrase in quotation marks.
3. State the scope once, plainly: Boolean models, not people.
4. Refutations go in the post at the same weight as confirmations.
