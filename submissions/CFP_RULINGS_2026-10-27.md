## Ruling f: the two EGOS drafts side by side

The author's Google Doc "Algorithmacy - EGOS Short Paper Submission" (modified 11 Oct 2026) targets
sub-theme 03 (Hyper-Real). The repo arm [`egos2027_st64_rules_of_play/`](egos2027_st64_rules_of_play/)
targets sub-theme 64 (Rage Against the Digital Machine). EGOS allows one short paper per main author.

| | Sub-theme 03 draft (Google Doc) | Sub-theme 64 arm (repo) |
|---|---|---|
