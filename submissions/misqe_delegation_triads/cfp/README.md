## Dates (verbatim)
- "Special Issue full paper submission deadline: March 1, 2027"
- "First editorial review sent to authors: May 1, 2027"; resubmission "July 1, 2027"; final accepted papers "October 1, 2027"; publication "December 2027".
- The ICIS 2026 workshop stage (abstracts due 1 Sep 2026) has passed. "Workshop participation is not a prerequisite for submission to the Special Issue".

## Format (verbatim, MISQE policies)
- "The manuscript does not exceed 7,500 words (for Research Articles) / ranges between 1,800 - 3,500 words (for MISQE Insight)."
- Research Articles are based on primary research. Insight articles may rest on secondary research or interviews.
- "Submissions should provide clear, accessible insights for executive and practitioner audiences."
- "All papers must be original and not have been previously published or under consideration for publication in any other journal."
- Mark the submission for the special issue on "Agentic AI".

## Scope lines (verbatim)
- "Overseeing Agents in Action: how organizations design and operate guardrails, monitoring, audit, compliance, trust, and intervention mechanisms when agents act with limited supervision".
- "Reorganizing Work and Leadership: how structures, roles, routines, and management practices change when agents handle execution or coordination".
- "We discourage purely technical papers without a managerial dimension, as well as studies in which substituting "GenAI", "machine learning", or "automation" for "agentic AI" would not change the paper's substance."
- The editors want "evidence-based perspectives grounded in empirical research and industry practice".
