# Decision Experience Design in Physical Space

Corrected text of Roger Hunt's essay, archived 4 October 2026, with the record of the citation repair.

**Title.** Decision Experience Design in Physical Space: Algorithmic Intermediation, Material Enforcement, and the Limits of Choice Architecture.

The essay uses the definition of decision experience design from the published Part 1 and applies it to physical and phygital settings. It does not number itself. It sits beside the other pieces in this series: Part 1 in `../published/`, the ethics draft in `../part2_ethics/`, and the decision-markets outline in `../part3_prediction_markets/`.

This folder has no `DRAFT.md`, `literature/references.bib`, `literature/cited_keys.txt`, or `build_references.py`. Those files elsewhere in the series belong to other documents: `../literature/` builds the reference list for `../REVIEW.md`, and `../part2_ethics/literature/` builds the list for the ethics draft. Neither draft is this essay, so neither bibliography was edited. The reference list for this essay is the one at the end of `source_v3.txt`.
