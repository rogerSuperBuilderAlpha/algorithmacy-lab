**Thesis under test (author ruling, 2026-10-03).** Kalshi and Polymarket present as prediction and decision
markets and are sports betting in that dress; the dress is the design failure. The market computes a
probability, and the interface asks the user to trade.

**How to read the two gates.** Hanson passes on academic work: he wrote the theory, and all ten required
papers were read in full. Mansour passes on a different footing, and the essay should say so. He has
published no academic work on markets (his 2019 MIT thesis is on deep learning), so his argument is
reconstructed from Kalshi's court briefs, its CFTC self-certifications and his interviews, and each premise
is tied to the scholarship those documents cite.

is untested (H14; X3 below), and stakes are not what makes the number good (X5 below).

## 4. What changed for the outline

1. **The volume claim verifies with a denominator.** Kalshi: 80% (Pew, notional taker volume, July 2024 to
   April 2026). Polymarket: 39% on Pew; the facet D run on Polymarket's own data gives 65.7% on an
   undocumented unit. Write "two fifths to two thirds" for Polymarket.
2. **Three outline claims need correcting.** Kalda et al. do not "rule out" nudges (`kalda2021smartphone`);
   Rockloff et al. removed all direct marketing, analysed 227 of 405, and call the analysis exploratory
   [checked] (`rockloff2026direct`); the Khamenei death clause predates the market, so "a rule written
   afterward" is wrong as stated (facet D).
3. **One outline claim must be withdrawn.** The Fiedler et al. numeracy null is not in the abstract and the
   full text was not obtained (`fiedler2019myopia`).
4. **One gap narrows.** A study of Kalshi's display now exists: Sah et al. (2026, arXiv, qualitative, 360
   Reddit posts). No experiment on lay reading of a cents price exists.
5. **Two transfers need a caveat.** Rasooly and Rozzi ran on a play-money platform (`rasooly2025manipulable`);
   Cowgill and Zitzewitz's 25% accuracy gain is the Ford comparison only, at p = 0.104 (`cowgill2015corporate`).
6. **The essay's ground is unoccupied.** Hanson, Mansour, the courts and the law reviews argue function,
   structure and category. None of them analyses what the interface asks of the user.
