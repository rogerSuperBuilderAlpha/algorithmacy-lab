## (b) What the search found

**The phrase.** I found no work that uses "decision experience design" for a physical or phygital setting in OpenAlex, Crossref or Consensus. Part 1's own search (2026-09-30) found no scholarly use of the phrase at all, so this result adds only that the physical variant is also absent.

**Choice architecture and the phygital.** I found no paper whose title or abstract joins "choice architecture" with "phygital" or with "servicescape". The two literatures appear not to cite each other at this level. Abstract search cannot see body text, so facet B must check whether servicescape reviews discuss nudging inside the paper.

## (c) What this means for the run

- The gap claim available so far is narrow: no located work applies choice-architecture or decision-design analysis to the phygital under those names. It is an abstract-level result.
- Barlow and others 2026 may already state the "physical holds still, digital adapts" contrast that the adjudication test rests on. Facet A reads it in full first, and facet X states it as a priority claim against Part 4 if it does.
- Mele's 2023 review supports the counter-case line that the term's theory is thin. Facet X owns it.
