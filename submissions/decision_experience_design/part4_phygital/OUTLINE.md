## (a) Thesis

The author's statement (2026-10-04): decision experience design is not only a software or digital concept.
The essay reviews physical decision experience design, starting from the issues raised by researchers who
study physical/digital hybrid experience — the phygital.

The run tests four forms this thesis can take. `README.md` states them; in short: (1) the algorithmic
intermediary reaches into physical space; (2) a physical or human arrangement can itself be an intermediary;
(3) physical design is only choice architecture; (4) the physical layer is where the ruling becomes a fact
and where exit is priced.

## (b) Author rulings
