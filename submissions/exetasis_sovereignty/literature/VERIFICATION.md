# Citation verification

[Maintained by Claude. Each row is checked by hand against a database record (Crossref, the publisher or a library catalogue). An entry is copied into `references.bib` only when its status is **Verified**. Entries taken from the parent library cite their card id in the "record" column.]

**Status values:**
- **Verified:** the record matches the author, title, year and venue.
- **Mismatch:** the record differs from what the draft says (note the difference).
- **Not found:** no database record could be located.
- **Pending:** not yet checked.

**Read depth:** full, abstract only, or record only.
