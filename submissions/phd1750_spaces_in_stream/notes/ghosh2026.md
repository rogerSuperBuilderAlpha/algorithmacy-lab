# ghosh2026: An Experimental Comparison of Cognitive Forcing Functions for Execution Plans in AI-Assisted Writing
Full citation: Ghosh, A., Sarkar, A., Lindley, S., & Poelitz, C. (2026). arXiv:2601.18033v1. PREPRINT. | Read: Abstract, §1, CFF design section (Table 2); results not read in detail → treat as [A+].
Method: controlled experiment n = 214, AI-assisted writing with AI-generated plans; conditions Assumptions CFF (argument analysis), WhatIf CFF (hypothesis testing), Both, None; think-aloud study n = 12 (Abstract).
Claim (Abstract): the Assumptions CFF most effectively reduced overreliance without increasing cognitive load; participants perceived WhatIf as most helpful.
Correction to the packet: neither Buçinca et al. (2021) nor Ghosh et al. state a formalism "D2 = F(D1)"; that notation is the packet's own.
