# kim2025: Fostering Appropriate Reliance on Large Language Models: The Role of Explanations, Sources, and Inconsistencies
Full citation: Kim, S. S. Y., Vaughan, J. W., Liao, Q. V., Lombrozo, T., & Russakovsky, O. (2025). In Proceedings of the 2025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '25). ACM. | DOI: 10.1145/3706598.3714020 | Read: full text body (arXiv 2502.08554v1 author version, §1–§6); appendices skimmed only.

Question: Which features of LLM responses shape users' reliance, and how? (§1)
Claim: Explanations raise reliance on both correct and incorrect answers; sources and visible inconsistencies reduce overreliance on incorrect answers (Abstract; §4.2).
Method: Study 1 think-aloud, N = 16, ChatGPT-4o, June 2024 (§3.1). Study 2 pre-registered 2×2×2 within-subjects (answer correct/incorrect × explanation × sources), N = 308 Prolific, 12 hard binary factual questions; responses pre-generated with ChatGPT-4o / Perplexity (§4.1). Mixed-effects regressions.
Key results (Table 1; §4.2):
- Correct answer, no sources: explanation raised agreement 78.2% vs 67.2% (β = .60, p = .002).
- Incorrect answer: accuracy lower with explanation, 17.2% vs 21.8%; highest with sources only, 31.8%.
- Sources, incorrect answer: agreement 68.2% vs 78.2%.
- Follow-up question rate fell with explanation: 28.2% vs 71.4%.
- Inconsistent vs consistent explanations (incorrect answers): agreement 69.7% vs 83.3%, accuracy 30.3% vs 16.7% (§4.3.1).
- 189 of 308 participants never clicked a source (§4.3.2).
Limitations: objective QA only; one static response, not interaction; 50% LLM accuracy; hard questions (§5.3).
My concerns (mine): responses shown whole, not streamed — so nothing here on temporal presentation.
Quotes: "the properties that make such explanations intelligible and compelling may be precisely those that lead to overreliance." (§5.1)
Cited by me for: the automation-bias facet in LLM form — fluent explanations raise reliance; visible unreliability cues reduce it. A decision space is a place to make such cues visible.
