## Candidate angle [DRAFTED, for ruling]

**Working title:** What the Chief Phygital Officer Must Audit: Decision Experience Design for Phygital Business.

- **Claim.** The call proposes a Chief Phygital Officer (CPO) who "audit[s] outcomes" across physical and digital environments. The audit needs an object. Decision experience design supplies one: where, in a hybrid journey, an algorithm commits an outcome, and whether the customer or employee bound by it can see and contest it at that point.
- **Call topics answered:**
  - why phygital is more than omnichannel;
  - the CPO;
  - leadership competencies for hybrid ecosystems;
  - the ethics of AI-powered decision-making in phygital workplaces;
  - trust-building strategies.
- **Lab constructs used:**
  - decision experience design, from `../decision_experience_design/`, whose Part 4 phygital research already maps ride-hail kerbs and airport gates;
  - algorithmacy, as the competence the CPO's organisation must build.
- **Register.** *Business Horizons* is managerial. The piece would be a framework article with cases and an audit checklist, not a theory paper.
