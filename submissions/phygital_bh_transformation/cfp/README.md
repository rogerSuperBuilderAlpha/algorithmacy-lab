## Aim (compressed)
- **The diagnosis.** The managerial idea of phygital is fragmented and reduced to technology tools. The call lists three faults with current definitions:
  1. they miss the dynamics of moving between physical and digital;
  2. they overlook the connectors and enablers;
  3. above all, they do not put human-centric innovation first.
- **The definition it adopts.** Phygital means Batat's (2024a) "holistic and integrative ecosystem" definition, extended by the Phygital Research Paradigm (2024b). Phygital is not omnichannel.
- **The leadership proposal.** The call proposes a Chief Phygital Officer to replace the Chief Digital Officer. The CPO would oversee research, set strategy, implement models and audit outcomes.

## Topics (the call's four headings, abridged)
1. **Transformation and the human-centric approach:**
   - beyond omnichannel;
   - from technology-first to human-first;
   - sectors: retail, healthcare, education and entertainment;
   - AI and immersive technology;
   - accessibility and ethics.
2. **Leadership and organisational strategy:**
   - the CPO;
   - leadership competencies;
   - organisational structures;
   - employee productivity;
   - the ethics of AI decision-making in phygital workplaces;
   - automation, privacy and trust.
3. **Entrepreneurship and innovation:**
   - phygital-first business models;
   - funding;
   - AI market analysis;
   - sustainability and digital waste;
   - social impact.
4. **Strategic vision:**
   - roadmaps;
   - performance measurement;
   - AI analytics;
   - emerging human–technology interaction;
   - decentralised and AI-powered economies.
