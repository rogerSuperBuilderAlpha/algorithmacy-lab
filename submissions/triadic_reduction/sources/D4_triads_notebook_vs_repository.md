### **3. Key Refinements and Discoveries Made in iit-playground**

While the Triads notebook presents a cohesive theoretical synthesis, iit-playground reveals several critical mathematical nuances and empirical discoveries that refine the notebook's claims:

#### **1. "Genuineness" (Distinction Adicity) vs. "Wholeness" (System \\Phi) Do Not Coincide**

In Tab 1, algebraic teridentity and system-level integrated information are treated as virtually synonymous. However, the exact computations in org\_frontier/thinkers/peirce/paper.md prove that **a genuine triad and an irreducible whole are distinct properties that cross in all four directions**:

1.  **Whole without Genuine Triad:** A pure cycle of one-input dyads (e.g., A' = B, B' = C, C' = A) forms an irreducible whole (\\Phi = 2.0), but contains *no* genuine triad (its maximal distinction has adicity 2). Wholeness arises purely from dynamical return, not triadic mechanism binding.
2.  **Genuine Triad without a Whole:** An exogenous-object sign relation (O' = O, S' = O, I' = S \\leftrightarrow O) contains a genuine triadic distinction spanning \\{O, S, I\\}, but factors globally (\\Phi = 0) because the object is an emit-only source.
3.  **Both:** The canonical control triad (A' = M, M' = A \\land B, B' = M) and genuine giving (R' = G \\land T) possess both \\Phi = 2.0 and adicity 3.
4.  **Neither:** Disconnected monads or uncoupled mutual dyads.

#### **2. The Cause-Side vs. Effect-Side Asymmetry (Resolving the Kempe–Burch Debate)**

When testing whether one-input wirings could generate triads, the lab refuted its initial hypothesis: 78 of 89 one-input wirings reached adicity \\ge 3.

  - The instrument located the exact mathematical split: **Every adicity \\ge 3 occurred on the effect side at branch points** (one node read by multiple others, i.e., Kempe's junction / Burch's branching teridentity).
  - Conversely, **maximum cause-side adicity across all 89 wirings was strictly 2**. No assembly of one-input rules ever caused a node to be *jointly determined* by two others. The lab thus demonstrated that Herzberger's valency restriction holds strictly on the cause side, while Burch's branching teridentity manifests on the effect side.

#### **3. Binary Classification vs. Continuous Synergy Metric**

Tab 2 formulates continuous equations for synergy loss (\\Delta\\text{Syn} and Total Synergistic Effect \\mathcal{L}\_{\\text{TSE}} via Gaussian PID). In contrast, org\_frontier/classifier/ explicitly notes that \\Phi magnitude in Boolean networks is sensitive to state encoding and should be treated strictly as a **binary structural classifier** (literacy vs. algorithmacy), rather than a continuous readability metric.
