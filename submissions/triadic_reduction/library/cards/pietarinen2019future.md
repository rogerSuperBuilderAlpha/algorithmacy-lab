## What the talk may use it for
Pietarinen's edition is now the standard source for Peirce's late writings on existential graphs (1895–1913). The Peirce passages on teridentity that the modern category-theory papers quote, such as the two-triple-points remark and the Lady Welby "bristling with microscopic points of teridentity" letter, are in its volumes or the manuscripts behind them. The talk should use it only to point to where those passages are now edited. No page has been checked.
