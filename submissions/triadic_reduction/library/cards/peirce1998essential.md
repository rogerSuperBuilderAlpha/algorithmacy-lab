## What the talk may use it for
EP2 is the modern reading edition of Peirce's later writings, and a Peirce specialist in the audience may expect its page numbers next to CP. The talk may cite EP2 pages only after checking them in a copy. Until then, cite CP paragraphs (peirce1931papers) and the original printings (peirce1870description, peirce1892critic, peirce1897logic).
