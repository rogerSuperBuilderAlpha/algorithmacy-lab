## What the talk may use it for
The article has not been read, so everything here is as reported by koshkin2022reduction. As reported by Koshkin (p. 6), it gives an informal exposition of Burch's PAL. Koshkin (p. 14) also credits it with the argument that teridentity is non-degenerate on any domain with two distinct elements a ≠ b. If the first two places of R(x,y,z) could be filled independently of the third, R(a,a,a) and R(b,b,b) would force R(a,a,b), which teridentity lacks. This is the same move as the last step of herethcorreia2006teridentity's Theorem 2 proof. The talk can use the argument, attributed as Koshkin reports it, as the plainest statement of why teridentity is not a product of lower-arity relations. It should cite Kerr-Lawson directly only after reading the article.
