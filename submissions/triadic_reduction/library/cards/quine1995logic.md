## What the talk may use it for
The essay matters for what it leaves out. Koshkin 2022 names it as a late text on Peirce's logic, four
decades after the 1954 reduction, in which Quine still does not connect that reduction to Peirce's thesis. The
talk can use it only through Koshkin's report until someone reads it. Any statement that Quine "never"
addressed the thesis rests on Koshkin, not on a reading of Quine.

## What the preliminary sources claim about it
- Koshkin 2022 (arXiv 2406.14058 v1, n. 1, p. 21) — "Quine only discussed the reducibility to triads clause in 1935, and made no mention of Peirce, PRT or what his 1954 reduction to dyads meant for it then, or later. Not in 1981, when he simplified the reduction procedure in the 4th edition of Mathematical Logic, and not in the 1995 essay Peirce’s Logic." — the essay is named in the text only. Koshkin's bibliography does not list it.
