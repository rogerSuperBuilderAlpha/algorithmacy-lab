## What the talk may use it for
Tononi and co-authors use the phrase "the great divide of being" in this essay; it does not occur in the IIT 4.0 paper. The phrase names the line between intrinsic entities, meaning complexes that exist for themselves, and extrinsic entities that exist only relative to something else. The talk may use it for IIT's intrinsic/extrinsic distinction, cited to v1 of the essay (2022), whose title and wording differ from the current version.

## Loci

- v1 p. 8 (§3.3, "Intrinsic entities, extrinsic entities, and the great divide of being") — "Between intrinsic and extrinsic existence, then, passes the most fundamental of divides—the great divide of being. This is the divide between what truly exists in an absolute sense, in and of itself—namely conscious, intrinsic entities—and what only exist in a relative sense, for something else." — the phrase and its gloss.
