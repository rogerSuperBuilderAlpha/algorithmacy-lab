## What the talk may use it for
Burch's own short journal statement of PAL and of his proof sits in this paper. It is open access through Elsevier's open archive, so a reader with an ordinary browser can get it even though this pass could not. The abstract alone establishes three things. Burch frames the reduction thesis as a "valental" result, a result about the number of argument places. The paper only sketches his proof, which the abstract says is "to be published in a forthcoming book" (the 1991 monograph). It also offers PAL as a bridge to topological graph theory. The talk may cite it for Burch's valence framing. It cannot yet cite it for the exact theorem or the operation list, because neither was read.
