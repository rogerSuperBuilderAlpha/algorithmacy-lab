## What the talk may use it for
Stjernfelt's book is the standard modern monograph on Peirce's theory of the proposition (the dicisign), and it sits in Peirce semiotics rather than in the logic of relations. If S4 needs one sentence on how Peirce scholars, as opposed to logicians, now treat the triad, this is a candidate citation. What it says about teridentity or the reduction thesis is unread and must not be asserted.
