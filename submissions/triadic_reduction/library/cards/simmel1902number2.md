## What the talk may use it for
The triad typology. At pp. 165-66 Simmel says the "tri-unity" yields three typical group-forms that two elements cannot produce and that more than three only enlarge: (1) the non-partisan and mediator (from 166), (2) the *tertius gaudens* (from 174), (3) *divide et impera* (from 182). At p. 166 he sets aside the distant third: when the third stands too far from the two, there is no reciprocity embracing all three, only "configurations of twos." That sentence is the English of the 1908 "Zweierkonfigurationen" (simmel1908soziologie, p. 103). The *tertius gaudens* passage at p. 178 states the leverage condition exactly: the third's needed force is set by the balance between the two, not by his own strength.

**Slide-safe (all matched on the page image):** p. 165, the third as "the decisive modification of the configuration from within"; p. 166, "configurations of twos"; p. 167, mediation that "separates in the very act of uniting"; pp. 167-69, the non-partisan and mediator; p. 172, the arbitrator; p. 174, the *tertius gaudens* heading; p. 178, leverage and "paralyze each other"; pp. 182-83, *divide et impera*. Name the edition on the slide ("Simmel 1902, trans. Small"), because Wolff's 1950 wording differs.

## Notes
- The 1902 text is Small's translation of Simmel's manuscript before the 1908 revision; wording differs from Wolff 1950, which translates the 1908 German. Quote one edition per slide and name it.
- Small already uses "dyad" and "triad": "the dyad-group" (I: 44) and "these combinations of the triad scheme" (II: 182), both on the page image, and in the text layer "the dyad type" (I: 41), "from the triad to the dyad type" (II: 180), "the dyad and triad configurations" (II: 189) and "the dyad and triad combinations" (II: 192). Wolff's notes say the terms are not Simmel's, and that is true of the German, but Small used them in English in 1902 (see simmel1950sociology).
- p. 175 reads "the reciprocity within the tetrad" where the sense (and Wolff 1950: 154, "within the triad") requires triad; a fresh OCR of the page image also reads "tetrad", so the slip is in print.
