## What the talk may use it for
Bellucci's book reconstructs the development of Peirce's speculative grammar, the part of logic that classifies signs, across his career. It is a citation for how Peirce scholarship now places the triadic sign relation inside logic. What it says about the reduction thesis or teridentity is unread and must not be asserted.
