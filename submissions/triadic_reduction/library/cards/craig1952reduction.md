## What the talk may use it for
The note is one page and works over the natural numbers. Countably many relations of any degree on the natural
numbers are all definable from a single symmetric dyadic relation, using truth functions and quantification
over the natural numbers. Quine 1954 (n. 1) names this Church–Craig–Quine result as the one its own
result is "distinct from", because it assumes a universe of natural numbers. Without that assumption Quine
needs pairs.

## Loci
- p. 188 (publisher transcription only) — "Where R1, R2, …, ad infinitum are any classes or relations (of any degree) of natural numbers, they can all be defined in terms of a single symmetric dyadic relation of natural numbers together with the truth functions and quantification over natural numbers." — the result, restricted to natural numbers.

## What the preliminary sources claim about it
- (task brief) "Craig & Quine 1957 (reduction to a symmetric predicate)" → corrected. The paper is Craig and Quine, "On reduction to a symmetric relation", JSL 17(3): 188, 1952. The 1956 paper titled "Reduction to a symmetric predicate" is Cobham's (cobham1956reduction). Ackermann's joint review of Quine 1954 and Cobham 1956 appeared in 1957.

## Notes
The companion paper is Church and Quine, "Some theorems on definability and decidability", JSL 17(3): 179–187 (1952). Julia Robinson reviewed the Craig–Quine note in JSL 18(3): 269 (1953), DOI 10.2307/2267427. Neither was read.
