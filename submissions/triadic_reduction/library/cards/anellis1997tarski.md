## What the talk may use it for
The chapter has not been read, so everything here is as reported by koshkin2022reduction and conarroe2020relations. As reported by Koshkin (p. 3), Anellis is the source for the claim that Peirce elaborated and defended the reduction thesis only after reading Kempe's 1886 *Memoir*. Koshkin also reports that Anellis reads Tarski as suggesting there are irreducible relations of every finite adicity, which is the strongest challenge to the positive clause. For S3/S4 this is the bridge from Peirce's logic of relatives to Tarski's relation algebra, the line along which Quine-style reductions and the later clone-theoretic work run. The talk may cite the chapter, as Koshkin reports it, for the Kempe timing and the Tarski reading. It should not attribute either claim to Anellis in his own words until the chapter is read.
