## What the talk may use it for
This paper ties Burch's PAL to the standard algebra of relations. It drops Burch's rule that juxtaposition (Cartesian product) may come only at the end of a construction. With that rule gone, positive PAL (teridentity, permutation, product, join of two hooks) generates exactly the *relational algebra* ⟨Q⟩_RA of Pöschel–Kalužnin, i.e. the pp-definable relations or co-clone. Full PAL with negation generates exactly the Krasner algebra ⟨Q⟩_KA, which gives first-order expressive power (Theorem 9 with Theorem 6). The irreducibility claim is stated for **PAL without teridentity as an atom**, written ⟨·⟩_PAL∖{id3}. Theorem 13: for finite A with |A| ≥ 2 and R2 = all unary and binary relations, ⟨R2⟩_PAL∖{id3} ⊊ ⟨R2⟩_PAL = Rel(A), and likewise for positive PAL. The paper proves only the positive case (Remark 14, a valency-≤2 argument); the proof for full PAL with negation "is omitted" and deferred to a manuscript. The relational product needs no teridentity: ρ∘σ = δ_{m,m+1}(ρ × σ), one join of two hooks (proof of Theorem 9).

## Loci
PDF page numbers of the author copy; the LNAI pagination was not seen.
- p. 1 (abstract) — "we show that the expressive power of PAL is equivalent to the expressive power of Krasner-algebras (which extend relational algebras)." — the main equivalence result.
- p. 2 (Introduction) — "while Burch allows juxtaposition (which corresponds to the cross product) only at the end of the process of term construction, we allow it at arbitrary places. Due to this modification, the proofs Burch is providing for Peirce's thesis do not hold anymore." — Burch's restriction and why it matters.
- p. 3 (Def. 2) — "Please note that relational algebras contain relations of arbitrary (finite) arity and are much more general than Tarski's relation algebras" — "relational algebra" here is *not* Tarski's calculus of relations.
- p. 8 (Def. 7) — "the teridentity just "splits" one edge into three "directions" and needs no relation labels" — teridentity as the branch point in the graph notation.
- p. 11 (§3) — "equals the set DA of all diagonal relations" — ⟨∅⟩_RA, the relational algebra generated by nothing, already contains every diagonal relation, teridentity included.
- p. 11 (§3) — "At first we show that it cannot be (positively) generated by relations of lower arity." — the irreducibility claim, made inside PAL without id3.
- p. 12 (Theorem 13) — "Let A be a finite set with at least two elements" — the scope of Theorem 13.
- p. 12 (after Theorem 13) — "The proof is relatively technical and we do not have enough space for the full proof, therefore it is omitted here and we refer to [HerP]." — the negation case is not proved in this paper.
- p. 12 (Remark 14) — "such that every vertex has valency at most 2" — the positive-case proof is a valency argument.
- p. 12 (Remark 14) — "if an edge connects two relations" (these can be replaced by their relational product) — composing two dyads through a shared hook is a relative product and involves no branch.
- p. 12 (Remark 15) — "With Theorem 13 the first part of Peirce's thesis is proved, that we need (at least) ternary relations to build all relations from a set of basic relations."
- p. 12 (Remark 15) — "This approach works if the domain A is sufficiently large, i. e. the cardinality of the domain is at least as large as the cardinality of the relation to be decomposed." — on Herzberger's reducibility construction.
- p. 12 (Remark 15) — "he allows to extend the underlying domain by new elements generated through the so-called hypostatic abstraction." — Burch's reducibility clause on finite domains enlarges the domain.

## What the preliminary sources claim about it
- hp-2004-bib (TR-S4-019, -048, -103): LNAI 2961: 337–351 → verified. Cite the Springer chapter (DOI above), not the TU Dresden PDF.
- hp-2004-2006-bib (TR-S4-098) → verified for this half. See herethcorreia2006teridentity for the other.
- hp-2004-expressive-power-comparison (TR-S4-134, -151) → verified, with one correction. The comparison is with relational algebras in the Pöschel–Kalužnin sense and with Krasner algebras (Theorem 9). First-order logic enters only through Theorem 6.
- positive-pal-equals-relational-algebra (TR-S4-152) → corrected. Positive PAL equals the relational algebra ⟨Q⟩_RA, which is closure under (R1)–(R5), i.e. pp-definability (Theorem 9(i)). The paper warns that this is "much more general than Tarski's relation algebras".
- positive-pal-equals-relational-algebra (TR-S4-156) → refuted. PAL *with* negation equals the Krasner algebra (Theorem 9(iii)). Neither version equals "classical Tarskian relational algebra".
- full-pal-equals-krasner-first-kind (TR-S4-153) → corrected. ⟨Q⟩_PAL = ⟨Q⟩_KA holds (Theorem 9(iii)), but the paper never says "of the first kind".
- pol-inv-galois-krasner (TR-S4-155) → corrected. The Pol–Inv and Aut–Inv Galois connections appear only in the |A| = 2 proof sketch of Proposition 11. Relational algebras (co-clones) belong to Pol–Inv, and Krasner algebras to Aut–Inv, on finite A.
- teridentity-diagonal-relations (TR-S4-157) → corrected. ⟨∅⟩_RA = ⟨∅⟩_pPAL = D_A, the set of all diagonal relations (§3). Teridentity is one of these, and it generates them all in PAL (Proposition 16).
- hp-2004-teridentity-positive-nonderivable (TR-S4-154, -160) → corrected. Theorem 13 states non-derivability for **finite** A with at least two elements, for full and for positive PAL. Only the positive case is proved here (Remark 14).
- hp-2004-teridentity-positive-nonderivable (TR-S4-159) → corrected. Remark 14 shows that a vertex-minimal positive graph without id3 has only pending edges, so it evaluates to σ1×σ2×σ3 or ρ×σ: Cartesian factorizations. "Diagonal pattern projections" is not in the text.
- hp-2004-positive-only (TR-S4-161) → corrected. The *proof* in the paper covers positive PAL only. The *theorem* also claims the full-PAL case and defers its proof.
- hp-2004-open-question-negation (TR-S4-162) → corrected. The paper asserts the negation case with the proof omitted; the proof appeared in herethcorreia2006teridentity. "Could full Krasner algebras construct =3" is the wrong question, since every Krasner algebra contains all diagonals. The open item was PAL *without teridentity*, with negation.
- hp-removed-burch-restriction (TR-S4-150, -201, -202) → verified. Juxtaposition is allowed "at arbitrary places" (p. 2). These are PAL results linked to relational clones, not "relational-clone proofs".
- hp-2004-closure-coordinate-identification (TR-S4-158) → corrected. The formula =3 = ∆(ζ(= × =)) is from dau2006instances (p. 108), not from this paper. It uses ∆, identification of the first two coordinates (R3), not δij.
- relative-product-needs-branch (TR-S1-005) → refuted. ρ∘σ = δ_{m,m+1}(ρ × σ) joins exactly two hooks (Theorem 9 proof); Remark 14 uses the same move to eliminate internal edges. Teridentity is what PAL needs for *conjunction on shared variables* (Fig. 4, G6 = ρ ∩ σ uses two id3 nodes), not for the relative product.
- dyadic-pp-clone-excludes-teridentity (TR-S4-014, -070, -071, -081, -082, -109, -203, -205) → refuted as stated. Every relational algebra contains D_A (§3), so =3 ∈ ⟨Rel^(≤2)(A)⟩_RA = ⟨Rel^(≤2)(A)⟩_pp for every A. The true theorem is id3 ∉ ⟨Rel^(≤2)(A)⟩_PAL∖{id3} (Theorem 13 here for finite A; herethcorreia2006teridentity Theorem 2 for any A with |A| ≥ 2).
- teridentity-plus-dyads-generate-all (TR-S4-016, -206) → corrected. Theorem 13(a) gives ⟨R2⟩_PAL = Rel(A) for finite A with teridentity *and negation* available. Theorem 13(b) claims the positive version, ⟨R2⟩_pPAL = ⟨Rel^(≤2)(A)⟩_RA = Rel(A), from "the known fact" [PösK79, 1.1.22] ("Remarks concerning the Proof", p. 12). **Conflict:** that right-hand equality fails for |A| = 2. On {0,1} every binary relation is bijunctive and pp-composition preserves bijunctivity, so ⟨Rel^(≤2)({0,1})⟩_RA is the bijunctive co-clone, not Rel({0,1}); koshkin2025completeness Theorem 4 (p. 14) proves that ¬I3 is "non-bijunctive, and hence irreducible to binary relations on Boolean domains", following Schaefer 1978. For finite |A| ≥ 3 the equality holds (koshkin2025completeness Theorems 2–3; Bodnarčuk et al. 1969). The talk must not state Theorem 13(b), or "teridentity plus dyads generate everything" in its positive (pp) form, for |A| = 2 without this caveat. Theorem 13(a), with negation, is not affected.
- relational-clone-definition (TR-S4-009, -010, -011) → corrected. Def. 2 lists (R1) the diagonal Δ_A, (R2a) cyclic shift, (R2b) transposition, (R3) identification of the first two coordinates, (R4) relational product and (R5) adding a fictitious coordinate. Intersection is derived (R12), not basic. Theorem 6 gives the equivalence with closure under ∃, ∧, = formulas (pp). TR-S4-009 is incomplete, TR-S4-010 is right in substance, and TR-S4-011 has the list wrong.
- teridentity-diagonal-definition (TR-S1-026, -034, -041, -046) → verified: id3 := {(a,a,a) | a ∈ A} (Def. 7). "Join-irreducible" does not occur (see teridentity-join-irreducible on herethcorreia2006teridentity).
- hp-refined-pal (TR-S4-006) → verified: this paper allows juxtaposition "at arbitrary places" (p. 2), and herethcorreia2006teridentity uses one join operator where Burch used two (its p. 2). dau2006instances is the third paper of the group.
- prt-1990s-universal-algebra (TR-S4-190) → corrected: Herzberger 1981 and Burch 1991 work in algebraic logic. This paper made the link between PAL and relational algebras/co-clones in 2004 (Theorem 9), and koshkin2025completeness says so: the thesis's "connection to universal algebra and pp-decompositions was only pointed out in 2004 by Hereth and Pöschel".

## Notes
- The author PDF has 14 letter-size pages and the LNAI chapter has 15, so the page numbers above are not LNAI page numbers.
- Theorem 13(b)'s right-hand equality holds only for |A| ≥ 3; for |A| = 2 it conflicts with koshkin2025completeness Theorem 4 (see above). Koshkin does not flag the conflict: he cites this paper ([11]) as the one that "comes closest" to spelling out the finite-domain result (koshkin2025completeness p. 3). No correction by Hereth Correia or Pöschel has been seen. herethcorreia2006teridentity and hereth2011logic prove only the strict inclusion, which is unaffected.
- NOT FOR SLIDES: carder's own check. An earlier pass verified the |A| = 2 failure by enumeration (majority polymorphism, x⊕y⊕z = 0). That check is the carder's, not a printed result; cite koshkin2025completeness Theorem 4 instead.
- The Herzberger and Burch statements in Remark 15 are this paper's reports; neither primary was read (see herzberger1981theorem, burch1991reduction).
- [HerP], "Peircean Algebraic Logic and Relational Clones", was cited as a manuscript in preparation. The full proof later appeared in Hereth's 2008 TU Dresden dissertation (per hereth2011logic), which has not been read.
