## What the talk may use it for
This is the one text in which Quine discussed Peirce's reduction thesis, according to Koshkin 2022. It was
a review of CP vol. III (*Exact Logic*), the volume that holds Peirce's logic of relatives. Koshkin says Quine
discussed only the clause that every relation reduces to triads, the positive clause, and says it briefly.
Nineteen years later Quine published his reduction to a single dyadic predicate (quine1954reduction) without
mentioning Peirce. If the talk uses the 1935 passage, it has to quote it. Nobody on the project has read it.

## Notes
- Which review? Quine reviewed three volumes of the *Collected Papers* for *Isis*: vol. II in Isis 19: 220–229 (1933, doi 10.1086/346737), vol. III in Isis 22: 285–297 (doi 10.1086/346904) and vol. IV in Isis 22: 551–553 (Feb 1935, doi 10.1086/346931), per Crossref and wvquine.org. Koshkin's "1935 review" is taken here to be the vol. III review, because vol. III is *Exact Logic* and holds the logic of relatives, and because the vol. IV review runs to only three pages. That is an inference. Koshkin's bibliography lists neither review, so a reading has to confirm which one holds the passage.
- The issue date is December 1934, while Koshkin and wvquine date the review 1935. Cite it as Isis 22 (1934–35): 285–297, or give Crossref's date, and keep the year the same everywhere in the talk.
