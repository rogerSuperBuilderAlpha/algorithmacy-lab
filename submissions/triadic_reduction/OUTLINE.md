### Slide 3: The argument is arithmetic (S1 · 55 s)
```
2 + 2 − 2 = 2
3 + 3 − 2 = 4

"artiads, or even-ads, can produce only artiads"   (Monist 1897, p. 183)
"no number of straight roads put end on end will give more than two termini"   (CP 1.363)
```
- **Carries:** P2, P3.
- **Shows:** peirce1897logic p.183, and peirce1890guess CP 1.363 p.188 (both page images).
- **Beat:** Two dyads joined at one blank leave "only … two blank places", so even plus even minus two stays even. Triads are odd and reach every adicity. Chains and rings are all dyads can make.

### Slide 4: The branch is the triad (S1 · 50 s)
```
"every node of bonds is equivalent to a relative"   (1897)

Kempe 1886: a triad = unit λ + three links
Peirce 1892: Kempe "virtually shows that my algebra is perfectly adequate to expressing that A gives B to C"
```
- **Carries:** P4, P5, C1.
- **Shows:** peirce1897logic pp.170–171; kempe1886memoir §330; peirce1892critic CP 3.424.
- **Beat:** The arithmetic holds only if a three-way junction counts as a relation. Peirce argued that it does (CP 1.346: "You may think … is not a triadic idea. But analysis will show that it is so."). Against Kempe he conceded expressibility, got "by adding to the universe of concrete things the abstraction 'this action.'", and denied composition, "but not to surrender it". From its first round, the dispute concerns the junction.

### Slide 8: 1915 and 1954: one dyadic predicate for everything (S3 · 75 s)
```
Löwenheim 1915, §4 Satz 6: a new domain of pairs (n² elements)
Herbrand → Kalmár 1936 → Quine 1954:
a theory with "only one predicate letter, and it a dyadic one"
assuming "a fragment of set theory"
```
- **Carries:** P15–P18, C3.
- **Shows:** lowenheim1915moglichkeiten pp.463–64; kalmar1936zurueckfuehrung p.137; quine1954reduction p.180.
- **Beat:** Quine proved a translation theorem for interpreted theories over a pair-closed universe. He does not name Peirce on the pages read; his footnote cites Kalmár and Church–Craig–Quine. Later readers turned it into a refutation of Peirce. Burch writes that the thesis was "doubted by many, especially after the publication of a proof by Willard Van Orman Quine".

### Slide 9: Every reduction mints a third (S3 · 65 s)
```
Kempe: the unit λ
Peirce 1892: 'this action'
Löwenheim: pairs of elements
Quine: {x, y}

Expressible over a larger universe ≠ composed from pairs among the same parties
```
- **Carries:** C3b, P5.
- **Shows:** kempe1886memoir §330; peirce1892critic CP 3.424; lowenheim1915moglichkeiten p.464; quine1954reduction p.180. Label this slide as the talk's own argument.
- **Beat:** Each reduction adds an object that stands for the tuple, and Peirce had conceded exactly that kind of expressibility in 1892. Koshkin says pairing "conceals triadicity". In coordination, the minted object is a third party, so the reduction moves the third and does not remove it.

```

on exactly what constructive resources are to be allowed"   (SEP §14)

Koshkin: "It cannot simply be that Peirce and Quine are both right"   (2022)
```
- **Carries:** P20, P24, P19.
- **Shows:** burch2021sep §14 (verified); koshkin2022reduction p.4 (**add the locus first**; fallback is p.20, "PRT is not gerrymandered, it is a deep fact about the fine structure of relations").

### Slide 12: The lab's move: give the junction a direction (S5 · 80 s)
```
Triad = a party jointly determined by two others, inside a whole that does not factor

A → M ← B      M' = A ∧ B,  A' = M,  B' = M
Φ=2.000000 core=('A', 'M', 'B') adicity=3 PASS
```
- **Carries:** D2, D3, P27, P28.
- **Shows:** CI thinkers-peirce-h1-irreducibility, control string.
- **Beat:** IIT 4.0 (Albantakis et al. 2023) reads a system's cause–effect structure. A dyad becomes a one-input rule, a relation becomes an irreducible distinction, and adicity is counted on the cause side and the effect side separately. The PASS certifies that M is determined by A and B together. Say once: our classifier used to call any Φ > 0 triadic, and we now require joint determination as well.

### Slide 13: Composing pairs: the prediction failed, then was located (S5 · 85 s)
```
89 one-input wirings, n = 3, 4
Pre-registered H1: REFUTED   adicity counts={2: 11, 3: 66, 4: 12}
Split post hoc: max cause-side adicity over all=2
branched wirings=78 … Φ>0 in 0
```
- **Carries:** P29, P30, C6.
- **Shows:** CI thinkers-peirce-h1-irreducibility (four strings). copy_CCA "(cause 2, effect 3)" is spoken only.
- **Beat:** Branches produced three-term facts because the rendering left out Peirce's premise that a node is a relative. Split cause from effect and his arithmetic reappears: no branch gives 2, a branch gives 3 or more, and every excess sits on the effect side. Both facts follow from the definitions: one input means one cause, and a branch leaves some node unread, so the whole factors. This is a calibration, not a discovery.

### Slide 16: What the argument licenses (S6 · 115 s)
```
Among fixed parties, one-input mediation never composes joint determination.
Joint determination in a whole exists.
So algorithmacy has a target literacy cannot build.

Boolean models, not people.
```
- **Carries:** D5, P35-bridge, P36-grain, P44–P46, C9, C10.
- **Shows:** no quotation and no number.
- **Beat:** Restate the conditional in the causal sense. The Quinean reply either mints a jointly determined node or merges the parties. Under M′ = A ∧ B, when A = 1, M follows B, so the worker must infer the other side. That is conditional necessity: an ontological argument about the target, not about triads as primitives.
