## Title
**Do Triads Reduce to Dyads? The Ontology of Algorithmacy** (the author's choice; the drafted options were
"Can a Triad Be Built from Pairs?", "Welded into One Fact" and "The Third That Pairs Cannot Make").

## Question
Literacy coordinates two people through a medium, and algorithmacy coordinates them through an algorithmic third. If every triad reduces to pairs, algorithmacy is literacy applied twice. Do some triads resist reduction in the sense that matters for coordination?

## Thesis (the only one)
Algorithmacy is distinct from literacy because the two competences answer to different forms of coordination.
- **Literacy** answers to one-input mediation: a medium whose next state answers to one party at a time, however it is chained or looped.
- **Algorithmacy** answers to joint determination in a whole: a third that both parties determine, which acts back on both, in a system that does not factor.

Whether triads reduce to pairs depends, in logic, on the constructive resources allowed (Herzberger, Burch). The regimes split on the status of an undirected three-way junction. Causal determination gives that junction a direction. Among fixed parties, one-input determination never composes into joint determination, and joint determination inside a whole exists. So the antecedent "some triads are irreducible" holds in the sense that matters for coordination, and algorithmacy has a legitimate target.

This is **an ontological argument for the necessity of algorithmacy**, in two precise senses:
- It is ontological about the *target*: the kind exists as structure and cannot be composed from literacy's kind.
- Its necessity is *conditional*: wherever coordination takes this form, the competence it demands is not a composition of competences for one-input mediation.

## What that licenses
- Peirce's negative clause is a valency theorem, conditional on counting a three-way junction as a relation, and Peirce stated it in 1870.
- Löwenheim and Quine establish expressibility with one dyadic predicate over a pair-closed universe. They do not establish composition from pairs among the same parties. Every such reduction mints the third (λ, 'this action', pair-elements, {x, y}); that point is the talk's argument.
- IIT 4.0 separates what the logical debate ran together: fan-out (effect side), fan-in (cause side) and wholeness (Φ). This locates the disagreement and does not resolve it.
- On the lab's Boolean models:
  - one-input wirings never exceed cause-side adicity 2, a near-definitional calibration against Peirce's arithmetic;
  - joint determination in a whole exists in two motifs, the control family and the pragmatic sign.
- Literacy's coordination is a chain even though every sign is triadic, because the sign triad sits inside one reader.
- A human arbitrator meets the same criterion. The target is triadic coordination, and its mass algorithmic instance is a historical premise.
