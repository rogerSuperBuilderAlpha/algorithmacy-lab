## Five most consequential problems, ranked

1. **The slide 13 "Causal Collapse Theorem" does not exist, and the lab refutes its content.** One-input channels that close into cycles are Φ = 2.0 wholes: copy_BCA, dyad_mutual, and 8 of the 89 wirings (REGISTER TR-S5-123; ci/reproduce.json:78, 81, 83; CLAIM.md:69-70).
2. **Slide 14 misreports the lab's own numbers.** First, the 89 wirings are not all Φ = 0: 8 have Φ > 0, and the "Dyadic Ring" of row 2 is one of them. Second, the 5 integrated forms range from Φ = 0.21 to 2.0, and only one reads 2.0. Third, no run measured synergy, so "Synergy Without Whole" and "Whole Without Synergy" relabel joint determination as a quantity that was never computed.
3. **Teridentity is equated with PID synergy and with "measure-theoretic synergy" as a pillar** (slides 12 and 15; script S9, "pure causal synergy"). No source makes this link (williams2010decomposition.md:44). The lab ran no PID. By the carder's arithmetic the AND control is not pure synergy (Red ≈ 0.311 bit).
4. **The logic results are mis-scoped to "clones", "project-join" and "primitive positive" operations, where they fail** (slides 8, 11; script S6, S7). Under pp and projoin, =₃ = (x=y)∧(y=z), and ternaries reduce to binaries on \|D\| ≥ 3. The irreducibility results hold in PAL only: Burch with his restriction, and Hereth Correia & Pöschel. Burch 1991 is unread. The author name is Hereth Correia & Pöschel (2006). The Koshkin year and content are also off (2022 vs 2024 vs 2025), and "strict" ternarity is wrong.
5. **The Quine material has errors of fact.** The predicate is F, not J or ∈. The pair is Kuratowski's, not Wiener–Kuratowski. "Quine's Fallacy" is a title the lock forbids (CLAIM.md:46). "Infinite universe required" is false as a requirement, since Löwenheim's pair-domain is n². "Fatal blow to Peirce" implies an aim that Quine never had. **Also:** the "complexus of dyadic relations" quote is attributed to 1870 but is CP 2.274, c. 1902.
