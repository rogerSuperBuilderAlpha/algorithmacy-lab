## Slide 1 — title
### Do Triads Reduce to Dyads?

## Slide 2 — the question
### If triads reduce to dyads, algorithmacy names nothing new
Literacy: a medium that answers to one party at a time.
Algorithmacy: a third that both parties determine.
Do some triads resist reduction — in the sense that matters for coordination?

## Slide 7 — Simmel

```

## Slide 8 — three is not enough
### Three parties are not yet a triad

## Slide 10 — every reduction mints a third
### Every reduction mints a third
Kempe: a unit · Peirce: "this action" · Löwenheim: pairs · Quine: {x, y}
Expressible over a larger universe ≠ composed from pairs among the same parties

## Slide 12 — the lab's move
### Give the junction a direction
Triad = a party jointly determined by two others, inside a whole that does not factor.
```
A → M ← B      M′ = A and B;  A′ = M;  B′ = M
Φ = 2.0   core {A, M, B}
```

## Slide 13 — composing pairs
### Composing pairs: the prediction failed, then was located
89 one-input wirings: pre-registered H1 REFUTED
Split afterwards: cause side never above 2
Every excess on the effect side — the branch

## Slide 14 — Φ alone is not the test
### Φ alone is not the test
Wholes with no triad: mutual dyad, copy ring, imitation of giving — Φ = 2.0
Joint determination in a whole: the control, genuine giving, the pragmatic sign
Joint determination without a whole: Simmel's majority — Φ = 0

## Slide 15 — two forms of coordination
### Two forms of coordination
Literacy: writer → text → reader — a chain
Algorithmacy: A → M ← B, M read by both — a whole
Every sign is triadic. The coordination is a chain.
The target is older than AI: Simmel's arbitrator has the same form.

## Slide 16 — what follows
### A target literacy cannot build
Among fixed parties, one-input mediation never composes joint determination.
Joint determination in a whole exists.
So algorithmacy has a target of its own.
Boolean models, not people.
