## Sub-theme 64
- **Title:** "Rage Against the Digital Machine? Rethinking Humanness and the Possibility for Human Connection in Times of Emerging Technologies"
- **Convenors:** Frank Martela (Aalto University), Sophia Galière (Université Côte d'Azur), Johan Alvehus (Lund University)
- **Lines from the call:**
  - "Who sets the rules of play in organizations mediated by opaque algorithms? How do new digital tools reshape the balance of power and authority within the organization?"
  - "How do workers, leaders, and algorithms co-construct new rules of the game—bending, hacking, or resisting the systems that seek to manage them?"
  - "How do digital traces, status indicators, or AI analytics redefine what counts as presence, effort, or belonging?"
  - "We welcome conceptual, empirical, and methodological contributions".
