written. Moving the three missing rivals in at their present length would add 1,155 words and nearly
fill the section, so each family gets a paragraph or two, and the hearings' detail stays in
`../../lima_pdw/literature/steelmans/`.

6,000 between them. The difference — about 3,500 words — is new writing, and most of it is §4.
