# Citation audit of `ASSEMBLED_2026-10-10.md`

10 October 2026, by Claude. Scope: all 99 reference entries, every in-text citation, every quotation and
page number. The fourteen corrections listed in section 4 are already applied to `MAPPED_2026-10-10.md`
and `ASSEMBLED_2026-10-10.md`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **All 99 references are real.** None is fabricated.
- **Every in-text citation has an entry and every entry is cited** (read by eye, then confirmed). One
  entry, Orlikowski & Scott (2023), was uncited; it is now cited.
- **23 quotations checked. 19 exact and on the stated page. 3 needed fixes** (now made). One has exact
  words and a page number I could not verify (Zhou et al.); it carries an `[AUTHOR: …]` flag in the text.
- **Eight uses claimed more than the source supports.** All eight are reworded.
- **What I could not do:** read most sources in full. 46 entries rest on the abstract or the database
  record only, and six books rest on reviews or a catalogue record. Those uses are accurate to what I
  could see and no further.
