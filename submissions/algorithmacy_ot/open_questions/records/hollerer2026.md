# Höllerer, M. A., Quack, S., & Delbridge, R. (2026). "Catalysing & Crystallizing" and "Conversations & Controversies": Facilitating debate in Organization Theory.

## What it argues
The co-editors introduce a fourth section of the journal, "Catalysing & Crystallizing", and set it beside the existing "Conversations & Controversies" section. The older section curates exchanges of two or three papers that deepen and sharpen debates already salient in organization and management studies. The new one is a guest-edited special themed section of three to five papers, solicited through an annual call, that brings in emerging or peripheral topics, ideas from neighbouring disciplines and new conceptual vocabularies, with the aim of agenda setting. A table contrasts the two sections on purpose, focus, format, initiation, editorial model and intended outcome. Both sections must meet the journal's general standard: a coherent theoretical argument that significantly contributes to understanding of organizations and management. (reader's paraphrase)
