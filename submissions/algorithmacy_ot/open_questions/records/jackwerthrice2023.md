# Jackwerth-Rice, Koehrsen & Mattes (2023). The Resources of Institutional Entrepreneurs in Different Structural Settings.

## What it argues
The article asks which resources institutional entrepreneurs (IEs) use, and shows that the answer depends on structural setting. Drawing on Giddens, Bourdieu and Fligstein, the authors distinguish free and positional field resources from personal resources, each of which can be material, cultural, social, symbolic or political. They then review the 40 most-cited case studies of institutional entrepreneurship. Central IEs rely on organizational positional resources; peripheral IEs rely on free resources and on personal resources, above all skills; in emerging fields resources must be imported from mature fields, and early peripheral entrepreneurs tend to be superseded by central organizations later. Field position makes no clear difference to who becomes an IE. (reader's paraphrase)
