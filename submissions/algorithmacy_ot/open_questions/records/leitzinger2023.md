# Leitzinger & Waeger (2023). Keep Your Eyes on the Prize: A Goals-Based Approach to Studying Social Movements in Markets.

## What it argues
The review claims that research on social movements and markets has neglected the content of movement goals and the early stages of movement emergence. The authors sort market-based movements into three goal types: alteration (change firm or industry practices), creation (build new market categories) and elimination (remove products, industries or markets). They then follow each type through a four-phase life cycle (emergence, action, interaction, settlement) and argue that the initial goal shapes tactics, target responses, risks such as cooptation or mission drift, and outcomes. Each phase ends with research opportunities, and the discussion adds goal emergence, goal selection and movement interactions, plus a call to widen the contexts studied. (reader's paraphrase)
