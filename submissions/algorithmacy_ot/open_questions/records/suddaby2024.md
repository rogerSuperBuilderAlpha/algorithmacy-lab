# Suddaby, R. (2024). What Theory Is, What Theory Was: Whence and Whither Theory.

## What it argues
Suddaby critiques Abend's essay, book (2023) and article (2008) as a call for action over abstraction. He argues, first, that the call is not new: social science has long swung between grand theory and pragmatic empiricism, and organization theory has already settled into middle-range, grounded and mechanism-based work. Second, Abend misreads pragmatism, since James and Dewey integrated theory into practice rather than abandoning it. Third, the difficulty of defining organizations or institutions is better met by process views (Abbott, Weick, Morgan) that attend to what a phenomenon "was" as well as what it "is", not by giving up construct clarity, which Abend himself uses well. Finally, he likens Abend's "used tomorrow" pragmatism to Craig Venter's theory-free data mining and warns that AI-driven, data-mined science fails in dynamic, self-aware social contexts; organization theory should produce syncretic knowledge. (reader's paraphrase)
