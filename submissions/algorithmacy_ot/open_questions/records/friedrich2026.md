# Friedrich (2026). Beyond Human Democratic Organizing: Posthuman Affirmations.

## What it argues
(reader's paraphrase) Organizational democracy theory rests on humanist assumptions (rational individuals, rights, formal equality) and so leaves out the ecological and material dependencies that sustain organizations. The author calls this the Anthropocentric Dilemma of Democracy and uses Rosi Braidotti's posthuman feminism to rework it. A speculative vignette set in 2050 shows an assembly that includes a heatwave, an extinct bee and a future child. From it the paper builds three registers of posthuman democratic organizing: acknowledging heterogeneous assemblages, attuning zoe/geo/technobodies, and cultivating an affirmative relational ethics. Each register closes with propositional questions for organizations. The paper also offers a speculative, affirmative mode of "critical meta-theorizing" and draws out implications for democratic organizing, posthuman organization theory and affirmative theorizing.
