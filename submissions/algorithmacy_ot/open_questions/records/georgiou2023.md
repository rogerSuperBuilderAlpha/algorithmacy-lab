# Georgiou, A., & Arenas, D. (2023). Community in Organizational Research: A Review and an Institutional Logics Perspective.

## What it argues
Thornton, Ocasio and Lounsbury (2012) added community to the inter-institutional system, but they built the ideal type mostly from studies of local communities. Georgiou and Arenas review 172 papers (1991-2020) from highly ranked journals, group them by bibliographic coupling into communities of place, practice, users and firms, and match each type against the original description of the community logic (pattern matching). They find one variant of the logic per type, with a shared root metaphor, a common boundary. They redefine the community logic as creating and maintaining boundaries within which members form "thick" relationships. They answer Mutch's (2021) challenge that community is a social context, not a logic, and they set out implications for community-organization relationships and a research agenda. (reader's paraphrase)
