# Bartunek, Hansen, Cable & Ianniello (2025). Generative Resistance: The importance of enabling differences.

## What it argues
(reader's paraphrase) Organizational change research treats resistance as the shadow of change and control, and paradox research treats it as a condition to be dissolved. The authors argue that resistance has value in its own right and name three contributions: it surfaces deep societal conflicts, it builds group and organizational identity, and it sustains differences over time. Three narrative vignettes carry the argument: World Vision's brief reversal of an employment policy, US Catholic sisters' response to a Vatican Apostolic Visitation, and Texas death-penalty defence attorneys engaged in "preserving the record" (p. 10; wording corrected by verifier). The theoretical payoff is a "neither/nor" stance that sits beside the "both/and" stance of paradox theory and keeps contradictions alive rather than resolving them. The practical payoff is advice to change agents and recipients about reading resistance in conflicts that cannot be settled locally.

## Open questions
None stated. (reader's paraphrase) The article has no section headed future research or limitations. The "Implications for generative practice" section (p. 14; locator corrected at hand-check) and the "Conclusion" (pp. 14-15) give advice to leaders, change agents and change recipients, and the closing hope is stated as a hope, not as a request to researchers: "Hopefully, however, the narratives about resistance we have presented will stimulate new and productive ways of leading, managing, and responding to organizational change" (p. 15, "Conclusion"). I did not code that as an invitation to research.

## Implicit openings
1. (reader's inference) "Of course, sustaining tensions over time is not at all easy." (p. 12, "Contradictory and interrelated elements") leaves open what conditions let actors hold differences in "reflexive dialectic tension" rather than shutting conversation down, as in the World Vision case.
2. (reader's inference) "While this vignette does not include such movement over time, the other two vignettes do." (p. 8, World Vision vignette) leaves open whether publicly surfaced conflict later produces movement, and under what circumstances.
3. (reader's inference) "It will likely be very challenging (cf. Reynolds & Trehan, 2003), as indicated in all three illustrations." (p. 14, "Differences foster learning") leaves open how the capacity to hold differences is developed.
