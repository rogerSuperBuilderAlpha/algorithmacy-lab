### Fu et al. (2025): what the abstract alone may carry

Cited as "(Fu et al., 2025, abstract)" every time, the introduction may carry four things:

- that they study consumers' attribution of responsibility for service failures where a platform and an individual provider are both involved, in attribution theory;
- the finding in their words: consumers "transfer responsibility for service failures to a platform, even after recognizing the individual provider as the source of the problem";
- the three conditions under which consumers "are more likely to attribute responsibility to the platform": "high (vs. low) intermediation platforms", "severe (vs. mild) service failures", "encounter (vs. core) service issues";
- the evidence: "A text analysis of 24,183 consumer complaints and 4 experimental studies".

It may not carry what Fu et al. do not ask or study, anything on the provider's side, how intermediation was measured, an effect size, which result came from complaints and which from experiments, or "measure whom".
