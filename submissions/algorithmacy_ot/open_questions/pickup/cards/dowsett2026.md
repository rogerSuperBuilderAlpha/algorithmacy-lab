## What the work says

From qualitative research with creative workers (designers), Dowsett argues that earlier tools left the creative worker in control, whereas GenAI mimics the signature skill of creative work, displaces expertise in some stages and tasks of the creative process, and lets novices act in expert domains. Experts resist GenAI for ideation but benefit from handing it routine tasks, which qualifies Hsu and Bechky's claim that experts gain less than novices.
