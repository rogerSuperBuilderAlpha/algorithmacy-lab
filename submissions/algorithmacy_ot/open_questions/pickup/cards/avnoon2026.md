## What the work says

Avnon arranges ethnographies of expert work with machine-learning AI along a continuum of four arrangements: "almost" (near-automation with humans still in the loop), "too much" (algorithmic management and platform labour), "yay" (new markets, roles and co-productions) and "nah" (experts who ignore or override AI), and argues that no expertise has been wholly replaced. On expertise she asks rather than answers: deskilling of trainees is named as the coming problem, and "only time and further study will tell" (p. 8).

## Caution

The full text confirms the abstract reading and settles the earlier guess about the Stark citation: it does sit in the algorithmic-management ("too much") arrangement, but only as one name in a list, six times over, so the work counts as citing stark2024 without engaging it. The triage label "possible_answer" for candidate 5 does not hold; the review maps arrangements and poses the expertise question without offering an answer.
