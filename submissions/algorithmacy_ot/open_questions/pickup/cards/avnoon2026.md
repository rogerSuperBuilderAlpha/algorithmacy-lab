## What the work says

Avnoon sorts ethnographic studies into four arrangements of expert and AI work — near-replacement ("no human in the loop"), AI that is "too much" (algorithmic management and platform work), AI that opens new markets, roles and co-productions ("yay"), and AI that experts ignore ("nah"). On the abstract, the review places algorithmic management as one arrangement among four and does not pose candidate 1, 3, 7 or 8 as such.
