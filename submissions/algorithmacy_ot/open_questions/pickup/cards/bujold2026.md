## What the work says

Bujold, Gaudet and Parent-Rocheleau model the algorithm as an intermediary in the worker–organization exchange: organizational choices set the system's materiality, workers judge it through procedural, informational and distributive justice signals, and trust in the system carries over to trust in the organization and to reciprocity. They conclude that social exchange principles still apply but "require some theoretical modification" (abstract).

## Caution

The intermediary here stands between a worker and an organization, not between two people, and the model concerns attitudes (justice, trust, reciprocity) rather than what the worker must be able to do. It is the nearest work in this batch to algorithmacy's third position, but it does not take the system as evaluating and binding both sides. Abstract only.
