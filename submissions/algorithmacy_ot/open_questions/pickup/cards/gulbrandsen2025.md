## What the work says

Gulbrandsen and Just review Nordic studies of digital public debate through affordances and sort participation into human-, issue- and technology-centric types, each bottom-up or top-down; their technology-centric studies show platforms organising publics, so that the platform's organising and the public's communicating are one process. They never connect this to Stark and Vanden Broeck's call: they treat it at the level of democracy and the public sphere, not of the organization's own communication field or its genres.

## Caution

The fit to candidate 1 is my reading. The authors cite Stark and Vanden Broeck only for "platforms remain businesses" (p. 160) and do not take up OQ-stark2024-06 as such. The participants they discuss are citizens and institutions, not workers.
