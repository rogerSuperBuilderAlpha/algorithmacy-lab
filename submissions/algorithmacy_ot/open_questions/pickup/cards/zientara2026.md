## What the work says

Zientara argues that user acceptance — continued use, compliance, adaptation — is a poor proxy for the ethical legitimacy of algorithmic management where workers face power asymmetry and constrained choice, because acceptance may reflect "necessity, habituation, or lack of alternatives rather than moral endorsement" (abstract). He replaces it with three evaluative questions, the first of which is candidate 8's, and calls for governance that foregrounds accountability, power and justice.
