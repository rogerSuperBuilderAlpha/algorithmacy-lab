## What the work says

Phillips argues that institutional theory, organizational identity and sensemaking have not been updated for organizations "constituted by" intelligent technologies, and traces the neglect to four causes, the first being the framing of technology as exogenous. On the abstract this is the structure horn of candidate 7 (OQ-stark2024-08, technology as organizational structure) pressed by the author of the artificial-persons question (OQ-phillips2024-09).
