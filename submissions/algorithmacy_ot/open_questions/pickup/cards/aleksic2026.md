## What the work says

Aleksić studies employees' experience of broad-application AI through affordances in three essays: an HR chatbot survey across three multinationals (chatbot use goes with higher autonomy, competence and relatedness), a qualitative study of "shadow AI" and "agency crafting" across temporal orientations, and a conceptual chapter on generative AI as a "medium" for creative action (pp. 235–237). The thesis argues for an employee perspective against managerial views of AI as a bundle of functions.
