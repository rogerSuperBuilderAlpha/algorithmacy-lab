## What the work says

From an exploratory qualitative study of GenAI use in large organizations (consulting deliverables), Hubert argues that performance moves from the result to its defensibility, and names "job crafting algorithmique des preuves": the collective capacity to place human checkpoints where attribution forms, so that each output becomes a traceable chain of evidence (p. 23). Where such evidentiary artefacts are absent, users either work around the tool or depend on it blindly, and juniors risk losing the skill of synthesis (p. 25).

## Caution

A doctoral conference chapter in French with an exploratory sample. The "blind dependence" remark is one sentence, not a finding developed from data, and concerns reliance on the tool's output, not on guidance that displaces a colleague. The answer is organizational (provenance logs, review routines, HR practices), not an individual capacity, which sets it apart from algorithmacy's answer.
