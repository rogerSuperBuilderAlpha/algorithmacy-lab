## The triage question answered

The "second triad" is not a mediating third between people. Decius takes the triad of social cognitive theory — reciprocal causation among person, behaviour and environment — and adds a second, AI layer coupled to the human one.

## Candidates touched

**7. Artificial persons — takes_up.**
> "This conceptual article develops a Human–AI Social Cognitive Theory that extends social cognitive theory's triadic reciprocity (i.e., reciprocal causation among person, behavior, and environment) to conjoined human–AI agency." (abstract)

**3. Partner or medium — passing (party side).**
> "The theory conceptualizes human and AI characteristics, behaviors, and environments as two coupled layers" (abstract)

## What the work says

Decius models human and machine agency as two coupled layers, each with its own characteristics, behaviours and environment, and states propositions about how within-layer and cross-layer interactions shape cognitive, relational (trust, coordination) and structural (roles, rules, governance) outcomes. On the abstract, the AI is a second agent beside the person, which is the party premise of candidate 3 and close to the "artificial persons" premise of candidate 7.

## Caution

Abstract only; I could not see where or how Decius cites Phillips et al. The name "triad" is a false friend for algorithmacy's triad (two people and an intermediary): the two share a word, not a structure. Whether the full text places a second human anywhere in the coupled layers is unknown.
