## What the "second triad" relates

The second triad relates components, not persons. Bandura's triadic reciprocity links three components of one human agent: characteristics (cognitions, beliefs, dispositions), behaviour, and environment (p. 6). Decius "mirror[s]" that triad into a parallel AI layer — AI characteristics (model-side properties), AI behaviours (episodic outputs) and the AI environment (pipelines, audit, governance) — and calls the result "a dual-layer triad of characteristics, behavior, and environment for both humans and AI" (p. 10). His propositions then run within a component across layers (Propositions 1–3, e.g. human behaviour ↔ AI behaviour), across components and layers (Propositions 4–6), and from these to cognitive, relational and structural outcomes (Propositions 7–9). The relata are therefore six components, three human and three AI; the "second triad" is the AI copy of person–behaviour–environment.

> "my central response is to mirror and thereby extend Bandura's triad: characteristics, behaviors, and environments appear in the new theory both within the conventional human layer, in Bandura's sense, and within an AI-related layer." (p. 4)

**No third party evaluates and binds two people.** The human layer is one person, or at most "people and collectives" (p. 25), facing one AI. Second persons appear in three places and none is bound by the system: human–human exchanges as an outcome domain (pp. 29–30), an experiment in which people "learn they are evaluated by an AI rather than a human" (p. 15), with no second person whose verdict the AI applies, and one sentence on platform "Transparency measures about ranking criteria, contractual counterparties, and review quality" (p. 30), which Decius does not develop. Algorithmic management enters only as AI replacing a supervisor (p. 4).

## Candidates touched

**7. Artificial persons — takes_up (party side).** Class unchanged, premise hedged. The AI is a co-agent under "conjoined agency", but Decius disclaims personhood:
> "I use this concept not to claim that currently available AI possesses human-like intrinsic intentionality, but to capture situations in which human and AI contributions become jointly consequential for decisions and outcomes." (p. 3)

The structure side appears only as outcomes: the AI environment "can reconfigure social structures, norms, roles, and rules" (Proposition 3b, p. 16).

**3. Partner or medium — takes_up (party side).** Revised from passing. Decius argues against the medium picture, saying AI "cannot be treated only as background context or a neutral instrument" (p. 2) and does "not merely mediate action" (p. 7), and he recommends the partner picture to practitioners:
> "Individuals benefit from treating AI as a collaborative partner that can augment judgment while preserving human agency." (p. 35)

The AI is never placed between two people, so the "technology between people" setting of the candidate is absent.

**8. Accountability — answers (liability side).** New. The practical implications give a distributed answer: "Responsibility should follow effective control" (p. 35), and
> "the theory implies that accountability should be allocated across the chain of design, deployment, oversight, and use, rather than attributed either to a single end user or to AI as if it were an autonomous bearer of responsibility." (p. 36)

The preprint does not cite stark2024, so this is an answer to the question without engagement with its asker.

## What the work says

Decius extends social cognitive theory by doubling its triad of characteristics, behaviour and environment into a human layer and an AI layer, and states eighteen directional propositions (1a–6d) and three outcome propositions (7–9) about how the six components shape one another and yield cognitive, relational and structural outcomes. The AI is a second agent beside the person, with derived rather than intrinsic intentionality, and the practical advice is to treat it as a "collaborative partner" while allocating accountability along the design-to-use chain.

## Caution

The word "triad" is a false friend: Decius's triad is person–behaviour–environment, doubled, and algorithmacy's is two people and an intermediary; the two share a word, not a structure, and the full text confirms that no second person is bound by the system. The Phillips et al. citation is incidental (SCOT), so the work bears on candidate 7 by its own argument, not as a reply to OQ-phillips2024-09. Preprint, not peer reviewed; propositions untested.
