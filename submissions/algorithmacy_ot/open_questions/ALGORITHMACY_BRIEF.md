## The concept in one sentence

Algorithmacy is "the ability to do interdependent work with another person through an intermediary
that is opaque, binding, and adaptive" (ASM, Introduction ¶7). GDOC and PAPER call its genus a
*sensibility* that works through competencies; ASM calls it an *ability*. The genus is unsettled
(AGENDA item 2).

## The structure it answers to

- **An irreducible triad.** A participant, a human counterpart, and an intermediary that "evaluates
  and binds" both (GDOC Introduction ¶2; PAPER §2 ¶2) [locators corrected at S12]. Neither participant chose the intermediary, and it holds
  the verdict (Simmel's arbitrator, GDOC §4.1).
- **Not a dyad.** The system is neither the person's counterpart ("a direct dyad") nor a channel
  between two people ("a moderated dyad") (ASM, Introduction ¶1–2).
- **A sequence of forms.** "oracy belongs to direct dyads and chains of them, literacy to dyads
  moderated by a text, and algorithmacy to triads" (ASM, Abstract).

## Its three parts

| Part | Called for by (ASM) | Restores (GDOC, after Okhuysen & Bechky 2009) |
|---|---|---|
| Interpreting an outcome to find its cause (system, counterpart, or both) | opacity | accountability |
| Specifying intent so it survives the intermediary and reaches the counterpart | bindingness | shared understanding |
| Keeping track of a rule that changes without notice | adaptivity | predictability |

The parts form a cycle the person repeats. People with more turns at it hold more of the ability than
others in the same formal position (ASM, Introduction ¶7).

## Two failure modes

Collapsing the triad into a person-and-machine dyad (pacifying the algorithm, ignoring the counterpart),
or treating the platform as a neutral channel (ignoring the evaluator) (GDOC §4.3).

## What the paper claims to give organization theory

- "the co-opted person's side" of platform organization as Stark and Vanden Broeck (2024) describe it
  (ASM, Introduction ¶8; GDOC §5.2 heading).
- A single criterion for comparing communicative capacities: the form of the relation, or "what stands
  between coordinating actors" (GDOC Discussion ¶1; ASM).
- A shift in the opacity debate "from technical transparency to practical, situated competence" (GDOC
  Discussion ¶2).
- An account of why task deskilling and rising relational demand go together (GDOC §5.2).
- Settings beyond gig work: generative AI evaluating professional work (all three versions).

## Level of analysis and limits

The construct is an individual's capacity, exercised in an interaction, under an organizational form.
It measures no one; the paper is a theory article. It does not claim the triadic structure is new: the
contribution is naming what a person inside the structure must be able to do.

## Scoring rule

A question fits only if algorithmacy answers it **without changing the definition above**. If an answer
needs a different genus, level or scope, record the change and cap the score at 3.
