## Key question 2 — Where is each reference cited, and does the source support each use?

### Takeaway
Of roughly 60 citing clauses, most are supported. Every one of the ten direct quotations I could check (seven from Hinds & von Krogh, one Hancock, one Guzman & Lewis, one Cameron) is verbatim and on the stated page. The problems cluster in four places: (1) Hinds & von Krogh is a data-free thought experiment that the manuscript twice calls a "published account" of an arrangement scholars have "documented", and three of its conjectures ("may", "might") are reported without the hedge; (2) Anthony et al. is placed in the person–system dyad camp on abstract depth, when its abstract proposes a *system* view (design, implementation, use) that is at least arguably not dyadic; (3) Elish (2019) and Hargittai (2002) are cited in the Discussion for claims their texts do not make ("absorb quietly … lower the pressure to change"; "unequal help from peers"); (4) DeVito (2021) is cited as finding that the practice "is sometimes taught" when DeVito only proposes teaching routes.
