prompted this arm) and `journal_scan_memo.md` (a live scan of nine candidate journals'

decision-support case.

**Status flags** (from each card's own S2 adversarial-verification section): ✓ confirmed, no
issues found on re-check. ✓* corrected — S2 found and fixed a real error (wrong quote, wrong
page, misattribution); the card is now accurate but carries the history in its S2 note. ⚠ could
not verify — the source could not be re-opened this round; use with the caution the card already
states.

**Parties-modeled census** (`grep -l "Parties modeled:\*\* two humans + mediator" *.md`, exact
string match): **12 of 99** cards carry that phrase — Corti 2023, Friedman & Nissenbaum 1996
(NRMP case), Hansman/Midkiff/Pritchett 1993–95 (CPDLC party line, 3 cards), Hirsch et al. 2017,
Russ 2015 (prescriber–pharmacist alert), Suchman 1994/1995 (2 cards), Vallor 2010, Winograd 1994,
and Winograd & Flores 1986 — against 87 that model a dyad (human–technology,
human–technology–world, designer–stakeholders) or an artifact-level relation with no second human
at all. Two of the twelve are qualified on inspection: Vallor 2010's own card notes its mediator
"is treated as a medium, not a party" (fails the objective axis by the card's own account), and
Hirsch et al. 2017 carries an S2 "could not verify" flag. The stricter count — sources that model
a mediator actually pursuing something of its own — is smaller still. That gradient, not just the
raw 12/99, is the empirical answer to RQ9.

- ✓ [Kudina, O., & Verbeek, P.-P. (2019). Ethics from within.](kudina2019.md) — full text;

- ✓ [Rosenberger, R., & Verbeek, P.-P. (2015). A field guide to postphenomenology.](rosenbergerverbeek2015.md)
  — partial (snippets, pp. 9–41); the immersion relation is perceive-and-act with no learning
  clause; "relational strategy" (p. 29) is literacy in the field's own vocabulary.
- ✓ [Wellner, G. (2024). A postphenomenological guide to AI regulation.](wellner2024.md) — full
  text; the adaptivity axis stated inside the school ("different responses ... even to the same
  user at the same time and place," p. 10), with the arrow reversed and "relegation" named; still
  one "I," remedy in regulation and disclosure.

**Central finding for RQ1:** Verbeek's framework has no threshold — "technologies always
mediate" is explicitly the "extensionist" view his theory rejects. The channel/party split has no
standing inside postphenomenology as it stands; Φ's threshold must be argued as an *extension* of
the theory, not a reconciliation with it. Corti et al. is the only canonical source with a
two-human schema, and even it has no independent-objective mediator.

**Adaptivity finding for RQ1 (2026-09-18 pass):** Verbeek is fixed, not static. His mediators are
active (2014, p. 77) and his smart environments "register and monitor events, and react and
intervene" (2014, p. 83; 2011, pp. 121–135; Rosenberger & Verbeek 2015, pp. 21–22), but no Verbeek
text distinguishes a mediation settled at design time from one re-fitted at run time: "adaptive"
and "algorithm" do not occur in *Moralizing Technology*, the volume containing his 2014 chapter
prints Floridi & Sanders' adaptability criterion (pp. 195, 198) without his taking it up, and his
one dynamic notion, multistability, varies on the human side over a fixed artifact. Kudina &
Verbeek (2019) add a temporal axis (value dynamism) on the social side only. The axis is stated
inside postphenomenology by Wellner (2024, p. 10), later and outside Verbeek's schema. The
"static script" critique is dead; the survivor is "postphenomenology names read-and-react
environments and has no design consequence for a mediator that learns the people it mediates."

## Cluster H — Principle I: agentic parity through counter-delegation (RQ8), added 2026-09-18

Sorted by mechanism. The sort is the deliverable: the paper must not conflate the two families.

**Counter-delegation proper — a client-side agent acting for one party inside the coordination, in real time:**

- [Brunton, F., & Nissenbaum, H. (2015). *Obfuscation*.](bruntonnissenbaum2015.md) — reopened; chapters
  1, 4, 5 read from a circulated EPUB. TrackMeNot and AdNauseam are the strongest existing automated
  client-side counter-agents; chapter 4's free-riding and pollution tests already weigh harm to other
  data subjects, but as bystanders, not co-determined counterparts. Individual, unilateral, non-contesting.

- [Viljoen, S. (2021). A relational theory of data governance.](viljoen2021.md) — upgraded to VoR
