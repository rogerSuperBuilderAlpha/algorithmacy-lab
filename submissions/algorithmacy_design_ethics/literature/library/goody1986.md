## Relation to the argument

This paper's use. On its table of contents this is the institutional Goody, and the one the paper's history section needs for "how documentary record replaced memory as the ground of trust": chapter 3's title names the file and the bureau, chapter 4 the letter of the law. It is therefore the right citation for a competence/institution claim, and it does not on its face depend on the cognitive great divide of Goody and Watt 1963 or Goody 1977. But that is an inference from titles. Whether Goody in 1986 restates the cognitive thesis in the institutional chapters, and whether he claims that writing *caused* the state and law or only that they took written form, cannot be settled without reading pp. 87–170.
