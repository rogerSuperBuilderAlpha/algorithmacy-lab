## Framing check: what "algorithmacy" means here, against what already exists

A Google Doc research report ("The Invisible Scripts of the Machine: Historical Parallels Between
the Print Revolution and the Age of Opaque Artificial Intelligence") prompted this arm. It argues a
print-revolution/AI-opacity structural parallel and proposes an "AI Context Engine" combining
mechanistic interpretability and C2PA content provenance, grounded in Langdon Winner, Shannon
Vallor, Peter-Paul Verbeek, and Martela & Steger's meaning-in-life framework. Its own use of
"algorithmacy" is a loose synonym for critical AI/algorithm literacy: a **dyadic** frame, one human
reading or auditing one opaque system.

That does not match either construct already live in this lab:

1. **The Φ/IIT triadic-coordination construct**
   (`../../org_frontier/README.md`, `../../org_frontier/essays/literacy_or_algorithmacy.md`): a
   coordination form is dyadic when its cause-effect structure factors across the
   worker–system–counterpart partition (demands *literacy*), and triadic when the structure stays
   irreducible under exact Φ (demands *algorithmacy*). Formal, machine-verifiable on small Boolean
   models, the dissertation's spine.
2. **The Stark/Selznick "coordinative cooptation" construct** (`../algorithmacy_scaffolding/`, an
   unfinished course paper): algorithmacy as "the interpretive, tactical, and cognitive capability
   required to navigate, contest, and steer systems of coordinative cooptation." No Φ machinery;
   grounded instead in organizational sociology (Stark, Selznick, Aneesh) and a cognitive-science
   novice/expert frame (literacy, oracy, numeracy). Already carries a four-affordance HCI design
   section — Seamful Boundary Disclosure, Counterfactual/Branching Exploration, Modular Visual
   Chunking, Cognitive Forcing Functions — each backed by a verified primary source (Eslami 2015
   FeedVis; Rader, Cotter & Cho 2018; Ehsan et al. 2024 Seamful XAI; Arawjo et al. 2024 ChainForge;
   Wu, Terry & Cai 2022 AI Chains; Buçinca, Malaya & Gajos 2021; Bo, Wan & Anderson 2025).

Both existing constructs share the structural feature the Gemini report's framing lacks: **a third
party interpreting and binding two human parties**, not a human auditing a machine alone. That
difference is load-bearing, not cosmetic — it is exactly what separates algorithmacy from generic
AI/algorithmic literacy in this lab's usage, in both of its existing forms.

**Working definition for this piece**, reconciling the two: *algorithmacy is the competence a
person needs when an algorithmic third party sits between them and another human party, interprets
both, and commits determinations neither controls — irreducible in the Φ sense when no direct
channel between the two humans survives, and experienced, organizationally, as coordinative
cooptation into the third party's own objectives.* The Φ reading gives the structural test for
when the competence is required; the cooptation reading gives the lived, organizational account of
what navigating it demands. This piece treats them as two lenses on one competence, not rival
claims.

**What the Gemini report contributes, as raw material, not as citable source:** the
print-revolution structural analogy (useful — the lab already runs a version of this in
`algorithmacy_scaffolding`'s literacy/oracy/numeracy history); a theory stack the corpus has barely
touched (Verbeek appears once, in `../hospitality_phygital/manuscript/CLAIMS.md`, cited alongside
Ihde 1990 as the mediation-theory source for AI as "constitutive mediator" — confirmed, "GRAZE"
status, not yet used for algorithmacy; Vallor, Winner-the-philosopher as distinct from this lab's
unrelated prose-style "Winner pass" convention, value-sensitive design, mechanistic
interpretability, and C2PA provenance all have **zero** hits anywhere in the corpus — genuinely new
ground); and two named technical transparency mechanisms (mechanistic interpretability, C2PA) that
nothing here currently engages.

**Caveat, per this lab's standing rule** (`../../CLAUDE.md`: "Verify every citation/quote/number
against the source before it enters a draft"): **the Gemini report's own 193-entry Works Cited
list is unverified.** It was generated, not checked, and must not be cited in this piece without
independent primary-source verification. Treat the report as a hypothesis-generation document —
a map of what to go read — not as a source.
