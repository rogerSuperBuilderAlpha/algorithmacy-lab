## Claim (correct altitude)
Hegel's Teile/Glieder distinction and the state-as-organism passages draw the same aggregate/whole boundary the partition (Φ) test draws — the two criteria agree on the severed hand and on the shape a rotating middle term leaves — but they draw it from opposite directions: a Glied is defined teleologically, by its reciprocal contribution to the organism's self-maintenance, while the partition test reads only resistance to a cut, so the overlap is real and the gap (direction-of-definition, being-for-one's-own-persistence) is the finding, not an anticipation of Φ.

## Refutation condition
The partition (Φ) test reads a member as partition-visible — cutting it destroys what the whole does — while Hegel's Glied is teleologically defined by reciprocal contribution to the organism's self-maintenance. If the two criteria coincide on the state organism, that is a real positive result; if they come apart (the likely case), the post reports exactly where, and that gap IS the finding. Endpoints: if the state-triad encoding captured everything Hegel means by 'member' (reciprocal service, self-maintenance of freedom, each moment the others' ground), the criterion would have reached the teleology and the two holisms collapse into one, refuting Post 1's two-holisms premise; if it captured nothing, the membership BRIDGE (the Hegel homology, not Φ) would be mere decoration. Neither happens; the documented middle is the result. Narrower owed test that could still fail: a φ-matched attractor pair (one self-maintaining, one not) to check whether ANY observable over the cause-effect structure recovers self-maintenance — not yet run.

## Primary passages (pinned editions)

- **EL §198 Remark (Anmerkung), under Absolute Mechanism** (Brinkmann & Dahlstrom (1830/2010a), Cambridge) — _[corrected]_  
  'the state is a system of three syllogisms' — German: 'Wie Sonnensystem, so ist z.B. im Praktischen der Staat ein System von drei Schluessen. 1. Der Einzelne... 2. ist der Wille, Taetigkeit der Individuen das Vermittelnde... 3. aber ist das Allgemeine... die substantielle Mitte.' CORRECTION-TO-CARRY: the draft v3 asserts this 'sits in the main paragraph of §198... not in a Remark.' That is wrong. The state/solar-system passage is Hegel's Anmerkung (Remark) to §198: the terse main paragraph is the E-B-A mechanism-syllogism text ('Der angegebene Schluss (E-B-A)...'), and the state sentence is a separate remark paragraph. Confirmed three ways: (a) hegel.de typography marks it class 'pa' = Anmerkung, parallel to the confirmed §195 Anmerkung and distinct from the main-paragraph class and the 'pz'=Zusatz class; (b) the passage card el_198_three_syllogisms.md itself labels it '§198 (Remark)'; (c) hegel-system.de independently cites it 'Enz Anm.§198.' The earlier paper that called it a Remark was RIGHT; v3 introduced the error. Revert to Remark, or cite '§198' plainly without the main-paragraph claim.
- **EL §216 (Zusatz for the hand; main paragraph for means/purposes)** (Brinkmann & Dahlstrom (1830/2010a), Cambridge) — _[gated]_  
  Severed hand 'a hand in name only' sits in the §216 ZUSATZ — German: 'eine Hand, welche vom Leibe abgehauen wird, [ist] nur noch dem Namen nach eine Hand, aber nicht der Sache nach' (Aristotle's tag). The line the draft quotes as 'all members are reciprocally momentary means as much as momentary purposes' is in the §216 MAIN PARAGRAPH — German: 'so dass alle Glieder sich gegenseitig momentane Mittel wie momentane Zwecke sind.' Both are legitimately '§216' (B&D prints the Zusaetze), but precision: hand = Zusatz, means/purposes = main paragraph. B&D exact English wording is a print gate.
- **EL §218** (Brinkmann & Dahlstrom (1830/2010a), Cambridge) — _[verified]_  
  Paraphrase only in the draft ('a living thing maintains itself precisely by each part reproducing the others'). German §218 main paragraph confirms the sense: 'Diese Taetigkeit der Glieder ist aber nur die eine des Subjekts, in welche ihre Produktionen zurueckgehen, so dass darin nur das Subjekt produziert wird, d.i. es sich nur reproduziert.' Faithful paraphrase; B&D wording unquoted, so no verbatim gate.
- **EL §135 (main paragraph + Zusatz)** (Brinkmann & Dahlstrom (1830/2010a), Cambridge) — _[verified]_
