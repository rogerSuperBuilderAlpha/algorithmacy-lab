# Post 1 — Two Holisms: grounding digest

*Stage-1 research digest (Fable grounding + Opus adversarial verification, run wf_ad0ba178). The rewrite draws from this file plus the source cards in `../cards/`. Verification verdict: **sound**.*

## Claim (correct altitude)
Hegel's "the true is the whole" (a developmental, self-knowing holism) and the lab's "a whole is what no partition preserves" (a synchronic, causal holism) are two genuinely different holisms that answer the same question — when is a many also a one — and the series reads them against each other as homologous boundary-drawings, never as identity or anticipation.

## Guard check
Clean on anticipation: the draft explicitly forbids it twice ("Hold off" against reading the two sentences as one thought; "without claiming he wrote it first"). Clean on demotion: none of the banned framings (calculator, decorative, hollow, unnecessary) appear, and every self-limiting description is answered by the affirmative case in the same section ("a detector for the very additivity Hegel diagnoses"; "it hears something the theory is deaf to"; the ally-not-bottom-rung section exists precisely to refuse the demotion). Two watch items for the author's revision: (1) "low instrument," "bottom-rung test," and "that deafness, which is a poverty almost everywhere" are ladder-relative positioning that stays safe only while the paired affirmative clauses survive editing — if those paragraphs get trimmed, trim the self-deprecation first; (2) "mine knows nothing at all" concedes more than IIT itself claims (Tononi & Boly 2025 read the Φ-structure as intrinsic existence); this is a deliberate choice not to lean on IIT's consciousness claim and it keeps the homology clean, but it should not harden elsewhere in the series into "Φ is a blind mechanical number."

## Refutation condition
If the difference between the two holisms reduces to synchronic-vs-diachronic bookkeeping, the series premise thins. The draft meets the bookkeeping version head-on ("The difference is not two clocks": run the partition test over the whole developmental film and it still never meets a subject — the gap was never about duration). The harder form is Ng's living-unity reading — that Hegel's holism is not under-formalized coordination but a different kind of thing entirely (diachronic self-constitution, constitutive telos, self-partitioning Ur-teil, self-knowledge) — which Post 1 introduces and concedes force to, and Post 4 tests rather than assumes away.
