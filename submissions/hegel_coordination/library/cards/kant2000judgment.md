## Summary
Kant's third Critique is Post 4's foil: the natural end as cause and effect of itself, parts reciprocally cause and effect of their form, nature that "organizes itself" — but taken as a REGULATIVE, not constitutive, concept, and famously no "Newton of a blade of grass." Hegel's move (via Kreines and Ng) is to make inner purposiveness constitutive where Kant left it regulative.
