## Summary
The 1830 Encyclopaedia Logic (main paragraphs and Remarks) supplies the syllogism doctrine (§§181–193, "everything is a syllogism"), the Objectivity ladder statement (§194) and the inner-purposiveness Remark (§204), the Whole-and-Parts / severed-hand / self-maintenance membership passages (§§135, 216, 218), and the state-as-three-syllogisms Remark (§198R) under Absolute Mechanism. The series reads these as the same aggregate/whole boundary the Φ test draws, from the opposite (teleological) direction.
