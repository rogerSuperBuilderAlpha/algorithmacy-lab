## Summary
The 1807 Phenomenology supplies two of the series' load-bearing exhibits: the Preface's developmental holism ("the true is the whole," substance-also-as-subject) that Post 1 sets against the lab's synchronic holism, and the master–slave dialectic (¶¶178–196) that Post 2 reads as Hegel's first proof that a whole bound by domination dissolves on its own terms. Post 8 also draws on ¶590 (absolute freedom and terror). The series reads these as homologous boundary-drawings, never as an anticipation of Φ.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci
- Preface ¶20: "The true is the whole. However, the whole is only the essence completing itself through its own development. This much must be said of the absolute: It is essentially a result, and only at the end is it what it is in truth." [verified].
- Preface ¶3: "the whole is the result together with the way the result comes to be"; "the unadorned result is just the corpse that has left the tendency behind" (both in ¶3) [verified].
- Preface ¶17: "everything hangs on apprehending and expressing the true not as substance but rather even more as subject." [verified].
- Preface ¶18: "it is only this self-restoring sameness, the reflective turn into itself in its otherness." [verified].
- ¶177: "the I that is we and the we that is I" — the destination announced before the struggle [verified].
- ¶178: "Self-consciousness is in and for itself while and as a result of its being in and for itself for an other; i.e., it is only as a recognized being" — published wording; the marxists.org draft reads "exists…by way of…"; italics on "in and for itself" a proof gate [verified with delta].
- ¶¶190–193: "The master likewise relates himself to the thing mediately through the servant"; "what the servant does is really the master's doing"; "a form of recognition has arisen that is one-sided and unequal"; "The truth of the self-sufficient consciousness is thus the servile consciousness" [verified; draft/published deltas: "to the master"/"in the master's own eyes"; "thus"; dropped "topsy-turvy"].
- ¶¶194–196: "the fear of death, the absolute master"; "through work, this servile consciousness comes round to itself"; "only stubbornness, a freedom that remains bogged down within the bounds of servility" [verified].
- ¶199: the Stoic "free within all the dependencies of his singular existence, whether on the throne or in fetters" (draft PDF: "individual" for "singular" — Pinkard's einzeln→singular revision) [verified with delta].
- ¶590 (absolute freedom and terror): "The sole work and deed of universal freedom is thus death, namely, a death which has no inner amplitude and no inner fulfillment"; "the coldest, emptiest death of all, having no more meaning than chopping off a head of cabbage or swallowing a mouthful of water." The marxists.org draft reads "is in fact death" and "no inner extent"; the cabbage/water sentence matches. "thus"/"amplitude" may be CUP-2018 revisions [gated on the physical CUP copy].
