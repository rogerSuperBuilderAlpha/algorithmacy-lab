## Summary
Kreines reads Hegel's holism as EXPLANATORY (a metaphysics of grounds and reasons, immanent kinds), not efficient-causal — the anchor for Post 1's point that Hegel's whole is a whole of reasons, a different species from the synchronic causal-partition kind. His own term is "insubstantial holism" (ch. 7). Used to draw the fault line the post must address, not to license a proto-Φ reading.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci
- Hegel's holism is "the metaphysical topic of the explanatory reasons why things do what they do, or are as they are"; "insubstantial holism" (ch. 7); three ascending orders of explanatory completeness (Introduction) [gated — page ≈ 9 for the definition].
- "not a brute, necessitating law of nature, but rather the kind of being that engages in reasoning" [gated].
