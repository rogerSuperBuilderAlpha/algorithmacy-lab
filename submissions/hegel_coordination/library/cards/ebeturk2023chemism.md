## Summary
Ebeturk is a Post 4 threat: he argues Hegel's teleology is misplaced and that the logic of Life / internal teleology should precede external teleology, allowing a direct passage from Chemism to Life. His p. 57 sentence — the chemical process as the self-determination of the concept — names the very axis on which the two orderings dissociate, so the post takes it for a counter-verdict, not a concession.
