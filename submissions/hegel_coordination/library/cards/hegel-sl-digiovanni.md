## Summary
The Greater Logic supplies Post 3's syllogism material (the objective syllogism, the Mechanism chapter's "real middle term," free mechanism as a system of three interlocking syllogisms) and Post 4's Objectivity ladder (mechanism → chemism → teleology), with the chemism "crossing exhibit" and the transition to Life. The series treats the objective syllogism and the mechanism/teleology cut as boundaries of the same shape the partition criterion computes — a homology, not an anticipation.
