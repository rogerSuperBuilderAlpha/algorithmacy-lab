# Series references — Hegel / coordination (nine posts)

Alphabetized APA-7 master list, one entry per work, for the nine-post series. It is the
companion to the verified card deck in `cards/` (one card per source, with loci and gate
status). Entries are formatted to be consistent with `check_editions.py`: the pinned-edition
signature substrings (translators, publisher, "Original work published …") are present, and
banned markers (Knox/Dyde for PR, Wallace for EL, Miller for SL, MIT Press for Honneth) are
kept out of the canonical entries.

## Pinned editions (the reconcile's fixed points)

- **Philosophy of Right (PR)** — Wood/Nisbet, Cambridge University Press (1821/**1991a**). The
  only PR translation the series quotes. Cite by § until the physical Nisbet page is checked.
- **Encyclopaedia Logic, main paragraphs and Remarks (EL main)** — Brinkmann/Dahlstrom,
  Cambridge University Press (1830/**2010a**).
- **Encyclopaedia Logic, Zusätze (EL Zusätze)** — Geraets/Suchting/Harris, Hackett
  (1830/**1991b**). The series pins ALL Zusätze here even though B&D carries a selection;
  `check_editions.py` enforces the split per locus.
- **Phenomenology of Spirit (PhG)** — Pinkard, Cambridge University Press (1807/**2018**).
  Miller (1977) appears only as a deliberate wording contrast.
- **Science of Logic (SL)** — di Giovanni, Cambridge University Press (1816/**2010b**); the
  German Suhrkamp *Wissenschaft der Logik II* (Werke 6, **1979**) anchors the German wording.
- **Recognition family** — Brandom, *A Spirit of Trust* (2019); Pinkard, *The Sociality of
  Reason* (1994); Pippin, *Practical Philosophy* (2008); Honneth, *The Struggle for
  Recognition* (Anderson trans., **Polity**, 1992/1995 — NOT MIT Press).
- **IIT anchors** — Oizumi, Albantakis, & Tononi (2014, IIT 3.0, e1003588); Albantakis et al.
  (2023, IIT 4.0, e1011465).

APA assigns the a/b year-suffix per document, so the letters legitimately differ across posts;
the reconcile checks the edition string, not the suffix.
