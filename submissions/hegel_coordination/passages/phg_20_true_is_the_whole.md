# PhG ¶20 — "The true is the whole"

**Locus.** *Phenomenology of Spirit*, Preface, ¶20 (Miller and Pinkard paragraph numbering agree here).
**Status.** Locus standard in the literature; exact wording to be quoted from Pinkard (Cambridge, 2018)
at drafting. The companion thought — the true as "the result together with its becoming" — is also
Preface material; pin its paragraph when the Pinkard text is open.
**Series use.** Post 1's Hegel anchor: his holism is developmental and self-comprehending, which is what
the partition criterion does not and cannot mean by "whole."
**Watch.** Do not quote from memory; the sentence is one of the most misquoted in Hegel.
