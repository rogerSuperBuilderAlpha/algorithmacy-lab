# "Bei sich selbst im Anderen" — locus PINNED (2026-07-10, round-2 research)

**The phrase.** Freedom as being at home with oneself in one's other — post 8's anchor and the bridge to
coordinative sovereignty.
**Status.** **Locus pinned as a family of two.** The phrase has a primary home and a practical home,
confirmed by direct fetch of both sections this run (workflow `wf_d2b3aa21-355`, see
[`../literature/DEEP_RESEARCH_ROUND2.md`](../literature/DEEP_RESEARCH_ROUND2.md) #19):

1. **Definition — *EL* §24 Zusatz 2** (German confirmed at hegel.de): "die Freiheit ist eben dies, in
   seinem Anderen bei sich selbst zu sein, von sich abzuhängen, das Bestimmende seiner selbst zu sein";
   and "Freiheit ist nur da, wo kein Anderes für mich ist, das ich nicht selbst bin." (Note: Zusatz **2**,
   not Zusatz 1.)
2. **Practical / will — *PR* §7** (Remark and Addition): concrete freedom "in friendship and love," where
   "a man is not one-sided, but limits himself willingly in reference to another, and yet in this
   limitation knows himself as himself."

Post 8 cites the pair: EL §24 Z2 for the definition, PR §7 for the will/social version. There is no single
canonical locus, so the post names the family and says so.

**Correction to carry.** "Encyclopaedia §158A" is a mis-pin: the love passage is *PR* §158, not *Enc.*
§158. Do not cite Enc. §158 for this phrase.

**Rule.** Quote from the pinned translations at drafting — EL §24 Z2 from Geraets/Suchting/Harris (Hackett
1991), PR §7 from Nisbet (Cambridge 1991); the marxists.org PR text is an older translation. The German
above is verified; the standard English wording was not fetched this run.

**Scholarly hook.** Ayon Roy, "In seinem Anderen bei sich selbst zu sein: Toward a Recuperation of Hegel's
Metaphysics of Agency," *Epoché* 11/1 (Fall 2006): 225–255 (page-range endpoint 255 vs 256 unresolved —
verify against the print issue). Vieweg reads PR §7's concrete freedom the same way ("Bei-sich-selbst-sein
im Anderen," in friendship and love).

**Companions (loci standard).** *PR* §5+R (the freedom of the void), §260 (concrete freedom).
