# PR §§250–256 — the Korporation

**Locus.** *Philosophy of Right* §§250–256, with §253R (the corporation as "second family," honor) and
the §255 pairing of family and corporation as civil society's ethical roots.
**Status.** Locus standard. Quote from Wood/Nisbet at drafting.
**Series use.** Post 7 and HC4: is Hegel's argument integrating-work or constraint-holding? His own
century's guild abolitions are the natural constraint-lifting experiment against his claim.
**Watch.** Hegel explicitly distinguishes the Korporation from the closed medieval guild (§255R); the
post must not flatten that distinction, since it is exactly the conduit/integrator line.
