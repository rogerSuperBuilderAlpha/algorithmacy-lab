# PR §183 — civil society as the external state

**Locus.** *Philosophy of Right* §183: civil society as "the external state [äußerer Staat], the state
of necessity and of the understanding [Not- und Verstandesstaat]," with §183's "system of all-round
interdependence" formulation.
**Status.** **Verified 2026-07-10** (locus confirmed against public sources; wording above is the common
rendering — quote from Wood/Nisbet at drafting). §189R names the economists (Smith, Say, Ricardo).
**Series use.** Post 5's hinge: universal interdependence whose unity Hegel himself calls external —
the aggregate/configuration boundary drawn in 1821.
**Watch.** Marxist readings of this section carry a century of freight; the post uses Hegel's own
verdict, not Marx's radicalization of it.
