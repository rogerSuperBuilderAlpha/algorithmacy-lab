# PhG ¶¶178–196 — lordship and bondage

**Locus.** *Phenomenology of Spirit*, Self-Consciousness, ¶¶178–196 (standard numbering; "Independence
and Dependence of Self-Consciousness: Lordship and Bondage").
**Status.** Locus standard. Quote from Pinkard at drafting.
**Series use.** Post 2: recognition as constitutive relation (¶¶178–184, the doubling of
self-consciousness); the master's recognition-deficit as the self-undermining of domination-held binding
(¶¶191–193). Post 8 reuses ¶178's "self-consciousness exists only as recognized."
**Rival readings to cite.** Honneth (recognition as normative demand), Brandom (statuses instituted by
reciprocal attitudes), Kojève (the anthropological reading the post should *not* silently adopt).
