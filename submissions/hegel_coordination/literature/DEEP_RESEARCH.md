# Deep-research literature map (2026-07-10)

One large research run (5 angles, 25 sources fetched, 91 claims, 25 adversarially verified 3-vote,
24 confirmed / 1 refuted). This file is the standing literature map for the series. It is not itself a
citation source: every anchor here still gets a `passages/`-style card and a page-verified quotation

**Verification key.** `[✓]` verified 3-0 in this run · `[○]` surfaced in search, not independently
verified here — **verify before citing** · `[✗]` refuted or a correction to carry.

## The strategic finding (favorable, and load-bearing)

The series' signature bridge — Hegel's "when is interdependence a whole?" read against a
*causal-irreducibility / Φ* criterion — **appears genuinely unmade.** The two closest prior-art programs
were fetched and read in full and neither touches it (see Layer 3). So the novelty claim the series can
make in public is specific and defensible: *no one has read Hegel's aggregate/whole boundary against an
integrated-information criterion, and Hegel's own upgrade condition (recognition, and beneath it inner
teleology) is not that criterion.* Keep the claim at exactly that altitude — not "Hegel anticipated Φ."

## Layer 1 — per-post anchors and threats

### Post 1 — Two holisms
- **Anchor `[○]`** James Kreines, *Reason in the World: Hegel's Metaphysics and Its Philosophical Appeal*
  (OUP, 2015). Reads Hegel's Logic as a "metaphysics of reason" (grounds/conditions of explanation).
  Surfaced as the load-bearing anchor for Posts 1 and 4; not independently verified in this run.
- **Also `[○]`** Robert Stern and Charles Taylor on Hegelian holism — named, not yet located/verified.
- **Threat `[✓, medium]`** Karen Ng, *Hegel's Concept of Life* (OUP, 2020): "life is logical cognition in
  the shape of immediacy… the necessary presupposition of cognition in its self-conscious form" — Hegel's
  whole is a self-comprehending living unity, which no synchronic causal-partition criterion captures.
  This is the sharpest threat to the whole series and it lands first here.
  (NDPR review, ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/hegels-concept-of-life-self-consciousness-freedom-logic/.)

### Post 2 — Master–slave as a dissolution argument
- **Anchor / threat `[✓, medium]`** Robert B. Pippin, *Hegel on Self-Consciousness: Desire and Death in
  the Phenomenology of Spirit* (Princeton UP, 2011): the life-and-death struggle is a contest over
  "epistemological authority, not mere biological survival" — combatants "fight because there are as yet
  no established rules for deciding what counts as a good reason." The recognition argument is
  irreducibly **normative**, which is exactly the Post 2 threat (and the Layer 2 Pippin anchor).
  (NDPR review by Michael Inwood.)
- **Also `[○]`** Honneth, *The Struggle for Recognition*; Robert R. Williams, *Hegel's Ethics of
  Recognition* and *Recognition*; the anti-Kojève turn — named in the plan, not verified in this run.

### Post 3 — The syllogism / mediated triad
- **Anchors `[○]`** Klaus Vieweg, *Hegel's Political Philosophy* (OUP) — incl. the chapter "The State as
  a System of Three Syllogisms" (EL §198); Sebastian Stein on the Doctrine of the Concept. Both surfaced
  via the plan and the §198 card's secondary note; neither verified here.
- **Threat (structural).** The standing objection — the syllogism is a *logical*, not causal-structural,
  form — is not yet met by a located source. Post 3 needs one, or it collapses into a footnote of Post 4.

### Posts 4 & 6 — Mechanism / teleology / organism  *(the most exposed pair — see Gaps)*
- **Anchor `[✓, high]`** James Kreines, "The Logic of Life: Hegel's Philosophical Defense of Teleological
  Explanation of Living Beings," ch. 13 in *The Cambridge Companion to Hegel and Nineteenth-Century
  Philosophy*, ed. Beiser (Cambridge UP, 2008), 344–377. "Kant argues for a generally skeptical view of
  teleological explanation of living beings; Hegel responds that Kant should instead defend such
  explanation." (scholarship.claremont.edu/cmc_fac_pub/90/)
- **Anchor `[✓, high]`** Karen Ng, *Hegel's Concept of Life* (OUP, 2020): Hegel takes Kant's inner
  purposiveness from **regulative to constitutive** — organic teleology, not mechanism, is the model for
  the Concept. This is what lets Hegel win the "mere mechanism" objection.
- **Threat `[✓, medium]`** — the *same* Ng/Kreines teleology is the threat: it grounds wholeness in
  self-maintaining life, a *different and arguably anti-causal* upgrade condition. A reviewer will say the
  causal-irreducibility reading imports the very synchronic-mechanistic criterion Hegel's inner-teleology
  critique rejects. Post 4 is built for Hegel to win; the honest form of the win is that he replaces the
  criterion, not that he anticipates it.

### Post 5 — Civil society: interdependence without unity
- **Anchor `[○]`** Norbert Waszek, *The Scottish Enlightenment and Hegel's Account of Civil Society*
  (Kluwer, 1988) — the Smith/Say/Ricardo reception (PR §189R). Named, not verified here.
- **Threat `[✓, high]`** Philip J. Kain, "Hegel and the Failure of Civil Society," *The Owl of Minerva*
  46 (2014–15): 1–23 (DOI 10.5840/owl20156812). Frames the deficiency as **economic-distributive** (class
  polarization, pauperization), not a formal failure of wholeness — and argues Hegel has a workable
  social-democratic remedy (corporations + taxation).
- **`[✗]` Correction to carry:** do **not** attribute "Hegel has no solution" to Kain — that is the
  Marxist reading he *disputes*. His thesis is that Hegel's remedy "could actually work." The threat to
  Post 5 survives regardless: the deficiency reads as substantive-economic, not structural non-wholeness.

### Post 7 — The Korporation
- **Anchor `[○]`** George Heiman, "The Sources and Significance of Hegel's Corporate Doctrine" — named in
  the plan, not verified here. Recent *PR* critical guides (Cambridge) also to be located.
- **Threat.** Not yet located as a source; the guild-distinction (PR §255R) is the internal tension to
  exploit, and Hegel's century's guild abolitions are the natural constraint-lifting experiment (HC4).

### Post 8 — Freedom as being-at-home-in-one's-other
- **Anchor `[✓, high]`** Alan Patten, *Hegel's Idea of Freedom*, Oxford Philosophical Monographs (OUP,
  1999): an original "civic humanist" reading; freedom "developed, realized, and sustained" through
  institutions (Sittlichkeit), not an inner state. Standard companion to Neuhouser and Pippin.
- **Anchor `[○]`** Frederick Neuhouser, *Foundations of Hegel's Social Theory: Actualizing Freedom*
  (Harvard UP, 2000) — surfaced as *the* Post 8 anchor and a Layer 2 target (reconstructs "social
  freedom" as the standard by which family/civil society/state count as rational). Preview only; verify.
- **Open `[○]`** Has anyone mapped Hegel's negative/concrete freedom onto **Hirschman's exit/voice**? Not
  found in this run. A candidate genuinely-unmade connection — worth a targeted search and, if empty, a
  small novelty claim for the coordinative-sovereignty bridge.

### Post 9 — Aufhebung / sublation
- **Anchor `[✓, medium]`** SEP, "Hegel's Dialectics" (Julie Maybee): *aufheben* "means both to cancel (or
  negate) and to preserve at the same time"; the result "contains" the negated one; three moments
  (understanding EL §80, dialectical §81, speculative §82); the movement is "driven by necessity…
  nothing extraneous is introduced." The immanence point is what distinguishes sublation from any
  external/mechanical partition operation — load-bearing for the honest reading of the core-merger bridge.
- **Prior art that touches this post directly — see Layer 3 (Fuchs).**

## Layer 2 — positioning targets (the terrain to distinguish from, not rediscover)

The common thread, cleanly sourced: all four give a **normative-attitudinal** account of what upgrades a
social aggregate into a whole — statuses instituted by reciprocal recognition, in a space of reasons. The
series' criterion is **causal-structural** (partition-sensitivity of a cause-effect structure). State
the contrast early and cite it; the difference is the series' whole reason to exist beside them.

- **`[✓, high]` Brandom, *A Spirit of Trust* (Harvard/Belknap, 2019).** "Normative statuses such as
  commitment, responsibility, and authority are instituted by social practices of reciprocal
  recognition." The ancient/modern shift is a change in the *ontology of normative status* (objective
  facts → attitude-determined); the resolution is attitudinal (magnanimity, trust, forgiveness). The
  social whole is upgraded by a recognitive *attitude*, not a causal reconfiguration. (Making It Explicit
  supplies the deontic-scorekeeping background.)
- **`[✓, medium]` Pinkard, *Hegel's Phenomenology: The Sociality of Reason* (Cambridge UP, 1994).** Reason
  is a social achievement constituted through interpersonal recognition; self-consciousness emerges within
  Spirit as a normative "social space." A space-of-reasons answer, not a causal-structural one.
- **`[✓, high]` Honneth**, *Freedom's Right* (Columbia UP, 2014) and *Recognition: A Chapter in the
  History of European Ideas* (Cambridge UP, 2021); see J.-P. Deranty, "Recognition in a Historical Key,"
  *Journal of Social and Political Philosophy* 1(1) (2022), DOI 10.3366/jspp.2022.0024. Ties recognition
  to "social freedom," increasingly via the *history* of the concept — offers no causal-structural
  criterion of social wholes. (Honneth's *Struggle for Recognition* itself `[○]` not separately verified.)
- **`[✓, medium]` Pippin** (as Post 2). Self-consciousness is a normative/apperceptive achievement
  (claiming authority for judgments), not a causal fact; recognition is the acknowledgment of normative
  authority. Pippin distinguishes norms ("followed by a subject") from "empirical, psychological laws of
  thought" — the exact normative-vs-causal line. *Caveat:* this norms-vs-laws sub-claim verified only
  2-1, and Inwood notes a structural *precondition* (the other must already be recognized as
  self-conscious); treat as the weakest Layer-2 item.
- **`[○]` Neuhouser** (as Post 8) — the fifth target, social-freedom variant; verify.

## Layer 3 — prior-art / rediscovery scan

Two families exist; **neither makes the series' bridge.** But the series must name them, because a
reviewer will.

**A. Hegel formalized / Hegel as cybernetics.** A real tradition — position against it explicitly.
- `[✓, high]` **Lawvere–nLab formalized Logic.** Clarence Protin, "Hegel and Modern Topology,"
  arXiv:2501.02367 (2025), summarizing Lawvere (1991) and Schreiber (cohesive higher topos; *Aufhebung*
  as an adjoint modality). Full-text verified: **no** recognition, no *Philosophy of Right*, no IIT, no
  causal irreducibility, no social wholes; its "Φ" is a phenomenological-space symbol, not
  integrated-information Φ. Its formal mereology concerns *organisms/fusion*, never social configurations.
  The most prominent "formalized Hegel" program, and it leaves the social-ontology-via-Φ bridge open.
- `[○]` **Gotthard Günther** — formalized Hegelian dialectic as many-valued/**polycontextural**
  ("trans-Aristotelian") logic, "operational dialectics," for self-referential cybernetic systems /
  non-binary computing. THE major precedent for "formalize the dialectic." Touches method and logic, not
  a causal-irreducibility criterion of social wholes. Verify the claims before citing; the positioning
  point (method-formalization ≠ our social-ontology criterion) is safe now.
- `[○]` **Leif Weatherby** — Hegel/cybernetics/media (Cabinet essay surfaced; his *Transplanting the
  Metaphysical Organ*, 2016, and recent AI work are the likely book anchors). Verify.
- `[○]` **"Hegelian Phenomenology as Second-Order Cybernetics and System Theory,"** *Threads* 5 (2017):
  reads the Phenomenology as second-order cybernetics on **observer-inclusion**, not causal
  irreducibility or partition-sensitivity. Does not pre-empt.

**B. Computational Hegel / dialectics-as-algorithm.**
- `[✓, high]` Abdali et al., "Self-reflecting Large Language Models: A Hegelian Dialectical Approach,"
  arXiv:2501.14917 (2025). Operationalizes the dialectic as a **prompt pipeline** for individual LLM
  self-reflection (self-sublation, temperature annealing, majority voting). Full-text verified: no Φ, no
  causal irreducibility, no part/member boundary, no recognition, no social ontology. Does not pre-empt.

**C. Sublation ↔ emergence — the one hit that touches a specific post.**
- `[○]` **Christian Fuchs**, "Dialectics and Emergence" (fuchsc.net): maps **Aufhebung directly onto the
  emergence of new system qualities** — old qualities preserved in the new whole. This is prior art for
  **Post 9 / HC6** specifically. The lab's distinctive move against it: Fuchs offers a general
  emergentist analogy; the lab has an *exact computed* structure (q210/q211 merged cores) where a smaller
  whole persists as a member of a larger one. Position HC6 against Fuchs, don't rediscover him. Verify.

**D. Genuinely absent (reportable novelty, pending confirmation).** No hits surfaced connecting Hegel to
network/graph-theoretic **social** ontology, to complexity/emergence of **social** wholes (beyond Fuchs's
general dialectics-emergence), or to formal mereology of **social** configurations (Lawvere's mereology
is of organisms). And nothing on Hegel + IIT/Φ. If a targeted follow-up confirms these gaps, each is a
small, honest novelty note.

## Gaps and risks

- **Most exposed: Posts 4 and 6.** The scholarship that lets Hegel win the mechanism objection (Ng,
  Kreines) grounds wholeness in inner **teleology / self-comprehending life** — a *different* upgrade
  condition that a good reviewer will wield *against* any causal-partition reading. The series must
  concede this openly (it already plans to, in Post 4's refutation condition) and make the concession the
  content, not paper over it.
- **Most embarrassing single omission: Karen Ng, *Hegel's Concept of Life* (2020).** Ignoring it leaves
  the organism/teleology posts open to the charge that they mistook Hegel's living self-relation for a
  causal-mereological criterion. Read it before drafting Posts 1, 4, 6.
- **Secondary exposure: Post 5.** Kain reads civil society's deficiency as economic-distributive and
  remediable, not structural non-wholeness — the post must hold the two registers (normative-economic vs
  structural) apart.
- **Coverage gaps to close before the relevant posts draft** (`[○]` items above): Kreines *Reason in the
  World*; Stern/Taylor (Post 1); Honneth *Struggle*, Williams (Post 2); Vieweg/Stein (Post 3); Waszek
  (Post 5); Heiman (Post 7); Neuhouser *Foundations* (Post 8); the Hirschman exit/voice search (Post 8);
  Günther/Weatherby/Fuchs verification (Layer 3). None blocks the plan; each blocks its own post.
