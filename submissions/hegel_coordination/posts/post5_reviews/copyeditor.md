## 4. Minor mechanical items (fix before posting)

1. **Ellipsis, L232 (§244 quote) — the one quotation-mechanics defect.** `living... that` is a
   run-together three-dot with no spaces. APA 7 wants three spaced periods (`living . . . that`) or the
   ellipsis character with surrounding spaces (`living … that`). The digest renders it `living … that`.
   Correct to match. (The three other `...` instances — L386, L387, L393 — are all inside the sourcing
   note, which is deleted; no action needed there.)

2. **Italicize statistical *N*, L153 (and L414, deleted).** APA sets the sample-size symbol in italics:
   `*N* = 4`. Currently `N = 4`. The threshold variable is already correctly italicized (`*k*`), so this
   is an internal inconsistency as well. Fix L153; L414 is in the deleted note.

3. **Percent style, L155–L161.** "three percent" is spelled out three times while the paired counts are
   numerals ("12 of 400"). APA prefers numerals with the symbol (`3%`) alongside numeric data. Narrative
   spell-out is defensible, but if you want APA-strict consistency, switch to `3%`. Low priority; pick one
   convention and hold it. (L415 "three percent" is in the deleted note.)

4. **Start-of-quote capitalization, L231 ("when") and L234 ("Poverty").** The Nisbet originals read
   "When…" and "poverty…"; the manuscript flips each to fit its syntax. APA 7 (8.31) permits changing the
   first letter of a quotation to upper/lower case without brackets, so both are **acceptable as-is** —
   logged for completeness, no change required.

5. **Em-dash density.** 52 em-dashes across the body — far above the repo house style of ≤1 per paragraph
   (CLAUDE.md rule 5), with several paragraphs carrying 3–4. Not an APA violation and not a citation issue,
   but flagged because the house style calls it a priority tic. En-dashes are all correct in ranges
   (§§79–82, 758–777, 46(1–2), 43–65). No action required for APA; consider trimming for house style.

6. **Heading case, L381.** "Sourcing note (delete before posting)" is sentence case while every body
   heading is title case. Moot — the section is deleted before posting.
