## Verdict: MINOR revision. Single most important fix: two verbatim Hegel quotations are cited as if sighted directly against the pinned Nisbet edition when the digest confirms both were verified only through secondary quoting sources — one of which (Werner) never appears in the piece at all.

Specifically: the §5 Remark "fanaticism of destruction" quote (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §5, Remark) was verified verbatim only via Knowles (2002) quoting Nisbet — never sighted directly in Wood/Nisbet. The §158 Addition love quote (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §158 Addition) was verified only via Laura Werner's article quoting Nisbet — and Werner appears nowhere in this draft, not in-text and not in References. Both facts are honestly disclosed in the "Still gated" section of the sourcing note (which will be deleted before posting) — but the in-text citation format in the body itself gives no indication of this. A reader of the published post sees "(Hegel, 1821/1991a, §5, Remark)" and reasonably assumes direct verification against the pinned Cambridge edition. That is exactly the situation APA 7 §8.6 covers: when a quotation's wording is confirmed only through a secondary source, the primary work is named but the citation runs through the secondary source ("as cited in").

The draft has already set the right precedent for this exact problem twice over — the "raised to the status of an actual shape and passion" clause and the PhG ¶590 "thus...amplitude" sentence were both downgraded from quotation to paraphrase for precisely this reason (unverified against the primary text). The §5R fanaticism clause and the §158 Addition passage are verbatim-confirmed, just not against the primary — so paraphrasing is not required, but the citation format must say so.

**Fix, §5R (line 44):**
> ...annihilating any organization which attempts to rise up anew" (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §5, Remark).

→
> ...annihilating any organization which attempts to rise up anew" (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §5, Remark, as cited in Knowles, 2002).

**Fix, §158 Addition (lines 99–102).** Two viable paths, in order of preference:

*Path A — add Werner and cite through her, once her exact year/volume are confirmed (they are not confirmed here, so do not paste a fabricated year):*
> ...and of the other with me" (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §158 Addition, as cited in Werner, [YEAR]).

with a matching References entry once the bibliographic details are pulled from the Redescriptions OA PDF (title is known — "Love at the Heart of the State" — year, volume, issue, pages are not yet in hand from the digest and must not be guessed).

*Path B — the safer, immediately postable fix, matching the standard already applied twice in this same draft:*
> Hegel makes the point in the register of exit himself: love, in the Addition to §158, is the consciousness of a unity with another so complete that I am no longer isolated in myself, and I win my self-consciousness only by giving up my independent existence and by knowing myself as one with the other and the other as one with me (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §158 Addition).

Path B costs the verbatim block-quote feel of the passage but requires no new research and removes the integrity gap entirely; recommend it unless Werner's details can be pinned down before posting.
