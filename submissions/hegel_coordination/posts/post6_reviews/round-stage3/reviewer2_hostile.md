### The temptation / §278R

**Flag 3 (high) — an epigram contradicted by its own quote.**
> "The *Philosophy of Right* states the point flatly, and about the state, not the body: 'the so-called parts of an animal organism are not parts, but members or organic moments whose isolation and separate existence constitute disease' (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §278R)."

The clause "and about the state, not the body" is the antithesis machine misfiring: the sentence it introduces is grammatically and explicitly *about the animal organism* — the body. Hegel deploys the body as the analogy for the state; the quoted sentence is the biological premise, not a claim about the state. A hostile reader flips straight from your framing to your quote and catches it. The German sense ("separate existence" for *Für-sich-Bestehen*) still owes the Nisbet print check, but that is a gate, not an error. Rewrite:
> "The *Philosophy of Right* states the point flatly, and states it of the animal organism in the middle of an argument about the state: 'the so-called parts of an animal organism are not parts, but members or organic moments whose isolation and separate existence constitute disease' (Hegel, 1821/1991a, §278R). He reaches for the body to say what he means by a member of a state."

### What a Member Is, for the Instrument

**Flag 4 — technical imprecision an IIT reader will catch.**
> "where φ_s is the integrated information a given subset carries, and the winner is the argmax over every way the system could be partitioned"

The major complex is the argmax over **subsets** of their φ_s; each subset's φ_s is itself the minimum over that subset's partitions. The outer operation is subset-selection, not partition-search — and your own verbatim quote ("the subset that maximizes φ_s") says subset. Conflating the two undercuts the sentence built to display the receipt. Rewrite:
> "where φ_s is the integrated information a given subset carries — itself the value that survives the subset's own worst cut — and the winner is the argmax over subsets, not over partitions."
