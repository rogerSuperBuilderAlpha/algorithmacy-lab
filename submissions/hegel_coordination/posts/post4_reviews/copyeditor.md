### Flagged

  itself... and equally... to sublate the other moments external to it and promote itself at their
  cost, but no less to sublate itself and make itself a means for the other." In di Giovanni (digest
  #2, Q19) "to produce itself **and equally** to raise its particularity to universality, to sublate…"
  is continuous: there is no omitted text between "produce itself" and "and equally," so the first
  ellipsis is spurious, and the second omits "to raise its particularity to universality,". Correct to:
  "to produce itself and equally… to sublate the other moments external to it and promote itself at
  their cost, but no less to sublate itself and make itself a means for the other" (one ellipsis).
- **A2. Dropped original emphasis in the Kreines thesis quote (ll. 230–233).** Digest #5: "emphases in
  original on do/is/can." The published sentence italicizes *do* manifest, *is* explicable, *can* have.
  APA 7 requires reproducing the source's italics (no "[emphasis in original]" note needed). Restore:
  "that living beings *do* manifest true 'internal purposiveness,' that their structure and development
  *is* explicable in teleological terms, and that we *can* have objective knowledge…"
- **A3. Two Mechanism-chapter quotations have no entry in this digest (ll. 18–19 and 58–60).** "real

- **A4. Book titles inside quotations are not italicized.** L. 237, inside the Kreines quote:

## B. APA 7 in-text mechanics

  formatted, including citation placement after the closing period with no trailing period.
- **B2. First name in text (l. 242).** "Karen Ng pushes the same move deeper" — APA uses surname only:
  "Ng (2020) pushes…". This also fixes B3 for Ng.
- **B3. First narrative mentions lack the year.** L. 229 "the register Kreines names" — first mention
  of Kreines; the (Kreines, 2008, p. 346) parenthetical only arrives in the next sentence. Make it

  mentions without year — "Kreines can argue" (l. 313), "Koch's claim" (l. 322), "on Ng's reading"
  (l. 353) — are lower-stakes but a strict pass would repeat the year at the first mention in each new
  section.
- **B4. Inconsistent Remark/Addition notation.** L. 33: "§194, Addition 2"; l. 195: "§204R". Pick one
  convention ("§194, Addition 2" / "§204, Remark" spelled out, or "§194A2" / "§204R" abbreviated) and
  apply it in both places. (The sourcing note itself mixes them the same way, ll. 447.)
- **B5. Hybrid narrative citation (l. 199).** "Hegel calls inner purposiveness '…' (1816/2010b,
  p. 654)": APA's narrative form is Hegel (1816/2010b) … (p. 654); alternatively keep the full
  parenthetical (Hegel, 1816/2010b, p. 654). As written the year-only parenthetical after a narrative
  author is nonstandard.
- **B6. Ellipsis style.** Unspaced "..." at ll. 221 and 224. APA 7 uses three spaced periods ". . ."
  (or the standard ellipsis character consistently). Whichever the venue prefers, make ll. 221/224
  match each other and the fix in A1.
- **B7. Compound-date convention — clean.** Every SL citation is 1816/2010b, every EL citation is
  1830/2010a, and Kant is 1790/2000 at every occurrence (checked all: ll. 19, 33, 44, 52, 74, 88,

- **B8. Short-form page-only citations** ((p. 646), (p. 649), (p. 663) etc.) always follow a full
