**VERDICT: Sound scholarship, non-compliant apparatus — the citation content held up under independent spot-checking (every reachable Sans quote is verbatim and correctly paged), but the manuscript has one malformed APA structure, one 40+-word quote set run-in, and four quotations attributed to Sans that are actually Hegel's words quoted by Sans.**

**Single most important mechanical fix:** the Hegel 2010 / 2010b pairing is malformed. APA 7 (Manual §§8.19, 9.47) keys same-author/same-date disambiguation to the reference-list date element — which is (2010) for both Cambridge translations; the differing original years (1816, 1830) appear only in the in-text compound and do not lift the requirement, and a "2010b" with no "2010a" is malformed under any reading. Assign suffixes alphabetically by title: *Encyclopedia…* = **2010a**, *The science of logic* = **2010b** (E precedes S, ignoring "The"). That **reverses the current assignment**: every "(Hegel, 1816/2010, …)" becomes "(Hegel, 1816/**2010b**, …)" and every "(Hegel, 1830/2010b, …)" becomes "(Hegel, 1830/**2010a**, …)", in the list and in text, and the *Encyclopedia* entry moves ahead of the *Science of logic* entry in the list.
