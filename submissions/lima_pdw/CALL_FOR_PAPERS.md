# Call for applications — the governing requirements

**Transcribed 26 August 2026** from the call supplied by the author. This is the workshop's own
statement of what it wants, and it outranks this arm's assumptions about the same questions. The
submitted package is a separate record at [`ABSTRACT.md`](ABSTRACT.md); the acceptance is
LIMA2026-002.

**Paper Development Workshop in Organization Studies for Advanced PhD Students and Early Career
Scholars.** Universidad de Piura UDEP, Lima, Perú, 5–7 October 2026. Co-sponsored by *Organization
Studies* and *Organization Theory*, delivered with senior/associate editors and editorial board
members.

## What the workshop is designed to do

- strengthen the theoretical contribution and positioning of manuscripts
- refine framing, argumentation, and **journal fit**
- **articulate future research directions and agendas** building on ongoing work
- give first-hand insight into the editorial and review processes of *Organization Studies* and
  *Organization Theory*

## The distinctive aim, quoted

> A distinctive aim of this PDW is to support scholars working from and on Latin American and other
> emerging contexts, fostering dialogue between local empirical realities and global organization
> theory debates **without reducing such contexts to mere empirical sites**.

Priority in selection goes to applicants from the Latin American region.

## Format

Keynotes by the Co-Editors-in-Chief of both journals; paper roundtables in small groups with
dedicated editorial mentorship; panels on publishing strategy and academic careers. Each paper is
discussed in a small group, and **participants review the other manuscripts in their roundtable**.
Face-to-face participation is strongly encouraged.

## Who it is for

Advanced PhD students (typically post-proposal), early career researchers and junior faculty, and
scholars preparing manuscripts for submission in organization studies. **Both theoretical and
empirical papers are welcome, provided they engage substantively with organizational scholarship.**

## Costs

Free. Meals and refreshments provided. Travel and accommodation are the participant's own.

## What this arm had wrong, and what it means

Three discrepancies between the call and the arm's own files. None is urgent; all three should stop
being repeated.

1. **The registration deadline is 1 September, not 15 August.** [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md) opens on
   "Registration closes **15 August** (two days)" and built a schedule around that urgency.
   Registration was completed 14 August and the place is confirmed, so the error cost nothing.
2. **Notification was due 1 July; this abstract was accepted 13 August.** Six weeks late against the
   published schedule. Worth knowing only because it means the conveners were running behind, which
   bears on how quickly they will answer anything asked of them before 10 September.
3. **The disclosure letter's send-by predates the acceptance.**
   `correspondence/LIMA_ORGANIZERS.md` was drafted with a send-by of 29 July, and the acceptance
   arrived 13 August. Either the letter was drafted against the call's published notification date,
   or the acceptance date in [`README.md`](README.md) records when this arm learned of it. The open
   question in [`AGENDA.md`](AGENDA.md) item 2 — whether the letter ever went — is unaffected.
