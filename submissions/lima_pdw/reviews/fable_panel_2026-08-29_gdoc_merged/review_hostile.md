## Verdict

**Major revisions.** The single most damaging unresolved issue: **the paper's only empirical contact with the world — one pilot participant — behaved in the pattern the paper's own Table B1 lists as the disconfirming criterion for *specifying intent*, and the manuscript narrates that incident as support for the construct, contradicting its own description of the incident within the same paragraph.** At a live workshop, someone will read lines 312 and Table B1 aloud back to back, and the room will go quiet.

---

## Step 0 — register and bar

This is a solo-authored construct-development paper in the Suddaby (2010) genre, written deliberately formal, aimed at *Organization Theory* / *Organization Studies* readers, with a sustained per-construct hearing template that is an analytic structure, not a tic. I hold it to the bar of Rahman (2021), Cameron (2024), and Suddaby (2010), and to the author's own house style: named agents, claim-first, first person for the analytic moves, dense sentences punctured by short ones, zero self-narrated rigor. I do not attack the formality; I attack where the paper fails its own chosen standard.

# Part 1 — Theoretical rigor and argument structure

## Finding 1 (most damaging). The sole pilot datum is spun, and the spin contradicts the sentence before it

Line 312, "Instruments and Status," verbatim:

> "a participant navigating the intent-specification module resolved the communicative constraint by routing their explanation through an external language model to optimize parsing legibility, **entirely bypassing direct engagement with the human peer counterpart**. This emergent adaptation exemplifies how participants dynamically mobilize secondary computational tools **to satisfy the dual demands of machine parsability and peer comprehension**"

Those two sentences cannot both be true. The first says the participant addressed the machine only and bypassed the peer entirely. The second says the same act satisfied "peer comprehension." Now Table B1's own falsification criterion for *specifying intent* (line 599):

> "Participants achieve consistent evaluative success through isolated technical optimization without adopting dual-audience encoding."

The one behavioral episode the empirical program has produced is closer to that disconfirming pattern than to the confirming one. That is not fatal — one incident settles nothing, and the honest reading (the pilot surfaced a rival outcome the protocol must now distinguish) is actually a *stronger* methods story. But the manuscript instead performs alchemy: it relabels machine-only routing as dual-audience encoding one sentence after describing it as counterpart-bypass. A hostile discussant needs thirty seconds to find this. It is the claim most likely to sink the paper in the room, because it is empirical, quotable, and self-inflicted.

Compounding it: the pilot's N is concealed. Line 582 says "Diagnostic testing of the interview protocol across initial **pre-qualitative runs**" — "runs," plural, and "pre-qualitative," a category that does not exist. Three "instrument refinements" and two sections of "operational insights" hang on an undisclosed number of participants. If it is one participant, write "one participant." A term invented to lower the evidentiary bar ("pre-qualitative") reads as exactly that.

## Finding 2. Mutual bindingness is asserted, never demonstrated — and the failure boomerangs through the paper's own exclusions

The construct's definitional differentiator, stated repeatedly ("evaluates and binds both parties" — six verbatim occurrences), is what separates algorithmacy from all seven rivals in Table 1. At the flagship site (line 291), the submitter is bound: her progression turns on a score she cannot inspect. The peer evaluator "contribute[s] assessments to an opaque algorithm whose ultimate weighting logic remains hidden from them." That is *opacity toward* the evaluator, not a *binding determination upon* her. Nowhere does the manuscript state what the gate's verdict does to the reviewer's own standing. (The org-theory panelist reached this independently; I reached it independently too, and I add two consequences that reviewer did not draw.)

First consequence: the exclusion of Cotter (2019) boomerangs. Line 166 excludes visibility labor because "the platform algorithm evaluates and binds the creator alone." The paper thereby concedes that one-sided bindingness disqualifies a case from the construct's domain. If the Hult gate binds only the submitter, the paper's own criterion disqualifies the paper's own site.

Second consequence: apply the two-sided test across the claimed core domain (line 188: "on-demand dispatch networks, algorithmic piecework queues, and peer-evaluation systems") and ask which real settings survive. On Uber, ratings bind the driver; the passenger faces matching, not verdicts. On delivery platforms, same. The setting that most cleanly satisfies mutual bindingness in the wild is algorithmic matchmaking — both daters are scored, ranked, and deprioritized by the same opaque adaptive system — and the paper excludes dating in the conclusion (line 326: "These comparisons bound the construct within the coordination of interdependent work"). That "interdependent work" restriction appears **nowhere in the definition, scope-conditions, or construct sections**; the abstract says "coordinating with another person," full stop. A scope condition introduced on the last page, whose function is to exclude the best real-world instance of the construct's defining property, is not a boundary condition. It is a patch.

The pincer, stated plainly for the author to answer at the workshop: as intermediary bindingness increases (dispatch), counterpart-facing communication thins toward zero — a courier does not "specify intent" to a customer through the algorithm. As counterpart-facing communication thickens (Upwork), the paper demotes the setting to "a hybrid boundary" (line 188). The construct's novel content is largest exactly where its scope conditions are weakest. Unless the author can name a class of real settings that is simultaneously mutually binding, opaque, adaptive, *and* rich in through-the-intermediary counterpart communication — other than the course he built — the domain of the construct may be close to empty.

## Finding 3. Designed opacity cannot demonstrate intrinsic opacity — and the "concealed from everyone" claim is unexplained

Line 543:

> "This multi-site architectural variation demonstrates that opacity constitutes an intrinsic property of algorithmic coordination rather than an idiosyncratic feature of a single software user interface."

The three sites in that variation (Cursor Boston, Hult, Trinidad) were all designed by the author, and the empirical-strategy section says so: "cyclical evaluations directly informed ongoing pedagogical, **gate-mechanism**, and instrument redesigns" (line 285). Three variants of one designer's decision to withhold information demonstrate nothing intrinsic about algorithmic coordination; they demonstrate that the designer consistently designed opacity in. The inference is circular. (This is adjacent to, and not the same as, the already-flagged role-duality question: role duality concerns the researcher's relationship to participants; this concerns the validity of an inference from researcher-built artifacts to a claim about a class of organizational forms.)

The same designer-relation breaks a factual claim in the Introduction. Line 29: the weighting "remains concealed from everyone: the submitter, the peer reviewers, **and the instructor**, who holds no vote." The instructor built and iteratively refined the gate mechanism. In what sense are the aggregation weights concealed from the person who configured them? There is an answer that would make this true and genuinely interesting — weights randomized at deployment, or cryptographically committed and unread — but the manuscript never states any concealment mechanism. Appendix B deepens the hole: students "construct and inspect the codebase itself" (line 551), yet opacity survives in "concealed aggregation weights." Where do the weights live, if not in code the students can inspect? Until the concealment mechanism is specified, the site's central property is an assertion about a black box whose builder is the author.

## Finding 4. "Directly satisfying" a condition the paper admits it has not checked

"Why Hult Instantiates Coordinative Co-optation" (line 291) declares the scope conditions met — and its own next paragraph (line 293) concedes:

> "the formal research design **is documenting** the exact pairing architecture: whether the platform algorithm deterministically allocates peer reviewers to specific submissions or allows participants to self-select into evaluation queues."

Matching of *unchosen parties* is the defining mechanism of coordinative co-optation on the paper's own account of Stark and Vanden Broeck (line 48: platforms "match unchosen parties"). If reviewers self-select into queues, the parties choose each other, and the site loses the enrollment property that makes it an instance of the fifth form. The paper asserts the verdict in one paragraph and admits the evidence is pending in the next. The same paragraph defers the exit-rights question ("cataloging the precise institutional constraints governing participant exit options"), which matters because a student's exit option — dropping a course — has no analogue to "unilateral account termination," the sanction that anchors the form's definition at line 48. Either document the pairing and exit architecture before claiming instantiation, or write "the site instantiates the form conditionally on two parameters we verify before fielding."

## Finding 5. Adaptivity is satisfied by conflation — and the conflation biases the site toward the construct's own falsification

The definition, line 33: "Adaptivity means **the underlying evaluation rule** shifts dynamically over time." The site's satisfaction claim, line 291: "Because **the underlying codebase** against which submissions are evaluated evolves continuously as participants merge contributions, the evaluative benchmark shifts dynamically over time, directly satisfying the scope condition of environmental adaptivity."

A moving codebase is not a moving rule. The rule — the aggregation weights, the pass threshold — may be perfectly static at Hult while the substrate churns. Worse for the paper: codebase merges in a shared course repository are *publicly observable events*, and Table B1's falsification criterion for *keeping track* (line 600) reads: "All detected evaluative changes trace directly to public announcements or compiler updates, collapsing tracking into technical monitoring." If the only drift at the site is merge-driven and merges are visible in the repo history, the site is structurally rigged to produce the disconfirming pattern for the third operation. The paper needs either evidence that the *rule* shifts (do the weights change? does the threshold?) or an argument for why substrate drift under a fixed rule still demands *keeping track* rather than `git log`.

## Finding 6. "Derived deductively" is claimed three times and demonstrated zero times — and RQ4 concedes the deduction cannot count its own dimensions

The abstract, line 116, and line 204 all announce operations "derived deductively from the informational and relational deficits of the triad," explicitly contrasted with Long and Magerko's inductive compilation. The derivation is never shown. What the paper actually does is pair three structural properties with three operations by assertion (opacity→interpreting, constrained channels→specifying intent, adaptivity→keeping track). Why exactly three? Why not a decomposition of interpreting into system-facing and counterpart-facing components — a split the paper itself needs two pages later? The tell is RQ4: if the operations were genuinely deduced from the triad's structure, the dimensionality would be settled by the deduction, not left "an independent, fourth constitutive operation" for psychometrics to adjudicate (line 302). You cannot both claim deductive derivation and outsource the dimension count to a factor analysis.

And the candidate fourth operation cannot decide which parent it is being distinguished from. Line 212 subsumes attribution "directly within *interpreting*." Line 302 frames RQ4 as testing whether attribution is independent "rather than a facet of *interpreting*." Line 304 then says "The formulation of RQ4 explicitly decouples error attribution from ***keeping track***." Three statements, two different parent operations. Pick one and propagate it.

## Finding 7. The rival-collapse defense against Zhou et al. is definitional fiat

The paper's answer to "why can't Zhou and colleagues just add three counterpart items?" is line 128: "Appending additional survey items cannot resolve this theoretical omission because the foundational definition links only the focal worker and the algorithmic apparatus." Definitions are amendable; their authors amend them in every scale revision. The claim that a validated construct cannot be extended by its own authors is the weakest possible moat. The defensible version of the novelty claim — and the item-level analysis of Items 6, 8, and 11 earns it — is narrower: none of Zhou et al.'s twelve items reaches the counterpart, their remediation channel runs to the machine, and extending their construct to the counterpart axis would require re-deriving it from a triadic architecture, which is precisely this paper's contribution. Say that. The current sentence invites the reply "so they'll append the items and cite you in a footnote."

Adjacent and uncited: the *interpreting* operation, the only one with any pilot traction, is the one most heavily covered by existing person-system sensemaking work — Rahman's reverse-engineering (cited), but also the algorithmic imaginary and folk-theories literatures (Bucher's 2017 "algorithmic imaginary"; the CHI folk-theory line, e.g., DeVito and colleagues). Neither appears in the manuscript; a communication-studies discussant will name them within minutes and ask what *interpreting* adds beyond a counterpart clause. Verify those references before citing back — but the gap is real: the operation with the most evidence is the one with the least novelty, and the operations with the most novelty (specifying intent, keeping track) have, respectively, one contrary datum and a site rigged toward disconfirmation (Findings 1 and 5).

## Finding 8. The main text sells hypotheses the appendix retracts

"Semantic Relations and Hypothesized Covariation" (line 192) announces "testable empirical predictions across four established measurement traditions," with directional latent-correlation bands. Appendix B, line 578: "Given a cohort size of approximately fifty students, **the panel is not powered to perform exploratory or confirmatory factor analyses**," and the real tests are deferred to Study 2's pooled 200–250 (Appendix C). A reader of the main text believes this paper's empirical program tests the nomological network; a reader of the appendix learns it cannot. State the power limitation in the main text where the hypotheses are introduced, not three thousand words later.

In the same section of the design: "maintaining complete anonymity from the investigative team" (line 310; again at 576, 553). Client-side redaction of names does not anonymize a narrative about a specific pull request in a ~50-person course the investigator designed and runs; the incident is the identifier. This is not an ethics-clearance point (settled elsewhere); it is an accuracy point. "Complete anonymity" is a claim the design cannot deliver — write "identity-protected" or "pseudonymous with re-identification risk managed by X," and say what X is.

## Finding 9. Persisting defects the merge did not fix

- **The definition still splits across locations.** Abstract and Introduction (lines 17, 31) define interpreting over the *intermediary* ("interpret an opaque, adaptive intermediary... track its shifting parameters"); the Constitutive Operations bullet (line 206) defines it over "both the algorithmic intermediary **and the unseen human counterpart**." The counterpart clause is the paper's entire novelty, and it is absent from the two definitions a skimming reviewer will actually read. Flagged on 08-28; still true.
- **The uneven-distribution property is still circular.** Line 245: "If the capacity were uniformly distributed, it could not account for the persistent performance and coordination variance observed among participants." This assumes the capacity explains the variance in order to establish that the capacity varies — while the Rahman hearing (line 142) has already granted that dependence and evaluation shocks are "a formidable rival hypothesis" for that same variance. Flagged on 08-28 in substance; restated, not repaired.
- **The declared-then-bridged boundary.** Line 180: decoupling capacity from standing "preserv[es] a strict theoretical boundary between micro-level capacity and macro-level governance." Line 259: the same construct supplies "an explicit aggregation mechanism that links micro-level behavior to macro-structural stability," and Propositions 1–3 make claims about procedural justice, inequality, and regulatory levers. Capacity-vs-standing and micro-vs-macro are different distinctions; the paper announces the strict version of one and then crosses the other. Drop "strict," name the boundary as capacity/standing only, and the propositions stop looking like trespass.

## Finding 10. Smaller theory problems

- **The flagship metaphor is dyadic.** The Orientation's anchor image (line 15) is "an experienced pilot operating an automated flight-control system" — a person-system dyad, the exact configuration the paper spends six thousand words distinguishing itself from. There is no second human on the far side of an autopilot. The paper's own pull-request scene (line 29) is the right opening image and sits a page too late.
- **"Systematic review" (abstract) overclaims.** No search protocol, no corpus, no stated selection procedure for the seven constructs; and line 56 admits the four evaluation criteria are "detailed later in this section" — i.e., stated at line 172, *after* all seven hearings they allegedly governed. Criteria presented after the sample they select read as retrofitted. Call it a comparative construct review and move the four conditions ahead of the hearings.
- **Simon as decoration, twice.** "We formalize algorithmacy to a satisficing threshold in Simon's (1997) behavioral sense" (line 23) and "adopting an aspiration level in Simon's (1997) sense" (line 253). Satisficing describes a decision-maker's search behavior; it is not a license for a theorist to stop theorizing, and citing Simon to dignify "we deliberately stop short" is jargon-substitution. Suddaby (2010), already cited, is the honest warrant for bounding a construct paper.
- **"A Paradox the Construct Raises Rather Than Resolves" contains no stated paradox.** The section delivers a decoupling, an aggregation mechanism, and three propositions. The implied tension — individual mastery stabilizes the regime that burdens individuals — is Cameron's (2024) manufactured-consent point, which the section itself concedes; the increment ("leaves unspecified the individual capacity") is real but is not a paradox. Retitle or state the paradox in one sentence.
- **Hayek stretch (question, not assertion).** Line 42/186 makes "an unrestricted right of refusal" definitional to the market archetype "(Hayek, 1945)." Hayek 1945 is about dispersed knowledge and the price system; I do not recall a right-of-refusal argument there. Is this cite carrying a Weberian/Williamsonian point under Hayek's name? Verify.
- **Manky (question).** Line 174 reads Manky's drivers inferring "passenger trustworthiness" for safety as evidence of a "counterpart-directed interpretive sensibility" awaiting formalization. Screening a stranger for danger is not coordinating interdependent work with a counterpart; by the paper's late-arriving "interdependent work" scope (line 326), this evidence may be out of the construct's own domain. Reconcile.

# Part 2 — AI-slop audit

The good news first, because it is measurable: **zero** agentless "has been / have been" constructions in 13,000 words, zero banned emphasis openers (no "Crucially," "Importantly," "Notably"). The lexical hygiene layer is clean. The slop here lives one layer up.

**Em-dash count: 10 in the body** (13,006 words) = 0.77 per 1,000. That is not overuse — it is a quarter of the house floor (~3/1k), and the deficit is doing damage: constructs are glossed in trailing subordinate clauses instead of welded appositives, which is one reason every definition runs 40 words. No em-dash complaint is warranted; the opposite one is.

**Rhythm: uniform maximal density.** 483 body sentences, mean 26.9 words — on the venue mean — but only ~27 sentences under 12 words, and most of those are artifacts (headers, stat fragments). The genuinely short declaratives that exist are all the *same* sentence: "This distinction establishes an explicit theoretical boundary" (line 88), "This departure from computer-mediated communication is structurally definitive" (line 84), "This exit strategy marks the definitive theoretical boundary of platform literacy" (line 152). The corpus this paper is styled on punctures dense builds with short turns ("It does not." "The claim is contested."); this manuscript's only punctures are verdict-epigrams of one recycled shape. Read any page aloud: no sentence breathes.

**The recycled thought experiment.** The ceiling-mastery gambit runs four times: "an actor who demonstrates complete mastery across every dimension of Spitzberg's model" (line 78), "An individual may achieve comprehensive mastery within human–machine communication" (line 88), "Achieving ceiling performance across all seventeen competencies" (line 112), "Ceiling performance on Zhou et al.'s twelve-item scale" (line 128). It is a good move once. Four instances is a template, and a reader who notices the template stops crediting the move. Keep the Spitzberg and Zhou instances (the two rivals where it bites hardest); compress the other two to a clause.

**The uniform coda.** All seven hearings close on the identical inheritance move: "Algorithmacy directly inherits Spitzberg's normative evaluative criteria..." / "We adopt this operational distinction..." / "We adopt the optimization goal parameter..." / "We adopt Long and Magerko's foundational literacy genealogy" / "Algorithmacy inherits Zhou and colleagues' scale development protocol..." / "Algorithmacy directly incorporates three foundational insights from Rahman's framework" / "Algorithmacy inherits two foundational insights from Sutherland and colleagues' account." The hearing template itself is legitimate analytic structure — I do not flag it. The coda uniformity is a tell: seven sections ending on the same verb in the same slot. Vary the exit: close one hearing on the rival's strongest unanswered point, another on a forward hook to the next construct.

**Lexical drumbeat.** "structural/structurally" ×82 (6.3 per 1,000 words — more frequent than "the construct's" own name); "counterpart" ×72; "intermediary" ×46; "opaque" ×45 + "opacity" ×13; "binding" ×35; "opaque, adaptive" ×12 and "opaque, binding" ×12 as frozen collocations; "theoretical boundary" ×7, "structural boundary" ×4, "theoretical vacancy" ×3; "micro-foundation(al)" ×15. The construct terms are partly earned; "structural" is the intensifier crutch — "structural impasse," "structurally definitive," "structural reversion," "structural vacancy," "structural omission" — in most instances the sentence loses nothing without it. Cut half.

**Performed rigor / self-narrated candor** (the house style bans every one of these; each quote exact):
- Line 293: "**To maintain methodological transparency**, two specific operational parameters of the gate mechanism warrant explicit identification as ongoing design specifications in the empirical protocol." — announcing the virtue of disclosure instead of disclosing.
- Line 553: "**The research design secures methodological trustworthiness through an auditable research trail** (Pratt, Kaplan, & Whittington, 2020; Tracy, 2010)." — a sentence about the design's virtue, two citations, zero content. What is in the trail?
- Line 261: "This aggregation mechanism **yields testable, falsifiable predictions** at the collective level." — state the predictions (the next sentence does); do not also advertise their falsifiability.
- Line 212: "**This structural boundary remains empirically falsifiable.**" — same move.

**Passive hiding the agent, concentrated where agency matters most** (Appendix B, i.e., where the author's own hands are on the apparatus): "Role duality **is managed** through deliberate structural safeguards" (553), "Ethical oversight **was established**" (578), "The protocol **was refined**" (586), "participant intake... **is strictly anonymized**" (553). Who manages, established, refined, anonymizes? The author. The one place the house rule ("named agents") earns its keep — the researcher describing his own interventions — is the one place the manuscript goes agentless.

**First person, against the author's own calibration.** Solo-authored paper; "we" ×19, authorial "I" ×0. The house file is explicit: solo work takes "I" (Rahman 2021, Cameron 2024, Suddaby 2010 all do), at 4–8 per 1,000 words for the analytic moves; this manuscript runs a royal "we" at 1.5 per 1,000. Either recruit a coauthor or own the moves.

**Nominalization / jargon-substitution**, quoted: "we formalize algorithmacy **to a satisficing threshold**" (23); "**construct crystallization**" (545); "no empirical **casings** that the derived operations cannot accommodate" (611); "an **abductive sufficiency criterion**" (611); "**pre-qualitative** runs" (582); "under precisely that **structural reversion**" (238). Each has a plain equivalent the venue would prefer.

# Closing note

The genuine strength, which no amount of hostility dissolves: **the seven hearings are real readings.** Item-level engagement with Zhou et al. (Items 6, 8, 11, the r = .37/.04 discriminant pattern), page-level engagement with Rahman's client interviews and the platform's anonymity assurances, the honest naming of Rahman's dependence findings as a rival hypothesis the paper must control for — this is the kind of close rival-reading that most construct papers fake with a citation string, and it is why the vacancy claim, narrowed properly, will probably survive the room even after everything above lands.

The one thing only the author can supply, which no reviewer can fabricate or verify from the text: **the facts of the gate.** Whether the weights actually shift, how they are concealed and from whom, whether the verdict does anything to the evaluator, whether reviewers are assigned or self-select, and the true pilot N. Every load-bearing empirical claim in this manuscript resolves to a system one person built and data one person holds. Until those five facts are on the page, the paper is asking the workshop to take its own site on faith — and this workshop, unlike this reviewer, will be polite about it exactly once.
