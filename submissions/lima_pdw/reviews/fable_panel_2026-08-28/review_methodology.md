# Review — Qualitative / Mixed-Methods Methodologist

**Manuscript:** "Algorithmacy: A Competency for Coordinative Co-optation" (OS/OT PDW, Lima, October 2026)
**Reviewer lens:** action research (Coghlan & Brannick), abductive analysis (Timmermans & Tavory), insider/practitioner research, critical incident technique, mixed-methods panel design.
**Date:** 2026-08-28

---

## Verdict

**Major revisions.** The single most important fix: bring the quantitative arm's ambitions into line with what a single course cohort can supply. As written, the survey panel promises latent-factor dimensionality, discriminant validity against Zhou et al.'s (2025) validated scale, and pre-threshold latent correlations — tests that require samples in the hundreds administered with a scale whose items do not yet exist and whose generation step appears nowhere in the design. Either state the expected N and downgrade the panel to what it can actually do (item piloting, descriptive within-person change), or move the psychometric program explicitly to studies 3–4. Everything else in this review is repairable by wording; this is a feasibility fault line. (The most urgent *mechanical* fix is smaller: the advisor's initials — "PB" — appear in the body text at "Two of PB's six diagnostic questions." No workshop reviewer will know who PB is.)

---

## Step 0 — Register and bar

Register: an OS/OT conceptual PDW paper in first-person plural — a Suddaby-style construct paper carrying a concise empirical strategy in the main text and the full protocol in an appendix. Bar: the methods standard of the paper's own genre anchors (Rahman, 2021; Cameron, 2024) — a design that a top journal's reviewers would accept as a credible pre-data protocol, judged as a protocol, not as findings.

## Part 1 — Methodological rigor and argument structure

### 1.1 The insider-action-research frame: invoked for its license, not yet carrying its obligations

The paper's use of Coghlan and Brannick is half right, and the half that is right is genuinely right. The core-project / thesis-project distinction is their own vocabulary, used accurately: the two-year GauntleTT program is plausibly the core action research project (constructing, planning, taking, evaluating action across five institutional configurations), and the paper's account of what each cycle's evaluation changed in the next cycle's constructing — site change altering who evaluates whom, gate change altering what a participant can see — is the most convincing action-research writing in the manuscript. Scoping the not-yet-collected data as Paper 3 via that distinction is legitimate, not evasive; Coghlan and Brannick built the distinction for exactly this situation.

The half that is wrong: the *thesis* cycle, as designed, is not action research, and the design's own ethics architecture is what makes it not action research. Insider action research is premised on engaged, collaborative inquiry with organizational members — first-, second-, and third-person practice, in Coghlan and Brannick's own frame. The thesis cycle described in Appendix B severs every one of those relationships by design: intake is fully anonymized, no records track invitations, transcripts route through a third-party endpoint, and the survey panel firewalls identity behind self-generated codes. Students are subjects, not co-inquirers; no organizational change is named as the thesis cycle's intended action; the "action" (redesigning course, gate, and instrument across cohorts) belongs entirely to the core cycle the paper has already fenced off. What remains is a well-specified insider *research* design — Brannick and Coghlan's (2007) "In defense of being native" is the correct citation base for it, and the paper already cites it — wearing action research's label to legitimate role duality without taking on action research's participatory obligations. A hostile referee will put it exactly that way. The fix is cheap: keep the core/thesis distinction, name the core cycle as action research, and name the thesis cycle as insider-sited qualitative and survey research whose access and instrument derive from the action research that preceded it. The Riordan quotation (used twice, verbatim — see Part 2) can then do its work once, for the core cycle, where it belongs.

### 1.2 The abductive apparatus: more operationalized than most, with three imported foreign parts

Credit first: this design operationalizes Timmermans and Tavory further than most published papers that cite them. Revisiting, defamiliarization, and alternative casing are each named *and* given a procedure; the sensitizing-concepts framing carries Bowen's (2006) precedent of concepts discarded as evidence of rigor; the negative-case criterion (a participant who satisfies the gate through technical mastery alone) is exactly the disconfirming instance the construct needs to be able to produce; and the Gioia first-order compatibility argument is handled with a sophistication reviewers rarely see. Table B1's disconfirming-evidence column is the design's best single artifact.

Three parts of the apparatus, though, are imported from alien epistemic traditions and sit badly:

**(a) The blind second coder.** A "second coder blind to this paper's derivation of the three operations" who "codes independently" imports an intercoder-reliability logic from a positivist coding tradition into an abductive design that runs on revisiting and recasing, not on agreement. The design also never says what the blind coder codes *with* — if she is blind to the operations, she cannot apply them as sensitizing concepts, so her codes and the focal analysis are not comparable products; and no procedure is given for what happens when the coders diverge (consensus? adjudication? both kept?). Pratt, Kaplan, and Whittington (2020) — whom the paper cites — argue precisely against treating reliability mechanics as the warrant for qualitative trustworthiness. Either specify the blind coder's actual function (an independent first-order pass whose divergences feed the discard log would be coherent) or cut her.

**(b) Saturation as the sufficiency criterion.** "Theoretical saturation across all four modules" grafts a grounded-theory stopping rule onto an abductive design, and attributes it to Pratt (2009), whose actual argument is that qualitative sufficiency has no generic template — the citation licenses the *absence* of a boilerplate criterion, not this particular one. Abduction stops when surprise is worked through and the casing stabilizes, which is a different (and defensible) rule; state that one.

**(c) Defamiliarization via transcription — with no transcription.** Timmermans and Tavory's defamiliarization runs through the analyst's own labor of transcribing. In this design an AI agent generates the transcript, auto-redacts names, places, and dates in real time, and the participant then edits or deletes text before submission. The estranging labor being cited does not occur, and the inscribed record that "revisiting" will return to is participant-curated and machine-redacted — both facts in tension with the analytic architecture built on returning to the same inscribed observation. One honest sentence acknowledging that the transcript is a jointly produced, participant-edited artifact — and saying what defamiliarization means when the analyst did not transcribe — would close this.

Two smaller winces in the same section. The critical incident technique is invoked ("Each module operationalizes the critical incident technique") with no citation — Flanagan (1954) is absent from the references, and a methods reviewer will check. And the CIT's defining discipline is probing to behavioral specificity, which the harness implements as *one* automated reframing prompt per module; whether a single fixed reframe suffices is an empirical question the pilot has already answered in the negative for two modules ("general summaries accepted where a specific incident was needed"). The tightened protocol may still be one probe short.

### 1.3 The AI-administered interview: the design's largest unexamined instrument effect

The paper studies how participants coordinate through an opaque AI intermediary — using an interview administered through an AI intermediary whose probing behavior, summarization tendencies, and conversational steering the participant cannot inspect. The instrument reproduces the triad it studies: participant, AI interviewer, unseen researcher. The manuscript says nothing about this. It is either the design's most interesting reflexive feature (the interview *is* a coordination episode of the kind under study, and could be analyzed as one) or its most serious validity threat (an LLM interviewer's variance across sessions is uncontrolled, and participants performing for an AI may report differently than they would to a person — a demand characteristic Mercer's citation does not cover, because Mercer's is about the human instructor). Either way it needs a paragraph. A referee at *Organization Studies* will not miss it, and the author is better positioned than any reviewer to say which of the two it is.

### 1.4 The self-generated-code panel: the mechanism is sound; the arm built on it is not

The self-generated confidential code does solve the specific problem the advisor flagged — anonymity from the researcher with within-person linkage across waves — and the paper is right to call it a design change touching consent language rather than a caution. The text handles the IRB status accurately and honestly (exempt determination for the original design, change flagged for confirmation), and I raise nothing further on that front. Two problems remain on the methodological side, one known to the literature and one specific to this design:

**Linkage failure.** The self-generated identification code literature (Yurek, Vasey, & Havens, 2008, is the standard cite; the design cites none of it) reports match failures of 10–30% across waves as participants misremember their own codes, and the rate worsens with interval length — this panel's third interval is twelve weeks. On a single course cohort, three-wave attrition compounded by linkage failure can halve the usable linked N. The design should cite the SGIC literature, adopt one of its standard remedies (multi-element derived codes rather than free-chosen ones), and state an expected linkage rate.

**The arm's ambitions exceed the site — and the scale does not exist.** This is the review's central finding. The panel promises to "evaluate latent-factor dimensionality, internal coherence, and discriminant separation," with pre-threshold latent correlations against Zhou et al.'s dimensions. Three things stand in the way. First, N: Zhou and colleagues needed samples of 213–275 *per study* for their factor analyses and 225 for their three-wave panel; a single credit-bearing course section cannot approach this, and the manuscript never states an expected N anywhere. Second, sequencing: latent correlations require an algorithmacy *scale*, and no item-generation step exists anywhere in the design — Zhou et al. generated their 14 initial items from 99 interviews, while this design's qualitative corpus is collected in the *same semester* the panel already runs, so wave 1 (week 1) would administer items derived from data not yet coded. The paper never says what the panel's waves actually contain apart from the wave-2 Zhou module. Third, a single wave-2 administration of the discriminant module cannot separate trait covariance from occasion variance. The repair is structural, not cosmetic: let Paper 3's panel do what a small cohort can do — pilot candidate items generated *from the harness corpus as it accrues*, describe within-person change, estimate test-retest behavior of the Zhou scale in a builder population — and move formal scale validation and discriminant testing to study 3, where the design already gestures at a larger population. Appendix C's second study should say this openly rather than promising EFA-grade inference from a classroom.

### 1.5 The Hult site justification: honest flagging of the wrong things to leave open

Flagging the two unconfirmed facts rather than inventing them is the right instinct, and the disconfirming-criteria posture throughout the design earns the paper real credibility. But the two open questions are not peripheral details, and one of them is definitional. The paper's own definition of coordinative co-optation opens with matching: "an algorithm matches parties who did not choose each other." If reviewer pairing at Hult is self-selected, the site fails the form's first clause, and the section titled "Why Hult Instantiates Coordinative Co-optation, Not a Hybrid Form" has argued a conclusion its own evidence leaves open. Exit and appeal rights bear on bindingness the same way. A section title that asserts instantiation cannot coexist with an unresolved instantiation condition; either confirm before submission or retitle conditionally ("Why Hult Is a Candidate Instantiation…").

There is also an awkwardness the paper should not let a referee discover first: the investigator *built and repeatedly redesigned* the gate. A reader will reasonably ask why the person who built the system does not know whether it assigns reviewer pairs. Presumably the true answer is mundane (the mechanism changed across cohorts, or pairing is configurable and the spring 2027 configuration is undecided) — but then *that* is the accurate statement, and it is stronger than an unexplained epistemic gap: the pairing rule is a design parameter the author controls and will fix before fielding, not a fact about the world awaiting discovery. Say so. An unconfirmed fact about one's own artifact reads as not having looked; an unfixed design parameter reads as a decision pending, which is what it is.

### 1.6 The Trinidad strategic-site argument: a claimed natural experiment with no records

The Merton / Eisenhardt-and-Graebner framing is deployed accurately in form, and the no-pre-sorted-workforce argument ("that behavioral variance had no conventional explanations to hide behind") is the appendix's most striking sentence. But the same appendix reports that Trinidad's operational logs were not preserved and that gate mechanics are being reconstructed through a retrospective self-protocol — the investigator's own memory, interviewed by an automated agent. The strategic-site virtue is therefore claimed for observations that were never recorded. As a context-of-discovery narrative — this is where the construct's intuitions formed, and why — the section is valuable and should stay. As a strategic-research-site justification in Merton's sense it cannot stand, because the phenomenon's tractability was never captured in inspectable form. Reframe the section's job: Trinidad explains where the construct came from; Hult is the strategic site, and the Merton/Eisenhardt apparatus should attach to Hult (where it will also have to survive §1.5's repair).

### 1.7 The core/thesis scoping and "Paper 3": legitimate, and mostly policed

Is calling the not-yet-collected data "Paper 3" a way of outsourcing empirical accountability? On balance, no. A PDW full paper presenting a construct plus a pre-data protocol is a recognized genre; the paper states what would count as evidence against the construct in every module (Table B1), specifies falsification criteria for the candidate fourth operation, and repeatedly declines to let the pilot do findings work. The scoping survives. Two residues of the pre-revision overclaiming remain, though, and both undercut the discipline the paper otherwise maintains: Appendix B calls the harness and panel "two calibrated instruments" when one instrument has been run exactly once, two of its four modules have never met a live response (Table B1, row 4: "not yet piloted against a live response"), and the panel has never been fielded in its revised form; and the abstract says the empirical strategy was "piloted across two developmental transcripts" when the paper's own pilot section states that one transcript was read informally and the second has not been read at all. "Calibrated" and "across two" are exactly the words the advisor's overclaiming flag was about. Fix both (exact lines in Part 3).

### 1.8 The two claims a hostile referee attacks first

Named, per the panel brief: **(1)** "The empirical program follows insider action research" (Empirical Strategy, first sentence) — attacked as label without obligations, per §1.1; survives only after the core/thesis reframing, which the paper's own materials fully support. **(2)** "The panel evaluates latent-factor dimensionality, internal coherence, and discriminant separation" (Appendix B, Longitudinal Survey Panel) — attacked on N, on the nonexistent scale, and on sequencing, per §1.4; does **not** survive as written, and no rewording short of restructuring the arm's claims will save it.

## Part 2 — Slop audit, methodological-credibility items only

A separate reviewer holds the full copyedit brief; I flag only what damages the design's trustworthiness from my lens.

**Self-narrated rigor.** The project's own style authority bans sentences that comment on the paper's care, and the extant-constructs review contains several that perform methodological virtue instead of exercising it: "engaging each on its strongest theoretical ground before establishing the analytical limits of its scope"; "Rejecting this assumption requires first addressing a reductive reading of Spitzberg that would render the critique trivial"; "This framework presents the most critical analytical challenge in the review, where any superficial critique based merely on node enumeration inevitably fails"; "a multi-stage scale development protocol that mirrors the empirical standards our own research program advances" (self-praise routed through a rival). Worst of all, in the site section: "with the precision a workshop reviewer is entitled to expect, and we flag rather than guess at them" — a sentence that ranks its own candor while addressing the reviewer directly. Every one of these weakens the very credibility it advertises; the steelmanning should be visible in the argument, not announced.

**Hedge-stacking on the pilot.** The not-evidence disclaimer appears at least five times: "neither is evidence for the construct" (main text); "None of it is presented as Paper 3's findings"; "neither counts as evidence for or against the three constitutive operations"; "none of them a finding about algorithmacy"; "an argument for the instrument's readiness, not for what participants' accounts will show." One flat statement, placed once, reads as honest scoping. Five read as anxiety, and anxiety reads as a paper that does not trust its own scoping — the opposite of the intended effect. Keep the Appendix B instance (the fullest), and one short main-text instance; cut the rest.

**Verbatim repetition.** The Riordan quotation ("require[s] a practitioner of science…") appears twice in full, in the main-text Empirical Strategy section and again in Appendix B's positionality section. The house rule is one strong instance; per §1.1, it belongs to the core cycle, so keep the Appendix B instance and let the main text paraphrase.

**The templated ceiling-performance counterfactual.** Five construct sections run the same thought-experiment shape — "an actor exhibiting comprehensive mastery/competence within X … encounters a structural breakdown/impasse" (Spitzberg, HMC, AI-MC, AI literacy, Zhou). The counterfactual is the paper's genuine boundary test and should stay — once at full length, and thereafter compressed to a clause, because the fifth full performance of the same move reads as generation, not analysis, and invites exactly the "armchair test" dismissal the design section then has to fight.

## Part 3 — Line-level revisions

Highest-value flags, with paste-ready replacements in the paper's register.

**(1) The "PB" leak** (Why Hult Instantiates…, ¶2). Current: "Two of PB's six diagnostic questions are not yet established with the precision a workshop reviewer is entitled to expect, and we flag rather than guess at them: …"
Replace with:
> "Two features of the gate remain unconfirmed: whether the algorithm assigns which peers review which submissions, or whether pairing is left to self-selection; and what exit or appeal rights a participant holds if she disputes a verdict. Both are design parameters of the spring 2027 course rather than open empirical facts, and we fix them in the course design before Paper 3 is fielded; the assignment question bears directly on whether the site meets the form's matching condition, so no claim in this section should be read as settled until it is."

**(2) The section title** (same section). Current: "Why Hult Instantiates Coordinative Co-optation, Not a Hybrid Form."
Replace with:
> "Why Hult Is a Candidate Instantiation of Coordinative Co-optation, Not a Hybrid Form"
(or restore the assertive title after the assignment rule is confirmed algorithmic).

**(3) "Two calibrated instruments"** (Appendix B, core/thesis paragraph). Current: "…an established research site, two calibrated instruments, and two harness transcripts, one of them piloted."
Replace with:
> "…an established research site, two instruments revised through a first round of piloting, and two harness transcripts, one of them read."

**(4) The abstract's pilot claim.** Current: "…piloted across two developmental transcripts and formally fielded as Paper 3…"
Replace with:
> "…revised against an initial developmental transcript and formally fielded as Paper 3 in a specialized software-engineering course at Hult International Business School."

**(5) The action-research frame** (Empirical Strategy, first sentence). Current: "The empirical program follows insider action research (Coghlan & Brannick, 2014): the investigator is the instructor who built and repeatedly redesigned the course this paper studies…"
Replace with:
> "The empirical program distinguishes, following Coghlan and Brannick (2014), a core action research project from the thesis project conducted about it. The core project is the two-year program itself — the investigator built and repeatedly redesigned the course, the gate, and the instruments across five institutional configurations, each cycle's evaluation feeding the next cycle's constructing. The thesis project, Paper 3, is insider research on that program's final configuration (Brannick & Coghlan, 2007): it takes no further action within the studied sessions, and its instruments are anonymized precisely to hold the instructor's engagement apart from the students' accounts of it."
(Then let the Riordan quotation appear once, in Appendix B, attached to the core project.)

**(6) The survey panel's claims** (Appendix B, Longitudinal Survey Panel, closing sentences). Current: "…whereas the panel evaluates latent-factor dimensionality, internal coherence, and discriminant separation from neighboring constructs."
Replace with:
> "…whereas the panel does what a single course cohort can support: it pilots candidate algorithmacy items generated from the harness corpus as it accrues, describes within-person change across the three waves, and administers Zhou, Lei, Liu, Huang, and Hou's (2025) scale at wave 2 as a first, descriptive check on convergence. Formal tests of latent-factor dimensionality and discriminant separation require samples this site cannot supply — Zhou and colleagues' own analyses ran on 213 to 275 respondents per study — and belong to the third study of the research agenda (Appendix C)."
(Appendix C, study 2, needs the matching change: reassign the pre-threshold latent-correlation test from the Hult cohort to the study-3 population.)

**(7) The linkage mechanism** (same section). After "…re-enters it at each subsequent wave," add:
> "Self-generated codes carry a known cost: participants misremember them, and matched-wave losses of ten to thirty percent are typical (Yurek, Vasey, & Havens, 2008). The panel therefore derives each code from stable personal elements rather than free choice, and we treat the realized linkage rate as a reported outcome of the design, not an assumption."

**(8) The AI interviewer** (Appendix B, harness protocol section). Add, after the paragraph on the in-editor architecture:
> "The instrument reproduces, in miniature, the structure it studies: the participant coordinates with an unseen researcher through an AI intermediary whose probing behavior she cannot inspect. We treat this as a validity consideration rather than an ornament — an automated interviewer's session-to-session variance is uncontrolled, and accounts performed for a machine may differ from accounts performed for a person — and Paper 3 logs the full prompt-and-probe sequence of every session so that instrument behavior can be examined alongside participant responses."

**(9) Self-ranked candor** (delete wherever it survives revision (1)): any residue of "we flag rather than guess," "the precision a workshop reviewer is entitled to expect," and, in the constructs review, "This framework presents the most critical analytical challenge in the review…" — replace the last with a claim-first opener:
> "By its own formal definition, AI-mediated communication is triadic: two human interactants and a computational agent. The critique therefore cannot rest on counting nodes."

**(10) CIT citation** (harness protocol section). After "Each module operationalizes the critical incident technique," insert "(Flanagan, 1954)" and add the reference: Flanagan, J. C. (1954). The critical incident technique. *Psychological Bulletin, 51*(4), 327–358.

## Findings, ranked most-damaging first

1. **The quantitative arm is infeasible as specified** (Instruments and Status; Appendix B, Longitudinal Survey Panel; Appendix C, study 2). Latent-structure and discriminant tests promised on a single course cohort, with no stated N, no algorithmacy items, and no item-generation step — while the qualitative corpus the items must come from is collected in the same semester the panel already runs. Fix per Part 3, items 6–7.
2. **"PB" appears in the manuscript body, inside a site-justification whose own matching condition is unconfirmed** (Why Hult Instantiates…, ¶2). The assignment-of-reviewer-pairs question is definitional to coordinative co-optation, so the section's assertive title outruns its evidence; and since the author built the gate, the open question should be presented as a pending design decision, not an unknown fact. Fix per Part 3, items 1–2.
3. **The action-research frame claims a license without its obligations** (Empirical Strategy, ¶1; Appendix B, Research Question and Positionality). The thesis cycle takes no action and severs engagement by design; it is insider research enabled by prior action research, and saying so is both more accurate and more defensible. Fix per Part 3, item 5.
4. **Three foreign parts inside the abductive apparatus** (Appendix B, Harness Protocol and Abductive Design; What Paper 3 Will Do): a blind second coder with no specified codebook or divergence procedure, saturation as a stopping rule misattributed to Pratt (2009), and defamiliarization-via-transcription cited for transcripts no analyst transcribes — compounded by participant editing and real-time redaction of the record that "revisiting" depends on. Flanagan (1954) uncited.
5. **The Trinidad strategic-site argument rests on unrecorded observations** (Appendix B, Trinidad Field Setting). "No conventional explanations to hide behind" describes variance never captured in inspectable form; reattach the Merton/Eisenhardt apparatus to Hult and let Trinidad be the context of discovery.
6. **The AI-administered interview is unexamined as an instrument effect** (Appendix B, Harness Protocol). The design's most interesting reflexive property, currently invisible. Fix per Part 3, item 8.
7. **Residual overclaims and credibility-costing prose**: "two calibrated instruments"; the abstract's "piloted across two developmental transcripts"; five repetitions of the not-evidence disclaimer; the Riordan quotation twice verbatim; self-narrated rigor in the constructs review and site section. Fixes per Part 3, items 3–4, 9, and Part 2.

## Closing note

The paper's biggest genuine strength, from this lens, is its disconfirmation architecture. Table B1's fourth column — a specific, falsifying observation named for every module — together with the negative-case criterion, the discard log, the Bowen precedent for losing sensitizing concepts, and the pre-specified conditions under which the candidate fourth operation would be promoted or the tripartite structure would stand, amounts to a construct paper that has told its future critics exactly where to aim. Very few pre-data designs in this literature do that, and the pilot section's discipline in reporting instrument failures rather than construct findings is the same virtue practiced. Protect it through revision; it is worth more than any of the prose.

The one thing only the author can supply, which no reviewer can fabricate: the facts of his own gate. Whether the algorithm assigns reviewer pairs, what exit or appeal rights exist, what the spring 2027 configuration will actually be, and how many students will sit in the room — these are not literature questions, and every structural argument in the empirical half of the paper (the site's instantiation of the form, the panel's feasibility, the bindingness of the verdict) waits on them. The author built the system; the paper needs him to look at it.
