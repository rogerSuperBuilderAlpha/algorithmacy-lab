# Departures from the accepted abstract

**Superseded as a working ledger, 2026-08-13.** The manuscript keeps its own, and keeps it where
reviewers will see it: `dissertation/current/paper2/PAPER.md` opens with a first-page note — typeset

## The four deltas, as the manuscript states them

1. **Order.** The coordination genealogy leads and the construct follows, rather than the reverse.
2. **Evidence.** The abstract promised empirical evidence from a Caribbean cohort. The paper carries
   a research design and a formation account. No data exists.
3. **The third component.** Temporal tracking, not machine orchestration. §7 gives the reason and
   treats the cut as a worked example of the admission rule.
4. **The protocol.** The approved protocol is the Hult sixteen-week study, waves 1/4/16, not the
   GauntleTT eight-week design the abstract's method section described. GauntleTT is where the
   construct was formed; Hult is where it is studied. The abstract's IRB sentence was ahead of itself
   and has been corrected to the organizers in writing.
