# Citation traps — live hazards

Sources a reviewer can open and find wrong, or that an agent will retrieve as the wrong
work. Lifted from the 18 August [`REVISION_MEMO.md`](../REVISION_MEMO.md) housekeeping
section and from [`REF_VERIFY.md`](../../../dissertation/current/paper2/design/REF_VERIFY.md).
[`AGENDA.md`](../AGENDA.md) item 13 already names the Zhou collision.

| Slug or cite | What it actually is | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `eisenhardt1989` | "Agency Theory: An Assessment and Review" — **not** "Building Theories from Case Study Research." Same author, journal, and year. | Do not cite this slug for the case-study paper. |
| `polanyi1944` | **Karl** Polanyi, *The Great Transformation*. Not Michael. | Keep the slug; do not attribute Michael Polanyi's tacit knowledge to it. |
| `sandberg2010` | Sandberg & Alvesson on gap-spotting. | §8's anchor is `sandberg2000`. This slug is a different paper. |
| `sandberg2000` | Understanding human competence at work. No Crossref abstract. | Read before rewriting §8. Lima card is `metadata_only`; dissertation card is `abstract_plus_reviews`. |
| `west2019` | Sarah Myers West, *Data capitalism*. Not the moderation folk-theories paper. | Different West. |
| `emerson1962` / `emerson1976` | **Richard** Emerson (power-dependence), not **Robert** Emerson (fieldnotes). | Check the first name before citing. |
| `langleyleyshon2021` | **Paul** Langley, not **Ann** Langley. | Different Langley. |
| `rittergemunden2003` | *Network competence…*, JBR 56(9), 745–755, DOI `10.1016/S0148-2963(01)00259-4`. A ranked search returns the 2004 *business strategy* paper (JBR 57(5), 548–556) first. | Rank 2 is the paper §4 needs. Recorded in `REF_VERIFY.md`. |
| Zhou et al. 2025 | Two papers, overlapping teams, same year. **APJHR** 63(2): Zhou, Lei, **Liu**, Huang & Hou — the algorithmic-competency **scale**. **HRM** 64(6): Zhou, Lei, **Cooke**, Huang & Zhang — an attribution study. Dissertation `zhou2025.md` is the HRM paper. Lima `zhou2025apjhr.md` is the scale. | Never write "Zhou et al. 2025" bare. The scale is §9's rival; the attribution paper belongs in §1. |
| Cotter & Reisdorf (2020) | Title is "A New **Dimension** of (Digital) Inequality," not "Horizon." | Canonical Lima slug: `cotter2020`. |
| Dogruel, Masur & Joeckel | **2022** by issue year (`dogruel2022`). | Do not cite as 2021. |
| Cotter, practical knowledge | **2024** (`cotter2024`). | Not 2023. |
| Long & Magerko (2020) | 17 competencies and 15 design considerations. | Canonical slug `longmagerko2020`; fuller review edition at `long2020`. |
| Toner-Rodgers (2024) | **Withdrawn.** MIT stated it had no confidence in the data. Still circulates in secondary summaries of AI-heterogeneity evidence. | Do not cite. |
| `org_frontier/.../christin2020ethnographer` | Wrong PDF attached — it is Abebe et al., FAT\* '20. | Lab-level trap, not a Lima card. Do not inherit the file. |

Adler & Heckscher (2006) is on Paper 2's reference list and was not found in the body on the
18 August manuscript review. It is an orphan, not a wrong paper; see [`COVERAGE.md`](COVERAGE.md).
