## accountability-contestation (56)

### Full text

- **[Alfrink, K., Keller, I., Kortuem, G., & Doorn, N. (2023). Contestable AI by design: Towards a framework. *Minds and Machines*, 33(4), 613–639.](cards/alfrink2023.md)**

  This is the definitional spine under §5's decisive move.
- **[Bovens, M. (2010). Two concepts of accountability: Accountability as a virtue and as a mechanism. *West European Politics*, 33(5), 946–967.](cards/bovens2010.md)**  
  This is the paper's strongest external warrant for the §5 hinge, and the paper does not yet use it.
- **[Colquitt, J. A. (2001). On the dimensionality of organizational justice: A construct validation of a measure. *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 86(3), 386–400.](cards/colquitt2001dim.md)**

- **[Lyons, H., Velloso, E., & Miller, T. (2021). Conceptualising contestability: Perspectives on contesting algorithmic decisions. *Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction*, 5(CSCW1), Article 106, 1–25.](cards/lyons2021.md)**

- **[Okhuysen, G. A., & Bechky, B. A. (2009). Coordination in organizations: An integrative perspective. *Academy of Management Annals*, 3(1), 463–502.](cards/okhuysen2009.md)**
