Status, one word:

- **installed** — one of the eleven; on the dissertation shelf as of 2026-08-19
- **differs** — review edition in `cards/` is not the `library/` / dissertation body
- **identical** — `cards/` was copied from `library/` (sweep never wrote it)
- **lima** — sweep card only; dissertation already had a card
- **diss-only** — no Lima card; use the dissertation shelf
- **unread** — nothing past abstract or metadata; a full read could change a section
- **trap** — a known retrieval or naming hazard; see [`TRAPS.md`](TRAPS.md)
- **orphan** — on the reference list; in-text cite not found in the 18 August review
