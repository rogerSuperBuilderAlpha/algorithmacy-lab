## What it argues

The same team that coined algorithmic competencies here works out the platform side of the relation. *Platformic management* names the way an online labour platform governs work without employing anyone: it structures the market, sets the terms of visibility and matching, and delegates the residual coordination to the parties. The analytic device is **boundary resources** — the APIs, profiles, ratings, escrow, messaging and search facilities the platform exposes to workers and clients. Workers do not simply submit to platformic management; they appropriate boundary resources, and their autonomy is a function of how much room those resources leave.

The paper's contribution to this cluster is the account of where competence has purchase. Autonomy on Upwork is neither given nor taken but negotiated through what the platform makes available, which means a worker's capacity to read and work the boundary resources translates directly into working conditions. That is the structural claim underneath the competence framing in the 2019 chapter and the 2020 *Work, Employment and Society* article, and it is why the same programme produced all three.

## Relation to the argument

**Resource, and a useful corrective.** This is the paper that keeps the competence lineage from collapsing into individual heroics: whatever workers can do depends on what the platform exposes. That is friendly to §6's division — some withheld conditions a worker can supply privately, one she cannot — and it supplies an independent vocabulary for the same asymmetry. The manuscript can cite it for the proposition that the coordinated party's room to manoeuvre is set by the coordinator's design choices, which is exactly what makes a rise in average algorithmacy not a good outcome on its own (§8).

It is not a rival. Platformic management is a property of the arrangement, not a competency of the worker, and boundary resources are the coordinator's instruments. If anything the paper strengthens the manuscript's §4 asymmetry point: the coordinator's capacity is theorised here in detail while the coordinated party's is left as appropriation.
