## What it argues

Forty Facebook users, interviewed before, during and after a probe that revealed which of their friends' stories the feed had suppressed. Ten distinct folk theories surfaced. Four arose from ordinary use — Personal Engagement (interaction raises a friend's rank; held by 80% of aware users), Global Popularity, Format, and Narcissus (you see more from friends like you) — and the probe generated more, including a Control Panel theory and an Eye of Providence theory. The load-bearing result is behavioural and selective. Users made plans from their theories and acted on them, but only on the two theories that cast them as an active party with power over the inputs. Theories locating agency in other users or in Facebook itself went unused, because they gave the user no lever. The title names one tactic: like a story to signal the friend, then hide it to keep the feed unchanged. Business-page owners liked their own posts; some users unfollowed popular friends to leave open space for others. Several described the required behaviour as deceiving or prodding the algorithm.

## Relation to the argument

This is the closest thing in the cluster to a demonstration that the two halves of common understanding come apart in practice, which is the claim §7 rests the two-facet split on. Users read the system (inbound) and then engineered inputs to make the system carry what they meant (outbound), and the paper shows the second is conditional on the first in a specific way — theories were acted on only when they granted a lever. That is a stronger and more useful statement than §7's "different practices with different occasions": it says the outbound facet has an enabling condition that the inbound one supplies, so the two are asymmetrically coupled rather than merely distinct. Section 7's driver example (expert at reading the rating, unable to get a complaint recorded) is the reverse case, and having both directions of dissociation would make the split much better evidenced.

The self-liking and like-then-hide tactics are also the earliest documented instances of what §7 calls intent specification — compressing what one means into the signals a system will carry, with the loss deliberately chosen.

## Caution

N=40, one city, one platform, 2013–2014 News Feed, folk theories elicited in a lab rather than observed in the wild, and a probe phase the authors say cannot establish causation. Consumer feed management, not a coordination relation with a counterpart whose wants are at issue — the "counterpart" here is a friend whose posts are being ranked, not a party to a transaction. The paper's own normative tilt is toward seamful design. Cite for the existence, content and behavioural use of folk theories under opacity, not for prevalence.
