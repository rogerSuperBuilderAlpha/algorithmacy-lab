## What it argues

The book-length extension of the 1993 argument into practical advice on composing a qualitative manuscript: how to build the theoretical framing that makes a contribution visible, how to move between field material and claim, how to handle quotations and vignettes so they carry evidential weight, and how to write a method section that earns rather than asserts trust.

## Relation to the argument

The reference to hand a co-author or a committee member who asks how the eventual paper should be structured. For this manuscript specifically, its guidance on quotation is live: the interview instrument's verbatim-marking rule exists so that quotable lines survive collection, and the writing-up literature is where the reason for that rule gets stated.

## Caution

Two editions, different pagination; cite the one actually used. A craft book — it will not settle a design dispute, and in a word-constrained paper it is the first of the writing-up citations to cut.
