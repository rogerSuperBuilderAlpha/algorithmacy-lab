## What it argues

Bowen addresses head-on the awkwardness of researchers who claim grounded theory and also begin with concepts. Sensitizing concepts, he argues, are legitimate in an inductive design provided they function as starting points for inquiry rather than as categories for sorting, and he works this through a study of community-based antipoverty projects in Jamaica, showing how the concepts guided the constant-comparative coding and where the data forced them to move. He ties the practice explicitly to trustworthiness techniques and to the coding process.

## Relation to the argument

This is the applied citation that Blumer needs. It demonstrates, in a published study, the exact move the protocol commits to — concepts declared in advance, coding that could and did revise them — and it does so in an open-access venue a reviewer can check in a minute.
