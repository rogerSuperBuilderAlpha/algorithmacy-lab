## What it argues

Savolainen analyses user discussions of shadow banning — a controversial and possibly non-existent form of moderation — as **algorithmic folklore**: beliefs and narratives about moderation systems passed on informally and often in tension with official accounts. The methodological claim is that discursive mobilisations of the term give access to the experiential dimension of algorithmic governance rather than to its actual practices. The substantive finding is bleak and precise: the constitutive logics of these platforms run counter to the values of good governance — clarity and stability of norms, consistency of enforcement — and users are left struggling, desperately, to form expectations about system operation and to police themselves according to perceived rules, in a state of dependency and frustration.

## Caution

Discourse analysis of user discussions; the paper is explicit that it does not establish whether shadow banning occurs. One phenomenon, consumer platforms. "Folklore" is the analytic frame and carries its own commitments. Read at abstract depth.
