## What it argues

Weiss writes the practical book on interview studies: choosing respondents, building the research partnership that makes candour possible, constructing a guide that produces concrete accounts rather than generalisations, marking and sorting excerpts, and writing up. His central technique is pressing for the specific instance — what happened, in what order, and what the respondent did — on the grounds that generalisations conceal and incidents reveal.

## Relation to the argument

The clearest published statement of the discipline the collection instrument tries to automate: "it was confusing" is a characterization, "the third build, when the review came back about integration" is an incident, and the agent is instructed not to leave a block until it has one. Weiss is the citation that makes that rule a method rather than a preference, and he is also the source for treating the interview as a partnership the interviewer has to build — which is exactly the thing an LLM interviewer may or may not be able to do.

## Caution

The 1993/1994 date discrepancy is real; pick one and use it consistently. Weiss assumes a human interviewer throughout and his account of the research partnership is the strongest reason to doubt that an agent can substitute for one — cite him honestly, including where he cuts against the instrument.
