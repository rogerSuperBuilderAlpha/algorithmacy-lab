## What it argues

Herr and Anderson's durable contribution is the positionality continuum: insider studying self, insider in collaboration with other insiders, insider collaborating with outsiders, reciprocal insider–outsider teams, outsider collaborating with insiders, and outsider studying insiders. Each position implies different validity threats and different defences, and the book insists that a dissertation state which position it occupies before it argues for its rigour.

## Relation to the argument

The cleanest way to say, in one sentence and with a citation, exactly where this study sits: an insider collaborating with a Paper-2-blind outsider (the second coder) and a third party outside the programme (consent holder, ledger maintainer). Naming the position on a published continuum is worth more to a reviewer than any amount of assurance, and the mixed team the protocol already specifies is the position the literature rates most defensible for an insider dissertation.

## Caution

Written for education dissertations and for action research; the continuum transfers cleanly, the rest does not. Book-length, so a page cite is expected for the continuum itself.
