## What it argues

The field's integrative review, organised through Edwards's contested-terrain lens. Its central contribution is a typology of six algorithmic control mechanisms, the **6 Rs**, sorted into three managerial functions: directing work through *restricting* and *recommending*, evaluating it through *recording* and *rating*, disciplining it through *replacing* and *rewarding*. Algorithmic control differs from earlier technical and bureaucratic control by being more comprehensive, instantaneous, interactive and **opaque**. The review then catalogues worker responses on both sides — resistance through exit, voice, gaming and manipulating metrics, and building **algorithmic-decoding solidarity**; and consent.

## Relation to the argument

The 6 Rs are the closest thing the organisational literature has to §7's table, and comparing the two shows what §7's admission rule buys. Kellogg, Valentine and Christin partition by *what the system does to the worker*; §7 partitions by *what the worker can do about a withheld condition*. The two are duals, and the paper could say so: a facet-by-mechanism crosswalk would show that rating and recording generate the interpretation problem, restricting and recommending generate the specification problem, and the continuous updating that makes all six adaptive generates the tracking problem. That is a stronger demonstration that the derivation has teeth than the machine-orchestration cut alone.

**Algorithmic-decoding solidarity** is also the review's name for the collective version of asymmetric interpretation, and it is the term the manuscript should use when it concedes, as `bishop2019` forces, that some of the work is irreducibly social.

## Caution

A review rather than a study; the 6 Rs are a synthesis of a young literature, not a measurement, and the response catalogue is assembled from other people's cases. The frame theorises control *over workers a firm employs or directs*, which sits at the edge of the arrangement this paper is about. The contested-terrain lens is normatively loaded and the manuscript takes the mechanism content without the resistance narrative.
