## What it argues

Nutbeam operationalizes the WHO definition of health literacy into a three-level hierarchy that has become the field's standard schema. *Functional* health literacy is basic reading and numeracy sufficient to understand health information and instructions. *Communicative* (also rendered "interactive") health literacy is the more advanced tier: cognitive and social skills used together to extract meaning from health information, discuss it with others, and apply it to changing circumstances — a literacy exercised through interaction with another person, not solitary comprehension. *Critical* health literacy is the top tier: the capacity to analyze information critically and use it to exert control over life events and the social determinants of health. The three levels are cumulative, and each successive level carries greater personal autonomy.
