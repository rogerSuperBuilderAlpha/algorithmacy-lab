## What it argues

The finding that made the folk-theory literature possible: 62.5% of 40 Facebook users did not know the News Feed was curated at all. Shown the difference between the algorithmic feed and an unfiltered one through a purpose-built tool, FeedVis, participants reacted first with surprise and anger, and were most upset when close friends and family turned out to be absent. The paper's sharpest observation for this cluster is misattribution: users blamed missing stories on their friends' choices not to post rather than on the algorithm's choice not to show. Followed up two to six months later, satisfaction had largely returned to where it started, but awareness had produced more active engagement and a stronger felt sense of control.

## Relation to the argument

The misattribution finding is the cleanest empirical statement of what §7 calls the inbound half of withheld common understanding, and it is more damaging to the naive picture than §7 currently allows. When the mediator is invisible, a party does not merely infer the counterpart's wants imperfectly — she attributes the mediator's determination *to the counterpart*. The driver who reads a low rating as this rider's judgement, when it was the aggregation rule's, is making the same error at higher stakes. Asymmetric interpretation therefore has a precondition the facet statement leaves implicit: the worker must first know there is an intermediary to interpret. Section 7 assumes the triad is visible to the worker; Eslami et al. show that on a consumer platform it usually was not.

The follow-up result is a second gift and a second complication. Awareness raised engagement and felt control without raising satisfaction, which is a hint that the competency and the outcome are separable — good news for §11's protocol, which needs to measure the two independently.

## Caution

Forty users, one city, one platform, 2013–2014 News Feed, and a probe that manufactures the awareness it then measures; the authors are explicit that the design cannot establish that particular seams cause particular reasoning. Consumer social media with nothing economic riding on it. Read at abstract depth here; the companion `eslami2016` was read in full and carries the folk-theory taxonomy, so cite this one specifically for the unawareness rate, the misattribution, and the longitudinal follow-up.
