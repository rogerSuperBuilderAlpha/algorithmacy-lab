## What it argues

Interviews with 77 high-performing eBay business sellers in France and Belgium, all above 95% positive ratings. Customer evaluations run through the platform's algorithm generate power asymmetries that hierarchical-power theory cannot describe: managerial observation is replaced by algorithmic monitoring, and the assumptions about managers, subordinates and the exercise of agency do not hold. The setting is explicitly **triadic** — platform operator, buyers, sellers — generating multiple accountabilities on two connected levels. Three structural features follow: a one-way evaluation right (buyers rate sellers who cannot rate back), a visibility gap (seller records public, buyer records hidden), and blocked exit, because reputation cannot be rebuilt elsewhere. Sellers describe the apparatus as a sword of Damocles.

## Relation to the argument

The empirical statement of §9's triad, from the field's flagship journal, in a case with no employment relation anywhere in it, and §9 already cites it there. What the card adds is the **visibility gap** as the sharpest available evidence for §5's directional partition. Seller records are public and buyer records hidden, which is common understanding withheld asymmetrically in exactly the sense §5 means — not merely absent between two parties but engineered to run one way. The one-way evaluation right is the same structure for accountability: the seller answers for every interaction through a record she does not author, and nothing answers to her. Section 5 states that as a claim; Curchod et al. document it as a platform design.

The population is also useful to §10 for an uncomfortable reason. All 77 sellers are high performers above 95% positive. That is a ceiling-restricted sample, and it means the study cannot speak to variance in competency — the workers who read the system badly are not in it. Section 11's cohort design should not repeat the selection.

## Caution

One platform, one product category of it, two countries; the authors' contribution is a theory of power and agency, not a coordination form, and the fourth-form placement is the dissertation's. eBay business sellers are established micro-firms rather than gig workers. The sample's restriction to high performers is a real limit on any use for distributional claims.
