## What it argues

Blumer's target is the definitive concept — the concept that fixes its referent by a set of attributes and then instructs the researcher to go find instances. Social life, he argues, does not present itself in classes that stable, and so definitive concepts either falsify what they touch or float free of it. His alternative is the sensitizing concept, which gives the researcher a general sense of reference and a direction in which to look at empirical instances, without prescribing what will be found there. (The formulation above is a paraphrase: the article's exact wording was not verified against the text.) The concept directs where to look; it does not say what will be seen.
