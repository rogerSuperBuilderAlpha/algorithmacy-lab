## What it argues

Sander argues that data literacy conceived as the skills to *use* data is inadequate to a world of big-data systems, and proposes **critical big data literacy** — an extended literacy placing awareness of big-data systems and critical reflection on them at the centre rather than at the periphery. The empirical component surveys online resources that aim to foster such literacy and then tests citizens' responses to them in a qualitative multi-method study with three measurement points. Interactive, accessible tools with appealing visualisations and constructive advice worked best; participants also generated suggestions for future tools.

## Relation to the argument

The clearest instance of the "critical" family of literacy constructs, and a neighbour rather than a rival on both of §8's tests. Sander's literacy is deliberately curricular — the object of study is *resources that teach it*, and the finding is which teaching materials work. A capacity whose research programme is an evaluation of instructional tools cannot be the capacity §10 says develops through participation because the rule is undisclosed. It also sits at firm or institutional level as often as individual: the tools are built and deployed by civil-society organisations, which is closer to §8's capability than to a competency held by an individual in a role.

On the triad, critical big data literacy is a relation between a citizen and a data-processing apparatus, with the apparatus's operators as the implicit second party. No counterpart, and no determination binding two people. The critical turn moves the construct toward *evaluating* the system's legitimacy rather than *coordinating through* it, which is a different axis altogether — §9's five cells sort by what the algorithm is made to be, and critical literacy makes it an object of political judgement, arguably a sixth cell the manuscript could name.

Use this card to prevent an easy objection. A reviewer who thinks the manuscript has ignored critical data studies can be answered with a construct that names itself critical, defines itself explicitly, and still has two positions.
