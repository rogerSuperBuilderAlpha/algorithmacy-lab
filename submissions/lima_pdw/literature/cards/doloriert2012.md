## What it argues

Doloriert and Sambrook organise a scattered literature into a form usable by organizational scholars, tracing autoethnography's disciplinary development and the criticism it has attracted, mapping how its epistemological positions have shifted, and naming three emergent possibilities for organizational work — telling stories otherwise silenced, examining the mundane and distorted in academic life, and collaborative or co-produced autoethnography with others in the setting.
