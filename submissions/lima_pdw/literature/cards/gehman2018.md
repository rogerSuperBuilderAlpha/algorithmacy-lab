## What it argues

A curated conversation in which the principals of three approaches — Eisenhardt's multiple-case method, the Gioia methodology, and Langley's process strategies — set out what each is for, where each fails, and how a researcher should choose. The organizing claim is theory–method fit: the approach follows from the kind of theory the study is trying to build, not from taste or training.

## Relation to the argument

The single most efficient citation for the paper's central methodological decision. It lets one sentence say that the design follows from the question — process questions about how a working theory of a gate is built call for Langley's strategies, not for a psychometric panel — and it does so in the voices of the people who own the competing methods. Reviewers who would contest any one approach have a harder time contesting all three at once.
