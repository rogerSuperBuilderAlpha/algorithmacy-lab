## What it argues

Media synchronicity theory replaces the richness hierarchy. Dennis, Fuller and Valacich argue that media should not be ranked at all; they should be described by five capabilities — transmission velocity, parallelism, symbol sets, rehearsability, reprocessability — and matched to the *communication process* rather than to the task. Two processes matter: **conveyance**, transmitting new information, which is served by low-synchronicity media that allow rehearsal and reprocessing; and **convergence**, reaching shared meaning about information already held, which is served by high synchronicity. Most tasks require both, in alternation, so no medium is best and the appropriability question is always about the process at hand.

Their earlier HICSS statement (1999, doi:10.1109/hicss.1999.772701) is the first version.

## Relation to the argument

Convergence is the concept §5's "common understanding, withheld" needs. Dennis et al. define it as the process of reaching *shared* meaning — mutual, checkable, jointly held — and show it requires high-synchronicity exchange with rapid feedback between the parties. The arrangement in §7 supports conveyance in one direction (the worker submits; the outcome arrives) and supports convergence in neither, because convergence requires a partner who can respond and the partner is unmet. **Saying "the form withholds convergence" is more precise than "withholds common understanding" and connects the paper to a theory that specifies the mechanism.**

Rehearsability and reprocessability are also directly useful to **intent specification**: they name the capabilities a worker exploits when packaging a submission for a gate, and they are the capabilities the paper's mediator does supply. That the arrangement is generous with rehearsability and null on convergence is a precise characterisation of the asymmetry, in an established vocabulary, aimed at an information-systems audience the Lima venue will contain.

## Caution

A theory paper with limited direct testing; its empirical record is mixed and the five capabilities have proven hard to measure independently. It theorises media as *chosen* by communicators, which again does not hold in the paper's arrangement. Do not import the appropriability propositions; take conveyance/convergence and the capability vocabulary.
