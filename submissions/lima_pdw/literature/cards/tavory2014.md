## What it argues

The book-length development of the 2012 argument. Tavory and Timmermans work out what a research design looks like when abduction rather than induction is the engine: theoretical preparedness before the field rather than a blank slate, systematic defamiliarization of what the researcher already finds obvious, and "alternative casing" — deliberately asking what else this case could be a case of. They also treat the researcher's own social and intellectual position as a resource that generates surprises rather than a bias to be scrubbed out.

## Relation to the argument

Cite the book rather than the article when the paper needs the *design* claim — that committing a coding scheme and a refutation condition in advance is compatible with, and in fact required by, abductive theorizing.
