## What it argues

Eisenhardt and Graebner revisit the 1989 roadmap from the editors' side, addressing the objections case researchers actually meet: why theoretical sampling is not a convenience sample, why a single case can be justified, how to present evidence within a page limit, and why replication logic rather than statistical logic governs the argument. Their treatment of the single-case justification is the part most often cited.
