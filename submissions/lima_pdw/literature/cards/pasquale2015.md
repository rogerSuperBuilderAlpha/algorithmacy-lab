## What it argues

Pasquale's book-length case is that the firms which rank, score and route modern economic life — search, reputation, finance — operate as black boxes by design and by law, and that the asymmetry runs one way. They know an enormous amount about the people and businesses they score; the scored know almost nothing about the scoring. Trade secrecy law, contract, and complexity each contribute, and the result is a decision apparatus with real consequences and no obligation to explain itself. Pasquale's remedy is institutional rather than individual: qualified transparency, auditability, regulatory access to models, and legal duties that give the scored a standing they currently lack.

## Relation to the argument

Two services, one of them a warning. Pasquale supplies the general statement of the asymmetry §5 partitions, and he supplies it in a register the paper needs — the account is about *standing*, not about cognition. His remedies are forums: regulators with access, auditors with subpoena power, statutory rights to explanation. That is exactly the institutional route §6 names, and it is why §6 can say accountability is supplied institutionally or not at all. Read against §7, Pasquale marks the boundary of the competency claim from the other side. He never proposes that the scored party get better at reading the box, because in his frame that would be a category error: the problem is a missing legal relation, not a missing skill. A paper that derives a private competency from the same withholding has to say why it is not proposing the training-workers-to-absorb-a-withheld-standing move Pasquale would reject, and §6's division is that answer.

## Caution

A trade and legal-scholarly monograph, argued at the level of the economic form; no worker population is studied and no coping practice is documented, so it cannot evidence any facet in §7. Its normative charge is heavy and explicit, and this paper's use should stay descriptive. The book predates the platform-labour literature it is often cited alongside, and its cases are finance, search and reputation scoring rather than coordination between two enrolled parties. Not read in full; cite for the black-box thesis and the institutional-remedy framing, not for any specific claim about a named firm.
