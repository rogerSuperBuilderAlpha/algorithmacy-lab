## What it argues

Berger works through three of her own studies — one where she shared the participants' experience, one where she did not, and one where her position shifted mid-project — to show concretely how the researcher's position shapes access, the questions that get asked, the answers participants give, and the interpretation that follows. Her conclusion is that reflexivity is a practice with specific techniques, not a paragraph of disclosure.

## Relation to the argument

The citation for the sentence §10 of the protocol already writes — "the investigator is the instrument" — with the addition that it names what to *do* about it. Her point that position shapes what participants are willing to say is the mechanism behind the risk the protocol concedes and cannot design away: some participants will say what they think their instructor wants to hear.
