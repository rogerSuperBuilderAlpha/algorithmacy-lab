## What it argues

The introduction to AMJ's special research forum on process studies, in which the four authors distinguish process research that treats change as a variable from process research that treats organizing as constituted by activity and flow, and set an agenda for the latter: temporality taken seriously, mechanisms shown rather than inferred, and analysis that keeps events in their sequence.
