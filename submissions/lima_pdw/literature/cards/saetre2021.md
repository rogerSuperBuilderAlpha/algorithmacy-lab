## What it argues

Sætre and Van de Ven take abduction apart into distinct generative processes rather than treating it as a single inferential leap, and they insist that anomalies — findings that the standing theory did not predict — are the raw material of theory generation. Their contribution is to make abduction teachable and reportable: which anomaly, which candidate explanation, why that one survived.

## Relation to the argument

The paper's most useful function here is the anomaly. Paper 2's §4 refutation conditions define in advance what would count as an anomaly for the accountability null, and Sætre and Van de Ven give the AMR-level vocabulary for reporting one. Where Timmermans and Tavory warrant the stance, this warrants the write-up of a surprise if one arrives.
