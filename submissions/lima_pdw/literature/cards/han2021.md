## What it argues

Han and colleagues address the practical problem that interview chatbots fail in patterned ways, and build tooling that profiles a chatbot's conversational behaviour automatically and generates design suggestions for fixing it. The premise is that interview-agent quality is an engineering property that can be measured and iterated rather than a matter of prompt craft.
