## What it argues

A multi-sited ethnography comparing web journalism, where real-time traffic analytics arrived, and criminal justice, where risk-assessment instruments did. The professionals respond the same way in both. First, **decoupling**: managers describe data-driven organisations while the professionals below largely ignore the tools — Chartbeat installed on every computer at a pseudonymous magazine whose writers said they did not look at the numbers; risk scores filed unannotated at the back of hundred-page court files whose first fifty pages carried handwritten notes. Second, a repertoire of **buffering strategies**: *foot-dragging* (refusing to log in, bypassing scores), *gaming* (clickbait headlines and homepage-timing negotiation; in courts, case selection to protect incarceration numbers and "criteria tinkering," probation officers manipulating inputs to reach the risk score they judged adequate), and *open critique*. The two fields hold distinct algorithmic imaginaries, which explains why journalists interpret and legal professionals reject.

## Relation to the argument

**Criteria tinkering** is the outbound facet observed in a population that has what §7's subject lacks — tenure, licensure, a professional community — and it is the cleanest illustration of what that difference does. A probation officer who manipulates the inputs to reach the score she judged adequate is compressing intent into the signals the system accepts, with the crucial addition that she is not afraid of being caught doing it. Section 8's competency-versus-skill argument and §6's institutional route both gain from the comparison: the same practice reads as professional discretion when the practitioner holds standing and as gaming when she does not.

The decoupling finding is also a boundary condition §7 should acknowledge. Christin's professionals could ignore the instrument. The worker §7 is about cannot, because the instrument is the channel through which work is routed and priced, and the difference is structural rather than dispositional.

## Caution

Salaried professionals inside organisations, one step to the side of the enrolled-party case. The finding cuts against a naive algorithms-strip-all-discretion story — in both sites professionals substantially retained discretion by buffering. Cite for the professions apparatus and the buffering repertoire, not as evidence that the algorithm wins.
