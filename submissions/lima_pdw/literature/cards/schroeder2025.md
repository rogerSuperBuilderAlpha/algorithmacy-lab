## What it argues

Based on interviews with qualitative researchers about how they actually use LLMs, Schroeder and colleagues map the uses, the tensions and the stated intentions. Their central methodological worry is context collapse: an LLM obscures the provenance of what it produces, so a researcher cannot tell which part of an output came from the data and which from the model, and therefore cannot assess either the validity of the output or the model's influence on the analysis.

## Relation to the argument
