## What it argues

Eight authors turn abductive analysis into an explicit, auditable coding procedure. They set out how a team moves between theoretical categories and in-vivo codes across coding cycles, how existing qualitative material can be re-analysed abductively without pretending the analyst is naive, and how the resulting decisions get documented so that a reader can follow them.

## Relation to the argument

This is the missing operational half of the Timmermans and Tavory citation and the closest published match to what §7 of the protocol actually specifies: a committed initial scheme, open first-cycle coding, a second coder, and a documented record of which anticipated codes died. Where a reviewer asks "how do you hold a facet as a sensitizing concept without it becoming a coding frame," this is the citation that answers procedurally rather than by promise.

## Caution
