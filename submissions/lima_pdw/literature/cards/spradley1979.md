## What it argues

Spradley's developmental research sequence turns ethnographic interviewing into a taught procedure: grand-tour and mini-tour questions that get informants narrating actual occasions, descriptive then structural then contrastive questions, and domain, taxonomic and componential analysis of the resulting talk. His governing rule is to use the informant's own terms and to build categories from them rather than importing the analyst's.

## Relation to the argument

The direct ancestor of two rules the collection instrument enforces. "Introduce no vocabulary of your own — use the words the person has already used" is Spradley's principle stated operationally, and "one incident per block, minimum" is the grand-tour question doing its work.
