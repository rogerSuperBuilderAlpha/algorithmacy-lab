## What it argues

Schildt argues against the emerging consensus that generative AI threatens qualitative research. Improving LLM capabilities create both opportunities and threats for professions, and critics of AI in qualitative work overlook the standing lesson of technology studies — that a technology's effects depend on the social practices it is embedded in. Chatbots *can* produce shallow analyses and fabricated quotations; his claim is that this outcome is not inevitable and that the discipline should specify the practices that make the difference rather than issue a blanket prohibition.
