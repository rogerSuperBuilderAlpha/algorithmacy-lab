## What it argues

Mantere and Ketokivi treat scientific reasoning in organization science as a matter of argument rather than of algorithm, and they show that induction, deduction and abduction each impose different obligations on what the researcher must make explicit. Their target is the ritual invocation of a method label in place of an actual chain of inference.

## Relation to the argument

Useful as a second, less-cited companion to Timmermans and Tavory when a reviewer suspects the abductive label is doing no work. It supports the protocol's most defensible feature — that every inferential commitment (coding scheme, refutation conditions, saturation rule, discard log) is written down before the data arrives, so the argument can be inspected rather than trusted.
