## What it argues

Three authors from three methodological traditions argue that the received division of labour — theory proposes, method disposes — misdescribes how organizational research actually works. Theory and method constitute each other: what counts as an observation depends on the theory that made it observable, and a method choice is already a theoretical commitment about what kind of thing the object is. They press scholars to stop treating method sections as neutral technical appendices and to argue the fit.

## Relation to the argument

This is the citation that licenses the paper's central methodological move — swapping a psychometric panel for a qualitative design because the questions changed.
