## What it argues

Barley observed the introduction of CT scanners into two radiology departments in the same US city over a year. The technology was identical. The occupational groups were the same — radiologists and technologists. The outcomes were not: in one department the scanner's arrival produced a decentralised, more egalitarian order in which technologists gained discretion; in the other it produced a centralised order in which radiologists retained control. Barley's theoretical move is to treat technology as an **occasion for structuring** rather than a cause of structure — the same object triggers a script-level renegotiation whose outcome depends on the local sequence of interactions, not on the machine.

## Relation to the argument

The organisational-theory ancestor of §1's premise, and the one a *Organization Studies* / *Organization Theory* audience will expect the paper to know. Identical technology, matched sites, divergent outcomes, and the divergence explained by neither the technology nor the participants' prior skill. This is what "same system, different outcomes, and the technology does not explain it" looks like when it is properly demonstrated: two cases, held constant by design, traced longitudinally.

Two uses follow. First, it lets §1 assert the general phenomenon on organisation-theoretic ground rather than borrowing thin support from the AI-productivity economics, where the phenomenon keeps turning out to be explained. Second, it is a check on the manuscript's own move: Barley located the residual in *interaction sequences between people*, which is exactly the resource §1's driver does not have — she has no colleague whose account she can check against her own. A structuring account requires a social setting; co-optation withholds it. That contrast is an argument the paper could make and currently does not.

## Caution

Two departments, one observer, 1980s medical imaging; interpretive and longitudinal, with no effect sizes and no quantitative outcome. Barley is not making a claim about individual variation at all — the units that diverge are departments, and the mechanism is collective structuring, not private competency. Presenting it as evidence that individuals differ in outcomes would misread it. Old, and the technology is nothing like an algorithmic dispatcher.
