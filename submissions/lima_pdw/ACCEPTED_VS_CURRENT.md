# What Lima accepted, and what the qualitative swap actually changes

*18 August 2026. Written against [`ABSTRACT.md`](ABSTRACT.md), the locked verbatim package accepted as
LIMA2026-002 on 2026-08-13, and `dissertation/current/paper2/PAPER.md`.*

The author's framing: **the research arm moves from quantitative to qualitative, and the rest of the
accepted package stands.** Measured against the accepted text, that framing holds — and it makes the
disclosure owed to the conveners smaller than the manuscript's own four-delta note implies. One item
is larger than the method swap, and it is not the method.

## What the conveners accepted

| Element | As accepted |
|---|---|
| **Title** | *Algorithmacy: A communication competency construct for triadic mediated coordination, with empirical evidence from a Caribbean AI engineering cohort* |
| **Research question** | "Why do equivalently positioned participants achieve divergent coordination outcomes when working through identical algorithmic systems?" |
| **Construct** | Algorithmacy — "the competency required to coordinate effectively with a human counterpart through an opaque, adaptive intermediary" |
| **Components** | Five: asymmetric interpretation, intent specification, machine orchestration, implicit acquisition, stratified fluency |
| **Theoretical move** | The triad breaks the dyadic inheritance in CMC, HMC, AI-MC, algorithm sensemaking, AI literacy |
| **Site** | GauntleTT, Trinidad and Tobago — "constitutive of the contribution, not merely convenient for it" |
| **Method** | The **standard scale-development sequence** (Hinkin, 1998; Lambert & Newman, 2023; MacKenzie et al., 2011). Paper 3 = three-wave panel, weeks 1/4/8, factor structure, measurement invariance, discriminant and predictive validity against program operations data |
| **Contribution** | "a validated measure of algorithmacy" |

## What the qualitative swap changes, and what it does not

**Unchanged, and this is most of the package.** The research question survives verbatim — a
qualitative study answers "why do equivalently positioned participants diverge" at least as naturally
as a panel does. The construct definition survives. The triadic framing and the dyadic-inheritance
critique survive untouched; they are theory, not method. Trinidad as constitutive survives, and the
swap arguably strengthens it.

**Changed by the swap, and cleanly.**

1. **The method section and its three anchor citations.** Hinkin, Lambert & Newman, and MacKenzie et
   al. are scale-development sources. They come out; qualitative-method warrants go in. This narrows
   the research job considerably — see below.
2. **The contribution claim.** "A validated measure" becomes something a qualitative study can
   deliver: an account of how the competency is acquired and where it fails, with the measure as
   downstream work rather than the deliverable.
3. **Predictive validity against operations data** — weekly deliverable scores, peer evaluations,
   placements — has no qualitative counterpart and simply goes.

**The protocol already exists.** `dissertation/current/paper3/design/S1_PROTOCOL.md` is a complete
qualitative protocol: two interview waves, non-participant observation of review sessions, a dated
rule-change ledger, abductive analysis with the facets as sensitizing concepts rather than a coding
frame, a blind second coder, a discard log, and — most valuable for a roundtable — **pre-committed
refutation conditions** in §4 for the accountability null. The swap is not a design that must be
invented. It is a design that must be connected to the accepted abstract and to a site.

## The item that is larger than the method

**The dissertation's architecture no longer matches the one the conveners were shown**, and this is a
bigger delta than quant-to-qual.

| | As accepted | As it now stands |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | Integrative review establishing algorithmacy as distinct; dyadic inheritance; domain, dimensionality, nomological network | "Standing and revocability: what five coordination literatures hold" — a coordination-forms review, ~41,800 words across two parts |
| Paper 2 | Item generation and content validation via SME review and pilot | "The Competency a Form Demands" — deriving the construct from what the co-optation form withholds |
| Paper 3 | Three-wave panel at GauntleTT | Qualitative study, currently written for Hult |

Two reviewers read the accepted abstract. A manuscript arriving from a differently-shaped dissertation
needs one paragraph explaining the shape, not three papers of reconstruction. That paragraph is
cheaper than it looks, and the first-page note is where it goes.

**Three smaller deltas, all already disclosed in the manuscript's first-page note:** five components
became three facets plus two properties; the IRB sentence was ahead of itself and was corrected; no
data is in hand.

## What this narrows

**The research job gets much smaller and much more tractable.** My earlier plan scoped a narrative-
genre literature — Van Maanen, Anteby, at-home ethnography — on the theory that the paper was becoming
an insider story. Under the author's framing it is not becoming that. It is a construct paper whose
empirical arm is qualitative, which needs a **qualitative-method warrant**, not a narrative-genre
apprenticeship.

And the repo already holds it. `org_frontier/research/qualitative/` carries a ten-topic literature
watch with `deep_research_report.md` and per-topic bibliographies covering Gioia and inductive theory
building, Lincoln–Guba trustworthiness, the intercoder-reliability debates, and — directly on point —
**methods for studying opaque algorithmic systems** (Christin on refraction, comparison,
triangulation). That transfers to this paper as-is.

What is genuinely missing is small: a citable anchor for abductive analysis, one for the
sensitizing-concepts move, and one for qualitative work where the investigator is embedded in the
site. Three to five references, not a new literature.

**Situated learning stays on the list.** §10's claim that the competency develops through
participation and not instruction is Lave and Wenger's thesis, uncited, and the accepted abstract
makes the same claim in its own third paragraph — "develops through participation in intensive
AI-mediated coordination rather than through standard tool instruction." Under a qualitative arm that
studies acquisition directly, the gap gets more conspicuous, not less.

## The site question, which the swap reopens in the author's favour

The accepted abstract names GauntleTT and calls Trinidad constitutive. The manuscript's delta 4 moved
the study to Hult because that is where the approved *survey* protocol pointed. A qualitative arm does
not inherit that constraint automatically: `S1_PROTOCOL.md` targets Hult by choice, not by necessity,
and its IRB application is unfiled.

Whether the qualitative study runs at Hult, at GauntleTT, or across both is now an open design
decision rather than a settled one — and running it where the accepted abstract said it would run is
the option that requires the least explaining. It is also the option with no records, no protocol and
no prior approval, so the cost is real. **This is the author's call and it gates the method section.**

## What the manuscript needs, in order

1. **Rewrite §11** as the qualitative arm proper, connected to the accepted abstract's promise rather
   than presented as a study the paper merely licenses. The pre-committed refutation conditions in
   `S1_PROTOCOL.md` §4 belong in the manuscript; they are the strongest thing the design has.
2. **Correct the gate description.** §11's "the gate's votes are private" is false at the Hult site —
   reviews and upvotes are public, the **tally** is withheld. At Cursor Boston an LLM screens and the
   cohort votes, with each participant seeing only their own score. All three arrangements withhold at
   a different layer, which is a better fact than the one the paper currently asserts.
3. **Fix the novelty claim.** Three named rivals now: Zhou, Lei, Liu, Huang & Hou (2025); **Jarrahi &
   Sutherland (2019), who coined "algorithmic competencies"**; and Dogruel et al. (2022). The last two
   are cited in the author's own IGI chapter at `chapter.md:95`.
4. **Swap the method citations** and add the three-to-five qualitative anchors.
5. **One paragraph in the first-page note** on the dissertation's shape.
