# Outline — superseded by the manuscript

**Do not draft from this file.** The Lima manuscript exists and is the author's:
`dissertation/current/paper2/PAPER.md`, "The Competency a Form Demands: Algorithmacy and the
Co-optation Column" — roughly 9,400 words across twelve sections. The six-section outline this file
used to carry was written from the accepted abstract, before the manuscript was found, and it
described a paper the author had already decided not to write.

The spine is a seventh question added to a published anatomy of coordination forms — *what competency
does a form demand of the party it coordinates?* — asked of all four columns, with algorithmacy
derived from what co-optation withholds. The result the abstract calls the main one is the hole: of
three integrating conditions, two can be supplied privately by a worker and one cannot, which bounds
what naming a competency can accomplish.
