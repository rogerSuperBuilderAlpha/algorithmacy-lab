## 2. Paper 1 of This Dissertation — ~520 words + one table

**Job:** establish the open cell and hand it to §3. Nothing else. §3 and §4 only ever use the
taking-a-place clause from each form, so that is what the prose keeps.

**The table does the comparative work** (model: Cameron 2024, Table 1 — `models/cameron2024.md`).
Five forms × Paper 1's six questions, with the open cell visible as a blank rather than narrated.
That absorbs the detail the four form-paragraphs currently carry and makes the argument visual.

**Prose, after the table:**

1. Paper 1's question and its comparative precedent (Powell, Williamson, Bradach & Eccles, Ouchi);
   the six questions; Okhuysen and Bechky (2009) supplying the three integrating conditions.
2. **Own the mapping.** *Repairs review finding 6.* The triad-onto-classics attribution is Paper
   1's move, not Simon's, Weber's, Hayek's, Powell's or Granovetter's. Hayek (1945) says nothing
   about accountability, contract, or reputation, and *The Use of Knowledge in Society* is the one
   paper in the set every OS/OT reviewer has read. Write "Paper 1, drawing on Hayek (1945), read
   that statistic as delivering…" throughout.
3. The four answered forms in one grouped paragraph, each reduced to how a person takes her place:
   accepting a zone, reading a posted term, investing in a relation she chose, taking a seat.
4. Coordinative co-optation (Stark & Pais 2020; Stark & Vanden Broeck 2024). Keep "She is
   enrolled." — it is mid-section and it works. **Verify or drop "prices their work"**: the locked
   introduction's version of the list has match, rank, close, and no Stark card exists in the Lima
   library to confirm the added function.
5. **The open cell, bolted down.** *Repairs 19 August finding 2.* "How a person takes her place" is
   not one of the six graded questions. Fold it into the mechanism cell: Paper 1 defined a form's
   mechanism as the operation that secures it *and the standing of the party that operation
   reaches*, and it is the second half that goes missing. Not "one cell remains open."
6. Close on a forward hook into §3, not on the epigram.

**Cut:** the per-form condition recitals; the resource-dependence sentence; "Paper 1 treated forms.
This paper treats the person who has to find her place in one of them." (the table plus the hook
does that work).

## 3. Extant Constructs — ~1,950 words, seven hearings kept

The section is the paper's strength and its length is earned. The changes are surgical.

### 3.0 Opening — two additions

- **The selection rule, made honest.** *Repairs review finding 5.* The seven are selected by one
  question — what each construct makes of the algorithm — so a hostile reviewer can call the
  finding an artefact of the sieve. Say why the sieve is right: constructs that already include the
  counterpart exist on the other shelf, and relational coordination presupposes exactly what this
  arrangement removes — parties who can see, reach, and answer one another. This closes the
  cheapest relabelling attack available, which is not "algorithmacy is Zhou plus items" but
  "algorithmacy is relational coordination performed under opacity."

### 3.8 Close — rebuilt

*Repairs review finding 2, 9, 10, and 19 August finding 3 and 10 together.*

1. **Delete the recital.** The seven constructs are announced (L68), heard (L70–113), then recited
   again with full citations in a 95-word sentence. The third pass is cadence. Open instead on
   "Seven constructs, seven boundaries, one shape."
2. **The documented negative result.** Replace "we read seven and none has it" with the field's own
   audits: Oeldorf-Hirsch and Neubaum reviewed 50 algorithmic-literacy studies from 96 screened
   across four databases; Gagrčin, Naab and Grub cover 169; Iyamu and colleagues chart 12 against
   Nutbeam's model, whose *communicative* level is a slot for exactly this competence, and report it
   "largely absent from both conceptual frameworks and measurement instruments." The field was
   handed the slot and filled it with system-facing measurement.
3. **Abidin's chiasmus.** Visibility labour is the one construct on the shelf with a real named
   human counterpart, and it excludes the algorithm by its author's own choice. Every construct that
   centres the algorithm has no counterpart; the one with a counterpart has no algorithm.
4. **Scope the contribution claim, and name the prior art.** This is the finding that costs the most
   if left alone. The *position* is not unnoticed: Katsh and Rifkin named the fourth party in 2001;
   Curchod, Patriotta, Cohen and Neysen (2020) — **cited in this draft already, for the least of
   what they show** — named the platform–buyer–seller triad in *ASQ*, held open by a visibility gap;
   Cameron (2024) drew the algorithmic labor triangle in the same journal and is uncited; Healy and
   Pekarek (2024) draw it again in the work-and-employment literature. All theorise power, consent,
   or vulnerability. **None writes a competence for the party the triangle traps.** The claim
   survives scoped to competence constructs and dies unscoped, four times over.
5. Close on a forward hook into §4 — the competence written for a position the field has already
   mapped — not on "the missing piece is a position, not a variable."

**The four scope conditions** the claim needs, from the package's §5, stated or implied: competence
constructs only; the counterpart as a party to the interaction, not the system's makers, peers, or
downstream beneficiaries; through a binding intermediary that reads both parties, not a channel,
tool, delegate, or disclosed fourth party; and a claim about a position, never about a name.

## 4. Algorithmacy — ~1,150 words, subsectioned

The construct section carries Suddaby's four jobs and then a fifth the draft has never had. Give it
subsection headings so a PDW panel can find the part it wants to develop.

### 4.1 Scope conditions

Keep the v1 paragraph — it is the section's best. Add the negative cases already implied and the
edge case (Upwork-style markets: the platform matches and scores, the client can still leave).
**Add the sentence the locked introduction is owed:** these are also the terms Paper 1's open cell
demands. *Cashes the cheque at locked L44 — "it fulfills the requirements Paper 1 defined" — which
§4 currently never returns to.*

### 4.2 Semantic relations, stated as predictions

*Repairs 19 August finding 8.* v1 asserts distinctness by assuming it ("if they already measured
the same thing, the semantic relation would collapse"). Move the real answer up from §3: Zhou's
*Understanding* should correlate moderately with interpreting and their *Leveraging* with specifying
intent; *Embracing* has no counterpart, because an attitude toward the platform's efficiency is not
an operation performed through it. Rahman's reactivity is orthogonal on a different axis — he cuts
by engagement, this construct cuts by direction and time. Register the falsifier: correlations high
enough to collapse the distinction would kill the construct.

### 4.3 The three operations, and why not four

*Repairs 19 August finding 9.* v1 shows a counterpart-including competence needs more than
interpretation. It never shows it needs exactly three. Two additions:

- **The nearest fourth candidate, refused.** Rahman's fifth cage component — *who influences the
  score*, client or platform — is the only fourth operation the paper's own sources offer. Say why
  it does not become one: uncertainty about who moved the score is a failure of interpretation, not
  a separate operation. Then register what would overturn that: field incidents where "who moved
  the score" will not collapse into interpreting.
- **The literacy family marks its own limit.** *New, from the package.* Long and Magerko run the
  literacy-family argument themselves, and their construct still runs person-to-system — so shelf
  membership settles nothing, and the analogy in the locked introduction is doing naming work, not
  analytic work. Say where it breaks: each older member carries two operations, one receptive and
  one expressive, because text and number hold still while a person reads them. Keeping track is
  what a competence acquires when the medium is also a moving party.

### 4.4 Competency, not skill, not capability

Keep the Sandberg treatment — his figures are now confirmed by OCR (six, ten, four; twenty of a
department of about fifty; Volvo Car Corporation), so the flag can close.

**Fix the anchor.** *Repairs 19 August finding 1 — the costliest single sentence in v1.* Spitzberg
and Cupach (1984) are cited for an ability residing in a party, and their relational model was
written to reject exactly that. The repair is a gift, and Spitzberg (2006) discharges it without the
book: competence is judged by both parties, and that is the standard this arrangement cannot meet,
because the counterpart never reaches a position from which she could judge. What the construct
names is what a party supplies when the relational test has nobody to administer it.

### 4.5 Properties: acquired by participation, unevenly held

Keep both, keep the falsifiers. Add the contrast the package supplies: SNAIL's validation criterion
is course efficacy — it measures what a course can install — and algorithmacy predicts no course
could install this.

### 4.6 What the construct buys — **new, and the section the workshop exists to develop**

*Repairs review finding 3.* v1 builds the construct and never says what changes if it is real. A PDW
panel is being asked to develop a contribution the paper has not stated. Write:

- What a coordination researcher can ask that Kellogg, Valentine and Christin's control frame does
  not — control asks what the system does to people; this asks what the person must be able to do,
  and the two questions have different remedies.
- Whether the form's viability depends on how the competency is distributed, and what a form full of
  low-algorithmacy participants selects for.
- **Cash "satisficing."** The locked abstract calls algorithmacy "a new, satisficing construct" and
  the word never recurs — a term of art from Simon left as decoration in the paper's most visible
  sentence. Name the aspiration level: the construct is not the complete account of the person in
  this form, and it clears the open cell because it is the first that includes the counterpart.
- **Wing's four requirements as the principled negation.** Transparency, accountability,
  contestability and consent are what the ODR literature says a fourth party owes; the withholdings
  §4 names are near-exactly their negation. That makes the withholding list principled rather than
  assembled. *Card depth — do not quote Wing without the primary.*
- **Curchod's visibility gap** as the published antecedent of the interpreting operation, in one
  clause.

## 5. Early Empirical Work — ~700 words

Repairs, not growth. This section's job is to be honest at a scale a reviewer will respect.

1. **Fix the site mapping.** *Repairs review finding 7 — the construct's own site is misdescribed in
   the construct's own vocabulary.* Peers are the **counterpart**; the **gate** is the intermediary;
   the rule is adaptive because the gate's *effective criteria* move with the codebase. And argue
   the hard case instead of asserting it: opacity is a property of the person–arrangement pair, and
   what stays unobservable to participants who build these systems is not the code but the vote and
   the criteria behind it. Delete "We argue fit from…, not from employment status" — a sentence
   about arguing standing in for the argument.
2. **Introduce the gate before using it.** "A review gate that votes in private" appears twice in
   §§3–4 before §5 names it. First use gets "a peer gate of the kind §5 describes."
3. **Design tense in the methods paragraph.** *Repairs review finding 8.* v1 claims abductive
   analysis and first-cycle coding in the present tense; the arm's own `interview/METHODS_AUDIT.md`
   records no codebook, coding rule, memo file, or discard log. Abduction is the claim that bites,
   because Timmermans and Tavory give it machinery — inference from surprise — and a design with no
   record of what surprised the analyst cannot show it abduced anything. Write it as design, add
   **Bowen (2006)** for sensitizing concepts, and say plainly that no coding cycle is complete.
4. **Expand the position paragraph.** *Repairs 19 August finding 7.* Role duality plus non-free
   consent is the objection an OS/OT reviewer raises, and it currently gets one clause and no
   citations, while the answering literature is all carded: Brannick and Coghlan (2007) on insider
   research, Anteby (2013) on involvement and distance not being ends of one continuum, Ferguson,
   Yonge and Myrick (2004) on faculty recruiting their own students, Mercer (2007) for conceding the
   residual. Model the specificity on Cameron, who drove for three years.
5. **The sample statement, with the number.** *Repairs review finding 1 and 19 August finding 6.*
   ⚠ **This is where v2 collides with the lock.** The intake bucket holds **two** objects as of 26
   August (the second uploaded 22 August, unread). The locked abstract says "several responses
   obtained to date." §5 must state the N — evasion is what both reviews flagged — so the draft will
   carry the number with a non-printing note at that line. The author decides: unlock and correct
   both together, or field before 10 September and let the sentence become true. **The two sentences
   must move together**; correcting §5 alone puts a contradiction inside a manuscript going to
   reviewers.
6. **Pair the first incident with Zhou.** Zhou's couriers hold an appeal channel and it reaches the
   apparatus; this participant found no channel at all.
7. **Cite Tracy (2010)** beside the Pratt trio, or cut the orphan entry.
8. **Delete "We organize what is visible so far by the interview's own blocks, not by the three
   operations as if they had been found."** Justifying the outline is the house style's named
   defect; state the organization by doing it.
9. **The one thing no reviewer can fabricate.** §5 reports what the participant *did*; the paper's
   own claim is that competence is a *conception* of the work. What did she think the gate was
   doing? If the transcript holds it, that sentence is worth more than any repair above, because it

## 6. What This Paper Cannot Say — ~400 words

Keep the honesty; it is the section reviewers will trust. Three changes:

1. **Add the level-shift concession.** Spitzberg and Cupach hold that measurement at one level

2. **Inoculate against the 2026 rivals.** Anyone searching this literature in October finds Hong et

   about a name, because names in this field are cheap and getting cheaper.
3. **Break the metronome.** §6 runs five of six paragraphs to a negation.

## The prose problem, stated once for whoever writes v2

The register passes every lexical band and fails above the lexicon. Two signatures:

- **The antithesis metronome.** Twenty-one ", not X" / "X rather than Y" constructions in 4,299
  words, and seventeen of the twenty-three paragraphs in §§4–6 land on a negation — three
  consecutive in §5, five of six in §6. Every instance defends itself; the uniformity does not, and
  the house style names this engine as the first default to kill. The cure is variance, not
  deletion: keep the contrast where the wrong alternative is one a reader would actually reach for,
  and let the rest end on the fact. "Bentley IRB issued an exempt determination on 11 May 2026" is a
  better paragraph ending than any verdict about it.
- **Four performed-rigor sentences** (v1 L134, L138, L146, L164) that the lexical count scored as
  zero, because they narrate the paper's care rather than announcing it. All four are deleted or
  absorbed above.

**Not slop, and not to be "fixed":** "A person takes her place" across the five forms; "treats it as
an object of" across the hearings; the L126 triple; the short punctures ("She is enrolled."). Those
are the house rhythm working.
