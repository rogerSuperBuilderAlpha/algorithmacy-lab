# Cut plan — body to 10,000 words (2026-09-01)

**Target:** body (Introduction through Conclusion, excluding abstract, references, appendices) from
**18,079 → ~10,000 words.** Cut of ~8,000. The author's three instructions govern the allocation:
keep the extant constructs; consolidate the development of algorithmacy as a construct; consolidate
the research methods and proposals.

**One constraint the instructions collide with.** The Extant Constructs block alone is 10,149 words.
Keeping it *intact* leaves no room for anything else. The plan below keeps all **eight hearings** and
Table 1, but compresses each hearing to what carries the demarcation. Nothing is dropped as a
hearing; the depth is what goes.
