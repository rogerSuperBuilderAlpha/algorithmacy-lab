## 1. What was accepted, and what that obliges

The conveners accepted an extended abstract that promises a **scale-development contribution with a
validated empirical arm**: a three-wave panel across multiple GauntleTT cohorts testing factor
structure, measurement invariance across waves and cohorts, discriminant validity against algorithm
sensemaking and AI literacy, and predictive validity against program operations data. The acceptance
letter says the conveners weighed "the potential of the proposed paper to develop into a manuscript
suitable for *Organization Studies* or *Organization Theory*" and "the stage of development of the
project."

A PDW manuscript is a **paper under development**, not a finished submission. That gives room on
polish. It does not give room to arrive without the empirical arm the abstract sold.

## 3. Two seams to close before drafting

**Seam 1 — the fourth-mechanism claim. Closed 2026-08-13 by the verification pass.** Stark & Vanden
Broeck's own abstract runs the parallel: *"Whereas actors in hierarchies command, in markets they
contract, and in networks collaborate, on platforms they are co-opted."* The fourth term is
**platforms**, and what they theorize is a *style of management*, not a coordination mechanism; they
never engage Powell. Stating it their way keeps the citation honest, keeps the claim precise in a
venue whose editors published that paper, and reconciles the abstract with the lab's **H5 REFRAME**
(`coordinative_sovereignty/research/research_database.md:23`). Recorded as
[`DEPARTURES.md`](DEPARTURES.md) row 1 — a wording change, not a change of position.

**Seam 1a — Zhou et al. 2025, which the verification pass surfaced as the real exposure.** It
validates the *first* algorithmic competency scale (four dimensions, five samples,
*Asia Pacific Journal of Human Resources*). The abstract waves it off as measuring technical
knowledge; its dimensions say otherwise. An argued discrimination — ideally an empirical one — is now
a requirement of §4, not an option. See [`literature/REFERENCES.md`](literature/REFERENCES.md).

**Seam 3 — the abstract's five components are stale, and Paper 2 has already replaced them.** The
accepted abstract says algorithmacy is "theoretically operationalized across five specific components:
*asymmetric interpretation, intent specification, machine orchestration, implicit acquisition,* and
*stratified fluency*." The dissertation's Paper 2 (`dissertation/current/paper2/PAPER.md`) now says
something different, and says it deliberately:

- **The construct has three facets, not five** (§7, "Algorithmacy: Three Facets, Derived"): asymmetric
  interpretation, intent specification, and **temporal tracking**. Each is derived under one admission
  rule — a facet is what a worker supplies in the absence of a withheld condition. Accountability is
  withheld and *nothing* can be supplied individually, so it yields no facet; Paper 2 keeps the empty
  cell visible.
- **Machine orchestration is explicitly cut** (§7): "Machine orchestration is not a facet, and the cut
  is the rule working... Earlier statements of this construct named it as a fourth component. It
  answers no withheld condition." It answers a *tool affordance*, it is tool-indexed and dated — "not a
  competency of a ride-hail driver in 2019 and will not describe the same behaviour in 2029" — and
  Paper 2 relocates it to the validation design as a behavioural indicator of the three facets composed.
- **Implicit acquisition and stratified fluency are properties, not facets** (§10): "Two claims about
  algorithmacy are properties of the competency rather than facets of it." Each carries its own
  falsifier. Listing them as components alongside facets is a category error the dissertation has
  already corrected.

So the abstract's list conflates three levels — it names two real facets, one cut candidate, and two
properties, while omitting the actual third facet. **The lab instrument is not the thing out of step:**
`../../org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/` measures counterpart inference, signal compression, and rule-change
tracking, which is Paper 2's three facets exactly, and H1a registers a three-factor structure.

**Already done in the manuscript.** `PAPER.md` §7 presents the three facets as derived, shows the
orchestration cut as a worked example of the admission rule, and §10 gives the two properties their own
section with a falsifier each. Delta (3) of the first-page note discloses the change to reviewers. This
seam is closed at source; what remains here is only to keep the lab's own files from repeating the list
of five.

**Seam 2 — which cohort the pre-registration covers. Closed at source, and the lab side needs one
addition.** `PAPER.md`'s first-page note, delta (4): "the approved protocol is the Hult sixteen-week
study, waves 1/4/16 — NOT the GauntleTT eight-week design the abstract's method section described."
[`../../org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/`](../../org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/) is therefore the right and only
registered study, and it matches the manuscript. The residue is small but real: the Zhou discriminant
block added to that instrument on 2026-08-13 sits under protocol 260511078 and **may need a Research
Progress & Review Form before W2 fields** — see that study's amendment log.
