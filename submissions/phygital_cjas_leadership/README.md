## Candidate angle [DRAFTED, for ruling]

**Working title:** Leading Through the Algorithm: Phygital Leadership as Triadic Coordination.

- **Claim.** In a phygital workplace, leadership runs through a third party, the AI tool that schedules, ranks and recommends. Leader and employee coordinate through it. The leadership competence the call asks competency models to add is algorithmacy: the capacity to recognise, interpret, engage and contest that mediation, held by leaders and employees alike.
- **Call topics answered:**
  - "the role of data-driven systems and AI tools [to] co-construct leadership decision-making";
  - rethinking competency models;
  - human–technology interaction in leadership models;
  - workplace equity;
  - the cognitive and emotional labour of leaders.
- **Lab constructs used:**
  - triadic coordination;
  - coordinative sovereignty (`../coordinative_sovereignty/`);
  - algorithmacy (`../algorithmacy_ot/`);
  - the algorithmic-management literature already held for the Spaces project (Kellogg, Rahman, Möhlmann).
- **Article type.** CJAS takes conceptual pieces, reviews and empirical studies, per the call. A conceptual article is the shortest route; a review of phygital and digital leadership is a second option.
