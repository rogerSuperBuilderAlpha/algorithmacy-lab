## 4. Surgical edits (weakest dims only: E, D; light H/F)

Preserve claims, citations, and evidence. No invented sources, numbers, or arguments. Change diction, ownership, scope markers, and rhythm only.

**Edit 1 — E (own the coinage)**  
**Original:**  
> We propose the concept of **algorithmacy**.  
**Rewrite:**  
> I propose the concept of **algorithmacy**.  
**Rationale:** Contested naming claim wears *I*, as Winner does with "I shall offer."

**Edit 2 — E + G (roadmap ownership)**  
**Original:**  
> In what follows, we trace the genealogy, cognitive architecture, and institutional affordances of this sensibility. We begin by examining… We then turn…  
**Rewrite:**  
> In what follows I trace the genealogy, cognitive architecture, and institutional affordances of this sensibility. I begin by examining… I then turn…  
**Rationale:** Functional signposting stays; kill anonymous *we*-theater.

**Edit 3 — E + C (own the literacy cut)**  
**Original:**  
> Yet the corrective suffers from a disabling flaw: it mistakes an engine of coordination for a sheet of inscribed text.  
**Rewrite:**  
> Yet I take the corrective to suffer a disabling flaw: it mistakes an engine of coordination for a sheet of inscribed text.  
**Rationale:** The grant ("obvious wisdom") is already fair; the cut should be owned.

**Edit 4 — E (own the Silicon Valley cut)**  
**Original:**

**Rewrite:**  
> I take this to be historical myopia.  
**Rationale:** Historiographic judgment, not a free-floating verdict.

**Edit 5 — E + D (scope the cognitive implication)**  
**Original:**

**Rewrite:**

**Rationale:** Replace hollow intensifier; mark the claim as owned and scoped.

**Edit 6 — D (scope the transparency failure)**

**Rationale:** Ties the absolute to existing Bo et al. evidence; no new data.
