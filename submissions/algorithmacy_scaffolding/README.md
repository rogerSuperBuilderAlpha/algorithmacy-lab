# Algorithmacy Scaffolding (course paper)

**Status:** research phase — building the bibliography before drafting prose.

**Topic:** a lit review comparing novice/expert cognitive architectures across literacy, oracy,
and numeracy, then introducing algorithmacy — the sensibility to navigate David Stark's "platforms
coopt" coordination mode — as an analogous novice/expert shift, with a brief historical section
(Reich's New Property, the gold-standard-to-floating-FX shift, common-law litigation) arguing the
shift is not new but is newly universal. Closes with HCI/interaction-design scaffolding
considerations for moving novices toward algorithmic literacy.

**Relation to other arms:** distinct from `../lima_pdw/` (OS/OT organizational-theory framing of
algorithmacy) and `../coordinative_sovereignty/` (IGI chapter on coordinative sovereignty — off
limits, worked in a separate session). This paper reuses established vocabulary (algorithmacy,
coordinative cooptation) but is a new piece with its own cognitive-science spine and its own
bibliography below, independent of the other arms' libraries even where sources overlap.

**Not yet decided:** which of the three candidate HCI case studies (prompt-engineering interfaces,
algorithmic feed controls, algorithmic workplace scheduling) carries the strongest empirical
grounding for the design-considerations section — see `RESEARCH_DOSSIER.md` for the recommendation.
