# Argument: Accountability in the triad

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026. For the author's ruling. Sources are card paths in this repo.]

**P1.** Accountability is a relation between an actor and a forum. The actor must explain and justify its conduct, and the forum can question it and pass judgment with consequences.
- *Warrant:* this is the standard definition in the accountability literature, and FAccT work on algorithmic accountability uses it.
- *Sources:* `lima_pdw/literature/cards/bovens2007.md` (full text); `lima_pdw/literature/cards/wieringa2020.md` (abstract depth, for the FAccT uptake only).

**P2.** When an algorithmic intermediary evaluates two parties and binds them both, neither party is a forum for the intermediary, and each party's outcome depends on the intermediary and the counterpart at once. Its rules are also opaque in a way that disclosure does not cure.
- *Warrant:* on marketplaces and booking sites the operator turns one party's reviews into a verdict on the other, and the evaluated party cannot see how much of the verdict came from the platform and how much from the counterpart. The operator can then distribute, deflect and deny accountability.
- *Sources:*
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/curchod2020.md` (triad among operator, buyers and sellers)
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/rahman2021.md` (p. 976, the five components of the invisible cage; the evaluated worker cannot tell how much client and platform each contributed)
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/starkvandenbroeck2024.md` (p. 14: accountability "distributed, deflected, and denied")
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/burrell2016.md` (three kinds of opacity, the third intrinsic to machine learning at scale; abstract depth)
  - `hospitality_phygital/library/cards/scottorlikowski2012accountability.md` (ratings subvert hierarchical accountability in travel)

**C.** Accountability for an AI-mediated relationship must be specified as a three-place relation. It needs a forum that both parties can call on together. It also needs the parties to have the competence to tell whether an outcome came from the counterpart or from the rule: interpreting results, specifying intent across the filter, and tracking rule changes. Two-place accountability (system to affected person) cannot assign responsibility in a triad, because it hears only one of the two people whose joint work the system judged.
- *Warrant for the step:* if P1 defines accountability by an actor–forum pair, and P2 shows that a triad has two affected parties and no shared forum, then a two-place mechanism leaves out the counterpart whose conduct is half the evidence.
