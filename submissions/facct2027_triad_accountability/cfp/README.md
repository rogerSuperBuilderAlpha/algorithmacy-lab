## Format (verbatim)
- "Submissions must be no longer than 14 pages, excluding references (most papers are between 12 and 14 pages)."
- An extra page is allowed for "ethics, adverse impacts, and other statements".
- The ACM single-column template (`acmart`, `manuscript,screen,review,anonymous`). Submissions must be anonymized.
- A "Generative AI usage statement" is required.
- Submission is through OpenReview. "the registered list of authors cannot be modified."
- The abstract field has no stated length limit. The title and abstract can be changed until the paper deadline.

## Archival choice (verbatim)
- "Non-archival papers will only appear as abstracts in the proceedings".
- The archival or non-archival choice is made "at the time the paper is registered (at the abstract submission deadline)".
- Allowed under the non-archival option: "Conference-length versions of papers that are already under review at a journal, but which have not yet been published in that journal: Authors interested in this option must select the non-archival option".

## Scope lines nearest this paper
- Topics: "Organizational factors in fairness, accountability, and transparency"; "Resistance, refusal, and contestation of computational systems"; "Labor and economic impacts of computational systems"; "Interpretability/explainability".
- "Research from all fields is welcome, including algorithmic, critical, human-centered, legal, philosophical, sociological, statistical, and theoretical research."
- Out of scope: work "focused on purely hypothetical concerns".
