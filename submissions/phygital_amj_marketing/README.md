## Candidate angle [DRAFTED, for ruling]

**Working title:** Marked or Performative? Decision Spaces as a Test of Human-Centric Phygital Marketing.

- **Claim.** A phygital touchpoint (a virtual concierge, a smart kiosk, a recommendation at the shelf) is human-centric to the extent that it marks the points at which the stakeholder can still decide. A touchpoint that explains itself without marking those points is the "performative inclusivity" the call asks authors to audit.
- **Call topics answered:** technological fatigue and cognitive strain; virtual agents and digital concierges; performative inclusivity and ethics-washing; virtual agents in stakeholder relationships.
- **Lab constructs used:** decision spaces and marking (the Spaces in the Stream theory, `../phd1750_spaces_in_stream/` and the private `spaces/` project); algorithmacy as the competence whose uneven spread makes unmarked touchpoints an inclusion problem.
- **Possible evidence:** a short vignette or online study in the design of Paper C's Study 2, if the author wants an empirical piece; otherwise a conceptual article with an audit framework.
