## Research question

Stated once in the draft, §1, after the Apple case and the operator-compliance paragraph:

> For an actor whose economic coordination runs through an algorithmic intermediary it cannot feasibly leave, what form of sovereignty is available, and what competence does exercising it require?

It is not restated as a question later. §10 operationalizes it as measurement. No other wording of the question exists in the draft; do not invent a second.

## Thesis, and its shape

**The claim, recast, locked.** A firm can satisfy every literate duty Europe’s digital-sovereignty program imposes, and those it coordinates still hold no share in the algorithmic determinations that bind them. Literacy cannot, in principle, confer that share. Further duties of the same kind cannot supply it.

Where a mediator still binds after the parties it stands between could reach each other directly, the autonomy available to the coordinated actor is standing within the mediation: the power to shape the terms on which one is mediated, to contest the mediator’s arbitrary power, and to hold a share in the governance of the arrangement. A counterfactual on the forbidden tie sorts the mediators an actor can leave from those it can only govern, and yields a priority rule: exit-restoring instruments against contingent mediators, voice-building instruments against necessary ones, applied function by function. The competence this standing requires, algorithmacy, is continuous with algorithmic literacy in its parts and discontinuous in its object; it is a precondition of exercising the standing, never a substitute for it.

**The shape.** Aytac’s: dated scene, full thesis by the second movement, coinage in one stipulation then a paradigm case, the rival’s best case before its failure, objections in the objector’s indicative, diagnosis or concede-then-contain, a short verdict the later pages carry. Rawls’s order already in the draft: main idea, rival, coordination, device and principles, worked case, institutions, two sovereignties, competence, close.

**What the thesis is not.** The compliant complementor captured by ranking. That cell exists and may be mentioned; it is not the political claim. The claim’s subject is the operator who satisfies the duties and the people that operator binds.
