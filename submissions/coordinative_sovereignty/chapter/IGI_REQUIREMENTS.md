# IGI Global chapter requirements, and where this manuscript stands

Captured 2026-08-17 for *Algorithmacy and Sovereignty*, in the IGI Global Scientific Publishing volume
*Organizational Implications of Digital Sovereignty in the Age of AI* (ed. Samuel Fosso Wamba). Chapter due
2026-08-30.

## There is no downloadable chapter template

Worth stating plainly, because `HANDOFF.md` and the READMEs had recorded the template as a missing artifact
to go and fetch. **IGI publishes no chapter template file.** The Contributing Author Checklist points at the
Before You Write page for guidelines and links no template; the only organization-and-formatting `.docx` on
IGI's server is the *journal article* one, which is a different genre and carries IGI house heading styles
rather than the APA 7 headings the chapter guidelines require.

For chapters, the specification below **is** the template. Authors submit a plain Word manuscript that
follows it, through eEditorial Discovery.

## The subtitle is four characters over, and it is not just a formatting nit

The volume editor's condition #4 asked for a title revision, and the author declined it — the accepted
abstract, the conference deck, and the poster all circulated under the current title, and `HANDOFF.md` §0c
holds a drafted note explaining that choice. The 50-character subtitle limit is an independent, mechanical
reason the title may come back at production regardless of that argument.

Four characters is a small edit. Options that stay inside the limit, none applied:

- "Toward a Coordinative Sovereignty for Platform Firms" (52 — still over)
- "Coordinative Sovereignty for the Platform Era" (45)
- "A Coordinative Sovereignty for the Platform Era" (47)
- "Toward Coordinative Sovereignty" (31)

Dropping "Toward a" to reach "Coordinative Sovereignty for the Platform Era" keeps every content word the
circulated title carried. **This is the author's call**, and it interacts with the note to the editor: if the
title changes for the character limit, that note needs rewriting, because it currently defends keeping the
title exactly as circulated.

## Items that are not the manuscript

The checklist requires these alongside the chapter, and none is in this repository:

- **Biographical sketch**, 100–150 words, third person, for each of the three authors.
- **Copyright agreement**, signed digitally in eEditorial Discovery.
- **Affiliation and contact details** — postal address, email, phone — for each author.
- **Professional copy edit.** IGI states the manuscript "must be professionally copy edited prior to final
  submission" and sells a partner service. The chapter has been through a style pass, a ten-lens panel, a
  five-seat panel, and a mechanical QA pass, none of which is a professional copy edit in IGI's sense.
  Whether the volume enforces this is the editor's to say.

## Heading style: the one open question

IGI tells chapter authors to "format all headings to match APA 7th edition guidelines." APA 7 headings are
**not numbered**. This manuscript numbers its sections §1–§10, and nine internal cross-references point at
those numbers by name ("section 5.2", "section 7").

The numbering may well be fine — many published IGI chapters carry numbered sections, and the volume editor
may prefer them. But no IGI page authorizes it, and if production strips the numbers, nine cross-references
break silently. Ask the editor before submission. If the numbers have to go, the cross-references need
rewriting to name the sections by title instead, which is a mechanical pass over nine sentences.
