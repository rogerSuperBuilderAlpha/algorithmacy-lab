## What the research changed

Six findings alter the argument rather than its wording. They drive the restructure.

1. **Repetto is an ally, not a critic.** `[V]` The chapter answers a concept-stretching charge he never made;
   his article argues multi-actor accounts "neither stretch the concept… they explore ways to innovate the
   concept of sovereignty as a whole." Two defensive passages become one supporting citation.
2. **The pairing thesis dies as phrased.** Habermas already pairs a named competence (*öffentliches
   Räsonnement*) with a medium that makes authority answerable. "This chapter adds a different axis" is false.
   What survives is narrower and still the chapter's own: the competence is **unevenly held within an era**,
   and under non-exit that unevenness decides who can contest. This answers the editorial pass's standing
   question about what the historical frame buys.
3. **The diagnostic's real differentiator is node versus edge.** `[V*]` Brokerage, make-or-buy, and
   essential facilities all run a counterfactual on a **node**. The bypass test runs one on an **edge** — it
   adds the forbidden tie and keeps the mediator. They disagree on the chapter's own franchised-dealer case.
   Two of the three differentiators the chapter implicitly claims (function-level; rule-based) do not hold;
   remedy-linkage does.
4. **The OTA case becomes the answer to Reviewer 2.** `[V]` Three of its four facts are wrong, and the
   corrected data is better than the error: as the parity gate was dismantled, European OTA share **rose**
   19.7%→29.6% while direct bookings **fell** 57.6%→50.9%. Substitutability predicts erosion; the
   function-split predicts what happened.
5. **Algorithmacy's novelty narrows.** DeVito (2021) — a work the chapter cites as an ally — already defines
   an integrated, adaptive competence. The integration claim must be conceded by name; the **triadic setting**
   and the **competence → standing** link survive untouched.
6. **The chapter states a false regulatory fact.** `[V]` Directive 2024/2831 confers nothing until
   transposition on 2 December 2026, three months after submission.
