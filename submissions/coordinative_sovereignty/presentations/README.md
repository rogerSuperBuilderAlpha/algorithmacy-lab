# Presentations — "Algorithmacy and Sovereignty"

Presentation materials for the paper, prepared for the Society of Business Ethics Annual Meeting 2026.

- `algorithmacy-sovereignty_deck.pptx` — the talk deck (11 slides). It walks the paper's arc: the
  compliance gap, the three sensibilities and their sovereignties, digital sovereignty as extended
  literacy, the mediated triad and why literacy cannot reach it, algorithmacy, the bypass test
  (exit or voice), the definition of coordinative sovereignty, and the implications for scholars,
  managers, and policy.
- `algorithmacy-sovereignty-poster.pptx` — the one-slide conference poster covering the same argument.

The paper itself lives in [`../chapter/`](../chapter/); the current draft is the Word file there.
