# Verification pass 2 — the grounded claims

A fact-check (not a refutation) of the claims the database marked grounded: confirm each cited source's
details, confirm the chapter attributes it accurately, and surface any strong recent work. Three clusters, 36
sources. The purpose after pass 1's two fabrications was to make sure the inherited bibliography was clean.

## Result: the inherited bibliography is clean

All 36 sources are real, correctly cited, and accurately attributed. No misattributions. No fabrications among
the sources carried over from the accepted abstract. The claims H3, H4, H7, H8, H9, H14, H16 and their
supporting concepts (literacy, algorithmic management, non-domination, relational autonomy, involvement, commons
governance, functional/platform sovereignty, exit/voice) are verified.

## Two DOI discrepancies — resolved directly, not on agent say-so

The verification agents proposed alternate DOIs for two sources. Agent-supplied DOIs were the unreliable element
in pass 1, so both were checked against the publisher page:

- **Kellogg et al. (2020), "Algorithms at Work."** Agent proposed `10.5465/annals.2020.0041`. The AOM Annals
  page carries `10.5465/annals.2018.0174`, which is what the chapter already had. Agent wrong; **no change**.
- **Markell (2008), "The insufficiency of non-domination."** Agent proposed `10.1177/0090591707310084`. The SAGE
  page confirms `...310084`; the chapter had `...310220`. Agent right; **corrected in the chapter.** This is
  the one genuine citation error the pass caught.
