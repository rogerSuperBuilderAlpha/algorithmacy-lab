## Summary
Bratton proposes that the apparently unrelated forms of planetary-scale computation, including smart grids, cloud computing, mobile software, smart cities, universal addressing, and ubiquitous computing, can be read as one coherent whole, an accidental megastructure that is at once a computational apparatus and a new geopolitical architecture. He calls this whole The Stack. The argument is speculative design theory rather than empirical study, and its central claim, as the publisher description puts it, is that planetary-scale computation has changed our geopolitical realities, so that sovereignty must be re-described in terms of this composite rather than of territory alone. Only the publisher description was opened this session; the book's layer model and its detailed claims are not summarized here.
