## Use in the chapter
The audit proposes it in three places. U13: the oral-order sentences of §2.1 (law as recitation, oath as witnessed speech, restricted authorized speech, no cross-year comparison) need a source, and chapters 3 and 4 supply the contrast between oral and written administration and law. S37: §2.2's "Scott (1998) shows the modern state as a creature of the archive" is single-sourced and chapter 3, "The state, the bureau and the file," is the second source. O5: the abstract and §1 state as historical fact that "the competence and the accountability have always arrived together"; the audit says either own this as thesis or attach Innis 1951 and Goody 1986. Role: support, MUST. The chapter currently cites only Goody 1977 and Goody and Watt 1963; this volume is the institutional rather than cognitive Goody and is the one §2 actually needs.
