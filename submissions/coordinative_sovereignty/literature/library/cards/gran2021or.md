## Use in the chapter
Not yet cited. Audit row S12 proposes it as a second source beside Cotter and Reisdorf for the §4 claim of a socioeconomic gradient in algorithmic knowledge. The study is a Norwegian survey of internet users that sorts respondents into awareness clusters and relates awareness to age, education and gender; the audit stresses that this evidence is citizen-level, about feeds and recommendations, not about firms, so the chapter's inference that competence concentrates in large platform-dependent firms (gap G1) must be stated as an inference.
