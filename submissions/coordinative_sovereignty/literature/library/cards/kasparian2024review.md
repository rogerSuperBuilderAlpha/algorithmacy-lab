## Summary
Kasparian reviews Trebor Scholz's Own This! (Verso, 2023) as a new milestone in Scholz's three contributions: conceptualizing platform cooperatives, building a field of scholarship, and fostering a movement. The review walks through the book's seven chapters and epilogue: alternative ownership models for the digital economy, including user and worker cooperatives and DAOs; federation-based scaling toward a pluralistic digital commonwealth; redefining the value cooperatives create beyond what GDP measures; the union-coop model and the forgotten affinities between unions and cooperatives, with SEWA in India as the example; data cooperatives, data commons, and data democracy, with the Swiss health data cooperative MIDATA; a speculative letter from 2035; and a practical guide to starting a platform co-op. Drawing on his own research on worker-recuperated enterprises in Argentina, Kasparian frames the book as scholar-activism that seeks legitimacy for socioeconomic alternatives by uniting unions, cooperatives, commons movements, open-source communities, and feminist struggles in a common narrative.
