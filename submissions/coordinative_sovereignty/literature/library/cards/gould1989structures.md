## Use in the chapter
Not cited in the chapter's current text. The sweep marks it MUST for §3, with reach into §5.2 and §5.4: it is the formal sociology of brokerage the chapter's mediator vocabulary presupposes, partitioning brokerage into five roles (coordinator, itinerant broker or consultant, gatekeeper, representative, liaison) by the group membership of broker and brokered parties. The gatekeeper versus liaison distinction in particular underwrites the chapter's split between a mediator whose position is a gate over access to its own group and one that integrates across groups, which is close kin to the contingent versus necessary diagnostic of §5.2 and the portfolio-of-mediators point in §5.4. Role: support; pair with Stovel and Shaw 2012 on brokerage's instability.
