## Use in the chapter
Cited in §8: "CoopCycle's member cooperatives decide pay schemes and allocation rules on one-member-one-vote terms (Fortuny-Sicart et al., 2024)", as the open or federated counter-example to closed national platforms like WeChat and RuNet. Audit row S33 flags this as unverified: the abstract does not say it, and van Doorn and Badger (2020) describe federation-level democratic governance with pay and dispatch set locally. The chapter should soften the sentence or supply a page locator from the full text, and add van Doorn and Badger as a second source. Role: support for the platform-cooperativism example in §8, currently carrying more than its abstract warrants.
