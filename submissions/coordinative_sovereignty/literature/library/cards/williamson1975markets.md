## Use in the chapter
Cited twice. In §3 it anchors the trichotomy of coordination mechanisms, "markets coordinate through price, hierarchies through authority" (§3), beside Adler, Powell, and G. F. Thompson. In §5.2 it supplies one of the counterfactual tests the chapter's bypass test generalizes: "Transaction cost economics asks whether a firm should perform a transaction internally or buy it (Williamson, 1975)" (§5.2). Role: support. Ahrne and Brunsson (2011) and Kornberger et al. (2017) are the organization-theory challenges to the trichotomy the chapter must answer.
