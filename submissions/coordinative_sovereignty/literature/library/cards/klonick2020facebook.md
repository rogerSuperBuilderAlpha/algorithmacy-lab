## Summary
Klonick's Feature traces the creation of Facebook's Oversight Board, an external body with authority to review the company's content-moderation decisions. She situates it in Facebook's fifteen-year evolution into one of the most powerful arbiters of online speech, governing through a semi-public body of Community Standards that function as a system of laws. Drawing on interviews and on access to the design process, she reconstructs the events and influences that led to the decision to create the Board, the global consultation, the drafting of the charter and bylaws, and the mechanisms meant to secure structural and financial independence through a trust. She presents the Board as a novel articulation of internet governance, and concludes that it has great potential to set precedent for user participation in private platform governance and for a user right to procedure in content moderation, while noting that Facebook retains exclusive control over the rules and that the Board's independence depends on arrangements the company constructed.

## Use in the chapter
Cited in §7: "Platform-run oversight boards offer a weaker private analog" with "some authority to review the platform's determinations" (Klonick, 2020). The chapter uses it for the promise and limit of a standing institution of voice constituted by the platform it oversees. Audit S31 notes that only one such board exists, that the plural overstates, and recommends writing "Meta's Oversight Board" and adding Douek (2019) as a second source. Role: support.
