## Use in the chapter
Not cited. Audit S14 notes that §4's claim that the strong literacy thesis "did not survive the ethnographers" rests on Scribner and Cole and Street, and recommends adding Halverson, whose title announces the implosion of the thesis, while softening "did not survive" to "was cut back by". G8 adds that §2 rests on the Goody and Ong great divide without conceding the contest that §4 concedes, and that one sentence in §2 citing Street, Finnegan and Halverson would close it. Role: contests the cognitive version of the literacy thesis; the chapter's own position is that its claim concerns coordination rather than cognition.
