## Summary
The press release announces that the Commission has made legally binding, under EU antitrust rules, the commitments Amazon offered to close its investigations into the marketplace. As the headline states, the commitments do three things: they bar Amazon from using non-public marketplace seller data for its own retail business, they ensure equal access for sellers to the Buy Box, and they ensure equal access to the Prime programme, which the release ties to Amazon's dual role as the operator of a marketplace and a seller competing on it. The page as rendered this session showed the headline and the lead sentence; the detailed terms of each commitment (the second Buy Box offer, carrier choice for Prime sellers, duration and monitoring) were not read and are not summarized here. The release is the primary official record that the Commission treated seller data use, the Buy Box gate and the Prime logistics tie as distinct, separately remediable conduct, which is what the chapter's portfolio argument needs from it.

## Use in the chapter
Section 5.4 decomposes a large marketplace into four mediators: fulfilment through logistics (necessary), matching (partial), the gate on "which seller a buyer sees, a toll sellers pay to pass" (contingent), and first-party resale (reducible). The audit (U7) marks the decomposition as uncited and proposes Khan 2017 and this release, since the commitments map onto three of the four: Prime logistics, the Buy Box gate, and the dual role. Role: support, and an official record that a regulator has already treated these functions as separable, which is what the chapter's "portfolio of mediators" argument requires.
