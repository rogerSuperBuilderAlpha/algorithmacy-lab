The gaps file (C4) and strand 5 mark it MUST for §3 and §5.4 as the canonical organization-theory statement of the platform as a fourth form, coordinated by rankings, ratings and reviews rather than by price or authority, published three years before Stark and Pais (2020), whom the chapter cites for the same move. The role is position against: the chapter's §3 currently presents the "fourth term" as Stark and Pais's, and a reader in organization theory will expect Kornberger, Pflueger and Mouritsen to be named first, with the chapter saying what its "opaque, interested mediator" adds to "evaluative infrastructure". The concept is also the natural rival to the chapter's §5.4 portfolio-of-mediators model, since it treats the evaluative layer as a single infrastructure rather than a bundle of separable functions.
