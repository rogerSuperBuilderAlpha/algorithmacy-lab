## Summary
Spreitzer develops and validates a multidimensional measure of psychological empowerment in the workplace, defined as intrinsic task motivation expressed in four cognitions: meaning, competence, self-determination, and impact. Second-order confirmatory factor analyses on two complementary samples establish the convergent and discriminant validity of the four dimensions and their contribution to a single overall construct. Structural equation modelling then tests a nomological network, with hypotheses about antecedents such as locus of control, self-esteem, access to information, and rewards, and consequences such as managerial effectiveness and innovative behaviour. The results give initial support for the construct validity of psychological empowerment, and the article closes with directions for future research. It is the canonical measurement paper for felt empowerment inside an organization.
