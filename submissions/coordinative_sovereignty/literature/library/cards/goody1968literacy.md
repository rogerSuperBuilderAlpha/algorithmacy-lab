## Use in the chapter
Not cited in the chapter. The Evidence Audit (E10) proposes it, with Harris (1989) and Clanchy (2013), as the replacement support for the §2.2 sentence "Literacy was a clerical monopoly for most of its history," which the audit finds anachronistic as a universal and asks to be reworded as "a restricted competence held by scribes, clerics, and officials." The volume's comparative case studies of restricted literacy are the evidence for that narrower claim. Role: support for the corrected wording. Note that the chapter's existing Goody citation is the 1977 Domestication of the savage mind, a different work.
