## Use in the chapter
Cited in §4: the strong orality-literacy thesis "that writing restructures the mind, did not survive the ethnographers, who showed literacy's cognitive effects to be practice-specific and socially embedded (Scribner & Cole, 1981; Street, 1984)". Audit row S14 and gap G8 judge "did not survive" an overstatement and propose "was cut back by", adding Halverson 1992 on the implosion of the literacy thesis. Scribner and Cole's Vai study is the canonical demonstration that cognitive effects attach to specific literacy practices rather than to literacy as such, which is what the softened wording needs.
