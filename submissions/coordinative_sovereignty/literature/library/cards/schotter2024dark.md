## Use in the chapter
Not cited. Strand 2 lists it as a SHOULD for §8 on aspiration adaptation under dependence. According to the Ivey research summary opened this session, the paper identifies market, technological and structural dependence as the channels through which a dominant platform controls the firms that rely on it, and finds that such firms fall into cycles of reactive adaptation in which they adjust their own goals to the platform's changes instead of pursuing their own strategy. That is the firm-level counterpart of §8's "compliant but captured" strand and of the chapter's claim that a dependent firm's standing is a capability problem rather than a compliance one. Role: support for §8's managerial implications; it should be read before being cited, since only a secondary summary was opened.

## Key quotations
