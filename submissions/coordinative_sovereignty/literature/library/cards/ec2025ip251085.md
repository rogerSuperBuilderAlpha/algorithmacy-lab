## Summary
The press release announces the Commission's first non-compliance decisions under the Digital Markets Act. Apple was fined 500 million euro for breaching the anti-steering obligation: app developers distributing through the App Store must be able to inform users free of charge of offers outside the store and to conclude contracts there, and the Commission found that Apple's restrictions prevented developers from fully benefiting from alternative distribution channels, while consumers could not be steered to cheaper offers. Meta was fined 200 million euro for its "consent or pay" model on Facebook and Instagram, which did not give users the required specific choice of a service that uses less personal data but is otherwise equivalent. The release presents the two decisions as the first enforcement of the Act's obligations against gatekeepers.
