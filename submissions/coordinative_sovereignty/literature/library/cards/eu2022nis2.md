## Summary
The NIS 2 Directive replaces the 2016 NIS Directive and lays down measures for a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union. It widens the scope of the earlier instrument to a larger set of sectors, divides covered organisations into essential and important entities, and imposes on them risk-management obligations, incident-reporting duties and governance responsibilities, with management bodies made accountable for compliance. It requires Member States to adopt national cybersecurity strategies, designate competent authorities and computer security incident response teams, and cooperate through the Cooperation Group and the CSIRTs network; it also harmonises supervisory powers and penalties. The Directive was adopted on 14 December 2022, published in the Official Journal on 27 December 2022, entered into force on 16 January 2023, and had to be transposed by 17 October 2024. In the chapter's terms it is a security and resilience instrument, addressed to operators rather than to the persons coordinated through them.
