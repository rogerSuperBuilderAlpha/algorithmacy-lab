## Use in the chapter
Cited in §3 as the second of four anchors for the trichotomy "markets coordinate through price, hierarchies through authority, and networks through trust and reciprocity (Adler, 2001; Powell, 1990; G. F. Thompson, 2003; Williamson, 1975)". The role is support; Powell is the canonical statement that networks are a distinct form rather than a hybrid between market and hierarchy, so the attribution of "trust and reciprocity" to the network form is standard. No audit flag. The live issue for this sentence is the rival reading from partial-organization theory (gap C5: Ahrne and Brunsson; Kirchner and Schüßler) that platforms are markets with organizational elements added, which dissolves the trichotomy the "fourth term" move presupposes.
