## Use in the chapter
Cited in §3: "The platform occupies the mediating position that J. D. Thompson (1967) named a mediating technology, linking parties who would otherwise never transact" (§3). The chapter uses Thompson's typology of technologies (long-linked, mediating, intensive) to show that the topology of the platform is old and that what is new is opacity, adaptivity, and interest. Role: support. The initial "J. D." is needed in text to distinguish from G. F. Thompson (2003), also cited in §3.
