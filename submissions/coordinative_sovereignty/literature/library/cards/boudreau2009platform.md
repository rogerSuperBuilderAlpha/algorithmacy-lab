## Use in the chapter
Not cited in the chapter's current text. The sweep marks it MUST for §3, where the chapter presents the platform as an opaque, interested mediator that sets and enforces the rules of coordination: this chapter is the canonical statement that multi-sided platforms act as private regulators of their ecosystems, using instruments beyond price, including rules, standards, and access control. It belongs in §3 as support, anchoring the claim that the mediator governs rather than merely matches, and it bears on §5.3 because a private regulator is precisely the kind of counterpart against which voice, not exit, is the available lever.
