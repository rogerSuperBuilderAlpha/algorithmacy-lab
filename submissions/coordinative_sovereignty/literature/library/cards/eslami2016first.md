## Use in the chapter
Cited in §4: "ordinary users build and revise folk theories of the feeds that curate them (Eslami et al., 2016)", as the HCI anchor for the claim that the practice of inferring how a platform works is already documented. The role is support. The audit (S10) flags it as a single source and proposes Rader and Gray (2015) as the second; strand 1 adds that Bucher (2017), the algorithmic imaginary, precedes it and should carry the lineage before Eslami and DeVito. No overreach flagged, but the chapter should not attribute to this paper any claim about platform workers; its setting is Facebook News Feed users.
