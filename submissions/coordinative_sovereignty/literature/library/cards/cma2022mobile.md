## Summary
The CMA's market study examines the mobile ecosystems built around Apple's iOS and Google's Android: operating systems, app stores and browsers, and the businesses that depend on them. Using internal documents, survey evidence and submissions over a year-long study, it finds that the two firms hold an effective duopoly, that users rarely switch between the two ecosystems, and that app developers have no practical route to consumers on each platform other than the respective app store. The report documents the levers that follow: control over app distribution and review, mandatory in-app payment systems with commissions, restrictions on alternative distribution, and control of browser engines. It concludes that these features give Apple and Google substantial and durable market power over app developers and users, sets out a range of possible interventions, and refers mobile browsers and cloud gaming for a market investigation while anticipating the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers regime.
