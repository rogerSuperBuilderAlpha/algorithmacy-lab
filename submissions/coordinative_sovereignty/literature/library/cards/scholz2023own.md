## Summary
Written from Kasparian's review. Own This! is Scholz's book-length update of his 2016 platform cooperativism pamphlet, combining research, testimony and cases in seven chapters and an epilogue. After an introductory survey of alternative paths, it sets out ownership models for the digital economy (ESOPs, federated, user-owner and social ownership) and concentrates on worker and user cooperatives; it then argues for federation-based scaling through a pluralistic "cooperative commonwealth" of mutually supporting organizations; it proposes ways to frame and measure the social value cooperatives create that GDP cannot capture; it traces the shared history of unions and cooperatives and proposes union-coop models, with SEWA in India as an example; it surveys data cooperatives, data commons and DAOs, using MIDATA's member-controlled health data as illustration; and it closes with a speculative "letter from 2035" and a practical guide to starting a platform co-op. The reviewer presents it as a scholar-activist intervention meant to give the movement a common narrative and legitimacy.

## Use in the chapter
§7 cites Scholz (2016) for the claim that cooperatives "would give coordinated actors a share in governing the mediation by making them owners," adding that "the limits are scale and capital." Strand 4 proposes this book as the SHOULD-cite update, and the audit (S27) notes the limits are only loosely in Scholz and suggests Mannan and Pek (2024) for them. The book's chapter on federation-based scaling addresses the scale limit directly and its data-cooperative chapter connects the cooperative route to the data-intermediary row of the §7 table. Role: support, replacing or supplementing the 2016 citation; the chapter's deeper objection, that "ownership does not make a necessary mediator leaveable," is not one Scholz engages.
