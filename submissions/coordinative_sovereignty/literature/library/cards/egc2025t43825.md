## Summary
This is the Official Journal notice of Apple's action for annulment against the Commission's DMA non-compliance decision of 23 April 2025, in which the Commission found that Apple's App Store anti-steering rules breached Article 5(4) of the Digital Markets Act and imposed a fine of 500 million euros. Apple asks the General Court to annul Decision C(2025) 2090 final in whole or in part, in the alternative to annul specified articles or reduce the fine, and to order the Commission to pay costs. The five pleas in law are that the Commission misinterpreted and misapplied Article 5(4) DMA; that it erred as to the lawful scope of the cease-and-desist order; that it violated Apple's rights of defence and the principle of good administration; that there was no legal basis for imposing a fine; and that the fine is erroneous or disproportionate. The case was lodged on 7 July 2025 and remains pending; the Commission stated on 18 August 2026 that it welcomes Apple's revised terms and is not considering periodic penalties.

## Use in the chapter
Section 8 cites it in "a decision Apple has appealed (Case T-438/25)" after noting the April 2025 fine for restricting how developers steer users outside the App Store. Role: legal instrument documenting that the anti-steering enforcement, the chapter's example of a regulator running the edge test on a contingent gate (§5.2), is contested and unresolved. The audit confirms "pending" remains correct and asks for a submission-week re-check. The first plea, on the meaning of Article 5(4), is the one that bears on the chapter's argument, since it concerns whether the DMA's steering obligation reaches the gate or only the rail.
