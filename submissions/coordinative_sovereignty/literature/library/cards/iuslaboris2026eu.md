## Summary
This Ius Laboris insight, written by an associate at the Spanish member firm Sagardoy and updated on 3 June 2026, is a transposition tracker for Directive (EU) 2024/2831 on improving working conditions in platform work. It restates the Directive's purpose, improving working conditions and the protection of personal data in platform work, its scope, which covers persons performing platform work in the EU regardless of where the platform is established, and the transposition deadline of 2 December 2026. The substantive content is an interactive map, maintained by the alliance's member firms, showing for each member state whether implementing legislation has been adopted, drafted, or not yet started. As of the June update the map showed no member state with complete transposition, with several in drafting or consultation. The page is practitioner grey literature and should be cited for status at a stated date rather than for analysis.

## Use in the chapter
Section 8 states that "No member state had fully transposed the directive as this chapter was written, so the standing it describes is a legislative commitment, not yet a present fact." The audit (U18) asks for a dated tracker and a stamp of "as of August 2026"; this page is the proposed source. Role: support for the transposition-status sentence. Because the status will change before and after the December 2026 deadline, the in-text cite should carry the date and the chapter's submission-week re-check (audit item 7) should refresh it. It can sit beside the Directive's own entry (eu2024platformwork) and, for the legal content of the algorithmic-management chapter, Aloisi (2024).
