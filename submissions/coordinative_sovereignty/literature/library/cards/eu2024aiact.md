## Summary
The Artificial Intelligence Act is the European Union's horizontal framework for AI, adopted 13 June 2024 and published in the Official Journal on 12 July 2024. It classifies AI systems by risk, prohibits certain practices, imposes obligations on providers and deployers of high-risk systems, and sets transparency duties and a governance structure with an AI Office. Article 4, headed AI literacy, is the provision relevant here: in its original form it required providers and deployers to take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy among staff and other persons operating or using AI systems on their behalf, taking account of their training, the context of use, and the persons affected. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI of 8 July 2026, in force 27 July 2026, softened that duty to one of supporting the development of a sufficient level of AI literacy. The Act is the clearest statutory instance of a competence requirement attached to algorithmic systems, which is why it matters to a chapter about algorithmacy.

## Use in the chapter
Section 9 states that "the AI Act obliges providers and deployers to ensure a level of AI literacy among staff" as a compulsory analog to the market in algorithmic advice. Role: legal instrument. Two corrections apply: the Act has no reference-list entry (audit U5), and the verb "ensure" no longer tracks Article 4 after the Omnibus amendment (audit E3), so the sentence should say that Article 4, as amended, requires providers and deployers to support the development of AI literacy. The chapter can use the weakening itself: a literacy duty diluted from ensuring to supporting is an example of disclosure-era instruments retreating from the competence they presuppose.

## Key quotations
Original Article 4: "Providers and deployers of AI systems shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf" (Art. 4, as read at artificialintelligenceact.eu, pre-amendment text)
