## Use in the chapter
Cited in §5.4 on the platform as a portfolio of mediators: "cut at the thin crossing points, where the interface between functions is narrow enough that separating them costs little (Baldwin, 2008)". The role is support for the chapter's criterion that a platform's functions can be separated where the interface between them is thin, which is how the diagnostic distinguishes the necessary from the contingent mediator inside one firm.
