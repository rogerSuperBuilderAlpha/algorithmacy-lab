## Term ownership summary
Algorithmic sovereignty: geopolitics (Liang & Li) and Indigenous data (Brown et al.). Platform sovereignty: not a title-term (Cohen, D'Cunha, Lehdonvirta, Törnberg). Coordination rights: Paul. Infrastructural power: Mann via Valdez, Rahman & Thelen. Algorithmic citizenship: Cheney-Lippold. Technological citizenship: Frankenfeld, Gardenier et al. Algorithmic resistance: Treré/Bonini/Heemsbergen; Magalhães critic. Relational sovereignty: Bannerman (cited). No literatures: computational sovereignty, network sovereignty, mediated autonomy, sovereignty of the user, data agency (one FAccT 2024 paper https://doi.org/10.1145/3630106.3658930, unopened).
