### Finding 1 — §7 closes on a policymaker; §8 opens a literature file

**Claim.** Once §5.5→§6 carries voice, the next costly section joint is §7→§8. §7 spends its end-focus on the diagnostic telling a policymaker where to put each institution. §8 opens on how current research operationalizes organizational digital sovereignty. Those are not the same thread. The open is also a citation-final structural sentence.

**Join.**

> End (§7, ll. 766–767): No single instrument is likely to deliver coordinative sovereignty. It is the cumulative standing a stack of such institutions confers, and the diagnostic is what tells a policymaker where to put each one.
>
> Start (§8, ll. 771–773): Current research typically operationalizes organizational digital sovereignty as a single capability built from regulatory and infrastructural components (Fratini et al., 2024; Hummel et al., 2021; Schmuntzsch et al., 2025).

**Why it damages this lens.** Mechanism 1 and 6: the tail names a placement problem for a policymaker; the head names a measurement habit in the literature. "The typology here separates" (l. 773) is the intended bridge, and it is not on the join. The reader must invent that the diagnostic that places instruments also splits the capability research treats as one. Mechanism 2: the first sentence of a section is a structural split (one capability versus two), and it ends on a three-cite parenthesis. The next sentence recovers with "The typology," which should have been the end-focus of the open, or the tail of §7.

**Fix.** End §7 on the split the diagnostic produces. Open §8 on that split, cites in subject position. Paste-ready: Part 3, Fix A.
