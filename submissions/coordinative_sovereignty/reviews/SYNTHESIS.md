## The spine fixes (structural; each flagged by 2+ reviewers)

1. **Own the popular/democratic register of "sovereignty" early — THE headline fix (digital sov + political
   theory, both name it #1).** Surface in §1/§2 the functional definition now smuggled into the conclusion:
   sovereignty as *the standing to hold power to account through the medium in which power is exercised*.
   State outright that the construct **specifies** (adds attributes, narrows extension) rather than stretches,
   which defeats Repetto's concept-stretching charge. This one move also retires the Bodin error (#2),
   dissolves Markell's irony (he attacks the sovereign-mastery picture the *name* reinstates), and stops the
   authority-vs-standing slippage.
2. **§5.3 Bodin/Mill error (political theory + digital sov).** Bodin is supreme, indivisible *state*
   authority — the un-bypassable node, not exit; Mill's private sphere is the *non-interference* Pettit later
   calls insufficient. Neither is "the sovereignty of exit," and neither is in the reference list. Replace with
   the relational-autonomy tradition's own foil (the self-sufficient chooser / the independence ideal).
3. **§5 conflates two counterfactuals (org theory + Reviewer 2).** §5.1 factoring/irreducibility and §5.2
   bypass are different tests; a mediator can be irreducible-as-is yet contingent-under-bypass, so
   triadic/irreducible does **not** entail necessary. Separate them explicitly; this also keeps the unseen
   IIT apparatus honestly subordinated.
4. **§2.3 change-in-kind inconsistency (Reviewer 2 + digital sov + media).** The chapter calls digital
   sovereignty "extended literacy" (same kind) yet needs algorithmacy to *be* a change in kind, and blurs two
   grounds for the boundary (reflexive triad vs. opacity-as-degree). Make consistent: digital = an *extension*
   of literacy; algorithmacy = the change in kind, grounded in the reflexive triad (a party read while the
   system optimizes), not in opacity-as-degree. And don't flatten digital sovereignty to "a move to centralize
   control" — it misreads Couture & Toupin (grassroots/emancipatory variants), cited in the same sentence.
5. **Algorithmacy = integration + triad-embedding, not novelty of the parts (media + platform).** The honest,
   survivable claim is the *integration* of inferential/translational/temporal competence *inside the triad*,
   reading a counterpart while the system optimizes. Stop dismissing Möhlmann (sensemaking), Bucher
   (anticipatory compliance), DeVito (folk theories), Pangrazio & Selwyn / Sander as mere "spectatorship" —
   they are from-within and already temporal. Illustrate the *triad* (the counterpart present), not the
   seller-vs-ranking dyad (defined triadically, illustrated dyadically).
6. **"Text-driven law cannot reach the coordination layer" → "the *disclosure* modality cannot; the emerging
   *standing* modality is the fix" (law).** As written it invites a counter-example list — the in-force
   instruments already reach ranking/automated decisions: P2B Reg. 2019/1150 (absent), DSA Arts. 20–21/27,
   DMA 6(5)/6(7), GDPR Art. 22, DGA 2022/868. Reframing vindicates the thesis. Interoperability (DMA 6(7),
   essential-facilities) is voice-via-access on a *necessary* mediator, not pure exit — fixes the exit/voice
   filing.
7. **The diagnostic is make-or-buy / substitutability without a divergence case — narrow it honestly
   (Reviewer 2 + platform + org theory).** Claim the real contribution explicitly: a *function-level,
   rule-based, remedy-linked* classification, distinct from Burt's structural holes, Williamson make-vs-buy,
   and essential-facilities doctrine. Concede the plain counterfactual carries the chapter; the Φ machinery is
   the companion paper's; stop implying "computable" is delivered here.
8. **Co-optation drops Selznick's defining concession (org + platform).** Selznick's co-optation grants a
   seat; the platform keeps enrollment and drops the concession — near its antonym. Confront formal-vs-informal
   co-optation. Cite Stark & Pais (2020), not only Stark & Vanden Broeck (2024); own the extractive reading
   rather than laundering it through Stark's more neutral sense.
9. **Anthropomorphism — interest belongs to the firm, not the algorithm (platform + org).** §3/§3.1 give the
   algorithm "objectives of its own." Locate interest in the firm, adaptivity in human reconfiguration. Use
   Simmel's *tertius gaudens* (the neutral word "mediator" fights the interested-third argument). Fix the
   Thompson: J. D. Thompson's (1967) *mediating technology* is the canonical concept for the mediated triad;
   the chapter cites G. F. Thompson (2003, networks) — VERIFY which is cited/meant.
10. **Hirschman is half-earned (political + digital sov + platform).** Add the lazy-monopoly point (necessity
    is where voice is *weakest* — the productive tension the chapter drops); resolve cooperativism (exit in §6
    vs. voice in §7 — it is ownership-transformation, a third response); engage exit-undermines-voice.
11. **§3.3 bottleneck-rent error (platform).** "A source of rent independent of any service" contradicts the
    cited Rochet–Tirole / Armstrong–Wright. Separate bottleneck *power* (topological) from *rent* (disciplined
    by multihoming); attribute "competitive bottleneck" to Armstrong & Wright.
12. **Ostrom asserted, not defended (political).** The platform is owned by an adversarial third; the move is
    Ostrom-*via-enclosure*, not naive "self-govern the platform."
