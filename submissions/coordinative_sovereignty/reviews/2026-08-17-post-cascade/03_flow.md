## Step 0 — Register and bar

Impersonal academic chapter; first person banned. House cohesion (eight mechanisms) is the bar: tail-head linkage, end-focus, rename the thread entity, gloss-welding, reformulation, forward hooks, backward hooks, grouped parallel evidence. Semantic restatement is judged at section scale — the same conclusion re-landing in different words as if it were a new finding — not at the sentence scale, where reformulation is a mechanism.

The uncalibrated sovereignty register was run for locators only. It reports tail-head on 38.9% of sentences, citation-final on 8.9% (zero runs of three or more), and 71.7% of paragraph joins sharing no entity across the break. That last figure is a mechanical miss: it treats heading lines, the Keywords block, and synonym/pronoun hooks as empty. Locators were used as pointers; each flagged join was inspected in the text. No z-score is treated as a fail.

This seat judges whether a sentence hands the next sentence something to hold, and whether a section close names what the next stretch walks. It does not re-litigate construct license, cases, or citation mechanics.

## Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage

### Finding 1 — §5.3 closes on institutions; §5.4 opens on a portfolio

**Claim.** The last sentence of §5.3 has the grammar of a local forward hook and names the wrong next stretch. Two subsections of diagnostic work still remain.

**Join.**

> left exactly where it commands the least leverage, and whatever force it has must be supplied by
> institutions. Building those institutions is what the literate and digital forms of sovereignty leave
> untheorized. (§5.3, 518–521)
>
> ### 5.4 A platform is a portfolio of mediators
>
> A real platform is several mediators wearing one name, and the diagnostic classifies functions, not firms. (523–525)

**Why it damages this lens.** A forward hook is supposed to name what the next stretch walks. “Building those institutions” names §7. The next stretch is the portfolio split and then the hotel case. The chapter knows how to plant a long-range pointer when it means one: “section 7 places it among the voice instruments” (255), “section 4 argues” (149), “section 9 takes that up” (650). This closer does not do that. It reads as the next move. A reader who follows the hook walks into the wrong room and has to reorient on the heading.

The damage is local. The heading recovers it. The argument does not break. The join still fails.

**Fix.** Close §5.3 on the residual lever just drawn — voice where exit is gone — and point the institution-building claim at §7 by name, or save it for the §6→§7 weld, which already carries it. Open §5.4 by retrieving the contingent/necessary pair the portfolio is about to wear as one name.

### Finding 2 — “A closer relative comes later” is a dangling hook

**Claim.** Mid-§5.2 a sentence plants a forward hook and then withholds the relative for thirty lines while the stretch walks three other tests instead.

**Join.**

> The test resembles three others, and the operation differs from each. A closer relative comes later.
> Brokerage research asks what a broker gains from standing between disconnected parties… (443–445)

The closer relative is the ancillary-restraints doctrine at 473–477 (“European competition law already runs this counterfactual on contract terms”). Between the promise and the landing sit the node/edge distinction, the franchised dealer, and the App Store payment gate — the load-bearing demonstration of the section.

**Why it damages this lens.** The hook names a next item the next sentences do not deliver. Brokerage, Williamson, and essential facilities are the “three others,” not the closer relative. The reader holds an IOU across the chapter’s most important technical passage. That is the opposite of tail-head: the tail announces a guest who is not in the room.

**Fix.** Cut the sentence. The ancillary-restraints paragraph can introduce itself: it is already written as a recognition (“already runs this counterfactual”), not as a payoff to a planted tease. If the kinship needs a flag, put it on the sentence that actually arrives.

### Finding 3 — §8→§9 shares no entity across the break

**Claim.** After the morning spine and the §0j welds, the next major handoff was not welded.

**Join.**

> Openness and control are properties of the infrastructure.
> Coordinative sovereignty is a property of the coordinated actor's relation to it, and the two do not track

**Why it damages this lens.** §8’s last paragraph is WeChat, the RuNet, CoopCycle, the fediverse, the New York Drivers Cooperative. Its close is the independence of infrastructure-properties from standing. §9 opens on a measurement program and names nothing from that close — not standing, not the two sovereignties, not the cases. “The empirical program begins with measurement” is a heading restated as a sentence.

This is the same class of miss the evening panel caught at §6→§7 and §7→§8, one section later. It is less costly: future-research sections often restart, and direction one can be reached from the typology earlier in §8. The local join still does not hold.

**Fix.** Open §9 on the independence just asserted. The two constructs vary independently; that claim is now a measurement problem. Direction one (algorithmacy) and direction two (coordinative sovereignty, under a necessary mediation) then walk what the hook named.
