### 1. §3.3 still defines opacity by denying a weaker reading (L288–289)

**Claim.** The last decorative antithesis of any weight is the two-beat at the rent turn. The positive sentence does the work. The foil denies a by-product reading the preceding paragraph never offered.

**Quote.**
> Opacity is not merely a by-product of this arrangement. It preserves the rent. (L288–289)

**Why it damages style.** This is the antithesis machine in its textbook form: *X is not merely Y. It is Z.* The paragraph before it is about self-interest and authored coordination, not about whether opacity is accidental. O'Reilly, Cohen, and Sekar then earn the rent claim on their own. The foil is a throat-clear. Berlin (L58–59), Street (L382–383), and the App Store rail/rule split (L468) stay: those deny a neighbor the reader is already reaching for.

**Fix.** Lead with the rent. See R1.

### 2. One sentence-initial agentless passive still has its agent on the page (L277)

**Claim.** HANDOFF's older "zero sentence-initial agentless passives" line is still an overclaim, but only just. The evening's two named passives were rewritten. One leftover opens on the patient.

**Quote.**
> The single-homing side is competed for and often subsidized. (L277)

**Why it damages style.** Armstrong has just named the competitive bottleneck. "Each platform" is the subject of the sentence before. The next sentence puts platforms back in the agent slot ("Application stores show the pattern"). L277 is the one beat that hides them. Mid-sentence passives where the agent arrives in the same clause (L35, the platform chooses) are not this defect.

**Fix.** Keep platforms as the subject. See R2.

### 3. §4 performs the DeVito concession twice before DeVito speaks (L312)

**Claim.** The novelty-narrowing is closed as argument. The delivery still announces that nothing is new, then says the integration is not new either, then lets DeVito do both jobs.

**Quote.**
> None of those three parts is new, and the integration of them is not new either. DeVito (2021) defines algorithmic literacy as … and his account of adaptive folk theorization already holds that capacity together across platforms that keep changing. (L312–317)

**Why it damages style.** Double "is not new" is self-narrating rigor in the same family as the retired "naming it strengthens the claim." The concession the chapter needs is DeVito's holding-together, which the next sentence already states with `already holds`. The frame is surplus. "What algorithmacy adds is the setting" (L329) is the earned turn; it does not need a two-beat negation in front of the literature.

**Fix.** State that the literature already names the parts and holds them together, then let DeVito define. See R3.

No fourth finding. `practised` (L44) is a one-word BrE miss, recorded in Part 2, not stretched into a finding. `The same holds here` (L648) and `enough here` (L405) are the licensed leftovers from earlier passes — a Pettit application tag and a companion-scope pointer. Leave them.
