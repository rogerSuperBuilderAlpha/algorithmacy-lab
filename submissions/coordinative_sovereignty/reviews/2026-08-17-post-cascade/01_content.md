## Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage

No finding on this lens rises to major or minor. Two nits, both local, neither reopens a closed item.

### Finding 1 (nit) — Art. 5 trigger compressed after a careful split

**Claim.** Section 8 now correctly separates the platform-work directive's employment presumption from Article 25. The sentence that names the presumption then paraphrases the statutory trigger as "where the platform controls the work."

**Quote.**

> The same directive's rebuttable presumption of employment, where the platform controls the work, is a different instrument. (802–803)

**Why it damages this lens.** The rest of the paragraph is the chapter's best legal move: transposition tense is future, Art. 25 is quoted as the voice clause, and reclassification is filed as a change of coordination form rather than standing inside the triad. That care makes the trigger the one clause a labor-law referee will mark. Article 5(1) of Directive (EU) 2024/2831, retrieved this session (**verified**, EUR-Lex HTML of OJ L 2024/2831), applies the presumption "where facts indicating direction and control, in accordance with national law, collective agreements or practice in force in the Member States and with consideration to the case-law of the Court of Justice, are found." "Controls the work" is the chapter's theoretical reason the presumption would move a worker into a hierarchy (803–804). It is not the statutory predicate. Member states define the facts; the Union text does not.

**Fix.** Keep the split. Replace the appositive with the statutory predicate. Paste-ready text in Part 3.

### Finding 2 (nit) — §7 lets firms "collectively bargain" in worker grammar

**Claim.** Two paragraphs after the bearer split, collective bargaining is extended to "platform-dependent firms" and given worker objects (assignments, deactivation).

**Quote.**

> The instruments do not all reach the same bearer. Article 22's contestation runs to natural persons and the platform-work directive's rights to workers, while the Platform-to-Business Regulation's complaint-handling and representative standing run to business users. (716–718)

> Where platform workers or platform-dependent firms can organize, they can negotiate over how assignments are made, how deactivation is decided, and how ranking is governed, and can establish a standing channel through which disclosure becomes consequence. (738–740)

**Why it damages this lens.** The first passage is exact. The second blurs it. Firms do not collectively bargain over assignments and deactivation; they use trade associations, P2B representative actions, and (if the Omnibus fails) complaint-handling. The objects named are the directive's worker objects. Section 8 then does the firm case through P2B and the worker case through the directive. The wobble is one sentence, and it does not undo the split. A referee who has just been told the repertoires differ will notice the next page treating them as one organizing scene.

**Fix.** Keep workers on collective bargaining; move firms to the representative channel already named. Paste-ready text in Part 3.

## Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions

Highest-value only. Two clauses.

**§8, 802–803 (Finding 1).**

Replace:

> The same directive's rebuttable presumption of employment, where the platform controls the work, is a different instrument.

With:

> The same directive's rebuttable presumption of employment, which applies where facts indicating direction and control are found, is a different instrument.

**§7, 738–740 (Finding 2).**

Replace:

> Where platform workers or platform-dependent firms can organize, they can negotiate over how assignments are made, how deactivation is decided, and how ranking is governed, and can establish a standing channel through which disclosure becomes consequence.

With:

> Where platform workers can organize, they can negotiate over how assignments are made, how deactivation is decided, and how ranking is governed. Where platform-dependent firms can organize, the available channel is representative standing and complaint-handling, not the labor-law bargain, and the object is ranking and terms rather than assignment and deactivation.

## Verdict

**Accept-with-nits.**

Zero major. Zero minor. Two local nits (Art. 5 trigger wording; one §7 bearer sentence). The §0j cascade landed. The ten older closed items still hold on this lens.

**Single most important fix.** Line 802–803: say "facts indicating direction and control," not "where the platform controls the work."

The App Store payment gate shows node ≠ edge on a platform. Apple's fine, P2B's proposed repeal, and the platform-work directive's two instruments show high digital sovereignty beside low coordinative sovereignty, and the reverse pairing still arriving. WeChat / RuNet / CoopCycle / Mastodon / NYDC show that openness and control do not track standing. That is what a content referee is for, and it is on the page.
