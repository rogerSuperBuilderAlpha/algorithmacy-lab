Sage / *New Media & Society* 28(1), 423–447, DOI 10.1177/14614448241291137, fetched this
session, gives **Emilija Gagrčin**. HANDOFF §1 had “corrected” the other way. The use of

### S2. §8 opening asks Fratini and Hummel to operationalize a construct they do not — citations (primary-source miss)

Lines 771–772:

> Fratini et al. (2024), Hummel et al. (2021), and Schmuntzsch et al. (2025) operationalize
> organizational digital sovereignty as one capability…

Fratini produces four *national* models and is already used correctly at 259–261 (content
and citations agree). Hummel is a review of *data* sovereignty. Neither operationalizes
organizational digital sovereignty as one capability. Content treated this sentence as a
successful weld (scholars in subject position). Citations is right on the source-use; the
weld can stay with different subjects.

**Fix.** Leave Fratini at 259–261. Let Schmuntzsch (or Hartmann’s chapter, if the author
adds that entry) carry the organizational-capability claim. Do not add Hartmann without a
matching reference.

Paste-ready copy lives in `04_citations.md` Part 3.
