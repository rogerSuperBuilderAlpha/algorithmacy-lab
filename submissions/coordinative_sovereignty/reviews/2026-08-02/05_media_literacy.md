### Finding 1 (highest damage) — Kind-claim and DeVito-narrowing now pull apart — §2.3 / §4

**What the chapter claims.** §2.3 (144–148): "Two changes in kind mark the sequence… Speech to writing is finished… Writing to algorithmic mediation is under way, and section 4 argues that it is a change of the same order." §4 (364–371): oracy, literacy, and algorithmacy "name three coordination regimes… Each regime makes possible forms of authority the prior regime cannot instantiate, and in that infrastructural sense the transitions are changes in kind."

**What the novelty paragraph now says.** Lines 305–327: none of the three parts is new; their integration is not new; DeVito already holds awareness, strategic use, and revision across changing platforms; "What algorithmacy adds is the setting"; Gagričin is cited for *domain-specific constructs rather than a single general capacity*.

**The seam.** A change "of the same order" as speech→writing is an Ong-scale media-historical claim. Indexing a competence to a triadic setting, after conceding the parts to DeVito, is a Gagričin-scale *specification within a family*. Those are different magnitudes. A literacy referee who accepts the DeVito concession will ask why the chapter still needs the larger claim. Domain-specificity answers "is this a relabeling?" with "it is a named subdomain." It does not by itself answer "is this a change in kind of communicative medium?"

The defense is available in the chapter's own words and is not yet stated as the load-bearing pin: the kind-claim attaches to the **regime** (opaque, adaptive, interested mediation that constitutes coordination), not to the **parts of the competence**. The competence is practice-specific (Street); the regime is infrastructurally discontinuous (persistent/comparable document vs. continuously retuned mediator that scores a counterpart-relationship). One clarifying sentence after line 327, or tightening 367–371 so "infrastructural sense" carries that contrast against text, would close the seam. Without it, the DeVito concession looks like a quiet demotion of the media spine.

**Rank:** first. This is the only finding that could move a media-literate referee from accept to revise.

## Part 2 — Style / slop audit

Register-aware. The chapter remains cleaner than most drafts against house tics. Residual issues for this pass:

**1. Antithesis density (decorative contrasts).** Rough count of `, not` / `rather than` / `is not a/the/an` / related forms is high for ~11,800 words of body (~30 hits). Many are earned (functions not firms; innovation rather than dilution). A few in the media spine are decorative doubles. Example, lines 146–148:

> "Writing to algorithmic mediation is under way… Digital sovereignty sits inside the second era rather than opening a third: a compliance officer is a reader…"

The positive claim ("sits inside the second era") suffices; "rather than opening a third" restates it. Cut the pivot or keep one contrast per paragraph.

**2. Agentless passive where the agent is the point — §3.** Line 191: "its compliance is produced by the system's ordinary operation." Name the system as agent: "the system's ordinary operation produces its compliance."

**3. Metronomic punchy opener — §4 vignette.** Lines 346–347: "None of that is reading, because… and none of it is modeling…" Earned once; the double "none" is the metronome. Compress to one negation.

**Not slop — keep:** "She has read nothing; there was nothing to read" (340–341). "A compliance officer is a reader" (147–148). Oracy standing-to-speak sentences (100–103).

No self-narrating honesty phrases found. No first person. Em-dashes rare. Definitional triad still appears in abstract, §1, §6, Key Terms — acceptable for abstract + definition + glossary; §1 could compress (nit for copyeditor).

## Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions

**1. After §4 line 327 (or fold into 367–371) — pin kind to regime, novelty to setting:**

> The novelty against DeVito and Dogruel is therefore the index, not the skill-list. The change in kind claimed in section 2.3 attaches to the coordination regime — an opaque and adaptive mediator that constitutes the tie between counterparts — not to the inferential, translational, and temporal capacities taken one by one. Those capacities are continuous with algorithmic literacy. The regime they must work is not continuous with the persistent and comparable document.

**2. §2.1, lines 104–105 — relocate oral limits from medium to practice:**

> Within its range that sovereignty was real. Beyond its range it stopped. No practice available to an oral community fixed its law in a stable text, set today's ruling beside last year's, or carried a rule past living memory.

**3. §2.2, after line 111 — inoculate the affordance triad:**

> Persistence, portability, and comparability are potentials, not automatic effects of the written mark. Each governed only where institutions — the archive, the census, the periodical press — were built to exploit it.

**4. §3, line 191 — name the agent:**

> …and the system's ordinary operation produces its compliance.

**5. Optional, §2.2 Innis — use the actual axis (one sentence):**

> Innis (1951) distinguished time-biased media that favor durable, tradition-heavy authority from space-biased media that favor administration and reach; algorithmic mediation sits at the administrative extreme.

## Verdict

**Accept with nits** — leaning **minor** only if Finding 1 is left unaddressed before press.

**Single most important fix:** state explicitly that the Ong-scale change-in-kind attaches to the **coordination regime** (document vs. opaque adaptive triad-constituting mediator), while algorithmacy's novelty against DeVito is the **triadic index** of an already-documented skill cluster. The chapter already believes this; the page still lets a referee hear two different magnitudes as one claim.

**Biggest genuine strength:** the rebuilt §4 neighborhood — DeVito conceded, spectatorship deleted, counterpart made to work in the eBay/seller passages, Cotter & Reisdorf restoring the inequality gradient, Scribner & Cole / Street still pinning cognition off the table — plus §9's honest diffusion answer (compulsion and a compliance-teaching market). That is media/literacy scholarship doing its job inside a sovereignty chapter.

**The one thing only the author can supply:** a one-sentence decision on magnitude. Is "change of the same order as speech→writing" the claim to defend at press, with regime-level infrastructure as the warrant? Or should §2.3 soften to "a coordination discontinuity of that order is underway," leaving "change in kind" only at §4's infrastructural formulation? The panel cannot choose; the volume referee will notice if both phrasings stay unloaded against each other.
