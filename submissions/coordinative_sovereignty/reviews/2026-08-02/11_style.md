# Style review — Algorithmacy and Sovereignty

**Manuscript:** `chapter/chapter.md` (body through §10; ~11,889 words)
**Bar:** direct and clear; limit jargon, explain instead; no throat-clearing or meta commentary.
House invariants (`CLAUDE.md`): no first person; cut decorative antithesis; no self-narrating honesty;
say each point once; vary punchy openers.
**Standpoint:** prose only. No theory redesign. No new citations.

---

## Step 0 — Register

Impersonal APA chapter for an interdisciplinary digital-sovereignty volume. The register is already
correct (zero first person; named agents in active voice). What fails the bar is not voice but habit:
the prose announces its own moves, defines by contrast, and opens §5 on systems jargon before the
bypass question the reader needs. Judge against "state the claim; gloss the term; cut the frame."

### §4 Algorithmacy
Mostly clean. The eBay vignette teaches. Leave.

### §5 Diagnostic — densest failure zone
**§5.1** opens on `factors` / `constitutive` / `algorithmacy` before the reader has the bypass. Lead
with the plain question; gloss or postpone the systems vocabulary. Cut "The argument here needs only…"

**§5.2** heading defines by negation ("and what it is not"). "needs stating exactly" (L419) is
self-narration. "naming it strengthens the claim" (L463) performs rigor. "A caution belongs with the
test" (L476) is the honesty-tic family. "instructive, because the operation differs" (L436–437) is
throat-clearing. Decorative `rather than a finding of legality` (L468).

**§5.3** "here the argument turns on" (L485) — cut; Hirschman can open the sentence.

**§5.5** "Two qualifications belong with the result" (L572) — cut the frame; keep the two facts.

### §6 Definition
Highest local antithesis rate. "The neighbourhood is occupied…" / "The nearer neighbours mark…" are
placemarker openers. "Three clarifications guard the definition" is self-narrating care. Ananny
restated at L664 — callback. Decorative `rather than` at L629, L635, L644–645, L672 — keep at most
the relation-vs-actor contrast; cut the rest to positive claims.

### §7 Building standing
Clean for this lens. Leave.

### §8 Implications
Worst meta cluster in the chapter: "For scholars/managers/policymakers, the argument…" (L749, L784,
L803). Lead with the claim each audience gets. L772 `arriving rather than arrived` and L823
`qualification the argument needs rather than a counterexample` — state positively.

### §9 Future research
Democratization paragraph (L865–872) stacks three `rather than`s. Keep one contrast (compulsion vs
franchise extension); state the market-teaches-compliance point positively.

### §10 Conclusion
Low meta. Theory pile in L892–895 is earned recapitulation. Leave structure; no new frames.
