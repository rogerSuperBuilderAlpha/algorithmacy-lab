## Output format (every review file)

Write to the path assigned below. Structure:

1. **Title block** — lens name, manuscript title, venue, your standpoint.
2. **Step 0 — Register and bar** — confirm the register; state what you will judge hard.
3. **Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage** — section-aware. For each finding: claim, quote with
   line numbers, why it damages *your* lens, what would fix it. If a section is clean for your
   lens, say so in one sentence and move on.
4. **Part 2 — Seat-specific audit** — content: cases/facts/scope; argument: joints and license;
   flow: cohesion joins; citations: mechanical + load-bearing; style: slop types with quotes.
5. **Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions** — highest-value fixes only, ready to drop in. Style seat:
   worst three stretches only.
6. **Verdict** — major / minor / accept-with-nits. Single most important fix. Biggest genuine
   strength. The one thing only the author can supply (if any).
7. **VERIFY** — empirical or citation claims that need sourcing before press.
8. **Closed-item re-check** — for each of the ten items below, **Holds** / **Regresses** /
   **Not this seat** (one line each).

Be specific. Prefer fewer, sharper findings over a laundry list. Do not soft-pedal; do not invent
problems to fill the form.
