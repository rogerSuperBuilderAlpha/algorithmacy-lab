## Consensus spine (do these before press)

Rule: an item lands here only if **two or more seats** named it, or **one seat** found a
**primary-source miss**.

### S1. Rewrite the abstract so it matches the body — content + argument

The body argues a standing gap after disclosure and thin channels. The abstract still states
a see/predict/contest vacuum (ll. 5–6) and a “different competence” that “decides which
institutions to build” (ll. 10–13). §1 walks the vacuum back in the next two sentences
(ll. 30–33); §4 concedes DeVito (ll. 334–335); §7–§8 sort instrument *class*, they do not
deduce a build list.

**Fix.** First sentence: comply, obtain disclosure and a complaint channel, still lack
standing over the determination. Drop “usefully see.” Narrow “different competence” to the
triadic setting. The counterfactual *sorts* which class of institution is available.

### S2. Tighten the COM(2026) 178 demand clause — citations, primary-source miss

Ll. 692–694 hang “mixed evidence of demand” on the report. The fetched COM(2026) 178 says
mixed evidence *overall*, greater technical complexity, and **limited** demand. New-providers
and too-early clauses hold.

**Fix.** Say limited demand, or “mixed evidence overall, with limited demand.” Do not leave
“mixed evidence of demand.”
