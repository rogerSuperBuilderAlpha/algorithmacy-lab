## Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage

The graph is closed. Sections 1–4, 6, 9, and 10 are clean for this lens: every author–date resolves, Anderson initials hold, and the previously open locators (Repetto Abstract; Sekar & Siddiq Abstract; DeVito p. 339:3 with source spelling `crystalize`; Case C-264/23 para. 53; Pettit 2016 pages 47–68) still sit on the page. Three defects remain.

**1. COM(2026) 178 gloss compresses “mixed evidence” onto demand (L692–694).**  
Claim: “messaging interoperability has brought new providers into the market while judging it too early to extend the same obligation to social networking, on mixed evidence of demand.”  
The report (CELEX 52026DC0178, fetched this pass) does say new providers have entered messaging, including a European SME, and that it is “too early to extend the interoperability obligations currently imposed on messaging services to online social networking services.” It does not say the evidence on demand is mixed. The external study showed “mixed evidence and, in particular, … greater technical complexities … and that demand … was limited.” Demand is characterized as limited; mixedness belongs to the study as a whole. HANDOFF §0c claimed the gloss was “confirmed verbatim.” The two load-bearing clauses hold; the causal tag does not. A referee who opens the report will treat the clause as a mis-paraphrase of the only Commission document the chapter uses to show a regulator running the diagnostic.

**2. Curchod et al. (2020) quotation still has no locator (L342–343).**  
“an ‘implicit coalition between buyers and the platform owner’” is a direct quotation. The phrase is verbatim in the article abstract (and again in the discussion of the accepted manuscript). Repetto and Sekar & Siddiq were closed with `(Abstract)` for the same reason. This one was not. It is the only remaining unlocated quotation in the body, and it carries the §4 triad.

**3. European Commission (2025a) title is truncated past the clause the chapter needs (L1027–1028).**  
The entry reads *Proposal for a regulation as regards the simplification of the digital legislative framework (Digital Omnibus)* (COM(2025) 837 final). The official title on CELEX 52025PC0837 (fetched this pass) is the amending-and-repealing title: it names the repeal of Regulations (EU) 2018/1807, (EU) 2019/1150, (EU) 2022/868, and Directive (EU) 2019/1024. Those four identifiers are what license both the §7 DGA move and the §8 P2B repeal. APA 7 allows a short title; dropping the repealing clause from the only bibliographic handle for two load-bearing claims is the cut a copyeditor would restore.
