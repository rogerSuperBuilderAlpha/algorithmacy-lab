## Step 0 — Register and bar

The register is already correct: impersonal APA chapter, zero first person, named agents in active voice. The Annals first-person rule does not apply. What this seat judges hard is habit, not voice: decorative antithesis, self-narrating rigor, metronomic punchy openers, agentless passives where a named agent is available, unearned definitional contrasts, BrE residue, Key Terms parallelism, and recurrence of `the argument` / `this chapter` meta frames. The 2026-08-02 pass claimed those frames cleared (`the argument` = 0; `this chapter` held only as companion-scope and dating). This read checks whether the clears held and whether a replacement tic took their place.

## Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage

Abstract, §4 (eBay week), §7, and §10 are clean for this lens. Leave them.

### 1. §5.5 still performs its own caution (L581–588)

**Claim.** Cutting the old frame ("Two qualifications belong with the result") left the qualifications stacked in one sentence. The prose still announces how narrow the evidence is instead of stating the two facts and the residual claim.

**Quote.**
> The 2024 judgment postdates the last year of these data, the increase itself owes something to mobile search and to consolidation among the agencies, and the function-split predicts the absence of the erosion the rival reading expects rather than the increase that occurred, so this is narrower than a natural experiment and the judgment sets up a test a later study can run. (L582–585)

**Why it damages style.** Three hedges, one decorative `rather than`, and a self-grade ("narrower than a natural experiment") share a single sentence. A referee hears the chapter managing its own warrant. The next sentence already sorts the two readings; it does not need the pile in front of it.

**Fix.** Split the hedges. State what the function-split predicts. Drop "this is narrower than." Keep the later-study hook. See R1.

### 2. Key Terms reintroduce the meta the body cleared (L1296–1348)

**Claim.** The glossary puts `this chapter` back twice and defines four terms by `rather than`. Partial mediator also breaks the four-kind parallelism the body taught.

**Quotes.**
> In its regulatory strand this chapter treats it as a literacy-based form of governance (L1297)
> Oracy, literacy, and algorithmacy are the three this chapter tracks (L1313)
> What distinguishes it from adjacent literacy constructs is the triadic setting rather than the parts or their integration. (L1309–1310)
> rather than dealing with each other directly. (L1317)
> The test operates on a tie rather than on the mediator itself (L1325)
> rather than the mere absence of interference. (L1347–1348)

Necessary / contingent close on remaining power (voice / exit). Partial closes on a response ("The response is to contest…"). Reducible closes on routing around. The break is visible in a table the glossary is supposed to be.

**Why it damages style.** A volume glossary is the first place an editor checks parallelism and the last place meta commentary belongs. Two `this chapter` frames here undo the body's post-pass count for a reader who never reaches §8.

**Fix.** Define positively; cut the chapter-as-narrator; give Partial the same remaining-power close as its three neighbors. See R2.

### 3. "The argument" is gone; "here" and "needs care" took the slot

**Claim.** The named frames from August 2 are absent. A smaller self-location tic remains: the chapter still points at its own advancing, taking, and pressing.

**Quotes.**
> though it needs care, because Selznick used it for two things. (L195)
> The claim advanced here concerns coordination (L377)
> The plain counterfactual that follows is enough here (L404)
> a position taken here, and one a reader may decline while keeping the rest. (L642)
> The problem pressed here is structural. (L659)

**Why it damages style.** Same family as "needs stating" / "naming it strengthens the claim": the sentence comments on the chapter's care or location before the content lands. L404 is the mildest (a scope pointer after the companion is named). L195, L377, L642, and L659 are the ones that still perform rigor.

**Fix.** State the distinction, the claim, the priority, or the problem. Cut the warrant. See R3.

### 4. One leftover unearned contrast at the §3 open (L160)

**Claim.** The section's second sentence denies two motives nobody has yet offered.

**Quote.**
> The failure is not a shortfall of political will or a gap in drafting. It follows from what the coordinated actor is enrolled in. (L160–161)

**Why it damages style.** The positive sentence does the work. The `is not a` foil is decorative: no prior sentence accused the instruments of cowardice or bad drafting. Earned neighbors (Berlin at L56, Street at L382, Ostrom at L627) stay.

**Fix.** Delete the middle sentence.

Sections §1–§2, §5.1–§5.4, and §8 are otherwise inside the bar. The August 2 rewrites to those stretches held.

## Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions (worst three stretches)

Line numbers are current `chapter.md`.

### R1 — §5.5 qualifications pile (L581–588)

**Cut:**
> What the decade covers is the sequence from the 2015 abandonment of wide parity to the national enforcement of 2021. The 2024 judgment postdates the last year of these data, the increase itself owes something to mobile search and to consolidation among the agencies, and the function-split predicts the absence of the erosion the rival reading expects rather than the increase that occurred, so this is narrower than a natural experiment and the judgment sets up a test a later study can run. It is still enough to sort the two readings: an actor treating the agency as one leaveable thing would have read the direction of travel backwards, and a hotel that spent the decade trying to leave mistook the necessary function for the contingent one.

**With:**
> What the decade covers is the sequence from the 2015 abandonment of wide parity to the national enforcement of 2021. The 2024 judgment postdates the last year of these data, and the increase itself owes something to mobile search and to consolidation among the agencies. The function-split predicts that dependence would hold once only the contingent gate opened; it is silent on the rise that occurred. The judgment sets up a test a later study can run. The sequence is still enough to sort the two readings: an actor treating the agency as one leaveable thing would have read the direction of travel backwards, and a hotel that spent the decade trying to leave mistook the necessary function for the contingent one.

### R2 — Key Terms meta, antithesis, and Partial (L1296–1348)

**Cut / replace these entries only.**

**Digital sovereignty** — cut `this chapter treats it as`:
> **Digital sovereignty:** The capacity of a state, organization, or community to exercise control over its digital infrastructure, data, and technologies. In its regulatory strand it is a literacy-based form of governance, attained by reading and holding to account what a digital system discloses; its industrial strand works by jurisdiction and ownership.

**Algorithmacy** — last sentence, cut `rather than`:
> What distinguishes it from adjacent literacy constructs is the triadic setting.

**Communicative competence** — cut `this chapter tracks`:
> **Communicative competence:** A capacity for working the medium through which authority is exercised and contested. Oracy, literacy, and algorithmacy are the three such competences, each paired with a form of sovereignty and each unevenly held before it was common.

**Mediated triad** — cut the trailing contrast:
> **Mediated triad:** A coordination in which two parties are bound together through a third party that interprets both and commits outcomes neither controls.

**Bypass test** — last sentence, cut `rather than`:
> The test operates on a tie, which distinguishes it from tests that ask whether a mediator could be removed or replaced.

**Partial mediator** — close on remaining power, matching the neighbors:
> **Partial mediator:** A mediator that keeps a constitutive role under the open tie while losing part of its integration: real integrating work alongside a bypassable gate. Exit is available against the bypassable part; only voice remains against the integrating one.

**Non-domination** — keep the earned foil in the form the body already uses (L604–605):
> **Non-domination:** The republican conception of freedom as the absence of another's arbitrary power, a stronger condition than the absence of interference. A coordinated actor is dominated to the degree the mediator's power over it is unconstrained by rules the actor can contest.

## Verdict

**Accept-with-nits.** The August 2 style pass held on the tokens it named. What remains is residue: one overloaded caution in §5.5, a glossary that put `this chapter` back, and a four-instance `here` / `needs care` tic. No section has fallen back to the pre-pass meta cluster. No first person. No `computable`.

**Single most important fix:** R1, then R2. A referee who reads for prose will trip on L582–585; a volume editor who reads the glossary will trip on the two `this chapter`s.

**Biggest genuine strength:** the concrete openings and the eBay / OTA / App Store worked cases are still intact and still do the teaching. Do not decorate them.

**The one thing only the author can supply:** none for this seat.
