effective voice inside the mediation, rather than independence from it or control over it.

## Relation to the rest of the lab

Coordinative sovereignty is the political form of algorithmacy. Algorithmacy is the competence
to read that one is coordinated through a system; coordinative sovereignty is the standing to
act on it.
