## What it argues

Abstract-only card; the full text was not read. Per the abstract, Gambino, Fox and Ratan say the computers-are-social-actors framework (CASA), derived from the media equation, explains how people communicate with media and machines that show social potential. Many studies have challenged CASA, yet it has not been revised. They argue it must be expanded because people, technologies and the ways people use them have changed. Where CASA holds that humans mindlessly apply human-human social scripts to media agents, the authors argue that humans may develop and apply human-media social scripts. They say the extension explains earlier dissonant findings and bears on human-machine communication, HCI, human-robot and human-agent interaction, AI and computer-mediated communication.
