## What it argues

Abstract card: I read the OpenAlex abstract (an editor's headnote), not the essay. It says Mori, a robotics professor at the Tokyo Institute of Technology, wrote an essay about 40 years earlier in which he "hypothesized that a person's response to a humanlike robot would abruptly shift from empathy to revulsion as it approached, but failed to attain, a lifelike appearance", the uncanny valley. The essay first appeared in the Japanese journal *Energy* in 1970. This 2012 item is the first publication of an English translation "authorized and reviewed by Mori". Crossref lists three authors (Mori, MacDorman, Kageki). The abstract says nothing about motion, animation or characters beyond "computer-graphics animation" as one area researchers explored.
