# Silvio (2010), Animation: The new performance?

## What it argues

This card rests on the Crossref abstract only; I read no text of the article.

Silvio sets animation beside performance as a trope for human action. Her abstract says that from the 1950s
to the 1990s "the trope of performance was elaborated across a range of academic disciplines", and that now,
"as animation is being remediated through digital media", scholars and members of online communities "are
beginning to use animation as a trope for human action on/in the world". She gathers recent scholarship "in
order to outline a general animation model". She first presents "some of the characteristics of animation
that allow it to draw connections between social, technological, and psychic structures". She then examines
"some of the ways that the models of animation and performance interact in contemporary subcultural
practices". The record lists the keywords animation, performance, remediation, media ideologies and
techno-cultures.

The abstract names no projection/introjection contrast, no "numerical disjunct" and no "structuring trope".
Those come to the paper only through L. Stark (2024).
