## What it argues

The abstract says the authors wrote an earlier article, "The Myth of Persistence of Vision", in the *Journal of the University Film Association*, fall 1978 (credited to "Anderson and Fisher"). In it they "offered a considerable volume of evidence" that persistence of vision was "an inaccurate and inadequate explanation" for the apparent motion in a motion picture. They thought the article had laid the matter to rest and expected never to hear the term again outside a historical context. The abstract stops there. It does not say what the 1993 revisit finds or what the authors offer in place of persistence of vision.
