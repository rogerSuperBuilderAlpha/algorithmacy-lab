## Abstract (~200 words)

Problem (agents move the finish line; conversion-era design fails); setting (opaque, co-optive,
triadic intermediary); move (LLM interface as animation); model (three competencies select animator's
techniques: staging and labelled sources, key poses with provisional in-betweens, anticipated change
between moves; selection and placement rules); propositions (seven, testable); contribution and limit
(no test; competencies proposed, not validated).
