## 4. What animators know (~1,500) — *Perception; HF and design*

- **4.1 Principles, keys and other hands** — Lasseter: eleven principles as codified "rules of the
  trade"; pose-to-pose vs straight-ahead; the timing chart; the machine in-betweener that overshoots
  and the extra keys that lock it; staging; anticipation (what, not why); change between moves.
  Thomas & Johnston only as quoted.
- **4.2 Craft carried into interfaces** — Chang & Ungar (interpretation of change; offloading to
  perception; §4 limits; remote actions); Thomas & Calder (indirect change; telltale; liking without
  performance); Gonzalez (one change per frame; decision accuracy); Dragicevic (one principle tested;
  transfer warning).
- **4.3 Craft carried into robots, chatbots and LLMs** — Takayama (forethought: sureness up, accuracy
  flat); Schulz (27 studies; perception outcomes; timing and staging untested); Ribeiro & Paiva and
  Lewis (life-likeness; puppetry against Disney; the calibrating robot read as a shrug); Pinhanez
  (anticipation before a charge; bunraku); Kurian (Disney scaffolds through child development; persona
  craft against over-reliance); Zhang & Davis (keyframes with LLM in-betweens).
- **4.4 The method warrant** — May, Dean & Barnard: craft transfers through an account of the
  receiver; their limits; this paper's account is algorithmacy.
