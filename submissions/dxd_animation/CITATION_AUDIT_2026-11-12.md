## Method

1. **Lists.** 38 reference entries; every in-text citation has an entry and every entry is cited.
   Jachimowicz et al. (2019) appears only inside a flag note, so it has no entry.
2. **Metadata.** Crossref record (`api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>`) for each DOI; DataCite for the two
   arXiv DOIs; the arXiv API record for Vaithilingam et al.; the Open Library edition record for
   Thomas & Johnston; the author's own files for Hunt 2026a–d.
3. **Claims.** Each quotation, number and page searched in the source's text layer (or, for pointers,
   in the lab card that holds the full-text read) and read in context. Earlier today and yesterday the
   lead also re-found every locus the draft uses; this pass repeated the check for the author's own
   texts, the pointer cards and the numbers not yet checked.
4. **Retraction.** Crossref's `updates:` filter for the eleven load-bearing sources (Lasseter; L. Stark;
   Takayama et al.; Chang & Ungar; Thomas & Calder; Gonzalez; Kahneman & Klein; Hoerl; Pinhanez;
   Szafir et al.; Zhang & Davis): no retraction or correction notice for any.
