## The common build pipeline

Each arm walks the same six stages. The per-arm sections below name only what is specific to it.

1. **Verify the call in a browser.** Eight arms rest on a blocked or aggregator-only page: *ISJ*,
   *I&O* Hype, *I&O* Assemblages, the *Organization Science* Agency INFORMS PDF, *Personnel
   Psychology* (Wiley), the *JITCAR* and *SIJ* author instructions, the EGOS short-paper PDF, and the
   AlgorLit date (page says 15 Nov, calendar link says 15 Oct). Record each read in the arm's
   `cfp/README.md` with the date, and fix length, format and prior-publication rules there.
2. **Settle the rulings** above that touch the arm.
3. **Card the sources** the FLAGS name as missing or abstract-depth, in
   `lima_pdw/literature/cards/` or the arm's own `literature/`, at full-text depth before any
   quotation. The OT arm's `CITATION_DEPTH.md` rule applies: quotable only at full text.
4. **Draft to the outline.** Abstracts are finalized in `ABSTRACT.md`; papers go in a new
   `PAPER.md`, mirroring [`ecis2027_algorithmacy_is/`](ecis2027_algorithmacy_is/) (PAPER.md,
   `figures/` with a generating script). Anything Claude writes is marked as Claude's draft.
5. **Audit.** A tiered citation audit against the cards, a claim check sentence by sentence, the
   FLOW_AND_COHESION self-check, and an overlap check against the audit's split table and the OT
   manuscript. Prose findings are recorded, not applied; the author rules.
6. **Hand off and record.** The author reads aloud and submits. On submission, update the arm's
   README status, `CALENDAR.md`, `README.md` and `NOW.md`, and freeze the sent text as
   `*_SUBMITTED.md`.
