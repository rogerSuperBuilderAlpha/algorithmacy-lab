## What it argues
Two of Disney's 'Nine Old Men' set out the studio's twelve principles of animation and the working methods behind characters that audiences believe in.

Key claims:
- Twelve principles (e.g. squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, timing, appeal) make drawn characters feel alive.
- Believability comes from the audience's emotional reading, not literal realism.
