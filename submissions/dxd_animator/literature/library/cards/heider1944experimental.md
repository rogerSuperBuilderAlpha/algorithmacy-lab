## What it argues
Viewers shown a short film of moving triangles and a circle described it as a story of characters with motives, showing that people read agency and intent into simple motion.

Key claims:
- People attribute intentions and personality to geometric shapes based on movement.
- Perceived causality and character come from patterns of motion, not appearance.

## Relation to the argument
The cleanest evidence for the triad: the viewer sees a 'character' that an 'animator' produced from very little. Explains why users read a chat agent as someone.
