## What it argues
A three-factor theory of when people anthropomorphise: when human-like knowledge is accessible (elicited agent knowledge), when they want to understand and predict (effectance), and when they want social connection (sociality).

Key claims:
- Anthropomorphism rises with effectance and sociality motivation.
- It is a predictable inference, not a mistake of the naive.

## Relation to the argument
Ties pillar 1 (cognitive, emotional human) to pillar 3: the user will build a character out of the agent, so the designer should treat that as an animator would.
