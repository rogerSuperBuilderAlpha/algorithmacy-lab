## What it argues
Lasseter, then at Pixar, explains the traditional principles of hand-drawn animation (squash and stretch, timing, anticipation, staging, follow-through, overlapping action, slow in and out, arcs, exaggeration, secondary action, appeal) and shows how they apply to 3D computer animation.

Key claims:
- Principles developed for hand-drawn animation carry over to computer animation.
- Timing and anticipation are what make motion readable and believable.

## Relation to the argument
The most direct bridge from the animator's craft to computing. A candidate toolkit for pillar 3.
