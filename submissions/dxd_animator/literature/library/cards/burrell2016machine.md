## What it argues
Burrell distinguishes three forms of opacity in machine-learning systems: intentional corporate secrecy, technical illiteracy, and opacity that comes from the scale and nature of the models themselves.

Key claims:
- Opacity has three different sources with different remedies.
- The third form persists even for experts.

## Relation to the argument
Defines 'opaque' in pillar 2. The third form means DXD cannot design opacity away; it has to design with it, as animators do with missing frames.
