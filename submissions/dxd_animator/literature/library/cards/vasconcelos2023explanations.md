## What it argues
Five studies (N = 731) showing that explanations reduce overreliance on AI when they lower the cost of checking the AI's answer, framed as a cost-benefit choice by the user.

Key claims:
- Overreliance is a strategic choice about whether to engage with the task.
- Explanations that make verification cheaper reduce overreliance.

## Relation to the argument
Gives DXD a lever: design so the decision is cheap for the human to check, rather than asking the human to trust.
