## What it argues
Experiments on three datasets testing whether AI explanations improve joint human-AI decision accuracy; explanations raised acceptance of AI advice whether or not it was correct.

Key claims:
- Explanations did not reliably make the human-AI team better than the better of the two alone.
- Explanations increased reliance, including on wrong recommendations.

## Relation to the argument
A warning for DXD: making the agent's reasoning more visible does not by itself help the user decide well.
