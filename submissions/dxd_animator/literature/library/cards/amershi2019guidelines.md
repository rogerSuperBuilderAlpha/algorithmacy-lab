## What it argues
Eighteen design guidelines for human-AI interaction, built from over 150 recommendations and tested with 49 practitioners on 20 AI products.

Key claims:
- Guidelines cover four phases: initially, during interaction, when wrong, and over time.
- Practitioners could apply them and found frequent violations in current products.

## Relation to the argument
The standard checklist that DXD extends. Most guidelines are about the AI's behaviour; DXD asks what they imply for the user's decision path.
