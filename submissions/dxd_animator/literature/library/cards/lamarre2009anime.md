## What it argues
Lamarre reads Japanese limited animation through its technical apparatus, especially the multiplanar image and the gaps between layers, as a way of thinking about technology.

Key claims:
- Limited animation works with, not against, its missing movement.
- The layered, 'open' image creates a different relation to the viewer than cinematic depth.

## Relation to the argument
Limited animation is the direct analogue of an LLM's missing frames: a style that makes gaps part of the experience. A source for pillar 3 beyond Disney realism.
