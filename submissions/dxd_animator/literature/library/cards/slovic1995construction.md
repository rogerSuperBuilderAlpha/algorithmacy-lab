## What it argues
Slovic reviews evidence that preferences are often built on the spot during elicitation rather than read out from a stable store.

Key claims:
- Preference reversals show that how a question is asked changes the answer.
- Preferences are constructed in the process of choosing.

## Relation to the argument
If preferences are constructed, the experience of deciding shapes the decision. This is the core reason the experience, not the funnel, is the design object.
