## What it argues
The authorised English translation of Mori's 1970 essay proposing that affinity for a human-like robot rises with likeness, then drops sharply near full likeness before rising again.

Key claims:
- Near-human likeness can produce unease.
- Movement amplifies the effect.

## Relation to the argument
Suggests that the more an agent tries to seem human the more it risks unease; animators often choose stylisation instead, which is a design option for DXD.
