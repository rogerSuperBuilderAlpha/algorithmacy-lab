## What it argues
Gillespie shows how companies such as YouTube use the word 'platform' to speak to users, advertisers and regulators at once, presenting themselves as neutral while serving several parties.

Key claims:
- 'Platform' is a discursive move that hides competing interests.
- Intermediaries position themselves to several audiences at once.

## Relation to the argument
Grounds 'triadic' in pillar 2: the user and the shop are never alone; the platform speaks to both.
