## What it argues
A textbook-theory of animation as a distinct art, covering definitions, orthodox and experimental animation, narrative strategies, and representation.

Key claims:
- Animation is not a sub-genre of film but its own form with its own methods.
- Orthodox and experimental animation sit on a continuum.
