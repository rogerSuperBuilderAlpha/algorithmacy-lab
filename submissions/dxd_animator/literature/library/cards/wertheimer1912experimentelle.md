## What it argues
Wertheimer's experiments on apparent movement between two briefly shown stationary lines, including the 'pure' (phi) motion seen without a moving object; a founding text of Gestalt psychology.

Key claims:
- Seen motion is given directly, not inferred from two positions.
- Apparent motion can be seen with no object seen to move (phi).

## Relation to the argument
The perceptual basis of 'missing frames': motion is constructed by the viewer. Read together with Anderson & Anderson (1993) on what it does and does not explain about film.
