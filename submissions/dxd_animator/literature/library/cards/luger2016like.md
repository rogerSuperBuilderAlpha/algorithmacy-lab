## What it argues
Interviews with 14 regular users of conversational agents (e.g. Siri, Google Now) showing a gap between expectation and experience, and how users adjust what they ask.

Key claims:
- Users' expectations were set high and then not met.
- Users limited themselves to tasks they had learned would work.

## Relation to the argument
Early evidence of the 'embarrassingly short' chat interface. Users shrink their goals to fit the agent, which is the opposite of DXD.
