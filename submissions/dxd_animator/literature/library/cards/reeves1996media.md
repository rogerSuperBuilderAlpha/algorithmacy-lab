## What it argues
Experiments showing that people apply social rules (politeness, flattery, gender stereotypes) to computers and media, even when they know they are machines.

Key claims:
- Social responses to media are automatic.
- Media need few cues to trigger them.

## Relation to the argument
The user engages the agent as a social character (pillar 1) produced by someone else (pillar 3's triad).
