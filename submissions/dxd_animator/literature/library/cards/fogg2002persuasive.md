## What it argues
Fogg introduces 'captology', the study of computers as persuasive technologies that change attitudes or behaviour, as tools, media and social actors.

Key claims:
- Computers persuade in three roles: tool, medium, social actor.
- Persuasion is a design goal that can be studied and engineered.

## Relation to the argument
The intellectual root of conversion-rate design. DXD shifts the goal from persuading the user toward an end to helping the user reach their own decision.
