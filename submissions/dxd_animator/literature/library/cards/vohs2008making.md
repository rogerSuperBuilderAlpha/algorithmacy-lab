## What it argues
Laboratory and field studies showing that making many choices was followed by poorer self-control on later tasks, read by the authors as a limited-resource effect.

Key claims:
- Making choices, more than merely thinking about options, was followed by reduced self-regulation.
- The authors interpret this as depletion of a limited resource.

## Relation to the argument
Supports the 'biological, emotional' human in pillar 1: deciding costs the user something, so surfacing only the decisions that matter is a design duty. Note that ego-depletion findings have faced replication challenges since 2016; cite with care.
