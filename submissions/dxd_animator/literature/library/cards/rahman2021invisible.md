## What it argues
An ethnography of an online labour platform showing how an opaque, changing rating algorithm created an 'invisible cage' and how workers reacted.

Key claims:
- Opaque evaluation makes users guess at the rules and change behaviour in response.
- Opacity can be a means of control.

## Relation to the argument
Shows opacity and cooption working together on human users; DXD must account for how users adapt to a platform they cannot see into.
