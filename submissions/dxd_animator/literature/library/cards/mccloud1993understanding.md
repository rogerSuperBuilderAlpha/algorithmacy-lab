## What it argues
McCloud explains comics in comics form, including 'closure': the reader fills the gap between panels (the gutter) and so takes part in making the action.

Key claims:
- Readers complete what is not shown between panels.
- Simplified, iconic figures invite readers to identify with them.

## Relation to the argument
Closure is the 'missing frames' of pillar 3 in another medium: the user completes the gap. DXD can design the gap rather than hide it.
