## What it argues
The Andersons argue that 'persistence of vision' is a myth in film theory and that seen film motion is better explained by short-range apparent motion, and that the passive viewer implied by the myth should give way to an active, meaning-seeking one.

Key claims:
- Persistence of vision does not explain film motion.
- The 'phi phenomenon' is also often misapplied to film; short-range apparent motion fits better.
- Viewers engage film actively.
