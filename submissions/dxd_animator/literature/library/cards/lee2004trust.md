## What it argues
A review of trust in automation that defines trust as an attitude toward an agent under uncertainty and argues that design should aim for appropriate reliance, not more trust.

Key claims:
- Misuse and disuse follow when trust is out of step with an automation's actual capability.
- Display design can calibrate trust.

## Relation to the argument
Pillar 1's emotional human: users rely on agents through trust, and DXD should calibrate it, not maximise it.
