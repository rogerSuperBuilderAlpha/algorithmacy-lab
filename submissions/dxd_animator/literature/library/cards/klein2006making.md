## What it argues
Klein, Moon and Hoffman review the main traditions of research on sensemaking and set out how they differ from related ideas such as situation awareness and creativity.

Key claims:
- Sensemaking is a motivated, continuous effort to understand connections in order to anticipate and act.
- It is distinct from (though related to) situation awareness and comprehension.
