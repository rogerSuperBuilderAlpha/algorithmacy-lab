## What it argues
An automated crawl of about 11,000 shopping websites that found 1,818 dark-pattern instances across 15 types and 7 categories, and flagged third parties that sell such patterns as services.

Key claims:
- Dark patterns are common on shopping sites, especially popular ones.
- Some are supplied by third-party vendors, not built by the site.

## Relation to the argument
Shows the raincoat-funnel problem at scale and that a third party often sits inside the interface, which ties pillar 1 to pillar 2 (the triadic platform).
