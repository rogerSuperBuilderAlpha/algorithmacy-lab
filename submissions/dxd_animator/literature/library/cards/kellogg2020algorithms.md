## What it argues
A review of algorithmic control at work organised as the 6 Rs: restricting, recommending, recording, rating, replacing and rewarding, plus how workers resist.

Key claims:
- Algorithmic control is more comprehensive, instantaneous, interactive and opaque than earlier control.
- Workers respond with 'algoactivism'.

## Relation to the argument
Evidence that platforms co-opt the people who use them. Pillar 2's 'cooptive' applies to users as well as workers.
