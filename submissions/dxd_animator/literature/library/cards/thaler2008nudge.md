## What it argues
Thaler and Sunstein argue that choice architects can steer people toward better choices, by their own lights, without removing options ('libertarian paternalism'); defaults are the main example.

Key claims:
- There is no neutral design of a choice.
- Nudges should be easy and cheap to avoid.

## Relation to the argument
The best-known statement that design shapes decisions. DXD takes the premise and changes the goal: not steering toward a chosen end but helping the user find theirs.
