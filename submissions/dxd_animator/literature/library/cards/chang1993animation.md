## What it argues
Chang and Ungar argue that user interfaces should borrow from cartoon animation (solidity, exaggeration, reinforcement) to make objects on screen feel real, illustrated with the Self programming environment.

Key claims:
- Cartoon techniques make on-screen objects easier to perceive and follow.
- Realism of feeling matters more than physical accuracy.

## Relation to the argument
Prior art for pillar 3 inside HCI. DXD extends it from animating objects to animating a decision path.
