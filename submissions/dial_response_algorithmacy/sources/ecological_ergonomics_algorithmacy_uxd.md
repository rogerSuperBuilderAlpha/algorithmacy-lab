Algorithmacy is confronting its Gutenberg inflection point: a historical rupture marked by the sudden, uncoordinated overproduction of synthetic media running on primitive, cognitively hostile interfaces. Just as the civilizational transition from oracy to literacy was choked for over a millennium by ergonomic interface failures—most notoriously *scriptio continua*, which overwhelmed human working memory, mandated continuous oral subvocalization, and confined textual engagement to a sacerdotal scribal caste—contemporary interaction with artificial intelligence is crippled by the linear prompt scroll, the "transparency paradox," and unstructured floods of synthetic artifacts. Modern interfaces treat foundation models as conversational interlocutors rather than high-dimensional computational substrates, forcing users to arbitrate stochastic token streams through brute-force ocular scanning and retrospective error checking.

To navigate the cognitive fallout of this abundance, contemporary human-computer interaction (HCI) and user experience design (UXD) discourses have retreated into the romanticized mystification of "taste" and "judgment". Promoted by technology luminaries and design theorists as the ultimate, unassailable moats of human distinctiveness in an automated economy, these concepts operate as sociotechnical evasions. Deconstructed through Pierre Bourdieu's sociology of culture, "taste" functions as an unexamined proxy for *habitus*, class exposure, and institutional pedigree, converting a structural defect in interface legibility into an individualized virtue of connoisseurship. Concurrently, "judgment" acts as a cognitive dumping ground: an uncompensated, high-load epistemic verification tax that offloads the burden of policing hallucinated, probabilistically averaged outputs onto human working memory.

Resolving this crisis requires abandoning calls for individual aesthetic vigilance and executing an architectural transformation equivalent to the graphic and spatial revolutions of early literacy. Scribes and early typographers liberated human cognition not by demanding greater interpretive stamina, but by inventing word separation, syntactical punctuation (*distinctiones*), and the random-access apparatus of the bound codex. To achieve an equivalent liberation, Algorithmacy UXD 2.0 must construct "Algorithmic White Space". Grounded in the neuronal recycling of visual cortex, Wolfe's Guided Search architecture of pre-attentive spatial vision, Gibsonian direct ecological affordances, and O'Regan's sensorimotor contingencies, this paradigm replaces conversational prompt-and-response feeds with spatialized latent canvases, continuous parametric controls, and pre-attentive state signaling.

## The Ergonomic Tax of Scriptio Continua and the Cognitive Neuroscience of Early Literacy

The historical record of writing reveals that physical inscription long predated visual legibility. Throughout classical Greco-Roman antiquity, written texts were produced in *scriptio continua*—continuous, unbroken streams of upper-case letters devoid of word spacing, punctuation, accents, or paragraph breaks, inscribed upon continuous papyrus rolls. This graphic format was not an incidental aesthetic convention but the physical manifestation of an interface architecture that conceptualized writing solely as a passive acoustic recording of speech. Ancient literacy was an oral-auditory performance; the written scroll operated merely as a mnemonic script for vocalization.

```
SCRIPTIO CONTINUA OCULOMOTOR AND COGNITIVE PIPELINE
[Continuous Letter Ribbon]
       │ (Parafoveal targeting blinded; no word length cues)
       ▼
[Serial Ocular Saccades] (Hyper-short steps, elevated fixation durations)
       │ (Parallel visual orthographic parsing fails)
       ▼
[Compulsory Oral Subvocalization] (Phonological loop activation via motor speech)
       │ (Auditory feedback loop required for segmentation)
       ▼
[Acoustic Processing Bottleneck] (Wernicke's area & auditory cortex resolve syntax)
       │ (Working memory saturated with acoustic buffers)
       ▼
[Sacerdotal Scribal Monopoly] (Textual access restricted to elite interpretive caste)
```

The ergonomic friction imposed by *scriptio continua* severely taxed human neurobiology. Keith Rayner's foundational eye-movement psychophysics demonstrates that fluent, efficient reading relies on an asymmetric perceptual span extending 3 to 4 character spaces to the left of fixation and 14 to 15 character spaces to the right in left-to-right alphabetic orthographies. Visual reading is executed via ballistic saccades averaging 7 to 9 letter spaces, interspersed with fixations lasting 200 to 250 milliseconds. The primary driver of saccadic targeting is the parafoveal preview benefit: while the fovea decodes word N, the parafovea extracts spatial boundary markers and length metrics for word N+1, allowing the oculomotor system to land the subsequent saccade cleanly near the center of the next lexical unit.

When spaces are eliminated, eye-tracking paradigms show that reading velocity plummets by 30% to 50%. Deprived of inter-word boundaries, parafoveal vision cannot guide the eye to optimal landing sites. Saccades become erratic, fixation durations swell, and regressive saccades—normally comprising 10% to 15% of eye movements in skilled readers—escalate dramatically to resolve systemic lexical ambiguities.

| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **Oculomotor Targeting Mechanics** | Serial character-by-character saccades; absence of parafoveal word-length cues; elevated fixation durations. | Ballistic saccades (7–9 characters); parafoveal targeting guided by blank margins and word boundaries. |
| **Neurofunctional Route** | Dorsal attentional stream into Broca's area, motor cortex, and Wernicke's auditory processing loop. | Ventral occipitotemporal stream directly into the Visual Word Form Area (VWFA); bypasses motor speech. |
| **Working Memory Burden** | Heavy phonological loop saturation; short-term buffers consumed by acoustic segment reconstruction. | Cognitive offload onto spatial page layout; visual parsing operating in parallel without phonological decay. |
| **Navigational Topology** | Linear, unindexed scroll; continuous temporal unrolling; high motor latency for information retrieval. | Spatial random access via 2D codex leaves, pagination, chapter headings, running headers, and indices. |
| **Sociotechnical Access Model** | Monastic and imperial scribal monopolies; reading as an esoteric, performative, elite virtue. | Democratized, silent, private comprehension; distributed scholarship and autonomous epistemic inquiry. |

This oculomotor breakdown reverberates throughout visual neuroscience. Stanislas Dehaene's neuronal recycling hypothesis demonstrates that cultural acquisitions do not build bespoke cerebral circuitry; rather, they repurpose pre-existing, phylogenetically older cortical mechanisms. Reading recycles the Visual Word Form Area (VWFA), located in the left lateral fusiform gyrus of the ventral occipitotemporal cortex. In literate individuals, the VWFA performs ultra-fast, parallel visual recognition of letter combinations and whole words within 150 to 200 milliseconds of fixation. However, this parallel recognition engine requires discrete visual boundaries.

Without visual spacing, the ventral stream fails to isolate orthographic entities, kicking processing out of parallel visual pathways into high-load, serial parieto-frontal attentional networks and mandatory auditory loops. Ancient readers had to sound out letter clusters phonetically, relying on auditory feedback to decode syntactic phrases. Silent, introspective comprehension was extraordinarily rare, not due to cognitive deficiency, but because the interface imposed a punishing working-memory bottleneck.

The dismantling of this cognitive bottleneck was achieved through systematic information architecture. In the late 7th and 8th centuries, Insular scribes in Ireland and Northumbria, confronting Latin as a foreign liturgical tongue without native phonological intuitions, began introducing regular white spaces between words to visually untangle grammatical units. This aerated text was integrated into the Carolingian minuscule standard and paired with the punctuation system of *distinctiones*. Codified by Isidore of Seville in his *Etymologiae*, this punctuation system utilized a single dot (*punctus*) placed at three distinct physical elevations:

- *Subdistinctio* (a baseline dot) marking a minor grammatical pause (the comma);
- *Media distinctio* (a mid-height dot) marking a major medial colon pause where the local statement is grammatically complete but the conceptual thought remains unfinished;
- *Distinctio* (a high dot) signaling the completion of the full rhetorical and conceptual period (*periodus*).

Concurrently, the adoption of the bound parchment codex superseded the continuous papyrus scroll. By segmenting text into discrete, two-dimensional pages, scribes established a spatial coordinate system. The structural apparatus of the codex—running headers, leaf numbers, capitula divisions, marginal concordances, and alphabetical indices—transformed reading from an unindexed temporal progression into a random-access visual search landscape. These ergonomic interventions externalized cognitive decoding onto graphic space, allowing the human visual system to parse meaning silently, rapidly, and autonomously.

## The Triadic Structure of Algorithmacy and the Pathology of the Generative Scroll

The historical transition from oracy to literacy finds its conceptual successor in the shift toward algorithmacy. Oracy required the immediate, dyadic, co-located coordination of speaker and listener. Literacy introduced an inanimate symbolic medium that dissociated author from reader across time and space, establishing an informational substrate that conveyed meaning without autonomously acting upon it. Algorithmacy, by contrast, constitutes a triadic coordination form: human agents coordinate with other agents, or execute complex tasks, through an algorithmic third party that actively processes, transforms, optimizes, and generates information according to autonomous statistical criteria.

```
      ▲                      │

      │                      ▼

      ▲                      │
      │ (Telemetry / Audits) │ (Autonomous Generation / Mediation)
      │                      ▼
[Task Environment, Systemic Outcome, Secondary Human Stakeholder]
```

Despite the triadic complexity of this coordination, the dominant interface model of generative AI remains a regression to the primitive mechanics of the scroll. The single-input prompt window paired with a vertically scrolling, conversational chat feed is the *scriptio continua* of our era. Users are inundated with unbroken ribbons of synthetic tokens, uncurated galleries of image variants, and dense Explainable AI (XAI) feature-attribution trees. The interface offers no spatial map of the model's internal representations, forcing the operator to read every generated token serially to catch catastrophic hallucinations or structural errors.

This cognitive hostility drives the "transparency paradox" in human-AI interaction. While conventional HCI wisdom posits that increasing algorithmic transparency linearly builds trust and improves decision quality, empirical deployments reveal an inverted-U relationship. Dumping granular technical explanations—such as high-dimensional saliency heatmaps, attention token weights, or verbose linguistic chains of thought—floods the visual and semantic buffers. Rather than fostering calibrated trust, excessive transparency triggers slow, analytical System 2 cognitive processing that magnifies sensitivity to system imperfections while exhausting mental capacity.

The mathematical dynamics of this collapse are formalized by the Human Identity and Autonomy Gap (HIAG) framework, which models human autonomy A_t as a continuous stochastic process governed by information-induced cognitive load:

dA_t = μ(I_t) A_t dt + σ_A A_t dW_t^A

Here, I_t ∈ [0, I_max] represents the instantaneous rate of algorithmic information presentation (e.g., token streaming speed, rationale density), σ_A > 0 defines autonomy volatility, and W_t^A is a standard Brownian motion capturing environmental noise. The drift function μ(I_t) models the non-linear cognitive impact of information processing:

μ(I) = μ_0 − β I − γ I²

where μ_0 represents baseline cognitive stamina, β is the direct marginal cost of information acquisition, and γ is the acceleration coefficient of metacognitive exhaustion. This formulation demonstrates the existence of a critical threshold I*, beyond which information volume drives autonomy drift into negative territory:

I* = (−β + √(β² + 4γμ_0)) / 2γ

When an interface continuously exposes the user to information rates I_t > I*, the human agent enters a regime of systemic cognitive drain. Autonomy A_t decays inexorably toward an absorbing cognitive capacity boundary B(WM):

B(WM) = B_0 − β_WM · WM

where WM denotes individual working-memory capacity. Once A_t ≤ B(WM), executive control collapses. The operator either falls into uncritical automation bias (passively accepting erroneous AI proposals) or abandons the system in frustration.

This depletion is propelled by recursive metacognitive self-evaluation. Confronted with raw token dumps and post-hoc explanations, the human operator must continuously deliberate: *"Is this hallucinated? Did the model fail to parse my prompt constraints, or is its reasoning superior to my own?"* This internal friction consumes working-memory buffers in precisely the same manner that vocalizing unspaced Latin syllables paralyzed the readers of antiquity. Current interfaces offload computational friction directly onto human working memory.

## Critical Deconstruction of Aesthetic Mystification in UX Design

Rather than addressing these systemic human factors failures, contemporary design discourse has embraced an ideological defense mechanism, elevating "taste" and "judgment" into sacred, irreproducible human attributes. Design evangelists and software executives assert that because generative models have commoditized production, professional value now hinges exclusively on these two sensibilities. Under rigorous sociological and cognitive examination, this rhetoric reveals itself as an obfuscation of defective tooling.

### Taste as Sociotechnical Evasion: The Institutional Moat

Within the tech industry, "taste" is commonly characterized as an intuitive, ineffable gift that allows senior practitioners to identify superior outputs among hundreds of model-generated permutations. Yet, as Pierre Bourdieu established in *Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste*, aesthetic choices are never autonomous or biologically innate. Taste is the embodied manifestation of *habitus*—a set of internalized dispositions acquired through class socialization, elite education, and immersion within prestigious cultural institutions.

```
THE BOURDIEUIAN EVASION LOOP IN ALGORITHMACY UXD
[Interface Defect: Unstructured Generative Floods]
                   │
                   ▼
[Cognitive Failure: User Overwhelmed by Stochastic Debris]
                   │
                   ▼
[Ideological Attribution: Failure Framed as Lack of "Taste" / "Discernment"]
                   │
                   ▼
[Class Re-entrenchment: Cultural Capital Mobilized as Gatekeeping Moat]
                   │
                   ▼
[Ergonomic Absolution: Tooling Left Primitive; Sacerdotal Caste Preserved]
```

When generative software floods the screen with dozens of stylistic variations, demanding that the user apply "taste" to select the winner, the system offloads its inability to represent design rationale onto the operator's cultural capital. Practitioners who successfully navigate this deluge do not possess a magical cognitive faculty; they possess internalized cultural templates that allow them to match current class-coded aesthetic conventions.

Just as ancient rhetoricians celebrated the ability to decipher *scriptio continua* as proof of aristocratic intellectual superiority—thereby naturalizing a class monopoly over legal, administrative, and religious literacy—the contemporary design discourse celebrates "taste" to convert broken interface mechanics into an exclusive professional moat. It conceals the underlying truth: the software has utterly failed to externalize system constraints, operational goals, and aesthetic logic into legible, manipulable interface affordances.

### Judgment as Cognitive Dumping: The Uncompensated Verification Tax

While "taste" is deployed to aestheticize interface failure, "judgment" is deployed to functionalize it. Industry discourse champions human-in-the-loop workflows: *"The machine generates 50 UI layouts, wireframes, or code blocks in seconds, and the human uses judgment to curate what ships"*. This model re-imposes the precise working-memory bottleneck that decades of ergonomic design fought to eliminate.

| **"Judgment" as Oversight** | High-level executive decision-making directing automated production. | An uncompensated verification tax; manual, post-hoc quality assurance over hallucinations. | Rapid exhaustion of working-memory buffers; collapse into the HIAG absorbing barrier B(WM). |
| **Statistical Preference Matching** | Models capturing and aligning with human aesthetic and functional intent. | Conditional probability optimization over training corpora; averaging historical patterns. | Erasure of structural constraints; designers compete on the model's home turf of statistical mimicry. |

Forcing a human to evaluate dozens of high-fidelity synthetic artifacts against a matrix of subtle visual, structural, accessibility, and security constraints induces severe vigilance decrement. "Judgment" is a euphemistic label for manual, retrospective quality assurance. The professional is reduced to an epistemic janitor, scanning ungrounded tokens for plausible errors, broken edge cases, and hallucinations. Because the underlying foundation model lacks perceptual or physical grounding in the task domain, it generates outputs that look polished but are structurally invalid. The cognitive burden of identifying these discrepancies falls squarely on the user's working memory, driving A_t toward the disengagement boundary.

### Conflating Statistical Preference Matching with Epistemic Governance

The third ideological distortion is the conceptual collapse of statistical preference matching into genuine epistemic governance. Foundation models are engines of conditional distribution matching. Through training objectives like next-token prediction and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), they learn to minimize divergence from human-generated corpora, optimizing for what is statistically typical across historical distributions.

Authentic design judgment, however, is fundamentally counter-statistical. Groundbreaking architectural, technological, and typographic innovations emerge from the deliberate violation of historical patterns, the introduction of novel boundary constraints, and the principled refusal of corporate consensus. Framing the designer's primary role as exercising "taste" over model-generated suggestions traps the practitioner within the model's closed training distribution. It reduces design governance to passive stylistic consumerism, while stripping the operator of meaningful control over underlying constraint architectures, data provenance, and causal mechanisms.

## Perceptual and Action Foundations of Ecological Algorithmacy

To construct an ergonomic alternative to the scribal scroll, Algorithmacy UXD must be anchored in three foundational pillars of cognitive psychology and vision science: pre-attentive spatial vision, Gibsonian ecological psychology, and sensorimotor contingencies.

### Pre-Attentive Spatial Vision and Visual Search Architecture

Human vision is not a uniform camera sensor; it is a two-tiered processing system. The initial pre-attentive stage processes the entire visual field in parallel, extracting primitive physical dimensions without conscious effort or working-memory load. The subsequent focal attentional stage is strictly capacity-limited, deploying serial fixations to scrutinize complex semantic information.

Jeremy Wolfe's Guided Search model (from GS2 to GS6) demonstrates how early visual features drive human attention by constructing a global "priority map". Pre-attentive feature channels extract low-level properties across the optical array:

- Spatial frequency (coarse scene outlines vs. high-detail textures);
- Luminance contrast and sharp spatial gradients;
- Chromatic opponent boundaries (red-green, blue-yellow channels);
- Motion transients, flicker, and spatial velocity vectors;
- Surface orientation, binocular disparity, and relative depth planes.

In the Guided Search framework, bottom-up feature differences (salience) combine with top-down task demands to direct saccadic attention exclusively to the peaks of the priority map. If a property cannot be decomposed into these pre-attentive visual features, it cannot guide the ocular motor system; it must be searched for serially through high-cost focal fixations.

Current generative interfaces abuse this visual pipeline. By communicating system uncertainty, algorithmic lineage, and model boundaries via text labels (*"Confidence: 68%"* or *"Source: Scraped Web Data"*), current tools bypass parallel visual feature maps completely. The operator is forced to deploy serial fixations across every token, overwhelming working-memory buffers.

An ecological algorithmacy interface encodes model internals directly into pre-attentive visual dimensions. Epistemic uncertainty is registered as low-spatial-frequency luminance desaturation or contour degradation, allowing the visual system to distinguish speculative from verified outputs instantly. Constraint violations manifest as localized chromatic pop-outs that capture bottom-up attention automatically. By mapping system states directly to pre-attentive feature primitives, the interface provides visual scaffolding that preserves focal working memory for high-level creative synthesis.

### Gibsonian Ecological Psychology and Direct Affordances

In *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception* (1979), James J. Gibson demolished the Cartesian premise that organisms perceive the world by constructing internal mental representations from ambiguous retinal inputs. Gibson established that perception is ecological: organisms directly detect information structured within the ambient optical array.

```
GIBSONIAN DIRECT AFFORDANCE COUPLING
[Ambient Optical Array] ──(Optical Invariants)──> [Direct Affordance Pickup] ──> [Motor Action]
          ▲                                                                           │
          └─────────────────────(Continuous Dynamic Feedback)─────────────────────────┘

INDIRECT REPRESENTATIONAL GENERATIVE CHAT
[Symbolic Prompt] ──> [Stochastic Black Box] ──> [Token Ribbon] ──> [Mental Guessing Loop]
```

The primary units of ecological perception are *affordances*—objective, physically real action possibilities provided by the environment, scaled directly to the observer's physical capabilities. Affordances are perceived directly through the extraction of *invariants*: higher-order mathematical relationships within optical flow fields (e.g., texture density gradients, horizon-ratio intersections) that persist across exploratory movements.

Contemporary generative AI interfaces sever this direct ecological loop. A blank text prompt box contains zero optical invariants regarding the underlying foundation model's parameter manifold, probability landscape, or failure surfaces. The user is cast into an indirect, inferential mode, forced to concoct mental "folk theories" and engage in speculative prompt engineering to divine the black box's behavior. The interface provides no visual affordances for action, only a blank receptacle for symbolic text.

Ecological Algorithmacy UXD restores direct perception by externalizing algorithmic manifolds as continuous visual surfaces. By mapping latent vector dimensions to tangible geometric invariants, spatial boundaries, and visible texture gradients, the system makes action possibilities directly observable. The user does not mentally hypothesize what the algorithm might do; they visually perceive the operational limits and navigational pathways of the model as immediate Gibsonian affordances.

### Sensorimotor Contingency Theory and Dynamic Agency

Perceptual awareness cannot be understood as the passive registration of incoming sensory signals. J. Kevin O'Regan's Sensorimotor Contingency (SMC) theory demonstrates that perceptual experience consists of the active mastery of lawful dependencies between motor actions and sensory feedback. To perceive a physical or digital property is to possess implicit mastery of how sensory input transforms when the body moves relative to it. If a motor action produces arbitrary, delayed, or non-deterministic sensory consequences, the sensorimotor loop fractures, and the agent's experience of direct causal agency collapses.

Generative interaction patterns routinely violate sensorimotor contingencies:

1. **Temporal Discontinuity**: Submitting a prompt initiates an asynchronous processing delay during which motor feedback is absent, severing the sub-100ms motor-sensory loop required for cognitive agency.
2. **Stochastic Non-Determinism**: Submitting identical prompts yields divergent outputs, eliminating lawful, predictable contingencies between user action and system response.
3. **Kinetic Decoupling**: Typing strings of characters bears zero structural or physical isomorphism to the spatial, tonal, or morphological transformations occurring within the generative output.

To re-establish agency, Algorithmacy UXD 2.0 embeds deterministic, continuous sensorimotor loops directly into generative workflows. Interaction must shift away from discrete linguistic turn-taking toward continuous control fields. By utilizing real-time parametric manipulation—such as direct spatial dragging, latent vector scrubbing, and multi-dimensional haptic controllers—the system couples motor interventions directly to deterministic transformations in the generated media with sub-50ms latency. The user stops acting as a detached supervisor auditing stochastic proposals; they become an embodied agent directly navigating an elastic, mathematically continuous computational substrate.

### Operationalizing the Four Pillars of Algorithmic White Space

To operationalize Algorithmacy UXD 2.0 in practical design systems, the four core interventions function as an integrated human factors architecture:

#### 1. Algorithmic Pacing and Visual Budgeting

To reverse the autonomy depletion modeled by the HIAG framework, systems must enforce dynamic cognitive budgeting. Rather than maximizing token throughput or displaying endless galleries of generative variations, the interface acts as an ergonomic governor. By monitoring operator dwell time, interaction cadence, and visual complexity, the system continuously calculates the critical information rate I*.

When accumulated cognitive load approaches the working-memory absorbing boundary B(WM), the system actively throttles generative presentation. It clusters synthetic variations into bounded, structurally distinct candidate sets (restricting active branches to 3 or 4 candidates, matching human working-memory limits) and introduces structural cognitive rests. Generative outputs are segmented into modular, visually isolated components separated by functional white space, allowing users to parse structural relationships without triggering metacognitive exhaustion.

#### 2. Pre-Attentive State Signaling and Visual Syntax

Natural language text blocks must be decommissioned as the primary medium for system explainability. In their place, Algorithmacy UXD 2.0 implements an ambient visual syntax grounded in Wolfe's Guided Search primitives:

- **Epistemic Uncertainty Mapping**: Rather than presenting textual confidence scores (*"82% probability"*), uncertainty is rendered directly into the visual representation via spatial frequency modulation. Verified, high-probability tokens and graphic structures appear with sharp, high-spatial-frequency outlines and strong luminance contrast. In contrast, speculative, low-confidence regions are displayed with softened, low-spatial-frequency contours and attenuated luminance.
- **Provenance and Lineage Planes**: The training and retrieval lineages of generated assets (e.g., enterprise database extraction, open-web training corpora, fine-tuned domain parameters) are mapped to distinct chromatic opponent planes and subtle binocular depth layers. The human visual system separates these sources pre-attentively, eliminating the need to read attribution footers or navigate tabular XAI trees.
- **Constraint Boundary Alerts**: Systemic violations of functional, physical, or brand constraints trigger localized, high-salience visual alerts—such as chromatic opponent shifts or micro-motion transients—that instantly seize bottom-up attention in the global priority map.

#### 3. Spatialized Latent Exploration and Non-Linear Canvases

The conversational chat feed must be replaced by spatialized canvases that externalize the underlying geometry of the model's latent manifold. Leveraging architectures like Form Forge and differential subspace search, the interface maps high-dimensional latent variables to navigable two-dimensional surfaces and three-dimensional coordinate volumes.

Users traverse these spaces using spatial landmarks, topographic heatmaps, and visual contour lines. Instead of issuing sequential text prompts and scrolling backward through ephemeral chat histories, the designer navigates an interactive territory where spatial distance represents semantic, formal, and structural divergence. Non-linear branching trees allow operators to fork design iterations, backtrack along spatial paths, and preserve visual provenance. This spatial organization shifts search from an exhausting, memory-heavy temporal retrieval task into an intuitive exploration of an externalized cognitive landscape.

#### 4. Embodied Co-Action and Continuous Sensorimotor Control

The final pillar replaces symbolic text prompting with real-time, continuous control fields. Anchored in O'Regan's sensorimotor contingency framework, the interface operates with deterministic, sub-50ms feedback loops. Rather than issuing declarative text commands (*"Make this UI layout feel more technical and authoritative"*), the designer engages directly with reified manipulation instruments—such as semantic sliders, spatial deformation brushes, and multidimensional control vectors.

Manipulating a control immediately and proportionally deforms the generated artifact along semantically mapped latent trajectories. When the user reverses their physical movement, the generated artifact reverses its transformation identically, restoring the lawful mathematical contingency between physical effort and sensory feedback. Natural language prompting is relegated to coarse, high-level initialization; the substantive work of shaping, refining, and governing synthetic artifacts is executed through embodied, continuous co-action.

The evolution of human intellect has never progressed by demanding heroic endurance from minds trapped in broken operational environments. It has advanced by architecting external, graphic, and physical environments that offload cognitive processing directly onto sensory systems. The contemporary retreat into "taste" and "judgment" within AI interaction design is an ideological dead-end: a modern revival of scribal gatekeeping that excuses defective tooling while driving the human mind toward cognitive collapse. By anchoring Algorithmacy UXD in the cognitive neuroscience of visual parsing, pre-attentive guidance, Gibsonian affordances, and lawful sensorimotor contingencies, interface architecture can overcome the scribal era of synthetic abundance. In building "Algorithmic White Space," design will transform algorithmacy from an exhausting, performative verification tax into an intuitive, direct perceptual extension of human creative agency.
