# *The Feed* (hexagonal UX and System 0)

**Kind.** Television series (Amazon Prime; near-future London).

**Canonical references.** *The Feed* (2019); brain-implant social network; developer “hexagon” architecture scenes; off-grid extraction resistance.

**Seed reading.** Drawn from [`../essay.md`](../essay.md) § “The Feed: Hexagonal Information Architecture and System 0 Cognition.”

## Interface paradigm

Omnipresent neural implant streams memory, emotion, advertising, and AR into the sensorium; physical screens disappear. Developers manipulate interlocking glowing hexagons — a spatial, modular image of ports-and-adapters architecture rather than linear IDE coding.

## Algorithmacy / triadic coordination

**Totalized.** The algorithmic third party sits on optic nerve and hippocampus. Human-to-human coordination is co-opted at the level of perception and memory. The subject no longer “uses” the algorithm; the algorithm is part of the architecture of the self. When the system’s objectives diverge (hack, possession), autonomy collapses.

## Cognitive integration

System 0: pre-attentive scaffolding where the algorithm filters reality and decisions before conscious deliberation. Cognitive colonization risk when the preprocessor’s mandate and the person’s diverge.

## Historical analogy

The codex with hierarchical indexing (e.g. Eusebian Canons): complex cross-reference and non-linear navigation as structured architecture — here pushed into neural-spatial overlays rather than bound pages.

## Modes of resistance

Off-grid isolation; surgical hardware extraction to sever the triadic gaze. Extreme form of algorithmic / obfuscatory resistance (compare milder subversive affordances such as data pollution or disabling location).

### Implant as channel, not party
**Author:** seed contrast (import) · **Date:** 2026-09-20

A contrasting take treats the Feed as a high-bandwidth wire: Φ = 0 mediation if the implant only conveys and the social dyad still factors. On that reading, “possession” scenes are the moment the wire becomes a party — algorithmacy begins at hijack, not at implant.

### Engagement layer as development-as-product
**Author:** Roger Hunt (drafted by agent) · **Date:** 2026-09-21

The seed separates a user layer (implant, sensorium, feed) from a developer layer (hexagons, ports, adapters). The engagement-layer reading collapses them. The hexagon wall is edited live while the population is plugged in, and every implant’s streamed memory and reaction is the material the developers rearrange; the “user” side and the “developer” side are one cooptive stack, and manipulating adapters *is* the product the company ships, moment by moment. Development is not upstream of the Feed. It is the Feed. Extraction resistance then reads as refusing to be development material, not merely as leaving a network.
