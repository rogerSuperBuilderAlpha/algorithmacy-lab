# The engagement layer: development as product

**Status:** signed alternative reading across the catalog (2026-09-21). Cultural / HCI track; no exact-Φ results.

**Author of the lens.** Roger Hunt. Drafted into the packet by agent from the six `works/` entries and the DIAL response draft.

## The lens

Under literacy the product is the finished artefact, and the layer at which people meet it is the layer of use. Under algorithmacy the layer at which people meet the system is the layer at which the system is still being made. Reading a mediator’s outputs, training it by response, toggling its variables, taking control back from it, and coding around its classifiers are development acts, and they are the acts the third party runs on. **The development process is the product.** The layer to design is therefore the engagement layer — where steering is the ordinary mode of use — not a product layer that hides the mediator behind a stable surface.

The seed essay ([`essay.md`](essay.md)) reads the same six works for how users *navigate, resist, and internalize* triadic mediation. This note re-reads them for **what development act the user performs at the interface layer**. The two readings share every scene and disagree about what the scene is a fiction *of*.

## Where this reverses the seed

The seed essay closes on a tiered remedy: designers should set “information budgets,” personalize transparency thresholds, and give “a highly literate algorithmic user … access to the deep structural variables” while “a novice should receive abstracted, heuristic explanations.” That is the literacy remedy. It keeps a designer upstream deciding how much of the finished product each class of reader may see, and it reproduces the coding-elite / cybertariat split the essay itself diagnoses in *The Matrix*.

The engagement-layer alternative holds that tiering transparency by expertise is the wrong axis. If the development process is the product, the novice is already developing the shared third party — by swiping, by intervening, by speaking in code — and abstracting the steering surface away from them does not protect them; it hides their own contribution from them while keeping it available to the vendor. The design task is to make the engagement layer legible and operable for everyone, and to raise collective algorithmacy so that operating it is ordinary. The transparency paradox is real as a load problem. It is not a licence to return to producer → consumer.

This reversal is recorded here and as a signed alternative on each work file. The seed essay is not rewritten; see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Six works through the lens

| Work / paradigm | Interface layer engaged | Development act performed | What UX-only coding would miss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *The Matrix* ([`works/the_matrix.md`](works/the_matrix.md)) | Operator console: raw syntax, skill loads, exits | Running the simulation’s build pipeline from inside; the console is the product the crew lives on | Files Cypher’s reading as spectacle or elite literacy, not as a seat at the station that develops the shared third party |
| *The Feed* ([`works/the_feed.md`](works/the_feed.md)) | Implant sensorium and developer hexagon wall as one stack | Editing ports / adapters live while the population is plugged in; streamed reactions are the material rearranged | Splits “user” implant from “developer” hexagons, so the cooptive stack disappears into two layers |
| *Upload* ([`works/upload.md`](works/upload.md)) | Admin layer under the hotel skin: object properties, bandwidth flags | Doing Horizen’s development work from the customer seat | Codes property toggles as resistance or fraud, and the skin as a necessary abstraction rather than a literacy costume |
| Embodied co-pilot ([`works/embodied_co_pilot_avs.md`](works/embodied_co_pilot_avs.md)) | Handoff and override controls; folk theories of the vehicle | Continuous co-development of a policy shared with strangers; each intervention is training data | Treats the vehicle as a finished tool and the handoff as a trust-calibration UX problem |
| Omniscient curator ([`works/omniscient_curator.md`](works/omniscient_curator.md)) | Behavioral steering surface: swipe, linger, click | Users developing the recommender for one another; no finished product state exists | Records folk theories as superstition about a dyadic platform rather than partial developer models |
| Algospeak ([`works/algospeak_resistance.md`](works/algospeak_resistance.md)) | Public language passing a classifier; off-platform side-channels | Community ships the working protocol over the vendor’s classifier; evasion and retraining co-develop the moderator | Sees literacy under censorship, missing that the public is developing the third party without a console |

Every act in the third column is performed by a party that a designer → user framework — including Belousov et al.’s (2027) DIAL coding, re-coded in [`../draft.md`](../draft.md) §4–5 — would file as a user or recipient.
