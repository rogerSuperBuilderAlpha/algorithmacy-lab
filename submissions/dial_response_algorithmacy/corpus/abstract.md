# Abstract

Algorithmacy names the competence demanded when one human coordinates with another through an algorithmic third party that interprets both sides and acts on the exchange. Speculative fiction supplies detailed models of that triad under different interface regimes. *The Matrix* stages raw-syntax reading as elite neuronal recycling against *scriptio continua*; *The Feed* stages System-0 neural scaffolding and off-grid extraction; *Upload* stages object-oriented subversive affordance inside a monetized simulation. Adjacent paradigms — human-agent teaming in semi-autonomous vehicles, recommender oracles negotiated by folk theory, and algospeak as infrapolitical resistance — extend the taxonomy beyond the screen. Across these cases, interface design stratifies who can parse or displace the third party, and the transparency paradox marks the designer's bind between overload and opacity. The essay is a cultural and HCI reading of the construct; it does not report exact integrated-information results.
