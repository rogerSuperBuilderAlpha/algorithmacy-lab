## Summary
From MIT's Center for Collective Intelligence. Computes Φ on three settings: four-person work groups (higher Φ correlates with higher collective intelligence), Wikipedia editor groups (higher Φ correlates with higher article quality), and internet-scale human-computer communication over six years. Explicitly borrows Φ from neuroscience for a non-consciousness use: "Phi was originally developed by neuroscientists as a measure of consciousness in brains, but it captures, in a single mathematical quantity, two properties that are important in many other kinds of groups as well: differentiated information and integration."
