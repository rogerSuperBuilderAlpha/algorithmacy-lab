## Summary
Preregistered, theory-neutral adversarial collaboration (n=256, fMRI+MEG+iEEG), funded via Templeton World Charity Foundation's ARC-COGITATE programme, testing IIT vs. Global Neuronal Workspace Theory predictions. Published conclusion: results "align with some predictions of IIT and GNWT, while substantially challenging key tenets of both theories."

## Relevance to draft.md
DEEPENING/major update for §2.2's hedge — this is the empirical fact behind "not used as a weapon or a shield": IIT has now been subjected to a large preregistered adversarial test and survived it only partially, which is awkward both for the unfalsifiability charge (an untestable theory cannot be tested) and for any claim that IIT is vindicated. Content decoding was found in posterior AND inferior frontal cortex (contra IIT's posterior-only prediction in some readings); posterior responses were sustained; synchronization ran between early visual and frontal cortex rather than posterior-posterior sustained synchrony as IIT predicted; GNWT's predicted stimulus-offset ignition was absent.
