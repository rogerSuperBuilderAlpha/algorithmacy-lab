## Summary
From WildChat/LMSYS interaction logs, extracts implicit user feedback and tests it both as a window onto user behavior and as a fine-tuning signal. Feedback is informative about users but unreliable as a learning signal — helps on short questions (MTBench), not longer complex ones (WildBench).

## Relevance to draft.md
DEEPENING/complication for §4.5 and §5.2 — worth citing because it complicates the paper's picture productively: the sanctioned channel is a poor instrument even on the operator's own terms, sharpening rather than weakening the paper's point that the channel's shape is chosen for collectability, not fidelity.
