## Summary
Same Indian cohort (~90 adults, illiterate to highly literate). Literacy enhanced responses to non-orthographic visual input in early visual areas and increased text/face representational similarity, with no reduction in response to non-orthographic stimuli. Conclusion: reading "recycles existing object representation mechanisms but without destructive competition."

## Relevance to draft.md
CAUTION/COMPLICATION for §2.3's word "colonises" (following Dehaene & Cohen's own metaphor), which implies destructive displacement. This paper finds co-option without destruction.
