## Summary
68 interviews, ethnography, 87 documented strikes/protests, comparing service-platform and gig-platform architectures. Technological, legal, and organizational dimensions of "platform architecture" jointly determine whether grievance converts into collective contention — architecture isolating in one register can enable coordination in another.
