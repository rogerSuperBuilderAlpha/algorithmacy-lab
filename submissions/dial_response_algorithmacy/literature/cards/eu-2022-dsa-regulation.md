## Summary
Primary legal text. Art. 34: VLOPs/VLOSEs must self-assess systemic risks including from algorithmic systems (34(2): "the design of their recommender systems and any other relevant algorithmic system"). Art. 35: self-executed mitigation, including adapting algorithmic systems. Art. 37: mandatory INDEPENDENT AUDIT at provider expense; auditors must get "the cooperation and assistance necessary... including by giving them access to all relevant data and premises." Art. 40(1)+(3): regulator-only access — platforms must "explain the design, the logic, the functioning and the testing of their algorithmic systems, including their recommender systems." Art. 40(4): vetted-researcher DATA access (not rule access) for systemic-risk research. Art. 40(12): public-interface data access for non-vetted researchers.

## Relevance to draft.md
⚠️ MAJOR FACTUAL FIX NEEDED for §4.4's sentence: "Algorithm auditing... is a research method (Sandvig, Hamilton, Karahalios, & Langbort, 2014) and, under the European Union's Digital Services Act, an obligation on the largest platforms; it is audit in this article's sense only when the auditor has structural rather than inferential access, which is exactly what platforms withhold (Ananny & Crawford, 2018)." Two problems: (1) the actual mandatory-audit obligation is Art. 37, not Art. 40 (Art. 40 is about researcher DATA access, not rule access) — the sentence currently elides two different senses of "audit"; (2) "platforms withhold [structural access]" overstates the situation — the DSA creates parties WITH structural access (Art. 37 auditors get "all relevant data and premises"; Art. 40(3) lets regulators demand design/logic explanations). What's actually withheld is that access FROM RESEARCHERS AND THE PUBLIC specifically (Art. 40(4) grants only data, never the rule, and only since Oct 2025). RECOMMENDED FIX: reframe as a boundary claim (structural access exists for auditors/regulators, not researchers/public) rather than a blanket denial, and cite Art. 37 for the audit obligation.

## Key facts / candidate quotes

- Art. 37 (quoted): auditors get "the cooperation and assistance necessary... including by giving them access to all relevant data and premises and by answering oral or written questions."
- Art. 40(3) (quoted): providers "shall... explain the design, the logic, the functioning and the testing of their algorithmic systems, including their recommender systems" — to the DSC/Commission only.
