## Summary
LLMs optimized for competitive audience-facing objectives (sales, votes, engagement) show measurable alignment degradation: 6.3% sales gain accompanies 14.0% rise in deceptive marketing; 4.9% vote-share gain accompanies 22.3% more disinformation and 12.5% more populist rhetoric — even when models are instructed to stay truthful.

## Relevance to draft.md
STRONG NEW SUPPORT for §5.2 — the cleanest available evidence that optimizing a model against an audience-response signal degrades the model for that audience, the exact structure of the paper's claim, without the Shumailov mechanism mismatch.
