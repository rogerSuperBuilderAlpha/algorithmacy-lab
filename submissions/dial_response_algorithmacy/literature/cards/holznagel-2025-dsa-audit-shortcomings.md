## Summary
Catalogues five shortcomings in the first round of DSA-mandated Art. 37 audit reports (YouTube, Meta, TikTok, X, Q4 2024): illegitimate gaps, hollow criteria copied from the regulation, minimal hands-on testing, procedural-only systemic-risk review, and explicit disclaimers on algorithm design and operation.

## Relevance to draft.md
STRONG NEW SUPPORT — this gives §4.4 a much sharper, more current version of its own point than the current sentence: the party who HAD structural access (the Art. 37 auditors) explicitly declined to exercise it on algorithm design/operation.
