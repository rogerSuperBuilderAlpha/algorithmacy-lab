## Summary
Five-country ethnography (Italy, Spain, Mexico, China, India): 68 courier interviews plus digital ethnography inside dozens of worker-created private chat groups (WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, WeChat). Groups afford communities of practice, resistance, and mutualism precisely because they sit outside the platform's line of sight.
