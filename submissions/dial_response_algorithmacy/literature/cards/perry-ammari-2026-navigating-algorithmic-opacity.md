## Summary
16 semi-structured interviews with US semi-autonomous-vehicle drivers, explicitly framed as folk theorization. Uses anthropomorphic vocabulary — a "theory of perceptual overload," a vehicle that is "hesitant" on unprotected left turns — and identifies complex intersections, adverse weather, and rural roads as sites of acute opacity.
