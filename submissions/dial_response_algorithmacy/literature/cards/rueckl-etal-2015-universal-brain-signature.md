## Summary
Skilled adult readers of Spanish, English, Hebrew, and Chinese did identical semantic categorization on spoken and written words during fMRI. Across all four, proficient reading showed the same signature: convergence of speech and orthographic processing onto a common network, regardless of alphabetic/logographic or opaque/transparent script.
