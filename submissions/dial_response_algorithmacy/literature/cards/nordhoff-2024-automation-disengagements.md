## Summary
Semi-structured interviews with 103 Tesla Autopilot/FSD Beta users, coded into five categories including "the human operator's perception of the automation." Drivers read unnatural behaviour as evidence about the system's competence and disengage on that reading; one participant links harsh phantom braking directly to Tesla's radar-to-vision sensor transition.
