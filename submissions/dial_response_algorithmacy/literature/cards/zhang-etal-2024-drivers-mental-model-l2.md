## Summary
Questionnaire study of 357 Tesla Autopilot owners and 357 non-owners; mental model measured along three dimensions (functional condition, sensorimotor preparation, automation monitoring). Drivers on average hold inadequate mental models of L2 automation; counterintuitively, drivers with BETTER mental models show LOWER trust and reliance.
