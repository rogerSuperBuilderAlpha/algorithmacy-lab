## Summary
Argues falsificationism is the wrong evaluative frame for consciousness science; adversarial collaborations should be occasions for theoretical self-improvement, not elimination; a research programme can rationally retain core commitments while revising auxiliaries. Offers three criteria: prediction vs. accommodation, structural relevance to the theoretical core, and boldness.
