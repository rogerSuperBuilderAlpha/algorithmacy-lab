## Summary
Interviews with 103 real FSD Beta users (Discord, Reddit, Tesla Motors Club). Explicitly anthropomorphic, mental-state language — "drunk toddler," "teenage student driver," "babysitting" ("Imagine having a 12-year-old on your lap") — and frequent manual disengagements ("four or five disengagements per mile" in one report). Also documents deliberate handoff-defeating behavior: weighting the steering wheel to spoof the torque sensor, resting a knee on the wheel, 44 miles of eyes-off driving, sleeping at the wheel.
