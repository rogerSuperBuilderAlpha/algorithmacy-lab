## Summary
30 interviews, 4 focus groups (22 users), and participant-drawn "rich pictures" in Costa Rica. Two folk theories emerge: Spotify as a personified social being that recommends through surveillance, and Spotify as a computational machine offering an individualised experience through the right kind of "training."
