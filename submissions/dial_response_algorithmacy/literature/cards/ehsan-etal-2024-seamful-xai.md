## Summary
Transfers "seams" from 2003 ubicomp to 2020s AI systems (sociotechnical/infrastructural mismatches); builds a design process around them, tested via scenario-based co-design with 43 AI practitioners and end-users. Reported outcome: helped participants foresee harms, name underlying seams, locate them in the AI lifecycle, and leverage seamful information to improve explainability AND user agency.
