## Summary
Separates a "folk" conception of work (importance, effort, deserving compensation) from a "political economy" conception (bargaining asymmetries). Data contribution fails the folk test (explaining reluctance to regulate it) but passes the political-economy test (giving functional reasons to regulate it like labor anyway).
