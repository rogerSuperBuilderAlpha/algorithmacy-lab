## Summary
Independent, contemporaneous work to Shumailov et al., using state-of-the-art image models across three "autophagous" loop variants differing in how much fresh real data enters each generation. Without enough fresh real data, quality (precision) or diversity (recall) progressively degrades within a few generations — "Model Autophagy Disorder."
