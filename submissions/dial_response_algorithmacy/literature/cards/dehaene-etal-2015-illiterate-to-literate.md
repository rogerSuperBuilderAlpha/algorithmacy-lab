## Summary
The same authors' decade-later synthesis of the 2007 VWFA/neuronal-recycling claim, built around illiterate/ex-illiterate/literate comparisons rather than child development. Collects evidence that reading acquisition reorganizes visual cortex, phonological networks, and their interconnection, restating neuronal recycling in mature form.
