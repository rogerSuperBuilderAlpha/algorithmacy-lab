## Summary
Treats informational infrastructure as political in its capacity to shape social/cultural life; develops "programmed sociality" through case studies (Facebook ranking, engineering participation, news programming). Chapter "Neither Black Nor Box: (Un)knowing Algorithms" resists the sealed-mechanism picture.

## Relevance to draft.md
