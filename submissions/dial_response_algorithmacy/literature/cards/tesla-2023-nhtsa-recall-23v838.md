## Summary
Official recall covering 2,031,220 vehicles. Tesla concedes: "in certain circumstances when Autosteer is engaged, the prominence and scope of the feature's controls may not be sufficient to prevent driver misuse" of an SAE Level 2 system. Underlying ODI investigation EA22002 identified at least 13 fatal crashes with apparent foreseeable driver misuse; the OTA remedy increased alert prominence and changed engagement/disengagement mechanics.
