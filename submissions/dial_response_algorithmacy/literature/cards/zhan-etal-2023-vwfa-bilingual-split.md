## Summary
Unsmoothed 1.2mm 7T fMRI in 21 English-French and 10 English-Chinese bilinguals. In English-French readers, VWFA resolves into small patches with near-complete overlap between languages. In English-Chinese readers, most patches overlap but additional patches respond specifically to Chinese writing — and, unexpectedly, to faces.
