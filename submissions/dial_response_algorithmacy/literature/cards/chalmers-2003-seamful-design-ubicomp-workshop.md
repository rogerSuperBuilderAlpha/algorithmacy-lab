## Summary
READ IN FULL by the research agent. Argues features designers treat as infrastructure defects (GPS dead zones, WiFi coverage gaps, positioning error) are useful interactional resources once revealed to users; builds "seamful maps" overlaying signal strength so users can decide where to go/when to sync. Closing section "Design for Appropriation": underlying mechanisms should be "literally visible, effectively invisible" — invisible for ordinary interaction, but revealable "when the task is to understand or even change the tool." States the anti-pastoral case directly: engineers treat these features as problems to solve, but "users can and often do find ways to use them as solutions for their own problems."
