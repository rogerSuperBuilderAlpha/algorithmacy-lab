## Summary
Working with a US civil-rights organization and its community-group coalition, found the need was LEGIBILITY IN LAY LANGUAGE, not explanation. Built the Algorithmic Equity Toolkit: a policy-facing AI definition, an assessment flowchart, a system-decomposition worksheet, and vendor-probing questions — explicit aim of building communities of citizens able to act politically when local government adopts algorithmic tools.
