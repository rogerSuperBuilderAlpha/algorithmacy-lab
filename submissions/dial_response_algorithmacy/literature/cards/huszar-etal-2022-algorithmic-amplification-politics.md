## Summary
Massive-scale randomized experiment on Twitter (control group ~2 million daily active accounts), personalized timeline vs. chronological control. In 6 of 7 countries, the mainstream political right received higher algorithmic amplification; far-left/far-right were not amplified more than moderates, contrary to the prevailing story.

## Relevance to draft.md
