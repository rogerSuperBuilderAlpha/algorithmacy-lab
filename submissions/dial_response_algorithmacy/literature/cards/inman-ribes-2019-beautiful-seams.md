## Summary
Traces how "seamlessness" became an implicit, privileged design virtue in ubicomp and then CHI, and how seamful design answered it (configurability, appropriation, revealing complexity/inconsistency). Refuses to treat seamless/seamful as rivals: each distributes DOWNSTREAM USER AGENCY differently — the real question is strategic revelation or concealment of human and technological operations.
