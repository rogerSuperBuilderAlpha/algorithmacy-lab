## Summary
Ten six-year-olds scanned six or seven times each across the first months of reading acquisition, tracking VWFA formation longitudinally and within-subject, rather than inferring formation from a literate-vs-illiterate cross-sectional contrast.

## Relevance to draft.md
DEEPENING of §2.3's "literacy colonises a region of left ventral occipitotemporal cortex" — direct empirical upgrade of the Dehaene & Cohen (2007) claim: watches the VWFA form in real time rather than inferring formation from converging cross-sectional evidence.
