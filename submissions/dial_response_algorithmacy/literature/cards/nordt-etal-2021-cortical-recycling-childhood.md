## Summary
Longitudinal fMRI in school-age children over one to five years. Word- and face-selective regions in ventral temporal cortex expand and sharpen while limb-selective regions shrink and lose preference; increases in word/face selectivity are directly coupled, within individual children, to decreases in limb selectivity.

## Relevance to draft.md
DEEPENING/complication for §2.3's "colonises." This is the best direct evidence that literacy trades cortical territory for something — but the donor tissue is limb-selective cortex, not face-selective cortex as "colonises" might imply. Pair with hervais-adelman-etal-2019 (which found no face-recognition cost): the trade is real at the level of cortical territory and not destructive at the level of behavior.
