## Summary
Two studies (N1=54 generating explanations across 14 driving scenarios, N2=382 rating them) produced the HEADD dataset of 1,347 natural-language AV-behavior explanations, coded into descriptive/teleological/mechanistic/counterfactual modes. People spontaneously take the intentional stance; teleological (purpose-attributing) explanations rated significantly higher quality than counterfactual ones — perceived teleology is the single best predictor of perceived explanation quality.
