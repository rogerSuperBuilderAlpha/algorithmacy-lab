## Summary
N≈500 study: anthropomorphism of the driving system correlates strongly with trust (r=.72) and with praise assigned to the ADS (r=.60); both anthropomorphism and trust drop as outcome severity rises (near-miss → collision).

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT for §4.1's "the anthropomorphism is not confusion, it is consequential" move — quantifies that attributing mind to the system tracks how much people rely on it.
