## Summary
19 TikTok creator interviews on algospeak — invented/respelled words ("le$bean" for "lesbian") meant to pass the classifier while remaining legible to the intended audience. Creators anticipate how the classifier will parse a video and encode around it; the lexicon circulates as shared community property.
