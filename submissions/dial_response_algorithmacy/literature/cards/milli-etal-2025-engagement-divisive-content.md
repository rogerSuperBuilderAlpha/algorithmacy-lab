## Summary
Preregistered two-week algorithmic audit, 806 regular Twitter users, February 2023. Relative to reverse-chronological baseline, engagement-ranking amplified anger (0.47 SD, p<0.001), partisanship (0.24 SD), out-group animosity (0.24 SD). Users rated the algorithm's political selections WORSE than chronological (−0.18 SD, p=0.005).

## Relevance to draft.md
PROBABLY THE STRONGEST SINGLE SOURCE for §5.2's "contribution as extraction" — the signal aggregated from everyone's clicks produces a feed no individual prefers.
