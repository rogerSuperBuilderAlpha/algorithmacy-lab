## Summary
Longitudinal resting-state fMRI of illiterate adults in northern India, scanned before/after six months of Hindi literacy training. Functional connectivity between occipital cortex and subcortical structures (superior colliculus, pulvinar) increased over training, tracking individual gains in letter knowledge and word decoding.

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT, and the strongest available, for §2.3's claim "a reading brain is a brain whose cause–effect structure has been rebuilt." This is the only design in the literature with genuine causal warrant — an intervention in adults with a dose-response relationship between training and connectivity change — matching the paper's own "cause-effect structure" framing better than activation-based evidence.

## Key facts / candidate quotes
- Six-month Hindi literacy intervention in previously illiterate adults.
