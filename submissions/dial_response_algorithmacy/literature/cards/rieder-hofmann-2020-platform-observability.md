## Summary
Rejects the "openable black box" image, proposes "observability": platforms monopolize analytical capacity and treat behavioural information as a private asset, producing an information asymmetry no one-off transparency disclosure can close. Accountability requires continuous, collaborative, institutionally supported knowledge production.

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT for §5.4's "the parties without access" half-sentence — lets the paper distinguish two deprivations currently fused: parties who can't READ the rule vs. parties who can't WRITE it. The authors' point is that even well-designed reading rights leave specification power untouched, which is the paper's own argument, arrived at from the governance side.
