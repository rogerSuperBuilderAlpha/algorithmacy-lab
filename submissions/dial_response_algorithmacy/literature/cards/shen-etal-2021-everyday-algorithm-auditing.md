## Summary
Formal audits carry blind spots surfacing only in everyday use after deployment; documents cases where ordinary users, acting together in normal interaction, detected and publicized harmful algorithmic behaviors expert auditing missed.

## Relevance to draft.md
LOAD-BEARING for §5.5's "make contribution legible" move and the "collective algorithmacy" claim specifically — the strongest available evidence in HCI that the competence to catch harmful algorithmic behavior is DISTRIBUTED across a user population, not resident in any individual expert. This is literally what "collective algorithmacy" claims and currently has nothing backing it.
