## Summary
Foundational (25-year-old) theorization of "free labor": activity that is "simultaneously voluntarily given and unwaged, enjoyed and exploited" — building sites, modifying software, reading/posting to lists. Not that users are duped; digital-economy cultural production runs on activity its producers experience as pleasure and self-expression.
