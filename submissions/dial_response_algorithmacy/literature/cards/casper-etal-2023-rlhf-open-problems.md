## Summary
32-author survey of ~250 papers, sorting RLHF failures into tractable (feedback quality, annotator poisoning, partial observability, data bias) and fundamental (humans can't supply feedback on complex tasks; doesn't scale to superhuman models). Proposes auditing/disclosure standards.

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT — best single anchor for §4.5/§5.2's claim that the channel is designed so what can be taught is what the operator wants learned: feedback solicitation, source, format, and aggregation are all operator choices, each a documented failure surface. Auditing/disclosure proposal is close to the paper's own implicit normative point about contributor visibility.
