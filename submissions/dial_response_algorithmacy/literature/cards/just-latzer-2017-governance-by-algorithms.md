## Summary
Algorithmic selection has become a source of social order and SHARED SOCIAL REALITY, functioning the way mass-media reality construction once did, but through automated selection. Treats algorithms as institutions — governance by technology — raising individualization/commercialization/inequality while lowering transparency/controllability/predictability.
