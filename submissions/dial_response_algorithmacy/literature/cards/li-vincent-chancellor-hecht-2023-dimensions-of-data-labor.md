## Summary
Names systems (ChatGPT, search engines) as dependent on user-generated data from interaction, with producers having no say in capture, use, or benefit. Six dimensions of data labor: legibility, end-use awareness, collaboration requirement, openness, replaceability, livelihood overlap.

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT — two of the six dimensions (legibility, end-use awareness) are precisely the mechanism by which labor gets "coded as consumption" in §5.2's final sentence, giving a vocabulary for WHY the coding holds, not just that it does. Current, ACM-venue statement of the position — matters for showing the argument is alive in the field, not only in 2000s-2010s sociology.
