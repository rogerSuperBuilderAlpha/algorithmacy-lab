## Summary
Defines contestable AI as systems "open and responsive to human intervention throughout their lifecycle," a procedural relationship between decision subjects and human controllers. Names five system features and six development practices — built-in safeguards, interactive control over automated decisions, explanations of system behavior, human review/intervention requests, among others.
