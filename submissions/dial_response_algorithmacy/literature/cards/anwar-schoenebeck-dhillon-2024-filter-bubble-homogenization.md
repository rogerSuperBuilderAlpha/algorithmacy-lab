## Summary
Decomposes "filter bubble" worry into inter-user diversity (how different people's consumption is from each other) and intra-user diversity (variation within one person). Agent-based simulation: behaviour-based recommenders act mainly on inter-user diversity — making people more similar to each other — without much affecting within-person variation.
