## Key facts / candidate quotes
- Abstract (verbatim, from repository record): "Theories of consciousness have a long and controversial history. One well-known proposal — integrated information theory — has recently been labeled as 'pseudoscience', which has caused a heated open debate. Here we discuss the case and argue that the theory is indeed unscientific because its core claims are untestable even in principle."
