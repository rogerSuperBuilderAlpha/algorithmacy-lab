## Summary
Neutral third piece in the same Nature Neuroscience issue as the charge and reply. Full text read by the research agent. Defends IIT's scientific status while declining to defend IIT itself, distinguishing IIT's untestable "core claim" (the Φ-consciousness identity) from its testable/valuable "formal specificity." Cites "weak IIT" (mediano-etal-2022-weak-iit) as an existing, named response that drops the identity claim while keeping the formal apparatus.

## Key facts / candidate quotes
- "We have defended the scientific status of IIT, but this does not mean we defend IIT."
- "The charge of pseudoscience is serious... Is it justified? We do not think so."
- "IIT-Concerned et al. risk affirming the antecedent: pseudoscience makes untestable predictions so, if there are untestable predictions, then it must be pseudoscience."
- "IIT's 'core claims' may be impossible to test, at least with current methods. This is certainly the case for one of its most central claims: that Φ fully accounts for (has an 'explanatory identity' with) the quantity of consciousness, rather than merely being correlated with it. But many testable predictions do follow from IIT."
- IIT "has the potential to be 'progressive', rather than 'degenerate'" (Lakatosian framing).
- "In the face of the consciousness enigma, IIT has every right to be wrong, rather than being forced to carry the stigma of being 'not even wrong'."
- "Thanks to its basis in formal axioms and postulates, IIT has a mathematical specificity about what consciousness is, where it appears, and what it is like that other theories so far lack."
- On numbers: the Nature Neuroscience letter drew "just over 100 — somewhat down from the 124 signatories of the preceding preprint."
- On weak IIT (strongest single line for the hedge): "Another false opposition is within IIT itself... IIT-Concerned et al. overlook that potentially good responses already exist, such as the 'weak IIT' proposed by Mediano et al. In 'weak IIT', measures of integrated information are proposed that no longer claim an explanatory identity with the quantity of consciousness (as in standard IIT), but which preserve the explanatory insight that 'integrated information' in experience should be reflected in the underlying neural dynamics... A major benefit of dropping the identity claim is that approximations (rather than proxies) are immediately available. With conceptual modesty often comes empirical fertility."
