## Summary
Distinguishes "strong IIT" (identifies consciousness with maxima of integrated information) from "weak IIT" (tests pragmatic hypotheses relating aspects of consciousness to broader information-dynamics measures, without the identity claim). A named, established, 65+-citation piece of vocabulary.
