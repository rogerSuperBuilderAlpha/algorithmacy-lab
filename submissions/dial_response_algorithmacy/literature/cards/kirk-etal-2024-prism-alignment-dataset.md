## Summary
1,500 participants from 75 countries, census-representative UK/US subsamples, linked sociodemographics and stated preferences to fine-grained ratings across 8,011 live conversations with 21 LLMs, targeting value-laden/controversial topics deliberately.

## Relevance to draft.md
NEW SUPPORT for §4.5's "whose preferences" question — strongest empirical demonstration that the feedback channel is not a neutral pipe: who is recruited determines what the mediator learns, and the mediator is then met by people never in the sample.
