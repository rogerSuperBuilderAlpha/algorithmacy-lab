## Summary
Diagnosis: platform software encodes a narrow governance model built around moderators/admins; communities wanting anything else fall back on laborious manual effort. PolicyKit lets ordinary community members AUTHOR governance procedures as short policies the platform executes, including policies governing how policies change. Demonstrated: random jury deliberation, multi-stage caucus, reputation systems, a Wikipedia RfA-style promotion procedure.
