## Summary
The original "pseudoscience" open letter/commentary. Read in full by the research agent. Its target is narrower and more conditional than its reputation: opens on media coverage of IIT, not IIT's internals, and its central claim is conditional — "until the theory as a whole... is empirically testable, we feel that the pseudoscience label should indeed apply." Explicitly disclaims a stronger verdict ("Our consensus is not that IIT and its variants decidedly lack intellectual merit") and concedes the adversarial-collaboration tests "only tested some idiosyncratic predictions... not really logically related to the core ideas of IIT."

## Relevance to draft.md
DEEPENING for §2.2's hedge ("the contested status of IIT as a complete theory of consciousness is not used as a weapon or as a shield"). The charge is aimed at the theory as a whole (the Φ-consciousness identity and panpsychist entailments) and explicitly exempts "auxiliary components trivially shared by many others or already known to be true" — i.e., a paper borrowing only the formal apparatus sits outside the letter's stated scope, and this can now be said with a citation.

## Key facts / candidate quotes
- "The media, including news articles in both Nature and Science, have recently celebrated the Integrated Information Theory (IIT) as a 'leading' and empirically tested theory of consciousness."
- "Given its panpsychist commitments, until the theory as a whole — not just some hand-picked auxiliary components trivially shared by many others or already known to be true — is empirically testable, we feel that the pseudoscience label should indeed apply."
- "Our consensus is not that IIT and its variants decidedly lack intellectual merit."
- On the adversarial collaboration: "the studies only tested some idiosyncratic predictions made by certain theorists, which are not really logically related to the core ideas of IIT."
