## Summary
PDF extracted and read directly by the research agent. Tesla's own language: "Safety Event or Fleet Learning video clips may be several MBs and are collected only in limited scenarios," and "Certain advanced data collection (e.g., Fleet Learning or Cabin Camera Analytics) is opt-in and can be controlled via Controls > Software > Data Sharing." Data generated "upon event triggers, such as a collision, software failure, or service request."
