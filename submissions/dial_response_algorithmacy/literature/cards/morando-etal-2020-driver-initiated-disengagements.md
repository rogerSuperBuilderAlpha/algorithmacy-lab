## Summary
Codes 298 highway driver-initiated, non-critical Autopilot disengagements from 19 Tesla owners in naturalistic driving. Off-road glances fell from 36% (Autopilot engaged) to 24% (after manual resumption); in 33% of events drivers weren't holding the wheel immediately before disengaging.

## Relevance to draft.md
STRONG NEW SUPPORT for §4.6's "manual override of a driving policy where it defeats the designed handoff" — the cleanest fielded evidence that driver-initiated override is ROUTINE, not exceptional, and occurs from states the designed handoff protocol didn't anticipate.
