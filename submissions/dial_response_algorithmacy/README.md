Also serves as the argument for Roger Hunt's PHD1750-3 (HF700-1, Foundations in Human Factors) independent study — see the course crosswalk in [`SOURCE.md`](SOURCE.md#phd1750-3-course-framing-current-spine) and the ingested topic/rationale statement in [`sources/phd1750_topic_and_rationale.md`](sources/phd1750_topic_and_rationale.md).

**Working title.** Designing the future of coordination: Algorithmacy, integrated information, and the engagement layer in speculative fiction.

## What this packet is

A secondary re-coding of Belousov et al.'s fixed science-fiction corpus, extended by the six works catalogued in [`corpus/works/`](corpus/works/), against a different unit of analysis: the triad of party, algorithmic third party, and counterpart. Three substitutions run through the article — brain–computer interface → algorithmic third party; input discrepancy → IIT cause–effect reconfiguration; user–technology dyad → cooptive triad. A reach × act framework yields eight interaction types; five themes follow. The earlier 3,000-word commentary is preserved in git history and its argument is absorbed into §1–2 and §5.5.
