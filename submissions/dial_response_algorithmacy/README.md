# Response to Belousov et al. (IJHCS 2027) — DIAL through algorithmacy

**Status:** draft commentary packet (2026-09-21). Cultural / HCI track. Not an exact-Φ study; no new lab numbers claimed here.

**Target venue.** *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies* — commentary / letter responding to Belousov, Macey, Bujić, Ojell-Järventausta & Hamari (2027), “Designing the future of consciousness.”

**Working title.** Design Is Cooptive: Algorithmacy as the Missing Ontology of Digitally Induced Altered States.

## What this packet is

A short critical response to Belousov et al.’s science-fiction analysis of digitally induced altered states of consciousness (DIAL). The packet keeps their taxonomy useful and relocates the ontological error: the paper inherits a literacy-style split between designers who make systems and users who merely experience them. Under algorithmacy, interaction is contribution that reshapes a shared algorithmic third party.
