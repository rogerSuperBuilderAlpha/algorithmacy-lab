## Corpus re-use

The article's corpus is selected for a different thing than Belousov et al.'s: scenes of the algorithmic mediator's *development* (a party reading or writing its rule), not scenes of a finished technology already altering a consciousness. The primary corpus is the six-work catalogue (M1–M6, documented in [`corpus/works/`](corpus/works/), tagged `catalog`), purpose-built for that criterion. Belousov et al.'s 25-novel corpus (their indices A1–A25) is drawn on opportunistically, not held fixed as a base sample — 23 of the 25 qualify against the development criterion and are re-coded as a secondary analysis, without re-reading the novels; two (A16, A19) were tested and excluded, reported rather than dropped silently. Evidence for every scene is tagged in [`corpus_coding.md`](corpus_coding.md): `extract` (in their article), `canon` (widely documented, not in their article), `to verify` (author's recollection; check before submission), `thin` (mediator present, reading/writing scene supported on one side only, work stays in), `excluded` (mediator present, no operation on its structure shown on either side, work is not in the corpus). Their CC BY 4.0 licence permits quotation of the extracts they published; block quotations in `draft.md` §4 are the passages they quoted, cited by their index and section.
