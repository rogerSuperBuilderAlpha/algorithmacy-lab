# Claim — Sci-Fi Interfaces and Algorithmacy

**Title.** Algorithmacy and the Triadic Interface: Decoding the Future of Human-Computer Interaction in Speculative Fiction.

**Question.** What do speculative interface paradigms show about the competence demanded when coordination runs through an active algorithmic third party?

**Thesis.**

Speculative fiction is a laboratory for algorithmacy as a triadic competency. *The Matrix*, *The Feed*, *Upload*, and adjacent HCI extrapolations (embodied co-pilots, recommender oracles, algospeak) model how interface design stratifies who can read, negotiate, or displace the third party — and who remains subject to it under literacy-only habits.

**What that licenses**

- Algorithmacy is not literacy extended to a new medium: the third party curates, filters, modulates, and intervenes; it is a party, not a wire.
- Interface paradigms (raw syntax, neural-spatial feed, immersive GUI, embodied HAT, invisible curation) change cognitive load, exclusivity, and available resistance modes.
- Historical literacy architecture (scriptio continua → word separation → codex/indexing) is analogy, not identity: algorithmacy recycles cognition for predictive, co-optive mediation.
- Resistance (bypass, extraction, subversive affordance, folk-theory override, algospeak) is evidence about how users try to restore dyadic reach or displace the mediator's gaze — not proof that any real platform has a measured Φ.
- The transparency paradox names a design tension: more backend visibility can deplete autonomy; abstraction can also hide extractive agendas.

**Scope**

Cultural / HCI reading. No exact-Φ results. Any follow-on probe that maps interface opacity or folk-theory membership to core structure lives under `org_frontier/`, registered and reproducible, not in this packet.

**Do not claim**

- That any film or series "proves" irreducibility.
- Fabricated Φ numbers or field measurements of viewers, drivers, or platform users.
- That IIT-as-consciousness is at stake; the lab borrows Φ for causal irreducibility of coordination forms.
