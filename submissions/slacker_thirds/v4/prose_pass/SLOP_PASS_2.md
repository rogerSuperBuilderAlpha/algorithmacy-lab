# Slop pass 2 — em-dash census and paragraph-by-paragraph edit ledger

Date: 2026-08-11. Target: `chapter/chapter_v4.md`.
Trigger: author verdict that the draft still reads as machine prose; measured cause an
em-dash rate of 23.3/1k against a 42-essay corpus mean of 4.12 and max 10.88 (z = +7.6).

**Result: 114 body em-dashes → 34 (7.0/1k, z = +1.2, inside the corpus band).**
Body 4,899 → 4,841 words. Gate G4 PASS after every section; carryover clean throughout;
landing lines 23% (threshold 25%); all seven [DISC:…] markers and [VERIFY:A17] untouched;
final sentence "One gate refused them." untouched.

Colons 71 → 76 (15.2/1k), semicolons 27 → 26. The colon rate was already high before this

offset by two colon deletions (the cut "And a correction:" sentence, the Uber restructure).
No new semicolons.

Method note: dash cuts were, in order of use, full stops (most common), deletion of the
parenthetical (twice, where the content was carried elsewhere), commas for short restrictive
asides, parentheses for source-hedges and mechanical glosses, and a small number of colons
for genuine list unpackings. Kept dashes are almost all of two kinds: a quoted line of the
film dropped mid-sentence as a beat, and a gloss welded onto a coined term.
