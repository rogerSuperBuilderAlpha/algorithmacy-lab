# Scene ledger: budget for the v4 rebuild

Built 2026-08-10 against `research/scene_inventory.md` (the 35-segment mapping and its ranked
missing-scenes list), `research/slacker_transcript.md` (12,437-word subtitle-derived transcript,
no speaker names, no stage directions), `chapter/chapter_v3.md`, and `research/film_slacker.md`.
Segment numbering is the inventory's; this ledger does not renumber. Nobody on this project has
watched the disc — the transcript carries dialogue only, so every claim below stays inside what
dialogue can support. Where a claim needs a shot, an object, a gesture, or a continuity fact the
transcript cannot supply, the cell says `NEEDS DISC` and names exactly what would settle it.

**Coverage in one line.** 35 segments; v3 engages 5 (two of those only partially) and mentions 3
more without discussing their content; 27 are untouched. 13 of the 35 are disc-dependent for at
least one claim a use of the scene would want to make.
