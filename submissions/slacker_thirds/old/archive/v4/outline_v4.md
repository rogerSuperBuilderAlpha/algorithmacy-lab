## A. Rules that bind every section

1. **Nothing visual.** No shot, no camera movement, no take length, no film format, no gesture,
   no prop, no location continuity, no claim about anything visible rather than audible
   (`ASSUMPTIONS.md` A3, unchanged by A3a). A census of what can be heard is not a description of
   what happens on screen, and the chapter may not let the first quietly become the second.
2. **No section opens by announcing what it will do.** No "this section argues," no "I turn now
   to," no forecast of the section's own moves. Each section below carries an instruction for its
   first sentence; follow it.
3. **Every factual assertion traces to a source ID.** Three kinds are in play, and the draft must
   keep them apart:
   - `CLAIMS.md` rows, cited by ID with class. **Class A and B** may be written from. **Class C**
     must be marked second-hand *in the body*, not only in a note. **Class D may not be asserted
     at all.**
   - **Census facts**, cited as `CENSUS n→n+1` with the coders' verdict. These have *no*
     `CLAIMS.md` row — see §J, finding 1. They are authorized by `ASSUMPTIONS.md` **A3a** and must
     carry its five conditions everywhere they appear.
   - **Ledger facts** (segment numbering and line ranges), cited as `LEDGER seg n`. Same
     authorization, same conditions.
4. **The A3a conditions travel with the count.** Band, never a point estimate; every equivocal
   case listed; the method stated in the chapter; marked subtitle-derived with the transcript
   named as the instrument; stated as revisable by the disc.
5. **Verbatim film quotation is class D unless a retrieved published source carries the same
   wording.** A3 blocks "the audio wording of every quotation the chapter draws from dialogue" and
   A3a did not lift that. Two lines are quotable because a class-A secondary source carries them:
   *"Withdrawing in disgust is not the same thing as apathy"* (S4-065, class A; wording variance
   at S4-066 must be noted) and *"There is no structure. The underlying order is chaos"* (S4-067,
   class A; variants at S4-068, S4-069). **Everything else spoken in the film is reported in
   substance, never inside quotation marks.** The census is a claim about what is audible, and
   reporting the substance of a seam line is part of asserting it; setting that substance in
   quotation marks is a claim about exact audio wording, which is a different and blocked claim.
6. **The abstract's commitments are contractual** (`ASSUMPTIONS.md` A5). Simmel's third → §3.
   Austin/camera → §1 and §3. Uber → §5. Training → §6. The closing questions about
   contestability → §7. A5 permits renaming *hosting*/*engineering* to **reach**/**handoff**; it
   does not permit dropping any of the five.

### §5 — The queue and the cut · 800 words

**The claim.** Uber's queue and the film's cut answer the handoff question the same way and hold
different capacities, and one of the routes into distribution has since closed on the kind of film
*Slacker* was.

**First sentence.** Start in the airport lot with the wait time, or in segment 27 with the guest

**Moves.**

| # | Move | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 5a | The queue. Joining the airport queue shows a dynamic estimate of the wait to the next offer, updated on how many riders are requesting, how many drivers are ahead, and recent flight arrivals; queue position is a stated priority factor for trip matches; refusing a request outright, or cancelling after accepting, can reset the position, while a rider's own cancellation does not. The driver cannot see what the passenger paid and is barred from asking. | 200 |
| 5b | The same question, different capacities. The queue hands a driver a specific next party, in sequence, and prices the exchange around him. | 5c | Enclosure, with the date. Smits's 2017 fieldwork found Amazon's route open: rights holders could submit films directly, and the process was "unfiltered, with no gatekeeping restrictions." The same company's page, retrieved 1 August 2026, reads: "Access to self-service submissions is by invitation only." One channel tightened, and it is the channel a film made for $23,000 would have used. | 140 |
