## Step 0 — Register and bar

**Register identified.** This is a first-person film-studies collection chapter. "I" steers; the named film section opens on a street and a credited name; the deferred third and "You seen Gary around?" are signature lines the piece is built around. First person, contractions, scene-first openings, and pointed concessions are off-limits as defects. Quoted dialogue and quoted scholarship are off-limits as defects. I will not convert this into Annals diction, and I will not apply the dissertation's no-first-person rule or the Annals first-person-as-labor rule.

**Bar I will hold it to.** House *invariants* only: named agents and active voice; claim-first; every abstraction touches a case; rhythm variation; no self-narrating rigor; no antithesis machine run as a tic; no inflation vocabulary; no agentless passive that hides who did the thing. Plus this seat's own job: slop as uniformity of optimization; unexplained jargon for a cross-disciplinary / film-collection reader; metaphor doing argumentative work with no literal nearby; collection fit / film-forwardness; word budget. Proposed rewrites stay in the author's diction, shorter or the same length, more concrete. If a rewrite comes out more nominalised than the original, it is deleted.

## Part 2 — Seat-specific audit

### Slop, as this house defines it

**Inflation vocabulary.** I searched the body for tapestry, delve, nuance, testament, crucial, foster, landscape, realm, navigate, furthermore, moreover, additionally, "it is important," "stated plainly," "as we can see," Notably, Crucially, Importantly. None. Still a real clean.

**Antithesis machine.** The house ban is "not X; it's Y" more than once a paragraph. I do not find that tic. Single load-bearing contrasts, which the house *allows*:

- "What interests me in that sentence is less the single drift than the two jobs inside it"
- "Simmel's examples unify — an enemy, a God — and Austin does not"
- "The promise returns. What never returns is the third as a third."
- "He permits aloofness, enmity, and the absence of any meeting. What he does not permit is a community of people who have not each, individually, claimed the same past."
- "Austin looks at first like a focus of that kind… But the people on screen share sidewalks and form no cluster."
- "Sharing a city is not sharing a past."

Once each, not stacked. The voice pass *dropped* several v10 couplets that were the tic: "I am not saying the film predicted a platform. I am saying…"; "This chapter cannot show that fluency. It can show…"; "The film's form is that holding, not Simmel's bond"; "That is not a collapse. It is the arrangement"; "The tautology is not a silence. It is a reason"; "The face is not gone. It is behind a rule." Do not "fix" the six that remain into softer prose. They are the piece being itself.

**Nominalization / jargon-substitution.** The body prefers verbs: Linklater films, Soldani writes, Simmel postpones, Royce waits, Reich described, the doorman charges, the cut selects. The nouns that sit in the subject slot are mostly the chapter's own toolkit (deferred third, reach, selection, algorithmacy, grant) and are defined. "Extra-diegetic join" is gone. I am not going to invent a nominalization crisis.

**Performed rigor.** Finding 3 is the residue. Otherwise absent. "I will call what he set aside the deferred third" is a naming move. "I am developing elsewhere a name, algorithmacy" is the one leftover virtue-narration of a coinage. **Agentless passives that hide agents.** Rare. "The same powers… are written down" is Finding 1's setup.
