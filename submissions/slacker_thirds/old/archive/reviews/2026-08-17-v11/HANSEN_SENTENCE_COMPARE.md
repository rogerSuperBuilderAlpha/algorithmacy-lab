## Kept (Hansen already writes this construction)

Quoted Hansen on the left; Hunt sentence on the right.

- "What interests me in this account is less the intellectual and artistic intertext than the connection it suggests…" → "What interests me in that sentence is less the single drift than the two jobs inside it…"
- "Whether or not one agrees with the postmodernist challenge…" → "Whether that agent is a person or an algorithm I leave open."
- "As early as 1926, Jean Renoir uses the phrase… A more specific usage of the term occurs in… After the Occupation, critics, notably André Bazin, began to speak of…" → Simmel named / Royce defined / Reich described (three named-agent sentences, not welded).
- "Yuri Tsivian distinguishes between two kinds of Americanism…" → Soldani, Stark and Pais, Feld 1981, Warner, Barns, Viljoen.
- "I am referring to this kind of modernism as 'vernacular'…" → "I will call what he set aside the deferred third…"
- "This is not to say that it meant the same thing." → "Warner's public is addressed." / "That is not the cut taking the city's job."
- "To be sure, Soviet montage aesthetics did not emerge fullblown…" → concessions in stride ("even if he walks into the next street").
- "But Gary never appears." kept as Hansen's short turn: "But what about other genres?"
- Simmel / Royce / Reich "gives" kept as three sentences: same construction as Renoir / Brasillach / Bazin, not a welded catalog.
- Taxi/driver walk kept as Hansen's Pordenone paragraph: several sentences walking a viewing.
