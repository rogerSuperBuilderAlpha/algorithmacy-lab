## 2. Adjudications

**A. Overall verdict (five minor).**
Minor wins. Morning's two majors (content film errors; argument architecture) are no longer
majors. Content's two residues would have been majors in v9; they are now local misdescriptions
on an otherwise accurate sidewalk. Argument's joints hold; v11 dropped the sentences that
demonstrated two of them. Flow, citations, and style describe a chapter that is already itself
on the sidewalk. Keep that. Do not use it to ignore the two film facts.

**B. Closed item 2: Content says regresses; Argument / Flow / Style say holds.**
Content is right about the *claim*. The banned phrases are gone. "Parties who never meet" and
"The cut that follows is selection" reassert what the phrases were banning. Argument is right
that "the cut that follows is selection" was meant to keep the cut from becoming a second
deferred third (closed item 12). That lock cannot be proved from an opening the chapter's own
note describes as a witnessing. Until the disc is watched, write only the taxi monologue, the
accident, the card, the son's house, and the rhyme. Keep "the cut is selection" for later joins
that actually select a new face (Gary; dawn). Item 12 still holds as apparatus. Item 2
**regresses**.

**C. "The film walks those refusals in that order": Argument and Flow want it back; Style says do not restore.**
Argument and Flow independently want the same sentence, for different jobs (a join the scenes
then satisfy; a hook so the Gary heading and the taxi open stop fighting). Style heard a
table-of-contents landing. It is not the umbrella. The umbrella four-landing is closed; this is
one itinerary at a section close. Adopt restore. Style's "do not restore" is adopted only as a
ban on putting the holds-strangers sentence back after the taxi, at the reach close, or in
conclusion P1.

**D. Doorman: Argument says grant, not a third of the binary; Flow/Style treat item 13 as holding.**
No contradiction. Item 13 asked that city and cut not swap, and that people may use reach. That
holds. The remainder — a doorman named as reach, as selection, and as "a third in person" — is
Argument Finding 2. Adopt: people on reach, cut on selection, door as Reich's grant. Do not
call that a return of "strict."

**E. Algorithmacy lock: Argument wants "This chapter cannot show that fluency"; Style called the v10 couplet a tic; Content says the hedge-drop aligns definition with exhibit.**
The v9 identity (algorithmacy = arguing with a door) has not returned. Note 4 and the close
still demote the word. What v11 cost is the body buffer. Restore the one restriction. It is
not an "I am not saying / I am saying" couplet. Do not add occurrences. Do not put the word in
the close.

**F. Headroom: Style says spend 300–500 on film; Argument says do not add theory; Flow says do not flatten scene-cuts to chase 83%.**
No contradiction. Spend on splitting the stamp, hedging the opening, maybe one more list-item
scene (Paul, PixelVision, funeral lift). Do not spend on Warner / Duranton / a second LSEG
venue. Flow already flagged those as second teachings after a film landing.

**G. Abstract drift: Argument says still major, not fatal, not absorbable without a letter.**
Adopted. Unchanged from morning. City/cut and the platform sting remain if an editor squints.
Hosting/engineering, camera ≈ Uber, and cinema-as-training are gone, not renamed. That is the
author's letter, not a sentence a reviewer can paste. v11 did not reopen it and did not close
it.
