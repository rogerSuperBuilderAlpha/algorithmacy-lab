# Argument review — hostile architecture

**Manuscript:** "'You seen Gary around?': The Deferred Third of *Slacker*" (`chapter.md` = v11)  
**Venue:** Bloomsbury, *Slacker: Answering the True Call — Essays on Linklater's Cult Classic*, eds. Sara Bizarro and Melissa Remark  
**Seat:** Argument. Hostile architectural referee. Not a copyedit. Not a scene census.  
**Standpoint:** I am trying to break the chapter's load-bearing joints. Closed v9 items that still hold are not re-opened. A voice-pass hedge-drop is a finding only when it costs a lock the morning panel required. I do not convert the prose to Annals register.

**Recommendation, up front:** minor revisions. The v9 joints that failed — five referents for "deferred third," three patrons with no derivation, a "strict" binary the middle stretch contradicted, algorithmacy identified with arguing at a door, "the platform removes it" — were repaired in v10 and are still repaired. What v11 did is drop the sentences that *demonstrated* two of those repairs. An editor can now follow one arrangement through three refusals if she is looking for the Origins close. She will not feel that join in the film section, and she will not recognise the chapter the collection accepted. The first is a paste. The second is a letter only the author can write.

This is a first-person film-studies collection chapter. "I" steers; scenes before theses; contractions; pointed concessions. That register is the assignment. I will not flag first person, contractions, or the signature lines the piece is built around ("You seen Gary around?"; the deferred third as a phrase). What I judge hard:

- Whether Simmel's postponement, Royce's membership test, and Reich's grant join in a way a reader can feel, or have fallen back into adjacent lectures.
- Whether reach/selection sorts the film's own cases, including the middle stretch and the doorman.

House invariants I will use only as argumentative pressure, not as a style pass: named agents, claim-first, every abstraction touches a case. When a construct never touches a case, or touches the wrong case, that is an architectural failure.

## Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage

### Finding 1 — The join is written once and not walked

**Claim.** v10 locked the honest join the morning panel asked for: one afternoon, three refusals of the same arrangement, walked in order. v11 kept the lock sentence and cut the walk. The heading still tells the older truth. A reader can parse the derivation. She will not feel it.

**Quote.** Origins now ends:

> Simmel gives the distance: a third that does not enter the pair. Royce gives the membership that distance refuses — no yes, no community. Reich gives the form the distance takes when a face still has to administer it, a grant spoken at a door. One afternoon, three refusals of the same arrangement: nobody introduced, nobody claimed, nobody given a reason, and somebody still there to be asked.

v10's next sentence is gone:

> The film walks those refusals in that order: a taxi and a body, a door, a missing friend.

The section that contains the join is still headed "Philosophical Origins in Simmel's Postponement and Royce's Canoe." Reich is in the last paragraph and not in the title. The film section then does walk taxi/body, then the door, then Gary — and never says it is walking the three refusals.

**Why it damages this lens.** A join a reader can feel is not a sentence that is true. It is a sentence the scenes then satisfy in view. Cut the walking clause and the film section is again a tour: Simmel on the opening cut, Reich at the Blue Bayou, Royce at Quackenbush's. Each scene still earns its local predicate. The derivation — distance, therefore no yes, and when a face still administers access the form is a grant — sits one heading back, in a section named for two patrons.

Test of a felt join, not of a written one: can a reader who starts at "The film opens on a street within the first five minutes" recover why these three names are one argument without paging up? On v10 she could; the order sentence was the last thing she had read. On v11 she cannot. Independently detachable patrons were the morning definition of adjacent lectures. They are still detachable. The claim that they are not is now doing the work the walk was doing.

The conclusion's roll call does not save this:

> Simmel describes the distance, Royce the membership test, Reich the grant, Stark and Pais the co-optive enrollment.

Four names, four jobs. That is the same cadence the morning panel called official. It is no longer a contradiction of a missing join. It is a restatement of a join the film pages declined to enact.

**What would fix it.** Put the walking sentence back, or give each film landing one local refusal ("nobody introduced"; "nobody given a reason"; "nobody claimed") so the Origins close is paid in the scenes. If Reich is a co-equal refusal, he belongs in the Origins heading or the heading should name the arrangement, not two of three patrons.

The Origins → film section is otherwise clean for this lens. The unify-versus-hold distinction is owned. Royce's second condition now holds on the Drag, so Gary is a near-miss rather than a default. Those are closed repairs. I am not re-opening them.

### Finding 2 — The doorman still will not sit in reach/selection

**Claim.** The middle stretch is now sorted. The doorman is named three ways in three paragraphs and is the one exhibit the two-term division still cannot absorb. This is not the old "strict division" fatal. "Strict" is gone. The remainder is the chapter's best scene.

**Quote.** In the long film paragraph:

> Those are people using the city's reach. The cut still chooses which of those uses we watch, and the doorman, the same afternoon, selects in person — a list, a refusal, a face — which is not the camera's job either.

At the head of Reach, Selection:

> The film keeps a division of labor between reach and selection that it never lets the city and the cut swap. It does let people, and one doorman, use the city's reach, which is still the arrangement holding without introducing.

Then, after Feld and Barns:

> The city supplies the one, the cut supplies the other, and the doorman, the same afternoon, supplies a third in person.

**Why it damages this lens.** People asking for a lift, passing a stamp, saying an address: that is reach being used. The morning panel asked for that paragraph. It is here. The binary sorts the extra-diegetic opening and close, and it now sorts the middle. Good.

The door is not that operation. A doorman who charges three dollars, checks a list, and says "Don't see you" is not using the city's holding. He is administering a grant. Calling that "use the city's reach, which is still the arrangement holding without introducing" is the weld v10 did not quite make. v10 kept "people, and one doorman, use the city's reach" and then, in a separate sentence, "That is not a collapse. It is the arrangement doing what it said: hold, do not introduce." v11 fuses them. The fusion misclassifies the exhibit. The door does not hold without introducing. It decides.

"Selects in person" and "supplies a third in person" then give him back the job the binary said only the cut has. A two-term division that must call the same man reach, selection, and a third is not a sort of the film's own cases. It is a sort of the city and the cut, with the chapter's best scene left over. Closed item 13 asked for that remainder to be owned, not re-absorbed as "still the arrangement."

Duranton and Puga remain a precedent for separability, not for an assignment of agents. That is a cheap underwrite, not a new crack. I mention it so it is not mistaken for support.

**What would fix it.** Keep people on reach. Keep the cut on selection. Give the doorman Reich's name — a grant spoken at a door — and stop making him a third of the binary. The Reach section already has the sentence that does this work if you cut the weld: the city and the cut do not swap; a face can still administer access inside that arrangement. That is the join from Origins, applied. It is not "supplies a third."

### Finding 4 — An editor will not recognise the accepted chapter

**Claim.** Vocabulary moved, then the argument moved. Reach/selection is hosting/engineering renamed. The rest is a different chapter. Drift is still major, still not fatal, still not absorbable without a letter. v11 did not reopen this. It also did not close it. Recognition is possible only if the editor is willing to take the city/cut split and the platform sting as the chapter she bought.

**Quote.** The accepted abstract promised:

> *Slacker* contains two distinct kinds of third: "hosting" and "engineering." … Austin hosts while the camera engineers. … The parallel between Linklater's camera and Uber's algorithm is formal and specific. Both select, rank, and filter among parties that never negotiate directly with each other. … I'll argue that cinema has trained viewers to read engineering thirds for a century.

The live chapter promises a deferred third, a sidewalk that still has a face, Royce's failed yes, Reich's grant, and a coined competence developed elsewhere. Uber appears as deactivation statistics, not as the camera's formal twin. Lofland, civil inattention, and the pap-smear pitch are gone. The training subject has changed: the abstract trained *viewers* by letting them watch an engineering third; v11 shows *participants* arguing with a door.

**Why it damages this lens.** Collection terms already flag the terminology change as an author-only reply. The live text is not a terminology change. It is a new spine (distance + three refusals + faced grant) under a renamed split (reach/selection) with a demoted use-object (algorithmacy) and a mechanism (keep the powers, withdraw the person) instead of a camera–Uber parallel. What remains visible if you squint: two kinds of third; Austin versus camera; platforms as the contemporary comparison; Stark and Pais; the film as making something about platforms visible; no return, no resolution.

An editor who assigned the slot for the camera–Uber / cinema-training / hosting-becomes-engineering piece will notice. The abstract's distinctive claims are not renamed. They are gone. The collection accepted the abstract *pending editorial review*. That is the moment an unannounced rebuild gets sent back or treated as a different submission.

I am not saying the old architecture was better. I am saying recognition is not available as a reading of the live text. It is available as a negotiation: city/cut is the descendant of hosting/engineering; the faced third is what the chapter now thinks the film can show. That negotiation is the letter. This seat flags the drift. It does not draft the letter.

**What would fix it.** Not a sentence I can write. The author tells the editors what moved and why the city/cut split plus the faced third is still the chapter they bought. If they wanted camera ≈ Uber and cinema-as-training, those claims have to come back or the slot has to be re-sold.

## Verdict

**Minor revisions.**

The chapter has a film argument and, as of v10, a theoretical join that can carry it. The film argument is: a city holds strangers and introduces none of them; a cut sequences some of them for us; in the middle they close meetings themselves; a door still has to say the rule; platforms keep the powers and withdraw the person. That is a *Slacker* chapter. The join is: one afternoon, three refusals of that arrangement, with somebody still there to be asked. v11 kept the join as a sentence and dropped the walk; it kept the binary's repair and misclassified the doorman; it kept algorithmacy demoted and dropped the lock. Those are revisions, not a rebuild. The superstructure that failed in v9 — five referents, no derivation, a strict division, a coined competence the film cannot show, a slogan the notes refuse — does not fail now.

**Single most important fix.** Walk the three refusals through the film section. Everything else (the doorman, the algorithmacy buffer, the abstract letter) is downstream of whether a reader can feel that the afternoon is one argument.

**Biggest genuine strength.** The Origins close plus the middle stretch. For the first time the patrons have a derivation, and the film has a paragraph that sorts extra-diegetic sequence from people using reach. The double opening and the Blue Bayou door-plus-stamp are still the scenes that make the chapter itself. Trust them. They do not need the postponement narrative to be true, and they now have a join that can use them.

**The one thing only the author can supply.** Whether the editors still want the chapter they accepted — hosting/engineering, camera ≈ Uber, cinema trained viewers — or will take a faced-third / city-cut rebuild under a deferred third. No revision I can paste answers that. Collection terms already put it on the author. Until that letter exists, every architectural improvement is work on a chapter whose warrant in the book is unsettled.
