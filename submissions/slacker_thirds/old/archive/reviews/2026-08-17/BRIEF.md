## Register and bar (every reviewer)

**Register:** first-person film-studies collection chapter in the Hansen/Bordwell manner. "I"
steers; scenes before theses; contractions; pointed concessions. That register is deliberate.
The dissertation's no-first-person rule and the Annals "I marks labor only" rule **do not apply**.

**Off-limits as prose defects:** quoted dialogue and quoted scholarship; accepted-abstract
verbatim lines if any survive; first person; contractions; a small number of signature lines the
piece is built around ("You seen Gary around?"; the deferred third).

**The cardinal rule:** make the chapter better at being itself. Proposed rewrites must be in the
author's diction, shorter or the same length, more concrete. Delete any suggestion that comes out
more nominalised, more hedged, or more journal-generic than what it replaces.

**House invariants that do still bind** (`~/.claude/writing-style.md`): named agents and active
voice; claim-first; every abstraction touches a case; verified citations; rhythm variation; no
self-narrating rigor; no antithesis machine run as a tic.

**Cohesion bar** (flow seat owns it; others may cite it): tail-head linkage; end-focus; rename the
thread entity; gloss-weld; reformulation; forward/backward paragraph hooks; group parallel
evidence. Mechanical locators from the uncalibrated `slacker` register (run 2026-08-17 on v9):
tail-head 39%; 81% of paragraph joins share no entity; 24.6% of sentences under 10 words (venue
mean 8.2%). Those are locators, not verdicts. The register's corpus is 42 essays from four
open-access film collections, not single-film *Slacker* chapters; do not treat a z-score as a
fail.

**Verification:** before proposing a factual or citation correction, say whether you retrieved the
source in this session (`verified`) or inferred (`unverified`). Do not assert a correction from an
unverified source — put it in VERIFY. Open pages where the network allows. Do not invent locators.

**Do not edit the chapter.** Write only to your assigned review file.

## Output format (every review file)

Write to the path assigned below. Structure:

1. **Title block** — lens name, manuscript title, venue, your standpoint.
2. **Step 0 — Register and bar** — confirm the register; state what you will judge hard.
3. **Part 1 — Findings, ranked by damage** — section-aware. For each finding: claim, quote,
   why it damages *your* lens, what would fix it. If a section is clean for your lens, say so
   in one sentence and move on.
4. **Part 2 — Seat-specific audit** — see the seat description.
5. **Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions** — highest-value fixes only, ready to drop in, in the
   author's voice.
6. **Verdict** — accept as-is / minor revisions / major revisions / reject. Single most
   important fix. Biggest genuine strength. The one thing only the author can supply.
7. **VERIFY** — empirical or citation claims that need sourcing before press.
8. **What this seat is not judging.**

Be specific. Prefer fewer, sharper findings over a laundry list. Quote the manuscript. Do not
soft-pedal; do not invent problems to fill the form. Each review is a complete critical
commentary from its lens, not a capped bullet list.

## Load-bearing claims this panel should press (not a findings list — questions)

1. Simmel postponed a third that unifies from a distance; the conflict-chapter return never
   restores the third *as a third*. Is that reading of *Soziologie* 103 / 321 defensible?
2. Royce's community of memory requires each member's yes; a day of unclaimed strangers fails
   it. Are the three conditions cumulative as used, and is "Gary" a fair test case?
3. The Blue Bayou door instantiates Reich's grant (price, list, refusal, withheld reason).
   The chapter admits the four powers are its taxonomy, not Reich's. Does the scene still
   carry the diagnosis?
4. Reach (city) and selection (cut) are a strict division of labour the film never lets
   collapse. Does the film actually keep that split, including the middle stretch where
   "people on screen have taken the joins over"?
5. Algorithmacy — fluency with determinations that arrive without reasons — is this chapter's
   term, unpublished. Does the film show that competence, or only the arrangement that would
   demand it?
6. The accepted abstract promised hosting/engineering thirds and a camera–Uber parallel.
   v9 promises a deferred third and a sidewalk that still has a face. Can an editor recognise
   the accepted chapter?
