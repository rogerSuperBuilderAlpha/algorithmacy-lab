Second, the missing rivals. Third, the selector needs restating. R1's two structural findings converge with R2's stipulation demand: "a selection no character makes" is contradicted by the film and by the chapter's own fourth section, "the cut" misdescribes hand-offs that happen inside single takes, and "third" slides between entity and condition after the chapter concedes the distinction. The repairs are a narrower claim (no character selects what the film follows at the transitions), consistent use of "the camera," and one stipulation paragraph at the end of the Simmel section.

Fourth, the platform section is the weak half. R3's four majors line up with R5's register finding: the no-address thesis ignores appeal channels and EU explanation rights, the Amazon exhibit is a category slip (a title declined, where the doorman refuses a person), the co-optation analogy inverts Stark and Pais (the city holds without co-opting; co-optation is what platforms added), and the doorman's addressability is silently upgraded into accountability.
