# Reviewer 4 — volume editors (philosophy / film studies)

Checked against the accepted abstract and the collection terms. Body verified at 3,865 words; notes 1,996; bibliography 823 (total 6,688).

## Findings

1. MAJOR — The anti-narrativity engagement conflates two authors, and the editor who holds the position will not fully recognize her view. The body cites Bizarro's Strawsonian reading but the Strawsonian machinery (Episodic/Diachronic self-experience) appears only in the endnote; the body recasts her claim as one about formal coordination, and "proves too much" is run against a composite whose target sentence ("frustration ... is the point") is Marlovits. Her actual thesis operates at the level of the self and is arguably untouched, even supported, by the chapter's externality claim. Fix: split the positions in the body; state the Strawsonian core in one plain sentence; direct "proves too much" explicitly at the formal extension; add one sentence granting that the coordination argument supplies the self-experience thesis its missing complement — the characters can be Episodic BECAUSE the city and the camera do the joining for them. That turns an attack into a contribution.

2. MAJOR — Divergence from the accepted abstract is large and undeclared; a cover letter is mandatory. Promised and absent: (a) hosting/engineering terminology (now reach/selection, drifted twice without telling the editors); (b) the named Uber parallel (the deactivation report is literally about Uber and Lyft drivers; name Uber once); (c) the century-of-training claim, now retracted in note 12 without acknowledgment — well lost, but the letter must own it. The Madonna Pap smear seller, the abstract's promised example, deserves one sentence. Fix: cover letter folded into the confirmation reply; name Uber in the platform section; consider one sentence or note recording that reach/selection supersede hosting/engineering.

3. MAJOR — Unfinished verification is visible in the submitted apparatus. Note 9: "wording to be checked against the article before quotation." Note 3: "I have not confirmed the witnessing from the Criterion disc." Note 2 sources all dialogue to a subtitle track it admits paraphrases, while the argument leans on exact spoken lines ("S-T-E-V-E. Steve plus three or four"). Fix: verify every quoted line against the Criterion disc; delete the to-do sentences; retain note 2's disclosure only for lines that remain unverifiable.

4. MAJOR — The notes endanger the word ceiling. Body 3,865 is inside 3,000–5,000, but 32 endnotes run 1,996 words (52 percent of body); body plus notes is 5,861 before the bibliography. Notes 12, 14, 16, 22 are mini-essays. Fix: confirm with the editors what the count includes; cut notes to citation plus at most one sentence of commentary (note 16 sheds the German philology; note 22 loses Van Alstyne, Murray, Cohen; note 12 loses the Bordwell paragraph). Target under 1,000 note words.

5. MAJOR — Three stretches will lose the non-academic half of the readership. (a) Simmel section, the "properly sociological interaction ... configurations of two" paragraph: translated Simmel syntax; paraphrase concretely (God and two believers never sit at one table; there are only ever pairs). (b) The 300-word Royce paragraph with the verbatim three-conditions digest: break in two; render the conditions in ordinary English (you can tell your life as a story; you can talk to the others; you all claim the same event as yours); trust the canoe. (c) "Reach, selection, and the refusal to resolve" paras 2–3: four apparatuses (Feld, Barns, Warner, Duranton–Puga) in ~350 words; keep Feld, move Warner and Duranton–Puga to notes, compress Barns to a clause. Same for Viljoen's "horizontal, population-based data relation": gloss as "grouped by what they have in common, not by anything they ever claimed."

(c) The four-part bibliography split is discouraged for a chapter; merge alphabetical, or confirm whether the volume wants chapter bibliographies at all given full-form endnotes. (d) Markdown [^n] must render as true endnotes. (e) US spelling clean; sentence-case headings acceptable. (f) Kegley (IEP) and Bizarro (APA blog) need URLs/access dates.

## Editors' decision note

Accept with revisions. This is the strongest formal argument we have received on the film's construction, and the Steve-at-the-door reading belongs in the volume; the "true call" hitchhiker even earns our title its epigraph. But it is not yet the chapter we accepted: hosting/engineering, Uber, and the century-of-training claim have vanished without a word, and the training claim is quietly retracted in a note — the revision letter must own all three. The apparatus contains visible to-do items and dialogue sourced to a paraphrasing subtitle track; both are disqualifying at final draft. Notes must shrink below the ceiling risk, and three identified stretches need translation for our non-academic readers. The one change that matters most: rewrite intro paragraph 5 so the Strawsonian position is stated in its own terms and the coordination argument is offered as its complement. Resubmit by October 1 for feedback.
