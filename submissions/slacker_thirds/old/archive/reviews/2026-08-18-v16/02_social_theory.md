# Reviewer 2 — social theory (Simmel, Royce)

Method: 1902 AJS serialization checked against the block quote and surround; Royce, The Problem of Christianity vol. 2 (1913 scan) checked for the definition, canoe passage, aloofness sentences; 1908 Soziologie TOC checked for the extent of Der Streit.

## Findings

1. MAJOR — The novelty sentence misdescribes its neighbors and omits the two closest competitors. Body: "where platforms have entered Simmel's triad at all in the recent literature, they have entered it as thirds of the between, brokers profiting from the parties on either side." Neuberger (KZfSS 2022), the closest published competitor, treats platforms as mediating thirds, and appears nowhere; Ajunwa's tertius bifrons (2020) is absent; note 17 itself concedes Stark and Vanden Broeck describe an outside-the-relation apex, contradicting the body's "only as thirds of the between." Fix: three-way taxonomy (between-brokers: Vallas & Schor, Ajunwa; mediating thirds: Neuberger; outside apex without Simmel: Stark et al.), and narrow the claim to what is unoccupied: nobody has recovered and named the third of the postponed passage.

2. MAJOR — The negative claim rests on a survey the chapter does not show. "The deferred third has remained where Simmel left it" is a claim about the whole sociology-of-the-third literature (Bedorf 2003; Eßlinger et al. 2010; Bedorf/Fischer/Lindemann 2010; Fischer 2013; Lindemann 2005), none cited. The claim holds per the research file, but a priority claim with an invisible evidentiary base will not be certified. Fix: add a note listing the Theorien des Dritten corpus and stating that its catalogues (messenger, parasite, translator, mediator, observer) contain no distant unifier as a named figure.

3. MAJOR — The chapter equivocates on "third" after conceding the grounds for the equivocation. "The third returns as the condition of a bond rather than as the member of a triad" is followed by continued entity-talk ("a third that will not give one"). Two slippages: the distant third is never a triad-member (only Zweierkonfigurationen obtain, as note 14 renders); and Simmel's postponed figure is defined by its unifying function, while the chapter expressly says "Austin does neither." The city is twice removed from the figure whose name it borrows. The architecture survives only if the usage is stipulated. Fix: one stipulation paragraph at the end of the Simmel section ("third" hereafter names the outside term of the second dyadic configuration; "the deferred third" names the postponed figure; Austin instantiates its geometry while withholding its unifying effect, and whether holding-without-unifying can ground membership is what the Royce section tests). Audit every subsequent "third."

4. MAJOR — The Roycean verdict on the Gary scene rests on interpretive divergence, which Royce's own example tolerates. The Tai-Nui men "may keep aloof... They may be enemies" (verified, 2:46): divergent valuation does not entail non-identity of the event; two believers with incompatible theologies share the one God. As written the argument would dissolve the Maori community the moment they disputed the voyage's meaning. Locate the failure in non-acceptance: the asker seeks the fact rather than incorporating it; the talker does not accept Gary's absence at all — he substitutes a different putative event (recruitment). Fix: reargue on non-acceptance grounds and meet the permissiveness objection explicitly. (Textual apparatus otherwise sound: 2:50 definition verified verbatim; Tai-Nui hyphenation is Royce's own; conditions match SEP §2.2.2.)

5. MINOR — "The triad chapter never redeems the promise" is technically true (Der Streit spans 247–336; the returns at 310/321 fall outside the triad chapter) but misleading: the promised "later connection" is arguably exactly p. 321. Fix: "The promise is not redeemed within the triad chapter; when Simmel makes good on it, two hundred pages later, the third has changed kind."

6. MINOR — The gloss on the ellipsis misdescribes the elided syntax. In both 1908 German and 1902 AJS the examples are inside the first sentence (dash/colon-appended), not "between those two sentences." Verified 1902: "produces a unification between them — beginning with the league of states... and extending to the 'invisible church'..." Nothing stands between the church example and the deferral sentence; the elision hides nothing argumentative, but the description of where the cut falls is wrong. Fix: check Wolff 145–46; quote the passage whole (the omitted matter is short) or emend the gloss.

7. QUERY — The p. 321 specifics (subject index entry "Distanz des vereinigenden Momentes 321"; both examples recurring) exceed what was verified this round. If the full-text search has been run, quote the p. 321 German verbatim in the note; if not, check before the note stands.

8. QUERY — Note 14's German needs exact-wording confirmation: the note italicizes Zweierkonfigurationen as though quoting the 1908 text; supply the full German sentence so the quotation/paraphrase boundary is inspectable; confirm "andern" vs "anderen."

9. MINOR — Terms of art used before definition, and the "two jobs" scheme suppresses the third act. "Grant" is deployed two sections before Reich licenses it; "reach" and "selection" are defined retrospectively; and the chapter's own analysis insists the grant "is neither the camera's job nor the city's holding," contradicting "two jobs, two agents." Fix: one-clause definitions at first occurrence; amend the intro to "two jobs and a third act performed at the doors."

10. QUERY — Homonymy in the Amazon quotation: Amazon's "selection" names title admission (the grant-function), not the chapter's term. A one-line note disambiguating would close the gap.

## Verdict

The primary-text spine is sounder than most submissions: Royce quotations, Tai-Nui spelling, the 1902 deferral sentence, and the German ground-of-deferral all check out, and the deferral-without-commentator claim is true per the research. But the chapter undersells its verification (2), overclaims against competitors its own notes half-concede (1), lets the central term slide between entity and condition (3), and rests its one Royce application on an inference Royce's own example blocks (4). All four repairable without structural change. Recommendation: MINOR REVISIONS, findings 1–4 mandatory, re-review of the revised Simmel and Royce sections.
