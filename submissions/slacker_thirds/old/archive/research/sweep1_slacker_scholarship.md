### 1a. Editors' introduction (Wilkins & Vermeulen)
**Verified at:** [Cambridge Core intro page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/refocus-the-films-of-richard-linklater/introduction-linklaters-itinerant-oeuvre/8B91B3BF24DE679C87F46EDA45BEBA77) (excerpt only; full text paywalled).
Central claim: the itinerancy is itself the coherence — an intentional creative strategy of moving between modes, citing Rob Stone's "cubist" framework (Linklater "rejects a single viewpoint"). The framing question of the volume is the oeuvre-level version of the single-film coherence question; nobody in the volume scales it down to the mechanism inside Slacker.

### 1b. Erickson, "The Myths of Slacker, Austin, and Richard Linklater" (pp. 15–33)
**Verified at:** [Cambridge Core chapter abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/refocus-the-films-of-richard-linklater/i-think-i-still-qualify-as-a-slacker-just-one-thats-currently-lucky-the-myths-of-slacker-austin-and-richard-linklater/85042859FE98BBD44FCD0A74A040A369); [De Gruyter](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9781474493840-004/html).
Central claim: Slacker has become emblematic of three overlapping myths — a generation, a city, and a director; the chapter interrogates the gap between the 1990/91 film and its later symbolic career (National Film Registry 2012; mourning Austin's transformation). She explains the film's cultural coherence (why it congealed into an emblem), not its formal coherence. Angle: Austin as myth → Austin as infrastructure.

### 1c. Boom, "On Drifts and Swerves: Linklater's Love for Lacunae" (pp. 67–82)
**Verified at:** [Cambridge Core chapter page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/refocus-the-films-of-richard-linklater/on-drifts-and-swerves-linklaters-love-for-lacunae/3E5CEB47A918ADF99314BCE4A9FD8EE4).
Central claim: Linklater constructs films as meandering journeys with deliberate lacunae; Slacker "maps out many of the interlaced and molecular lives" of drifting youngsters via "an itinerant and swerving camera," producing "a collection of tiny little universes." Coherence emerges through nomadic camera work and gaps. (Whether he invokes Lucretius/clinamen or the dérive: UNVERIFIED without full text.) Closest existing account to the camera-as-selector claim, but aesthetic/Deleuzian; does not ask who does the selecting or what the selection grants and refuses.

## 2. Rob Stone, The Cinema of Richard Linklater: Walk, Don't Run (Wallflower/Columbia UP, 2013; rev. 2nd edn 2018)

**Verified at:** [Columbia UP](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-cinema-of-richard-linklater/9780231165525/); [Columbia Scholarship Online chapter abstract](https://academic.oup.com/columbia-scholarship-online/book/20252/chapter-abstract/179315040?redirectedFrom=fulltext); [De Gruyter 2nd edn](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.7312/ston17920/html?lang=en); [CUP blog Q&A 2018](https://cupblog.org/2018/05/24/qa-with-rob-stone-on-the-cinema-of-richard-linklater-walk-dont-run-second-edition/); [CUP blog essay "Cityscapes, Flânerie and Fantastical Heritage"](https://cupblog.org/2018/05/24/cityscapes-flanerie-and-fantastical-heritage/); [2013 CUP interview](https://cupblog.org/2013/06/04/interview-with-rob-stone-author-of-the-cinema-of-richard-linklater/).
Slacker chapter: "The Form and Content of Slack." Reads Linklater through Modernist cinema; "the movement of these films is the movement of the characters therein"; flânerie as "philosophy in action"; long takes "represent the present continuous tense." Coherence answer: time and walking. Gap: flânerie is first-person, the walker chooses; in Slacker the camera chooses. In-chapter uses of Bergson/dérive UNVERIFIED page-by-page.

## 3. David T. Johnson, Richard Linklater (Contemporary Film Directors, University of Illinois Press, 2012)

**Verified at:** [Screening the Past review](https://www.screeningthepast.com/issue-36-reviews/richard-linklater-contemporary-film-directors-series/); [Austin Chronicle review](https://www.austinchronicle.com/screens/2012-05-04/richard-linklater/).
Central claim: Linklater's signature is "a fascination with temporality, particularly in attending to the present" (p. 7). Coherence answer: shared temporal mode. A distributive property, not a connective mechanism.

## 4. Maria Teresa Soldani, "The Performance of the Austin Indie Scene in Slacker: From the Body of a Scene to the Body of a Generation," Imaginations 7.2 (31 March 2017), DOI 10.17742/IMAGE.VOS.7-2.4. Open access.

**Verified (full text fetched):** [imaginations.space](https://imaginations.space/?p=9161); [journal record](https://journals.library.ualberta.ca/imaginations/index.php/imaginations/article/view/29352).
Central claim: Slacker transforms the Austin indie scene's collective body into a generational statement (Mannheim). The most explicit coherence account in the literature, four mechanisms: (1) 24-hour dawn-to-dawn frame (city-symphony form adapted from the European tradition — NOTE: this is her claim, usable as the citation for the frame); (2) spatial synecdoche (the Drag as microcosm); (3) collective performance (scene members playing themselves); (4) metafilmic self-reflexivity. Apparatus: screenplay-as-"Roadmap"; Debord's dérive and Bakhtin's chronotope; Deleuze's time-image. What holds the film together for her: shared duration, not causality. Gap: the dérive reveals; it does not select or grant access.

## Coherence question: the nine published answers

1. Relay/baton hand-off — Małecka 2015; Walters 1990.
2. Ideas/conversation as motor — Price 2003; Bartie; Speed 2007 (partially verified).
3. Slacker ethos / thematic unity — Małecka 2015; Miley 2019; Johnson 2012.
4. Austin/the scene as body — Soldani 2017 (fullest); Erickson 2022 (myth); Macor 2010 (production evidence); Psaltidis 2024 (DIY ecosystem).
5. City-symphony 24-hour frame — Soldani 2017.
6. Time/duration/eternal present, flânerie — Stone 2013/2018; Johnson 2012.
7. Drift, swerve, lacunae — Boom 2022; Wilkins & Vermeulen 2022; Soldani 2017.
8. Incoherence as the point — Marlovits 2013; Bizarro 2025.
9. Spectatorship as the unifying site — Oake 2004; Newman 2011; Radwan 2000 (UNVERIFIED in detail).

**Gap confirmed:** no published work connecting Slacker to algorithmic platforms, Simmel, or coordination/brokerage. No existing answer treats holding and selecting as two functions of one arrangement. Most directly extends Soldani and Boom; corrects Price and Stone; completes Erickson; answers Marlovits/Bizarro.
