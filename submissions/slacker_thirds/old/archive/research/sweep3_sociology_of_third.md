## 0. Primary anchor (verified)

The passage exists in English earlier than Wolff, in Simmel's own AJS serialization: "The Number of Members as Determining the Sociological Form of the Group: II" (AJS, 1902; full text: https://brocku.ca/MeadProject/Simmel/Simmel_1902b.html). The fetched text contains both moves:
- External unification: "the common relationship of isolated elements to a potentiality existing outside of themselves produces a unification between them — beginning with the league of states which is formed for defense against a common enemy, and extending to the 'invisible church,' which composes all believers into a unity, through the like relation of all to the one God."
- The deferral, 1902 translation: "This society-constructing mediation of a third element is, however, to be treated in a later connection." Simmel adds that "a real sociological reciprocity which embraces the three elements in a unity is not at hand."

No one in any cluster below comments on the deferral sentence as such. The passage's content has commentators; its deferral does not.

## 1. German "sociology of the third" revival

- Bedorf, Fischer, Lindemann (eds.), Theorien des Dritten (Fink, 2010) — existence verified ([JPortal](https://zs.thulb.uni-jena.de/receive/jportal_jparticle_00281624)); no English translation found.
- Joachim Fischer, "Turn to the Third" in Malkmus & Cooper (eds.), Dialectic and Paradox (Peter Lang, 2013), 81–102 — verified against [Fischer's publications page](https://fischer-joachim.org/en/publications/papers.html). Tertiarität functions: observation, arbitration/mediation, coalition/competition, substitution/representation, scapegoating ([project page](https://fischer-joachim.org/en/projects/social-ontology-turn-to-the-third.html)). No distant/outside third as distinct figure.
- Bedorf, Dimensionen des Dritten (Fink, 2003) — verified via [MUSE review](https://muse.jhu.edu/article/169098/pdf). Levinasian third of justice; co-present in the dyad, opposite pole from the distant third.
- Lindemann, "The Analysis of the Borders of the Social World," JTSB 35(1), 2005, 69–98 — [PhilPapers abstract](https://philpapers.org/rec/LINTAO): "Not the dyad but the triad must be understood as the foundational constellation." Her tertius: the (virtual, anonymous) observer whose anticipated perspective institutionalizes norms — absent-but-internal, not an outside unifier.
- Eßlinger, Schlechtriemen, Schweitzer, Zons (eds.), Die Figur des Dritten (Suhrkamp, 2010) — Koschorke preface fetched ([Suhrkamp PDF](https://res.cloudinary.com/suhrkamp/image/upload/v1742117482/44927.pdf)): figures are messenger, parasite, translator, trickster, rival; the third operates BETWEEN binaries ("no own position but relates positions on both sides"). No distant third in the catalogue.
- Cluster answer: the revival's thirds are in-between or absent-but-constitutive-inside. No one isolates Simmel's distant third as a distinct, named figure.

## 2. Simmel scholarship on the external-unification passage

- **Elizaveta Kostrova, "The Dyad and the Third Party," AVANT IX(2), 2018** — full PDF fetched (https://avant.edu.pl/wp-content/uploads/E_Kostrova-The_Dyad_and_the_Third_Party.pdf). Closest existing commentary: notes Simmel "observed the internal association of community that is often built on the basis of an attitude toward some external third party" (common enemies, love for Christ), then routes into phenomenology (Levinas, Waldenfels, Marion) and family studies. The external third is an example, not her concept.
- **Olli Pyyhtinen, "Being-with: Georg Simmel's Sociology of Association," TCS 26(5), 2009** — abstract fetched (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0263276409106353): "a third element is always included also in the dyad, be it only as an excluded third." Most sophisticated English Simmelian on thirdness; no separate treatment of the outside unifier or the deferral.
- Bart Nooteboom, "Simmel's Treatise on the Triad (1908)," Journal of Institutional Economics 2(3), 2006, 365–383 — [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/cup/jinsec/v2y2006i03p365-383_00.html). Instrumental use; no external third.
- Routledge International Handbook of Simmel Studies (ed. Fitzi, 2021) — [TOC fetched](https://www.routledge.com/The-Routledge-International-Handbook-of-Simmel-Studies/Fitzi/p/book/9780367559519): no chapter on the dyad/triad at all.

## 4. Simmel's third applied to media/technology

- **Christoph Neuberger, "Journalismus und Plattformen als vermittelnde Dritte in der digitalen Öffentlichkeit," KZfSS 74(1), 2022, 159–181** — fetched (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11577-022-00832-9; OA: [econstor](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/307291/1/s11577-022-00832-9.pdf)). The single closest published competitor. Explicitly Simmelian (mediating/separating/laughing thirds; cites Fischer, Lindemann, Bedorf, Koschorke). Platforms mediate "without producing content." Limits: German; public-sphere frame; his platform-third is the in-between mediator, not the outside unifier; no film, no city.
- Lambros Roumbanis, "On algorithmic mediations," European Journal of Social Theory (2025) — fetched (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13684310251319677). Algorithms as mediating third parties, but apparatus is Sartre's (practico-inert, seriality); Simmel incidental.
- Michel Serres, The Parasite — the third who lives on a relation by intercepting it; maximally inside the channel, the structural inverse of the deferred third. Koschorke credits Serres with "temporal priority of the third." Wolfe intro ([Media Theory excerpt](https://mediatheoryjournal.org/cary-wolfe-bring-the-noise/), UNVERIFIED); org-studies application (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ntwe.12253, UNVERIFIED).
- Simmel + infrastructure/city as third: nothing found. Money-as-third (Philosophy of Money, the "absolute means" outside every exchange it enables) apparently unworked as the distant third — open.

## 5. Stark & Pais / Stark & Vanden Broeck (verified in full text)

- **Stark & Pais, Sociologica 14(3), 2020, 47–72**, DOI 10.6092/issn.1971-8853/12221 ([page](https://sociologica.unibo.it/article/view/12221); [PDF](https://sociologica.unibo.it/article/download/12221/12280)). **No Simmel, no tertius, no "third party" anywhere — confirmed by full-text check.** But heavily triangular: "In a triangular geometry, platform owners co-opt the behavior of providers and users, enrolling them in practices of algorithmic management without managerial authority having been delegated to them." "Möbius organizational form" with "neither an outside nor an inside." Accountability "twisted rather than circular."
- **Stark & Vanden Broeck, Organization Theory 5(2), 2024**, DOI 10.1177/26317877241257213 ([Sage](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/26317877241257213); [author PDF](https://davidcstark.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/principles-of-algorithmic-managment.final_.pdf)). No Simmel/tertius (confirmed). "Whereas actors in hierarchies command, in markets they contract, and in networks collaborate, on platforms they are co-opted." Accountability "twisted" — "Neither vertical nor horizontal... peculiarly shaped feedback loops." Adopts Vallas & Schor's triangular structure.
- Upshot: Stark et al. built the structural geometry of the deferred third with zero Simmel and zero third-figure vocabulary. The Simmelian naming of their triangle is unclaimed.

## Claim space

Unoccupied formulation: a third that unifies isolated elements from outside, without standing between them and without entering the relation — the third Simmel names and then postpones — recovered as a distinct figure ("the deferred third"), read first through a film's city and then through platforms. Every element is free:

1. The deferral has no commentator; the 2021 Routledge Handbook has no triad chapter. Naming rights to "the deferred third" appear fully open.
2. The figure has no owner: the entire third-lineage runs on in-between thirds or constitutive-inside thirds; the distant third is exampled everywhere (common enemy, invisible church) and theorized nowhere.
3. Closest occupants in order: (a) Neuberger 2022; (b) Vallas & Schor 2020; (c) Stark & Pais 2020 / Stark & Vanden Broeck 2024 — the exact geometry with no Simmel: the chapter can name what they describe; (d) Kostrova 2018; (e) Ajunwa 2020.
4. No film reading exists. The triad literature has never been run through cinema.
5. The Royce extension is doubly free.

Defensible positioning sentence: others have put platforms in Simmel's triad as brokers between parties (Vallas & Schor; Ajunwa) or as mediating thirds of the public sphere (Neuberger), and others have described the platform's outside-the-relation apex without Simmel (Stark & Pais; Stark & Vanden Broeck) — no one has recovered the third Simmel deferred, the one that unifies without mediating, and no one has read it through a film.
