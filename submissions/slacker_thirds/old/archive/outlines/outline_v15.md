Governed by the fused claim below, which supersedes the 2026-08-18 "start over" lock once CLAIM.md is updated. Every section has one job. No section exists to display a source. Target body 4,000–4,500 words inside the 3,000–5,000 range; budgets below sum to ~4,300.

## Fused claim

**Research question (the chapter's question, reader-facing):** How do you end up next to someone you were never introduced to?

**Scholarly register (the conversation the question enters):** the coherence debate in the *Slacker* literature. One faction says the missing plot is the point (Marlovits 2013; Bizarro 2025). Berg named the shape — the daisy chain plot, *Slacker* his defining example — and explained nothing (Berg 2006, 24–26). The drift readings describe the camera's path and miss its office (Ramirez 2011; Soldani 2017; Stone 2013; Boom 2022; Price 2003).

**Thesis:** You end up next to a stranger because a deferred third does two jobs neither of you does. Austin holds the next person in reach and introduces nobody. The camera selects which person we see. On that sidewalk, access is still a grant spoken by someone — a price, a list, a refusal, no further reason — and the person who says it can still be asked. Platforms keep the holding, the selecting, and the grant, and withdraw the person.

## Open — the question in the first five minutes

**Job:** Ask the proximity question through the film's opening. State the two jobs. Enter the coherence debate. Fence Bordwell as ground-clearing.

**Budget:** ~550 words.

- A man gets out of a cab and a woman is already lying in the street. Nobody introduced them. The film does this for a hundred people and a whole day, and the day holds.
- The question is not why the film lacks a plot. The editors' own position has that right: there is no protagonist, no story, no thread, and the refusal is deliberate (Bizarro 2025; Marlovits 2013 — the viewer's frustration is the point). Concede all of it at the level of plot. The remainder is the question: strip every narrative binding agent and the film is still one thing, and everyone still ends up next to someone. Something is doing that work.
- Berg named this structure the daisy chain plot — one protagonist to the next, never revisited — with *Slacker* as the defining example. A taxonomy names; it does not explain who performs the jump or on what authority (note: Berg 2006, 24–26; verify exact wording at the library).
- Two jobs, in plain English: the city holds; the camera selects.
- Bordwell fence, reframed: he is right that network narratives are not symptoms of the internet, and his reasoning clears the ground. If the arrangement is older than the technology, the platform did not invent it. It staffed a vacancy. (Keep to two sentences. The vacancy line belongs to the close if it crowds the open.)

## The drift readings, split

**Job:** Take the nearest published accounts seriously and split them. Drift describes the path; the office is the point.

**Budget:** ~450 words.

- The best existing readings all reach for drift: the camera's dérive through pool halls and coffeehouses creating "a seamless urban tissue" (Ramirez 2011); the dérive that reveals a generation (Soldani 2017); flânerie as philosophy in action (Stone 2013/2018); the camera following "whatever person happens to walk into" the conversation (Price 2003); swerves and lacunae (Boom 2022, note only if space).
- Drift is aimless and first-person. Neither fits. The characters do not choose whom we see next, and the cut is not wandering. It decides, every time, and never goes back.
- Splitting drift yields the two jobs. The tissue Ramirez sees is the city's holding. The handoffs he notes ("the character who walks away participates in the next scene") are the camera's selecting. One observation, two offices, two agents.
- Erickson closes the section: the newest scholarship treats Austin as myth — the emblem a generation uses to mourn a city (Erickson 2022). The correction: Austin in the film is not backdrop or myth but working infrastructure. What kind, exactly, is the next section's question.
- Jeong gets one sentence here or a note: he alone saw the camera-agent ("the camera's hesitation about whom to follow") and then psychologized it. The camera is not hesitating. It is administering (Jeong 2021).

## Simmel — the deferred third

**Job:** Justify "deferred third" for the city's job. Unchanged from v14 in substance; add the 1902 anchor.

**Budget:** ~650 words.

- The taxi passenger and the woman in the street share a city. Nobody introduces them.
- Simmel's triad chapter knows the pair-adjacent thirds: mediator, *tertius gaudens*, *divide et impera*. Each already knows the pair.
- On the opening page, a third that unifies without standing between anyone — isolated elements unified by a common relation to something outside them; the enemy, the invisible church — then the postponement. Wolff: "will be discussed in a later context." The 1902 AJS serialization has the same sentence a half-century earlier: "This society-constructing mediation of a third element is, however, to be treated in a later connection" (new note; strengthens the philology, one sentence in body at most).
- I call what he set aside the deferred third: a third so far off it never comes between an identified pair.
- Simmel's examples unify. Austin does not. It holds the bus-station man and the people around the struck woman in one city and introduces nobody. That is why the city's job belongs under the postponement.
- Conflict-chapter return: the distance becomes a condition of a bond, not a third in a triad. That is enough.
- Discipline holds: no Neuberger, Burt, Obstfeld survey in the body. One acknowledging endnote now required by the record: platforms have entered Simmel's triad before, as *tertius gaudens* (Vallas & Schor 2020) and as mediating third (Neuberger 2022); both are between-thirds; the deferred third is not between (single note, three sentences maximum).

## Royce — holding is not membership

**Job:** Unchanged from v14. Holding is not membership; that is why Gary's absence is not a shared past.

**Budget:** ~450 words.

- Community of memory: each member accepts the same past as his own. Tai-Nui: they may be enemies, they may never meet, they must each claim the canoe.
- Three conditions, cumulative; the second (communication) and third (same claimed event) matter here.
- A day of unclaimed strangers cannot meet the test. Sharing a city is not sharing a past.
- Point forward: the film will ask after Gary and not produce him.

## Film — the two jobs, and someone still there to be asked

**Job:** Show holding, selecting, and the grant in the scenes. Largest section.

**Budget:** ~1,100 words.

- Taxi: Linklater talks; the driver never answers; "Man, shit. I should've stayed at the bus station." Location notes put the drop on 24th; the disc hedge on the hit-and-run stays (note carried from v14).
- After the drop: the struck woman, the jogger, the card pressed on a stranger, the son taken away. In the taxi he had said there is always someone getting run over. The rhyme is in the lines. The city holds both; the cut puts them in sequence; nobody introduces them. The first five minutes already show the split.
- Steve at the Blue Bayou: three dollars, the list he believes he owns, "Don't see you." Reich: access as a grant whose procedures augment the grantor's power; denial on undisclosed grounds "should no longer be tolerated" — a demand the door never reaches. The grant is still spoken to a person. Steve can answer the door even if the door will not answer him.
- The other door: the stamp copied wrist to wrist, over an objection that stops no one. A stamp is property, showable and transferable between strangers. The list was a grant, confirmable only in the grantor's own record. The contrast carries the platform section later; earn it here.
- Closing time: "It's past 2:00. Everybody out." The rule said aloud, addressed to people who could argue back.
- Gary: "You seen Gary around?" Nobody has. The talker converts absence into conspiracy. The sidewalk held both men; the cut framed them; nobody introduced them. Contemporaries in communication (Royce's second condition); neither claims the absence as the same past (third fails).
- People use the city's reach out loud: a lift, an address, the van, the stamp, the PixelVision camera with its terms said. The cut still chooses which use we watch. The doorman is neither job: he administers.
- Unpaid labor: Cokes, T-shirts, the funeral hitchhiker, the man at Les Amis waiting for the true call. The city holds him and hires him for nothing. Stark and Pais name the later version: co-optation, enrollment in management nobody delegated. Here, someone is still there to be asked.

## Platforms — same jobs, same grant, no address

**Job:** The person withdrawn. Pose the no-address question; do not answer it.

**Budget:** ~550 words.

- Amazon: unless otherwise indicated, no appeal, no further reason (living page, access date, note carried).
- Rideshare Drivers United: two-thirds deactivated; thirty percent given no reason.
- Drivers contribute without being hired; the counterparty that removes them has removed anyone who could be argued with.
- Steve's list failed in public and the group could still talk on the sidewalk. An app assigns a queue whether or not the driver assents.
- Viljoen: enrollment without a yes is not membership. Rideshare Drivers United exists because drivers organized to compel an answer — Royce's second condition rebuilt from below.
- Video Backpacker stays only as the one sentence that earns the allocation point: you cannot rewind the allocation. If it does not earn, cut it (v14 rule carried).
- Section ends on the posed question, unanswered: the film shows what it took to live with a grant when someone still had to say it. What it takes when nothing says it is a different question, and this chapter only shows where it became askable. (Guard: no named skill, no training claim, no research program. One paragraph.)

## Reach, selection, refusal to resolve

**Job:** Keep the two jobs from swapping. The film will not close Gary.

**Budget:** ~350 words.

- Feld: a focus produces a cluster. Austin looks like a focus and forms no cluster. What remains is reach.
- Warner: a public is addressed; it does not choose which two members meet next. That choosing is the cut's job.
- Duranton and Puga: match probability and match quality are already separable in the economics. The film shows the separation done by different agents.
- To produce Gary, specify the true call, or bring the bus-station man back at dusk would be to supply the introductions the arrangement does not make.

## Close

**Job:** Restate the two jobs and the grant without anyone to ask. Gary last.

**Budget:** ~250 words.

- Taxi and struck woman share a city. Steve shares a sidewalk with a doorman who can charge him, check a list, turn him away, and give no further reason.
- The platform keeps those acts and withdraws the person who had to say them. The arrangement was on the sidewalk first; the platform staffed the vacancy (if the vacancy line was not spent in the open).
- End on the question the city will not answer: whether anyone has seen Gary around.
