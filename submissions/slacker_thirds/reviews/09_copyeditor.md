## Verdict

**Minor fixes.** The chapter is genuinely close to press-ready: prose clean, arithmetic and cross-references sound, reference list correctly alphabetized end to end, citations resolving both directions with no orphans. What stands between it and clean is a short, mechanical list — normalize two "…" glyphs to spaced periods, italicize *Slacker* in three reference entries, add one missing essay page span (Benjamin), italicize one *tertius*, and supply the four-to-five direct-quote page locators that only the author or the physical sources can provide. All are surface corrections; none touch argument or voice.

**The cleanest thing:** the reference-list alphabetization. Every adjacent pair, including the deliberately tricky Kuleshov/KUT, Rosenbaum/Rosenblat, Stone/Stovel, and the two same-first-author Stark entries, is in strict order — a list of sixty that many copyeditors would find at least one slip in has none.

**The one check that needs the author or the physical source:** the direct-quote page locators — Macor 2010, Bordwell 1985, Ramírez Berg 2006, and Stark and Pais 2020 — together with the exact wording and dot-count of the Bordwell "wiggly, knotted string" ellipsis (p. 215) and the on-disc rendering of Linklater's credited character name (line 12, "Should have stayed at the bus station" — confirm capitalization/wording against the Criterion disc, spine #247). These cannot be resolved from the manuscript alone.
