# Seat 03 — major-revisions review (C)

**Verdict as submitted: major revisions.** Strong original insight; the Steve/Uber comparison is the most publishable contribution. Analogies must be identified as extensions or functional comparisons rather than identities.

## Findings adopted

- Holding, selecting, and granting do not operate at the same analytical level. Uber's selection allocates economic opportunities; the camera selects representations. Functional claim: city makes encounters possible, camera determines which encounter viewers follow, doorman controls access; platforms centralize comparable functions.
- "A property that every scene possesses cannot join any two of them" overstates. Shared tone coheres aesthetically; it does not explain succession.
- "A genuine refusal of coordination would play as shuffle" conflates narrative refusal with spatial unintelligibility. Fence with Ramirez: the walker who leaves participates in the next scene.
- Simmel: claim the revision directly. God and the enemy are external objects of common orientation; Austin contains the people. Define "deferred third" as an entity that structures relations without entering as mediator or member.
- Royce: not "the exact test." Condition 2: capable of communication *and* "in general, engaged in communication." Tai-Nui: aloof or hostile, not "never exchange a word." Distinguish factual belief from identity-forming acceptance on the Drag.
- Reich overreaches: guest-list place is not automatically "new property." Reproduce procedural asymmetry; do not equate stakes. *Goldberg* is for qualifying state-created entitlements, not all public largess.
- Note 25: scene establishes only that the doorman denies finding Steve; whether Steve pays or enters remains unconfirmed.
- Hand stamp: transferable *credential*, not property (copying may be unauthorized).
- "The reach comes with no management attached" is too categorical.
- PixelVision is a limited formal analogue, not an ancestor.
- "One act" → functionally comparable exercises of record-based discretionary access control.
- Review Center: preserve channels while separating them from decision authority.
