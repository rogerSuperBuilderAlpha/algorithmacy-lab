**Flags (exact quote → tell → rewrite):**

   here is the viewer, not the characters." (0 words.) Or, active and one word cheaper: "The camera
   coordinates the viewer, not the characters" — but that loses the topicalizing stress; the first

## Verdict

**Minor revisions — mechanical only.** The chapter is press-close: quotations verified against every
source the repo holds (all six new film quotes are verbatim to the transcript), arithmetic and dates
sound at every point I could compute, notes and bibliography resolving cleanly in both directions, the
list perfectly alphabetized, the body exactly at 4,998 words. Two things stand between it and clean:
the **broken §7 "three questions" callback** (a July 3 accepted fix lost in the rewrite, and now
colliding with §2's different three questions — the single most important fix) and the ***Slacker*-
italics regression** in three bibliography entries plus four notes (also accepted July 3, also
unapplied). Everything else is a one-line paste or an already-scheduled library/disc check.

**Biggest genuine strength, from this lens:** the quotation discipline. Sixteen-plus direct quotes, and
every one that a source in this repo can check — transcript, verification log, research records —
checks out exactly, truncations clean, interpolations correctly outside the marks. That is rare in any
manuscript and essentially unheard of at this citation density.

**The one thing only the author can supply:** the Criterion disc session — six new quotes, the credit
card's exact rendering, the Super-8 ending, and the club-scene attributions all wait on it, and no
reviewer can watch the film for you.
