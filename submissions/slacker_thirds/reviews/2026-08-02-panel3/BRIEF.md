## Step 0 — register (every reviewer)

This is a **first-person film chapter** in the Hansen/Bordwell register: "I" steers, scenes before theses, contractions, concessions taken in stride. That register is deliberate and off-limits as a defect. The dissertation's no-first-person rule does **not** apply here.

**Off-limits to style flags:** quoted material; accepted-abstract verbatim lines (see `abstract.md`); first person; contractions; em-dashes used as beats; load-bearing concessions.

**The bar:** make the chapter better at being itself. Proposed rewrites must be shorter or the same length, more concrete, in the author's diction. Delete any of your own suggestions that come out more nominalised or more hedged than what they replace.

## Four bans (every reviewer audits; lens 11 owns)

1. **Metaphor doing argumentative work** — a figure carrying a claim that has no literal statement nearby.
2. **Throat-clearing** — announcing that a point is coming, then delivering the point.
3. **Meta-commentary / forward-backward reference** — "as described above," "matters later," "worth keeping," roadmaps.
4. **Unexplained jargon** — terms a cross-disciplinary reader might not own, left bare.

Also audit AI-slop structural tells (negative parallelism / antithesis machine, metronomic landing lines, pseudo-cleft openers, decorative "rather than"). See `research/slop_trends_2026.md`.

## Verification rule

Before proposing a **factual** or **citation** correction, say whether you retrieved the source in this session (`verified`) or inferred (`unverified`). Do not assert a correction from an unverified source — put it in a VERIFY section. Open PDFs / pages where the network allows; do not invent locators.
