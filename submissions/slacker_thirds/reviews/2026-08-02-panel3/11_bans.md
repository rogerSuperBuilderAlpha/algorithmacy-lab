## Part 1 — Theoretical rigor and structure

Only three surviving ban hits affect the argument's load-bearing frame.

**1. §2 hides the broker/setting distinction inside network jargon.** The chapter's central claim about
brokerage is right, but the sentence asks the reader to know *formal apparatus*, *ordered triple*,
*measure*, and *point in a network* at once:

> The formal apparatus is built on an ordered triple of named actors, and every measure that came out of
> it is a property of a point in a network — which a setting does not have.

Paste-ready fix:

> The formal apparatus starts from three named actors: the two parties and the one between them. Every
> measure it produced scores a person in that network, and a setting is not a person.

**2. §4's answer to Poulaki still turns on a private metaphor.** This sentence carries the move from
Poulaki's system criterion to the chapter's ordered-visits claim:

> Her own floor is the answer: "A catalogue is not a system but a list of elements; a system needs
> relations between the elements in order to form itself."

*Floor* is doing conceptual work that the quotation can do without it. Paste-ready fix:

> Her own minimum for a system answers the objection: "A catalogue is not a system but a list of
> elements; a system needs relations between the elements in order to form itself."

**3. §7's climactic setting/selector answer returns to spatial metaphor at the point that most needs
plain definition.**

> When its choices stop being one route across a field the parties could cross by themselves, and start
> being the only route.

Paste-ready fix:

> When the parties could have found each other without it, it is still a setting. When it becomes the
> only way they can meet, it is a selector.

### Ban 1 — metaphor doing argumentative work

**B1. "Her own floor is the answer" (§4).** Live hit. See Part 1.

**B2. "One gate refused them, and it had a name. The earlier gate had responded" (§7).** The first
sentence has a literal referent: Susan Blodgett refused the video release. The second makes *gate*
carry the earlier acquisition process without naming who said yes.

Paste-ready fix:

> One refusal had a name. So did the yes before it: a paragraph in *Film Comment* reached a buyer, who
> telephoned relatives in Austin to ask whether the Dobie sellouts were real.

**B3. "Here the seat shifts" (§7).** *Seat* is a residue of the earlier apparatus and now carries the
turn from viewer-side selection to supplier-side selection.

Paste-ready fix:

> The landing page selects among titles for a viewer. The supplier side of the same platform shows where
> the powers are written down.

**B4. "When its choices stop being one route across a field..." (§7).** Live hit. See Part 1.

**B5. "What replaced that chain narrows what reaches a viewer twice over" (§7).** The chain is literal
enough in the prior paragraph. *Narrows* and *twice over* compress licensing and prominence before the
reader has the two steps.

Paste-ready fix:

> Streaming narrows what reaches a viewer in two steps, and the two steps are separate.

**Protected or cleared figures.** "Hand-holding" is Linklater's quoted word and sits close enough to the
quotation. "Bare chain" and "ordered walk" are glossed immediately. "Every link in the chain ... could
be telephoned" is almost literal and should stay. The closing "took nothing from them except a day, and
gave the day back watchable" rests on §5's literal powers and should stay.

### Ban 2 — throat-clearing

**B6. "One more thing the questions do not do is demote" (§2).** The next two sentences state the point.
The opener announces the author's guardrail.

Paste-ready fix:

> A selector that answers no to the third question is still a selector — the camera picks a person out
> of the crowd and knows nothing whatever about anybody.

**B7. "What I am claiming is narrower than it first looks, and it has prior art I should name" (§2).**
The prior-art paragraph is important. The first sentence talks about its own narrowness before making
the narrowed claim.

Paste-ready fix:

> Sociologists have reviewed the setting and the broker together before: Rivera, Soderstrom and Uzzi sort
> the causes of tie formation into assortative, relational, and proximity mechanisms, with Feld and Burt
> in the same reference list.

**B8. "Two qualifications, both real" (§2).** The concessions are strong. They do not need a credential
line.

Paste-ready fix:

> Mario Small and Leah Gose call routine organisations brokers outright, though the conditions they list
> for successful brokerage are all properties of institutional norms, and not one requires the third to
> know which two people are involved.

**B9. "Her account gives me two further things" (§4).** The paragraph works if it starts with the first
thing.

Paste-ready fix:

> What links *Gomorrah*, on her reading, is "primarily the setting ... and the character of Maria" — a
> setting and a selector, side by side, inside her own analysis.

**B10. "What a decade of subsequent work adds is a precise account..." (§5).** This is a light hit. The
sentence announces the coming list and then immediately announces that the list is unusual.

Paste-ready fix:

> A decade of subsequent work shows three things a selector can do that a camera cannot. They are not the
> standard list.

### Ban 3 — meta-commentary / forward-backward reference

**B11. "The camera's linking work is not a discovery and I am not claiming it as one" (§1).** The first
clause concedes priority. The second narrates the author's credit discipline.

Paste-ready fix:

> The camera's linking work is not a discovery. My question is what kind of coordinator a camera is.

**B12. "the two definitions above" (§2).** This is the banned backward-reference form.

Paste-ready fix:

> They sort thirds that operate where people are already reachable.

**B13. "it is the difference the rest of this turns on" (§4).** The sentence tells the reader how to use
the distinction.

Paste-ready fix:

> That is a real difference from Uber, because selectors do two different jobs.

**B14. "and later the landing page of a streaming service" / "and later Uber" (§4).** Both are forward
references in a paragraph that can define the two selector kinds without previewing later sections.

Paste-ready fix:

> Some choose what an audience sees next — a camera or a recommendation page. Some choose who does
> business with whom — a taxi dispatcher or Uber.

**B15. "All I can say is that surge has not been shown to know who the two parties are" (§5).** This
narrates the author's evidentiary boundary. The boundary itself is sound.

Paste-ready fix:

> No published evidence shows that surge knows who the two parties are, and a model that abstracts away
> from identity establishes nothing about identity.

**B16. "I will assert the differential pay and leave the personalisation where she leaves it" (§5).**
The evidence distinction matters, but the line makes the author's conduct the subject.

Paste-ready fix:

> The evidence supports differential pay. Personalisation remains where Dubal leaves it: plausible,
> contested, and not established.

**B17. "which is the scale to hold in mind before comparing any of this with a recommender" (§7).** The
scale is already concrete: sixteen theatres at widest.

Paste-ready fix:

> Orion Classics bought it and released it nationally, in sixteen theatres at its widest.

**B18. "what follows is the supplier's side of the same platform, which is where the powers happen to be
written down" (§7).** The clause tells the reader why the turn matters before making the turn.

Paste-ready fix:

> Amazon publishes its own account of how titles are licensed for Prime Video, and two of the three
> powers are on the page.

**Cleared or protected.** "The authors propose this rather than show it" is evidence marking, not
authorial self-commentary. "That is a ruling on a preliminary injunction and not a final judgment" is a
legal scope note and should stay. The final "I cannot answer" is a load-bearing concession in the
chapter's register and should stay.

Paste-ready fix:

### AI-slop structural audit

The chapter is clean at the structural level. It does not have filler transitions, canned paragraph
openers, fake balance, vague "observers say" attribution, booster adverbs, or a uniform sentence rhythm.
The follow-up improved the profile.

Three small clusters remain:

1. **Negative parallelism is concentrated in the last third.** Most instances earn their keep because a
   wrong alternative is live: "rather than the act of matching," "not a final judgment," "not between a
   cut and a match." The decorative ones are "the video release was starved ... rather than killed" and
   "contestable rather than proprietary."
2. **Numbered-list throat-clearing clusters in §5.** "The first is," "The second is," "The third is" are
   fine because the section really enumerates powers. "It comes to three things. They are not the
   standard list" is the slop edge.
3. **Repetition around "what" openers.** "What it does not argue," "What nobody has asked," "What makes
   these scenes," "What connects," "What holds," "What changed," and "What would make" are individually
   ordinary. They are noticeable as a cluster because the chapter's best paragraphs avoid that frame.
   No global rewrite is needed.
