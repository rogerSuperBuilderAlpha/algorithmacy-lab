## 2. Adjudications

**A. Overall verdict (2 major vs 3 minor).**
Major wins. Content's two film errors are the kind a collection editor circles
before she reaches Simmel. Argument's architectural crack is independent of
those errors and would remain if they were fixed. Flow/style/citations describe
a chapter that is already itself on the sidewalk. Keep that. Do not use it to
downgrade the majors.

**B. Algorithmacy: flow says carry it; argument says demote it.**
Argument is right about the *term*; flow is right about the *join*. The word is
coined in paragraph 3 as fluency with determinations "without anyone to answer
them," unused until the conclusion reintroduces it with "I called that
competence algorithmacy at the outset," and demonstrated as arguing with a
door, copying a stamp, saying a join out loud — the opposite of the definition.
Carrying it more would make the contradiction louder. Fix: align the definition
with the exhibit, or drop the word from the close and let the door/stamp/ask
stand. Do not add occurrences.

**C. The opening as the chapter's best exhibit (argument) vs a factually
unbridged opening (content).**
Content is right, and it damages argument's exhibit. If Linklater's passenger
is on the sidewalk when the woman is struck — which three published sources
say — then "two parties and a city, never three terms in one field" is false of
the opening, and the unnoticed-echo claim is false. The deferred-third reading
can still be argued of *other* joins (Gary; the people who never meet). It
cannot be proved from an opening the city's own film-commission page describes
as a witnessing. Until the disc is watched, write only the taxi monologue, the
accident, the card, the son's house, and the rhyme in the lines.

**D. "Steve is refused without a reason" (content) vs Reich's withheld reason
(argument).**
Content is right about the scene. The door says "Don't see you" / "You're not
on the list." That is a reason. What is withheld is why the list Steve believes
he owns does not have him. The four-power taxonomy can survive if the body
says so. "Without a reason" as a report of the scene cannot.

**E. Semantic restatement (flow) vs landing-line habit (style) vs three-patron
roll call (argument).**
The same defect, three names. The umbrella — holds strangers / arranges none /
a third you can argue with — re-lands in the opening, after the taxi, at the
reach close, and in the conclusion. Style heard it as epigram metronome.
Argument heard it as the three patrons being re-asserted rather than joined.
One fix serves all three: keep the umbrella once, in the opening; let later
sections close on their local predicates (the unnoticed rhyme if the disc
allows it; the door's list; nobody's yes; joins changing hands).

**F. Headroom: style says spend 400–600 on film; argument says do not add
theory.**
No contradiction. Spend on splitting the hitchhikers, hedging the opening,
maybe one more list-item scene. Do not spend on Lindemann / Fuhse / Neuberger /
Koschorke / Warner / Duranton. Flow already flagged those as literature resets
after film landings.

**G. Abstract drift: argument says major, not fatal, not absorbable without a
letter.**
Adopted. City/cut and the platform sting remain if an editor squints.
Hosting/engineering, camera ≈ Uber, and cinema-as-training are gone, not
renamed. That is the author's letter, not a sentence a reviewer can paste.
