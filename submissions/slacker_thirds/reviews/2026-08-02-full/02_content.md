## 1. Errors — fix before anything else

**CT1 (§7). The Bengesser landing-page sentence states the wrong denominator.** The chapter: "inside
a national catalogue the British share of what reaches the landing page is under ten percent." The
article's Table 2 rows are "No. of unique UK titles/co-pros present in national Netflix catalogue …
567 | 602 | 935 | 746" and "No. of UK titles/copros found on the Netflix landing (home) page (LP)
only over 7 days … 51 | 42 | 48 | 46." The denominators are British titles in each national
catalogue, so the supported claim is the outline's: fewer than one British catalogue title in ten
reaches the landing page (9.0, 7.0, 5.1, 6.2 percent). The article reports no total landing-page
counts at all, so "the British share of what reaches the landing page" has no basis. **Verified
against the version of record.** Surgical: "and inside a national catalogue fewer than one British
title in ten reaches the landing page."

**CT2 (§5). The Amsterdam quotation drifted.** The chapter: the human review was "little more than a
purely symbolic act." The project's own translation of ¶3.24 (note 52 says "Translations mine"):
the exhibits "in no way show that this act was much more than a purely symbolic act." The quoted
string does not occur in the established translation; the drift converts an evidentiary finding (the
notes do not show it was more than symbolic) into a positive characterisation. Surgical: requote —
the file notes "in no way show that this act was much more than a purely symbolic act."

**CT3 (§7). "The Dobie booked it on its own judgment" is unsupported and contradicts the note.**
Note 3's own source says "Mr. Linklater booked the original 16-millimeter film into Austin's Dobie
Theater, where it played a record 11 weeks." Nothing in the corpus gives the Dobie the booking
decision. The person-chain point survives either way. Surgical: "Linklater booked it into the Dobie,
which held it eleven weeks."

**CT4 (§7). "so a division's default starved the video release" garbles the correction.** The
research finding: the video release was starved by a division applying a default — Orion Home Video's
standard foreign-language-title treatment, Pierson's "They saw no evidence that *Slacker* should be
treated any differently" — not killed by the Chapter 11 filing. "A division's default" now reads as a
financial default, which is the opposite of the point (the film played three months past the filing).
Surgical: "so what starved the video release was a division applying its default treatment, not the
bankruptcy."

**CT5 (§7). The *Film Comment* anecdote has no citation, and its parts have two different sources.**
"A paragraph in *Film Comment* reached a buyer, who telephoned relatives in Austin to check the Dobie
sellouts" carries no note. The Criterion reprint of Pierson confirms the first half verbatim ("the
substantive *Film Comment* piece had reached me and Michael Barker at Orion Classics") and does
**not** contain the relatives call — checked end to end this pass. The relatives call is Macor's
account, page unconfirmed in the corpus (her sentence is quoted in `deep/2026-08-01_s7.md` only as
"clipped at 'screenings'"). Fix: add a note citing Pierson, "Slacking Off," for the tip, and Macor,
*Chainsaws, Slackers, and Spy Kids*, chap. 4, for the relatives call with the page to be confirmed in
print — the note style the chapter already uses for Moretti.

**CT6 (note 33). The *Papachristou* pin cites are wrong.** The note reads "at 156–58, 164." Against
the official U.S. Reports PDF: "encourages arbitrary and erratic arrests" is at 162 (and in the
syllabus at 156); "amenities of life as we have known them" and "are not mentioned in the
Constitution or in the Bill of Rights" are at 164; "poor people, nonconformists, dissenters, idlers"
is at 170. Surgical: "at 162, 164, 170."

**CT7 (note 58). Three drifts against the Ildirar & Ewing manuscript.** The paper: the experimental
group "lived in small isolated houses in the mountains south of Isparta, Turkey that had only
recently been connected to the electrical grid," and the control group is "from a similar geographic
and cultural background," with television experience. The note says "villages," says the district was
newly on the grid, and says the experienced twenty are "from the same villages." Small, but this
project has been burned by exactly this class of embellishment. Surgical: "from small isolated houses
in the mountains south of Isparta, Turkey, only recently connected to the electrical grid; the twenty
counted as experienced are from a similar background nearby and had had some television."
