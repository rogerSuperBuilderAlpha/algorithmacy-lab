## Locked claim

**Research question:** How do you end up next to someone you were never introduced to?

**Thesis:** This essay argues that *Slacker* shows how a modern platform works: an indifferent machine that packs people into the same space and picks who meets whom, so that connection, shared belief, and a yes are not required.

## Open through Mount Bonnell

**Job:** Ask the proximity question. Dispatch relay, mood, drift, daisy-chain, anti-plot, network-weaver. Name the apparatus. Land the thesis.

- Cab, hit-and-run, camera walks away with the other person. Opt-out would have been staying at the bus station.
- Walkers choose lifts, clubs, whether to talk. They do not choose the next body the camera takes.
- Camera is the web, not a weaver. City keeps placing another stranger within arm's reach.
- Typewriter, thrown camera named once as attempts, with a pointer forward; trashing the gear breaks nothing.

- Simmel = mechanics; Royce = test. Thesis sentence. Characters still talk. What goes obsolete is the requirement that they share a story, a belief, or a yes.

## Not the first to see a platform here

**Job:** Concede priority, claim the mechanism.

- Beauchamp 2019 ("melancholy artifact"; "democratic large-heartedness"); Bramesco 2021 and Broadley 2020 on the harmless local conspiracy theorists; Ravens 2026 on unproductive time vs "restless loops of dopamine," Linklater's "real conspiracy."
- They share: a lost openness, a cut at the internet, a handful of moments. Parallels are their point.
- The mechanism is the whole film: city puts two in reach, camera takes one, nobody asked. Working model, not portrait of what was replaced. Simmel first, then Royce.

## Royce's test for membership

**Job:** Show that being packed together is not a community. Do not cash this out as drivers passing the test.

- Community of memory / expectation: claim the same event as *that happened to me; that is who I am*.
- Film fails among the strangers on screen. Sharing a city is not sharing a history.
- Closer: indifferent apparatus; proximity + selection; no story / belief / yes. The logic was already running when the film was shot; the platform is the same logic with an app. Drivers packed and selected; they just have to be on it.

## No exit, so voice

**Job:** Exit fails; voice is what remains; voice coordinates nothing.

- Typewriter into the creek, *Ulysses* read over it; camera off Mount Bonnell ends the film. The second exit belongs to the film, not to anyone in it.
- Hirschman 1970, between Reich and the float: exit closed → voice, and voice needs an addressee.
- One grouped paragraph, six registers, one line and one reading each: explanation (moon man, *Conspiracy a-Go-Go*); taking (T-shirt terrorist; "you couldn't get a fuckin' TV?"); belief (Old Anarchist, "singular human beings," Hemingway Brigade); each other ("premeditated fun"; "scrap heap"); music (Ultimate Losers; guest list; stamp; "You can't be here"); refusal (café dictation; true call; "Withdrawing in disgust").

- Turn: none of it coordinates; the thesis in the characters' mouths. Hook: one place a voice still meets a face; walk down the Drag.

## The human gatekeeper

**Job:** Consent still has a body. The platform will keep the four moves and drop the body.

- Steve at the door: price, list, decision, no further reason. A person did it.
- Reich largess as callback ("the permission Reich named largess, back in 1964"): someone else keeps the record. *Goldberg* in the note.
- Addressability is not accountability. Co-presence is what the door has and the platform will lack.
- Stamp vs list. PixelVision states terms; the film's camera does not.
- Hitchhiker waits for "the true call" — still listening for a person. The web selects without that.
- Stark & Pais: software doing the camera's job, gatekeeper gone.

## The same machine without a face

**Job:** Same apparatus, last person removed. Packing and selecting without connection, belief, or a yes.

- Platform scholars reach for a between-third. *Slacker* already showed coordination without standing between anyone.
- Four Uber moves. RDU survey as evidence of how the moves land, not as a community.
- Review Center: address without a face. "Escalate to manager" is the dawn recorder; the RDU survey is voice organized; no loyalty asked because no yes asked. EU "shall be taken by a human being."
- Ratings field: click is a yes to the company, not to the grouping. Not shared belief, not connection, not consent to being coordinated.
- One paragraph: crowded city looks like lost counterculture; it is proximity. Platforms add selection. They do not restore connection, belief, or consent.
