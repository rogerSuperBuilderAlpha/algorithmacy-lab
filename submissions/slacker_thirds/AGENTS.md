## The only thesis

This essay argues that *Slacker* shows how a modern platform works: an indifferent machine that packs people into the same space and picks who meets whom, so that connection, shared belief, and a yes are not required.

City = spatial proximity. Camera = technical selection. Connection, belief, and consent can appear on screen; they do not coordinate. Do not argue that platform workers pass Royce.

Two framing rules added 13 September (evening). **Reactive, not predictive:** the platform logic is dated to Reich 1964 and the 1973 float; the film reacts to a machine already running and never predicts, foreshadows, or "links" to one. **No exit, so voice:** the typewriter and the camera fail as exits; what remains is voice (Hirschman 1970), and voice never coordinates.
