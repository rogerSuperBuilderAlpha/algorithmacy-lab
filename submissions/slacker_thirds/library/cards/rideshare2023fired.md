## What it does for the argument
Carries the empirical weight of the 'no reason given' claim. Note 32 already discloses that the
two-thirds figure pools temporary suspension with permanent removal, that recruitment ran
through union and community networks, and that the report acknowledges self-selection.

## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 5: one advocacy survey with acknowledged self-selection carries the chapter's
heaviest empirical load while Rahman's ASQ ethnography and Curchod et al.'s ASQ study exist and
are peer-reviewed. Also, per the law sweep: California had required an appeals process for two
years when this survey ran, which makes the thirty-percent figure evidence of a FAILED remedy -
a stronger fact than the one the chapter now draws from it.
