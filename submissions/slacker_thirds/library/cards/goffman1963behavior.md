## What it does for the argument
CIVIL INATTENTION - "enough visual notice to demonstrate that one appreciates that the other is
present ... while at the next moment withdrawing one's attention" (84). Recognition granted,
engagement withheld, in one gesture. Also the unfocused/focused-interaction split, which is this
domain's own two-jobs distinction.

## Loci
- 84 - the definition of civil inattention

## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 2: unfocused interaction (co-presence, mutual monitoring, no engagement) versus
focused interaction (a face engagement opened by someone toward someone) is holding versus
selecting, in print since 1963, done by world-side agents. The chapter's novelty is that its
SELECTOR is the camera rather than a person - defensible, and it must be said out loud rather
than left for a reviewer to say.
