## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 6: reach is described as uniform. Anderson, Lofland, and the encounter literature
all insist public space holds people differentially by race, gender, and class, and that its
refusals are as structured as its holdings. The chapter concedes this once, about doors, and
never about sidewalks.
