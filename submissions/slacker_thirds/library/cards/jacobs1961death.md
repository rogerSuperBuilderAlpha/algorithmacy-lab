## What it does for the argument
Two things the chapter needs. Sidewalk trust accumulates from "many, many little public sidewalk
contacts" whose precondition is that they oblige nobody - holding without membership,
normatively defended. And the PUBLIC CHARACTER: the stationed person who can be asked.

## Bearing on novelty
Jacobs's public character is the doorman's genus. It is the strongest available support for the
chapter's claim that the answerable person was a real urban institution and not a nostalgia.
