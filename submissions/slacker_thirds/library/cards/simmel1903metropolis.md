## What it does for the argument
RESERVE: the metropolis holds people in permanent contact and withholds acquaintance - "we
frequently do not even know by sight those who have been our neighbors for years." The chapter's
REACH job, stated by the chapter's own author in 1903, and not cited.

## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 1, the sharpest in this domain. The claim that the postponed third "has remained
where Simmel left it" is false of Simmel's own corpus, even if true of the triad literature. A
Simmel reader raises this immediately and the chapter has no sentence ready.
