## What it does for the argument
Cited for the platform playing tertius gaudens at 282, which is correct. The platform sweep's
threat 3 is that the article's actual thesis goes unmentioned: platforms as PERMISSIVE
POTENTATES that govern "not by expanding their control over participants but by relinquishing
important dimensions of control and delegating them to the other two parties."
