## What it does for the argument
The book-length Linklater study and the anchor of the drift answer - walking as the film's
organising motion. The chapter concedes the drift readings describe the camera's path precisely
and objects only to their grammatical person: flanerie and the derive are first-person figures
in which the walker chooses where to turn, and in Slacker the walkers choose nothing about what
we see.
