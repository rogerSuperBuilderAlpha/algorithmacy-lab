## What it does for the argument
Third places. Les Amis, Quackenbush's, Sound Exchange, the club are all third places.

## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 8, and it is a trap not yet stepped around rather than one fallen into. Oldenburg's
THIRD PLACE is a place-type - home, work, hangout. The chapter's THIRD is a party in a
relational geometry. Slacker is set almost entirely in third places and the chapter never says
the two thirds are unrelated. One sentence in a note, at the first cafe or bar, closes it
permanently.
