## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 2, would-change-the-argument. The robo-firing judgment: deactivation of four drivers
held to be a decision based solely on automated processing under GDPR art. 22, the asserted
human review dismissed as "not... much more than a purely symbolic act."

## Bearing on novelty
It forces the chapter to distinguish two things it runs together - a human IN THE DECISION LOOP
and a person IN THE DOORWAY. The doorman's properties were presence, priority in time,
publicity, and the possibility of being answered on the spot. Art. 10(5) supplies only the
first, and this judgment is the proof that supplying only the first supplies nothing.
