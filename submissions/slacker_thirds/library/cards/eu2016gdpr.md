## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 4. Art. 22(3) gives "at least the right to obtain human intervention on the part of
the controller, to express his or her point of view and to contest the decision." Placement: n.
34, opening.

## Bearing on novelty
The requirement of a human who can be told your side is not new and did not begin with the
platform-work directive: directly applicable EU law since 2018, a Member State obligation since
1995. The chapter's 'the law has begun trying' understates by three decades. The correction
costs one clause and makes the structural point better - the vacancy was legislated against long
before anyone thought it a novelty.
