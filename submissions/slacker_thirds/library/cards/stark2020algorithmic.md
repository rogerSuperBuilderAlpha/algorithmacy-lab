## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 1: the same article supplies the chapter's best quotation and its hardest
objection. Value is created "in a social and economic space that is neither inside nor outside
of the platform" (51-54), which contradicts the deferred third's defining outsideness. The
chapter can survive it - the GRANT is a relation, not a position - but only if it says so.
