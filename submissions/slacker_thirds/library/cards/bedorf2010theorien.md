## What it does for the argument
One of the two German collections the chapter uses to support its negative claim: the sociology
of the third catalogues its figures without arriving at a distant unifier.

## Bearing on novelty
The negative claim rests on two edited volumes read for absence. A round-2 sweep of the German
third literature is the honest way to defend it; at present it is asserted from tables of
contents.
