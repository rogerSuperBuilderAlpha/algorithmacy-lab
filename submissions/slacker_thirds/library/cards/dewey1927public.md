## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 3, high. The pragmatist name for people held together without introductions: "The
public consists of all those who are affected by the indirect consequences of transactions to
such an extent that it is deemed necessary to have those consequences systematically cared for"
(15-16); the naming at 35. And the chapter's structure stated in advance: "The local communities
without intent or forecast found their affairs conditioned by remote and invisible
organizations. The Great Society created by steam and electricity may be a society, but it is no
community" (98), with "the resultant public cannot identify and distinguish itself" (126).
Placement: §'Keeping the jobs apart', beside Warner (n. 38), or a note in the Royce section.
