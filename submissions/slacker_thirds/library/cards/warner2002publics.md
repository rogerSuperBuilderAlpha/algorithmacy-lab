## What it does for the argument
The useful contrast on the other side of the chapter's holding claim: a public is a relation
among strangers constituted by attention, addressed as a whole, with nobody choosing which two
members meet next.

## Loci

## Bearing on novelty
Sweep threat 5: the note is in the wrong chapter and the wrong register. A public is discursive,
constituted by attention to circulating text, not a spatial arrangement of bodies. The contrast
still works, but not as drawn. Also: Dewey 1927 is upstream of Warner and a reviewer in the
pragmatism domain will ask where he is.
