## What it does for the argument
The chapter's spine. Wolff's triad chapter supplies the three thirds Simmel does catalogue - the
non-partisan and the mediator (145-47), the tertius gaudens (154-62), divide et impera (162) -
and the chapter's move is that the postponed third, announced at 145 as still to be treated, is
not among them.
