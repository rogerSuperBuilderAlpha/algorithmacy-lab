## What it does for the argument
The counter-movement in the chapter's argument: the law legislating the doorman back into the
doorway. Art. 10(5) requires a human decision; art. 11 requires an explanation and a designated
contact person.

## Loci
- 10(5) - 'shall be taken by a human being', verbatim
- 11(1) - a 'contact person designated by the digital labour platform to discuss and to clarify the facts, circumstances and reasons having led to the decision', with 'the competence, training and authority necessary' - CITED BUT NOT QUOTED

## Bearing on novelty
SWEEP THREAT 6 and PANEL ADDITION RANK 10, at zero words. Art. 11(1) is stronger than the note
lets on: the EU legislated the answerer, and the chapter cites the article without quoting the
sentence. Quoting it strengthens the chapter - the vacancy is real enough to have been
legislated against - and forces the closing question to be re-pitched, from whether the doorman
can be restored to whether a statutory contact person is a doorman at all.
