## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 3, and the sweep calls its omission "the sharpest exposure." Section 13(4)-(7): the
worker "may... request the digital labour platform operator to make a representative of the
operator available to discuss the preliminary deactivation notice"; the operator "must make a
representative available for the discussion within a reasonable time"; a support person may
participate; and "a human representative... must consider the employee-like worker's response
(if any), including the discussion (if any)." Placement: at 'The law has begun trying to
legislate the doorman back into the doorway.'

## Loci
- ss 8, 11, 13; esp. 13(4)-(7)

## Bearing on novelty
This is the chapter's own sentence enacted. Australia does not merely require a human decision-
maker after the fact; it requires a person to be PRODUCED, on request, for a conversation,
BEFORE the deactivation takes effect. Omitting the one instrument that fills the vacancy while
asserting the vacancy is the chapter's most correctable exposure in this domain.
