## What it does for the argument
The click-wrap form of the withheld reason, in one contractual sentence: notice "indicating the
reason and any options to appeal, except where we have reason to believe that providing this
information will hinder the investigation or prevention of deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal
activity."

## Bearing on novelty
Nissenbaum's fourth barrier, ownership without liability, is exactly this form - and dates the
erosion to before the web's commercialization, which inoculates the chapter against presentism.
