## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 8, medium. "A sign, or representamen, is something which stands to somebody for
something in some respect or capacity. IT ADDRESSES SOMEBODY, that is, creates in the mind of
that person an equivalent sign... That sign which it creates I call the interpretant of the
first sign."

## Bearing on novelty
The chapter's whole platform argument turns on addressability. Peirce builds it into the
definition of a sign, and Royce takes the definition over wholesale - which would date the
chapter's key property to 1903 and give it a pedigree rather than a coinage.
