## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 6, medium. Royce wrote an essay about exactly the chapter's object - a place full of
people who did not grow up there. "The stranger, the sojourner, the newcomer, is an inevitable
factor in the life of most American communities" (68). His remedy is a province that
manufactures a claimable past: California's pioneers "seized upon every suggestion that could
give a sense of the unique importance of their new provincial life... This, to us often half-
legendary past, gave us a history of our own" (69-70). Placement: §'Keeping the jobs apart',
beside Feld.

## Loci
