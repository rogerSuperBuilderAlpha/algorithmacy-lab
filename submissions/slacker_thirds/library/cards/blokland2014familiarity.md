## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 1, would-change-the-argument. ABSENT TIES - recognition without interaction - produce
belonging. That is the counter-thesis to the chapter's Roycean verdict that holding is not
membership. Placement: §'Royce's test for membership', or a note at para. 47.

## Bearing on novelty
The chapter can answer it: public familiarity is repeated recognition of the same faces, which
the film's one-day, never-revisited structure precludes. Unanswered, it is the sharpest
available objection in this domain.
