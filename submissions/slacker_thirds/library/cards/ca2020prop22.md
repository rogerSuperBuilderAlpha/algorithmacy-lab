## Bearing on novelty
This reverses the chapter's inference. The thirty-percent-no-reason figure is evidence of a
FAILED remedy, not of an unregulated state of nature - a stronger fact for the chapter and a
different argument from 'that such remedies were thought necessary is itself evidence of the
vacancy'. That same sentence is where PANEL FINDING C4 locates the chapter's only agentless
passive, so one repair can close both.
