## What it does for the argument
SWEEP RANK 1, would-change-the-argument. "In some realms, the street-level bureaucrats have
vanished." The three-stage sequence - street-level, screen-level, system-level - is the
chapter's thesis, made twenty-four years ago about welfare counters rather than club doors, and
it puts a date on the vacancy a decade before the platform. Placement: §'The same jobs without
an answerer', first paragraph, or a note at 'the answerer of the doorman's kind ... does not
[exist]'.

## Bearing on novelty
The sweep supplies the honest answer to what Slacker adds: Bovens and Zouridis lose the clerk to
a system; the chapter shows the same loss where no bureaucracy ever existed, on a public
sidewalk, which makes the loss general rather than administrative. This is the single most
important card in the deck for the novelty question.
