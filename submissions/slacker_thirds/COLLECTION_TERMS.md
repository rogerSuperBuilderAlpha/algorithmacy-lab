## The AI-use policy — author decision, not an agent's

The editors attached **Bloomsbury's AI Policy for Authors and Illustrators (Dec 2025)** and pointed
specifically at "the specifics about private AI systems." This chapter's pipeline is AI-assisted
throughout — drafting, review panels, research packets, verification votes. Whatever the policy
requires (disclosure, a warranty the author cannot give, a limit on what may be machine-drafted) is
the author's to read and answer before the confirmation reply goes back. Nothing in this repo
should assume the policy permits the workflow that produced the current draft.
