# Flags: SPM2027

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026. Cautions kept out of the prose.]

1. **The biography is the author's to write.**
2. **Postphenomenology (Ihde, Verbeek) has no card.** An SPM audience will expect it. Card it before the talk.
3. **Simmel's arbitrator is chosen** by the parties. The abstract marks the platform difference ("neither party chose"). "Dyad" and "triad" are Wolff's words, not Simmel's.
4. **Jhaver et al.:** two authors worked at Airbnb, N = 15, and the paper has no page numbers. "Anxiety that attaches to the unseen judge rather than to the guest" is the author's reading of their algorithmic anxiety finding, not their sentence.
5. **Eslami et al. (2019):** only 3 of 15 interviewees knew of the filter, and "writing for it" is a reported intention.
6. **Fees:** $150 to $250, plus Mexico City travel or a virtual presentation.
