**Title:** The AI Proposes, the Oracle Disposes: An Oracle-Adjudicated Research Loop for Organization Theory

**Running head:** An Oracle-Adjudicated Research Loop

## Abstract
AI research loops face one problem above the others: the system that writes a claim also judges it. We propose a methodology that separates the two jobs. In an oracle-adjudicated research loop, AI agents do the generative work: they review prior results, search the literature, write competing hypotheses, design tests and draft the paper. Every empirical claim is decided by a deterministic, exact computation the agents cannot influence. Hypotheses are fixed before the oracle runs, and its answer is final. Organization theory suits the design because many of its claims are structural. Our oracle computes integrated information over formal models of coordination and returns an exact verdict on whether an arrangement is irreducibly three-party or factors into pairs. We have run the loop on 170 questions. Of the 512 hypotheses it fixed in advance, the oracle refuted 21%, including the loop's own proposed repair of Thompson's interdependence typology. An adversarial audit of one wave found four failure modes specific to AI-run research: non-severe testing (24 of 24 confirmed), leaky pre-commitment, self-administered gates, and vocabulary that no computation licensed. In the development stage we will add sealing, role separation, severity monitoring and claim tracing. We will release the loop with a pluggable oracle interface and open-weight support, and we will measure the loop's capabilities and limits against an independent human re-implementation.
