insider-risk programmes "learned long ago that rules are necessary but don’t finish the job": agent telemetry is the
system's own account, and only an independent reading of intent (which DTEX sells) tells whether an action matched
what anyone asked. The one sentence that reaches a person is about the delegating user of a compromised agent: "The
person is affected, not responsible." Customers and third parties do not appear. Commercial interest is plain (closing
