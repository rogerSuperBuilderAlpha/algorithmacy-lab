## Summary
Anthropic's consumer terms define "Actions" (things the service does on the user's behalf) and make the user
"responsible for all Inputs you submit to our Services and all Actions". They tell the user not to rely on Outputs or
Actions "without independently confirming their accuracy". If a user's account is linked to an employer's enterprise
account, the employer's administrator "may be able to monitor and control the Account". Accessed 2026-10-24.

## Key facts it relies on
- Actions: the Services may "take actions on your behalf, such as software manipulation, data processing, and system
  interactions (we call these "Actions")" (section 4, Generally; quotation marks as on the page).
- "You are responsible for all Inputs you submit to our Services and all Actions" (section 4, Rights and
  Responsibilities).
- "You should not rely on any Outputs or Actions without independently confirming their accuracy" (section 4, Reliance
  on Outputs and Actions).
- "the organization’s administrator may be able to monitor and control the Account, including having access to
  Materials" (Business Domains).
