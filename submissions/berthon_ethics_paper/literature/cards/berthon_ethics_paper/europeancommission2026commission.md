## Summary
The first citation of an AI Act harmonised standard in the Official Journal. The Commission publishes the reference of EN 18286:2026, "Artificial intelligence – Quality management system for EU AI Act regulatory purposes" (Annex), drafted by CEN and Cenelec under standardisation request C(2025) 3871, point 9 of Annex I, "on quality management systems for providers of AI systems, including post-market monitoring processes" (recital 3). From 14 October 2026 a provider in conformity with it is presumed to conform with the Article 17 requirements it covers (recital 1, recital 6). The decision was published 14 October 2026, four days after Nadella's post. The local file is the plain text I extracted from the CELLAR XHTML on 2026-10-24.

## Key facts it relies on
- Header: "Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/2274 14.10.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/2274 of 12 October 2026".
- Recital (1): systems "in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof, the references of which have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union, are to be presumed to be in conformity with the requirements covered by those standards".
- Recital (3): CEN and Cenelec "completed the work on the harmonised standard requested corresponding to point 9 of Annex I to that Decision regarding harmonised standard(s) and if necessary European standardisation deliverable(s) on quality management systems for providers of AI systems, including post-market monitoring processes. This resulted in the adoption of the new harmonised standard EN 18286:2026."
- Recital (6): "Compliance with a harmonised standard confers a presumption of conformity with the corresponding essential requirements set out in Union harmonisation legislation from the date of publication of the reference of such standard in the Official Journal of the European Union."
- Article 1: "The reference of the harmonised standard for high-risk AI systems drafted in support of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, listed in the Annex to this Decision, is hereby published in the Official Journal of the European Union."
- Annex, item 1: "EN 18286:2026 Artificial intelligence – Quality management system for EU AI Act regulatory purposes".

## Critical notes from the literature
- The decision supersedes the statement in `euomnibus2026` and `europeancommission2025reply` that no AI Act harmonised standard had yet been cited: one now is, for the quality-management requirement (Art. 17), which includes the serious-incident reporting procedures of Art. 17(1)(i).
- Who drafted EN 18286 (JTC 21 membership) is not in the decision; the only count remains `corporateeurope2025bias` against `europeancommission2025reply`.

## Where the argument uses it
- A4 (standards written by industry, binding through the presumption): **holds, now as fact rather than prospect**. The draft's conditional "Once cited in the Official Journal" (ll. 332-333) is satisfied since 14 October 2026 for the Art. 17 standard; the Public.Resource.Org reasoning (`cjeu2024publicresource`, paras 70, 74-75) applies to it.
- Narrowing: EN 18286 covers the provider's quality-management system; the Art. 9-15 requirement standards (risk management, transparency, human oversight) were not yet cited on 24 October 2026 (my search found none; see R2 review search log).
