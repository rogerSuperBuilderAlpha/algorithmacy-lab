## Summary
Abstract-level card; paraphrase only, translated by me from the Catalan abstract in Crossref. Lastowka (Rutgers
law) argues that copyright began as a law regulating the trade in printed works, not protecting authors; the
Statute of Anne changed that by vesting copyright in the author, which made pre-publication bargaining possible.
That settlement, he says, has broken: in cloud computing and Web 2.0, non-author intermediaries own the platforms
that are the tools of authorship, publicity and distribution, and so the ecosystem returns to the Stationers'
Company model, in which legal power over authorial production sits with the owners of the intermediary
technologies; authors become vassals bound to the lords of the platforms on which they work.
