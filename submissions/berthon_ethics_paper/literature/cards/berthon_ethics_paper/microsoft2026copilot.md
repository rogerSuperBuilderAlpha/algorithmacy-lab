## Summary
Microsoft's terms for consumer Copilot. They do not cover work or school (Entra) accounts. For a person using Copilot
to act, the terms assign the whole result to that person: "you are solely responsible for those Actions and any
results or consequences". They tell the user to "Always verify" and to "check the information"; and for purchases
through Copilot they make the user "responsible for reading and complying with the Merchant’s terms". Microsoft
disclaims responsibility for consequences of reliance. Accessed 2026-10-24.

## Key facts it relies on
- "“Tasks” refers to the automated set of actions that Copilot takes on your behalf at your request" (definitions).
- "Always use your judgment and check the information you get from Copilot before you make decisions or act"
  (section "How you use Copilot").
- "You are responsible for reading and complying with the Merchant’s terms that apply to your purchase" (section
  "Shopping experiences").
- "When you request that Copilot take Actions on your behalf, you are solely responsible for those Actions and any
  results or consequences" (section "Important disclosures & warnings").
- "Always verify the accuracy of information presented by Copilot before you rely on it. We are not responsible for
  any consequences that arise from your use of or reliance on Copilot" (same section).
- The terms "do not apply to users who sign in with a work, school, or organizational (Entra) account" (summary of
  changes).

## Critical notes from the literature
- Consumer terms only; enterprise Copilot runs under the Product Terms and the Code of Conduct
  (microsoft2026microsoft).
- The terms rest on Microsoft Services Agreement section 12 ("YOU BEAR THE ENTIRE RISK OF USING THE SERVICES"),
  which I read in `ms_services_agreement/page.html` and did not card.

## Where the argument uses it
- M10 (distrust as the reader's duty): holds, more strongly than E14. Where the application card says "always review
  and verify", the consumer terms add sole responsibility for agent Actions and a duty to read the merchant's terms.
- H19 (the duty to read placed on the person bound): a direct present-day instance; the user must read the merchant's
  terms the agent's purchase binds her to.
- A8 / O01 (consent): the terms are the consent instrument the objection would invoke; they allocate the whole risk to
  the consenting user.
