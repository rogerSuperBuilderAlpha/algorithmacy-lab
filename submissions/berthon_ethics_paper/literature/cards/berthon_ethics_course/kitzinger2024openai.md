## Summary
From the abstract only: Kitzinger argues that Plato's *Phaedrus* "is often used as a cautionary tale about the
futility of distrusting new communicative technologies". In the case of LLMs, she holds, "the Phaedrus analogy is substantially misplaced". LLMs
"exclude the free play of language, producing texts not only without writers, but also without writing (in
Jacques Derrida's sense)". The analogy "thus risks justifying the swift adoption of a commercial tool that is
poorly understood, demonstrably flawed, and reliant on laborious human interventions".
