## Summary
The Notarial Regulation implements the 1862 law. Article 193 restates the reading duty of art. 25 of the law and redefines it. A reading counts as complete ("íntegra") when the notary has communicated the content "to the extent necessary for full knowledge of its scope and effects, taking account of the circumstances of those appearing", whatever the reading procedure. The notary also attests that the parties declared themselves duly informed and gave free consent. A deaf party must read the deed personally or, if unable, use a sign-language interpreter chosen by that party. For a blind party, assent to the notary's reading suffices. Article 147 requires the notary to draft the deed according to the parties' common will and to inform them "of the value and scope" of the drafting. Its last paragraph, which was not annulled, requires the notary to insist on informing one party about clauses proposed by the other. The notary must also "give special assistance to the grantor who needs it".

## Key facts it relies on
- Art. 193, para. 1: notaries attest "de haber leído a las partes y a los testigos instrumentales la escritura íntegra o de haberles permitido que la lean, a su elección", and to having warned them "que tienen el derecho de leerla por sí."
- Art. 193, para. 2: "se entenderá que ésta es íntegra cuando el notario hubiera comunicado el contenido del instrumento con la extensión necesaria para el cabal conocimiento de su alcance y efectos, atendidas las circunstancias de los comparecientes."
- Art. 193, para. 3: the notary attests "que después de la lectura los comparecientes han hecho constar haber quedado debidamente informados del contenido del instrumento y haber prestado a éste su libre consentimiento."
- Art. 193, para. 4: a deaf grantor "deberá leerla por sí", and if unable, "será precisa la intervención de un intérprete designado al efecto por el otorgante". For the blind: "será suficiente que preste su conformidad a la lectura hecha por el notario."
- Art. 147, para. 1: the notary "informará a aquéllos del valor y alcance de su redacción, de conformidad con el artículo 17 bis de la Ley del Notariado."
- Art. 147, last para. (not annulled): "el notario insistirá en informar a una de las partes respecto de las cláusulas de las escrituras y de las pólizas propuestas por la otra ... y prestará asistencia especial al otorgante necesitado de ella."
- Amendment notes: art. 193 "Se modifica por el art. 1.97 del Real Decreto 45/2007"; art. 147 "Se declara la nulidad de los incisos destacados por Sentencia del TS de 20 de mayo de 2008".

## Critical notes from the literature
- The UINL principles (`uinl2005function`, Part IV para. 4) state the same duty of "adequate assistance" to a party "in a position of inferiority". Facet M3 notes that norm and practice may differ, and that the English notarial tradition is different.
- The 2007 wording of art. 193 is a late development. I did not read the earlier versions, so this card claims nothing about when "communication sufficient for full knowledge" replaced reading as the standard.
- For the blind, reading aloud remains enough.

## Where the manuscript uses it
- v4 (not yet drafted): proposed use. A civil-law system redefined "reading" as communication sufficient for "cabal conocimiento ... atendidas las circunstancias de los comparecientes" (art. 193). This is a standard of understanding fitted to the person, not of disclosure. Verifier: **narrower**. Art. 193 sets a standard for the notary's communication, aimed at full knowledge. What the notary then attests is the parties' own declaration that they are "debidamente informados" (para. 3). No step tests their understanding.
- v4 (not yet drafted): proposed use. A neutral official carries the burden: "asistencia especial al otorgante necesitado de ella" (art. 147).
