## Summary
The Digital Omnibus on AI amends the AI Act to simplify its implementation. For this paper its central change is to Article 4, the AI-literacy duty. The 2024 text required providers and deployers to "ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy" of their staff and agents. The replacement requires them only to "take measures to support the development of AI literacy" of the same people, and adds: "This obligation does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual." The Commission and Member States must "support and facilitate" providers' and deployers' efforts, in particular SMEs', and the Board adopts recommendations. Recital 8 gives the reason: stakeholders said stringent obligations "would not be suitable for all types of providers and deployers", and such obligations create "an additional compliance burden, particularly for smaller enterprises", while literacy "should be a strategic priority, regardless of regulatory obligations and potential sanctions." The Omnibus also postpones Chapter III (Arts 9–27, including 13, 14 and 26) to 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for Annex I systems, gives generative systems already on the market until 2 December 2026 to comply with Art 50(2) marking, and rewrites Art 50(7) on codes of practice. Signed at Strasbourg on 8 July 2026; in force on the third day after publication (24.7.2026).

## Key facts it relies on
- Art 1(5), new Art 4(1): "take measures to support the development of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf ... This obligation does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual."
- New Art 4(2): the Commission and Member States "shall support and facilitate the efforts of providers and deployers"; the Commission publishes "practical examples" of compliance.
- New Art 4(3): the Board adopts recommendations "taking into account European competence frameworks".
- Recital 8: "imposing such obligations creates an additional compliance burden, particularly for smaller enterprises"; names DigComp and the AI Literacy Framework for Primary and Secondary Education as frameworks the Commission and Member States "could take into account".
- Art 113(c) as replaced: Chapter III Sections 1–3 apply from "2 December 2027" (Annex III) and "2 August 2028" (Annex I).
- Art 111 new para 4: generative systems on the market before 2 August 2026 comply with Art 50(2) "by 2 December 2026".
- Art 4 of the Omnibus: enters into force "on the third day following that of its publication" (27 July 2026 by arithmetic; the text gives no date).
