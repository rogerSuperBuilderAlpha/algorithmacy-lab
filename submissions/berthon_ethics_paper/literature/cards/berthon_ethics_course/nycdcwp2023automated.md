## Summary

The notice adopts DCWP's rules implementing Local Law 144 of 2021, which "prohibits employers and employment agencies from using an automated employment decision tool unless the tool has been subject to a bias audit within one year of the use of the tool, information about the bias audit is publicly available, and certain notices have been provided" (p. 1). The rule went through two proposals (September and December 2022) and two hearings (4 November 2022, 23 January 2023). Section 5-300 defines the AEDT narrowly: a tool counts only if its "simplified output" is relied on solely, outweighs every other criterion, or overrules other factors, including human decision-making (p. 3). It also defines an "independent auditor" by exclusion of anyone involved with the tool or employed by, or financially interested in, its user or vendor (pp. 3–4). Section 5-301 requires the audit to compute selection or scoring rates and impact ratios by sex, race/ethnicity and their intersections, with worked examples (pp. 5–8). Section 5-302 governs historical and test data, § 5-303 publication of the audit summary, and § 5-304 the notice to candidates. The notice must explain how to request an alternative selection process, but "Nothing in this subchapter requires an employer or employment agency to provide an alternative selection process" (p. 9).

## Key facts it relies on

- p. 1: Local Law 144 of 2021 prohibits AEDT use without a bias audit within one year, public audit information, and notices.
- p. 1: hearings on 4 November 2022 and 23 January 2023; the Department "amends Title 6 of the Rules of the City of New York."
- § 5-300, p. 3: "to substantially assist or replace discretionary decision making" means relying "solely on a simplified output", weighting it "more than any other criterion", or using it "to overrule conclusions derived from other factors including human decision-making."
- § 5-300, pp. 3–4: an "independent auditor" is "capable of exercising objective and impartial judgment" and is not "involved in using, developing, or distributing the AEDT," nor employed by or financially interested in the user or vendor.
- § 5-301(a), p. 5: no use "if more than one year has passed since the most recent bias audit."
- § 5-301(b)–(d), pp. 5–7: selection rate, scoring rate and impact ratio by sex, race/ethnicity and intersectional category; a category under 2% may be excluded with justification.
- § 5-303(a), p. 9: the date and summary of the most recent audit and the distribution date must be public "Before the use of an AEDT."
- § 5-304(a), p. 9: the notice "must include instructions for how an individual can request an alternative selection process or a reasonable accommodation under other laws, if available. Nothing in this subchapter requires an employer or employment agency to provide an alternative selection process."
- § 5-304(b), p. 9: notice "at least 10 business days before use of an AEDT."
