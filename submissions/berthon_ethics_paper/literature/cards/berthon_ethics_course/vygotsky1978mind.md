## Summary
This collection, edited by Cole and colleagues from Vygotsky's writings, includes chapter 6, "Interaction between
Learning and Development", which introduces the zone of proximal development. This card rests on one-line search
snippets only, without printed pages. Joined from three overlapping snippets, the definition reads: the zone "is the
distance between the actual developmental level as determined by independent problem solving and the level of
potential development as determined through problem solving under adult guidance or in collaboration with more
capable peers." The zone "defines those functions that have not yet matured but are in the process of maturation",
the "'buds' or 'flowers' of development rather than the 'fruits'". Vygotsky holds that "the only 'good learning' is
that which is in advance of development", and that learning "awakens a variety of internal developmental processes".
The definition makes a more capable other part of the construct: what a person can do with help marks what they will
soon do alone.
