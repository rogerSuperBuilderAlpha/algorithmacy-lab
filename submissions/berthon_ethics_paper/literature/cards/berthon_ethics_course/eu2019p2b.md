## Summary
The P2B Regulation aims to ensure that business users of online intermediation services, and corporate website
users of search engines, get "appropriate transparency, fairness and effective redress possibilities" (Art 1(1)).
It applies where the business users are established in the Union and offer goods or services to consumers in the
Union, wherever the provider is (Art 1(2)). A "business user" is a private individual acting in a commercial or
professional capacity, or a legal person, offering goods or services to consumers through the service (Art 2(1));
"terms and conditions" are those the provider determines unilaterally (Art 2(10)). Art 4 governs restriction,
suspension and termination: reasons "on a durable medium" prior to or at the time a restriction or suspension
takes effect, and at least 30 days before termination of the whole service, with a chance to clarify the facts
through the internal complaint-handling system. Art 5 requires disclosure of the main ranking parameters but not
of "algorithms" (Art 5(6)). Art 11 requires an internal complaint-handling system run by the provider; Art 12
requires providers to name two or more impartial and independent mediators, while mediation stays voluntary and
court proceedings remain open. Small enterprises are exempt from Arts 11–12. It applies from 12 July 2020
(Art 19(2)).

## Key facts it relies on
- Art 4(1): for restriction or suspension "in relation to individual goods or services", a statement of reasons
  "prior to or at the time of the restriction or suspension taking effect ... on a durable medium".
- Art 4(2): for termination of "the whole of its online intermediation services", reasons "at least 30 days
  prior to the termination taking effect ... on a durable medium"; Art 4(4) lists exceptions (legal obligation,
  imperative reason, repeated infringement).
- Art 4(3): the business user may "clarify the facts and circumstances in the framework of the internal
  complaint-handling process referred to in Article 11".
- Art 5(6): providers are not "required to disclose algorithms".
- Art 11(1): providers "shall provide for an internal system for handling the complaints of business users";
  Art 11(5): not for small enterprises.
- Art 12(1)–(2): two or more mediators, who must be "impartial and independent"; Art 12(3): "Notwithstanding the
  voluntary nature of mediation"; Art 12(5): rights to judicial proceedings unaffected "at any time before,
  during or after the mediation process".
- Art 2(1): business users include a "private individual acting in a commercial or professional capacity".
- Header: Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 of 20 June 2019, OJ L 186, 11.7.2019; applies from 12 July 2020 (Art 19(2)).

## Critical notes from the literature
- Facet N2 (`literature/facets/N2_platform_law.md:77-84`) corrects the manuscript's "before an account is
  restricted": Art 4(1) says "prior to or at the time of"; Art 4(2)'s 30-day notice before termination is an ex
  ante safeguard the paper does not mention. It also reads Art 3(2)–(3) (notice before changes to terms, nullity
  for non-compliance; not re-read this session) as a legal constraint on re-declaring rules.
- Facet N2 (`:261`): P2B mediators are "independent and voluntary"; no independent body can bind the provider.
- Facet N2 (`:42`): the Platform Work Directive, Art 11(5), excludes persons who are also P2B business users, so
  the two instruments split platform workers between them.
- Ledger P5-027 grades "The checks listed above operate after the decision" wrong in part, citing Art 4(2).

## Where the manuscript uses it
- **Line 167, quotation check**: "requires a statement of reasons 'on a durable medium' before an account is
  restricted (Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, art. 4)". Exact-string check of "on a durable medium": **exact**,
  Art 4(1), 4(2), 4(4) (`32019R1150.txt:196-197`). The claim around it is **narrower**: Art 4(1) requires reasons
  "prior to or at the time of" a restriction, and concerns "individual goods or services", not the account as
  such; only termination (Art 4(2)) requires advance notice. "Before" should read "no later than" for
  restriction.
- **Line 167**, "For business users of platforms". Source: Art 1(2), 2(1). **holds.**
- **Line 171**, "The checks listed above operate after the decision." Source: Art 4(2). **does not hold** for
  P2B termination, where reasons come at least 30 days before.
- **Line 173**, "Under the Directive and the Regulation the first reviewer is the platform's own staff". If "the
  Regulation" means P2B: Art 11 (provider's internal complaint system). **holds**, but the referent is ambiguous
  among three Regulations (ledger P5-028).
- **Line 177**, the checks are "recent, regional, after the fact, and in the first instance the intermediary's
  own". Source: Arts 4(2), 11, 19. **narrower**: "after the fact" fails for Art 4(2); "first instance own" holds
  (Art 11).
- **Line 237**, each provision "places the first review inside the firm, and none supplies the binding
  independent party". Source: Art 11; Art 12(2)–(3) (independent but voluntary mediators). **holds.**
- **Line 367, reference entry** "Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 ... of 20 June 2019 on promoting fairness and
  transparency for business users of online intermediation services. *Official Journal of the European Union*,
  L 186, 11.7.2019." **holds** against the OJ header.
