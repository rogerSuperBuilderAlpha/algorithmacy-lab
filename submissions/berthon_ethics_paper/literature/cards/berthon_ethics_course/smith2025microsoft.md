## Summary
Smith announces Microsoft Elevate and the AI Economy Institute. Elevate merges Microsoft Philanthropies and Tech for Social Impact into one organisation for schools, community colleges and nonprofits. Microsoft pledges "more than $4 billion in cash and AI and cloud technology" over five years to K-12 schools, community and technical colleges and nonprofits. The Microsoft Elevate Academy "will help 20 million people earn an in-demand AI skilling credential ranging from foundational fluency to advanced technical training" in two years, working with LinkedIn and GitHub, and Elevate will advocate for public policies on AI education. Partnerships named include North Rhine-Westphalia, an "Hour of AI" with Code.org, and the American Federation of Teachers. The post frames the aim as "putting people first by equipping them with the skills, knowledge, and tools to thrive with AI", and asks whether AI is built "to replace people, or to help people thrive". The Institute's research will "inform Microsoft Elevate's skilling programs". The figures are pledges and targets, not results.
