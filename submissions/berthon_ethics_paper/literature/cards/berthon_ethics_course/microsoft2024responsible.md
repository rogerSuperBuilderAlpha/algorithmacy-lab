## Summary
Microsoft's first annual Responsible AI Transparency Report describes how the company builds generative applications, supports customers, and trains staff. On the pages read, its user-facing transparency is documentation and in-product notice. Since 2019 it has published "33 Transparency Notes", which give customers information on "capabilities, limitations, and intended uses"; in 2023 it required Copilots to publish Responsible AI FAQs and "user-friendly notices in product experiences": disclosure that the user is interacting with AI, "citations to source material to help users verify information", and disclaimers that AI may err (p. 25). It states that "Responsible use of AI applications is a shared responsibility between application developers and application users" and that developers should design mitigations to "minimize users' risk of overreliance", citing research prototypes such as an Excel add-in that "helps users assess AI-generated code" (p. 34). Training on the pages read is for employees: "99 percent of employees completed the responsible AI module" (p. 4), "This journey begins with educating all of our employees" (p. 29). Self-reported; no outside audit.
