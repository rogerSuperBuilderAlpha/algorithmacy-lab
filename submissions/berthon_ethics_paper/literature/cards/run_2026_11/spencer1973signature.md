## Summary

Abstract only; paraphrase. The only text reached is the article's opening sentence, which the OpenAlex record carries as its abstract. It states the generally accepted position that a person who signs a contractual document cannot dispute assent to its terms unless he establishes one of three defences: fraud, misrepresentation, or non est factum. The title shows that the article examines the rule in L'Estrange v. Graucob through signature and consent. What Spencer argues about that rule is not known from this source.
