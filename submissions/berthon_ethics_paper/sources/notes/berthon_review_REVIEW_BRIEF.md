## How to report

Write one file in this directory, named as your assignment says, headed "Agent-drafted review. The
author has not seen it." Use this structure:

1. **Verdict** in three sentences: what the paper does, whether it is ready for the venue, and the
   single most serious problem.
2. **Findings**, most serious first. For each: the section and a short quotation so the author can
   find it; what is wrong; the evidence (source, locator, or arm file and line); and what would fix
   it, stated as an instruction, not as replacement prose. Mark each **must fix**, **should fix** or
   **consider**.
3. **What works** and should be protected in revision.
4. **What you could not check.**

## Rules

- Report; do not rewrite. The author writes the prose. You may give a corrected fact, figure,
  citation or page number. You may not supply replacement sentences or paragraphs.
- Every claim that the draft misstates a source needs the source's actual words and a locator, from a
  text you opened this session or from an arm file that quotes it. If you cannot check, say so.
- Never cite from memory. Never invent a reference, DOI, page or quotation.
- **Never put any person's e-mail address in a request header, User-Agent, URL or payload.** Anonymous
  web requests only; no `mailto`; no Unpaywall. Do not copy e-mail addresses into your report.
- Do not use Chrome browser tools. Do not get around paywalls or bot walls. Do not run git. Write only
  your own report file.
- Be direct. A review that finds nothing wrong is suspect, and so is one that buries the worst
  problem under twenty small ones.
