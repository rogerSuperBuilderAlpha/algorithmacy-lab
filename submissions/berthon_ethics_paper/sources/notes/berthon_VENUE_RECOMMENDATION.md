(`V1_ethics_management.md`, `V2_is_technology_marketing.md`) and three web searches of its own. The

**If A\* is required:** *Information and Organization* (A\*) is the closest. It publishes the
neighbouring work on algorithmic ethics and genealogies of AI. The paper would need an
information-systems anchor it does not have, and the guidelines are unread. *Academy of Management
Review* (A\*, FT50) has precedent for AI ethics and for theory built from historical accounts, and is
a long shot for this paper as outlined.

**If the instructor's field matters:** *Journal of Public Policy & Marketing* (A) carries Berthon,
Pitt and Campbell (2019). It requires a policy contribution statement, and whether it takes a
conceptual paper of this length is unclear. *Business Horizons*, his usual outlet, is rated B.

## Ruled out, with the reason

- *Organization Studies*: empirical articles only since June 2025 (search extraction; check).
- *Academy of Management Perspectives*: "does not publish theory driven papers".
- *Business History*, *Business History Review*: require primary historical research.
- *Work, Employment and Society*: empirical sociology.
- *MIS Quarterly*, *Information Systems Research*: no realistic essay route.
- Best fits in kind that are not tier A: *Philosophy & Technology*, *Big Data & Society*, *AI &
  Society*, *Design Issues* (none on the ABDC list); *Ethics and Information Technology* (B).

## What the choice changes in the paper

- **For BEQ.** Lead with the moral frame now in 2.5 (domination; internal critique) and state the
  normative claim in the abstract. Cut the claim to "organizational and design theory". Read Pettit
  and Fricker in the original, since BEQ reviewers will. For the special issue, connect the gate and
  the record to the call's concern with illiberal power.
- **For *Organization Theory*.** Recast the contribution as a theory of the intermediary's position in
  organizing, with the ethics as implication. Decide how this paper relates to the author's other
  algorithmacy paper for the same journal, so the construct is not introduced twice.
- **For JBE.** Little change from the outline; add managerial implications.
