## Bucket 2 — confirmed, but needs reconciliation with this arm's own best finding

- **Martela & Steger, second bullet.** The draft treats M&S as a pure critique target
  ("providing users with cognitive coherence... induces learned helplessness and nihilism"). This
  contradicts the second harness's most important single finding: M&S's own definition of
  coherence, quoted verbatim in `martela2016.md`, is **predictability-shaped, not
  explanation-shaped** ("coherence centers on the perception that stimuli are predictable and
  conform to recognizable patterns," p. 534). That makes M&S an **ally** of a bounded-outputs
  design principle, not a target — providing predictability (not mechanistic transparency) is
  exactly what their own construct calls for. The draft's Prescriptive Limit paragraph needs
  rewriting around this, not research — the finding already exists and is doubly verified.

## Bucket 3 — needs new research: real terminology or citations this arm doesn't have yet

- **"Meaning-affording design" / "meaning-hostile environment."** No card anywhere in this library
  uses or grounds this pairing. Likely real anchors: positive computing (Calvo & Peters, *Positive
  Computing: Technology for Wellbeing and Human Potential*, MIT Press 2014); self-determination
  theory applied to design (Peters, Calvo & Ryan, "Designing for Motivation, Engagement, and
  Wellbeing in Digital Experience," *Frontiers in Psychology* 2018); possibly eudaimonic vs.
  hedonic HCI (Mekler & Hornbæk). Needs verification, not assumption — find out whether "meaning-
  affording" is anyone's actual term or needs replacing outright with SDT vocabulary (autonomy,
  competence, relatedness) which is far more citable.
- **Verbeek's "Constructive Technology Assessment (CTA)."** Not yet checked against any Verbeek
  card in this library. CTA is historically a Dutch STS term (Rip, Schot, and collaborators) —
  verify whether Verbeek treats it as his own constructive remedy anywhere in the already-verified
  cards (`verbeek2006.md`, `verbeek2011.md`, `verbeek2014.md`, `verbeek2015.md`) or whether this
  draft is conflating two adjacent but distinct traditions.
- **"Princeton/Bath word embedding studies."** Almost certainly Caliskan, A., Bryson, J. J., &
  Narayanan, A. (2017), "Semantics derived automatically from language corpora contain human-like
  biases," *Science* 356(6334): 183–186 — Caliskan and Narayanan were at Princeton, Bryson at
  Bath, which matches the institutional gesture. Verify and card properly; do not keep the vague
  attribution.
- **"Stanford's facial recognition research."** Likely **misattributed**. The well-known study in
  this space is Buolamwini, J., & Gebru, T. (2018), "Gender Shades: Intersectional Accuracy
  Disparities in Commercial Gender Classification," *FAT\* 2018* — Buolamwini was at MIT Media Lab,
  Gebru at Microsoft Research, not Stanford. Verify whether a distinct Stanford study exists that
  the draft actually means, or correct the attribution to Buolamwini & Gebru.
- **Current AAVE/LLM bias research.** The draft's framing is generic ("educational and evaluative
  LLMs routinely penalize AAVE"). Find what's actually current: candidates include Hofmann, V., et
  al. (2024), "AI generates covertly racist decisions about people based on their dialect,"
  *Nature* 633: 147–154 — recent, LLM-specific, and a stronger citation than a generic gesture.
  Also check Sap, M., et al. (2019) on racial bias in hate-speech detection as an earlier anchor if
  needed.
- **"Data nutrition labels."** Real term: Holland, S., Hosny, A., Newman, J., Joseph, J., &
  Chmielinski, K. (2018), "The Dataset Nutrition Label: A Framework to Drive Higher Data Quality
  Standards," arXiv:1805.03677. If the draft keeps this phrase, cite it properly alongside the
  already-verified `mitchell2019.md` (Model Cards).
- **"Counter-agency."** Needs a precise anchor. The already-verified counter-delegation cluster
  covers part of this, but "counter-agency" as used in the draft's closing line may want broader
  design-politics vocabulary — candidates: "algorithmic resistance" (Velkova & Kaun, 2021,
  *Information, Communication & Society*); "adversarial design" (Carl DiSalvo) — **note: DiSalvo
  was explicitly dropped from this arm's category-error harness** ("the Simmel reading carries the
  politics without them," per `RESEARCH_PLAN.md`) — revisit that call if "counter-agency" turns out
  to need his vocabulary specifically, rather than silently reintroducing him.
- **"Surveillance capitalism"** (used once, in the Vallor section) — if kept, should cite Zuboff,
  S. (2019), *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* properly rather than use the phrase as ambient
  vocabulary; check whether this term is already carded anywhere in the lab (the scaffolding arm
  cites Zuboff's earlier 1988 book for "informate," a different work).
