# Argument map

*Written by Claude on 2026-10-12 and revised on 2026-10-13 after the author's ten rulings (`SOURCE.md`),
facets I–K and the card library (`literature/facets/T_adjudication.md` §9). It maps the strongest
defensible form of the argument, which differs from the form the author first gave. None of it is the
author's text. The first-round map is in the git history of this file.*

**Depth marks.** `[card]` the source has a verified card in `literature/library/`, which carries the
locators. `[ok]` an auditor confirmed the passage in the source. `[abs]` abstract only. `[lib]` needs a
library copy before it is quoted. `[inf]` an inference by the lead or a facet agent with no source behind
it. `[author]` the author's ruling or proposal. Source keys are in `literature/references.bib`.

**Conclusion.** Word separation is a historical case of an intervention that is neither disclosure nor
training: it re-represented the page (P1–P6), and it guides the question without settling the answer
(P7). Disclosure, the usual remedy for an opaque algorithm, is a literacy-based intervention and has not
been shown to help a person navigate a mediated coordination (P8). Understanding there comes through
deciding, where feedback allows it (P9), which points to two candidate conventions (P10) in a triad
whose irreducibility Φ can gauge (P11). Whoever holds the convention, the record of courts, dealer
markets and platforms says the third party yields it only under outside force (P12). Nadella's insider
post addresses how an organisation contains a model it cannot read; what it offers a person is something
to read, and it does not address the people the model acts upon (P13). Decision Experience Design is the
discipline that looks for the missing conventions.

## What the argument no longer claims

| Dropped | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Spacing "ignited literacy" | No historian of literacy credits it; Saenger's book "is not a general history of literacy" (p. x); unspaced scripts support fluent reading (P4) |
| Spacing created silent reading | Knox (1968, pp. 432–435); Saenger himself has Ambrose reading silently (p. 8) |
| "The" move | Every parallel case is a bundle (facet F); the candidates in facet H are several |
| Nadella proposes "the exact opposite" | Shared premises and mechanisms (P9, P11 of the post); the difference is authority and seat |
| "Moving work to the artefact" marks the paper off from Nadella | A reference monitor moves work to the artefact too |
| Reading "no longer required the training" | The task was transformed and the scribe took on work (Hutchins 1995, pp. 280–283); schooling in letters and syllables continued (facet K). Saenger's p. 85 sentence is the nearest support and should carry the claim under his name |
| Spacing helped "most for learners" | Child learners of Chinese did worse with spaces (Bassetti and Masterson 2012); the modern learner evidence is mixed |
| People perform after-the-fact review badly, as the ground of the critique of the post | CERT practice endorses it; the direct evidence is one study (Kutasov 2025) |
| "Disclosure doesn't affect our ability to navigate it", stated flatly | Mixed in direction: some null, some harm, two gains outside the triad |
| "Pilots don't spend time reading the situation" | Both pilots monitor (Hutchins 1995); monitoring is the least-caught part of crew performance (Dismukes and Berman 2010) |

## Where the argument is exposed

1. **No convention has been tested in a triad with a behavioural outcome.** Every measured effect in
   P8–P10 comes from a person and a system, or from a clearinghouse with no objective of its own.
2. **P9 and P10 pull apart.** The best-evidenced convention removes a task from the person; the author's
   ruling has the person understand by acting. The paper has to say whether the two serve different
   decisions or compete.
3. **P9 depends on a condition the author's own Part 1 says the mediator breaks.** The feedback
   convention in P10 is the answer to that, and it is an inference `[inf]`.
4. **The precedent is a literacy-side intervention.** Word separation helped people read. P7 covers this
   only if the paper keeps the history as a guide.
5. **The contrast with the post rests on whom the controls serve.** It no longer rests on a showing that
   his human tasks fail.
6. **"Algorithmacy" has a prior printed use** in another sense (Oberschelp 1995, p. 121).
