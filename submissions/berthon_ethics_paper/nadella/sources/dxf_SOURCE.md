## The Nadella post

- **Reference.** Nadella, S. [@satyanadella]. (2026, October 10). *Models as insider risks in the super
  intelligence era* [Post]. X. https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2108931348857827686
- **Capture.** Read from the live page on 2026-10-12 (13M views at capture). The full text is kept in a
  local file outside this public repo, with paragraph numbers P1–P21 added for locators. Facet G quotes
  it by paragraph.
- **Summary (Claude's).** Organisations cannot trace a frontier model's behaviour to its causes, yet
  give such models sensitive data and the power to act. Nadella proposes treating models as insider
  risks: surround them with deterministic system design, human controls and operating procedures, and
  keep every control outside the model. He lists seven principles of "observability": model diversity,
  observe everything, verifiability, independent controls, independent auditability, containment and
  incident disclosure.
- **Short quotations.** Authority: "separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it" (P5).
  Records: every meaningful model action must leave "tamper-proof human readable evidence" (P15). On
  chain-of-thought transparency: "not sufficient or dependable" (P9). His closing line says the most
  trustworthy system is the one that lets us trust the model least (P21, paraphrased).
