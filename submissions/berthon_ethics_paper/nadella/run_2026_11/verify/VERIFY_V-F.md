## The three errors that would have done most damage

1. euaiact2026dates (and ADJUDICATION ruling 1): "art. 26(11) applies to Annex III systems from 2 December 2027"
   leaves out art. 111(2) as replaced by the Omnibus. Systems already in service on that date fall under the
   Regulation only after a significant change in design, and public-authority deployers have until 2 August 2030.
   The paper's dated list of present rights would overstate even the 2027 notice.
2. microsoft2026copilot: the revocation quotation cut "Unless prohibited by law,", and the Summary repeats the
   unqualified power. Quoted in the paper, it would misstate Microsoft's term. The quotation is fixed; the Summary
   needs the lead.
3. gadeke2020mugger: "nothing on firms" is a negative drawn from a partial read, and p. 211 discusses employers and
   workplace law, through Pettit. The paper's retail-domination call (call 1) leans on this card, so a reader who
   finds p. 211 could use it against the transfer. A smaller fourth: the euaiact claim that the Omnibus text has no
   "Article 26" was literally false, though its conclusion holds.
