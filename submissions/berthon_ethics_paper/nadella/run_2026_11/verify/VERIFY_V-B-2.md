## The three errors that would have done most damage

1. susser2019technology, p. 7: dark patterns quoted as designs that nudge users "into acting against their
   interests", with the authors' hedge "(or, at least, acting in the interests of the website or app)" dropped. The
   paper would have cited a stronger definition than the source gives.
2. bayamlioglu2021contest, Critical notes: says he contests Wachter et al. for treating the Art. 22(3) safeguards as
   "individually enforceable"; he endorses that reading ("rightly point out") and contests only their low-success
   point and their denial of an explanation right. Citing the two as opposed on that point would misstate both.
3. burrell2024domination, Summary: "the paper does not address consumers"; the unread end of §3 names
   consumers among those who resist systems. A gap claim in the paper resting on this card would be false.
