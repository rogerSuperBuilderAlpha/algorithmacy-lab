## The three errors that would have done most damage in the paper

1. ragas2007leer: the Summary calls the exclusion of highland rural voters the 1896 law's "stated aim", and the notes call it the law's "explicit target". That is Ragas's own reading in his conclusion (p. 133). He cites no legislator and does not analyse the 1896 debate. Used in 3.2, it would put an unsourced claim of intent behind the only non-US literacy gate in the facet.
2. kousser1974shaping: the Summary says the grandfather clause let registrars "pass or fail a man at will". On p. 58 the discretion belongs to the understanding clause ("to the registrar's satisfaction"). The grandfather clause worked by descent from 1867 voters. Section 3.2 turns on how the gate operated, so this would mislabel the mechanism.
3. levine1982functional, sandiford1994nicaraguan and yilmaz2011learning have no saved source (local_path null). They fail the standing guard that every card rests on a source fetched in the run or already on disk. Their content matches the records, but none can be indexed until the lead saves the record files. (Close behind: newland2024federal turns "grief and trauma from language loss" into "punishments … among the most common shared experiences", p. 87.)
