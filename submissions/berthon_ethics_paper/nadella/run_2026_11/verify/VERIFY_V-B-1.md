## The three errors that would have done most damage

1. microsoft2026services: "The 'any person' in §14(s)(ix)(1) is the only place the agreement names people outside the contract." False. All nine §14(s)(ix) restrictions name outside persons. Repeated in the paper, the claim would be refuted by anyone who reads the clause.
2. google2025secure_agents: the Summary says the paper "names no person outside that link" and that others appear "only" as victims of cross-user contamination. Risk 1 (PDF p. 10) names a wrongly e-mailed contact and "physical safety risks". The fact supports the paper's claim, but it is stated more absolutely than the source allows.
3. imda2026agentic: the card says outside-the-organisation duties "run between firms" (p. 28) and omits the same page's "End users" paragraph, which asks for information "to hold the organisation accountable". That understates the strongest public-body counter-evidence in the set. A close fourth is visa2025trusted, which calls the design "deployed" against its own Limits line and wrongly reports the press release as unreadable.
