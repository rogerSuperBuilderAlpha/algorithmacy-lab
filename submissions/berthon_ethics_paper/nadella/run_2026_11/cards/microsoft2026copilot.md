## Summary

Read in full: the terms page as saved on 2026-10-24, extracted to text and searched by section heading. These are
Microsoft's supplemental terms for consumer Copilot, effective 18 August 2026; they work together with the
Microsoft Services Agreement and do not apply to anyone who signs in with a work, school or organisational (Entra)
account. "You" is defined as the person accessing and using Copilot, and Copilot is a Service under the Services
Agreement. The terms tell the user to check what Copilot says before acting, and they make the user "solely
responsible" for Actions Copilot takes on the user's request and for their results. In the same section the user
agrees to indemnify Microsoft and hold it harmless against claims, losses and expenses, attorneys' fees included,
arising from the user's use of Copilot. For purchases through Copilot the user must read the merchant's terms, and
Microsoft disclaims liability for disputes between user and merchant. Microsoft may suspend or revoke access in its
sole discretion and without notice, and a user who thinks this a mistake may ask it to reevaluate through a form.

## Critical notes

- Consumer terms only. Enterprise Copilot runs under other terms (see microsoft2026microsoft in the library); the
  Services Agreement these terms join is carded as microsoft2026services, whose §14(s)(viii) makes the user
  "solely responsible for responding to" third-party claims and whose §16 says the Terms benefit no other person.
- "Actions" is capitalised in the "solely responsible" sentence but is not among the defined terms; the definitions
  define "Tasks". The page does not say whether the two are the same.
- The indemnity runs from the user to Microsoft and covers claims "arising from or relating to your use of Copilot",
  which on its words reaches claims by a person an agent's action affects. The page names no remedy for that person
  and no route for her to complain other than the general Report a Concern page.
- Microsoft does take on one loss elsewhere, for paying commercial customers: the copyright commitment
  (smith2023microsoft), which is not in these consumer terms.
- The live page was read on two dates (2026-10-24 and 2026-11-19); "past versions" are linked from the header but
  were not compared.
- Person acted on gets: record no (nothing in the terms gives her one); brake no; envelope no; standing no (she is
  not a party; "you" is the user, Definitions); loss cover no (the user is "solely responsible" for Actions and
  indemnifies Microsoft, Important disclosures & warnings).
- Corrected by V-F: the "Our decisions about Copilot" quotation now starts at "Unless prohibited by law, we may", the qualifier that opens the sentence and that the card had cut; the reevaluation clause now carries the page's condition, "restricted by mistake".
