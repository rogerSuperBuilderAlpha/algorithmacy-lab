## Summary

Read: the provisions that fix when art. 26(11) of the AI Act applies, located by text search of the two Official
Journal texts. Art. 26 sets the obligations of deployers of high-risk AI systems and stands in Chapter III,
Section 3. Its paragraph 11 requires deployers of Annex III high-risk systems that make, or assist in making,
decisions about natural persons to inform those persons that they are subject to the system. As enacted, art. 113
applied the Regulation generally from 2 August 2026, with Chapter III Section 4 earlier and art. 6(1) and its
obligations from 2 August 2027. The Digital Omnibus, art. 1 point (40)(b), replaced point (c) of art. 113's third
paragraph: Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3 (except art. 6(5)) now apply from 2 December 2027 for systems
high-risk under art. 6(2) and Annex III, and from 2 August 2028 for those under art. 6(1) and Annex I. Recital 40
gives the reason as the delayed availability of standards and guidance and the delayed setting-up of national
authorities. The Omnibus entered into force on the third day after publication on 24 July 2026, and its text does
not amend art. 26.

## Critical notes

- On these texts art. 26(11), as part of Chapter III Section 3, applies to Annex III systems from 2 December 2027;
  on 19 November 2026 it does not yet apply. What a deployer must do in between is not stated in either text read.
- Limits: arts. 6(2), 85, 86 and Annex III were not reopened for this card (they are on eu2024aiact; ADJUDICATION
  ruling 1 reports them). No act later than 24 July 2026 was searched for, and no consolidated version was
  compared. The extracted texts carry no OJ page numbers, so locators are by article, point and recital.
- The duty in art. 26(11) is to inform the person that a high-risk system is used; it names no explanation, record
  or remedy (those sit in arts. 85 and 86, not read here).
- Person acted on gets: record no (art. 26(11) gives notice of use only); brake no; envelope no; standing no; loss
  cover no — and the notice itself only from 2 December 2027, and only for Annex III systems (2026/1744, art. 1
  point (40)(b)).
