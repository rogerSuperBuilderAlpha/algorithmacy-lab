## Summary

Read: the provisions that fix when art. 26(11) of the AI Act applies, located by text search of the two Official
Journal texts. Art. 26 sets the obligations of deployers of high-risk AI systems and stands in Chapter III,
Section 3. Its paragraph 11 requires deployers of Annex III high-risk systems that make, or assist in making,
decisions about natural persons to inform those persons that they are subject to the system. As enacted, art. 113
applied the Regulation generally from 2 August 2026, with Chapter III Section 4 earlier and art. 6(1) and its
obligations from 2 August 2027. The Digital Omnibus, art. 1 point (40)(b), replaced point (c) of art. 113's third
paragraph: Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3 (except art. 6(5)) now apply from 2 December 2027 for systems
high-risk under art. 6(2) and Annex III, and from 2 August 2028 for those under art. 6(1) and Annex I. Recital 40
gives the reason as the delayed availability of standards and guidance and the delayed setting-up of national
authorities. Point (39)(a) replaced art. 111(2): for a high-risk system placed on the market or put into service
before the date Chapter III applies, the Regulation applies to its operators, deployers among them, only if the
system's design changes significantly from that date, and providers and deployers of systems intended for use by
public authorities must comply by 2 August 2030. The Omnibus entered into force on the third day after publication
on 24 July 2026, and its text does not amend art. 26.

## Critical notes

- On these texts art. 26(11), as part of Chapter III Section 3, does not yet apply on 19 November 2026. From
  2 December 2027 it applies to deployers of Annex III systems placed on the market or put into service on or after
  that date. For a system placed on the market or put into service before that date, the Regulation applies to the
  system's operators, deployers among them (art. 3 point (8)), only once its design changes significantly, and
  providers and deployers of systems intended to be used by public authorities must in any case comply by 2 August
  2030 (2024/1689, art. 111(2), as replaced by 2026/1744, art. 1 point (39)(a)). The texts read set no date by
  which a private deployer of an unchanged system already in service must give the notice. What a deployer must do
  before 2 December 2027 is not stated in either text read.
- Two points of reading in art. 111(2), both the Fable reader's. The paragraph speaks of "the date of application
  of Chapter III referred to in Article 113" and names no date; I take it as 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems
  and 2 August 2028 for Annex I systems, from new point (c) of art. 113 and recital 40, although Section 4 of
  Chapter III has applied since 2 August 2025 under point (b). Recital 39 speaks of the provider's obligations and
  of units placed on the market; the article says "operators", which covers a deployer, but neither text says in
  terms whether a deployer who first takes up, after the date, a unit of a type and model marketed before it owes
  the art. 26(11) notice. I read no guidance, consolidated text or case law.
- The rule for systems already in service is older than the Omnibus: art. 111(2) as enacted carried it with the
  date 2 August 2026. Point (39)(a) moved the cut-off to the later date and kept 2 August 2030.
- Limits: art. 6(2) and Annex III were not reopened for this card (they are on eu2024aiact; ADJUDICATION
  ruling 1 reports them); arts. 85 and 86 were opened in the Fable read for their subject only. No act later than 24 July 2026 was searched for, and no consolidated version was
  compared. The extracted texts carry no OJ page numbers, so locators are by article, point and recital.
- The duty in art. 26(11) is to inform the person that a high-risk system is used; it names no explanation, record
  or remedy (a complaint to the market surveillance authority sits in art. 85 and a right to explanation in
  art. 86; the logs of art. 26(6) are kept by the deployer and are not given to the person).
- Person acted on gets: record no (art. 26(11) gives notice of use only); brake no; envelope no; standing no; loss
  cover no — and the notice itself only from 2 December 2027, and only for Annex III systems (2026/1744, art. 1
  point (40)(b)); for a system placed on the market or put into service before that date, only after a significant
  change in its design (art. 111(2), as replaced by point (39)(a)).
- Corrected by V-F: the last Key fact said a search for "Article 26" finds no hit; the Omnibus does name art. 26, in "Articles 25 and 26" (art. 1 point (36)(a), art. 96(1)(a) guidelines). The conclusion stands: no point amends art. 26. The fact now says so with its locators.
