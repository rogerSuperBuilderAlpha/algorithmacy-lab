## Summary

Read in full (the blog copy; the X original was not opened). Nadella asks what "the net benefit of the AI platform
wave" is and answers with a platform test he attributes to "Bill" (Gates by context; the post gives no surname): a
platform is one where the economic value of everybody who uses it exceeds the value of the company that creates it. He cites Microsoft's partnership with OpenAI, the
"AI superfactory", AMD inference work and GitHub's Agent HQ as positive-sum examples. He sets the "real test" of the
era as gains across "the overall economy and society", illustrated by a pharma company, a manufacturer, a teacher
and a farmer. He urges every firm to keep "control of their own destiny and sovereignty" and warns against
partnerships that are "extractive in terms of value exchange". He recalls that the internet wave "hollowed out" some
sectors, naming local media, and calls for diffusion "with choice and control that is distributed".

## Key facts it relies on

- The question is framed for companies: how to "empower every company out there to get more out of this platform shift and build their own AI native capabilities and enterprise value". para. 1 [quote]
- The platform test: "a platform is when the economic value of everybody that uses it exceeds the value of the company that creates it." para. 2 (attributed to "Bill") [quote]
- The OpenAI partnership is offered as a positive-sum example: "Our investment helped them scale; their research accelerated our own innovation." para. 3 [quote]
- The "real test" is not a tech valuation record but "when the overall economy and society themselves reach new heights." para. 8 [quote]
- Individuals appear only in the illustrative list of para. 9, beside a pharma company and a manufacturer: "When a teacher personalizes lessons for every student. When a farmer predicts and prevents crop failure." para. 9 [quote]
- Capabilities are to be built "to achieve local success in each firm, which then leads to broad economic growth and societal benefits." para. 10 [quote]
- Firms must keep "control of their own destiny and sovereignty" and avoid partnerships that are "extractive in terms of value exchange in the long run". para. 10 [quote]
- The internet wave had "tremendous positive sum impact" yet some sectors "got hollowed out like local media"; this time diffusion should come "with choice and control that is distributed". para. 11 [paraphrase]
- Closing: "Each of us will need to play our part!" para. 12 [quote]

## Critical notes

The post is a chief executive's statement of strategy and carries the author's commercial interest; it reports
Microsoft's own announcements without evidence beyond them. Its unit of benefit and control is the firm: control,
sovereignty and capability are allocated to companies, and "society" appears as the downstream beneficiary of
firm-level success. It is consistent with the firm-level allocation in nadella2026frontier and nadella2026reverse.
It concedes a cost of the previous platform wave (local media), which the essay of 10 Oct 2026 does not.
It makes no promise of skilling or training to individuals; the teacher example is an illustration, not a pledge
(compare smith2025microsoft, Elevate).
Person acted on gets: record no; brake no; envelope no; standing no; loss cover no (the post addresses firms
throughout, paras 1, 10; individuals appear only as illustrations, para. 9).
