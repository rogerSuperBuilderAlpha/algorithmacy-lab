## Persons outside the deploying organisation

- P20: "those affected" by a failure or compromise; the only explicit outside party.
- P20: "industrywide" sharing, which reaches other firms, not individuals.
- P3: "A model provider", outside the deployer, named only as unable to relieve it of responsibility.
- P6, P19: standard-setters ("industry standards", "standardize on"), unnamed.
- P2: "on our behalf" implies parties acted on; none named. P19's "authorized person" is not placed inside or outside.

## Where the essay concedes a limit

- P2: mechanistic understanding "eludes us"; behaviour cannot be attributed to data or weights.
- P6: "Setting aside the hard problem of alignment"; existing standards may be "insufficient".
- P7: any capable actor "can make mistakes or be compromised".
- P9: chain-of-thought transparency is "not sufficient or dependable"; faithfulness is not yet achievable.
- P10: model-on-model checking ends in nested black boxes.
- P19: more advanced models "will require more advanced containment technologies".
- P20: assumes systems "do fail or are compromised".

## The four other posts on capacity-building, skilling or "scaffolding"

- Looking Ahead to 2026 (nadella2025looking): AI as "a scaffolding for human potential vs a substitute", posed as "the product design question"; the same post uses "scaffolds" for engineering ("rich scaffolds that orchestrate multiple models and agents"). Two senses of the word sit in one post; ruling 23 already treats the first as metaphor, not promise.
- Looking Ahead to 2026: calls for a new "theory of the mind" for humans "equipped with these new cognitive amplifier tools"; names no training, no curriculum and no recipient.
- A frontier without an ecosystem (nadella2026frontier): "you can never offload your learning"; the learning loop belongs to the firm, and employees' judgment becomes "part of systems". Capacity is the firm's human and token capital.
- The Reverse Information Paradox (nadella2026reverse): users' corrections are "exhaust" that should belong to the firm; Control, Capability, Choice, Cost, Compound are all firm-level.
- Positive Sum Future (**nadella2025positive**): "When a teacher personalizes lessons for every student" is the one image of individual capacity; otherwise "building broad capabilities" means capabilities "in each firm", and "choice and control" are distributed to firms.
- Across the four: capacity, learning and control are allocated to firms and their employees. No post mentions skilling customers, applicants or the public. The skilling promise is Microsoft's Elevate pledge (smith2025microsoft), not the posts.
