## What changed from v3

- **The charge falls on the architecture as built** (call 7). The essay's sentences name no reader (P15), no
  authoriser (P19) and no customer (P20); Microsoft's products and terms fill each gap with the deployer. A4 and CA
  now say so.
- **The domination route is split** (calls 1–2). Route (ii) judges standing relations and aggregated decisions; a
  single refund rests on route (i). No premise now claims that one refund is domination.
- **The law is listed as it stands in November 2026** (call 8; ruling 1 and erratum). Art. 26(11) leaves the
  present list; arts. 85 and 86 are narrowed; GDPR has three layers (ruling 2); *Moffatt* and OLG Hamm enter A8
  (ruling 4).
- **D03's precedent is a proof of build, not of duty** (call 11; ruling 19); **D04 adds s. 208 and its condition**
  (call 10; ruling 20).
- **Smaller narrowings**: A3 names Knox (ruling 15); A6's intent/position disanalogy is weaker than v3 said
  (ruling 14); the loss row says whose terms (rulings 5–6); the Copilot terms carry an indemnity (ruling 5).
