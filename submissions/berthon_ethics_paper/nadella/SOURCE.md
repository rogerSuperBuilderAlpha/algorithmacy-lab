## The limitation this workstream takes up

The human-factors paper is *Spaces in the Stream* (PHD1750; working copy `~/phd1750-litreview/paper/`, a private
repo; latest full text `gdoc_full_2026-10-23.md`). Its scope paragraph, at line 22 of that file, brackets the
macro-sociological, labour, political and ethical/moral layers of algorithmacy, naming platform labour governance,
market power and training paradigms, so that it can study only the perceptual layer: how attention detects a
decision space as text forms on the display. The same paragraph sets the boundary condition this workstream
inherits: marking a decision space is necessary but not sufficient, because a mark shows where the system commits
without settling how the person ought to respond or what standing they have. The paragraph is kept verbatim only in
the local corpus (`~/iit-playground/berthon_ethics_local/nadella/spaces_limitation_2026-10-23.txt`), because the
paper's repo is private and this one is public. The Spaces paper does not mention Nadella; its precursor,
`dxd_foundation`, set word separation against Nadella's post (`sources/dxf_*`).
