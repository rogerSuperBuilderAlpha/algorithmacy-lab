The four agree more than they differ. All four hold that informing the person is necessary and not sufficient
(K14), that the medium adapts (K09), and that the remedy belongs to the operator (D01). They differ on what
the history is for and on which wrong is central: the position (04–07), the burden and the medium (08), the
missing promotive design (01), or the missing conditions of learning (02). The working titles follow the theses
(M08), and Vallor's question (K15) opens only 01.

**Decision.** Choose one thesis; or merge 04–07 with 08, so that the five positions supply the historical spine
and the three wrongs supply the present-day reading; or keep them as separate papers. Whatever governs decides
which of X02–X05 matter.

### X06 How strong the safeguards claim is

The author's first draft says platform mediation shows "the total collapse of procedural buffers" (04 §5.3); his
second, "the complete absence of independent procedural safeguards" (05 §4.4; E06). The record shows platforms
are not without checks: EU law and contract law supply some (E05). What has no settled counterpart is narrower:
a check before the person is bound, independence at the first instance, and a separation of the counterparty
from the arbiter (E06; CHK-A15; CHK-D9). The narrowed claim was accepted into the author's Google Doc on
2026-10-13; the second draft, written the same day, reverted it, and the review lists this among "corrections
that reverted" (`berthon_review_REVIEW_SUMMARY.md`). The historical side is narrowed too: "sworn" notaries, the
Pragmática read "aloud" and "public," and a "requirement" of second interpreters are not in the record (H10;
CHK-A3; CHK-C12; CHK-C13). Version 07's abstract narrows the safeguards to notaries (P1-002). The objection
"the law already provides" (O02) depends on which form is chosen.

**Decision.** The narrowed claim (06/07) or the author's stronger one, which would need a source.

### X07 Disclosure: insufficient, or assumed sufficient?

The author's v4 instruction says Nadella "assumes disclosure is sufficient." The essay calls chain-of-thought
transparency "not sufficient or dependable," so 08 makes the narrower charge that its design toward people
"stops at legibility" (E13; V4N-1). The history did not begin at disclosure either: it began with the signature
as sufficient, and disclosure was the second of four stages (H24; V4N-3). Berthon, Pitt and Campbell file
education under informing; 08 separates the two, which departs from Berthon's framework (N16; V4N-6), and the
v3 change log leaves "the Berthon line in §1" open, since Berthon et al. treat informing as a first step, not a
failure. The empirical case against disclosure (E08) and the autonomy defence (O07) sit on either side.

**Decision.** Keep 08's narrower charge or source the stronger one; whether to tell Berthon that the paper parts
from his informing/guiding/restricting scheme; how the §1 Berthon sentence should read.

### X08 Scaffolding against responsibilization

08's third duty (D08) builds the competence inside the system. Its strongest objection is that this is
responsibilization by another name (O08; N17; V4N-5), and the review made the same point against the author's
critical-algorithmacy pedagogy, which asks students to audit and obfuscate after the paper says user-side
remedies fail (M05; REV-A5). Part 2 adds that skill alone never discharges the duty (D10) and that the
learning-sciences evidence is untested on systems that move (E17). Duty-to-scaffold is not itself new (V4N-4;
Scherer et al.; Vredenburgh), and financial education's effects are small (E18). Check-and-carry-risk (D06) and
the corrections (H23) are 08's guard against the objection.

**Decision.** Keep the scaffold as a duty with 08's four conditions; fold the author's pedagogy (M05) into it,
keep it as a separate section, or drop it.
