# Argument: measuring algorithmacy

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026.]

**P1.** Existing measures of competence with algorithms score a person's relation to a system: awareness and knowledge of algorithms, or a platform worker's understanding of, embracing of, leveraging of and remediating of algorithmic management. None of their items refers to coordinating with another person through the system.
- *Sources:* `lima_pdw/literature/cards/zhou2025apjhr.md` (Table 2; "No item measures a capacity to coordinate with the human counterpart", card); `lima_pdw/literature/cards/dogruel2022.md` (abstract depth).

**P2.** Generative AI increasingly stands between colleagues. It drafts reviews, filters submissions and gates work through automated checks, so the relational experience of GenAI at work includes coordinating with others through a system that evaluates both. In the lab's developer cohort, work passes a peer-review-and-vote gate and automated checks.
- *Sources:* `lima_pdw/literature/cards/hindsvonkrogh2024.md` (pp. 4, 6; conjecture); `org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/STUDY.md` (setting).

**C.** A valid measure of that experience needs items that refer to the counterpart and the system together. The study tests a three-facet scale (counterpart inference, signal compression, rule-change tracking). It tests the facet structure, invariance and growth over a 16-week cohort, and discriminant validity against the APJHR algorithmic-competency scale.
- *Instrument:* `org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/codebook.md` (12 items, 3 × 4); hypotheses H1–H4 in `PRE_REGISTRATION.md`, fixed before data collection (no external registry).
