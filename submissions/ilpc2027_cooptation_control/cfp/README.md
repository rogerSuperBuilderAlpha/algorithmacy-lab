## Format (verbatim)
- "Abstracts are externally evaluated and should be between 350 and 500 words. Key words should be given that indicate the focus of research and the methods used. The abstract should contain clear information about theoretical orientation, findings, methodology, and what contribution is being made to knowledge."
- "Abstracts of papers that are concerned solely with theoretical or conceptual matters will need to provide clear information on how they address and advance relevant debates."
- Submit to the general stream or a special stream through the platform.
