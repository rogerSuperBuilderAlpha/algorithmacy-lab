# Argument: Co-optation as control

[DRAFTED by Claude, 26 Oct 2026. For the author's ruling.]

**P1.** Labour process research locates control in the relation between employer and worker. Where it brings in customers, it treats them as an instrument the employer uses: management by customers.
- *Warrant:* the contested-terrain tradition and its algorithmic extension name control mechanisms that run from the employer to the worker.
- *Sources:*
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/kellogg2020.md` (full text; six mechanisms)
  - Fuller & Smith (1991), management by customers (**no card**; see FLAGS 3); `lima_pdw/literature/cards/wood2019.md` (full text; client ratings accumulate and cannot be contested)

**P2.** Platforms co-opt two parties at once. They enrol customers and providers in rating and ranking without making either an employee or an agent, then turn each side's evaluation into a verdict on the other. Selznick's co-optation absorbed outsiders into leadership; platform co-optation projects evaluative authority over outsiders.
- *Warrant:* the platform's own operators describe and design this three-sided structure, and marketplace studies document it.
- *Sources:*
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/selznick1949.md` (p. 13)
  - `dxd_foundation/literature/library/stark2020algorithmic.md` (pp. 48, 53)
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/starkvandenbroeck2024.md` (p. 14)
  - `lima_pdw/literature/cards/curchod2020.md`
  - `hospitality_phygital/library/cards/scottorlikowski2012accountability.md`

**C.** Under co-optation, the frontier of control runs through the relation between the two co-opted parties, not only through the employment relation. Workers' autonomy then depends on a relational competence: telling the counterpart's judgement from the rule's, making intent legible through the system, and noticing silent rule changes. It also depends on collective voice, because individual exit forfeits accumulated ratings.
- *Warrant for the step:* if control is exercised by binding two parties to each other's evaluations (P2), then the space workers can contest lies between them. That space can be held individually by competence or collectively by voice.
- *Sources:* `lima_pdw/literature/cards/hirschman1970.md` (voice, p. 30); `hospitality_phygital/library/cards/zervas2021reputation.md` (ratings do not travel across sites).
