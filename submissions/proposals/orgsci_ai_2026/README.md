# Beyond the Dyadic Straitjacket — *Organization Science*, AI-Enabled Frontiers

Stage 1 package for the *Organization Science* special issue "AI-Enabled Frontiers in Organizational
Science" (editors Gartenberg, Hasan, Pierce, Bail, Dai, Hauser, Rahman, and Zhang). The call asks for
a short proposal, a technical supplement, and a research artifact. The portal deadline is **1 November
2026**. The development workshop is **27–28 February 2027 (tentative)**. Final papers follow in summer
2027.

The working title is *Beyond the Dyadic Straitjacket: Bringing Integrated Information Theory (IIT 4.0)
and PyPhi to Organizational Coordination via a Self-Exemplifying Research Harness.* Part I is the
prospectus (1,992 words). It argues that exact system integrated information (Φ) over the
minimum-information partition separates four coordination regimes that organizational prose conflates,
and that the lab's harness — the scholar, the AI agents, and PyPhi — is itself the triad under study.
Part II is the technical addendum: formal mechanics, worked tables, and the verification compendium.
The current ten-slide deck, *Do Platforms Reduce to Pairs?*, carries that argument in the minimal
black-on-white style of the Port of Spain talk and adds a QR code to the lab.

## Status

The package was filed in the lab on 27 October 2026, ahead of the 1 November portal deadline. The v7
export is the text that answers [`STAGE1_REVIEW.md`](STAGE1_REVIEW.md). The prototype lives in this
repository at [`../../../irreducibility-pipeline/`](../../../irreducibility-pipeline/), and the lab
check `irreducibility-pipeline-reproduce` covers its 17 reference results. The workshop date is
**27–28 February 2027 (tentative)**; the call names no venue. [`STAGE1_REVIEW.md`](STAGE1_REVIEW.md)
reads Stage 3 as short *Science*/*Nature*-style articles plus a technical appendix.

An earlier v3 draft remains at [`../../orgsci_ai_frontiers_methods/`](../../orgsci_ai_frontiers_methods/).
The branch `submission/cfp-arms-2026-10` is where that draft was opened.

The addendum's numbers are evidence about Boolean coordination models. No worker is measured. Φ is the
instrument the proposal uses to explore whether a coordination form is irreducible.
