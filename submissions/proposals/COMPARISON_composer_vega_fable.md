## 0. The confound, stated first

Fable's verdicts are not directly comparable to Composer's and Vega's, because two things changed at
once: the reviewing model and the manuscript. The comparison therefore splits into two separable
questions, answered in §2 and §3:

1. **Revision efficacy** — did the Composer/Vega spine items stop appearing? (Answerable: a cleared item
   is one no Fable seat re-raises in its old form.)
2. **Panel capability** — what did Fable find that the earlier panels, reading a manuscript that
   *contained the same underlying problems*, did not? (Answerable for problems present in both drafts:
   the Shapley circularity, the Thompson gap, the quorum gloss, and the face-validity inversion all
   existed pre-revision and went unflagged twice.)

## 4. What Fable found that two prior panels missed (capability signal)

These problems existed in the pre-revision draft too. Composer and Vega read past them twice:

1. **The Shapley circularity** (05, 06 independently): the integration game's characteristic function is
   built from Φ, so "Shapley predicts complex membership" is Φ predicting Φ. Vega's formal skeptic asked
   for the characteristic function's *definition*; Fable's seats computed what the definition implies.
2. **The quorum's game-theoretic gloss is false** (05, 06 independently): 2-of-3 majority gives every
   player Shapley–Shubik 1/3 — no null players — contradicting both §3's mechanism sentence and §6's
   Null-Player row. A checkable mathematical error that survived two panels.
3. **Thompson's typology is a within-case constitution criterion** (03): pooled/sequential/reciprocal
   already orders arrangements by how jointly they determine, and the paper's quorum/cycle results refine
   that ordering without saying so. Both prior panels asked for a Thompson *citation*; neither saw he was
   an *ancestor of the criterion itself*.
4. **The face-validity inversion** (03, 08 independently): majority rule, redundancy, and slack are
   designed-for properties, and all score Φ = 0; unargued, the criterion condemns good design.
5. **The self-graded retrodiction** (08, 10 independently): the 51-catalog outcomes were known before the
   encodings were built. Vega pressed checkability; no prior seat named the circular evidential structure.
6. **Φ-version/measure non-uniqueness as a verdict problem** (01, 05, 06, 07): prior panels treated
   IIT's contested status as a consciousness-objection risk; Fable's seats point at the real formal issue —
   IIT 3.0 software vs 4.0 theory vs φ_s, state-dependence with no state policy, and sign-robustness of the
   headline verdicts across the Φ family.

Two further Fable findings are *not* capability signal, because the flagged text did not exist
pre-revision: the multihoming/leakage conflation (04) and the "every partition vs party partition"
tension as sharpened by the new §2 text (01, 06). Revision debts, recorded as such in the Fable synthesis.

## 6. Adjudicated joint recommendation (all three panels)

The Composer/Vega must-fix list is discharged; do not re-litigate it. The operative list is the Fable
synthesis's ten-item spine (`reviews_fable/SYNTHESIS.md`), of which the load-bearing five:

1. Pin the formalism: version, measure, state policy, party-partition scope; sign-robustness of headline
   verdicts.
2. Fix the quorum's game-theoretic gloss and define (or demote) the integration game; re-derive or
   downgrade the §6 game-theory row.
3. Reconcile "decidable" with the 4/10 flips via an encoding-reporting protocol — and take the editor's
   turn: encoding-as-contestation as a theme-2 power contribution.
4. Credit the ancestors precisely (Thompson above all) and add the face-validity paragraph
   (irreducibility ⊥ design quality).
5. Evidence hygiene: rebuildable encodings, catalog coding rules, soften "retrodictive traction";
   swap multihoming → leakage; fix the Kellogg miscite.

### Do not do (unchanged across all three rounds)

- Demote Φ to a decorative calculator.
- Delete the §3 exhibits or the necessary/contingent machinery.
- Pretend the submission is empirical platform measurement.

## 7. Bottom line

Three panels, two drafts, one trajectory. The first round established that the paper was the right claim
badly packaged; the revision fixed the packaging, and the second round's editor seat moved from
"revise-and-submit" to "would champion as an anchor essay." Fable then found the layer neither prior
panel checked: the formal apparatus is underspecified at exactly the points a serious review team will
verify, and one supporting result (the Shapley convergence) is circular as currently stated. The ideas
have now survived three hostile panels with the same survivors each time — the dissolution operators,
contingent-irreducibility-as-power, boundary-as-output. What the manuscript owes before January 2027 is
not another reframing. It is the engineering: pin the measure, fix the algebra, publish the encodings.
