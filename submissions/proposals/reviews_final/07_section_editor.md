# Handling guest editor's pre-submission read

**Manuscript:** "When is a combination a configuration? Integrated information and the constitution of organizational wholes"
**Special Themed Section:** Theorizing the Configurational Nature of Organizational Phenomena (Catalysing & Crystallizing)
**Role:** Handling guest editor, final pre-submission read
**Materials read:** manuscript (final_manuscript.md, ~8,500 words before references) and online supplement (final_supplement.md), nothing else.

## 1. Verdict

**Send to review** — and if the author fixes the two mechanical defects named in section 4 below before submission, I would champion it in the editorial meeting as the section's clearest theme-3 exemplar.

## 2. Fit with the call, theme by theme

The paper does something few submissions to a themed section manage: it does not answer one of the three questions, it argues that all three presuppose a fourth, and then supplies the fourth. "When is a combination a configuration at all?" is a legitimate prior question, and the opening paragraph earns the framing rather than asserting it — dynamics need an object, agency needs a whole, dialogue needs a common test. That is exactly the catalysing register the call asks for.

**Theme 1 (dynamics of configurations).** Strong fit, and the paper's best section. Section 5 answers the disorganizing question the call poses via Quattrone and Zilber, and answers it with a real theoretical claim: dissolution is not formation reversed, because it has its own operators — substitution, bypass, constraint-lifting — none of which inverts the constructive work of building a joint determination. Dissolution-by-slack (a unanimity rule drifting to two-of-three, nothing visible changing, the whole factoring) is the kind of result a verbal theory of disorganizing could not have produced. The formation/dissolution asymmetry, and the location of fragility at the fringe, at the bypassable position, and at the contingently held core member, give the dynamics theme something to build on.

**Theme 2 (agency, intentionality, causality, power).** Genuine fit, not gestured. The necessary/contingent irreducibility distinction is the paper's most original contribution: two elements with identical wiring, identical verdicts, identical core positions, separated only by a counterfactual — restore the forbidden tie and see who stays. Contingent irreducibility as "the formal shape of a configuration held together by power" is the first formal seat for power I have seen inside configurational theory proper, and the brokerage payoff (the admired *tertius iungens*, carried to completion, is self-liquidating — derived, not described) will land with network readers. The paper is also honest that the operator captures constraint-as-forbidden-tie cleanly and opacity-based power only partly. I would rather have that admission than a stretched claim. The section 7 point that encoding is itself political — a platform that gets itself modeled as pairwise contracts has factored itself out of accountability before any computation runs — connects the formalism back to power in a way reviewers on this theme will reward.

**Theme 3 (dialogue with traditions outside organization studies).** This is the paper's identity, and it executes the import by the discipline's own rule, which it names: keep the formal syntax, rework the semantics (Cornelissen, Höllerer & Seidl). Consciousness science is an unexpected neighbour, and the paper defuses the obvious objection early and again at the end: the borrowing is the partition mathematics, not the phenomenology, with population ecology and TCE as precedents for cores travelling without their home controversies. Section 6's correspondence table (game theory, platform economics, bargaining, communication theory) is disciplined about altitude — it distinguishes the Shapley row as internal coherence from the rows that are structural correspondences, which preempts the overselling charge a sharp reviewer would otherwise file.

**Out-of-scope screens.** Not a purely methodological contribution: the deliverables are concepts (constitution criterion, membership law, dissolution operators, necessary/contingent) usable "with or without the borrowed formalism," as the closing paragraph says. Not an empirical application: no case is measured, and the paper says so flatly. Not data-driven theorizing: the models are illustrations of stated encodings. The Boolean models sit squarely inside the call's "limited illustrations welcome where they advance theory" — each one exists to mark a conceptual distinction (quorum: interdependence without constitution; rotation: constitution without reciprocity; maximal wiring: density without constitution), which is illustration in the service of theory, not analysis in disguise.

## 3. The methods-trap read

This was my main worry going in, because the paper carries a lot of apparatus: two IIT versions, a toolbox citation, reachable-state scoping, a robustness recomputation, a ten-case sensitivity demonstration, and a supplement of transition tables. My read after this pass: the apparatus now functions as theory-discipline, not as a methods paper wearing an essay's clothes — with two residues that still smell of the lab.

Three things convert the apparatus into theory. First, the version-dependence result is given a substantive interpretation rather than a robustness ritual: "a constitution criterion needs zeros" is a theoretical argument for the 4.0 measure, and the binds/factors asymmetry (binds is family-robust, factors is measure-relative and flagged) is exactly the scoping a theorist needs and no more. Second, the encoding-sensitivity result is spent twice as theory — which rule flips a case *locates where the constitution lives*, and control of the encoding *is a form of power* — so the sensitivity analysis pays theoretical rent instead of just insuring the claims. Third, the supplement quarantines the tables; the essay proper works one construction in full (section 3's strict-mediation triad) and otherwise reports verdicts in prose. The catalog is handled with more candour than most submissions would risk: coders knew the outcomes, agreement is by construction, "the exercise disciplines the distinction rather than tests it." That sentence will save the paper from a methodologist reviewer.

The two residues. The manuscript's own header still carries an internal note ("Companion planning document: ot_configurational_nature_2027.md") and the supplement opens with lab plumbing — python module invocations and a named CI check ("registered as CI check `q215-phi-family-robustness`"). Journal readers should see "replication materials at [repository]," not the lab's command lines. These are trivial to fix and damaging to leave: they are exactly the artifacts that make a reviewer reclassify an essay as a methods pipeline's write-up.

**Essay form and length.** At ~8,500 words of body text the paper fits the journal's envelope with room for revision growth. It reads as an essay — voiced, claim-first, with an argument arc — but two stretches drift toward a findings parade: section 3's five-exhibit sequence and section 5's five-design-moves paragraph both read like result inventories, and the abstract is jargon-forward ("pivotality grades cores and peripheries" before any reader knows what pivotality is). Density, not structure, is the residual form risk.

## 4. Highest-leverage remaining asks

**(1) Deliver Part C or demote the four-of-ten claim.** The encoding-sensitivity figure is cited twice in the manuscript (sections 2 and 7) and does load-bearing work for the power argument, and the supplement's Part C is a placeholder: "will be included in the submission package." A twice-cited number whose backing is a promissory note is the single easiest thing for a hostile reviewer to flag, and the one I cannot defend at the editorial meeting. Either include the ten cases with their paired encodings and per-case verdicts, or soften the claim to "verdicts can flip under defensible re-encodings, as a demonstration in the replication materials shows" and cite it once.

**(2) Strip the lab residues and re-address the supplement to journal readers.** Remove the manuscript header's planning-document note and the "every number is exactly computed" self-certification (the specification paragraph in section 2 already does that work properly). Rewrite the supplement preamble as a reader-facing document: what the tables show, where the replication materials live, no CI checks, no module paths.

**(3) Thin the exhibit inventories and plain the abstract.** Section 3 needs three exhibits in text (quorum, rotation, dispatch), with the veto and maximal-wiring results compressed to a sentence each and their tables left in the supplement — the reader who has absorbed "connection is not constitution" does not need it proved five times at essay pace. Apply the same compression to section 5's five design moves (two in text, three to the supplement). Rewrite the abstract for a reader who has not yet met the vocabulary: the current version is accurate and nearly unreadable as a first contact.

## 5. Predicted review outcome

I would send this to one neo-configurational theorist, one scholar from the platforms/algorithmic-management stream, and one methodologically literate generalist. My honest forecast:

- **Most likely first-round outcome: major revision (R&R), not acceptance and not rejection.** Probability I would put around 60–65%, with perhaps 10% for minor revisions and 25–30% for rejection.
- The configurational theorist will like the complement-not-rival positioning and the Thompson sharpening (pooled factors, the closed sequential cycle binds — a case the verbal typology has no slot for), and will push on whether the criterion can ever be applied to a real arrangement without the encoding discretion swallowing the verdict. The paper has an answer (the discretion relocates and becomes theorizable), but expect a full round on it.
- The platforms reviewer will engage with the dispatch example and the necessary/contingent sort of intermediaries and will likely ask for one developed empirical vignette — which the paper must resist on scope grounds, politely, and the call gives it cover to do so.
- The main rejection risk is a reviewer who bounces off IIT entirely — either on the consciousness association despite the firewall, or on the learning curve of partition talk. The worked triad in section 3 is the right mitigation; asks (2) and (3) reduce the surface area that triggers the "methods paper" misreading, which is the other route to a hostile first review.
- Conditional on a serious revision, I would put eventual acceptance in this section at better than even. The paper is on-theme for all three questions, which is rare, and the guest-editor team's own commitments (imported formal cores, meaningful use of systems ideas, causal complexity) run in its favour.

## 6. Bottom line

This is the strongest theme-3 submission I expect to handle for this section: a genuinely new formal import, executed by the discipline's own rules for borrowing, that returns real theory on all three of the call's questions — a constitution criterion under dynamics' feet, a formal seat for power, and a dissolution account that answers the disorganizing challenge with operators rather than metaphors. The methods trap has been substantially escaped: the apparatus now argues rather than performs, and the paper says plainly what it does not do. What remains is housekeeping with teeth — a promissory supplement section that must be delivered or demoted, lab artifacts that must not reach reviewers, and two stretches of exhibit-inventory prose to thin. Fix those and I will not just send it to review; I will argue for it.
