## The new spine (structural; each flagged by 2+ Fable seats)

1. **Pin the formalism (01, 05, 06, 07, 08).** The manuscript cites IIT 3.0 software, IIT 4.0 theory, and
   PyPhi interchangeably and never states which Φ generates the verdicts; Φ is state-dependent and no state
   policy is given; 4.0's φ_s and Φ are conflated. "A definite truth value on a definite model" is true only
   of the (model, measure, state) triple. Fix: one versioning paragraph in §2 + show headline verdicts
   (quorum, rotation, dispatch, membership law) are sign-robust across the Φ family, or scope them to one.

2. **The quorum's game-theoretic gloss is false, and the Shapley convergence is near-circular (05, 06, 08).**
   In a 2-of-3 majority every player has Shapley–Shubik index 1/3 — no null players — so §3's "no single
   party is pivotal" is wrong in the outcome game, and §6's "the Null Player axiom is the substitutability
   collapse in a second notation" is contradicted by the paper's own flagship example. Separately, §4's
   Shapley values are computed in an "integration game" whose characteristic function is built from Φ
   itself: Φ predicting Φ, sold as two formalisms agreeing. Fix: define the characteristic function
   explicitly; distinguish outcome-game from integration-game pivotality; downgrade or carefully re-derive
   the §6 game-theory row. This is the most serious formal problem in the round.

3. **"Decidable" vs the four-of-ten flips (01, 02, 07, 08, 10).** §4 still says boundary disagreements
   become "decidable" while §2/§7 report 40% of cases flipping under defensible re-encoding. Reviewer 2's
   framing: the criterion relocates analyst discretion from QCA's visible condition list into transition
   rules. Fix: reconcile the language ("decidable relative to a stated encoding"), adopt a calibration-grade
   encoding-reporting protocol (QCA's own standard), and take the editor's constructive turn — promote
   encoding-as-contestation into a theme-2 power point: whoever controls the model controls the verdict
   about who is bound.

4. **The face-validity inversion (03, 08).** Majority rule, redundancy, and slack are things organizations
   build on purpose (Landau, Perrow, Cyert & March), and all score Φ = 0. Unargued, the criterion reads as
   condemning good design. Fix: one paragraph reframing irreducibility as orthogonal to design quality —
   Φ = 0 by substitutability is often the designed-for property (robustness), and the criterion measures
   constitution, not merit.

5. **Credit the remaining ancestors (01, 02, 03, 05).** Thompson's pooled/sequential/reciprocal typology is
   a within-case constitution criterion and the essay's quorum/cycle results refine its ordering; Doty,
   Glick & Huber (1993) already made the typologies checkable; Simon–Ando aggregation, Markov lumpability,
   and community detection are existing within-case decomposability tests; Schneider & Rohlfing's
   multimethod work is set-analytic within-case analysis. Fix: narrow the gap claim once more — the novelty
   is the *counterfactual-partition* test on *stated causal models*, and the quorum/maximal-wiring exhibits
   are exactly what differentiates it from the correlational/topological measures. The ammunition is
   already in the paper; it needs to be aimed.

6. **Evidence hygiene on the catalog and census numbers (01, 06, 08, 10).** The 51-intermediary
   retrodiction grades its own homework (outcomes known before encodings built); the 660-form and ten-case
   numbers are promised as rebuildable but no encodings appear. Fix: appendix (or online supplement) with
   the catalog list, coding rules, and per-exhibit encodings; soften "retrodictive traction" to
   "consistent with the record" unless a blind-coding pass is added.

7. **Platform corrections (04).** Multihoming ≠ disintermediation: the routing-around concept is *leakage*
   (Gu & Zhu 2021; Hagiu & Wright 2015), which is also the nearest unengaged rival to necessary/contingent.
   The §2 "rules readable at the application layer" sentence cites Kellogg et al. for the opposite of their
   finding — citation-integrity fix regardless of anything else. Power beyond forbidden ties (opacity,
   ranking control, non-portable reputation) is either an extension or an explicit scope limit; the
   restore-the-tie counterfactual is undefined for those mechanisms.

8. **Show Φ's added value inside necessary/contingent (03, 04, 08).** The restore-the-tie counterfactual
   needs no partition calculus, and the paper never exhibits a case Φ classifies correctly that the plain
   counterfactual misreads. Fix (affirmative, not defensive): exhibit one — e.g. a coalition-inside-the-
   whole case where the naive counterfactual misassigns the boundary and the complex catches it. Also
   relativize "necessity" to a counterfactual tie at stated cost (03): search frictions are costs, not
   prohibitions, so cost-economizing middlemen are Williamsonian integration misfiled as contingent.

9. **Define the load-bearing terms (06, 10).** "Strict mediation" is used four times with stakes attached
   and never defined; "commit" gets only an oblique gloss; §2's state-individuation sentence stacks three
   undefined terms. One worked model box (the editor's ask: elements, rules, cheapest partition, verdict in
   two minutes) solves most of this at a stroke.

10. **Style: one focused pass (09, 10).** Flatten ~a third of the epigram landings (six of seven sections
    close on one); de-passive the §2 cluster ("IIT was built…", "is encoded… is read") where the hidden
    agent is the analyst the paragraph is about; dedupe three verbatim repeats ("Surface description
    misleads in both directions" ×2, the microscope metaphor ×3, the gap sentence ×4). Antithesis 4.3/1k —
    under budget. Em-dashes no longer a top-three complaint.

## Bottom line

**Major revision, second round — different in kind from the first.** The packaging revision worked: the
paper now reads as the right claim at the right altitude, and the editor seat would champion it. What this
panel exposes is that the formal apparatus underneath the (now-correct) claims is underspecified at exactly
the points a serious review team will check: which Φ, what characteristic function, what encoding protocol,
what can a reader rebuild. None of the fixes demotes the borrow — several (differentiating Φ's
counterfactual partition from correlational decomposability tests, the Φ-beats-the-counterfactual exhibit)
require making the affirmative case *stronger*. The manuscript's ideas have survived three hostile panels;
what it owes next is the engineering.
