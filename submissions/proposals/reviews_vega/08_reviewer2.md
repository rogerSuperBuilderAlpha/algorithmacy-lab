Fair on strengths where earned.

## Step 0 — Register

Impersonal academic essay aimed at *Organization Theory*'s configurational special section. Named agents, active voice, claim-first sentences: the register is competent and not the problem. The problem is altitude. The essay writes as if a decision procedure borrowed from consciousness science settles the field's foundational question, while the evidence offered is a stack of tiny Boolean models and a correspondence table. Style clean-up is secondary; overclaim is primary. The slop audit (Part 2) treats antithesis density and usefulness-announcements; it does not police impersonal voice.

## Part 1 — Theory and structure, §§1–7

### §1 The unformalized foundation — gap manufactured, then filled

The thesis needs a gap large enough to justify importing IIT. §1 constructs one: the tradition "has no formal statement of the constitution claim" (lines 60–69), only surrogates — interdependence, fit, co-occurrence. That trilogy is tidy. It is also selective.

Complementarity economics (Milgrom & Roberts, already cited) is a formal account of when elements bind returns jointly. NK landscapes (Levinthal, Rivkin, already cited) are a formal account of interaction structure and decomposability. Simon (1962) on near-decomposability is a formal account of when wholes admit cheap cuts — and the essay later *uses* Simon as if he belonged to the new apparatus. QCA's necessity–sufficiency calculus is a formal account of combination, contested and mature. Calling all of these "symptoms" while reserved "constitution" for Φ is a rhetorical move, not a finding. A referee who reads Miller, Meyer et al., Doty & Glick, and Misangyi et al. already lives inside a tradition that treats wholes as theoretically real. The essay's "unformalized foundation" is closer to "unformalized in *this* mathematics."

The platform/contract paragraph (lines 71–86) does useful work in naming joint apparatus across ownership boundaries, but it also relocates the gap from configurational theory proper to platform scholarship's pairwise habits. Those are adjacent problems. Conflating them lets the essay claim a field-foundational hole while mostly illustrating mediated triads. State which gap is primary: constitution in the configurational canon, or joint determination in platform/contract arrangements. As written, both are recruited to make the import look inevitable.

**Strength worth keeping:** the consequences paragraph (boundary set by design; coupling as imagery; dissolution under-theorized — lines 88–96) is the real motivation. Furnari et al. (2021) and Quattrone & Zilber (2025) are apt anchors. The essay would be stronger if it led with *those* costs and stayed modest about having "no formal statement anywhere."

### §2 A borrowed formalism — provenance pressure, not demolition

The borrowing frame (Cornelissen, Höllerer & Seidl, 2021; ecology and TCE precedents) is the essay's best inoculation and should stay. Population ecology and TCE did import formal cores; that precedent is real. What a hostile referee still presses is not "IIT is merely a calculator" — that demotion would be wrong and is rejected here — but whether the *affinity* between IIT's object and organization theory's object has been earned, or merely asserted by shared vocabulary of "wholes" and "parts."

IIT was built to characterize the physical substrate of conscious experience (Tononi; Oizumi et al.; Albantakis et al.). Section 7 correctly says consciousness is not at issue. That hedge does not finish the provenance work. Organization theory borrows when the formal object maps: selection as differential survival, transaction as exchange under opportunism. Partition-irreducibility of a discrete dynamical system's cause-effect structure maps cleanly onto *that* object — a modeled transition system. It maps onto "organizational configuration" only after three heavy encoding commitments (§2, lines 166–173) redefine the target. The essay is honest that Φ is a property of the model these choices define. A referee will then ask: at what point does the "constitution of organizational wholes" become the constitution of the analyst's encoding? Section 7's sensitivity result (four of ten stylized cases flip) answers more sharply than the essay wants: the criterion travels with the encoding. That is a feature for careful modelers and a liability for a claim pitched as the field's missing foundation.

The "several hundred" coordination forms and continuous-integration reproducibility (lines 187–190) establish lab discipline. They do not establish correspondence between the lab's generative grammar of Boolean mediation and the organizational populations a special-section reader has in mind. Reproducible toys remain toys until the sampling frame is theorized.

**Do not gut the apparatus.** Φ, the complex, and the MIP are a serious formal theory of irreducibility. The ask is altitude: this essay illustrates a powerful lens on small causal models of coordination; it has not yet shown that the lens *is* the constitution claim Meyer et al. (1993) left informal. Contribution lies in principled exploration of joint determination under stated encodings. Overclaim lies in "the foundation exists… available for the price of a translation" (§7, lines 540–546).

### §3 Constitution: the criterion — where the essay earns and then overreaches

The five model contrasts (quorum, rotation, synchronization, dispatch, maximal wiring) are the manuscript's intellectual center. **This is a real strength.** The quorum result — maximal interaction density, interior thresholds, Φ = 0 — cleanly separates connection from constitution and will travel in seminars. The dispatch contrast (surface dyad, irreducible triad when the commit reads both sides) is the right platform exhibit. The maximal-wiring collapse blocks the network-surrogate shortcut. A hostile reader who came to reject the import still has to concede that interdependence-as-wiring is not enough, and that these examples make the concession vivid.

Then the prose inflates. "Irreducibility is the property the tradition's language has been reaching for" (lines 267–273), stapled to Tsoukas's conjunctive theorizing, claims priority over a verbal commitment by equating it with a computable partition test. Tsoukas's argument is epistemic and ontological in a humanistic register; it does not "become" non-factoring on a Boolean model without a mediating philosophical step the essay skips. Soften: the criterion *operationalizes one causal reading* of the conjunctive commitment. It does not capture what the tradition was "reaching for," as if decades of configurational work were a failed rescue of Φ.

The population claim — genuine triadic constitution "on the order of a tenth" of mediated three-party forms (lines 259–265) — needs more fence posts than it gets. Whose generative population? What sampling of rules, states, and party partitions? "On the order of a tenth" reads as a stylized fact about "the modern coordination landscape." It is a fact about a constructed ensemble under the lab's strict-mediation grammar. Report that ensemble as a model population; do not promote it to field topography. Invisible-without-a-criterion is fair; invisible-until-you-accept-our-sampling-frame is the honest clause.

### §4 Membership: cores and peripheries — promising, statistic-heavy

Exclusion of spectators and of a non-gating owner is org-theoretically sharp: org chart ≠ causal whole; ownership ≠ membership. The holistic-ceiling example (pivotal nodes that still fall outside because a tighter subcomplex wins — lines 305–312) is the best technical exhibit in the membership section; keep it.

The population statistics invite the Reviewer-2 objection. "In a population of 660 strict-mediation coordination forms, no element lacking bidirectional coupling entered the complex" (lines 296–298) confirms what IIT already requires (Albantakis et al., 2023). An organizational audience hears "law of membership"; a methods referee hears "the models instantiate the axiom they are built from." Say so: the result shows the axiom survives organizational encodings, not that organization theory independently discovered bidirectional coupling.

"Membership climbs from roughly four in ten… to nine in ten" (lines 301–303) and the Shapley-convergence claim need the same altitude discipline. Graded membership as a theoretical vocabulary for cores and peripheries is attractive. Selling it as a settled empirical law of organizational belonging overclaims what a Boolean reference population can bear. Pivotality predicting membership *within the ensemble* is a program result; Miller's (1996) "central orchestrating themes" do not thereby become Shapley values without another bridging argument.

### §5 Dissolution — strongest contribution, weakest evidential packaging

Three operators (substitution, bypass, constraint-lifting) and the necessary/contingent cut are the manuscript's best *theoretical* offer to the special section. Dissolution-is-not-formation-reversed is the right answer to Quattrone & Zilber, and the operators are usable. Necessary vs. contingent irreducibility is genuinely productive: same wiring, opposite constitution under a forbidden-tie counterfactual. The Simmel/Burt/Obstfeld recovery — *tertius gaudens* and hole brokers as contingent; *tertius iungens* splitting into integrator vs. self-liquidating joiner — is the rare place where borrowing *derives* a consequence the verbal literature circled. **Name this as the load-bearing contribution and defend it, rather than burying it under foundational rhetoric in §1–§2.**

Hostile presses that remain:

1. **"Catalog of fifty-one intermediary arrangements… a quarter… half"** (lines 383–390). Retrodiction about internet-era disintermediation is narrative packaged as taxonomy validation. Which fifty-one, what coding protocol, what false-positive rate among "necessary" labels? Without that, "the taxonomy earns its keep as retrodiction" is assertoric.
2. **Dealer as pure contingent conduit.** Franchise-law idealization (Lafontaine & Scott Morton, 2010) is citable, but "doing no integration franchise law does not force" overstates. Inventory, financing, and service are integrating work on any ordinary reading. Idealize openly or move the case to partial.
3. **Neighbor theories of bypass.** Essential-facilities doctrine, multihoming, and make-vs-buy already formalize bypassability and non-substitutable integration in adjacent languages. The essay's add is element-level membership relocation under a stated counterfactual on a causal model — say that. Do not write as if brokerage "never" derived the self-liquidating *tertius iungens*; Obstfeld's verbal theory could describe it; the essay formalizes the eviction.

Power-as-constraint-holding (lines 409–418) is the right formal seat for the call's agency theme *if* contingent irreducibility is not inflated into a general theory of organizational power. It is a theory of how some cores persist. Keep the scope.

### §6 One test in four vocabularies — the table that invites rejection

Table 1 is where a skeptical referee reaches for the rejection stamp. Five rows (IIT, Shapley, platform economics, bargaining, communication) are presented as "one test," then immediately qualified: only Shapley has computation behind it; the rest are "structural correspondences," and magnitudes are discarded (lines 434–447). That qualification is honest and saves the row from fraud. It does not save the *heading*. "One test in four vocabularies" (and five rows) promises consolidation the body walks back into analogy.

Problems in descending severity:

1. **Vocabulary ≠ theorem.** Rochet & Tirole / Armstrong on bottleneck and single-homing are models of price and affiliation, not MIP searches. Binmore et al.'s outside-option principle is about bargaining payoffs. Hancock/Kellogg transmit–transform–commit is a construct ladder in communication and AMS. Mapping each onto "bound / separable" is a useful heuristic exhibit after many model checks; it is not "one formal lens returns the verdicts at once" (lines 461–462).
2. **"The rows agree in sign on the models built"** — which models, how many disagreements suppressed, what counts as agreement when magnitudes are discarded? Sign agreement after the analyst builds models *to* exhibit agreement is confirmatory theater unless a protocol for failed correspondences appears.
3. **Relational sociology / systems thinking coda** (Emirbayer; Kimsey et al.; Tsoukas again) restages the foundation overclaim. Identity of "relational constitution" with "does not factor" is a philosophical thesis, not a consolidation result. Cut or quarantine.

The QCA division of labour (within-case constitution vs. across-case regularity — lines 466–475) is the one part of §6 that should survive almost untouched. It is clear, generative, and does not pretend four literatures collapsed into Φ.

**Recommendation:** retitle to correspondences and limits; keep Shapley as the computational bridge; demote platform/bargaining/communication to heuristic neighbor columns; delete "one test" rhetoric.

### §7 Boundaries and agenda — hedges that should have governed §1

The four boundaries (models are models; encoding is commitment; consciousness not at issue; one causal lens) are the correct altitude. The problem is placement. They arrive after six sections of foundation, law, consolidation, and landscape claims. A manuscript that opened with these boundaries and pitched "a formal causal reading of constitution, illustrated on small models, with three derived concepts" would be harder to reject. A manuscript that closes with them after claiming the missing foundation reads as after-the-fact containment.

Encoding sensitivity (four of ten flip) should be elevated earlier as a theoretical result about load-bearing rules, not stored in the limitations closet. The agenda items (re-derive the canon; theorize the fringe; map platform constraints) are sensible. The ride-hailing vs. partial-mediation contrast (lines 533–536) is concrete and should appear in §3 or §5 as a worked pair, of before the finale. The closing sentence's "for the price of a translational" swagger should go.

## Part 2 — Style / slop audit (register-aware)

Prose is cleaner than typical AI-cadence drafts. Residual Reviewer-2 irritants:

**1. Foundational swagger as refrain.** "Unformalized foundation," "the foundation exists," "at the foundation the distinction collapses into identity," "supplies one meaningful use at the foundation" — foundation is doing ceremonial work. Say once that the essay formalizes a constitution criterion; stop restaging the founding.

**2. Antithesis machine — moderate, partly earned.** "Connection is not constitution," "Dissolution is not formation run backwards," "Same position, opposite constitution" each earn one hit. Re-staging the not-X/is-Y shape across §3 and §5 for cadence should be cut. Density target: under five combined antithesis markers per thousand words; several paragraphs in §3 currently carry two.

**3. Self-narrating usefulness.** "Theoretical economy a pluralist field can actually use"; "available for the price of a translation"; "that is the kind of theoretical economy…" — delete the applause lines; leave the substantive claim.

**4. Microscope metaphor.** "Microscope slides of the argument" / "slides the microscope can hold" — once is charming; recurrence announces the metaphor instead of showing what the models do.

**5. Opening uniformity in §3.** Five "Consider…" exhibit openings in a row is outline voice. Vary.

Overall slop: **acceptable register, overconfident refrain.** Fix swagger and usefulness-announcements; the house antithesis problem is secondary.

## Part 3 — Line-level revisions (paste-ready)

**R1 — §1, shrink the gap claim (lines 60–69).** Replace the "no formal statement anywhere" stretch with:
> Configurational theorizing has rich formalisms for interaction, complementarity, fit, and set-membership in outcomes. What it still lacks is a partition test that takes one stated arrangement and answers whether its joint determination factors. The present essay offers that test for the causal reading of constitution, not a claim that prior formalisms were non-formal.

**R2 — §2, earn the mapping (after the five pieces).** Add:
> The formal object is a discrete dynamical system under a stated encoding. Organizational configuration enters when analysts accept that encoding as a model of joint determination. The essay's contribution is the disciplined use of partition irreducibility on such models, and the concepts that use makes available — not the discovery that organizations are conscious or that Φ is already what Meyer et al. meant.

**R3 — §3, fence the population rarity claim (lines 259–265).** Change to:
> In a constructed ensemble of mediated three-party Boolean forms used by the companion research program, arrangements whose worker, mediator, and counterpart all bind into one irreducible whole are uncommon — on the order of a tenth of that ensemble. That is a fact about the ensemble under stated generative rules. Whether the modern coordination landscape matches the ensemble is a separate empirical question the criterion makes askable.

**R4 — §3, soften the Tsoukas capture (lines 267–273).** Change "the property the tradition's language has been reaching for" to "one formalization of the causal reading of that commitment."

**R5 — §4, axiom vs. discovery (after lines 296–298).** Add:
> Bidirectional coupling as necessary for membership is required by the source theory. The organizational models show the requirement survives the encodings; they do not inductively establish it.

**R6 — §5, dealer idealization (lines 379–381).** Soften "doing no integration franchise law does not force" to "in the limiting encoding that strips inventory, financing, and service, the residual position is a conduit franchise law can forbid the parties from bypassing."

**R7 — §5, fifty-one catalog (lines 383–390).** Either (a) point to a coding appendix with inclusion rules and disagreement counts, or (b) demote to "illustrative sorting of a provisional catalog." Do not let retrodiction validate the taxonomy in one paragraph without protocol.

**R8 — §6, retitle and rewrite the consolidation claim.** Change heading and lead-in to:
> ## 6. Correspondences across vocabularies
> The irreducibility criterion aligns in sign with several tests neighbouring literatures already run. The strengths of those alignments differ, and only one has been computed rather than exhibited.

Then keep the altitude split already in the prose; delete "one test" / "returns the verdicts at once" / foundational identity with relational sociology.

**R9 — §7 → front matter.** Move the four boundaries (or a compressed version) into §2 after the modeling commitments, so the microscope claim never outruns its hedged status.

**R10 — Close.** Replace the translation-price flourish with a flat claim about what configurational scholars can use: criterion, membership, dissolution operators — as concepts illustrated on small models, portable with or without running Φ on every case.

## Verdict

**Reject and resubmit (major rewrite framed as rejection of the current pitch).** The essay contains a serious idea — partition irreducibility as a within-case constitution test, with membership and dissolution operators, and a necessary/contingent cut that gives power a formal seat — and the §3 exhibits are good enough that a revised manuscript could clear a tough OT review. The *present* manuscript packages that idea as the missing foundation of configurational theorizing, consolidates five literatures into "one test," and promotes ensemble frequencies into landscape facts. That packaging will not survive a skeptical referee, and it should not. Consciousness controversy is correctly parked; the failure mode is contribution altitude, not apparatus-bashing.

**Priority order for any revision that wants a second look:** (1) R8 — kill consolidation theater in §6; (2) R1–R3 — resize the gap and fence population claims; (3) lead with §5's operators and necessary/contingent as the special-section contribution, with §3's quorum/dispatch as evidence, not with "unformalized foundation"; (4) R9 — put boundaries where they govern claims.

**Most important fix:** Stop claiming the essay supplies *the* foundation of configurational constitution. Claim a formal causal criterion for when a modeled arrangement is one joint determination, show where that criterion cuts differently from wiring and co-occurrence, and derive membership and dissolution. That paper is publishable. The foundation-and-consolidation paper is not.

## Biggest strength

The **§3 quorum and dispatch results**, paired with **§5's necessary/contingent irreducibility**. Together they show something org theory actually needs: interaction can be maximal while constitution fails, surface dyads can hide irreversible joint commits, and two cores with identical wiring can differ by whether a forbidden tie is doing the binding. That is affirmative use of the integrated-information formalism on organizational encodings — exploration the field can use — without requiring the essay to annex the configurational canon's entire philosophical base.

## The one thing only the author can supply

A **sampling and coding protocol** for every population number readers are asked to remember (the ~1/10 triad rate; 660 forms; membership-by-pivotality climb; fifty-one intermediaries' quarter/half split) and a **failed-correspondence log** for §6. Without those, the statistics and the table remain persuasion. With them, a hostile referee can still argue about altitude but cannot dismiss the program as unreproducible swagger. Only the author, holding the lab's generative grammar and exclusion rules, can write that protocol into the paper.
