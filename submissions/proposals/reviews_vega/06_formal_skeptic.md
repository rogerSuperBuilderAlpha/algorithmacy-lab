## Part 1 — Theory and structure, abstract–§7

### Abstract and §1 — the methods-trap for a theory CfP

The essay names a real foundation gap: configurational theorizing presupposes constitution and has supplied surrogates instead of a criterion. That question belongs here. The risk is genre. A Catalysing & Crystallizing call wants conceptual advance; the manuscript delivers a runnable partition test, graded membership, and three dissolution operators on Boolean microscopes. A formal-methods referee will ask whether the contribution is a *theory of constitution* or a *method for scoring encodings* dressed as theory.

Davis, Eisenhardt and Bingham (2007) sit in section 7’s reference list, cited for elicitation burden. Incomplete. Their standards — simple theory, computational experiment as unpacking, results tied to constructs — should frame §1. Do the results *develop* theory (new propositions on formation, membership, dissolution) or *demonstrate* an imported measure on stylized cases? Sections 3–5 largely look like the former; population voice (“660 forms,” “fifty-one intermediaries,” “a tenth”) often sounds like the latter. Separate altitudes early: concepts for configurational theorizing versus frequencies in a modeled catalog under fixed encoding commitments.

### §2 — Borrowed formalism and Φ validity

The import rule (keep syntax, rework semantics; Cornelissen et al., 2021) is the right frame, and the consciousness quarantine in §7 is necessary. An IIT-skeptical referee will still press three validity points that §2 must anticipate rather than defer.

**1. Which Φ?** The essay cites Albantakis et al. (2023) and Mayner et al. (2018) for Φ, the complex, and the MIP. Experience debates are correctly quarantined; the *formal* uniqueness debate is not. Rival partition scorings can disagree on one transition system, so “constitution = Φ > 0” is a family, not one criterion. Keep: partition irreducibility is a principled lens on joint determination. Trim: “strictest sense available anywhere” (lines 121–123) — uniqueness the validity literature does not grant. Name the PyPhi operationalization and admit rivals could re-cut some slides.

**2. Three commitments are theory, not preface.** Application-layer encoding, state-individuation, and the party partition (lines 166–173) do most of the organizational work. A referee can grant IIT’s math and still reject the privileged cut or under-determined commits under opacity. Elevate: constitution is irreducibility *relative to* that triple. Without relativization, §3’s boundaries sound like claims about the world rather than about an encoding and cut family.

**3. Exact computability is scope.** “Models large enough to think with” (lines 186–188) is fine as microscope slogan. It does not license unaltered scaling to million-party platforms. Say whether coarse-graining, modular complexes, or sampled cuts are deferred, denied, or unnecessary — or silence reads as if small-system Φ *is* the organizational criterion.

### §3 — Constitution: theory keeps; evidence overruns altitude

The five contrasts — quorum, rotation, synchronization, dispatch, maximal wiring — are excellent illustrations. Connection without constitution (and the reverse) is what a formalization must show to displace surrogates. Keep them; they carry the affirmative case for a partition lens.

Evidential framing is the problem. Majority-gate Φ = 0 (lines 215–217) is exact *for the model*. “Configurational fact about the modern coordination landscape” (lines 262–265) claims field frequency; it is frequency in a mediated Boolean catalog under the party partition. Per Davis et al., state simulation findings as properties of the virtual system, then bridge carefully. Reclassify: irreducible triads are uncommon in the enumerated space — which motivates asking whether apparent platform triads factor once encoded. Not a landscape fact until field encodings exist.

### §4 — Membership: strong derivation, two formal gaps

Deriving the complex as membership is the cleanest payoff. Exclusion of spectators and pivotality as graded predictor are right. Two demands:

**Define the integration game.** Shapley / Null Player convergence (lines 320–329) is the best consolidation *if* computational rather than analogical. Is coalition value its Φ, its marginal contribution to system Φ, or something else? Without that clause, membership prediction is unauditable from the essay. Define it in §4 or §6.

**Holistic ceiling.** The counterpart-pair complex under all-required commit (lines 305–312) is a gift to local-measure skeptics — keep it. It also shows pivotality’s graded law has theory-generated exceptions. State the exception class once so law and ceiling do not fight.

### §5 — Dissolution operators and necessary / contingent

Substitution, bypass, and constraint-lifting as asymmetric operators are the strongest answer to disorganizing, and they pass Davis’s test: recomputation under rule change unpacks mechanisms verbal theory only gestured at. Keep the design-move catalog.

Necessary / contingent is where encoding sensitivity bites. Cases are observationally identical until the forbidden-tie counterfactual — correct — but in field material that “forbidden tie” is often itself an encoding choice. The fifty-one-arrangement quarter/half taxonomy (lines 383–386) is *classification under provisional models*, not internet-history retrodiction, unless coding was pre-registered without peeking at Φ. Demote “earns its keep as retrodiction” unless the protocol is stated.

Keep irreducibility-now distinct from necessary-under-bypass: contingent gates can show Φ > 0 today. Section 5 mostly gets this; the abstract still risks sounding as if Φ alone sorts them.

### §6 — Consolidation: altitude discipline is stated, then undercut

The table’s split between computed Shapley agreement and structural correspondence on other rows (lines 434–447) is the manuscript’s best self-policing. Keep it; enforce it in prose.

Undercut: “the distinction collapses into identity” (lines 452–453). Sharing a partition question is not program identity. Prefer: at the constitution question, traditions can share a test; they keep search, meaning, and set-analytic questions. Elevate the QCA division of labour (lines 466–475) as the template.

### §7 — Boundaries: close, three sentences short

“The models are models” and the ten-case / four-flip encoding note are the right altitude. Theorize the flips: when re-encoding changes the verdict, the product is *which rule carries constitution*, not a natural-kind label. Lean into that.

Still missing: Davis standards applied to §§3–5; Φ-family divergence; population frequencies as catalog facts. Consciousness quarantine and the interpretive-lens limit are adequate. Among agenda items, *Re-derive the canon* best fits a theory CfP — turn the microscope on Miles & Snow / Mintzberg before lab populations.

## Part 3 — Line-level revisions (paste-ready)

**R1 — §1, Davis altitude (after line ~96, before the roadmap).** Add:
> Computational illustrations below follow the discipline Davis, Eisenhardt and Bingham (2007) set for simulation as theory development: simple constructs, transparent rules, results stated first as properties of the models, then offered as concepts for configurational theorizing. Population counts reported from the microscope are frequencies in an enumerated model space under fixed encoding commitments, not measurements of organizations.

**R2 — §2, Φ-family honesty (after line ~159).** Add:
> The essay commits to one operationalization of integrated information as implemented for exact computation on small transition systems (Mayner et al., 2018; Albantakis et al., 2023). Rival partition scorings exist; where they diverge, the illustrations would mark sensitivity rather than invalidate the broader claim that constitution can be read as causal irreducibility under a stated cut family.

**R3 — §2, relativize the criterion (near lines 166–173).** Recast the commitments close as:
> Constitution, on this account, is irreducibility relative to a stated application-layer encoding, a state-individuation rule, and a party-respecting partition family. The verdict is a property of that triple.

**R4 — §3, demote landscape ontology (lines 260–265).** Change the rarity close to:
> Among mediated three-party forms encoded under these commitments, arrangements whose worker, mediator, and counterpart all bind into one irreducible whole are uncommon — on the order of a tenth of the enumerated population — while forms with direct party-to-party edges bind more often. That frequency is a fact about the model space; it poses an empirical question whether platforms that present as triads often factor once encoded the same way.

**R5 — §4, define the integration game (after line ~324).** Add:
> The integration game takes coalitions of elements as players; the characteristic function assigns to each coalition the integrated information of the subsystem it induces (or its marginal contribution to system Φ — authors: pick one and stick to it). Shapley value is then average marginal contribution under that function; Null Player marks elements whose marginal contribution is nowhere positive.

**R6 — Abstract / §5, keep Φ and bypass distinct.** Add one clause where necessary/contingent is introduced:
> Observationally identical cores with Φ above zero still split once a forbidden direct tie is restored: necessary members remain; contingent members leave.

**R7 — §5, demote retrodiction claim (lines 386–390).** Change to:
> On provisional models of fifty-one intermediary arrangements, roughly a quarter read as necessary integrators and half as contingent conduits. The pattern lines up with familiar dissolution histories where cheap direct ties displaced unconstrained middlemen while joint-condition integrators persisted; turning that alignment into a formal retrodiction requires a pre-registered coding of constraints and outcomes the present essay does not claim.

**R8 — §6, temper identity collapse (lines 452–453).** Change to:
> At the constitution question, systems perspectives, complexity science, relational sociology, and configurational theorizing can share a partition test for whether a named whole is one causal unity. They keep their other questions — search, meaning, across-case regularity — intact.

**R9 — §7, theorize encoding flips (after the ten-case sentence).** Add:
> Sensitivity is part of the contribution. When a defensible re-encoding flips the verdict, the theory has located which rule the claimed constitution hangs on — joint against sequential sign-off, commit against display — which is often the organizational quarrel that mattered.

**R10 — §2, trim uniqueness advertising.** Replace “in the strictest sense available anywhere” with “a mathematical account of when interacting elements constitute one integrated whole.”

## Verdict

**Major revisions** on validity altitude and genre fit — not on borrowing IIT. Partition irreducibility as a lens on constitution is credible; §3’s micro-results and §5’s operators are the right theory work for this CfP. What blocks acceptance: population frequencies voiced as landscape facts; consolidation peaking at “identity”; Φ uniqueness language; Davis et al. used as calibration hedge rather than simulation-as-theory standards. Encoding sensitivity is already present — theorize it (R9) and relativize the criterion to the three commitments (R3). None of this demotes the apparatus; it keeps Φ working at the altitude Boolean models can defend.

**Most important fix:** R1 + R4 — computational results are properties of models and enumerated spaces; stop calling rarity a fact about the modern coordination landscape.

## Biggest strength

The **§3 microscope battery** (quorum / rotation / synchronization / dispatch / maximal wiring). Exact, auditable separations of constitution from interdependence and wiring density — the affirmative case for partition machinery without inflating catalog counts into field ontology.

## The one thing only the author can supply

Whether the **Shapley consolidation uses subsystem Φ or marginal contribution to system Φ** as characteristic function, and whether that choice was fixed before membership correlations were read. A skeptic grants the convergence only if the game is defined tightly enough to reproduce; as written, the OT reader cannot check it from the text alone.
