## The spine fixes (structural; each flagged by 2+ reviewers)

1. **Population claims → enumerated model class OR Methods appendix (06, 08, 07, 10, 04).** The 660-form
   population, ~10% triadic rate, 4/10→9/10 membership climb, and 51-intermediary catalog do argumentative
   work. Hedge every instance to "in one Boolean coordination class" or add a short Methods appendix with
   encoding rules and reproduce pointer. Delete "configurational fact about the modern coordination landscape"
   unless backed by field data.

2. **Encoding sensitivity moves up (06, 08, 09, 10).** Four-of-ten flip on stylized cases (§7) and
   three modeling commitments (§2) should appear in abstract + §2 opening. Verdicts are on stated encodings;
   say so before population statistics.

3. **§6 table altitude (01, 06, 08, 02, 07).** Only Shapley row is derived; platform/bargaining/communication
   rows are structural correspondences on lab models. Soften "collapses into identity" with Emirbayer/Kimsey.
   Add **QCA row** and complementarity vignette: sufficient recipe across cases, aggregate within case
   (transmit-only dispatch).

4. **One platform case in main text (03, 04, 08, 07).** Dispatch exhibit (§3) is pedagogically strong but
   Boolean-only. Move Uber/Upwork (or equivalent) membership contrast from §7 agenda into §5 with stated
   rules. External reader must check encoding without private repo.

5. **Thompson (1967) mediating technology (03, 07).** J. D. Thompson's mediating technology is the canonical
   org-theory ancestor of the triad. One paragraph in §1 or §2: mediating technology is old; opaque interested
   adaptive mediation is new.

6. **Williamson / essential facilities as verbal precedents (03).** Constraint-lifting is make-or-buy with a
   counterfactual. Name Williamson and essential-facilities doctrine once; claim contribution is
   function-level, rule-based, remedy-linked — not novelty of bypass idea.

7. **Davis et al. (2007) framing forward (06).** Simulation-as-theory-building belongs in §2, not only §7
   limitations. Small Boolean models are theory probes, not population inference.

8. **IIT critics paragraph (06, 08).** One paragraph engaging formal Φ critiques (partition dependence, proxy
   validity) — not consciousness debate. Disarms hostile readers.

9. **House-style compression (09).** Cut "inspectable rather than asserted"; merge encoding refrain across
   §2/§7; vary "integrating work the others cannot reproduce."

10. **Abstract trim + provenance note (07, 08).** OTT word limit; add "on stated models" and brief IIT
    provenance ("formal core from consciousness research").
