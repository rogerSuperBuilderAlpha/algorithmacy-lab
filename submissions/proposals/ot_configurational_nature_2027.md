## What the call asks for

The section wants theoretical work on the configurational character of organizational phenomena. Its
premise is that configurational traditions — set-analytic, typological, complexity-based, network-based,
relational — share one insight: organizational phenomena are constituted through combinations, relations,
and interdependencies rather than independent attributes. The editors invite three kinds of theme: (1) the
dynamics of configurations, how they form, stabilize, and come apart; (2) agency, intentionality, and
causality in configurational accounts; (3) dialogue between configurational theorizing and other
intellectual traditions, explicitly including traditions from outside organization studies that developed
configurational ideas without the label. Welcome forms include theory papers, generative essays,
provocations, and cross-disciplinary syntheses. Out of scope: purely methodological contributions,
empirical applications, and data-driven theorizing. Limited empirical illustrations are welcome where they
advance theory.

The CfP names the lab's home terrain directly: "platforms, algorithms and human-AI assemblages (Hsieh &
Vergne, 2023; Glaser, Sloan & Gehman, 2024)" appears in its list of areas where configurational intuitions
have developed under other names.

## The pitch

Configurational theorizing rests on a claim of constitution: a configuration is a whole whose character
lives in the combination, and the elements do their causal work jointly. The tradition has rich
vocabularies for describing such wholes — fit, complementarity, conjunctural causation, equifinality — and
a mature set-analytic method for finding which combinations co-occur with an outcome. What it lacks is a
formal account of the constitution claim itself: a principled criterion for when a set of interdependent
elements is one configuration and when it is an aggregate of smaller ones, a way to say which elements
belong to the whole, and a theory of the operations that dissolve it.

Integrated information theory supplies exactly this, and supplying it is the paper. IIT is a neighbouring
discipline's configurational formalism in the CfP's precise sense: a body of theory, built in consciousness
science, whose formal core answers "when is a system irreducible to its parts?" A system's integrated
information (Φ) measures how much its cause-effect structure exceeds what the system's minimum-information
partition can carry. Φ > 0 says no way of cutting the system into parts preserves what the whole does:
constitution, stated causally and computed exactly on models small enough. The theory's *complex* names
which elements the irreducible structure binds — a membership account, missing from configurational
vocabulary, where a configuration's boundary is usually inherited from the research design. The borrowing
is formal. Φ measures causal irreducibility, and that content stands apart from IIT's contested status as
a theory of consciousness; no claim is made that any organization is conscious.

The paper's contribution to configurational theorizing is three concepts, each carried by the formalism
and each stated so it can be used without it:

1. **Constitution has a criterion.** A combination is a configuration when its cause-effect structure is
   irreducible over every partition of its elements, and an aggregate when some partition factors it.
   The criterion separates cases the surface description cannot: three named parties can factor into a
   dyad with a wire through it, and an arrangement that looks like a pass-through can be irreducible.
   Interdependence, the usual test, is weaker than constitution — a k-of-n quorum reads every member and
   binds all of them at unanimity, yet factors at every interior threshold, where each member is
   substitutable. Counting connections misses this; the irreducibility criterion does not.
2. **Membership is graded and computable.** The complex says which elements are inside the configuration,
   and across a population of models, membership tracks an element's causal pivotality to the joint
   determination. Configurations therefore have cores and peripheries as a matter of causal structure, an
   account of boundary that configurational theorizing currently sets by fiat.
3. **Dissolution has operators, and they are not formation reversed.** The formalism identifies specific
   operations that collapse a configuration: making an element substitutable, opening a bypass path around
   it, moving a threshold off its extreme. A distinction follows that speaks to the section's
   disorganizing question (Quattrone & Zilber, 2025): an element can be irreducible *necessarily*, because
   it does integrating work the others cannot reproduce, or *contingently*, because an external constraint
   forbids the others from reaching each other — the car dealer held in the triad by franchise law. One
   counterfactual separates them: restore the forbidden tie and read whether the element stays in the
   core. Contingent configurations dissolve when the constraint lifts; necessary ones survive it. The same
   distinction gives theme 2 its hook: some configurations persist because power holds a constraint in
   place, a durability no element's own rule contains.

The cross-disciplinary synthesis widens from there. The irreducibility criterion turns out to be the
common image of tests that four other literatures draw in their own terms: the null player and pivotality
of cooperative game theory, the bypassable route of platform economics, the binding outside option of
bargaining theory, the transmit/transform/commit ladder of communication theory. One formal lens returns
all four verdicts on the same model, which is the kind of consolidation across traditions the section is
convened to produce.

## Which themes it answers

Theme 3 is the primary home: the paper brings a formal tradition from outside organization studies —
one that developed configurational ideas without the label, as the CfP asks — into direct dialogue with
configurational theorizing, and it connects on the way to relational sociology (constitution through
patterned relations; Emirbayer, 1997), complexity science (emergence, thresholds, nonlinear collapse), and
Tsoukas's (2017) conjunctive theorizing, which the irreducibility criterion formalizes: a conjunctive
account is one whose model does not factor. Theme 1 is the secondary line: the dissolution operators and
the necessary/contingent distinction are a theory of how configurations come apart, and they answer the
CfP's question about whether disorganizing reverses organizing — it does not; formation builds joint
determination, while dissolution can proceed by substitution, bypass, or constraint-lifting without
touching what was built. Theme 2 enters through the constraint-held configuration: contingent
irreducibility is the formal shape of a configuration sustained by power rather than by any actor's
intention.

## Working title and abstract

**When is a combination a configuration? Integrated information and the constitution of organizational
wholes.**

> Configurational theorizing rests on a claim of constitution: organizational phenomena are wholes whose
> character lives in how elements combine. The tradition has rich vocabularies for describing such wholes
> and mature methods for finding which combinations co-occur with outcomes. What it lacks is a formal
> account of the constitution claim itself — a criterion for when interdependent elements form one
> configuration and when they factor into aggregates, an account of which elements belong to the whole,
> and a theory of how configurations dissolve that is not formation run backwards. This essay supplies
> that account by borrowing the formal core of integrated information theory. A combination is a
> configuration when its cause-effect structure is irreducible over every partition of its elements. The
> theory's complex names membership; pivotality grades cores and peripheries; and dissolution proceeds
> by substitution, bypass, or constraint-lifting. The constraint operator yields a distinction between
> necessary and contingent irreducibility. The criterion consolidates tests that cooperative game theory,
> platform economics, bargaining theory, and communication theory draw in their own vocabularies. Small
> Boolean models illustrate throughout; the essay is theory, and the models are its microscope.

The abstract is aligned with the revised manuscript; rework against the journal's word limit before
submission. The title choice between the question form above and a declarative alternative
("Constitution, membership, dissolution: …") is open.

## The argument, section by section

1. **The unformalized foundation.** Configurational theorizing's constitution claim, from Miller's gestalt
   through Fiss (2011), Misangyi et al. (2017), and Furnari et al. (2021). What the set-analytic method
   answers (which combinations co-occur with an outcome) and what it presupposes (that the combination is
   a whole). The gap: interdependence is treated as sufficient for constitution.
2. **The borrowed formalism.** IIT's formal core in organizational terms: elements, mechanisms,
   cause-effect structure, partition, Φ, complex. Presented as theory, with the computation (exact, via
   PyPhi, on small Boolean models) as the discipline behind the concepts. The borrowing tradition:
   organization theory took population thinking from biology and transaction costs from economics;
   integrated information is the next borrowing in that line.
3. **Constitution.** The criterion, and the two illustrations where it cuts against the surface reading:
   the quorum that binds only at its extremes, and the pass-through loop that is irreducible. What the
   criterion adds over interdependence, complementarity, and internal fit.
4. **Membership.** The complex as the configuration's causal boundary; graded membership by pivotality;
   cores and peripheries as computed structure. Contrast with boundary-by-design in QCA and
   boundary-by-nomination in network analysis.
5. **Dissolution.** The operators; necessary versus contingent irreducibility and the restore-the-tie
   counterfactual; the dealer case; what this says about disorganizing, durability, and the power that
   holds contingent configurations in place.
6. **The consolidation.** The four-literature convergence table, at the altitude the synthesis already
   fixed: the verdicts agree in sign on the models built, one correspondence has computation behind it,
   and the binary verdict discards the magnitudes the economic theorems are about.
7. **Boundaries of the borrowing.** The models are small and Boolean; verdicts are exact for the model and
   are evidence about the model; a documented share of modeled cases flip verdict under a defensible
   re-encoding, so encoding is a first-order theoretical commitment; and IIT's status as a theory of
   consciousness is neither used nor needed. A research agenda for configurational scholars closes: which
   canonical configurations of the strategy-structure typologies are irreducible on their own stated
   couplings, and which factor.

## Scope discipline and risks

- **The methods trap.** The CfP excludes purely methodological contributions, and a paper about a
  computable criterion drifts toward methods on its own. The guard: every section leads with the
  theoretical concept and uses the computation as warrant, and the paper proposes no procedure for
  readers to apply to data. The models are illustrations in the CfP's sense — limited, and in service of
  theory.
- **The empirics trap carries no risk here.** The lab has no field data to overclaim; the validation gap
  is stated once, in section 7, and the in-silico character of the illustrations fits the section's
  scope.
- **The consciousness objection.** A reviewer may hold IIT's contested status against the borrowing. The
  answer is in the paper's own terms — the formal content is used, the phenomenological claims are set
  aside — and precedent helps: organization theory's other borrowings (population ecology, transaction
  costs) also detached formal cores from home-discipline controversies.
- **Reviewer fit.** Set-analytic scholars may read "causal irreducibility" as competing with conjunctural
  causation. It complements it: QCA finds which combinations matter for an outcome across cases; the
  criterion says whether a given combination is one whole within a case. Section 4's contrast carries
  this and should be written defensively.

is that the formalism gives configurational scholarship a principled account of constitution, membership,
and dissolution it has so far stated only informally.
