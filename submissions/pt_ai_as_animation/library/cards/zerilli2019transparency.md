If that reading holds, the authors would object to the way c7 treats AI opacity as a special feature of this kind of system. Their line would be that human decision-making is also opaque, so the opacity does not mark AI off, and a demand that algorithms meet a stricter standard of transparency than people do applies a double standard. c7 then has to say what is special about opacity that is "necessary", and why the human comparison does not remove it.
