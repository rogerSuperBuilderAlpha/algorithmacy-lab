# AI development as animation (*Philosophy & Technology*) — opened 2026-11-08

[DRAFTED by Claude, 2026-11-08.]

`submission/pt-ai-as-animation`.

**Thesis (Roger's, with the model-centrism edit he accepted).** Creating artificial intelligence is
better conceived as creating artificial life. Model-centrism, the view that the model *is* the AI,
is the mistake: a working AI is the LLM plus "harnesses, virtual machines, efficiencies," and making it
is closer to bringing an animated character to life. Animation and AI share a necessary opacity,
co-optation and a triadic structure.
