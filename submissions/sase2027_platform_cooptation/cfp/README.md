**Event:** 39th SASE Annual Conference, "Rebuilding Foundations and Hope: Creative Reconstruction in a Moment of Disruption". University of Strathclyde, Glasgow, 30 June – 3 July 2027; virtual events 16–18 June 2027.

## Dates (verbatim)
- "The final deadline for submissions to mini-conferences is the 1st of November, 2026. This is different to previous years and earlier than submissions to networks (16th of December, 2026)."
- No timezone is stated.

## Format (verbatim)
- "To submit an individual presentation, you must include an abstract. Abstracts are expected to be 1,000 words, not including references."
- Mini-conferences recommend, but do not require, a full paper before the conference.
- "No paper or panel may be submitted twice to different networks or mini-conferences." "no author may present more than two papers".
- Portal: Oxford Abstracts.

## Correction to the 26 Oct search
MC04 is "Working time reduction", not algorithmic management. The fitting homes are:
- **MC09 "Technopolitics: Power, Competition and Contestation over Digital Infrastructures"** (Anwar, Rolf, Thelen, Cross, Lehuedé, Taylor, O'Reilly). Its call says: "Digital infrastructures (broadly defined) have become unavoidable and powerful intermediaries through which our socio-economic, political and ecological relations are organised." Its indicative panel is "Labour, class and exploitation in algorithmic capitalism and AI". **Due 1 Nov 2026.**
- **Network J, Digital Economy** (Beuscart, Ergen, Jourdain, Lehdonvirta, Mellet, Naulin, Vargha, Moreno). It covers "intermediaries and business models such as the platform economy, coordination instruments such as participatory evaluation systems, digital reputations". **Due 16 Dec 2026.**
