## *Personnel Psychology*: the setting has to show generative AI

The abstract cannot be made to fit by wording. Three routes, for the author:

1. **Add a generative-AI block to the protocol before wave 2.** If participants build with coding
   agents and generative tools (likely in an AI engineering cohort, but not recorded in the study),
   measure that use at waves 2 and 3 and report it as the setting's generative-AI exposure. This
   needs the Research Progress & Review Form the pre-registration already anticipates.
2. **Use the GauntleTT cohort**, which the Lima abstract describes as an AI engineering program, as
   the sample or a second sample. It needs its own IRB.
3. **Retarget.** Without generative AI in the setting, the scale paper fits a general measurement
   venue better than this special issue.

Until one is chosen, the abstract's first two sentences are claims about the world, not about the
study, and a reviewer will read them as such.

## What was checked for every arm

Required elements (length, headings, keywords, anonymity), the call's verbatim topic lines, the
venue's audience and genre (case journal, executive journal, phenomenology society), and whether the
abstract names the call's object in the call's own words at least once without quoting the call.
Overlap between arms was not re-audited; the 26 Oct audit's split table stands.
