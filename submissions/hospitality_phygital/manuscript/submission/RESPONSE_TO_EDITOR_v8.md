**What the paper does not claim.** The paper's cases are taken from published studies and judgments, and they illustrate its distinctions rather than test them; the five stages inherit that limit, since they order what the argument asks of a host and we have not tested them as an intervention. The paper's own step, the host's further duty during the stay, is argued as a moral claim. No law we read requires it, and the paper says what the law does require. The argument leaves aside guests who cannot report a failure and hosts who let rooms in their own homes.

**Length.** The body, from the introduction to the conclusion, runs to 8,737 words. The reference list holds 63 entries, with 22 cases and 10 items of legislation listed separately.
