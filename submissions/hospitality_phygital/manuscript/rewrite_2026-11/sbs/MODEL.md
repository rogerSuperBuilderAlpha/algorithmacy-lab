## Introduction (no heading in the manuscript) · 3 paragraphs · pp. 2–3

### M.001 · Intro ¶1 · p. 2 · ~26w
> 'Jacques Derrida's writings on hospitality make him … web of paradoxes and uncertainties.' Frame: *X's writings on hospitality make him, along with Y, perhaps the concept's foremost theorist*.

- **Move** — CANON-STANDING. A named figure is the subject, credited in a superlative softened by 'perhaps', and a second verb ('describe') adds what the writings contain.
- **Paragraph role** — first of five. Sets up the figure whose limits the paragraph ends on.
- **Research question** — none yet. It names whose account the question will be put to.
- **Thesis** — prepares it. The thesis is 'beyond Derrida', so Derrida comes first.
- **Register** — *make*, *describe*: plain. One figure, 'a rich textured web', with two adjectives. A second scholar is named in an aside.
- **Our slot** — open on the field's account of the host, with its authors as subject, credited without flattery.

### M.002 · Intro ¶1 · p. 2 · ~38w
> 'While it is conventional to begin … objectifiable conceptual determination'.' Frame: *While it is conventional to …, X (year: page) warns us that '…', as it '…'*.

- **Move** — CANON-CLAIM by quotation, behind a concessive clause that declines the conventional opening (a definition).
- **Paragraph role** — second. Lets the figure speak, twice, with a page number.
- **Research question** — none.
- **Thesis** — indirect. The canon's refusal to define is what the article's first step will answer.
- **Register** — *begin*, *warns*, *rebels* (inside the quotation). 'warns us' brings the reader in.
- **Our slot** — the field's own strongest statement about the host, quoted with its page.

### M.003 · Intro ¶1 · p. 2 · ~29w
> 'Yet, despite this chronic ambiguity, … ethics itself (Derrida 2001: 16-7).' Frame: *Yet, despite …, we are offered X's … assurance that A is B; not just a B, but B itself*.

- **Move** — CANON-CLAIM, the second and central one, turned on 'Yet'. The semicolon delivers a climb: an ethics, then ethics itself.
- **Paragraph role** — third; the pivot. What comes after it is criticism.
- **Research question** — indirect. This is the claim whose implications the article says it will draw out.
- **Thesis** — the thing granted before the 'beyond'.
- **Register** — *offered*; 'chronic ambiguity' and 'apparently un-Derridean' are light evaluations. Passive with the agent named in the possessive.
- **Our slot** — the claim in the canon that our argument starts from: that the host owes the guest protection.

### M.004 · Intro ¶1 · p. 2 · ~20w
> 'While immensely provocative, this claim … the depth one might wish.' Frame: *While immensely …, this claim is not really unpacked nor its implications drawn out*.

- **Move** — LIMIT, the first. Praise in a concessive clause, then what was left undone, as two agentless passives.
- **Paragraph role** — fourth. Opens the gap.
- **Research question** — gives its ground: there is something not yet drawn out.
- **Thesis** — first statement of the 'limitation' the thesis moves past.
- **Register** — *unpacked*, *drawn out*: mildly figurative, ordinary in the genre. 'one might wish' keeps the criticism polite. Short after two long sentences.
- **Our slot** — what the field's account leaves undone: it does not say where the obligation sits when someone else does the host's work.

### M.005 · Intro ¶1 · p. 2 · ~37w
> 'Furthermore, Derrida links this notion … hospitality in a global context.' Frame: *Furthermore, X links A to B, a link which is potentially very limiting for how we …*.

- **Move** — LIMIT, the second and the one the article is built on. Named agent, active verb, then an appositive that passes judgement.
- **Paragraph role** — last. Ends the paragraph on the phrase that will recur ('sovereign mastery') and on the setting ('global').
- **Research question** — direct ground. The limit named here is what the driving question later attacks.
- **Thesis** — states the assumption that the thesis says is 'being disrupted'.
- **Register** — *links*; 'potentially very limiting' hedges and intensifies at once. Key term introduced plainly and never varied afterwards.
- **Our slot** — the assumption our argument leaves behind: that the host's obligation lives in the host's person or motive.

### M.006 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~31w
> 'This article seeks to reaffirm … the limitations imposed by Derrida.' Frame: *This article seeks to reaffirm …, but also to suggest a way in which we can push beyond the limitations imposed by X*.

- **Move** — AIM, in two parts joined by 'but also': keep the canon, go past it.
- **Paragraph role** — first of six. Turns from the canon to the article.
- **Research question** — the first statement of it, as an aim (`MODEL_BRIEF.md`, statement 1).
- **Thesis** — its programme in one sentence.
- **Register** — *seeks*, *reaffirm*, *suggest*, *push beyond*. The article is the subject; 'we' appears inside the second part.
- **Our slot** — our aim in the same two parts: keep the field's account of what a host owes, and say where that obligation goes under delegation.

### M.007 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~23w
> 'When we interrogate the implications … space within practices of hospitality.' Frame: *When we interrogate …, we reveal a constitutive link between A, B and C*.

- **Move** — ARGUMENT-AHEAD, the first step, as a when-clause and a finding. The three nouns are the article's title.
- **Paragraph role** — second. Begins a run of four sentences that compress the argument.
- **Research question** — answers it in advance, in outline.
- **Thesis** — first support.
- **Register** — *interrogate*, *reveal*. Inclusive 'we' twice. Abstract nouns throughout.
- **Our slot** — our first step in a sentence: read as a role, the obligation has a holder, a start and a content.

### M.008 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~35w
> 'As it operates to form relations … of the home is crossed.' Frame: *As it operates to form relations between A and B, …, those distinctions also need to be policed, managed and controlled, even after …*.

- **Move** — ARGUMENT-AHEAD, the second step. Three pairs of opposites, then a triplet of passive verbs, then the clause the whole article turns on ('even after the threshold').
- **Paragraph role** — third. Carries the article's central observation.
- **Research question** — this is the observation the driving question on p. 10 asks about.
- **Thesis** — core support: what happens after the threshold.
- **Register** — *operates*, *form*, *policed*, *managed*, *controlled*. Passive without agent, used for what must be done by whoever hosts.
- **Our slot** — our central observation: the work can be handed on and the duty cannot, so the obligation persists after delegation.

### M.009 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~15w
> 'Such management and administration … and its statist implications.' Frame: *Such … takes us beyond X and its … implications*.

- **Move** — ARGUMENT-AHEAD, the consequence, short. Picks up the last sentence with 'Such'.
- **Paragraph role** — fourth. A beat between two longer sentences.
- **Research question** — none directly.
- **Thesis** — names where the argument lands: 'beyond sovereign mastery'.
- **Register** — *takes us beyond*. Two nominalisations as subject.
- **Our slot** — the consequence for the guest: standing survives, and what can fail is its exercise.

### M.010 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~27w
> 'Furthermore, the acts of welcoming … (feelings of belonging and nonbelonging).' Frame: *Furthermore, the acts of … work to produce X itself, its A (gloss) and its B (gloss)*.

- **Move** — ARGUMENT-AHEAD, a further claim, with two technical terms each glossed in brackets.
- **Paragraph role** — fifth. Adds the constructive half of the argument.
- **Research question** — none directly.
- **Thesis** — support: hospitality produces the space it governs.
- **Register** — *work to produce*. Coinages ('spatiality', 'affectivity') glossed at once in plain words.
- **Our slot** — what we build from the argument: a way to examine a touchpoint, and a statement of when a person must be reachable.

### M.011 · Intro ¶2 · p. 2 · ~34w
> 'I argue that if we are to retain … his assumptions are being disrupted.' Frame: *I argue that if we are to retain X's aim of …, we must pay attention to the ways that his assumptions are being disrupted*.

- **Move** — THESIS, owned, in conditional form: if the aim is kept, the assumptions must be examined.
- **Paragraph role** — last. The paragraph has been building to it.
- **Research question** — answered in one sentence.
- **Thesis** — the thesis itself (`MODEL_BRIEF.md`).
- **Register** — *argue*, *retain*, *pay attention*, *disrupted*. First person singular for the claim; inclusive 'we' inside it. A page reference in mid-sentence.
- **Our slot** — 'We argue that …': the obligation stays with whoever took up the role, and delegation removes the guest's path to anyone with authority to answer for it.

### M.012 · Intro ¶3 · p. 2 · ~33w
> 'The article begins by exploring … spatial and affective relational practices.' Frame: *The article begins by exploring what it means to say that …, arguing that …*.

- **Move** — ROADMAP, first section, with the section's claim attached in a participle.
- **Paragraph role** — first of eight. Starts the walk through the sections.
- **Research question** — none.
- **Thesis** — assigns the first step to a section.
- **Register** — *begins*, *exploring*, *arguing*. The article is the subject.
- **Our slot** — 'The article begins by …': what our first section sets out and argues.

### M.013 · Intro ¶3 · p. 2 · ~32w
> 'The second section outlines … (re)produce its affective dimensions.' Frame: *The second section outlines the implications of this claim for …, especially those that …*.

- **Move** — ROADMAP, second section, tied to the first by 'this claim'.
- **Paragraph role** — second.
- **Research question** — none.
- **Thesis** — assigns the second step.
- **Register** — *outlines*, *manage*, *(re)produce*. A bracketed prefix, one of his habits.
- **Our slot** — 'The second section …': delegation and the guest's standing.

### M.014 · Intro ¶3 · pp. 2–3 · ~25w
> 'The third section examines … focusing on refugee camps.' Frame: *The third section examines what an investigation of … might look like, focusing on X*.

- **Move** — ROADMAP, third section, naming the case at the very end.
- **Paragraph role** — third.
- **Research question** — none.
- **Thesis** — assigns the showing.
- **Register** — *examines*, *focusing*. 'might look like' is modest about what the section delivers.
- **Our slot** — 'The third section …': the touchpoint, across formats.

### M.015 · Intro ¶3 · p. 3 · ~43w
> 'These are non-state, non-sovereign, … an agent of international ethics.' Frame: *These are … spaces that are produced through …, thereby disrupting the assumptions of X and Y regarding who/what can be a host, a home and an agent*.

- **Move** — CASE-SCOPE in advance: why this case, in three hyphenated adjectives and a participle that says what the case does to the assumptions.
- **Paragraph role** — fourth. The longest sentence of the introduction; it justifies the case before the case appears.
- **Research question** — sharpens it: who or what can be a host.
- **Thesis** — support: the case disrupts the assumptions the thesis names.
- **Register** — *produced*, *disrupting*. A slash ('who/what'). A closing triplet in which each member is distinct.
- **Our slot** — why touchpoints in hotels, rentals and restaurants are the right setting: each hands a host's job to a system, and each has more than one firm behind it.

### M.016 · Intro ¶3 · p. 3 · ~31w
> 'I am suggesting here that … hospitality's 'home' to the state.' Frame: *I am suggesting here that X's focus on … tends to reinforce the instinct in [field] to restrict …*.

- **Move** — THESIS restated as diagnosis, hedged ('suggesting', 'tends to').
- **Paragraph role** — fifth. Returns from the roadmap to the claim.
- **Research question** — none new.
- **Thesis** — the second statement, aimed at the field as well as the figure.
- **Register** — *suggesting*, *reinforce*, *restrict*. First person, progressive tense; 'here' marks the move as local.
- **Our slot** — our diagnosis of the field: by looking for the obligation in the host's motive, the debate about commercial hospitality cannot say where it goes when no person hosts.

### M.017 · Intro ¶3 · p. 3 · ~27w
> 'Moving beyond sovereign mastery … the safety accorded to guests.' Frame: *Moving beyond X reveals the spaces, such as …, that actually do much of the …*.

- **Move** — ARGUMENT-AHEAD, the payoff of the second step: what becomes visible.
- **Paragraph role** — sixth.
- **Research question** — indicates the answer's direction.
- **Thesis** — support by promised result.
- **Register** — *reveals*, *do*; 'actually', his one use. A gerund phrase as subject. 'such as' attaches the instance.
- **Our slot** — what the role account lets us see: the parties, such as operators, brands and vendors, that now do much of the host's work.

### M.018 · Intro ¶3 · p. 3 · ~32w
> 'As such, I argue that … the call for 'Hospitality Now!'' Frame: *As such, I argue that X must become the centre of our analysis if we are to think through who, or what, is …*.

- **Move** — THESIS, third statement, as what analysis must now do. Conditional again.
- **Paragraph role** — seventh. The paragraph's last full sentence.
- **Research question** — second statement of it (`MODEL_BRIEF.md`, statement 2).
- **Thesis** — its methodological form.
- **Register** — *argue*, *become*, *think through*, *heeding*. A hyphenated coinage carries the claim. A quoted slogan ends it.
- **Our slot** — what the study of phygital hospitality must attend to if it is to say who answers to the guest.

### M.019 · Intro ¶3 · p. 3 · ~8w
> 'And, indeed, how they might do so better.' Frame: *And, indeed, how they might … better*.

- **Move** — a tail, as a fragment: the normative interest, held back to the last word.
- **Paragraph role** — last. Ends the introduction on 'better', which the conclusion takes up.
- **Research question** — adds its normative half.
- **Thesis** — opens the loop the 'wager' closes on p. 18.
- **Register** — a sentence fragment beginning 'And'. Plain words. The only fragment in the introduction.
- **Our slot** — the practical interest, briefly: what an organization would have to keep. A full sentence, not a fragment; Reviewer 3 objected to rhetorical effects.

## 'International hospitality and ethics' · 11 paragraphs · pp. 3–8

- **Research question** — none.
- **Thesis** — none.
- **Register** — *know*, *engage*. Inclusive 'we' twice. Bare 'It' with its antecedent one sentence back.
