What was read and what was not: the model's body, notes and reference list were read end to end. The version of record was not available, so italics, heading typography and journal page numbers are unknown.

## The research question, at every place it is stated

1. **As an aim** (p. 2, introduction ¶2): 'This article seeks to reaffirm the importance of hospitality … but also to suggest a way in which we can push beyond the limitations imposed by Derrida.'
2. **As a need** (p. 3, introduction ¶3): 'a micro-ethico-politics of hospitality must become the centre of our analysis if we are to think through who, or what, is heeding the call'.
3. **As the driving question** (p. 10, second section ¶5): 'But what becomes of the power of hospitality once this threshold is crossed?', followed by four more questions.
4. **As the case question** (p. 15, end of the second subsection): 'what specific identities and forms of agency are produced and enabled by the governmentalised hospitality offered within refugee camps'.

It is never put as a single interrogative research question in the introduction. The question proper arrives halfway through, after the reader has been given what is needed to feel it.

## The thesis, and its shape

- **Claim** (p. 2): 'I argue that if we are to retain Derrida's aim of transforming and improving the practice of international hospitality …, we must pay attention to the ways that his assumptions are being disrupted.'
- **Shape: with a named account, then beyond it, twice, then a setting.**
  1. Grant the canonical account and quote it at its strongest.
  2. Name two limits: it does not separate hospitality from other responsibility-taking, and it ties hospitality's power to the threshold and to sovereign mastery.
  3. First step beyond: say what concrete hospitality is (spatial, relational, affective).
  4. Second step beyond: follow power past the threshold, with a second theorist's vocabulary.
  5. Show both steps in refugee camps.
  6. Close on a tentative 'better'.
- The thesis is conditional in form ('if we are to …, we must …') and is stated three times before the first heading.

## Contribution moves, in order

1. Reaffirm the canon's importance for the paper's field (p. 2).
2. Determine the concept: a definition by necessary feature and by five things it is not (pp. 5–6).
3. Dispute a named scholar (Baker) in the text and again in a note (p. 6).
4. Carry a concept from another field (critical geography of home) to make the concept multi-scalar (pp. 7–8).
5. Expose the canon's limit on power through its own sources (Benveniste; the 'mastery' passages) (pp. 8–10).
6. Import a second theorist's triangle (Foucault) to see what the canon cannot (p. 14).
7. Read other people's ethnography through it (Hyndman; Agier) (pp. 11–17).
8. State a tentative normative conclusion as a 'wager' (p. 18).

## Register

Six things, from reading. `MODEL.md` collects the evidence sentence by sentence.

1. **Verbs.** warns, rebels, offered, unpacked, links, seeks, reaffirm, suggest, push, interrogate, reveal, policed, managed, controlled, produce, retain, outlines, examines, restrict, overlook, ignore, favouring, describes, constitutes, holds back, negotiate, tame, ward off, delimit, regulating, filtering, cordoning, traces, exposes, pursue, venture. Mostly literal verbs of saying, showing and controlling. A minority reach a little (tame, ward off, cordon off, venture, push beyond). None is extended.
2. **Metaphors.** Few and dead or near-dead: a 'web of paradoxes', a 'burning issue' (quoted), spaces that 'take on a life of their own', 'my wager'. No anecdote, no scene-setting story, no joke.
3. **The key terms are repeated, not varied.** 'Hospitality' appears in almost every sentence of the first two sections. 'Sovereign mastery', 'threshold', 'home', 'host' and 'guest' recur unchanged. He does not reach for synonyms.
4. **Abstraction.** High in the first two sections (nominal chains: 'the management and administration of hospitality'), low in the case (fenced lots, video shops, football matches). Each abstract stretch is anchored by a quotation or a small case within a paragraph or two.
5. **Naming.** He coins by hyphen ('micro-ethico-politics', 'ethico-spatial', 'being-at-home-with-oneself') and puts contested words in single quotation marks ('home', 'better', 'guests').
6. **What it does not do.** No aphorism that stands alone as a paragraph. No sentence that grades his own argument. 'Of course' once, 'actually' once and 'Indeed' seven times, as plain adverbs. No address to the reader as 'you' except inside the contrast between 'your space' and 'my space'.

## Precedence, as applied here

1. A trait the model and the venue share is followed.
2. A trait only the model has is learned and not required.
3. Where the house style (`~/.claude/writing-style.md`) and a genre rule collide, the genre rule is followed and logged (`DECISIONS.md`, D4).
4. Where a reviewer asked for something the genre does not do (a test explained; boundary conditions; a roadmap), the reviewer wins, and the form is taken from the genre as far as it goes.
