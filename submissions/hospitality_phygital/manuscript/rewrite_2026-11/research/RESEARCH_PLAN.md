## Method

- **Journal screen (Q2).** OpenAlex, source `S4210199845`, all 282 works, retrieved 10 Nov 2026. Titles and abstracts searched for three families of terms: obligation (obligat-, duty, owe, accountab-, answerab-, liabil-, innkeep-), technology (technolog-, digital, platform, algorithm, robot, automat-, AI, online, virtual, app, Airbnb, CouchSurf-, network hospitality, self-service, sharing economy, internet, social media, website) and voice (complain-, negotiat-, recourse, redress, appeal). 21 records have no abstract in OpenAlex and were screened on title alone. The table is `hs_screen.tsv` (no abstracts stored).
- **Reading.** Every source that v6 relies on is read in full, or cited expressly through the text that reports it. An abstract supports no claim beyond the abstract.
- **Cards.** New sources get a card in `library/cards/` in the existing format, with a locator on every quotation. I write each card from my own full read; one independent verifier then checks each card against the source text before it is indexed.

## What happens if the sign-in does not come

v6 is written so that it does not depend on the closed texts:

- a closed article is described only as far as its abstract goes, and the sentence says so in the ledger;
- a canonical source is cited through the journal article that discusses it ('as Bulley reads Derrida');
- a claim that only a closed source could carry is left out, and the outline records the refusal.

Each such place is flagged in `sbs/LEDGER.md`, so the flags can be cleared in one pass when the texts arrive.
