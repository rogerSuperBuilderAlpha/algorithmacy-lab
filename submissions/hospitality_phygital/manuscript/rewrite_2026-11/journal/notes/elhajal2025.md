# El Hajal (2025), 'Rethinking hospitality and tourism education: Beyond tradition and transformation'

*Hospitality & Society* 15:3, 295–306, doi 10.1386/hosp_00099_1. Short essay, one author. **Read in full**: accepted manuscript, University of Groningen repository (body about 3,000 words). Received March 2025, accepted July 2025.

## What it asks and argues

- **Aim, with the paper as subject:** 'This paper critically explores the tensions between tradition and transformation' in hospitality and tourism education (MS p. 2).
- **Thesis:** education should adapt to AI and industry change without over-aligning with industry, and should be treated as a public good.
- **Shape:** dialogic. A book launch webinar supplies five questions; each section answers one from the literature and the editors' remarks.

## Skeleton

Introduction (17%), which ends by listing the five questions as a roadmap; five sections headed by the questions ('Q1: …' to 'Q5: …'), 12–16% each; 'Concluding remarks: beyond tradition and transformation' (10%). No tables or figures. Each question section ends on a paragraph that answers it.

## What it shows about the journal

- **A second short piece that differs from the full-length plan.** Like Baah et al. (2023), it is short and organises itself differently: question headings rather than an argument's headings. The rulings in `JOURNAL_MODEL.md` remain those of the full-length articles.
- **An AI declaration.** Before the references, under the heading 'Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process', the author states that GPT-4 was used 'to enhance language, clarity and brevity' and that the author reviewed and edited the result and takes full responsibility (MS p. 9). It is the one AI declaration in the sample. It is in the accepted manuscript; whether the version of record prints it under that heading is not checked (the version of record is closed). It bears on the authors' AI acknowledgment (`DECISIONS.md`, conflict 1): this author declared language editing only, the use the journal's policy allows.
- **Self-description of method.** One sentence grades its own approach ('avoiding an over-reliance on description or anecdote', MS p. 3). It is the only such sentence in the sample, in a short piece; ruling 8 stands.

## What it says that bears on our paper

Little. It names AI's 'values of efficiency and control' as a possible challenge to 'hospitality's relational core' (MS p. 5), at the level of assertion. v6 does not cite it.

## Register

'This paper' as agent throughout; 'we' only inside the questions. Named contributors as subjects ('Marcela Fang notes', 'Tracy Harkison emphasises'). Many intensifiers and doublets (critical, profound, urgent; 'not just … but'), more than any regular article in the sample.
