# Moysidou and Stanley (2023), 'Theorizing gender in homestay settings: Mobilities and/as power relations'

*Hospitality & Society* 13 (the manuscript gives no issue or pages). Regular article, two authors. **Read in full**: accepted manuscript, Edinburgh Napier repository (17 pages of text). It is the most recent regular article in the sample, and it calls itself 'theoretical in nature' (p. 2) while reporting interview data.

## What it asks and argues

- **Aim, in the second paragraph.** 'this article therefore aims to broaden thinking about gender within critical hospitality studies and to cross-pollinate between disciplines' (p. 2).
- **Thesis.** Hosts do not hold all the power. A guest's power in a homestay follows from the guest's ability to move: 'mobilities seem to operate as proxy for power' (p. 15).
- **Shape of the thesis: a received assumption rejected, then replaced by a two-by-two scheme.** 'we reject a model that assumes, a priori, that hosts are powerful and guests are powerless' (p. 15). The replacement sorts what enables and constrains a guest into tangible and intangible, and is drawn as two figures.

## Introduction, move by move

1. **The gap in the field, with a named critic**: 'Until recently, gender has been defined and problematized rather narrowly within hospitality' (p. 1).
2. **Aim**, and the disciplines borrowed from.
3. **The material**, and the paper's status: 'The present article is therefore theoretical in nature, as our aim is to deepen the theorizing' (p. 2).
4. **Two framing ideas**, each tied to a named scholar.
5. **Roadmap**: 'The article is structured as follows. First, we locate the study … We then give a brief account … before the article wraps up with notes on where these ideas might go next' (p. 3).
6. **A paragraph on the authors themselves** and how that shapes their reading (p. 3).

## How it handles other people's work

- **Lashley's three domains are the starting map**, and are qualified at once: 'Lashley's domains are not mutually exclusive' (p. 4).
- **Derrida supplies the received view of the host's power**: welcoming 'denotes sovereignty over the space and a level of power over the visitor' (pp. 4–5, citing Caputo).
- **Scholars are subjects when they supply a tool**: 'Pennycook proposes problematizing practice as a nimbler model' (p. 5).
- **The journal's own writers appear**: Germann Molz (2012), Lashley and Lynch (2013), Lynch (2005).

## Passages that bear on our argument

- A guest's sense that a complaint will be heard is treated as power: 'Knowing how to complain, and feeling that the complaint will be acted upon, are latent forms of power' (p. 12).
- A guest who cannot leave does not complain. An au pair says 'I have to be here, I cannot leave' (p. 7), and the authors read this as immobility producing silence (p. 13).
- The settings studied are marked by 'A lack of formal contract, ethical accountability and clear communication' (p. 5).

## Conclusion, move by move

1. 'This paper has theorized …', with the disciplines drawn on (p. 15).
2. The claim in compact form: gender is 'something that one does' (p. 15).
3. **Scope**: 'the generalizability here is conceptual rather than inter/intra-contextual' (p. 16).
4. **Limitations, announced as such**: 'This paper is not without its limitations' (p. 16).
5. **Practice, announced as such**: 'In terms of practical implications, the findings may be of use to intermediary organisations' (p. 16).
6. **Research, opened with a question**: 'Where might the conversation go from here?' (p. 16), then 'Alternatively, scholars might turn to …' (p. 17).

Each of these is one paragraph of prose inside the single Conclusion. None has a sub-heading.

## Constructions

- Questions appear at the turn to future research and inside quoted material.
- Slashed and bracketed compounds are frequent: 'and/as', 'im/mobilities', 'que(e)ries'.
- **What it never does:** tables, propositions, numbered contributions.

## What this text shows about the journal

- By 2023 a regular article states its limitations, its practical implications and its research agenda in so many words, each in its own paragraph of the Conclusion.
- A drawn scheme is acceptable as the paper's contribution, as a figure.
- The introduction carries a full roadmap.
