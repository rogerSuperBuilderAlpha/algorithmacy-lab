# Germann Molz (2018), 'Discourses of scale in network hospitality: From the Airbnb home to the global imaginary of "belong anywhere"'

*Hospitality & Society* 8:3, pp. 229–251. Regular article, single author. **Read in full: version of record** (the PDF you supplied). This is the only regular article in the sample read as typeset.

## What it asks and argues

- **Focus, stated at the end of the introduction.** 'My focus in this article is on discourses of scale in the context of network hospitality, and specifically Airbnb' (p. 231). 'I explore how vocabularies and metaphors of scale are deployed to support certain, often competing, claims to belonging, to citizenship and to power' (p. 231).
- **Thesis.** Scale is not a neutral container. Companies, cities and residents invoke it to claim belonging and to move responsibility. The compact form: 'scale is a power play' (p. 234).
- **Shape of the thesis: one concept used as a lens on one case.** A concept from geography is defended against its critics, then walked across the case one scale at a time. She states the status of the lens herself: 'a kind of heuristic device' (p. 234).

## Introduction, move by move

1. **Two scenes, set side by side.** An Airbnb television advertisement, quoted in a display block; then a protest video from Barcelona (p. 230).
2. **Why the scenes are there, said outright.** 'I use these two videos to introduce this article because they show two sides of the same coin' (p. 230).
3. **From the scenes to the concept.** 'Both videos also hint at a discourse of scale' (p. 230).
4. **Focus and reach.** The focus sentence quoted above, with a claim that the approach travels beyond Airbnb.
5. **Roadmap by section.** 'The article begins by establishing the theoretical groundwork … After outlining some methodological considerations, it then examines … This analysis starts by exploring … before turning to consider …' (p. 231).

## How it handles other people's work

- **Each key concept gets its own subsection and its own lineage.** Her own term comes first, defined by restatement: 'In other words, these are the websites that facilitate …' (p. 231). Then its features in a run: 'It revolves around … It creates … Finally, it opens up …' (pp. 231–2).
- **The journal's own literature anchors the concept**: Lynch et al. (2011) is quoted, and Bell's 'hospitality is society' (2012) is named as the inspiration (p. 231).
- **Scholars are named with a first name and a trade**: 'brand consultant Rachel Botsman', 'anthropologist Anna Tsing', 'geographer Eugene McCann', 'Legal scholar Sara Light'.
- **She takes sides in the first person**: 'I think it is worth holding on to the term "sharing". I am very wary of …' (p. 233); 'I am not convinced that the notion of scale has become irrelevant' (p. 234); 'I sympathize with Marston and her co-authors' wariness' (p. 234).

## How it builds the argument

- **The same small job is done at the end of each subsection**: a paragraph that says what the scale showed. 'Leveled at the intimate scale of the home, scholarly research uncovers … It reveals … It also illustrates …' (p. 237).
- **Evidence is quoted material**: advertising copy, company reports with their own statistics, city action plans, newspaper reporting, other scholars' studies.
- **The limits of the evidence are stated in the method section, not at the end**: 'Admittedly, some of the questions I raise in this analysis are quite speculative'; the examples are 'meant to be illustrative, not comprehensive or definitive' (p. 235).
- **Signposts run through the body, in the first person**: 'We will start our journey at the scale of the home' (p. 235); 'Later, I will come back to this issue … But first, I want to finish …' (p. 241); 'As I noted earlier' (twice).

## Conclusion, move by move

1. 'In this article, I have offered a few ideas for …', then 'We have seen how …' (p. 245).
2. A disclaimer about what the lens is not: 'My intention with these stories of scale is not to reproduce scale as an ontological category' (p. 245).
3. Two paragraphs of claim, in long lists of who gains and who pays.
4. A turn to the field: 'it is our obligation as scholars to be equally attuned' (p. 246).
5. **Four directions for research, counted in prose**: 'For one thing … Another important avenue of research … Third … Finally …' (pp. 246–7). The second is legal: whether platforms 'are held accountable' (p. 246).

There is no limitations paragraph in the conclusion and no heading for implications.

## Register

- **Verbs (thirty):** launched, rolled out, strolls, promotes, protests, conjures, hint, spans, stitches, deployed, casts, facilitate, populate, revolves, usher in, combat, champion, delimits, expose, denaturalize, invoked, glide, tackle, exacerbates, pitched, corroborates, peddles, outsources, deflected, belies. Lively and often figurative.
- **Idiom is welcome.** 'geographers were at it again', 'no matter how you cut it' (p. 234), 'ticked all of the sharing economy boxes', 'At first blush' (p. 232), '(1400 of which are castles, apparently)' (p. 235), 'I think we should watch this space' (p. 246). One exclamation mark (p. 241).
- **Sentence fragments, for effect.** 'One billion guests in three million homes in 65,000 cities in 191 countries. Online and worldwide.' (p. 235). 'Difficult, but not impossible.' (p. 246).
- **The idiom describes the world; it never grades her own argument.** There is no sentence that calls her own answer obvious, boring or sharp.
- **Key terms are held fixed.** 'Scale' and 'network hospitality' recur unchanged throughout.
- **First person marks moves and stance**, about 7 per 1,000 words. An inclusive 'we' joins reader and author: 'We see how …'.

## Constructions

- **Questions are frequent and do work.** 'Who shares what with whom, when, where and for how long?' (p. 233). 'What do they owe each other?', of hosts and guests (p. 236). A run of three closes the section on temporal scales (p. 243).
- **'In other words'** welds a restatement onto a definition.
- **Lists inside sentences** run long: five to seven items.
- **What it never does:** tables, numbered propositions, a contributions list, a managerial implications section.
