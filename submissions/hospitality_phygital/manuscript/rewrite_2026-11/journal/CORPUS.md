# The journal sample

## How texts were chosen

- **Population:** the 282 works OpenAlex lists for *Hospitality & Society* (source `S4210199845`), retrieved 10 Nov 2026. 224 are closed, 58 have an open copy.
- **What counts:** conceptual or theoretical articles in the journal itself, plus the editorials in which the editors say what they want. Empirical articles are used only for how they frame theory, open and close.
- **Order of reading:** texts nearest our paper in kind first (conceptual, about hosts, power or technology), then the most recent.

## What this means for the model

`JOURNAL_MODEL.md` rests on **eight regular articles and two editorials read in full**, four of the ten from 2022 or later. The plan's target was twelve, with three on technology or commercial hosting; three of the seven meet that description (Bialski 2012; Germann Molz 2018; Varley et al. 2018). The gap is the closed list above. Also obtained on 10 Nov 2026: the open-access LNCS chapter of Baum and Laux (2026), outside the journal.
