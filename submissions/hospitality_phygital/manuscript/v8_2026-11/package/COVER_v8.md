## What v8 is

v8 keeps v7's argument and every finding of its research, and changes three things.

- **It sits in phygital hospitality research.** The theoretical context opens on Batat's definition of the phygital, the guest's movement between physical and digital places, AI as a constitutive mediator, and this journal's network hospitality. A new section walks the phygital arrangement party by party.
- **Pierre's sentences return** wherever they survive the claim audit and the reviewers, tagged in the Appendix.
- **It is written to the model of Germann Molz (2018)** in this journal: two contrasting scenes, the thesis, a roadmap, plain topic sentences, and closing paragraphs that hand on to the next section.

Part 5 gives the counts, what was checked, and the decisions in full. The placeholders marked [AUTHORS] or [AUTHORS DECIDE] in the letters are those decisions, each at the point where it matters.
