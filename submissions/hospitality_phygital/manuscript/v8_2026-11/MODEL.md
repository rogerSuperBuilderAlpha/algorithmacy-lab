Version of record, `paper.pdf` in this folder; transcribed from the text layer and checked against the page images.

Section codes: IN Introduction · TN, TS, TG Theoretical context (Network hospitality; The sharing economy and collaborative consumption; Geographies of scale) · ME Methodological considerations · SP Spatial scales (lead-in) · SH, SN, SC, SA, SY Spatial subsections (home, neighbourhood, city, nation, anywhere) · TE Temporal scales · DI Digital scales · CO Conclusion.

Paragraphs are numbered from the typeset first-line indents (pp. 230–247). A displayed quotation is one entry marked DISPLAY: it is someone else's prose, and her move is the display, not its sentences. Word counts are by eye.

Fields: **Move** (the job and the frame that performs it) · **Role** (place in the paragraph) · **RQ / Thesis** (what it gives each; 'none' is a judgment) · **Register** (named words) · **Slot** (what v8's answering sentence must do; Stage 4 may overrule).
