## How to read an entry

Each paragraph has a header — its model counterpart, its job, its target length — and notes on **register** (what the counterpart's diction asks of ours) and **watch** (what must be checked in Stage 5). Each row is one planned sentence:

- **O-id** — continuous; ids never move.
- **Answers** — the model entry (GM.nnn) whose job it does, or `—` with a warrant.
- **Move · role** — the job, and its place in the paragraph.
- **Owes** — what it gives the research question (Q) or the thesis (T); `—` for neither.
- **Support** — where the claim comes from: a v7 sentence (`S-nnn`, from `keystone_2026-11/sbs/assembly/final.json`, its Truth verdict carried), a Pierre sentence (`P4.41`, outcome per `PIERRE_LEDGER.md`; `[PB]` verbatim, `[PB-a]` adapted), a card (`card:key p. n`), or `new` with its source. A claim with no support is refused here, not in Stage 5.
- **w** — target words, guidance for shape.

Budgets (V8-D9): INTRODUCTION 700 · THEORETICAL CONTEXT 1,850 · APPROACH 450 · THE PARTIES 2,150 · DURING THE STAY 1,150 · AT THE TOUCHPOINT 1,650 · CONCLUSION 1,050. The row targets are held below 9,000 in total because v7's ledger estimates ran low against the prose; in Stage 5 a section that runs over its budget is cut at the paragraph level before assembly, and rows cut at the outline stay listed with the reason, so no id moves.
