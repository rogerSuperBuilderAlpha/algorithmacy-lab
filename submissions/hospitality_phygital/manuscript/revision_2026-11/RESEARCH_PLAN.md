## Verification protocol

1. **Hit → record.** Every candidate gets a Crossref or OpenAlex record (DOI, year, volume, pages)
   before it is written down anywhere outside a scratch file. No record, no lead.
2. **Record → full text.** Obtain the version of record. If only the abstract is reachable, the
   card says `read_depth: abstract` and no mechanism, finding or quotation is attributed to it.
3. **Full text → card.** `reading-papers` produces one card in `library/cards/` with frontmatter
   matching the existing schema, and a page or section locator for every claim the paper might use.
4. **Card → claim.** Each sentence drafted from the source carries its locator in a comment until the
   final pass. Novelty sentences use the form "we found no work that does X in [databases, dates]".
5. **Verifier pass.** A second agent re-opens the PDF and checks each locator against the quoted or
   paraphrased text; mismatches are flagged, not fixed silently.
6. **Hand-check.** Roger or Pierre reads every locator that supports a load-bearing claim in §2 or §3.
7. **Pre-submission.** `auditing-citations` on the manuscript and `references.bib`; the card index
   must pass `build_index.py --check`.

Subagent guards: no citing from memory; locators on every claim; mark abstract-only; return cards,
not reports; name UNVERIFIED leads as such.
