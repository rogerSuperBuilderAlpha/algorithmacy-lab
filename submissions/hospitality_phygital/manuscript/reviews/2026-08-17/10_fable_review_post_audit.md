## 1. The verdict

**Minor revisions — but one of them is not optional, and it is arithmetic.** The argument is sound, the venue fit is genuine, the construct is defended against its neighbours, and the audited repairs have made the paper more honest without making it weaker. What stands between this manuscript and submission is the word count. The body runs 8,987 words (including abstract, keywords, tables, and captions) and the 123 reference entries run a further 3,400. With notes and biographies still to come, the paper lands near **12,550 against a 9,000-word inclusive ceiling**. No referee reads an over-length paper kindly, and no editor sends one to referees at all. The single most important thing to fix is therefore the cut, and the single most important fact about the cut is that **it cannot come from the body alone**: the reference apparatus must lose roughly 45–55 entries, and Sections 7 and 8 must lose roughly 700 words between them.

## 2. The reasoning, section by section

**§1 Introduction — holds.** The vignette earns its place, and the paper's smartest move happens here: the deliberate deflation of "does the machine inherit the obligations?" ("Put that way, the answer is boring") into the practical question of whether the obligation stays *findable*. That reframing is what makes the paper publishable in a critical journal without a philosophy-of-mind detour, and it is fully earned. The positioning against Germann Molz (2026) is exactly the sentence this special issue's editor wants to see. The new paragraph engaging Lynch et al. (2011) is well placed, though its middle sentence is compressed to the point of opacity for a reader who has not read the founding statement.

**§2 From service recipient to guest — holds, and does the paper's hardest defensive work.** The paragraph distinguishing standing from consumer rights, procedural justice, contestability, and customer participation is the construct-collision defence a referee will demand, and it is dispatched in one paragraph with a clean payoff. One gap: the machinery of §§6–7 — voice, silence, and "a real alternative" as the price of declining — is Hirschman's exit-voice apparatus in all but name, and Hirschman is nowhere cited. One citation and a subordinate clause (~25 words) forestalls the objection. The rebuilt Pijls passage is a genuine improvement: it turns an audit correction into an argument.

**§3 The missing counterparty — holds.** Short, and the chain/break image is the paper's central figure, stated once and stated well.

**§4 Redistribution — mostly holds; two soft spots.** The arrangement-not-gadget framing, the discretion/authority distinction, the Christin repair, and the evidence-unevenness paragraph are all earned.

The rebuilt uniqueness claim is the section's most exposed step. Its first half — a welcome is "the only one of the four triggered by bare presence" — is defensible, though a referee steeped in commercial hospitality will note that what makes an arrival a *guest* rather than a trespasser is a prior booking. Its second half is weaker: a trustee who resigns and appoints a successor also stops being trustee, so fiduciary ownership is not obviously more separable from its role than hosting is, and "the only one" invites the counterexample. **Fix:** drop "also the only one" and keep the claim as a property of hosting rather than a comparison. Nothing downstream depends on the uniqueness; everything downstream depends on the non-delegability.

Second soft spot: the paragraph on language habits makes a real point, but it is a 120-word essay about prose inside a paper that must lose a third of itself. And the Möhlmann sentence closing the craft paragraph — "Neither turns into the other" — is a stub.

**§5 Conditions of hospitable mediation — holds.** The pattern-not-threshold move and the asymmetry result ("Accountability cannot be bought back with the other two") are the section's load-bearing steps and both are earned. The transparency inversion is the paper's most quotable analytic result. The Garcia repair is intellectually exactly right, though the sentence carrying it needs surgery. One both-ways sentence worth cleaning: the three conditions are introduced "not as imports from the study of organizational coordination but as what the host-guest relationship itself requires (Okhuysen and Bechky 2009)" — a citation to the import source inside a denial of importing.

**§6 Competence is not standing — the hinge, and it holds.** The Zhou analysis is the best piece of close reading in the paper. That is a conceptual paper doing empirical work, and it is the passage most worth protecting through revision — though its prose needs compression, because the same distinction is currently stated three times in four sentences. One arithmetic slip a referee will enjoy: "The distinction can be put in five words: competence is learned, standing is conferred" — that is six words.

**§7 Designing for interruptible hospitality — holds, but at 2,077 words it is a quarter of the body and carries most of the excess.** The interface/constitutional split is the section's real contribution and it is earned twice over. The seamlessness critique is properly scoped. The Fink citation — unpublished, cited twice, with an in-text apology — should simply go. The roadmap paragraph and Table 2 duplicate each other, as do the six-questions paragraph and Table 1 in §5.

**§8 Discussion — sound in content, out of voice in form, and the site of the biggest available cut.** The second contribution's closer repeats §6 nearly verbatim, and the well-being paragraph is 200 words of explicitly deferred theorizing that gates in eight references. Honest, but a paper 3,600 words over cannot afford a paragraph whose own last sentence disclaims it.

**§9 Conclusion — holds.** Tight, on voice, and the closer earns the title.

## 3. The prose, measured

- **Whole body:** 441 sentences, mean 19.8, median 17, 30% under 12 words, zero em-dashes. The manuscript as a whole is the second author's hand.
- **Sections out of voice:** §3 (mean 24.1) and above all **§8 (mean 27.1, median 25.5)** — the Discussion reads as a different, denser hand throughout.
- **The repairs split into two groups.** Reading as the same hand: Christin (mean 13.8), Pijls (13.2), the bare-presence passage (12.7 — the shortest-breathed prose in the paper), Martin/Waldman (15.2), the Lin split, and the §1 Lynch paragraph (15.0). **Not passing:** the **Zhou hinge** (six sentences, mean 25.2, *zero* under 12 — the only measured stretch in the paper with no short sentence at all); the **Garcia repair**; the **Spektor qualification**; the **Manfreda repair**; and the **Lind repair**, which carries two "rather than" contrasts within three sentences.
- **The contrast habit:** ten instances of "rather than," seven of them in the audited repairs and §8 — the new prose leans on exactly the construction the settled voice avoids.

The audit's *content* was integrated well; its *sentences* were integrated unevenly, and the fix is mechanical.

## 5. Passages that do not work

**(a) §5, the Garcia concession — right move, broken sentence.** Forty-nine words, one semicolon, three clauses fighting. Replace with:

> "The study's own authors read that result the other way: for them it argues for automating the adjustment out of the process. The mechanism is not in dispute. What is in dispute is whether a rule nobody at the desk can state is an inefficiency to engineer away or an accountability gap to close."

**(b) §6, the Zhou passage — one distinction stated three times.** Replacement for the stretch:

> "It is perfectly reasonable for a measure of worker competence to capture the worker's ability to launch one. What no such scale can capture is whether anyone is obliged to consider it. Filing an appeal is an action you can learn to perform. Being heard and answered depends on an institution agreeing to recognize it."

Saves ~45 words and restores the voice's rhythm. Also fix the count in the next paragraph: "five words" is six.

**(c) §2, the Manfreda repair's 44-word opener.** Split it:

> "A recent study of luxury lodges watches guests tidy their rooms for housekeeping, return glasses to the bar, and praise staff in their reviews. It frames all of this as altruism, generosity, something close to kinship, not as obligation (Manfreda and Harkison 2025)."

**(d) §4, the Möhlmann stub.** I cannot reconstruct what "Neither turns into the other" asserts or what it adds, and a referee will not be able to either. Cut both sentences; the paragraph ends better on Fuller and Smith.

**(e) §8, the human-centricity sentence — collapses as punctuated.** The zero-em-dash voice needs a colon here:

> "Human-centricity, on this reading, is about what the arrangement protects or withholds: guest agency, employee discretion, relational judgment. Keeping humans visible in the encounter is neither necessary nor sufficient for any of the three."

**(f) §7, the Lind repair's doubled "rather than."** Replace with "That comes with a boundary, and it is one to state, not hide" and "the whole argument for a real counterparty, not the appearance of one."

**(g) §7, the Fink apparatus.** An in-text apology for an incomplete reference. Cut Fink from both citation sites.

**(h) §8, the near-verbatim repeat.** The second contribution reprises §6 word for word. Cut it from §8.

## 7. What a referee at this journal will say

**Objection 1: "Standing is procedural justice, or contestability, wearing a new name."** Answered, and answered in the right place. The one adjacent construct not named is Hirschman's voice/exit, which the paper's own machinery makes conspicuous by absence. One citation closes it.

**Objection 2: "This is a service-management paper visiting a critical hospitality journal."** The citation base leans on service and management outlets. The paper's defence is structural and strong — it opens and closes inside this journal's own conversation. **The reference cut should protect the hospitality-studies spine and take its casualties from the service-marketing stacks.**

**Objection 3: anonymity and self-citation.** Under double-anonymized review, four Mosca-authored entries and four De Vos-cluster entries will read as an authorship signature, and one (Mosca et al. 2026b) is load-bearing. Keep the load-bearing ones, cut the generic ones, and check the anonymization pass treats the survivors neutrally.

**Objection 4: "You read Garcia et al. against its authors."** Anticipated and answered — the concession must survive the cut, in the repaired three-sentence form.

## 8. VERIFY — flagged, not asserted

1. **Reference count: 123 entries, not the 124 previously stated.** Recount before submission so the cover letter and the list agree.
2. **The reference list heading is duplicated.** A mechanical artefact of the audit merge; delete one.

4. **Direct quotes without page numbers.** Intellect Harvard requires page references: the Batat and Hammedi 2023 continuum quote and 'fluidify' (Batat 2024) both lack them. And "people and their machines hostages of each other" may be unmarked close quotation — check against the source.
5. **Fink 2025** may have been published since the audit; if so, complete the reference instead of cutting it.
6. **Superlatives I cannot audit:** "the first validated scale of algorithmic competency" and "we could find no study of this kind." Both hedged in good form; both should be re-searched close to submission.
7. **Whether the 9,000-word ceiling is hard and whether tables count toward it.** If the journal excludes references, item 1 relaxes and body trims alone nearly suffice. Confirm before cutting fifty references.
8. **British 'ize' compliance** looks consistent, but checked by eye, not exhaustively.

## Summary for the authors

The paper's argument survives a hostile read: the counterparty thesis, the competence/standing hinge, and the interface/constitutional split are all earned, and the audit repairs strengthened rather than patched it — the Pijls, Christin, Garcia, and Martin/Waldman passages are now doing argumentative work, not defensive work. Three repairs need their sentences rebuilt to match the hand that wrote the rest (Zhou above all, then Garcia and Manfreda), one uniqueness quantifier needs softening, one sentence is broken, one is a stub, and one number is wrong. Then the cut: references first, the well-being paragraph second, the duplicated tables third, and §7's line-fat fourth. Done in that order, the paper loses a third of its weight and none of its argument.
