### §2 From service recipient to guest (lines 29–41)

This section carries the paper's first target for me: is standing an answer to 'this is just contestability' or a relabel? My verdict is that it is an answer in extension and a relabel in definition, and the definition is what a referee reads.

In extension, the two come apart in both directions, and the paper shows both. Contestability without standing: the appeal that routes back to the party that wrote the rule, which raised no perceived accountability at all (Vaccaro et al. 2020, line 110) and which content-moderation users describe as cop, jury and judge in one (Are 2025). Standing without contestability features: the opaque algorithm with an empowered clerk beside it (line 83). A construct that separates cases in both directions is not a relabel.

In definition, line 33 says 'Contestability is a feature of a system: a route by which a decision can be challenged.' That is a 2019 definition. The contestability literature the paper itself cites has since relocated the term. Alfrink et al. (2023) treat it as both a system quality and a development practice; Hirsbrunner et al. (2025) argue that technical and legal measures 'may not be enough' and that contestation requires organizational culture and structural capability on the stakeholder's side (card, abstract); Yurrita et al. (2025) find that clarity about who is accountable is a precondition for contesting at all (card, abstract). In other words, the literature has already moved 'contestability' from feature to relation. The referee most likely to be assigned this paper works in that literature, and to him the §2 definition is a straw version and 'standing' is what he already calls meaningful contestability. The paper cites all three sources in §7 without conceding that they have made this move.

The fix is a concession that strengthens the claim. Say that the contestability literature has been arriving at the same place from the system's side, and say what standing adds: it names the guest's position rather than the system's property, and it holds whether or not any challenge is ever made. About fifty words. Drafted in Part 3, R1. **This is my single most important fix**, because it is the sentence at which the paper's central term is judged new or borrowed. [FIX TONIGHT]

The four-word taxonomy at line 35 (obligation, responsibility, authority, accountability) is stipulated honestly and used consistently afterwards. The welcome definition at line 37 is a definition and it does work at line 166. The rebuilt Pijls passage (line 41) is the section's best evidence. The Manfreda narrowing and the co-creation-scale clause answer my predecessor's F6 cleanly; 'We know of no instrument built for the second' is a scoped absence claim I cannot refute.
