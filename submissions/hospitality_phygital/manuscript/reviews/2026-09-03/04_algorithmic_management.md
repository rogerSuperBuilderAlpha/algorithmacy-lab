## Step 0 — Register note

The governing voice is the second author's: parenthetical citation, no em-dashes in the body (I count zero across lines 11–168), no bold in running text, first person plural on decisions. Every rewrite below keeps that. Two measurements matter for what follows. First, the body is short-breathed: my rough split gives a mean around 16–17 words with about 40 per cent of sentences under twelve, which is the author's hand and not a fault, but it means that a paragraph of nothing but short declaratives has no contrast left to work with. §7 line 124 (median sentence about nine words) and §6 line 112 (median about ten) are where the drumbeat sets in, and I treat that under the slop audit rather than as a register problem.
