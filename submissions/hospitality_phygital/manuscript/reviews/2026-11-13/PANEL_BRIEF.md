## Already found by the simulated referees and by Grok

Recommendations: R1 minor, R2 major, R3 minor. Grok's read is favourable (no recommendation); it checked no sources that it names.

1. **The further duty has several grounds that do not agree.** The abstract, introduction and conclusion say standing grounds it (ll. 5, 19, 177). 'During the stay' derives it from the guest's claim-right, via Herstein on Raz, plus means–end transmission via Kiesewetter (l. 115). Line 117 rests the guest-usable route on subjection and costly exit, and l. 183 derives it 'directly from the host's underlying duty'. Standing is defined as the guest's position 'when that obligation is recognized' (l. 41), so standing as a ground is circular, which was R2's first-round point. The text also drops Herstein's 'seemingly', and Kiesewetter's principle concerns necessary means and the deliberative 'ought'. (R1, R2, R3; Grok calls the step 'brisk' but coherent.)
2. **The binding conditions are stated inconsistently.**
   - The abstract makes the three conditions conjunctive.
   - Line 49 says 'None of these three elements suffices on its own', but then argues it for a different three (undertaking, reliance, vulnerability).
   - The brand verdict is disjunctive (l. 83), and for a platform holding out alone suffices (l. 93).
   - Line 109 says 'one rule holds consistently' (control), while package travel (l. 51) and the state (l. 107) bind without control.

   (R1, R2, R3)
3. **The delegation claim varies in strength.** The abstract says the firm 'keeps them even when contractors or algorithms carry out the work' (l. 5). The introduction and conclusion keep only the duty of care (ll. 19, 177). The body says 'liability narrows' (l. 71), and that 'each firm answers only for its own conduct' (l. 99) despite package travel (l. 51). The paper names two core duties but lists three (l. 75; receiving is in Table 1, row 1). The Hotel Proprietors Act cannot carry the safety duty: Al-Najar para 183 says the old innkeeper was not liable for criminal assault, and the safety duty comes from modern negligence (para 187). (R1, R2, R3)
4. **Who owes the further duty is unsettled.** The conclusion gives it to the party that controls the space (l. 183). But control may be shared (l. 73), a platform that holds itself out 'incurs a host's obligations' (l. 93), and no duty is derived for the vendor to receive and act on forwarded reports (l. 101), the link the paper itself diagnoses. (R1, R2, R3)
5. **The opening Garcia case.** 'Pricing authority shifts to a software model' (l. 15) contradicts 'managers kept the final say' (l. 11), which is what the card supports. 'Knowing that managers rarely intervened, the vendor designed …' attributes intent to an equilibrium model result. The 0.72 pass-through is given with a condition the card's quotation lacks. The study has no guests. The vendor section says the guest reaches a staff member 'who lacks discretion' (l. 97), but Garcia's managers had it. R2 and R3 suggest opening on Xu and Greiffenhagen. (R1, R2, R3)
6. **Misdescribed evidence.**

'Remedies after the fact' (l. 59) sits against the Consumer Rights Act's repeat performance (ll. 69, 121) and 'routinely prioritize live repair' (l. 121, resting on three instruments, one for platform workers). ## The bar

- **Do not manufacture problems.** If a section is sound, say so in a sentence and move on.
- **Restructuring is allowed**, because there are four weeks, but tag it **[CONSIDER]**. The authors decide.
- **Find what a second-round referee or the editor will catch.** That means:
  - a misdescribed source, or a quotation that does not match its source;
  - an overclaim, or a claim stated at different strengths in different places;
  - two claims that contradict each other;
  - an absence claim that a single citation would refute;
  - a referee point answered only on paper;
  - a hedge or condition that v8 carried and v9 has dropped.

## Standing rules

- **Verify before you assert.** If you say a source says something, check its card or saved text. If you cannot confirm a point, mark it **VERIFY** and say what you could not confirm. Never cite from memory as if checked: an invented citation, page or finding is the one unforgivable error here.
- **Anonymity is strict.** The body may not identify the authors, and should not name the special issue's editors. Any breach is a finding.
- **Locate everything.** Give the section name, the paragraph number within the section or subsection (prose paragraphs only: count one-sentence lead-ins; leave out lists, tables and table titles), the line number in DRAFT_v9.md, and a quotation of at most 15 words.
- **Suggested rewrites are suggestions.** They must be plainer, not more nominalized, passive or jargon-laden than what they replace, and they must keep the authors' wording wherever the wording is not the problem.
- **Write only your own file.** Do not create, edit or delete any other file, and do not commit.
