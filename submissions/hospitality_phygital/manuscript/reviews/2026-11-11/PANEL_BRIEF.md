## Already found by the simulated referees

1. **From duty to hearing.** The step from a persisting duty to a guest entitled to reach someone with authority, or to 'a hearing', is asserted, not argued. The bundle of duties (receive, sustain, protect, attend) contains no duty to hear, and attending is the one 'the law did not carry'. (R2, R3)
2. **Authority has two senses.** Delegation and standing ¶2 defines authority as power over 'a decision or the rule behind it'. The authority–answerability split ¶4 puts discretion on the single case and authority on the rule. (R1, R3)
3. **The test.** It asks no question about reachability in time and has no decision rule ('present in degrees' against 'passes'). The cases go beyond their sources:

4. **Reach of the role account.** Innkeeper law does not reach restaurants and bars, which the paper's own history leaves outside the common callings. Nor does it reach placed, non-paying guests (Petie et al.'s asylum seekers; Bulley's refugees). (R1, R2, R3)

## The bar

- **Do not manufacture problems.** If a section is sound, say so in a sentence and move on.
- **Restructuring is allowed**, because there are five weeks, but tag it **[CONSIDER]**. The authors decide.
- **Find what a second-round referee or the editor will catch.** That means a misdescribed source, an overclaim, an absence claim that a single citation would refute, a construct that collides with a published one, or a referee point answered only on paper.

## Standing rules

- **Verify before you assert.** Sources are carded in `submissions/hospitality_phygital/library/cards/`. If you say a source says something, check its card. The cards also hold the drafter's notes; rely only on the quotations and locators. If you cannot confirm a point, mark it **VERIFY** and say what you could not confirm. Never cite from memory as if checked: invented citations, pages or findings are the one unforgivable error here.
- **Anonymity is strict.** The body may not identify the authors, and should not name the special issue's editors. Any breach is a finding.
- **Locate everything.** Give the section name, the paragraph number within the section, the line number in DRAFT_v6.md, and a quotation of at most 15 words.
- **Suggested rewrites are suggestions.** The authors' own rule is that reviews are not applied to the prose by machine; their read-aloud decides. Your rewrites must be plainer, not more nominalized, passive or jargon-laden than what they replace.
- **Write only your own file.** Do not create, edit or delete any other file.

## Output

Write your review to your assigned path in this folder. As long as the findings need; 3,000–6,000 words is typical.

1. **Step 0, register note.** Two or three sentences on the register you are judging against.
2. **Part 1, rigour and argument**, section by section from your lens.
3. **Part 2, slop audit.** What reads as machine-made or mannered, with locations. Count tics only to point at passages; the question is whether a passage reads as written by a scholar for this journal.
4. **Part 3, line-level rewrites.** At most twelve, each with its location, the original and the suggestion.

Then the sections the synthesis depends on:

1. **Who I am.** One paragraph: your position, and what you came to this paper believing.
2. **The read.** Findings ranked by damage. For each: what the paper claims, what fails, why, the cost in words to fix, and a tag:
   - **[FIX]**: a careful author does it in under a day;
   - **[CONSIDER]**: a judgment call that is the authors';
   - **[CARRY FORWARD]**: needs a source, the co-author, or more than a few days.

   Mark overlaps with R1, R2 or R3.
3. **Where it is strong.** What must survive any revision.
4. **Best spend.** If you had 150 of the roughly 600 spare words, where would you put them? Draft the sentences, marked as suggestions.
5. **Verdict.** Accept, minor or major, and the single most important fix.
6. **VERIFY.** Anything that needs a source check.

Finish with a final message of at most 150 words giving your verdict and your three most damaging findings.
