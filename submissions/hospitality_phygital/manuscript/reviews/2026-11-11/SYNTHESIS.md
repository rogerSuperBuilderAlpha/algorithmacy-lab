## 2. What ranks first

**The step from duty to hearing ranks first.** DS ¶9 (L68) concludes that the guest can expect 'a hearing by someone with authority'. Test ¶1 (L86) derives accountability 'from the duty to answer'. Yet the bundle of duties in HO ¶6 (L38) is to receive, sustain, protect and attend, and none of these is a duty to hear. Seven reviewers reach the gap on their own (R2, R3, 01, 04, 05, 06, 07), and both major-revision referees make it their condition. Three things put it first:

- **It is the thesis.** The abstract, Intro ¶3 and Concl ¶2 rest on 'the guest's path to anyone with authority', and nothing argues the guest is owed that path.
- **It propagates.** Question 5, Table 1's top rows, Table 2's stage 4 and the appeal to Bovens 'in real time' all hang on it. Mending it mends findings 5, 7 and 31.
- **The repair is in the paper's own sources** (adjudication 1). It costs about 150 words, less the 76 saved by cutting DS ¶8 (L66), the paragraph the argument needs least (05).

The AI acknowledgment and the reply to R3.1 come first in time, because the editor will not send the paper out without them (07). They are a decision, not a finding, and only the authors can make it (§6.1).
