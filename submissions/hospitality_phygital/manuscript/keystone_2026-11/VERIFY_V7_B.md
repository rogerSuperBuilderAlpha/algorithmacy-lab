# Claim audit of DRAFT_v7, half B: Section 4, Conclusion, Abstract, Highlights

**Summary.** I checked 89 sentences, table cells and highlights against the saved full texts in `revision_2026-11/research/sources/` and `rewrite_2026-11/journal/corpus/`. Where neither held a passage, I used the card. The verdicts are 67 SUPPORTED, 1 SUPPORTED (card only), 15 PARTIAL, 4 LOCATOR WRONG and 2 OVERSTATED. No row is QUOTE MISMATCH, MISATTRIBUTED, CONTRADICTED or UNVERIFIABLE LOCALLY. Every quotation in this half matches its source word for word, apart from initial capitals. That includes the three lead-written cards (`ostp2022blueprint`, `tbscanada2019directive`, `binesh2026ai`). The faults are in scope, in locators and in two legal statements. The three most serious are these:

1. **S-158** drops the condition on Garcia et al.'s 0.72 figure, which holds only 'when the manager updates prices manually'. Across all updates the pass-through is 0.974. S-157 and S-158 also give working-paper pages under a reference to the *Management Science* version, which no one read, and the two sentences use different page schemes.
2. **S-222** says no law requires a route to a natural person with power over the single case. Two sentences earlier, S-220 cites the data-protection rules, and those rules give a person subject to a solely automated significant decision the right to obtain human intervention. S-251 adds 'general' to exclude that case, and S-222 does not.
3. **S-181** states AI Act art. 14 as a present requirement. Under the 2026 omnibus it applies only from 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and from 2 August 2028 for Annex I systems. The v6 adjudication had already flagged this point.

The thesis is held at 'can' everywhere except S-174. The v6 adjudication's other fatal and material faults do not recur in this half: non-delegability, the hearing, the hotel-pricing 'reach the managers', Bovens 'in real time' and the Batat relay.
