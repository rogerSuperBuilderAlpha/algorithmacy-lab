# Hand-check of the passages the argument rests on

**Method.** For each passage I located the words in the file saved under `manuscript/revision_2026-11/research/sources/<citekey>/`, read about 450 characters around the hit, and checked the wording, the locator and what the sentence is doing in its context (holding, dictum, headnote, a party's submission, a report of someone else). Where the saved file has no text layer I read the page image.

**Verdicts.** MATCH: wording and locator as the reader reported. NOTE: wording right, with a point the argument must respect.
