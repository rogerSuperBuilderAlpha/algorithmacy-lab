# Tier 1 reading list, by claim

**Rule.** A source is on this list only if a K claim rests on it or it could refute one. Each reader takes about eight sources. Closed texts are not on the list; they are in the fetch list at the end.

**Why the list is longer than the plan's 25.** The law scouts found and saved the primary texts of some forty cases and statutes, most of them short provisions or single judgments. Each bears on a named premise, and a case may enter only from its own text, so they are carded.

## Fetch list for the authors (closed; ranked by the claim that waits on it)

1. **Telfer (2000), 'The philosophy of hospitableness'**, or ch. 5 of her *Food for Thought* (1996): K1, K2. The field's philosophical source, known to us only through others' quotations.
2. **Hardimon (1994), 'Role obligations'**, *Journal of Philosophy* 91: 333–363: K3. He holds that a role's force 'flows from the role', against the view the paper takes.
3. **Simmons (1996), 'External justifications and institutional roles'**, *Journal of Philosophy* 93: 28–36: K3. The view the paper takes.
4. **Goodin (1986), 'Responsibilities'**, *Philosophical Quarterly* 36 (or ch. 5 of his 1995 book): K7. The nearest statement in print of the paper's own step.
5. **King (1995), 'What is hospitality?'**: K1. Two attributions in v6 rest on it second-hand.
6. **May (2015), 'Directed duties'**, *Philosophy Compass*: K6. The standard survey.
7. **Koo and Kwon (2026)**, *International Journal of Contemporary Hospitality Management*: K9. The nearest neighbour in hospitality.
8. **Goodin (1985), *Protecting the Vulnerable***, and **Scanlon (1998), ch. 7**: K3, grounds 3 and 2.
9. **Derrida, *Of Hospitality***, pp. 25, 77–79: K2.
