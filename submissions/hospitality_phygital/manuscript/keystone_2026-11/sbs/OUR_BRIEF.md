## The research question, and where v7 states it

1. **As an aim** (Introduction ¶3): to say what a commercial host owes a guest, on what ground and held by whom, and what that duty asks of a host that performs through other firms and through systems.
2. **As the driving question** (Delegation section ¶1, in the model's form): what becomes of the host's duty once the work of hosting is handed on.
3. **As the touchpoint question** (Touchpoint section ¶1): can a failure in the guest's case reach, while correction can still help, someone able to correct it?
4. **Answered** in the Conclusion ¶2.

## The thesis, and its shape

- **Claim.** A commercial host owes each guest it receives care for the guest's safety and, in what it undertook, performance with care. The duty binds through holding out, the guest's reliance and the host's control of the space the guest is in. When others do the work, what stays with the host depends on the duty, and several parties may hold the care duty at once. During the stay, the guest's claim justifies a further duty, owed to the guest: to keep some timely means by which a failure in the guest's case reaches someone able to correct it. Where only the guest perceives the failure, that means must include a route the guest can use without depending on the host's goodwill.
- **Strength, held at one level throughout.** Algorithmic mediation *can* move the person with power over the single case out of the guest's path. The paper does not say it removes the person (`ARGUMENT.md`, Step 7; DECISIONS_K K-D13).
- **Shape (the model's): with the field's account of the host, then two steps beyond it, then the touchpoint.**
  1. Grant the field its account: the host has power over the guest and owes the guest protection.
  2. Name two limits. The field's commercial debate looks for the duty in motive. The field names divided hosting and allocates no duty.
  3. First step: the role specifies; holding out, reliance and control bind; the law's two tiers show the content.
  4. Second step: what stays under delegation; several holders; the guest's claim during the stay and the further duty it justifies.
  5. Show both steps at touchpoints, with documented cases.
  6. Close on limits, studies from documented gaps, and what hosts can do.

## Contribution moves, in order

1. Reaffirm the field's account of the host's duty and of the host's power after the threshold (Section 2 ¶1-2).
2. Move the commercial debate from motive to role, with Telfer and Derrida as their quoting carriers allow (Section 2 ¶3-4).
3. Say why the duty binds: the role specifies; holding out, reliance and control bind, and do different work (Section 2 ¶4-6).
4. Show the content in law, in two tiers, and to whom it is owed (Section 2 ¶7-8).
5. Say what stays with the holder under delegation, and that several parties may hold the care duty (Section 3 ¶2-4).
6. The paper's step: the guest's claim during the stay justifies a further duty, owed to the guest, to keep a timely means of correction (Section 3 ¶5-8). Marked as ours.
7. At the touchpoint: where the person with power sits; who must be at the end of the route; a test of six questions; when the route is owed; the law's floor; what hosts can do (Section 4).

## Register commitments

1. **Registers named in the prose.** Where a sentence states a duty, it says whether the duty is the law's (with the jurisdiction), the field's, or the paper's moral claim.
