**What the paper does not claim.** The paper's cases are taken from published studies and judgments and illustrate its distinctions rather than test them, and the five stages inherit that limit: they order what the argument asks of a host, and we have not tested them as an intervention. The paper does not claim the duty for hosts letting rooms in their own homes.
