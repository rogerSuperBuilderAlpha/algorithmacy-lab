- [S] *Al-Najar v Cumberland Hotel* [2019] EWHC 1593 (QB) and [2020] EWCA Civ 1716. Summaries say a hotel, by inviting guests to stay, assumes a duty to take reasonable care to protect them from third parties' criminal acts, and that the claim failed on breach.
