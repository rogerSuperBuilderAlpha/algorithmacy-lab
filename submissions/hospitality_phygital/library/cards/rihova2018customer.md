## Summary

A practice-based study of how festival visitors co-create social value with one another, framed
by customer-dominant logic.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- 'The social value of such encounters may not be directly linked to the immediate service exchange but rather, it is formed in the process of tourists’ C2C co-creation.' (Introduction, ms p. 1) [full-text]
- Positions customer-dominant logic as distinct from service-dominant logic, emphasising value formed 'within experiences and practices situated in and influenced by customers’ own social contexts' (Introduction, ms p. 2) [full-text]
