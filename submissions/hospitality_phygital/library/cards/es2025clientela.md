## Summary

The law sets minimum quality standards for the customer service of two groups of firms selling to consumers in Spain: suppliers of listed basic services of general interest (water and energy, passenger transport, post, electronic communications, finance), whatever their size, and all other firms or groups that in the previous year had at least 250 workers, or turnover above 50 million euros, or a balance sheet above 43 million euros (arts 1, 2). Within that scope it forbids answering machines or similar means as the only means of customer service, and obliges the firm, when the customer asks, to supply "atención personalizada": a natural person who answers in real time, reachable from the main menu at any point of the interaction, with 95 per cent of such requests met, on average, in under three minutes (art. 8(1)-(2)). A dissatisfied customer may ask to be passed to a supervisor who is a natural person (art. 8(3)). The firm must resolve complaints with reasons within fifteen working days at most (arts 13, 17), carries the burden of proving compliance (art. 4(7)), and answers for the service whether it runs it or a third party does (arts 2(3), 3(11)). Breach is a consumer-law infringement sanctioned by the public authorities (art. 23). The law entered into force on 28 December 2025 and gives firms twelve months to adapt. All English wording below is my paraphrase of the Spanish.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

**Object and scope: who is bound**

- Art. 1 (p. 176177): "Esta ley tiene por objeto la regulación de los niveles mínimos de calidad y de la evaluación de los servicios de atención a la clientela de las empresas que presten determinados servicios de carácter básico de interés general y de las grandes empresas." My paraphrase: the law regulates minimum quality levels, and their evaluation, for the customer service of firms supplying certain basic services of general interest and of large firms. [full-text]
- Art. 2(1) (p. 176177) binds "todas las empresas, establecidas en España o en cualquier otro Estado" that supply, in Spanish territory, the listed basic services: "a) Servicios de suministro y distribución de agua, gas y electricidad;", passenger transport by air, rail, sea or inland waterway and bus or coach (b), "c) Servicios postales;", electronic communications (d) and financial services (e). The list names no lodging, hotel or tourism service. [full-text]
- Art. 2(2) (p. 176177) binds other firms by size. The law applies to "las empresas y grupos de sociedades, en el sentido del artículo 42 del Código de Comercio, establecidos en España o en cualquier otro Estado, que lleven a cabo la venta de bienes o la prestación de servicios diferentes a los recogidos en el apartado anterior en territorio español destinados principalmente a personas consumidoras y usuarias", provided that in the previous financial year, "de forma individual o en el seno del grupo de sociedades del que formen parte, hayan ocupado al menos a 250 personas trabajadoras, su volumen de negocios anual haya excedido de 50 millones de euros, o su balance de negocios anual haya excedido de 43 millones de euros." My paraphrase: firms and groups selling goods or services mainly to consumers in Spain are bound if, alone or within their group, they had at least 250 workers, or annual turnover above 50 million euros, or a balance sheet above 43 million euros. [full-text]
- The three size tests are alternatives and the group counts as one. Preamble III (p. 176172): the criterion "se ha modulado de tal forma que la presente ley resulte de aplicación a todas aquellas empresas que cumplan alguno de dichos requisitos, no considerándolos de forma cumulativa", so as to include suppliers "que operan a través de distintas sociedades pero que, en su conjunto, tienen capacidad económica y recursos para hacer frente a las disposiciones de la norma." [full-text]
- Outsourcing the service changes nothing. Art. 2(3) (p. 176177): "La ley será de aplicación independientemente del canal de comunicación elegido, de la inclusión del servicio de atención a la clientela en la estructura organizativa de la empresa o en la de un tercero o de la ubicación geográfica del punto de comunicación con las personas consumidoras y usuarias." Art. 3(11) (p. 176179) defines customer service as the firm's organisation of means for receiving queries and managing and resolving complaints, "independientemente de que sean gestionados por la propia empresa o por un tercero." Art. 9(1) (p. 176184) requires adequate human and other means "independientemente de que dicho servicio sea prestado de forma directa o no". [full-text]
- Sector rules come first. Art. 2(4) (p. 176177): the law applies "con carácter supletorio" to other consumer statutes and sector rules, "considerando siempre de aplicación preferente la normativa sectorial." Financial services and electronic communications follow their own rules (art. 2(5)-(6), p. 176178). [full-text]
- Paid contracts only. Art. 2(8) (p. 176178): "esta ley es de aplicación a la oferta onerosa de productos y servicios, cuando el consumidor o usuario los contrate a cambio de una contraprestación." [full-text]
- To whom the duties run. Art. 3(1) (p. 176178): "Clientela: las personas consumidoras o usuarias que hayan comprado un bien, a las que se le haya dirigido una oferta comercial personalizada o hayan celebrado un contrato con una empresa prestadora de servicios, independientemente de su carácter oneroso o gratuito y de que hagan uso efectivo del servicio y hasta que se extingan definitivamente todos los efectos de dicho contrato." My paraphrase: the clientele are consumers who have bought a good, received a personalised offer or made a contract with a service firm, until every effect of that contract has ended. [full-text]

**The requirement of a person**

- Art. 8(1) (p. 176183): "Se prohíbe el empleo de contestadores automáticos u otros medios análogos como medio exclusivo de atención a la clientela." My paraphrase: answering machines or similar means may not be the only means of customer service. [full-text]
- Art. 8(2), first paragraph (p. 176183): "A solicitud de la persona consumidora o usuaria, cuando una consulta, queja, reclamación o comunicación de incidencia se formule vía telefónica o electrónica, la empresa ha de garantizar una atención personalizada desde las opciones que ponen a disposición del cliente en su menú principal del canal telefónico. En este sentido, la utilización de contestadores automáticos, bots conversacionales u otros medios análogos deberá ofrecer a la clientela la posibilidad de solicitar una atención personalizada desde las opciones que ponen a disposición del cliente en su menú principal, en cualquier momento de la interacción, desde su inicio." My paraphrase: when a query, complaint or incident is raised by telephone or electronically and the consumer asks, the firm must guarantee personal attention from the options on its main menu; where it uses answering machines, chatbots or the like, the customer must be able to ask for personal attention from the main menu at any moment of the interaction, from its start. [full-text]
- Who the person is, art. 8(2), second paragraph (p. 176183): "A tales efectos, se considera atención personalizada la ofrecida directamente a través de una persona física operadora que contesta en tiempo real a la clientela, que deberá identificarse, en todo caso, al inicio de la conversación." My paraphrase: personal attention is attention given directly by a natural person, an operator, who answers the customer in real time and identifies themself at the start. [full-text]
- When, art. 8(2), third paragraph (p. 176183): "La atención personalizada se prestará a la mayor brevedad posible desde su solicitud por parte de la clientela, garantizando que el 95 % de las solicitudes de atención personalizadas sean atendidas, de media, en un plazo inferior a tres minutos desde que la clientela efectúe la solicitud." My paraphrase: as soon as possible after the request, with 95 per cent of requests met, on average, in under three minutes. [full-text]
- Escalation, art. 8(3) (p. 176183): "En caso de insatisfacción con la atención recibida por parte del operador, quien haya iniciado la comunicación podrá solicitar que se transfiera la comunicación a una persona física supervisora o a un departamento específico de calidad, que deberá atenderle en el transcurso de esa misma comunicación. Cuando no sea posible dicha transferencia en un plazo inferior a tres minutos, la empresa podrá contactar con posterioridad, siempre dentro del mismo día laborable en que recibe dicha comunicación." My paraphrase: a dissatisfied customer may ask to be transferred to a supervisor who is a natural person, or to a quality department, within the same communication; if transfer within three minutes is not possible the firm may call back within the same working day. [full-text]
- Art. 8(4) (p. 176183): "Las empresas no podrán cortar una comunicación con el cliente por razón de tiempo de espera elevado." [full-text]
- The definitions repeat the duty. Art. 3(11) (p. 176179): "En cualquier caso, deberá tenerse en consideración la obligación de atención por persona física (no de modo automático o maquinal), a demanda del cliente." Art. 3(14) (p. 176179): "Operador: persona física que presta la atención especializada en el servicio de atención a la clientela, con formación y capacitación adecuada en función del sector de la actividad." [full-text]
- What the person must have: training. Art. 9(2) (p. 176184): "El personal que preste atención personalizada a la clientela, así como quien diseñe y gestione los medios automatizados que se puedan utilizar a tales efectos, deberá contar con una formación y capacitación adecuada, en función del sector o de la actividad, que garantice la eficacia en la gestión que realice". The duty to train reaches those who design and run the automated means. No article gives the operator or the supervisor authority to decide, reverse or grant anything (whole text read; 'autoridad' occurs only of public authorities, and 'facultad' and its forms only of regulatory powers and of traders entitled to answer reviews). [full-text]
- The telephone rule, art. 10(3) (p. 176184): a call must be answered "garantizando que el 95 % de las llamadas recibidas sean atendidas, de media, en un plazo inferior a tres minutos", and a call does not count as answered "cuando dicha atención no permita a la clientela exponer el motivo de la comunicación y solicitar la atención personalizada por parte de un operador." [full-text]
- The stated reason, preamble III (p. 176173): the law is meant to cover present and future technology, "pudiéndose hacer uso de sistemas de inteligencia artificial que faciliten y sistematicen las comunicaciones entre las partes. No obstante, se considera fundamental que la clientela pueda acceder, si así lo desea, a una atención personalizada por parte de la empresa, que deberá ser prestada por personas formadas para ello." My paraphrase: firms may use artificial intelligence, but it is fundamental that customers can reach personal attention if they wish, given by trained people. [full-text]

**What the service must do, and by when**

- Art. 4(1) (p. 176179): firms within scope "deberán disponer de un servicio de atención a la clientela gratuito, eficaz, universalmente accesible, inclusivo, no discriminatorio y evaluable." [full-text]
- Art. 4(2)(b) (p. 176180): the service must let the customer "Reclamar con celeridad en caso de error, defecto, deterioro o cualquier otra incidencia;". [full-text]
- A complaint stops a cut-off. Art. 4(4) (p. 176180): "En el supuesto de contratos de servicios de tracto sucesivo, no podrá suspenderse la prestación del servicio por parte del empresario con posterioridad a la presentación de la reclamación si la reclamación está directamente relacionada con el motivo de la pretendida suspensión, salvo que el mantenimiento del servicio pudiera afectar a la seguridad de las personas o de las cosas y en tanto no tenga lugar la comunicación a la persona consumidora de la resolución expresa y motivada de aquella, salvo en los casos en los que así lo determine la normativa sectorial que resulte de aplicación." My paraphrase: under a contract for continuing services the trader may not suspend the service after a complaint is lodged, if the complaint goes to the ground of the intended suspension, until the consumer has been sent an express and reasoned resolution, save for safety and for contrary sector rules. [full-text]
- Channels, art. 7(1) (pp. 176182-176183): firms must accept complaints through "el mismo canal a través del que se inició la relación contractual, así como, al menos, la vía postal, telefónica y por un medio de comunicación electrónica". Art. 7(4) (p. 176183): fixed premises open to the public, "sean propios o franquiciados", must also accept them. [full-text]
- Reasons, art. 13(1) (p. 176186): "La resolución de las consultas, quejas, reclamaciones o incidencias deberá estar debidamente motivada.", answering every point raised, "sin que quepan contestaciones genéricas." [full-text]
- Hours, art. 14(1) (p. 176187): "El horario del servicio atención al cliente se ajustará al horario comercial de la empresa, independientemente de si la actividad económica se lleva a cabo a través de establecimientos físicos o por vía electrónica." For continuing basic services, art. 14(2): the service "estará disponible 24 horas al día, todos los días del año, para la comunicación de incidencias relativas a la continuidad del servicio." [full-text]
- Time to resolve, art. 17(1) (p. 176188): matters "serán resueltas en el plazo más breve posible en función de la naturaleza del problema y, en todo caso, en el plazo máximo de quince días hábiles desde su presentación, salvo que la normativa sectorial establezca un plazo distinto." Art. 17(2): for continuing basic services, queries or incidents about continuity of service "deberán ser respondidas en el plazo máximo de dos horas". Art. 17(3): billing matters "deberán ser respondidas en el plazo máximo de cinco días." [full-text]
- Internal hand-over, art. 9(1), second paragraph (p. 176184): "Asimismo, las empresas asegurarán la transmisión de información requerida por este departamento al resto de departamentos de la empresa y la coordinación entre los mismos, con pleno respeto a la protección de datos de carácter personal." [full-text]

**Sanction, proof and dates**

- Art. 23(1) (pp. 176190-176191): "El incumplimiento por las empresas de las disposiciones de esta ley será sancionado como infracción en materia de consumo", under the general sanctioning regime of the consumer statute and the applicable regional law. My paraphrase: breach is punished as a consumer-law infringement. [full-text]
- Art. 23(4) (p. 176191): "Este régimen de infracciones y sanciones tendrá en cuenta la reincidencia de la conducta, así como el resarcimiento del daño, a fin de proceder a la agravación o la atenuación de la sanción, respectivamente." Making good the harm lowers the sanction; the article gives the customer no claim. [full-text]
- How serious. Final provision 3, point Tres (p. 176202) rewrites art. 47(1)(q) of the consumer statute to make an infringement of "El incumplimiento de las obligaciones en relación con los servicios de atención al cliente incluidas en esta norma y en su normativa específica.", and point Cuatro rewrites art. 48(2)(a): "Las infracciones de los apartados f), g), i), k), m), n), ñ), p), q) y t) del artículo 47 se calificarán como leves, salvo que tengan la consideración de graves de acuerdo con el apartado tercero de este artículo." The default classification is therefore minor. I did not read art. 48(3) or the scale of fines. [full-text]
- Art. 4(7) (p. 176181): "Las empresas asumirán la carga de la prueba del cumplimiento de las obligaciones que les impone esta ley." [full-text]
- Entry into force, final provision 8 (p. 176205): "Esta ley entrará en vigor el día siguiente al de su publicación en el «Boletín Oficial del Estado»." Published 27 December 2025; the BOE consolidated page gives 'Entrada en vigor: 28/12/2025'. [full-text]
- Time to adapt, sole transitional provision, para 1 (p. 176191): "Las empresas incluidas en el ámbito de aplicación de la presente ley deberán adaptar sus servicios de atención a la clientela a las disposiciones de la misma en el plazo de doce meses desde su entrada en vigor." The preamble describes the same provision as giving "un período de seis meses desde la entrada en vigor de la misma para la adaptación a las novedades establecidas" (preamble III, p. 176174). The two do not agree; the operative text says twelve months. [full-text]

**The general rule for every trader, and the sector carve-outs**

- Final provision 3, point Dos (p. 176201) rewrites art. 21(2) of the general consumer statute, which is not limited by size: "Si tales servicios utilizan la atención telefónica o electrónica para llevar a cabo sus funciones deberán garantizar una atención personal directa, más allá de la posibilidad de utilizar complementariamente otros medios técnicos a su alcance." My paraphrase: customer-service offices that work by telephone or electronically must guarantee direct personal attention, whatever technical means they also use. The same point sets, in art. 21(3) (pp. 176201-176202), a general limit: traders must answer complaints "en el plazo más breve posible y, en todo caso, en el plazo máximo de quince días desde la presentación de la reclamación." [full-text]
- Preamble I (p. 176170) says art. 21 already required this: telephone or electronic services "deberán garantizar una atención personal directa, más allá de la posibilidad de utilizar complementariamente otros medios técnicos a su alcance." Preamble III (p. 176172) says firms below the thresholds stay subject to the consumer statute's provisions. [full-text]
- Telecoms get less. Final provision 5 (p. 176204), rewriting art. 65(2) of Ley 11/2022: "El operador podrá prestar el servicio de atención a la clientela mediante el empleo de contestadores automáticos u otros medios análogos, si bien, en última instancia, deberá garantizar una atención personalizada, sin que ello implique el derecho a acceder a una persona física supervisora." [full-text]
- Finance keeps its own regime. Final provision 2 (pp. 176192-176200) rewrites arts 29-31 of Ley 44/2002; the new art. 29(1) repeats the ban on automated-only service and requires personal attention "ofrecida a través de un operador o agente del mismo", with agents who "contarán con supervisores o superiores jerárquicos que gestionarán las posibles quejas que pudiera generar la atención recibida" (p. 176193). [full-text]
- The one place the text gives someone power over the outcome is in that finance regime. New art. 29(2) of Ley 44/2002 (p. 176194): "La decisión del Defensor de la Clientela favorable a la reclamación vinculará a la entidad." New art. 29(3) (p. 176194) requires the customer-service department to be separated from commercial and operational services, "de modo que se garantice que aquél tome de manera autónoma sus decisiones referentes al ámbito de su actividad". My paraphrase: a decision of the customer ombudsman in the claimant's favour binds the entity, and the department must take its decisions autonomously. The general regime of arts 1-23 has no counterpart. [full-text]

**What the text does not contain** (whole-text searches of the saved extract, 11 Nov 2026)

## Critical notes & threats

- **Hotels are inside only by size.** Lodging is not a listed basic service (art. 2(1)). A hotel company is bound if it, or the group it belongs to, meets one of the three size tests and sells mainly to consumers in Spain (art. 2(2)). An independent small hotel is outside this law and stays under the general rule in art. 21 of the consumer statute, which I read only as this law rewrites it. That a large hotel group falls within art. 2(2) is my reading of the words; I found no ruling or guidance and looked for none.
- **The person is defined by presence and training, with no stated power.** Art. 8 secures a natural person in real time and a supervisor on request. Arts 3(14) and 9(2) require training. Nothing requires that the operator or supervisor can change the outcome. The duties to resolve, give reasons and meet deadlines fall on the firm (arts 13, 17). K7's conclusion asks for "a person with power over the system's output in the single case"; the statute stops short of that.
- **A service standard, with one rule about timing.** The duties run for the life of the contract (art. 3(1)) and are not tied to whether the firm's performance can still be put right. The exception is art. 4(4): a complaint blocks suspension of a continuing service until a reasoned answer is given. The law does not define "tracto sucesivo" for that purpose, and whether a stay of several nights is such a contract is not settled by the text.
- **An average, not a guarantee to each customer.** The three-minute figure is met if 95 per cent of requests are answered within it "de media" (art. 8(2)).
- **Public enforcement.** Breach is an administrative infringement, minor by default (art. 23; final provision 3). The text gives the customer the service and puts the burden of proof on the firm (art. 4(7)), but states no private remedy. Whether a consumer can found a civil claim on these duties under general Spanish law I did not check.
- **Customers who did not contract.** The clientele are consumers who bought, were sent a personalised offer or made a contract (art. 3(1)), and the law covers paid offers (art. 2(8)). A guest placed by an employer or an agency is not obviously within it.
- **Not yet due on 11 Nov 2026.** Twelve months from entry into force on 28 December 2025 ends in late December 2026 (my computation from the transitional provision and final provision 8). The preamble's "seis meses" conflicts with the article.
- **Translation.** I read the Spanish and paraphrased. "Atención personalizada" is the statute's defined term; 'personal attention' is my rendering.
- Later treatment not checked, apart from the BOE consolidated page, which on 11 Nov 2026 showed no amending version.

## Where it bears on the argument

**K7 (channel from the guest): supports the conclusion in part, as enacted law for large firms and basic services; narrows it on power, on the ground and on remedy.** K7's 'confirms' list asks for "duties on traders to be reachable". This law is one: it requires a route from the customer to a natural person, on the customer's request, in real time, and forbids an automated-only channel (art. 8(1)-(2)), with a supervisor behind the operator (art. 8(3)). It also treats the customer's own report as what sets the firm's duties running (arts 4(2)(b), 17), which is P4's assumption written into a statute and no evidence that P4 is true. It does not state P3. It does not require that the person reached can decide. It rests on consumer protection and service quality (preamble II, IV), not on the firm's duty to perform with care. Art. 4(4) is the one provision that follows K7's timing line: for continuing services the customer's complaint holds off the adverse act until someone answers with reasons.

**K8 (holder under delegation): supports, narrowly.** The firm that deals with the consumer answers for its customer service whether it or a third party runs it (arts 2(3), 3(11), 9(1)), the group is counted as one firm (art. 2(2); preamble III), and franchised premises must take complaints (art. 7(4)). That is a statutory instance of work delegated and duty kept, for this duty only.

**What the paper may say on this source's authority**

- Spain's Ley 10/2025 forbids answering machines or similar means as the only means of customer service (art. 8(1)).
- Where a firm within the law uses automated means, the customer must be able to ask for a natural person from the main menu at any point of the interaction, and that person must answer in real time (art. 8(2)).
- The law requires that 95 per cent of such requests be met, on average, in under three minutes, and lets a dissatisfied customer ask for a supervisor within the same communication (art. 8(2)-(3)).
- The law binds suppliers of listed basic services and, among other firms, those with at least 250 workers, turnover above 50 million euros or a balance sheet above 43 million euros, counted across the group (art. 2(1)-(2)).
- The firm is bound whether its customer service is run in-house or by a third party (arts 2(3), 3(11)).
- Under a contract for continuing services, a complaint about the ground of an intended suspension bars the suspension until a reasoned resolution is communicated (art. 4(4)).
- Breach is sanctioned as a consumer-law infringement, and the firm bears the burden of proving compliance (arts 23(1), 4(7)).
- The preamble says firms may use artificial intelligence but that customers must be able to reach trained people if they wish (preamble III).

**What the paper may not say**

- That Spanish law gives a hotel guest, as such, a right to reach a person. The law names no lodging service and reaches hotel firms only through the size tests (art. 2).
- That the person reached must have authority to decide or reverse. The law requires a trained natural person and a supervisor (arts 3(14), 8(2)-(3), 9(2)).
- That the law lets the customer sue for breach. Its sanction is administrative (art. 23); general Spanish civil law was not checked.
- That the duty to supply a person within three minutes is owed to each caller. It is a 95 per cent average (art. 8(2)).
- That the law was already binding in full in November 2026. The transitional provision gives twelve months from 28 December 2025.
- That the law ties the channel to a duty of care or to the period in which performance is still possible. Only art. 4(4) links a complaint to a pending adverse act, and only for continuing services.
- That the same rule holds for telecoms and finance. Each has its own rewritten regime (final provisions 2 and 5), and the telecoms text denies a right to a supervisor.
- Anything in the Spanish as if it were an official English text. The English here is my paraphrase.
