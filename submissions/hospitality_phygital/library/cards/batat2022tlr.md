## Summary

Special-issue communication that introduces transformative luxury research (TLR). The framework has four spheres: luxury consumption (four tension-laden consumer profiles), luxury production (employees, suppliers, organization, community), macro-level stakeholders (institutions, policymakers, academics, technology firms) and well-being outcomes. It closes with six research areas, among them "luxury and digital well-being" and heritage protection "in the 'phygital' ecosystem".
