## Summary

A three-round Delphi study, structured by the Competing Values Framework, on how algorithmic
management (AM) will change the roles of hotel line managers. Twenty-two experts were recruited
(13 senior hotel practitioners in UK full-service hotels and 9 hospitality or tourism academics);
15 completed round 2 and 13 round 3. Experts forecast more efficiency and control, intensified
work, risks to motivation, collaboration and creativity, and a shift in line managers' role from
control to "algorithmic coaching": communication, performance, social and creative facilitation,
including translating algorithmic decisions for frontline staff.
