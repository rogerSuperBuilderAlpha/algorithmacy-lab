## Summary

The primary legal text. Article 86 gives a person affected by a decision a deployer takes on the output of an Annex III high-risk system a right to explanation. Locators below are PDF page numbers of the 144-page Official Journal file (not OJ page numbers). New numbering (rr-2026-11): section 9, jurisdiction boundary condition.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Art. 14(4)(e): high-risk systems must enable those assigned to oversee them "to intervene in the operation of the high-risk AI system or interrupt the system through a ‘stop’ button or a similar procedure that allows the system to come to a halt in a safe state" (art. 14(4)(e); Official Journal text saved at revision_2026-11/research/sources/eu2024aiact/aiact.txt) (added 10 Nov 2026 for DRAFT_v6) [full-text]
**Article 86, Right to explanation of individual decision-making** (PDF p. 110/144) [full-text]
- "1. Any affected person subject to a decision which is taken by the deployer on the basis of the output from a high-risk AI system listed in Annex III, with the exception of systems listed under point 2 thereof, and which produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects that person in a way that they consider to have an adverse impact on their health, safety or fundamental rights shall have the right to obtain from the deployer clear and meaningful explanations of the role of the AI system in the decision-making procedure and the main elements of the decision taken." [full-text]
- "3. This Article shall apply only to the extent that the right referred to in paragraph 1 is not otherwise provided for under Union law." [full-text]

**Article 113, Entry into force and application** (PDF p. 123/144): "It shall apply from 2 August 2026." Exceptions: Chapters I and II from 2 February 2025; Chapter III Section 4, Chapter V, Chapter VII, Chapter XII and Article 78 from 2 August 2025 (not Article 101); "Article 6(1) and the corresponding obligations ... shall apply from 2 August 2027". Article 86 sits in Chapter IX and is in none of the listed exceptions, so on the text of Art. 113 it applies from 2 August 2026 (a reading of the list, not a quotation). [full-text]

**Amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), in force from the third day after 24.7.2026** (omnibus .txt, point (40); recital at the paragraph beginning "Article 113 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 establishes") [full-text]
- Point (40)(b) replaces Art. 113 third paragraph point (c): "Chapter III, Sections 1, 2, and 3, with the exception of Article 6(5), shall apply from: (i) 2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III; and (ii) 2 August 2028 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(1) and Annex I". The card's earlier line that Art. 6(1) applies from 2 August 2027 is superseded. [full-text]
- Point (40)(a) replaces point (a): Chapters I and II from 2 February 2025, with new Art. 5 prohibitions (5(1)(ba), (bb), 5(1a), 5(1b)) from 2 December 2026. Point (40)(c) adds point (d): "Articles 102 to 110 shall apply from 27 July 2026." [full-text]
- The general date of application, 2 August 2026, is unchanged; the recital calls it "the general date of application". [full-text]
- Article 86 is not amended: the string "Article 86" does not occur in the Omnibus, nor does "Chapter IX" (whole-text search). On the text Art. 86 still applies from 2 August 2026. [full-text]
- Annex III is not amended by the Omnibus (its Annex I is: machinery moves from Section A to Section B). Art. 6 gains paragraphs on safety components, irrelevant here. [full-text]
- Caveat the text does not settle: Art. 6, the classification rule, sits in Chapter III Section 1, which now applies to Annex III systems from 2 December 2027. Whether an Art. 86 right can be exercised for an Annex III system before the classification rules apply is a legal question this card cannot answer. [full-text]

**Annex III, areas relevant to a hotel** (PDF p. 127/144), "High-risk AI systems pursuant to Article 6(2) are the AI systems listed in any of the following areas": no area names accommodation, hospitality, hotels or tourism. The two areas a hotel's decisions could be tested against by their wording: [full-text]
- Point 4(b): "AI systems intended to be used to make decisions affecting terms of work-related relationships, the promotion or termination of work-related contractual relationships, to allocate tasks based on individual behaviour or personal traits or characteristics or to monitor and evaluate the performance and behaviour of persons in such relationships." [full-text]
- Point 5 (essential private services): the listed uses are public-assistance eligibility (5(a)), "creditworthiness ... or ... credit score" (5(b)), life and health insurance risk and pricing (5(c)), emergency calls and triage (5(d)). Guest-facing hotel decisions (admission, pricing, ejection, ratings) do not appear in the text of 5(a)-(d). [full-text]
- Point 1(c) "AI systems intended to be used for emotion recognition" (biometrics, "in so far as their use is permitted under relevant Union or national law"). [full-text]

**Article 6(3)** (PDF p. 54): an Annex III system "shall not be considered to be high-risk where it does not pose a significant risk"; but "shall always be considered to be high-risk where the AI system performs profiling of natural persons." [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- Text-only: no interpretation of whether a given hotel system is high-risk beyond what Annex III's words say. Staff-facing systems (4(b)) are the textual hook; guest-facing ones are not named.
- Art. 86(1) excludes Annex III point 2 and requires a decision "by the deployer" with legal or similarly significant effect, so it does not on its face reach every guest-facing refusal.
- Application dates were amended by Reg. (EU) 2026/1744 (see Key facts). The manuscript must not say flatly that the right to explanation 'applies from 2 August 2026' for hotel systems: Art. 86 itself is unamended and in force from that date, but the high-risk classification rules it depends on (Art. 6(2), Annex III) apply from 2 December 2027. Say the right exists for Annex III high-risk systems and give both dates, or avoid a date.
