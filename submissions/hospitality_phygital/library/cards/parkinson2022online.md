## Summary

Case study of an online weight-management forum that became an online third place. Proposes a framework of online third-place characteristics and their effects on eudaimonic and hedonic well-being, with attention to unintended consequences.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "Unintended consequences of service are important yet infrequently examined in transformative service research." (abstract) [abstract]
- Finds that "social factors including social density, equity, and personalization are key to constructing an online third place that supports well-being" (abstract) [abstract]
- The framework helps managers "to identify and manage potential unintended consequences" (abstract) [abstract]
