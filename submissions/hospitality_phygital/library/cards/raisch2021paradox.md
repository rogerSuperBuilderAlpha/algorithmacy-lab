## Summary

Conceptual paper arguing that automation and augmentation cannot be neatly separated: they are interdependent across time and space and form a paradox that organisations must manage.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Defines the terms: "automation implies that machines take over a human task, augmentation means that humans collaborate closely with machines to perform a task" (AMR 46(1), p. 192, abstract) [full-text]
- Thesis: "we argue that, in the management domain, augmentation cannot be neatly separated from automation" (p. 192, abstract) [full-text]
- "These dual AI applications are interdependent across time and space, creating a paradoxical tension." (p. 192, abstract) [full-text]
- Prescription: organisations should adopt "a broader perspective comprising both automation and augmentation" (p. 192, abstract) [full-text]
