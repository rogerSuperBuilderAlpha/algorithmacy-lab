## Summary

Experiment with 179 undergraduates on voice in a goal-setting procedure, given before the goal was set, after it was set, or not at all. Both kinds of voice raised fairness judgments over no voice, with pre-decision voice raising them more.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "perceptions of control account for some, but not all, of the voice-based enhancement of procedural justice" (abstract) [abstract]
