## Summary

A survey of 305 local and foreign hotel guests, analysed by structural equation modelling, that
identifies three generic facets of hotel hospitality across star ratings and tests which predict
guest satisfaction. It is a measurement paper from the host–guest relationship perspective, not a
study of how host and guest negotiate.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Design: 'The responses of 305 local and foreign hotel guests in a questionnaire survey were analysed using a Structural Equation Modelling technique.' (abstract) [abstract]
- Facets: 'The generic facets of hotel hospitality were identified as personalisation, comfort, and warm welcoming.' (abstract) [abstract]
- 'Of the three, only personalisation and warm welcoming contributed significantly to predicting guest satisfaction.' (abstract) [abstract]
