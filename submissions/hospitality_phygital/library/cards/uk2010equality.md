## Summary

Section 29 forbids a service-provider to discriminate against, harass or victimise a person requiring its service, including by not providing it, and imposes a duty to make reasonable adjustments.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "A person (a “service-provider”) concerned with the provision of a service to the public or a section of the public (for payment or not) must not discriminate against a person requiring the service by not providing the person with the service." (s. 29(1)) [full-text]
- "A duty to make reasonable adjustments applies to— (a) a service-provider" (s. 29(7)(a)) [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- This is a ground-limited duty not to refuse, not the innkeeper's duty to receive any fit traveller who can pay. The innkeeper's duty is wider in one way (any ground) and narrower in another (travellers, room available).
- Read with uk1956hotel: the hotel proprietor carries both; a bank or shop carries only s. 29.
