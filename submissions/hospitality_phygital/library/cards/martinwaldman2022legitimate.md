## Summary

Factorial vignette survey on what makes algorithmic decisions seem legitimate. Common procedural mechanisms (notices, impact statements) do not help; an appeal process can create a legitimacy dividend for bad outcomes, unless arbitrary or morally dubious factors were used.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "many of the procedural governance mechanisms in practice today, such as notices and impact statements, do not lead to algorithmic decisions being perceived as more legitimate in general" (abstract) [abstract]
- "robust governance, such as offering an appeal process, can create a legitimacy dividend for decisions with bad outcomes" (abstract) [abstract]
- "when arbitrary or morally dubious factors are used to make decisions, most legitimacy dividends are erased" (abstract) [abstract]
