## Summary

Critical discourse analysis of scholarship, journalism and Airbnb marketing. It shows how scales (home, neighbourhood, city, nation, "anywhere", temporal and digital) are used "to assert power, assign moral responsibility, and make claims to belonging". The platform's digital scale lets Airbnb shed legal responsibility onto hosts and municipalities.
