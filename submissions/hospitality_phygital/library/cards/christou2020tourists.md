## Summary

Qualitative study (78 interviews) of how tourists view anthropomorphic robots. Tourists favour them over other robots yet report frustration, sadness and disappointment about robots in a human-driven industry.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "a total number of 78 interviews with tourists being retained in the study" (abstract) [abstract]
- "tourists favor the use of anthropomorphic robots over any other type of robot" and the use "may result in an overall enhanced experiential value" (abstract) [abstract]
- "informants also expressed frustration, sadness and disappointment vis-à-vis the use of robots in a human-driven industry" (abstract) [abstract]
- Warns that robots "should not give the impression that robots are replacing the human face of the organization" (abstract) [abstract]
