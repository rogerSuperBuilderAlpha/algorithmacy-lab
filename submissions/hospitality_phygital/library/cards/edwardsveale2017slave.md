## Summary

Calls the individual right to an explanation a 'transparency fallacy' and directs attention
towards systemic and ex-ante obligations instead. The third of the paper's three published nulls.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- The core null: explanation rights 'rely too much on individual rights for what are too often group harms' [full-text]
- Warns of 'some danger of research and legislative efforts being devoted to creating rights to a form of transparency that may not be feasible, and may not match user needs' [full-text]
- Extends the same objection to the GDPR's right to erasure and right to data portability, so the null is not specific to explanation [full-text]
