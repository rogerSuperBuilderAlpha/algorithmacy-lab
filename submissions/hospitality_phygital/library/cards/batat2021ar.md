## Summary

A qualitative case study of Le Petit Chef, a 3D video-mapped animated chef projected onto diners' tables. Data are secondary sources, interviews with 15 restaurateurs, and online phenomenological interviews with 20 diners recalling past visits. AR can affect the dining experience "positively or negatively" on five dimensions and can improve food well-being. Novelty wears off, and restaurateurs doubt AR's fit with an already busy social setting.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Design: "interviews with 15 restaurateurs" and "online phenomenological interviews ... with 20 participants who experienced 'Le Petit Chef'" (p. 5); "we did not follow and observe them in the restaurants during their experiences" (p. 11) [full-text]
- The projection's purpose, per the producers: "to make the waiting moments in restaurants more pleasant" (p. 4) [full-text]
- Delight: "the use of AR can generate delight among customers due to positive disconfirmation" (p. 10) [full-text]
- Staff stay present and social: AR "was perceived as an ice-breaker activity that facilitated social interactions among customers ... and staff" (p. 7); diner: "the atmosphere was great, the staff smiling" (p. 8); a diner "asked the waiters, and I ordered all of them" (p. 9) [full-text]
- Device-free and effortless: "no technological efforts were required of guests" (p. 8) [full-text]
- Negatives: restaurateurs were "skeptical because of the cost of the technology and the already highly animated nature of the restaurant experience"; diners find a repeat "boring"; "in some situations, such as a business diner or a romantic diner, the use of AR could be a distraction" (p. 9) [full-text]
- The study says nothing about pricing or booking rules, staff discretion or authority, or whether a guest can decline the projection [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- Diners' accounts are retrospective online interviews recruited from social media and forums (p. 5), not observation. "Rules steady" cannot be read from them.
- An editor-authored worked example remains a good move, provided the paper states its own reading separately from Batat's findings.
