## Summary

Abstract only. A PRISMA-guided systematic review with bibliometric analysis of 162 Scopus-indexed articles on AI governance and digital transformation in hospitality firms, published through August 2026. Parhi reports five thematic areas and proposes an 'ADAPT-H' governance framework of seven pillars, among them Accountability and Ecosystem and Vendor Governance. The abstract names the problem as fragmented "governance mechanisms ensuring accountability, transparency, data stewardship, and responsible innovation". It does not say to whom accountability is owed, which firm bears it, or whether guests have any route to correction. The gap search's full-text claims about franchise and vendor accountability are recorded under Critical notes, unverified.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Problem: "Artificial intelligence (AI) and digital transformation are reshaping hospitality operations, yet governance mechanisms ensuring accountability, transparency, data stewardship, and responsible innovation remain fragmented." (abstract). [abstract]
- Aim: "This study systematically reviews literature on AI governance and digital transformation in hospitality firms and develops a sector-specific governance architecture." (abstract). [abstract]
- Design and corpus: "A systematic literature review was conducted in accordance with PRISMA 2020 principles using Scopus as the sole database, resulting in a final corpus of 162 peer-reviewed articles published through August 2026." - Themes: "Thematic synthesis identifies five core areas: AI and algorithmic governance, data governance and cybersecurity, strategic governance of digital transformation, ethical and responsible AI, and fintech and decentralised governance." (abstract). [abstract]
