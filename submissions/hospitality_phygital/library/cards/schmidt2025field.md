## Summary

A field study in a working hotel. A concierge robot (Temi) stood in the reception area as an
alternative to human staff at the information touchpoint, and guests chose which to use. Two
incognito researchers surveyed guests after the interaction over three weeks (N = 99 and N = 101).
PLS-SEM and multi-group tests found indifferent effects of agent type on the hospitality
experience (inviting, care, comfort), satisfaction and revisit intention.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Self-selection, not random assignment: Temi was "stationed in the reception area, offering guests the choice to interact with it or human staff" (p. 565); "Our findings stem from a field study where the guests could choose between the mode of the service agent" (p. 571) [full-text]
- Sample: "N = 99 and N = 101", power 0.89 for a medium effect (p. 565) [full-text]
- "Our results suggest the indifferent effects of service agents (human vs. concierge robot) on the perceived experience of hospitality" (p. 571) [full-text]
- The authors' explanation is the task: "The nature of the studied touchpoint appears decisive ... By focusing on information provision—a task with clear functional boundaries—we demonstrate how robotic agents can perform effectively in defined operational contexts" (p. 571) [full-text]
- Robots "are not yet ready for all hospitality tasks, such as handling payments or managing check-ins" (p. 572) [full-text]
- Limits: single venue, single touchpoint, 200 participants, "preliminary steps towards generalization" (pp. 572-573) [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- (rr-2026-11) "Field experiment" overstates the design. Guests chose the agent, so the comparison is between self-selected groups.
- Indifference at an information desk is what the paper predicts where the three conditions survive. The source's own reason is the task's "clear functional boundaries" (p. 571), close to but not the same as v4's "little is at stake."
- It also disciplines the paper's use of Kim 2021: guests are not uniformly people-preferring.
