## Summary

Field experiment with 200 guests in a working hotel where a human and a robot were simultaneously available at an information point, finding no difference in hospitality experience, satisfaction or revisit intention.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Field rather than scenario, with both agents concurrently available to real guests [abstract]
- Reports indifference across experience, satisfaction and revisit intention [abstract]

## Critical notes & threats

- Indifference at an information desk is exactly what the paper predicts where the three conditions survive, and saying so turns a null result into evidence for the condition.
- It also disciplines the paper's own use of Kim 2021: guests are not uniformly people-preferring, and the paper should not want them to be.
