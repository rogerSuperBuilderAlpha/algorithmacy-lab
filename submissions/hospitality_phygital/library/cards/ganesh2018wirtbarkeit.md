## Summary

Reads Kant's cosmopolitan right, defined as limited to the conditions of "hospitality" (Wirtbarkeit), by analogy to the Roman and common law of innkeepers, and grounds cosmopolitan right in the innate right to freedom.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "Unlike for ordinary householders, hospitality for innkeepers is a legal obligation, not a matter of philanthropy: they have traditionally been deemed public officials with limited discretion to refuse travelers, and as fiduciaries of their guests strictly liable for losses to their property." (abstract) [abstract]
- Kant "includes “Wirtbarkeit” in brackets, a word that connotes innkeeping" (abstract) [abstract]
