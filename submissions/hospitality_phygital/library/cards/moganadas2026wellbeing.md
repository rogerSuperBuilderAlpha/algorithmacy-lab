## Summary

PRISMA review of 57 articles from 2014 to 2025 on digital transformation and employee well-being, synthesized into five aggregate dimensions, concluding that well-being depends on how intensified demands and available resources are configured and interpreted in context and that prior findings remain fragmented across technologies, disciplines and constructs.
