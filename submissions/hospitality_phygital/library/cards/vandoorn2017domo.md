## Summary

Conceptual article introducing automated social presence (ASP), the extent to which technology makes customers feel the presence of another social entity, with a typology of automated and human social presence in frontlines and a set of propositions.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

## Critical notes & threats

- v4 sentence: "We call AI a tool, a mediator, a facilitator, a co-creator, a form of social presence (Bitner et al. 2000; van Doorn et al. 2017; Li et al. 2021; Xing and Zhang 2026)." SUPPORT: YES for "a form of social presence". Van Doorn is the source of that label. The paper treats technology as a social entity that substitutes for or works alongside the employee, which fits the paper's point that the field names AI by what it does for the service, not by what it owes the guest.
- Service robots and service marketing, not hotels or algorithmic back-ends. The label describes a robot the customer perceives; an algorithm that is never perceived has no automated social presence in this sense.
- Bib vs Crossref: Crossref returns the short title "Domo Arigato Mr. Roboto"; the bib and v4 give the full title as printed. Online 2016, print 2017; bib uses 2017.
