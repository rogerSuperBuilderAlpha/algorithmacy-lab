## Summary

Reads Airbnb as an inhospitable form of institutional and algorithmic governance that constrains
hosts and guests alike in extractive relations, working at the intersection of platform
capitalism, algorithmic governance and tourism dwelling design. 'Guests without hosts' originally
described the blurring of hosting and guesting in network hospitality; the article extends it to
travellers stranded by disaster, unwelcome strangers at borders, and the replacement of
hospitality workers by robots and AI. This is the closest published neighbour to our paper's
question, in our target journal, in the current volume.
