## Summary

Sets out a critical agenda for hospitality management research, positioned between
managerially oriented hospitality management research and social-scientific hospitality studies,
and lists eight challenges for it.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Characterises the dominant tradition: hospitality management research 'is conceived of as essentially pro-business, preoccupied with managerial practice and issues of industry importance' (section 'The development of a critical approach') [full-text]
- Proposes three orientations, hospitality management, critical hospitality management and hospitality studies, with the first and third depicted as partly overlapping (same section, Figure 1) [full-text]
- Hospitality studies 'is broadly concerned with exploring the social, cultural, political and ethical dimensions of hospitality' (same section) [full-text]
- *The Service Industries Journal* 29(10), pp. 1465-1478 [metadata]
