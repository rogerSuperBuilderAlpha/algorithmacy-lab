## Summary

An "Insight & Foresight" essay asking whether hospitality service robots should be afforded
rights and responsibilities, examined through stakeholder theory and moral, ethical and legal
lenses. It surveys moral, ethical and legal motivations, impacts on customers, employees,
management and owners, and proposes a framework and a managerial checklist. Its conclusion is
that the question "is essentially about business governance and risk management", aimed at
clarifying accountability for liability, safety, data protection and consent.

## Critical notes & threats

The full text confirms the v4 reading. Responsibility here is harm, liability and governance (pp. 904, 911, 915), and the welcome is not discussed. One passage sharpens the paper's contrast. Sharma and Mattila propose asking how a firm should "translate" a human employee's responsibilities "into the service robot context" (p. 915). That is the heir question in its plainest form: functions and duties pass from the employee to the machine, and the question of who answers the guest becomes a question of liability.
