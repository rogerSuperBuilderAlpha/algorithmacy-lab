## Summary

Finds guests broadly positive toward service robots and still preferring human staff, particularly
where emotional dimensions and service failure are involved.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- 'Hospitableness is premised on service offered by humans and it mainly relies on emotional treatment by human staff' [full-text]
