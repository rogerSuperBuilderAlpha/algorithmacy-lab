Outline numbering (rr-2026-11-h): §2.3.

## Summary

Implements the EU Payment Accounts Directive in the UK: non-discrimination in access to payment accounts and the features of a "payment account with basic features".

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- "A credit institution must not discriminate against consumers legally resident in the [United Kingdom] ... when those consumers apply for or access a payment account." (reg. 18(1), list of grounds elided; bracket as amended by SI 2019/661) [full-text]
- "The conditions applicable to holding a payment account with basic features must not be discriminatory." (reg. 18(2)) [full-text]
- A basic account allows consumers to "open, operate and close the account" (reg. 19(1)(a)) [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- Banks are not common callings at common law (wyman1904law, p. 156: private callings may "sell or not as one pleases"), but regs. 18-19 forbid discrimination in access to payment accounts and fix the features of a basic account. So "a duty that applies before any harm" is not unique to hospitality, as R2.4 suspects.
- I did not read the regulations that make the offer of a basic account compulsory; do not claim a bank "duty to receive" from regs. 18-19 alone.
