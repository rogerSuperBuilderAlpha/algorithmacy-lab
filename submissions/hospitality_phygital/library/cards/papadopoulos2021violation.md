## Summary

Interview study of UK hotel workers showing why they do not enforce employment rights: organisational, institutional and individual factors make silence the dominant response.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Abstract: the article "discusses the organizational, institutional and individual factors making silence dominant in the sector" (PDF p. 1) [full-text]

- Context of "heightened levels of fear"; for precarious workers the UK individual-rights model has "no substance" (PDF p. 1, abstract) [full-text]
- Names the channels of silencing as institutional (weak unions, fragmented sector) and organisational (employer practices) (PDF p. 10, discussion) [full-text]
