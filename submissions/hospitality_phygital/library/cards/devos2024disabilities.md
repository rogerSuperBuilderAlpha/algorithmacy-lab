## Summary

Uses the psycho-emotional model of disability to study students with disabilities in an online MBA, producing a typology of enchantment, re-enchantment and disenchantment experiences and identifying enabling and disabling factors that lie outside the curriculum.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Typology has three states: enchantment, re-enchantment and disenchantment [abstract]

- The setting is a digitally mediated service, which is what makes the typology transferable [abstract]

## Critical notes & threats

- Re-enchantment is the valuable term. A positive/negative/neutral scheme has no name for the recovered case, and recovery is what the paper's affordances aim at.
