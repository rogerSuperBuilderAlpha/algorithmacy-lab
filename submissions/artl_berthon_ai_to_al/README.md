# *Artificial Life*: AI to AL (Berthon)

[DRAFTED by Claude, 6 Nov 2026.]

Berthon's paper asks when a human-supported lineage of machine-learning models becomes autonomous
enough to count as artificial life. It answers with reproductive-work accounting and a five-level
framework, from artefact to self-maintaining lineage. The target venue is *Artificial Life* (MIT
Press). This arm revises the paper toward that journal's standard, with Dorin & Stepney (2024),
"What Is Artificial Life Today, and Where Should It Go?" (*Artificial Life* 30(1)), as the model.
