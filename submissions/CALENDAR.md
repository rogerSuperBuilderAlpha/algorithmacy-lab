## OS/OT PDW Lima — LIMA2026-002 (accepted)

Paper Development Workshop in Organization Studies for Advanced PhD Students and Early Career
Scholars, **Universidad de Piura (UDEP), Lima, Peru, 5–7 October 2026**. Contact: Sophia, managing
editor, sophia@locus23.com. Dashboard/registration: https://locus23.com/wp/register-for-conference/

**Accepted title.** *Algorithmacy: A communication competency construct for triadic mediated
coordination, with empirical evidence from a Caribbean AI engineering cohort.*

**Terms.** ~~Confirm by registering by 2026-08-15~~ — **registered 2026-08-14, the place is
confirmed.** Full paper **2026-09-10**. Workshop is free and
feeds participants; travel and accommodation are the author's. Accepting commits him to attending in
person throughout **and to reviewing the manuscripts assigned to his roundtable** — a second,
unscheduled workload that lands somewhere between the September submission and the October workshop.

**The submitted package is `~/Downloads/OS-OT-Paper-Development-Workshop.pdf`** (saved 2026-07-28):
motivation letter plus the extended abstract. It is not in this repo and has no project folder here.
An earlier, purely theoretical version of the same application — addressed to a **Dublin** October 2026
PDW, no empirical arm — sits in Google Drive as "OS & OT Paper Development Workshop" (21 May 2026).

**Empirical site: GauntleTT**, an eight-week intensive AI engineering program in Trinidad and Tobago
that the author co-designed and manages with the Trinidad government and several NGOs. The abstract
commits to a three-wave panel across multiple GauntleTT cohorts — surveys at intake (W1), midpoint
(W4), and exit (W8) — testing factor structure, measurement invariance across waves and cohorts, and
discriminant validity against algorithm sensemaking and AI literacy, with predictive validity against
program operations data (weekly deliverable scores, peer evaluations, placements). It states IRB
approval was obtained a month before writing.

**Two open items this raises for the repo, not for the editors.**

1. **GauntleTT is not the field site the repo pre-registered.**
   [`../../org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/`](../../org_frontier/survey/cohort_algorithmacy/) develops the same Algorithmacy
   Competence Scale but registers **Hult, Fall 2026, sixteen weeks, W3 at week 16**. Same instrument,
   different population and a different wave schedule. Which one backs the Lima paper decides whether
   the pre-registration covers it.
2. **The abstract still calls co-optation "a fourth coordination mechanism."** The lab retired that
   claim: `coordinative_sovereignty/research/research_database.md:23` marks **H5 REFRAME** — not a new
   logic in Powell's sense, the novelty being the governance challenge of opaque interested mediation.
   The IGI chapter argues the corrected version. A roundtable reading both will find the seam.
