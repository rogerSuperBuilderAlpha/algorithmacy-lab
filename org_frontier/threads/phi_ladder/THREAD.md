# Thread — integration is quantized: triads land on a short ladder of Phi values

A prior for the catalog. Integration does not take a continuum of values. Across random three-party
coordinations the integrated information of the committing forms clusters on a short ladder of recurring
values, with gaps between the rungs. Ninety percent of triadic forms sit on one of four values — 0.415,
0.830, 1.000, 2.000 — and only a tenth fall off the ladder. The amount of coordination a triad carries is
quantized. Reproduce with `python org_frontier/threads/phi_ladder/phi_ladder.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

Three-party forms drawn with random rules, classified, and for each triadic form the maximum Φ across
reachable states is taken and tested against four candidate ladder values. The measure is how many forms sit
exactly on each rung and how many fall between.

## The arc

**Most triads sit on four rungs.** Of 1659 triadic forms, 616 carry exactly Φ = 0.415, 506 carry 0.830, 127
carry 1.000, and 251 carry 2.000 — 37%, 31%, 8% and 15%. Ninety percent of the triads land on one of these
four values. The integrated information of a random three-party coordination is not spread smoothly across a
range; it falls almost always on one of a few exact figures.

**The gaps are as real as the rungs.** Between the rungs there is little. The values 0.415 and 0.830 are the
program's documented coordination equilibria, and forms cluster on them and on the integer values 1.0 and
2.0, while the space between is nearly empty, only a tenth of forms off the ladder. The quantization is a
fact about how three Boolean parties can be irreducible: only certain partitions and certain symmetries are
available, and each fixes Φ to one of a few values.

## What the thread establishes

The integration a three-party coordination carries is quantized, landing on a short ladder — 0.415, 0.830,
1.0, 2.0 — in 90% of triadic forms, with the rungs the program's coordination equilibria and the integer
ceilings. As a prior for reading real coordination: the strength of an irreducible arrangement should not be
expected to vary continuously, but to sit near one of a few characteristic levels, and a measured value far
from any rung is a sign the model is off or the arrangement is unusual. The bottleneck and credit findings
of the other threads hold whatever the rung; this one says which rungs there are.

## Limits, honestly

The four ladder values are the ones the program documented for three-party forms, and the off-ladder tenth
includes a few forms above the Φ = 2 mediated ceiling, which other topologies can exceed; the ladder is a
property of this three-node population rather than a universal law. The rates are over random rules at one
seed. The quantization restates the program's Φ-ladder finding as a registered baseline. Everything is
in-silico, and a prior is to be tested against data.
