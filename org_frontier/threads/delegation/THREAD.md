# Thread — delegation moves standing from the worker to its agent

A prior for the catalog. A worker reaches the platform through an agent, a chain of worker, agent, platform
and counterpart, each pair coupled both ways. The question is whether the worker keeps its place in the
coordination or the agent takes it. The agent takes it. In the delegated chain the agent sits in the major
complex nearly twice as often as the worker it acts for, and the worker becomes the least central of the
four. Delegation moves standing to the party that stands between the worker and the platform. Reproduce with
`python org_frontier/threads/delegation/delegation.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

Four parties in a line: worker, agent, platform, counterpart, with each adjacent pair reading one another.
The worker reaches the platform only through the agent. Rules are random. The measure is how often each role
is in the major complex, over the forms with a defined complex.

## The arc

**The worker is the least central party.** Of 314 usable forms the worker is in the major complex in 103,
the agent in 185, the platform in 231, and the counterpart in 148. The worker, having handed its dealings to
an agent, is in the core least often of the four. Delegation does not keep the worker at the center of the
coordination; it moves it to the edge.

**The agent inherits and exceeds the worker's place.** The agent is in the core in 185 forms against the
worker's 103, nearly twice as often. The party hired to act for the worker becomes a more frequent member of
the irreducible coordination than the worker itself. Standing follows position, and the agent's position,
between the worker and the platform, is the more central one. The platform, at the chain's other hinge, is
the most central of all.

## What the thread establishes

Delegation moves standing from the worker to its agent. A worker that reaches the platform through an agent
is the least central party in the coordination, and the agent it acts through is in the core nearly twice as
often. As a prior for reading real coordination: a party that works through a representative should be
expected to hold a smaller place in the irreducible arrangement than the representative does, and the act of
delegating, far from extending the worker's reach, cedes its centrality to the agent who stands between it
and the system.

## Limits, honestly

Membership rates are over the forms with a defined complex; the chain's veto structure is scattered, as the
earlier series-of-mediators scout found chains decompose, so the load-bearing comparison is the per-role
membership rather than a single bottleneck. The architecture is one delegation chain with random rules at one
seed, four nodes, a registered baseline. Everything is in-silico, and a prior is to be tested against data.
