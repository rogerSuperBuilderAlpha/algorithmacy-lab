# Thread — the empty core is a property of the random sample, not of coordination

The committee that reviewed the cooperative-game synthesis pressed one objection above the rest. Every
headline rate — the core empty in 96% of forms, φ_s subadditive in 99% — was a frequency over random Boolean
truth tables, and a random truth table is far from a coordination. A prior study, q122, had already found the
value function well-behaved on the structured forms the dissertation uses. This thread runs the two pathology
rates — the empty core, and the dilution that empties it — on structured coordination forms, and they
reverse. On the strict-mediation mediated triad the core is non-empty in every triadic form and no pair
out-values the whole. The contestability the core-stability thread reported is an artifact of the random
sample. Reproduce with `python org_frontier/threads/structured_forms/structured_forms.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

The strict-mediation family is the architecture the program models a mediated triad with: the mediator reads
the two outer parties, each outer party reads the mediator, and the outer parties are connected only through
it. The update rules are drawn at random, so whether a form commits is open, but the wiring encodes the
coordination structure, with only the rules left to chance. The two rates to test are whether a stable
split of the integration credit exists — a non-empty core — and whether a pair out-values the whole, the
subadditive dilution the core-stability thread found empties the core in the random population. The curated
library of named, documented coordination forms gives an interpretable cross-check.

## The arc

**On structured mediated triads the core is never empty.** Over the triadic forms in the strict-mediation
family, the core is non-empty in every one, 110 of 110, against the 4% non-empty of the random population.
A stable division of the integration credit exists for each. The integration credit has a stable split after
all, on the forms that are actually mediated triads.

**The dilution that empties the random core is absent here.** No pair out-values the whole in any of the 110
forms, against 66% in the random population. The reason is structural: a strict-mediation triad that commits
binds all three parties into the major complex, a full triad, so the whole is the most integrated coalition
and no pair can exceed it. The dilution that drives the random empty core is a feature of forms whose major
complex is a proper subset, which the committed mediated triad is not.

**The named forms confirm it, with one telling exception.** Of the three triadic forms in the curated
library, two — the strict ATS bottleneck and the two-sided match, both the canonical S = W ∧ C triad — have
a non-empty core and pay the mediator a 0.67 share. The third, the false-dyad rideshare form, has an empty
core despite no dilution. Its counterpart reads itself as well as the mediator, so it carries intrinsic
activity, and that standalone worth makes the party's individual-rationality demand bite, emptying the core
through balancedness alone. A party with a life of its own can reintroduce the
instability the pure mediated triad does not have.

## What the thread establishes

The empty core and the subadditive dilution are properties of the random Boolean population, not of
coordination. On the strict-mediation mediated triad — the structure the dissertation models — the core is
non-empty in every triadic form and no pair out-values the whole, the reverse of the random rates. This
revises the core-stability thread: its "the integration credit has no stable split" holds for random forms
and fails for structured mediated triads, where the credit splits stably and the mediator takes a little
over half. The committee's objection was correct, and answering it makes the cluster's claim narrower and
truer: the dramatic pathology rates describe a coverage sample, and the coordinations the program is about
sit in the well-behaved corner of it.

## Limits, honestly

The 100% non-empty core holds for the strict-mediation family as generated here, where each outer party
reads only the mediator and so carries no standalone worth; the curated false-dyad form shows that a party
with intrinsic activity can empty the core through individual rationality even without dilution, so the
clean result is specific to the pure mediated triad. The curated library is nine forms, three of them
triadic, an interpretable anchor more than a population. The structured family is still random in its
rules and Boolean in its parties, so "structured" means a coordination architecture, not real organizational
data, and the bridge to organizations is still unbuilt. The result is the one the committee asked for: the
cluster's headline rates are sampling artifacts, and on the forms the dissertation theorizes the credit has
a stable split.
