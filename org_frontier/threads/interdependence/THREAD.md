# Thread — only reciprocal interdependence binds; pooled and sequential carry no integration

A prior for the catalog. Thompson's typology of interdependence — pooled, sequential, reciprocal — read
through exact Φ. Only reciprocal interdependence binds. Parties that contribute independently to a shared
mediator, or that pass work one way down a line, produce no integration at all. Only when the parties and
the mediator read one another both ways does the coordination commit. The classic ordinal typology comes out
binary here. Reproduce with `python org_frontier/threads/interdependence/interdependence.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

Three forms with random rules, each wiring one of Thompson's interdependence types. Pooled: the parties run
on their own and the mediator reads both, with no feedback to the parties. Sequential: a feedforward line,
the worker to the mediator to the counterpart. Reciprocal: the parties and the mediator read one another both
ways, the bidirectional star. The measure is whether the form integrates at all and whether it commits.

## The arc

**Pooled interdependence carries no integration.** With the parties autonomous and the mediator merely
reading them, the form integrates in none of the 600 draws and commits in none. Parties that share a
mediator without being shaped by it are not coupled into a structure: each runs independently, the mediator
watches, and there is nothing irreducible to find.

**Sequential interdependence carries none either.** A feedforward line, worker to mediator to counterpart,
integrates in none of the 600 draws and commits in none. Work passed one way leaves no loop, and without a
loop there is no irreducible cause-effect structure. The one-directional chain is, for integration, the same
as no coupling at all.

**Reciprocal interdependence is the only one that binds.** With the parties and the mediator reading one
another both ways, the form integrates in 252 of 600 and commits in 62, 10%. Only mutual coupling produces a
loop through which the parties hold one another to a determination, and only there does the coordination read
as irreducible.

## What the thread establishes

Of Thompson's three interdependence types only the reciprocal carries integration; the pooled and the
sequential carry none. The ordinal typology — pooled the weakest, reciprocal the strongest — comes out as a
sharp line through exact Φ: reciprocal commits and the other two never integrate at all. As a prior for
reading real coordination: an arrangement should read as an irreducible whole only where the parties act on
one another both ways, and arrangements of the pooled or sequential kind, however coordinated in the
ordinary sense, should not read as irreducible. This is the program's bidirectionality requirement in
Thompson's vocabulary.

## Limits, honestly

Each type is one wiring with random rules, a representative rather than a population; the pooled and
sequential zero is robust, and the reciprocal rate is the strict-star figure of the other threads. The
result is the bidirectionality requirement restated through a recognized typology. Random rules at one seed,
three nodes, a registered baseline. Everything is in-silico, and a prior is to be tested against data.
