# Thread — the named coordination forms, read through the cooperative game

A reference for the catalog. The catalog's priors are drawn from random forms; this thread grounds them in
the program's curated, documented coordination forms. For each named form it reads the verdict, the veto
player, and the mediator's share of the credit. The pattern is the catalog's. The committing forms put the
veto on the mediator and pay it about two thirds; the factoring forms scatter the bottleneck onto the
worker-system pair, onto a restored worker-counterpart channel, or nowhere. Reproduce with
`python org_frontier/threads/canonical_reference/canonical_reference.py`.

## Setup

The curated library of named forms — a chat dyad, a dyadic gig model, the strict ATS bottleneck, an ATS
form with feedback that factors, the false-dyad rideshare, a pure relay, the two-sided match, and a hierarchy
with a back-channel. Each is taken to its verdict, its veto set, and the mediator's Shapley share. The forms
are fixed, so the table is exact.

## The arc

**The committing forms put the veto on the mediator and pay it two thirds.** The three triadic forms — the
strict ATS bottleneck, the false-dyad rideshare, and the two-sided match — each have the mediator alone as
the veto player, and the mediator takes a credit share of 0.67, 0.58 and 0.67. A named, recognizable
coordination that commits is a strict-mediation triad whose platform holds the bottleneck and the larger part
of the credit, exactly the catalog's monotone-mediator profile.

**The factoring forms scatter the bottleneck.** The dyadic forms place it differently. The chat dyad and the
dyadic gig model leave the worker-system pair as the veto set, the two-party loop that carries them. The ATS
feedback form keeps the mediator as the veto player but factors anyway, a committed construction that does not
produce irreducibility. The pure relay has no veto player at all, a broadcast that integrates nothing. The
hierarchy with a back-channel moves the veto to the worker-counterpart pair, the direct channel the
disintermediation thread showed dissolves the mediator's hold.

## What the thread establishes

The named coordination forms read through the cooperative game as the catalog's priors predict. The
committing ones are strict-mediation triads with the mediator as veto player and a two-thirds credit share;
the factoring ones scatter the bottleneck according to their wiring — onto the worker-system loop, the
worker-counterpart back-channel, or nothing. As a reference for the catalog: the abstract priors and the
documented forms agree, and this table is where a reader can see the priors instantiated on coordination
models built from real cases rather than random draws.

## Limits, honestly

The library is eight named forms, an interpretable anchor and not a population, and two of them share a
coupling, so the table is a worked reference rather than a measured rate. The verdicts and veto sets restate
the corpus and classifier results; what this thread adds is the mediator's credit share and the side-by-side
cooperative-game reading. Everything is in-silico, and a prior is to be tested against data.
