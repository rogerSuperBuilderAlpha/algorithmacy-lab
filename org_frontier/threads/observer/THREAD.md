# Thread — an observer is never in the core; a member must act, not only watch

A prior for the catalog. A coordination often has an audience — a party that watches but is not watched,
reads the others but is read by none. This thread asks whether such an observer is part of the
coordination's core. It is not. A party with inputs and no outputs has no causal effect on the others, so it
cannot enter the major complex however closely it reads them. A party joins the core only when it is read as
well as reads. Reproduce with `python org_frontier/threads/observer/observer.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

A fourth party P is placed around the W–S–C triad. In the first architecture P observes the mediator,
reading S, and is read by no one. In the second P reads the mediator and is read by the counterpart, so it
also acts on the coordination. Rules are random. The measures are whether the mediator stays the veto
player and whether P is in the major complex.

## The arc

**The observer is never in the core.** When P reads the mediator but is read by no one, it is in the major
complex in none of the 164 usable forms, 0 of 164, and the mediator remains the veto player in every
integrating form. A party that only watches has no output, so it makes no difference to any other party's
next state, and a party that makes no difference cannot be part of an irreducible cause-effect structure.
The audience sits outside the coordination it watches.

**A party that acts can join.** When P is read by the counterpart as well as reading the mediator, it enters
the major complex in 49 of 203 forms, about a quarter. The same party, now with an output, is a candidate
member, bound in whenever its action matters to the irreducible structure. The mediator keeps its veto here
too. What moved P from outside to a possible member was not how much it reads but whether it is read.

## What the thread establishes

Membership in a coordination's core requires acting, not only watching. A party with inputs and no outputs —
an observer, an audience, an auditor who only reads — is never in the major complex, while a party that is
also read can join. As a prior for reading real coordination: a party that only receives, never acted upon
by the others, should be outside the irreducible core no matter how much information flows to it, and a
party becomes a candidate member exactly when its own state feeds back into the arrangement.

## Limits, honestly

The exclusion of an output-less party is a consequence of the way integrated information counts only nodes
with both a cause and an effect, so the direction is principled and the value is the clean contrast: the
same party reads the same mediator in both architectures, and only the one that is also read can join. The
quarter-of-forms membership in the acting case is over random rules at one seed, four nodes, a registered
baseline. Everything is in-silico, and a prior is to be tested against data.
