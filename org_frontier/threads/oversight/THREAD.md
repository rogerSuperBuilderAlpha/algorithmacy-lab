# Thread — oversight joins the top; it does not break the bottleneck or reach the parties

A prior for the catalog. Place a principal over the mediator — a regulator, an owner, a board — coupled to
it both ways, and ask what oversight does to a mediated coordination. It does not break the mediator's
bottleneck. The mediator stays the veto player in every integrating form. The principal joins the core,
becomes a co-bottleneck in some forms, and takes a substantial share of the credit, while the mediator's own
share rises and the two parties are squeezed toward nothing. Oversight joins the top of the arrangement.
Reproduce with `python org_frontier/threads/oversight/oversight.py` (seed 11).

## Setup

The W–S–C triad with a fourth party P over the mediator. In the first architecture P only observes the
mediator, reading S and read by no one. In the second the mediator heeds P and P heeds S, a two-way coupling
between the principal and the mediator. Rules are random. The measures are whether the mediator and the
principal are veto players, whether the principal is in the major complex, and each one's share of the
credit.

## The arc

**An observing principal is outside, as any observer is.** When P only reads the mediator it is in the major
complex in none of the forms, and is never a veto player, while the mediator is the veto player in every
integrating form. A principal who only watches the mediator is an audience, outside the coordination, the
observer thread's result in a new costume.

**A coupled principal joins the top.** When the mediator heeds the principal and the principal heeds the
mediator, P enters the major complex in 184 of 350 forms, just over half, and is a co-veto-player alongside
the mediator in 53 of 255 integrating forms. The mediator stays the veto player in every one of them, 255 of
255. Oversight does not displace the bottleneck. It adds a second party at the top, indispensable in some
forms, holding the arrangement jointly with the mediator in others.

**The credit moves up, not down.** With the principal coupled in, the mediator's credit share rises to 0.81,
above the 0.55 of the bare triad, and the principal takes 0.26, so the mediator and the principal together
hold more than the whole is worth and the two parties are left with a negative remainder. The oversight
layer enriches the structure and the credit it adds accrues to the top, to the mediator and the principal,
while the workers and counterparts the coordination is supposedly about are squeezed toward nothing.

## What the thread establishes

Adding an oversight layer over a mediator does not break the mediator's bottleneck and does not redistribute
to the parties. The mediator stays the veto player; the principal, if coupled rather than merely observing,
joins the core and the credit at the top, raising the mediator's share and taking a large one of its own,
while the parties are squeezed. As a prior for reading real coordination: a regulator or owner placed over a
platform, and genuinely coupled to it, should read as a second party at the top of the irreducible core,
not as relief for the parties below — and one that only observes should read as outside the coordination
entirely.

## Limits, honestly

The observing-principal result is the observer thread's, restated for a node over the mediator, so the
load-bearing finding is the coupled case: the principal joining the core, the co-veto in a fifth of forms,
and the credit moving up. The shares above one follow from the parties' negative Shapley in a non-monotone
game. Rates are over random rules at one seed, four nodes, a registered baseline. Everything is in-silico,
and a prior is to be tested against data.
