# Coordinative sovereignty — autonomy for a coordinated world

A new research project. In a platform-mediated world, coordination runs through third parties that set the
terms, and the old response — reclaim independence, refuse to be mediated — fails, because the mediator often
cannot be removed without dissolving the coordination itself. Trying to preserve the sovereignty of
independence leaves a coordinated actor less sovereign, not more. This project develops the alternative:
**coordinative sovereignty**, the autonomy proper to being irreducibly coordinated — standing and effective
voice within a mediation one cannot leave, rather than independence from it or control over it.

The project is distinct from **digital sovereignty**, which reclaims state or community control over digital
infrastructure. Digital sovereignty asks who owns the infrastructure; coordinative sovereignty asks what
autonomy the actor coordinating through it can have. It builds on the relational line in political theory
(Pettit's non-domination, Nedelsky's relational autonomy, Ostrom's commons governance, Hirschman's voice) and
on the lab's formal work on mediated coordination: the necessary/contingent test (q213) is a diagnostic for
which sovereignty is even available — exit where a gate is contingent, voice where a mediator is necessary.

## Status

Kickoff. The framing is the opening hypothesis, grounded in the literature and in the lab's mediated-coordination
apparatus, and the agenda is the program to build it out. No computational results yet; the first operational
question (agenda item 1) is whether a coordinated actor's standing can be read as its non-substitutable
membership in the irreducible core it is coordinated through.

## Relation to the rest of the lab

Coordinative sovereignty is the political form of algorithmacy. Algorithmacy is the competence to read that one
is coordinated through a system and which party the system binds; coordinative sovereignty is the standing to
act on it. The contingency catalog, the dual-function decomposition, and the disintermediation forecast already
sort real arrangements into the exit-available and voice-only cases the construct turns on.
