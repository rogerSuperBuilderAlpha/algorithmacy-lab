# Coordinative sovereignty — research agenda

Open questions, in rough order of priority. Each is a piece of the program the framing statement opens
(`coordinative_sovereignty.md`). The lab's asset is the formal apparatus for mediated coordination; the agenda
puts it to work on a political construct.

**Progress.** The chapter (`chapter/`) and its research passes (`research/`) cover much of this agenda. Item 1 is
addressed by the operationalization in `instruments/`: `formal_standing.md` reads standing as a coordinated
actor's Shapley pivotality and core membership in the integrated-information game, and
`coordinative_sovereignty_instrument.md` gives the survey construct, with a predicted correspondence between the
two. Item 4 (worked cases) is done in `research/worked_cases.md`. Item 5 (institutional forms) is chapter §7.
Item 8 (analogy modesty) is honored throughout `formal_standing.md`. Items 2, 3, 6, and 7 remain open.

1. **Operationalize voice under irreducible coordination.** The necessary/contingent test (q213) says when only
   voice is available. What makes voice *effective* inside a mediation that cannot be exited? Candidate formal
   handle: a coordinated actor's standing is its membership and pivotality in the irreducible core (q98,
   Shapley-membership). Ask whether coordinative sovereignty can be read as a party holding non-trivial,
   non-substitutable membership in the core it is coordinated through, and whether a design that raises that
   membership raises the actor's sovereignty.

2. **A clean distinction map.** Build the rigorous comparison: coordinative sovereignty against national/
   Westphalian, popular/democratic, individual (Kant/Mill), republican non-domination, relational autonomy, and
   each digital-sovereignty variant. The axes from the deep-research report — preserve-vs-constitute,
   state-vs-actor, control-vs-voice, exit-vs-voice — are the columns. The deliverable is a table plus the prose
   that defends each placement.

3. **Formalize the mediator's arbitrary power.** Non-domination turns on *arbitrary* power. The lab already
   models interested mediators and their rent (the interested-third-party and political-economy-of-mediation
   essays; Shapley rent). Connect: is a mediator's arbitrariness measurable as the rent it can extract without
   losing the coordination, and is coordinative sovereignty the constraint of that rent by the coordinated
   parties' standing?

4. **Worked cases.** Take the gig driver, the app developer, the marketplace seller, and the citizen in a feed.
   For each: classify the mediator (necessary / contingent / partial) with the catalog method, diagnose whether
   the situation calls for exit or voice, and name the coordinative-sovereignty move that fits. The app store is
   a partial case (a necessary distribution service plus a contingent payment gate), so the developer's
   sovereignty strategy differs by function — the dual-function decomposition applies directly.

5. **Institutional forms.** Which institutions realize coordinative sovereignty? Data trusts and cooperatives,
   platform works councils, interoperability mandates, oversight boards, Ostrom-style commons governance for a
   shared mediation. Assess each as a mechanism for effective voice under irreducible coordination, and ask
   which of Ostrom's design principles transfer from a shared resource to a shared mediator.

6. **The normative core.** Defend the project's central claim: that coordinative sovereignty leaves actors more
   sovereign than the preservation of independence does. The argument runs through the exit/voice hinge — where
   exit is foreclosed, effort spent demanding it is sovereignty forgone — but it needs to be made carefully,
   including the cases where demanding exit is right because the gate is contingent.

7. **A decision procedure.** From the above, a usable rule: given a coordinated actor and its mediator, when is
   the sovereign move to open the bypass (contingent gate, exit available) and when is it to build voice
   (necessary mediator, only voice available)? The contingency margin and the durability forecast already give
   most of the inputs.

8. **Where the analogy to the lab's Φ work is load-bearing and where it is only suggestive.** The mediated-triad
   apparatus is exact about coordination structure and silent about normative standing. The agenda must keep
   clear which claims the formal instrument supports and which are political-theoretic and argued, so the
   construct is grounded without overclaiming.
