# Editorial pass — prose, flow, and revision seams

A full editorial read of the chapter after the three research passes: an objective tic/style scan, a close read,
and an independent reviewer (general-purpose agent) tasked to be a tough editor. The evidence base was left
alone; this pass worked the prose and the argument's seams.

## Fixed

- **§6 near-verbatim duplicate (highest-value cut).** The "functional sovereignty / platform sovereign … the
  same relationship seen from the other end" point was stated twice, ~24 lines apart, same citations. Cut the
  second instance (the weaker "abstract's third category" paragraph), keeping a trimmed capstone.
- **The §3 co-optation seam.** The reframe had left the chapter saying platform enrollment "departs from"
  co-optation, then calling the phenomenon co-optation. Resolved by making the platform case a harder *variant*
  of Selznick's co-optation (the enrolled gain no seat, where Selznick's gained a limited one), so one term is
  used consistently.
- **§3-intro / §3.1 overlap.** §3.1's second paragraph repeated the reframed intro's market/hierarchy/triad
  contrast and re-declared "what makes the platform situation new." Trimmed to the interested-mediator point the
  subsection needs.
- **The promissory §5 diagnostic (top reviewer risk).** "One can compute whether it factors" read as if shown
  in the chapter; softened to attribute the computation to the companion work and to gloss "integrated-
  information measures" (how much of a system's joint behavior is lost under its least damaging split), keeping
  the diagnostic foregrounded while being modest about what this chapter demonstrates.
- **Antithesis at the high-visibility sentences.** The two definition sentences and the two closing sentences
  led with stacked "not X, but Y / neither-nor" scaffolding; rewritten to lead with the positive claim and carry
  the contrast after, per house style. Redundant "the situation is new" restatements (3–4×) cut to the two that
  earn their place.
- **Softened over-claims.** The abstract's "fourth situation" (which echoed the "fourth mechanism" reading §3
  disavows) to "a situation the prior sensibilities cannot govern"; §8's "answer the volume's invitation to
  measure" to "take up," since nothing is measured yet.

Net: body trimmed ~250 words (to ~10,340; ~11,590 with references, still over the 10,000 minimum), duplicate
phrases eliminated, every citation still resolves both ways.

## Flagged for the authors (argument-level, not editorial)

These are judgment calls better made by the authors than changed in an editorial pass:

- **The "partial" cell vs. the exit/voice binary.** Most real functions are mixes, and the clean managerial
  decision procedure is sharpest when functions sort to the two poles. Worth a sentence on how an actor acts on
  a *partial* verdict (contest the bypassable part, accept the integrating part).
- **What the oracy/literacy scaffolding buys.** After conceding the strong medium-determinism thesis was refuted
  and retreating to the weaker co-constitution claim, state explicitly what the historical frame adds that a
  direct account of opacity-plus-non-exit would not.
- **§7 is a survey.** It catalogs institutions each with a limit and asserts a "cumulative standing" without a
  case showing the stack at work; consider retitling to signal a survey, or adding one integrated illustration.
- **One example, three passes.** The App Store case appears in the abstract, §5.4, and §8; consider varying the
  platform across sections.
