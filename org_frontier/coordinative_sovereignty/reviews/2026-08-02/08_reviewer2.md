## PART 1 — Theory and structure, §1–§10

Findings ranked by damage within each section; global ranking in Verdict.

### §1 Introduction

The opening still works. Five instruments, three helpless actors, Berlin and Anderson on why independence is the wrong demand — concrete and load-bearing. Sovereignty-as-specification (ll. 69–80) holds.

**1.1 (moderate) — "decides" / abstract promissory force.** Abstract ll. 12–14: "A counterfactual sorts the mediators an actor can leave from those it can only govern, and the sorting decides which institutions are worth building." Same verb at §5.3 l. 482. Nothing in the chapter runs a counterfactual on a modeled system; every application is reasoned illustration. "Decides" is demonstrated-result language. Soften to "indicates" / "sorts which institutions are candidates."

No reopening of the old "exactly" / "computable" charges; those words are gone.

### §2 Sovereignty and its sensibilities

Clean for this lens. Oracy now carries authorized standing rather than raw speech (ll. 100–103) — the v2 fix holds. Digital sovereignty nested inside literacy (ll. 144–148) and the change-in-kind deferred to §4 is coherent enough that the old §2.3/§4 spine trap no longer kills the architecture. Move on.

### §3 The platform turn

Still the chapter's strongest empirical-theoretic section. Logic / topology / conjunction separation (ll. 173–192) is honest. Selznick narrowing (ll. 194–201) earns its keep. §3.3 rent argument is sourced.

**3.1 (moderate, recurring) — §3.2 still does not need §5.** Opacity, adaptivity, no channel, no leverage, literate modality (ll. 219–258) make the case for standing without irreducibility or bypass. The reader is convinced before meeting the diagnostic, then meets an apparatus aimed at a different question. One bridging sentence at the top of §5 remains unpaid: why does whether-the-mediator-survives-an-open-tie matter for domination, given that §3.2 already established opacity-plus-asymmetry? Without it, §5 looks like a second argument for the same conclusion rather than the load-bearing one.

### §4 Algorithmacy

Strong constructive section. DeVito concession and triad-indexed novelty (ll. 305–327) hold. Seller vignette earns length. Uneven distribution of competence (ll. 352–362) remains the chapter's best political claim and does not depend on §5.

Translational "render one's own conduct into the terms the system reads" (ll. 301–302) is still what keyword optimization and résumé-writing always were. The vignette's force is the *joint* management of buyer and system (Curchod), which is the triad — say that the translational part is novel only when exercised on the counterpart-under-scoring, and demote freestanding "render into system terms" as the differentiator.

### §5 The diagnostic — where the hostile read still lands

This is the press.

**5.1 (most damaging residual) — make-or-buy charge morphs; it does not die.**

Credit what the rebuild earned. The chapter now:

- separates factoring (§5.1) from bypass (§5.2);
- demonstrates node-vs-edge divergence on a **platform** case (App Store payment gate, ll. 452–458), not only the car dealer;
- names Burt / Williamson / essential facilities as node operations and claims the bypass operates on an edge (ll. 434–441);
- cites ancillary-restraints as the nearer relative (ll. 460–471).

That answers the prior charge *as stated* (node-substitutability / delete-the-mediator). A fair referee must mark the answer. A hostile one presses what the answer leaves:

**(a) Edge-restoration is still qualitative integrating-work judgment.** Every verdict in these pages — clearinghouse, dealer, App Store gate, OTA parity, OTA aggregation — is reached by asking whether the function does work the parties could not do for themselves under an open tie. No integrated-information quantity is computed. Lines 395–397 concede it: "The companion paper develops integrated-information measures... The argument here needs only the runnable counterfactual of the next section." If the counterfactual alone is enough, this chapter's diagnostic *is* the counterfactual — edge-substitutability / value-of-integration — and Φ is invoked but idle.

**(b) The ancillary-restraints paragraph nearly confesses the genealogy.** Ll. 460–465: European competition law "already runs this counterfactual on contract terms... the same question, asked of a clause. What the diagnostic does is generalize that operation from clauses to a mediator's functions." So the novelty claim is: ancillary-restraints, applied to functions, from the dependent party's seat, returning strategy rather than legality. That is a real repositioning. It is not a new formal test. A referee who knows competition law will say: you have rediscovered a familiar counterfactual and renamed it for managers.

**(c) No case where the plain edge-reasoning and the companion measure disagree.** Still unpaid from the prior panel. Without one borderline coordination, in these pages, where naive "can they deal directly?" and the graded measure come apart, the companion remains borrowed vocabulary.

**What would change my mind:** either (i) one worked borderline in this chapter where Φ and the plain counterfactual diverge and the measure is right, or (ii) an explicit framing sentence: "The diagnostic as used here is the qualitative edge counterfactual; the companion paper refines borderline and many-party cases." Option (ii) is cheaper and honest. Option (i) is what would convert the apparatus from decoration to necessity.

**5.2 (high damage) — §5.5 regresses the capability fix.**

§5.2 ll. 417–419 states the fix correctly: "It does not restore a prior relationship. It restores the capability of direct dealing."

§5.5 ll. 546–547 applies the old bug: "Restore the same tie for a traveler who has never heard of the hotel, and the tie carries no information, because there was no relationship to restore. Demand aggregation survives the bypass, and it is necessary."

That is exactly the construction `reviews_v2.md` finding 2 warned against. Under the capability reading just installed, the question is whether that traveler could reach that hotel at comparable cost if no rule or architecture stood in the way — Google search, destination marketing, brand discovery elsewhere — not whether a prior relationship existed. "No relationship to restore ⇒ necessary" makes every pure discovery function necessary by construction. The application undoes the definition.

**Paste-ready fix for ll. 546–548:** see Part 3.

**5.3 (high damage) — OTA "decisive" outruns the conceded confounds; actor-relative test meets aggregate data.**

The dating fix holds (ll. 569–571). The prediction fix holds: function-split predicts absence of erosion, not the rise (ll. 571–574). Good.

Then l. 574–577: "still decisive for the diagnostic." It is not decisive. The chapter itself names mobile search and three-agency consolidation (~nine-tenths) as mechanisms for the *increase*. Non-decline under parity opening is consistent with the function-split and also with switching costs, habit, app defaults, and search-ads concentration that have nothing to do with necessary aggregation. "Decisive" should be "consistent with" or "hard to reconcile with the single-substitutable-intermediary reading alone."

Worse structural mismatch: §5.2 insists classification is actor-relative (ll. 424–426) — aggregation contingent for a known chain, necessary for an unknown independent. Schegg 2024 numbers (ll. 565–566) are aggregate European overnight-stay shares. Aggregate dependence deepening cannot vindicate an actor-relative split without segmentation. The case as written applies a traveler-who-never-heard verdict to market-wide shares.

**5.4 (moderate) — §5.1 is ornamental.**

§5.1 introduces factoring and the companion's Φ measures, then says the argument needs only the next section's counterfactual (ll. 395–397). Factoring returns once at ll. 428–432 to warn against reading irreducibility as necessity — a useful caution — and never again drives a verdict. For a hostile reader, §5.1 exists to name an apparatus the chapter then declines to use. Either cut §5.1 to a short caution paragraph without promising measures, or use factoring once on a worked case.

**5.5 (low–moderate) — exit/voice mapping remains near-definitional.**

Ll. 484–485: "Exit maps onto bypassability, voice onto necessity." Necessary is defined as surviving the bypass. The mapping is elegant renaming. Content lives in the classification step, not in the Hirschman vocabulary. State that once.

**5.6 (credit) — partial now has its own action.**

Ll. 526–532: contest the bypassable part, accept the integrating one; Baldwin / Parmigiani. The prior objection that four categories act as two is partly answered. The §7 table still sorts mostly binary, but partial is no longer empty. Fair.

### §6 Coordinative sovereignty: a definition

Normative assembly is competent. Schlager & Ostrom proprietorship-without-title (ll. 617–624) is the right institutional shape. Bearer paragraph (ll. 626–633) marks the members-over-firm conflict as a position taken — the v2 fix holds. Muldoon & Raekstad correctly filed as ownership/voice, not exit.

**6.1 (moderate) — additive residue still thin.**

The construct "takes" non-domination, relational autonomy, Markell involvement, and Ostrom commons (ll. 592–624), then distinguishes itself from digital sovereignty (the far neighbor). The stretching worry comes from the near neighbors. What does coordinative sovereignty assert that their conjunction under unexitable mediation does not already assert? On the page, the residue is (i) pairing with the §5 diagnostic and (ii) the opaque interested mediator as object. (i) is hostage to 5.1 above; (ii) is a domain. One sentence stating the additive proposition is still owed (Part 3).

**6.2 (minor) — "effective voice" / "standing" loop.**

Ll. 587–589: currency is "effective voice — terms that hold because the coordinated actors have the standing to make them hold." Standing via voice via standing. Give voice one operational gloss independent of standing (e.g., a channel that has changed a mediator's rule or determination at least once), or mark the loop as stipulative.

### §7 Building standing

Sensible institutional survey. The table (ll. 692–700) does real sorting work. Diagnostic-as-aiming-device at the close (ll. 735–742) is fine.

**7.1 (minor) — "real institutional labor" still mostly binary.**

## PART 3 — Paste-ready revisions

**A. §5.5 regression (highest priority) — replace ll. 544–551**

From:
> Run the counterfactual on each function separately. Restore the direct hotel-to-traveler tie for a traveler who already knows the hotel exists, and the parity clause does nothing: it was held in place by a contract, and it is contingent. Restore the same tie for a traveler who has never heard of the hotel, and the tie carries no information, because there was no relationship to restore. Demand aggregation survives the bypass, and it is necessary.

To:
> Run the counterfactual on each function separately. For a traveler who already knows the hotel, restore the capability of direct dealing: the parity clause does nothing once the contract that forbade undercutting is lifted, and it is contingent. For a traveler who would not have found the hotel at comparable cost by any other channel, the same open tie still fails to reproduce the agency's aggregation, and for that booking the function is necessary. The verdict is actor-relative and split, and it names two different instruments. Against the parity clause the move is exit, pursued through contract law and competition enforcement. Against demand aggregation for travelers the hotel cannot otherwise reach, exit is unavailable and the move is voice.

**B. §5.5 "decisive" — replace ll. 574–577**

From:
> What the decade settles is narrower than a natural experiment and still decisive for the diagnostic: an actor treating the agency as one leaveable thing would have read the direction of travel backwards, and a hotel that spent the decade trying to leave mistook the necessary function for the contingent one.

To:
> What the decade shows is narrower than a natural experiment: dependence failed to fall when the contingent gate opened, which is what the single-substitutable-intermediary reading did not expect, and which the function-split allows. The Schegg figures are aggregate; they do not segment known-brand from discovery-dependent bookings, so they corroborate the split only at market level. A hotel that spent the decade on exit alone treated the portfolio as one leaveable thing.

**C. Honest framing for the idle companion — add after l. 397**

> As used in this chapter, the diagnostic is the qualitative edge counterfactual. The companion paper's integrated-information measures are for borderline and many-party cases this chapter does not grade.

**D. §8 predict — replace l. 753**

From: `and the two predict different outcomes.`  
To: `and the two would predict different outcomes once measured — a claim section 9 sets out to make testable.`

**E. §5 opening bridge from §3.2 — add at l. 380**

> Section 3.2 showed that disclosure cannot reach platform coordination. The further question is which coordinations an actor can leave and which it can only govern: opacity without exit is domination; opacity with a liftable gate is a different institutional problem. The diagnostic sorts those cases.

**F. §6 additive claim — add after l. 624**

> None of the four source theories, alone, holds that autonomy under an unexitable mediation is a distinct standing that requires its own institutions and its own competence. Their conjunction under the bypass condition of section 5 is what coordinative sovereignty names.

**G. Soften abstract ll. 12–14**

From: `and the sorting decides which institutions are worth building.`  
To: `and the sorting indicates which institutions are worth building.`

**H. Make-or-buy honesty inside the neighbor paragraph — optional insert at l. 468**

After "None of them returns a strategy to the party inside the coordination":
> The plain edge question is still a question about integrating value — closer to make-or-buy than the node tests are, and deliberately so. What this chapter claims is not a new formal measure of that value, but a seat and a use: the dependent party runs the counterfactual function by function and converts the answer into exit or voice.

## Verdict

**Minor revision.**

The rebuild answered the prior panel's two loudest demolitions: node-vs-edge is now demonstrated on a platform case, and the OTA dating/prediction overclaim is mostly disciplined. The chapter is close enough to submission that a competent revision pass clears it. It is not yet past a hostile volume referee who will ask what §5 adds once the companion is set aside and whether §5.5 applies the test it defines.

**Single most important fix.** Repair the §5.5 regression at ll. 546–547 ("no relationship to restore") so the application matches the capability-of-direct-dealing definition at ll. 417–419, and demote "decisive" to the correlational claim the confounds allow. That is one paragraph; it is load-bearing.

**Biggest genuine strength.** The digital/coordinative independence thesis paired with the uneven distribution of algorithmacy (§4, §8, §10): compliance can be perfect while standing is zero, and the competence that would make standing real concentrates like capital. That argument is original, cited, and does not need Φ.

**The one thing only the author can supply.** Either the honest framing sentence (Part 3.C) that this chapter's diagnostic is the qualitative edge counterfactual, or one borderline case in these pages where the companion measure and the plain counterfactual disagree. No reviewer can write that case from outside the companion work.
