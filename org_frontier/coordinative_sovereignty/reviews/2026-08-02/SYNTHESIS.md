## Spine fixes (do these before press)

### S1. Repair the §5.5 capability regression — lines 546–547

§5.2 correctly restores "the capability of direct dealing." §5.5 still says the traveler who has
never heard of the hotel has "no relationship to restore," which is the wording the one-head pass
told the rebuild to kill. Rewrite the aggregation half of the counterfactual in the capability
vocabulary already used at lines 417–426, and keep the actor-relative verdict.

### S2. Source or soften three load-bearing empirics

Flagged by most of the panel:

1. **Apple €500 million / April 2025** (lines 758–760) — law reviewer fact-checks the decision;
   still needs an in-text cite.
2. **P2B repeal proposal** (lines 764–766) — cite COM(2025) 837; soften "the only horizontal
   ranking-transparency duty" (DSA Art. 27 still exists).
3. **"Roughly nine-tenths"** of the OTA market (lines 573–574) — cite or drop.

Also confirm the gloss on **COM(2026) 178** (DMA review, lines 685–687).

### S3. Scope the §2.3 literacy thesis — digital-sovereignty lens, highest remaining for the volume

Line 132 ("in most of its current forms") and the Key Terms lock-in (lines 1269–1271) assimilate
digital sovereignty to reading. The European industrial strand — sovereign cloud, domestic
capacity, data localization, procurement — works by jurisdiction and ownership. Restrict the
literacy claim to the regulatory strand, name the industrial strand in one clause, and stop
letting the glossary overstate the assimilation. Related: name the data-trust / data-sovereignty
boundary in §7 (Hummel is already cited); stop §10 from equating digital-sovereignty instruments
with exit (lines 888–889), which contradicts §7's dual reading of interoperability.

### S4. Honest App Store node test — org theory + residual R2

Lines 452–458 prove node/edge divergence on a platform, which answers the prior panel. The node
half still asks whether another actor may process payment "**under the platform's own rules**,"
which makes non-substitutability true by construction. Rebuild the node question as practicable
duplication / opportunity structure (could another payment rail reach the same users on that
device?), keep the edge restoration, and leave the DMA anti-steering line as the regulator's edge
test. Reviewer 2's residual charge — that the plain counterfactual is still edge-substitutability
and that Φ never appears — is already the chapter's stated scope; do not reopen the companion
here. Narrow any remaining "none of them returns a strategy to the party inside" novelty claim
(lines 468–471) against Cutolo & Kenney and multihoming.

## Theory polish (one pass each)

1. **Hirschman mapping (political theory).** "Voice onto necessity" (lines 484–485) and "clearer
   one" (lines 501–504) overstate. Necessity forecloses exit and makes voice *harder*; the
   chapter's own lazy-monopoly correction is half-finished. State the productive tension: voice
   is the only remaining autonomy where it is also institutionally weakest.
2. **Opening hook vs the acquis (law).** Abstract and §1 say the firm "cannot see… or contest";
   §3.2 and §8 concede that P2B, DSA, and DMA already reach *see* and partly *contest*. Align
   the hook with the later concession. Pull GDPR Art. 22 out of the "literate wager" disclosure
   bundle (lines 247–253) — it is a standing modality already in hard law.
3. **Kind-claim vs setting novelty (media).** Ong-scale "change of the same order" (lines
   144–148, 364–371) and the DeVito-narrowed triadic *index* (lines 305–327) are different
   magnitudes. Pin the kind-claim to the *regime*; keep the novelty vs DeVito as the triadic
   index of an already-documented skill cluster.
4. **§5.1 for the interdisciplinary reader.** The bypass is the gift; the systems jargon at
   lines 394–395 loses non-specialists before it. One plain-language rewrite of the opening of
   §5.1.
