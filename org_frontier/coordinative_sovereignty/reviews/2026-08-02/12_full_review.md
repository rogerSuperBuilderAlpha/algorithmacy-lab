## Part 1 — Argument and content

The spine held on a full re-read. S1 (§5.5 in the capability vocabulary), S3 (§2.3 scoped to the regulatory
strand), and S4 (App Store node test as practicable duplication) all survive in context. The OTA case stays
a decade test, the App Store gate stays a node/edge divergence, and the novelty claim stays the triadic
setting. Three theory items from the synthesis were open and are now applied:

1. **Hirschman mapping (§5.3).** "Exit maps onto bypassability, voice onto necessity" overstated: necessity
   forecloses exit and leaves voice as the residual lever, and does not produce it. The rewrite states that,
   retires "a clearer one" in favor of "the difficulty is starker," adds the political-theory lens's loyalty
   clause (lock-in is involuntary retention, which suppresses exit and activates nothing), and replaces the
   is/ought slide at "each is the right move" with "each opens exit." Voice is now stated as the only
   autonomy left exactly where it is institutionally weakest.
2. **Art. 22 taxonomy (§3.2).** The GDPR contestation right is out of the literate-wager disclosure bundle.
   It now stands as case-level standing already in hard law, pointed at §7's contestability cell, with its
   limits stated: high threshold, thin practice, personal scope confined to natural persons, so the
   platform-dependent firm falls outside it. §7's floor sentence now says "natural person" to match.
3. **Kind-claim pin (§2.3, §4).** §2.3 pins the change of the same order to "the regime of coordination."
   §4 adds the two-scale sentence after the Gagričin index: the novelty against DeVito and Dogruel is the
   index and nothing larger, and the change in kind belongs to the regime the capacities must work.

Also applied: "None of them returns a strategy" narrowed to "none of the three," which scopes the claim to
the three neighbor tests and leaves Cutolo & Kenney and multihoming untouched (Reviewer 2 residual).

Reviewer 2's optional inserts F–H and the abstract's "decides"→"indicates" were not adopted; the synthesis
left them outside the spine. The companion-paper scope statement stands. Φ does not appear in the chapter
and "computable" remains absent.
