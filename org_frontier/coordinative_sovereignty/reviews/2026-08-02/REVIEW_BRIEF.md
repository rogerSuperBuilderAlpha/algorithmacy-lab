## Register and bar (every reviewer)

- Impersonal academic chapter. Named agents + active voice ("the chapter argues," "Floridi
  shows"). **No first person.** Judge against that target.
- APA 7 citations. "Author, 2026" self-cites and any block quotations are **off-limits** as
  prose complaints.
- House style (repo `CLAUDE.md`): cut the antithesis machine, self-narrating honesty, and
  metronomic punchy openers. Earned definitional contrasts against real neighbors are allowed;
  decorative ones are not. Agentless passives where a named agent is available are defects.
- Scope honesty: core results about Φ live in the companion; this chapter carries the plain
  bypass counterfactual. Flag any place the chapter promises "computable"/formal measures it
  does not deliver here.
- Do not fabricate citations. Flag VERIFY for empirics that look load-bearing and uncited.
- Line numbers refer to the current `chapter.md`. Quote short spans when flagging.

## Output format (every review file)

Write to the path assigned below. Structure:

1. **Title block** — lens name, manuscript title, venue, your standpoint.
2. **Step 0 — Register and bar** — confirm the register; state what you will judge hard.
3. **Part 1 — Theory and structure, §1–§10** — section-by-section. For each: what it claims,
   what lands, what fails for *your* lens. Rank findings by damage.
4. **Part 2 — Style / slop audit** — register-aware. Exact quotes, failure type, rewrite.
5. **Part 3 — Paste-ready revisions** — the highest-value fixes, ready to drop in.
6. **Verdict** — major / minor / accept-with-nits. Single most important fix. Biggest genuine
   strength. The one thing only the author can supply (if any).
7. **VERIFY** — empirical or citation claims that need sourcing before press.

Be specific. Prefer fewer, sharper findings over a laundry list. Do not soft-pedal; do not invent
problems to fill the form. If a section is clean for your lens, say so in one sentence and move on.
