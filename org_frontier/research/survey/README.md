# Survey research — measuring algorithmacy in people

The literature watch for the survey arm: scale development and psychometrics (confirmatory factor analysis,
measurement invariance, McDonald's ω, latent growth modeling), the existing measures of algorithmic literacy,
awareness, and competence, and the panel designs that track skill acquisition in workers coordinating through
systems they do not control. This is the reading behind the Algorithmacy Competence Scale and its panel.
