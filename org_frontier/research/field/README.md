# Field research — reading real organizations

The literature watch for the field arm: organizational ethnography and field methods for sociotechnical
systems, platform and gig-work fieldwork, eliciting decision and determination rules from interviews,
observation, and documents, and the inter-rater reliability and bit calibration that make a coded encoding of
real coordination defensible. This is the reading behind the nine-step field protocol.

## Topics

| # | topic | scope |
