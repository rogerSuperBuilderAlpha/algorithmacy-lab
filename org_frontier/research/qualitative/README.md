# Qualitative research — fieldwork on opaque coordination

The literature watch for the qualitative arm: qualitative methods for organizations (the Gioia methodology,
the Lincoln and Guba trustworthiness criteria, disagreement treated as data), sensemaking, and qualitative
studies of algorithmic management and the worker's experience of opaque algorithmic systems. This is the
reading behind the qualitative studies read against the lab's priors.

## Topics

| # | topic | scope |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [The Gioia Methodology and Inductive Theory Building](topics/01_gioia_inductive_method/REVIEW.md) | How first-order/second-order coding and the visible data structure produce transparent, rigor-defensible concepts from interview and field data. |
| 02 | [Lincoln-Guba Trustworthiness Criteria](topics/02_lincoln_guba_trustworthiness/REVIEW.md) | Credibility, transferability, dependability, and confirmability and the techniques (member checking, audit trails, thick description, reflexivity) that establish them. |
| 03 | [Weick's Sensemaking and Its Contemporary Critique](topics/03_weick_sensemaking_theory/REVIEW.md) | The retrospective, social, enactive model of sensemaking, its collapse dynamics, and recent half-century reviews mapping limits and open questions. |
| 04 | [Disagreement-as-Data and Intercoder Reliability Debates](topics/04_disagreement_as_data/REVIEW.md) | The reflexive-TA versus coding-reliability split and how coder and informant divergence can be instrumented as evidence rather than error. |
| 05 | [Methods for Studying Opaque Algorithmic Systems](topics/05_opaque_algorithm_methodology/REVIEW.md) | Christin's refraction, comparison, and triangulation and other strategies for empirically studying intermediaries whose internals are hidden. |
| 06 | [Mechanisms of Algorithmic Control at Work](topics/06_algorithmic_control_mechanisms/REVIEW.md) | The 6 Rs, contested-terrain framing, and information/power asymmetries through which platforms direct, evaluate, and discipline workers. |
| 07 | [Worker Folk Theories and Algorithmic Imaginaries](topics/07_worker_folk_theories/REVIEW.md) | How workers build actionable theories of systems they cannot inspect and how those theories shape system behavior in practice. |
| 08 | [Resistance, Gaming, and Labour Agency](topics/08_resistance_and_agency/REVIEW.md) | Tactical workarounds, out-of-app activity, gaming, and typologies of agency through which workers recover autonomy under algorithmic management. |
| 09 | [Perceived Fairness, Attribution, and Affect](topics/09_fairness_attribution_affect/REVIEW.md) | When workers read opaque systems as fair or abusive, and the roles of transparency, anthropomorphism, paranoia, and attribution in that reading. |
| 10 | [Contestation Rights and Algorithmic Governance](topics/10_contestation_and_governance/REVIEW.md) | Legal and institutional remedies (GDPR Article 22, rights to contest) that connect individual sensemaking of opaque systems to governance. |
