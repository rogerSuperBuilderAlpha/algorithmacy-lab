# Computational research — literature review

Search method: Scholar Gateway and Consensus academic-search tools plus iterated web search and source-page fetches, run 2026-06-23, confirming a DOI, arXiv ID, or stable publisher URL for every entry.

The computational program of algorithmacy-lab rests on a measurement claim: that the irreducible, whole-greater-than-parts structure of a coordinating system can be quantified by integrated information. Integrated information theory began as an account of consciousness, defining Phi as the effective information a system generates above its weakest partition (Tononi, 2004). Successive formulations sharpened the cause-effect machinery: IIT 3.0 derived postulates from phenomenological axioms and specified conceptual structures over mechanisms (Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi, 2014), and IIT 4.0 restated existence, intrinsicality, information, integration, exclusion, and composition in physical terms with a revised distance metric (Albantakis et al., 2023). The system-level quantity that aggregates this structure, phi-s, and its dependence on determinism, degeneracy, and connectivity fault lines is set out by Marshall et al. (2023). PyPhi supplies the reference implementation for exact computation on discrete networks (Mayner et al., 2018).

A program studying coordination through opaque algorithmic third parties needs Phi to travel beyond brains. Tononi & Koch (2015) frame integrated information as a property of any cause-effect system, supporting application to social and artificial substrates. The empirical anchor for coordination is Engel & Malone (2018), who compute integrated information over interacting people and show that higher group Phi tracks collective-intelligence task performance and the quality of Wikipedia output. That work connects to the collective-intelligence factor identified by Woolley et al. (2010) and to a transdisciplinary reading of information dynamics in complex adaptive and organizational systems (Souza Sant'Anna, 2026).

The structure being measured is the same structure organizational theory has long described. Thompson distinguished pooled, sequential, and reciprocal interdependence, each demanding a distinct coordination mechanism. Malone & Crowston (1994) recast coordination as the management of dependencies among activities and built an interdisciplinary taxonomy, revisited a decade later (Crowston, Rubleske & Howison, 2006). The cooperative-game reading of joint action supplies a complementary formalism: characteristic-function games and the Shapley allocation describe the surplus a coalition generates and each member's marginal contribution, while Bratman (1992) gives the philosophical conditions for shared cooperative activity. Recent surveys map these ideas onto engineered multi-agent systems (Sun et al., 2025).

The central obstacle is computational. Exact Phi requires searching over partitions and, in IIT 3.0 and 4.0, nested combinatorial optimizations, so the cost grows super-exponentially and rigorous computation is confined to systems of well under a dozen units (Mayner et al., 2018; Hosaka, 2025). This size ceiling forces the program toward proxies. Barrett & Seth (2011) introduced empirical, time-series measures applicable to data; Oizumi, Tsuchiya & Amari (2016) derived a geometric measure from information geometry; Tegmark (2016) organized the space of candidate measures by their factorization, distributions, and divergence choices. Comparative studies test which proxies track ground-truth Phi: Mediano, Seth & Barrett (2019) compare candidate measures in simulation, and Nilsen, Juel & Marshall (2019) evaluate approximations and heuristics against exact values. Upper bounds on integrated information constrain what any estimator can claim (Zaeemzadeh & Tononi, 2024), and graph neural networks now approximate the major complex and its Phi at larger scale (Hosaka, 2025). A recent integrate-and-fire study shows Phi can be nonzero in simulated spiking networks but is sensitive to noise and time constants (Danilczuk, Pokropski & Suffczynski, 2026).

Criticism shapes the program's claims. Cerullo (2015) questions the justification for the exclusion postulate and Phi's link to consciousness. Barrett & Mediano (2019) argue that Phi is not well-defined for general physical systems. Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi (2013) ground the macro-scale move by showing causal emergence can let a macro model beat its micro substrate. Mediano et al. (2022) propose separating a strong IIT, which identifies consciousness with Phi maxima, from a weak IIT that uses information-dynamic measures as pragmatic correlates; the lab's coordination work sits in this weak register, treating Phi as a structural metric of joint cause-effect power rather than a consciousness verdict.
