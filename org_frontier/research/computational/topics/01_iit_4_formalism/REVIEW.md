# The IIT 4.0 Formalism for Exact Phi

Integrated Information Theory 4.0 specifies exact integrated information by moving from phenomenology to physics in two steps: five axioms describing essential properties of every experience (intrinsicality, information, integration, exclusion, composition), prefixed by a zeroth axiom of existence, and corresponding postulates that any physical substrate must satisfy (Albantakis et al., 2023; Tononi et al., 2016). The formalism applies to a discrete system of units whose dynamics are given by a transition probability matrix, and it operationalizes each postulate as a constraint on cause-effect power assessed from the system's intrinsic perspective in a single state.

The distance metric at the core of 4.0 is the intrinsic difference (ID), derived as the unique measure satisfying causality, intrinsicality, and specificity (Barbosa et al., 2020). Unlike earlier earth-mover or relative-entropy distances, ID is point-wise and weighs a single specified state, yielding intrinsic information as the product of informativeness and selectivity. Mechanism integrated information (small phi) is then the ID-based irreducibility of a mechanism's cause and effect repertoires across their minimum partition (Barbosa et al., 2021). At the system level, integrated information phi-s quantifies how much a candidate substrate's intrinsic information over its own past and future is reduced by the system minimum partition, with the maximal substrate over this evaluation forming a complex (Marshall et al., 2023). Determinism, degeneracy, and connectivity fault lines drive phi-s, clarifying which architectures support high values.

Above the substrate, 4.0 unfolds the full cause-effect structure, or phi-structure: the distinctions specified by subsets of the complex together with the relations that bind overlapping distinctions, the sum of whose integrated-information values gives structure integrated information (Grasso et al., 2021). Theoretical work establishes upper bounds on distinction and relation phi, since mechanisms sharing units cannot simultaneously maximize irreducibility, which constrains achievable phi-structures (Zaeemzadeh and Tononi, 2024). Recent analysis reframes intrinsic cause-effect power as a tradeoff between differentiation and specification, both expressible through ID, refining the 4.0 measures (Mayner et al., 2025).

Two strands situate the formalism. Kleiner and Tull (2020) give an axiomatic, category-theoretic treatment encompassing IIT 3.0 and quantum variants, exposing the mathematical scaffolding that 4.0 instantiates. On the computational side, exact phi remains intractable beyond roughly a dozen binary units, motivating approximations and heuristics whose fidelity to the exact measure has been evaluated empirically (Mediano et al., 2019). The PyPhi toolbox provides the reference implementation for exact unfolding of discrete systems (Mayner et al., 2018), and serves as the supplement accompanying the 4.0 specification.
