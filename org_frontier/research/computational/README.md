# Computational research — exact integrated information and coordination

The literature watch for the computational core: exact integrated information and IIT 4.0, its application
beyond neuroscience to social, organizational, and artificial systems, organizational coordination theory,
the cooperative-game structure of joint action, and the size ceiling on exact Φ with the proxies proposed for
it. This is the reading behind the catalog of pre-disclosed priors the lab computes.

## Topics

| # | topic | scope |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [The IIT 4.0 Formalism for Exact Phi](topics/01_iit_4_formalism/REVIEW.md) | The axioms, postulates, distance metric, and phi-s definition that specify how exact integrated information is computed for a discrete system. |
| 02 | [PyPhi and Reference Computation](topics/02_pyphi_implementation/REVIEW.md) | The software, data structures, and algorithms used to compute exact cause-effect structures and Phi on small discrete networks. |
| 03 | [The Combinatorial Size Ceiling on Exact Phi](topics/03_phi_size_ceiling/REVIEW.md) | The super-exponential growth of partition search and nested optimization that confines rigorous Phi to systems of fewer than about ten units. |
| 04 | [Proxies and Approximations for Phi](topics/04_phi_proxies/REVIEW.md) | Empirical, geometric, decoding-based, and learned estimators that approximate integrated information at scales beyond exact computation. |
| 05 | [Validating Proxies Against Ground Truth](topics/05_proxy_validation/REVIEW.md) | Comparative simulation studies, upper bounds, and error analyses that test whether candidate measures track exact Phi on known systems. |
| 06 | [Integrated Information Beyond Neuroscience](topics/06_iit_beyond_brains/REVIEW.md) | Application of Phi and related measures to social, organizational, and artificial substrates rather than neural tissue. |
| 07 | [Integrated Information as a Coordination Metric](topics/07_group_phi_coordination/REVIEW.md) | Empirical use of group Phi to predict collective intelligence and joint task performance in human and hybrid groups. |
| 08 | [Organizational Coordination and Dependency Theory](topics/08_coordination_theory/REVIEW.md) | Thompson's interdependence typology and the Malone-Crowston dependency-management framework as structural descriptions of coordination. |
| 09 | [Cooperative-Game Structure of Joint Action](topics/09_cooperative_game_joint_action/REVIEW.md) | Characteristic-function games, the Shapley value, and shared-agency philosophy as formalisms for the value and structure of joint action. |
| 10 | [Critiques, Well-Definedness, and Weak IIT](topics/10_phi_critiques_weak_iit/REVIEW.md) | Arguments about Phi's definability, the exclusion postulate, causal emergence, and the strong-versus-weak IIT distinction relevant to non-consciousness use. |
