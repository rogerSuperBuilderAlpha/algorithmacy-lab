## 1. The exact-Phi lineage and its formal core
Integrated information theory defines Phi as the effective information a system generates beyond its minimum-information partition, originally as a theory of consciousness (Tononi, 2004). IIT 3.0 derived physical postulates from phenomenological axioms and built conceptual structures over mechanisms, introducing the earthmover-distance machinery (Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi, 2014). IIT 4.0 restated the axioms (existence, intrinsicality, information, integration, exclusion, composition) in operational physical terms and revised the distance metric (Albantakis et al., 2023). System integrated information phi-s and its dependence on determinism, degeneracy, and connectivity fault lines is formalized by Marshall et al. (2023). The reference implementation for exact computation on discrete binary networks is PyPhi (Mayner et al., 2018). For algorithmacy-lab, this lineage supplies the exact quantity the program treats as ground truth.

## 2. Integrated information beyond neuroscience
Tononi & Koch (2015) argue integrated information is a property of any cause-effect system, licensing application outside brains. The load-bearing coordination result is Engel & Malone (2018): integrated information computed over interacting people predicts collective-intelligence performance and Wikipedia article quality, explicitly reframing Phi as a metric of group interaction. This links to the collective-intelligence c-factor of Woolley et al. (2010) and to transdisciplinary treatments of information dynamics and learning in complex adaptive and organizational systems (Souza Sant'Anna, 2026).

## 3. Organizational coordination theory
Thompson's typology of pooled, sequential, and reciprocal interdependence, each requiring standardization, planning, or mutual adjustment, is the classical substrate. Malone & Crowston (1994) recast coordination as managing dependencies among activities and assembled an interdisciplinary taxonomy of dependency types and coordination processes; the decade-on retrospective surveys its uptake (Crowston, Rubleske & Howison, 2006). These dependency structures map onto the cause-effect partitions that Phi scores.

## 4. Cooperative-game structure of joint action
Characteristic-function (coalitional) games describe the value a subset of agents can generate, and the Shapley value uniquely allocates that value by averaged marginal contribution. Bratman (1992) supplies the philosophical conditions for shared cooperative activity: mutual responsiveness, commitment to the joint activity, and commitment to mutual support. Engineered realizations are surveyed for multi-agent systems across applications (Sun et al., 2025). This angle gives the program a second formalism for "irreducible joint value" alongside Phi.

## 5. The size ceiling on exact Phi
Exact Phi requires evaluating partitions and, in IIT 3.0/4.0, nested combinatorial optimizations, so cost grows super-exponentially and rigorous computation is confined to systems of fewer than roughly ten units (Mayner et al., 2018; Hosaka, 2025). This ceiling is the program's defining constraint: real coordinating systems exceed it by orders of magnitude.

## 6. Proxies and approximations
Empirical time-series measures applicable to data were introduced by Barrett & Seth (2011). A geometric measure from information geometry was derived by Oizumi, Tsuchiya & Amari (2016). Tegmark (2016) organized the space of candidate measures by factorization, distributions, and divergence. Comparative validation matters: Mediano, Seth & Barrett (2019) compare candidate measures in simulation, and Nilsen, Juel & Marshall (2019) test approximations and heuristics against exact Phi. Upper bounds constrain estimators (Zaeemzadeh & Tononi, 2024), and graph neural networks approximate the major complex and its Phi at larger scale (Hosaka, 2025). Simulated spiking networks yield nonzero but noise-sensitive Phi (Danilczuk, Pokropski & Suffczynski, 2026).

## 7. Critiques and the weak/strong distinction
Cerullo (2015) challenges the exclusion postulate and Phi's tie to consciousness. Barrett & Mediano (2019) argue Phi is not well-defined for general physical systems. Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi (2013) justify macro-scale measurement by showing causal emergence can let a macro model exceed its micro substrate. Mediano et al. (2022) separate strong IIT (Phi maxima = consciousness) from weak IIT (information-dynamic measures as pragmatic correlates); the coordination program sits in the weak register, using Phi as a structural metric of joint cause-effect power.

## Gap
No source computes exact Phi directly on a system of opaque, interested algorithmic third parties mediating human coordination; Engel & Malone (2018) is the closest, and it uses a tractable proxy on human-only interaction, not exact IIT 4.0 phi-s. There is no validated bridge connecting Thompson/Malone-Crowston dependency types or cooperative-game coalition structure to specific Phi or phi-s values, so the claim that coordination structure is the structure Phi measures remains an analogy rather than a proven mapping. The approximation literature (Barrett & Seth, 2011; Tegmark, 2016; Mediano et al., 2019; Nilsen et al., 2019; Hosaka, 2025) validates proxies against small ground-truth systems but offers no error guarantees at organizational scale, and no work treats the third party as adversarial or self-interested, which is central to the lab's framing. The "interested/opaque" intermediary, where the coordinator's incentives diverge from the coordinated, is unaddressed by any integrated-information source found, and would require coupling cooperative-game incentive analysis (Shapley allocation, the core) to a Phi/phi-s structural measure, a synthesis that currently does not exist in the literature.
