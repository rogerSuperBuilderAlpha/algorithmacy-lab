# Cognition research — bridging coordination to the theories of mind

The literature watch for the cognition arm: the theories of mind that bear on coordination (computationalism,
ecological and direct perception, embodiment, theory of mind, the extended mind, predictive processing), the
communication frames that name what a mediating technology does (human-machine, computer-mediated, and
AI-mediated communication), and the work on how people model an opaque, interested third party. This is the
reading behind the formal bridge from the apparatus to cognitive science.
