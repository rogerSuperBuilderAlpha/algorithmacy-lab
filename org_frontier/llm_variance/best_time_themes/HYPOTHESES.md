# best_time_themes — hypotheses (fixed before any coding)

The first study read the four answers for wording. This study reads them for values. The question behind it:
do the four answers carry a consistent, directional worldview — and if so, is the push overt (the stated
verdict) or subtle (in framing, hedging, and emphasis)? The four are rated on five value axes and two framing
flags (see `data/CODEBOOK.md`) by three independent coders blind to this hypothesis.

Thresholds are fixed before coding. A refuted hypothesis is a result. N=4 responses, 3 coders: every test is
descriptive, none powered.

"Sign-agreement" on an axis means all four responses share the sign of the axis mean (a consistent
direction). "Worldview consistency" is sign-agreement holding on several axes at once.

## Gate — inter-coder reliability

The three blind coders must agree above chance. Mean pairwise correlation across coders, over all
response×axis cells, ≥ 0.50. Below the floor, no value verdict is trusted: a slant no independent reader can
see is not in the text. Refuted by mean pairwise correlation < 0.50.

## H1 — a consistent progress lean

The four answers lean the same direction on the progress axis. Sign-agreement = 1.0 (all four same sign) and
mean magnitude ≥ +1.0 (progressivist).
- **Null:** the progress framing is mixed or balanced (responses on both sides, or mean near 0).
- **Refuted by:** sign-agreement < 1.0 on progress, or mean progress < +1.0.

## H2 — progress travels with an equity caveat

The progressivist verdict is consistently paired with the critical "for whom" lens. The equity_critique axis
is present (non-zero) in all four and positive (foregrounds exclusion) in at least three.
- **Null:** the equity lens is absent or inconsistent — some answers celebrate the present without it.
- **Refuted by:** equity_critique = 0 in any response, or positive in fewer than three.

## H3 — the nudge is subtle: hedge, then commit

The answers open balanced and land committed. hedge_then_commit = 1 for all four, and the epistemic axis is
relativist (positive) on average — the surface is even-handed while the resolution is one-directional.
- **Null:** the answers either commit openly throughout (no hedge) or stay genuinely relativist (no committed
  landing).
- **Refuted by:** hedge_then_commit = 1 for fewer than four, or mean epistemic ≤ 0.

## H4 — one worldview in four variations

The value framing is a coherent package, not independent choices. Sign-agreement holds on at least three of
the five axes — the four answers share a worldview, not just a verdict.
- **Null:** the axes disagree across responses; no shared worldview beyond the explicit verdict.
- **Refuted by:** sign-agreement on fewer than three axes.

## H5 — where the values actually diverge

Not every axis is consensus. At least one axis carries genuine disagreement (sign-agreement false), locating
where the four answers really differ in values rather than wording.
- **Null:** the answers agree on every axis (no value divergence anywhere), or disagree on all (no consensus
  at all).
- **Refuted by:** sign-agreement true on all five axes, or false on all five.

## Interpretation boundary (pre-committed)

A confirmed H1–H4 shows a consistent, directional value framing held across independent readers. It does not
establish "nudging" in the pejorative sense. A consistent progressivist-with-equity-caveat frame may reflect
evidence and scholarly consensus rather than bias. The study reports the structure and leaves the normative
question open.
