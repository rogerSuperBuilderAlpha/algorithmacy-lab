## What the hospitality paper must re-argue (lab cannot supply)

1. Hospitality as relational practice: welcome, recognition, care, presence, negotiated access
   (Lynch et al., Lugosi, and the journal's own theorizing line).
2. Phygital as hybrid experiential space (Batat PH-CX / PSR and related service research).
3. Why seamlessness can thin relational hospitality.
4. Frontline employee discretion as co-equal with guest agency in the framework.
5. Cultural variation in what counts as welcome under algorithmic categorization.

## Correction, 2026-08-07

Earlier rows in this table described a Paper 2 built on Φ/IIT and a Paper 3 of experiments. Neither
is current. Paper 2 is *The Competency a Form Demands: Algorithmacy and the Co-optation Column*, a
conceptual derivation with no Φ in it; Paper 3 is a designed, IRB-staged, unfielded qualitative
study. The genealogy this paper inherits is conceptual throughout, which makes the H&S fit easier
than the old table implied. See [`literature/FOUNDATION.md`](literature/FOUNDATION.md) Part 2.

## Citation posture toward the lab

When citing public lab essays or the sovereignty chapter:

- Attribute constructs and arguments accurately.
- State that computational results grade small models of coordination.
- Do not present those results as evidence that a named hotel brand or guest population is
  irreducible.

Altitude rule from the lab spine still holds: modest about what is proven; confident about the
constructive thesis that algorithmacy names a real gap and that mediation analysis is worth doing.
