## Summary

Reviews coordination research across organizational settings and identifies three integrating
conditions that any coordinating mechanism must produce: accountability, which establishes who
answers for what; predictability, which lets participants anticipate what will happen; and common
understanding, which gives participants a shared picture of the situation and of one another. This
is the published anchor from which the paper derives both its augmentative/substitutive condition
and its five affordances.
