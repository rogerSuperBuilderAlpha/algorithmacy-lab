## Critical notes & threats

- The full text is more forward-leaning than the card and the manuscript imply. Adoption is one of four priorities, not the organizing frame, and the review already calls for 'designing beneficial AI'. The manuscript's §1 characterization — a literature 'organized around adoption, acceptance and the redesign of service delivery' — describes the literature Tussyadiah reviews rather than her own agenda. Defensible as written, but if a referee reads it as a claim about Tussyadiah's position it will look unfair, and one clause would fix that.
- Its role is rhetorical rather than evidential, which makes it vulnerable to a reader who thinks the paper is attacking a straw position. The manuscript mitigates by conceding these are useful questions before moving past them.
- If the framing paragraph ever needs shortening, this is the citation to keep and Wirtz is the one to drop.
