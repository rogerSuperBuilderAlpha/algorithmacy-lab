## Summary

Field experiments on Airbnb finding that a single positive review closes the acceptance gap: once
a guest account carries a review, acceptance rates for guests with white-sounding and African-
American-sounding names become statistically indistinguishable. The paper's guest-side anchor, and
the source of its sharpest complication.
