## Summary

Argues that a pro forma human who rubber-stamps fails the effectiveness standard Article 14 sets, that meaningful oversight requires genuine authority to override, and that overseers without such authority become liability sponges absorbing responsibility without control. Also connects oversight to procedural rights: a reasoned decision, a hearing, an effective remedy.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- The effectiveness standard is argued to exclude rubber-stamping oversight [full-text]
- Liability sponge names an overseer who absorbs responsibility without holding control [full-text]
- Oversight is tied to the right to a reasoned decision, to be heard, and to an effective remedy [full-text]
