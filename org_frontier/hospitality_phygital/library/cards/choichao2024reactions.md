## Summary

Six experiments with 2,794 participants finding that when an outcome is unfavourable, AI deciders are perceived as fairer than human deciders, attributed to perceived unemotionality, with the gap erased by reminders of algorithmic bias.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- The effect appears specifically under unfavourable outcomes, which is the condition the affordance is designed for [abstract]
- Perceived unemotionality is the proposed mechanism [abstract]
- Sample totals 2,794 across six studies [abstract]
