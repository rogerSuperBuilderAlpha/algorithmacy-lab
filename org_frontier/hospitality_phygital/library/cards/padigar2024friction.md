## Summary

Distinguishes four kinds of friction by the desirability and value of the task the effort serves,
and warns that removing constructive friction — effort that is undesirable but valuable — can
destroy the value it was thought to obstruct. Puts the paper's anti-seamlessness argument into a
marketing journal's own terms.
