## Summary

Survey of 351 matched employee-supervisor pairs finding that algorithmic directing, scheduling and monitoring reduce voice through lowered felt responsibility for constructive change, while algorithmic feedback increases it, moderated by work locus of control.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Moderator tested is work locus of control, an individual disposition [full-text]
- No voice climate, silence climate or psychological safety construct appears in the design [full-text]
- Algorithmic feedback moves voice in the opposite direction from direction, scheduling and monitoring [full-text]
