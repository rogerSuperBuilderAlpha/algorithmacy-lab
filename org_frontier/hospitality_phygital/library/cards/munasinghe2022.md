## Summary

Proposes a triadic conceptualisation of the host-guest encounter in which the wider social and
cultural context forms a third element shaping what host and guest are able to do. Hospitality
therefore already has a triad, and ours is a different object — which the paper must say plainly
rather than leave a reviewer to notice.
