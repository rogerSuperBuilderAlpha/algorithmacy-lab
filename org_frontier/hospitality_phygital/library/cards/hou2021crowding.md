## Summary

Shows that the preference for human staff over service robots is conditional on the situation,
shifting with social crowding rather than expressing a fixed attachment to human contact. It is
the governor on the paper's human-accessibility argument: cited alone, Kim et al. would let the
paper read as nostalgia for the human.
