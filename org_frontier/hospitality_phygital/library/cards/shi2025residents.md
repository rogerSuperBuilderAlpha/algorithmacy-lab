## Summary

Develops and validates a five-dimension scale measuring what residents perceive they gain from interacting with tourists, built from a literature review, 52 interviews and two resident surveys, and shows it predicts interaction intentions and support for tourism.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Five dimensions: emotional lift, local pride, altruism, destination attraction, self-development [abstract]
- Respondents are residents of tourist cities, so the host is a community member rather than a commercial operator [abstract]
- The construct measured is a benefit accruing to the host, not a behaviour directed at one [abstract]
