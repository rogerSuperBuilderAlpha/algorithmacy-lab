## Summary

Shows that technologies introduced to standardize frontline decisions bring rough categories and
scripts that generate new discretionary work, as practitioners compensate for what the categories
miss. This forced a correction to the paper's argument: judgement is not displaced but pushed into
unrecognized compensating work, performed by people whose formal role no longer includes it.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Standardizing technologies do not eliminate discretion; they generate new discretionary responses and workarounds [abstract]
- Comparative ethnography across three public service settings [abstract]
