## Summary

Focus-group and comparative-case study across Italy and the UK showing that both over-communication and under-communication of corporate responsibility produce customer misjudgment and reduce confidence. Distinguishes confidence from trust and grounds the analysis in Freeman's integrated stakeholder approach.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Both excess and absence of responsibility communication produce misjudgment and lower confidence [full-text]
- Distinguishes confidence from trust, with knowledge and behavioural components [full-text]
- Treats the firm's account-giving as something stakeholders actively judge rather than passively receive [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- The account is about communication judged by an audience. Bovens' accountability is a relation in which a forum can require an answer and impose consequences. Naming that difference is how section 5 introduces the forum without appearing to import a foreign concept.
- Banking is a regulated sector where the forum exists in law, which makes the omission notable rather than careless: even there, the account is analysed as communication.
