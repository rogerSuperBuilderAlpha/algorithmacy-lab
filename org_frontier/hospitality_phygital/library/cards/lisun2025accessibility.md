## Summary

Defines algorithmic accessibility as the openness of a decision process to contestation and shows it is not sufficient on its own, with accountability in the form of appeal, redress and an answerable party proving pivotal for trust through procedural justice.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Accessibility is operationalized to include whether appeal mechanisms exist at all [abstract]
- Accountability rather than accessibility carries the effect on trust [abstract]
- The mechanism is procedural justice, the same tradition as the paper's voice citations [abstract]
