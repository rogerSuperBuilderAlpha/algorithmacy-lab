## Summary

Distinguishes residual corporate social responsibility, which is compliance rhetoric aimed at minimizing harm, from integrated corporate social responsibility, which is intrinsic stakeholder commitment expressed in redesigned business models. Urges the move from transactional company-stakeholder relations to collaborative company-partner relations with joint decision-making.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- Residual CSR is characterized as compliance-driven and harm-minimizing [full-text]
- Integrated CSR requires redesigned business models and shared responsibility rather than added activity [full-text]
- The prescribed shift is from transactional to collaborative relations with joint decision-making [full-text]

## Critical notes & threats

- The analogy is exact where it matters: a firm can perform every observable act of responsibility while occupying none of the position, which is what substitutive mediation does with hosting.
- It is an analogy and not a derivation. Section 4 still needs Okhuysen and Bechky to make the distinction checkable, and the manuscript should not let the analogy do work the condition has to do.
