## Summary

Replaces product-centric marketing logic with an experience-centric logic organized around seven components: experience, exchange, extension, emphasis, empathy capital, emotional touchpoints, and the emic and etic process. Opens on consumer empowerment as the driver of new brand opportunity.

## Key facts / verified quotes with loci

- The seven components are verified from the publisher's chapter list and chapter DOIs 10.4324/9781315232201-5 through -10 [metadata]
- Chapter one poses "What is consumer empowerment?" and "How is the empowered consumer driving new brand opportunities?" as framing questions [abstract]
- No second edition exists; the 2020 companion volume is a separate case-study book with a food, tourism and leisure chapter [metadata]

## Critical notes & threats

- The mapping is a resemblance and not an equivalence. Empathy capital is a firm's asset and care is an obligation, which is the difference the paper exists to press, so the mapping should be offered as a bridge the paper then walks past.
- Empowerment as the paradigm's stated aim is the more valuable half: coordinative sovereignty can be introduced as specifying what empowerment requires when the counterparty is an algorithm.
