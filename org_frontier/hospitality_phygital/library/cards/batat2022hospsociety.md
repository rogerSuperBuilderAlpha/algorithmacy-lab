## Summary

Qualitative study using focus groups and interviews with 35 participants, finding that spillover effects of luxury brand extensions depend on extension type, the brand's strategic orientation and consumers' familiarity with luxury gastronomy. Argues brand-extension research needs to move past product-focused frameworks.
