# Venue rulings

Standing decisions about where a source may come from. These are recorded once here rather than
repeated on every card, because they are properties of outlets rather than of individual papers.

A named absence is a finding. Three research rounds on this paper rejected sources on venue grounds
and none of those rejections was written down, so the same searches would have surfaced the same
outlets again and cost the same time to dismiss. That is the waste this file exists to prevent.

## Preferred venues, in order

*Hospitality & Society* · *International Journal of Hospitality Management* · *Tourism Management* ·
*Annals of Tourism Research* · *International Journal of Contemporary Hospitality Management* ·
*Journal of Hospitality Marketing & Management* · *Tourist Studies* · *Journal of Sustainable
Tourism*.

Then top organization, information-systems, service-research and HCI venues where the hospitality
literature is genuinely silent — *Administrative Science Quarterly*, *MIS Quarterly*, *Journal of
Service Research*, *Journal of Service Management*, *Management Science*, *New Media & Society*, ACM
CHI and CSCW, *Big Data & Society*.

## Excluded, with the reason

| outlet or class | ruling |
|---|---|
| MDPI titles, including *Sustainability* and *Tourism and Hospitality* | Excluded. Surfaced repeatedly on personalization, accessibility and AI-adoption searches. Volume without selectivity; a referee at a critical journal will read the citation as a signal. |
| *SHS Web of Conferences* | Excluded. Conference aggregator, no meaningful selection. |
| *GS WOW*, *IJSREM*, *Journal of Scientific Research and Reports*, *NPRC Journal of Multidisciplinary Research* | Excluded. Unindexed or pay-to-publish. |
| *International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science*, *Advances in Tourism Studies*, *Journal of Modern Hospitality*, *Journal of Rural Tourism* | Excluded. Surfaced on the AI-personalization search in round 1c; none is indexed in a way that survives scrutiny. |
| *Journal of Social Sciences Archives* | Excluded, and worth naming specifically: an item there mimics the title of the legitimate Mele et al. (2023) review in *Italian Journal of Marketing*. Check which one a search result actually points to. |
| Regional and national tourism journals without indexing | Excluded by default; admissible only where the paper is the sole source for a claim the argument needs, and then with the limitation stated in text. |
| The *Phygital Business Review* | Excluded, and this one needs explaining. It is the American Phygital Association's own outlet. It carries no ISSN, no DOIs and no indexing, and no summit proceedings volume exists. When the special-issue editor asked us to cite APA research, this was not a citable route. The paper instead cites the summit-linked special issues in *Journal of Macromarketing* and *Journal of Services Marketing*, which is where that work actually reaches print. `manuscript/submission/RESPONSE_TO_EDITOR.md` says so plainly and invites correction. |

## Two standing method rules

**Search summaries are leads, never sources.** Round 1c found a web summary crediting a paper to the
wrong two authors, and the round-2 pass found a rendered DOI wrong by a single digit with the author's
given name also wrong. Every citation is checked against Crossref or the publisher record before it
enters the bibliography, and the card records which.

**One pipeline caveat, for information rather than exclusion.** The *Journal of Macromarketing*
special issue linked to the APA Paris summit conditions special-issue consideration on paid summit
registration. The journal is a legitimate SAGE title and the papers are citable; the pipeline is
simply worth knowing about.
