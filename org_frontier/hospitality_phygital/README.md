# Hospitality phygital — algorithmic mediation and guest agency

A domain arm for the *Hospitality & Society* special issue on theorizing phygital hospitality
(APA Paris Summit 2026 track). The live deliverable is a conceptual paper that relocates AI from a
neutral service tool to a constitutive mediator in the hospitality encounter, and that names the
competences and design affordances that keep guest and employee agency intact.

The paper's working title:

> Who Hosts the Guest? Algorithmic Mediation, Guest Agency, and the Reconfiguration of Hospitality
> in Phygital Spaces

The abstract is locked in [`ABSTRACT.md`](ABSTRACT.md). The complete project plan sits in
[`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md).

## Thesis in one paragraph

Phygital hospitality is triadic coordination among guest, algorithmic intermediary, and human or
organizational host. Algorithms already perform hosting functions: recognition, routing, access
control, preference anticipation, and response. That participation redistributes knowledge,
discretion, and authority. The paper distinguishes **augmentative hospitality** (technology supports
human recognition, discretion, responsiveness, and care) from **substitutive hospitality**
(algorithmic direction displaces relational judgment). It introduces **hospitality algorithmacy** as
the situated capacity of guests and employees to recognize, interpret, engage, contest, and when
necessary circumvent algorithmic mediation — the hospitality-domain form of the lab's algorithmacy
construct, supporting **coordinative sovereignty** in the encounter.

## Relation to the rest of the lab

This arm **borrows constructs**, it does not re-run Φ. The mediated triad, algorithmacy, and
coordinative sovereignty already exist in the public lab and (when present) the nested private
dissertation. The hospitality paper re-specifies them for guest–algorithm–host encounters and for
a hospitality-theory audience. Exact Φ results remain evidence about Boolean models; they are not
claims about hotels, guests, or staff. See [`BRIDGE.md`](BRIDGE.md) for what transfers and what
must be re-argued.

Sibling arm: [`../coordinative_sovereignty/`](../coordinative_sovereignty/) — the political form of
algorithmacy. Hospitality algorithmacy is the encounter-level competence; coordinative sovereignty
is the standing that competence supports.
