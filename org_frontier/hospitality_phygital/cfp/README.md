## Aim (compressed)

Phygital hospitality is a hybrid experiential space. AI and immersive tools reconfigure embodiment,
presence, and interpersonal dynamics. Guests are active phygital actors. The special issue wants
human-centric strategies that enhance rather than dictate guest agency, satisfaction, and
well-being, in dialogue with PH-CX (Batat, 2024a) and phygital service research (Batat, 2024b).

## Journal-fit criteria (CFP)

Submissions should:

1. Advance hospitality knowledge on phygital transformation and service encounters / spatial
   practices.
2. Create interdisciplinary dialogue on socio-cultural dimensions of phygital hospitality.
3. Examine how phygital ecosystems reconfigure interpersonal relationships, cultural rituals, and
   the politics of space.
4. Bridge hospitality and phygital research on fluid guest–organization interactions.

## Topic hooks that match this paper

- Power structures and guest–staff relationships in phygitally mediated hospitality
- Socio-cultural dynamics of AI tools in hospitality spaces
- Human-centric strategies for positive transformation and guest well-being
- Inclusive / culturally sensitive design
- Guest perceptions of presence, embodiment, and relationality
