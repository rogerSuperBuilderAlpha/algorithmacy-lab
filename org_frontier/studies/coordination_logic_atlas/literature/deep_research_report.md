# Deep-research report — determination logic, integration, and threshold coordination

Stage-2 literature pass for the coordination-logic-atlas paper. Generated by the `deep-research`
workflow (parallel search → fetch → adversarial verification → synthesis) and reviewed. It decides
which of the atlas's headline results are novel and which are rediscoveries.

## 1. Parity binds more readily than monotone logic — a rediscovery

The atlas reproduces, in its inhibition theme and from q54, that parity mediators (XOR/XNOR) yield a
triadic verdict more readily than monotone ones (AND/OR). This is established. Griffith & Koch's work
on principled φ-like information measures already treats parity (XOR) as the canonical irreducible /
synergistic function against the reducible AND/OR cases, and the IIT reference implementations
(Albantakis, IIT 3.0 logic_gates) use AND/OR/XOR precisely to exhibit the contrast. The atlas's
parity result is a rediscovery and the paper frames it as such, citing the prior measures.

## 2. The extremes-only quorum law — novel as an IIT result, with strong external parallels

No source states the specific finding that a k-of-n quorum mediator is IIT-irreducible only at the
extremes (k = 1, k = n) and reducible at every interior threshold. But two literatures make the
result legible and give it standing.

- **Biology builds only the extremes.** The quorum-sensing literature models collective threshold
  responses with Boolean logic and describes exactly two integration architectures: an AND-like gate
  where both autoinducers are required (the k = n unanimity extreme) and an OR-like gate where one
  suffices (the k = 1 any-one extreme). No interior k-of-n quorum gate is described. Nature, where it
  combines quorum signals, builds only the two integrated extremes — a striking parallel to the IIT
  result rather than a statement of it.
- **Threshold dynamics break qualitatively at the margin.** Granovetter's threshold models of
  collective behaviour (1978) define a threshold as the number of others who must act before an actor
  acts — the quorum mediator's read-back structure — and show the aggregate outcome depends on the
  exact threshold distribution, not the average, with a one-unit change in a single threshold flipping
  the equilibrium discontinuously. Threshold coordination has qualitative breaks at the margin; the
  atlas locates the specific break that IIT-irreducibility marks.

So the contribution is the IIT-irreducibility map and the extremes-only law within it, against a known
backdrop in which threshold coordination is studied and where nature itself builds only the extremes.

## 3. Substitutability at interior thresholds — the known analogue

The atlas's mechanism — at an interior quorum each party is substitutable, so no party is individually
pivotal and the form factors — has a direct analogue. Granovetter notes that actors sharing a
threshold are functionally interchangeable in the model. Substitutability collapsing distinct
contribution is the same move as the Null Player / Symmetry axioms of the Shapley value (carried in
from the core-membership paper). The atlas shows it operating on the cause-effect structure.

## 4. Canalization and topology — the surrounding theory

Threshold and canalizing functions are central to Boolean-network dynamics. Nested canalizing
functions minimise sensitivity and promote criticality (Manicka, Marques-Pita, Rocha); non-monotone
Boolean automata networks have their own structure (Noual, Regnault, Sené). The atlas's topology
results (the directed-cycle / rotation irreducibility reproduces q11) sit inside this literature
rather than against it; the paper cites it as context for the determination-function space it sweeps.

## Net effect on the contribution

The atlas paper claims: (i) a systematic IIT-irreducibility map over the determination-logic space of
small coordination forms; (ii) within it, the extremes-only quorum law — novel as an IIT result,
paralleled by quorum-sensing's AND/OR-only architectures and Granovetter's threshold discontinuities;
(iii) the parity and rotation results, framed honestly as rediscoveries of known
parity-vs-monotone-irreducibility and prior lab work. The map and the quorum law are the contribution.
