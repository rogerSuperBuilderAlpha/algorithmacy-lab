**Definitions (fixed).**

## H1 — union scales like counterpart coalition

For every matched (style, k) cell in the panel, worker-union and
counterpart-coalition agree on Φ, n_core, and core==group. Null:
mismatch on any cell.

## H2 — vanishes past a size threshold (#97-like)

There exists k≥3 where the worker-union form vanishes (null complex or
peer group out of core). Null: union persists with core==group through
the largest k tested.

## H3 — different scaling law (vs counterpart)

Worker-union Φ(k) or n_core(k) differs from counterpart-coalition on
the weak or strong family. Null: identical trajectories (H1’s match).
