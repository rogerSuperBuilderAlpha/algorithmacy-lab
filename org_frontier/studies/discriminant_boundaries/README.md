# Discriminant boundaries — what algorithmacy is not

The fifth paper-pipeline category (B) through the full protocol: a pre-registered discriminant battery
testing whether the dyadic/triadic verdict separates algorithmacy from its neighbour constructs.

**The result.** The communication and management literatures separate these constructs on a transmit /
transform / commit axis (CMC transmits, AI-MC transforms on the sender's behalf, directive algorithmic
management commits). The exact-Φ verdict operationalizes that axis: the four three-party convey
constructs (CMC, AI-MC, advisory algorithmic management, sensemaking) read dyadic; directive algorithmic
management reads triadic; a sensitivity flip confirms commit-versus-convey is the operative variable.
Human-machine communication is the exception — a two-party dyad outside the comparison — which sharpens
the boundary: algorithmacy requires a third party.
