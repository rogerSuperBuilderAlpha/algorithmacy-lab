# template_coverage_census — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

Universe named before the run: the 256-form strict-mediation n=3 family (Q93 / `corpus.population`),
of which 24 are triadic under exact IIT-4.0 Φ_MIP. Secondary universe for F27: the 4096 arbitrary
three-node wirings with the cached cheap-feature panel (`probes/results/residual_panel.csv`, Probe 125).

The four catalog templates (relay / conjunctive / additive / free) are matched by *structural
signature*, not by literal identity to the catalog's worked Boolean form:

- **relay** — a 3-cycle of unary copies (each node depends on exactly one other); no joint determination.
- **conjunctive** — strict mediation; mediator a non-parity joint function of both parties; parties read
  the mediator. Covers the catalog AND instance and its OR / NAND / NOR / asymmetric-joint kin at Φ=2.0.
- **additive** — conjunctive determination with a parallel back-channel (catalog bypass mode `add`).
- **free** — sink already sources upstream; mediator off the unique path (reducible by construction).

## H1 — template residual among triadic strict-mediation forms

The four templates leave a nonempty residual among the 24 triadic forms. That residual is exactly the
parity-commit forms (mediator XOR or XNOR): eight forms, Φ_MIP = 0.5.

Null: every triadic form matches at least one of the four templates (residual count = 0), or the
residual is a mixed bag rather than exactly the XOR/XNOR band.

## H2 — residual is a fifth structural signature, not a new contingency cell

A worked parity form (XOR and XNOR mediators with party feedback) classifies as **intrinsic /
necessary** under the bypass-counterfactual — same contingency cell as conjunctive — so the gap is a
new structural template inside the necessary column, not a fifth contingency class.

Null: parity classifies contingent, partial, or reducible, or fails to stay in the core under bypass.

## H3 — holistic residual is the affine class (RESEARCH_AGENDA_50_V2 F27)

The ~5% of the 4096 wirings a full cheap-feature random forest misclassifies (Probe 131: 196 forms,
4.8%) are exactly the forms whose three update rules are affine over GF(2).

Null: the misclassified set is not the affine set (different cardinality, or empty overlap, or only
partial overlap).
