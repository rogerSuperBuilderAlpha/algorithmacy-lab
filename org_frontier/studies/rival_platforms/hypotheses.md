# Rival platforms — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (RESEARCH_AGENDA_50_V2 #32).** A worker on two competing
platforms — do the two cores compete for the worker, and does one win
(extends #73 to rivalrous platforms)?

**Already known (cited, not reopened).**
- #73 multi-role: X as worker on one platform and counterpart on
  another keeps separate cores (`{X,S1,C1}`), not a merged five-node
  whole.
- Multiparty multihome: `multihome_either` factors (dyadic);
  `multihome_both` stays triadic (Φ=4) — substitutable mediators
  collapse; all-binding does not.
- Q210 shared counterpart: AND bridge can merge two triads; OR does
  not super-add the same way.
- #29–#31 CONFLICT_ENCODING / MULTI_NASH_MAXPAY / UNION_MIRRORS_COAL —
  pointers only.
- Other lanes closed.

**Universe.** Exact binary IIT-4.0 Φ. Nodes `(W, S1, C1, S2, C2)` =
shared worker + two platform triads. Designed dual-platform panel;
candid N. In-silico Boolean abstractions.

**Definitions (fixed).**
- **Platform-i capture:** a max-Φ major complex (across reachable
  states) contains W and at least one of {Si, Ci}, and neither of the
  other platform's {Sj, Cj}.
- **Compete:** both P1-capture and P2-capture cores appear at the same
  max Φ (state-selected rivalry).
- **One wins / asymmetric capture:** capture cores for exactly one
  platform (W never joins the other at max Φ).
- **Span / shared:** a single max-Φ complex contains W and nodes from
  both platforms.
- **Drop / collapse:** no max-Φ complex contains W (platforms may still
  host W-free dyads).

## H1 — cores compete and one wins / captures the worker

At least one form shows platform capture of W (asymmetric one-wins, or
equal-Φ rivalry where each state selects one platform triad with W).
Null: no form has a W-bearing platform capture core.

## H2 — worker spans both / shared

At least one form has a max-Φ complex containing W and both platforms.
Null: no span form.

## H3 — worker drops from both / collapse

At least one form has no max-Φ complex containing W. Null: every form
keeps W in some max-Φ core.
