# dual_mediator_template_census — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (RESEARCH_AGENDA_V3 #1).** Beyond the five n=3 strict-mediation
templates (relay / conjunctive / additive / free / parity from V2 #27 /
`template_coverage_census`), does an **n=4 dual-mediator** census (series
cascade, parallel mediators, mediator-of-mediators) force a **sixth**
template, or do all triadic cores factor into products of the five?

**Already known (cited, not reopened).**
- **V2 #27 / `template_coverage_census`:** among 24 triadic strict-mediation
  forms, 8 escape the four catalog templates; they are exactly XOR/XNOR —
  the fifth template (parity). Relay / additive / free are catalogued but
  scarce in that slice.
- **V2 #15–#20 / V3 #4–#7:** composed topology (hierarchy, shared-mediator,
  necklace, ring-of-hubs, k-lift, hybrid FF+recurrent) — none reopen the
  n=3 template inventory.
- Faithful conjunctive triad Φ=2.0; parity triad Φ=0.5.

**Gap.** The five templates are n=3 inventory. Dual-mediator n=4 wirings —
series cascade, parallel seats, mediator-of-mediators — were not asked
whether they mint a **sixth** structural template or whether every triadic
core (and every product tetrad) stays inside the five.

**Universe.** Binary exact IIT-4.0. n≤4. Architectures:
- **series** — two mediators chained (AND–AND, AND–XOR, XOR–XOR);
- **parallel** — two mediator seats over the same parties (AND–AND /
  AND–XOR / XOR–XOR; parties read one seat, or AND of both);
- **mediator-of-mediators (MoM)** — outer gate over an inner mediator
  (copy, party-gate, two-mid AND, XOR-copy).

**Closed triad** = induced 3-node subsystem whose updates do not depend on
the excluded node. Match against the five via `match_templates` over node
permutations.

**Factors into five** = every closed triadic subsystem that is itself
triadic matches ≥1 of the five; every size-4 major complex has core Φ in
the known landmark set generated by sums of five-template atoms
({0.5, 2.0, 4.0=2+2, …}) rather than a new atom, and admits no closed
triadic escapee.

**Sixth template** = some closed triadic (structure=triadic) matches none
of the five — the n=4 analogue of the parity residual.

## H1 — instrument + five-template anchors

Faithful conjunctive triad: triadic, core Φ=2.0, matches conjunctive.
Parity triad: triadic, core Φ=0.5, matches parity.

## H2 — no sixth among closed triadic cores

On the dual-mediator panel, every **closed** induced triad with
structure=triadic matches ≥1 of
{relay, conjunctive, additive, free, parity}. Null: at least one closed
triadic escapee (candidate sixth).

## H3 — size-4 cores are products, not a new atom

Every cell whose major complex has size 4 has core Φ in {0.5, 2.0, 4.0}
(parity atom, conjunctive atom, or 2+2 product) and no closed triadic
escapee under H2. Null: a size-4 core with Φ outside that set, or a
closed triadic escapee nested in it.

## H4 — panel verdict

H1–H3 hold → `FACTORS_INTO_FIVE`.
H2 fails → `SIXTH_TEMPLATE`.
H2 holds but H3 fails → `NOVEL_TETRAD`.
H1 fails → `CONTROLS_FAIL`.
