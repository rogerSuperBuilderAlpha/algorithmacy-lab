# Thompson verdict bridge — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (RESEARCH_AGENDA_50_V2 #43).** Does the verdict correspond to
Thompson’s (1967) interdependence types — pooled dyadic, sequential the
chain / low, reciprocal triadic?

**Instrument.** Exact binary IIT-4.0 Φ (stock pin). N=3 AND family
unless noted. In-silico. No multivalued pin.

**Prior.** `questions/q43_thompson_interdependence/` ran a five-hypothesis
panel (ordering / pooled / sequential / reciprocal / primitives). This
study is the construct-validity bridge: one type→verdict table, an
alignment score, and the agenda’s three reading keys. Pointer only —
beyond-binary / PE / AI / formal / stoch / estimation / construct-omit
stay closed.

**Canonical predictions under H1**

| Thompson type | canonical form | predicted |
|---|---|---|
| pooled | independent-contribution relay | dyadic |
| sequential | pass-through chain (#57) | chain / low (Φ strictly below reciprocal, or non-tying intermediate) |
| reciprocal | cyclic feedback triad | triadic |

**Primary observables.** Structure; max Φ; major-complex core. Alignment
score = fraction of (type, form) rows whose structure matches the H1
prediction for that type (canonical three + three alternates; sequential
“low” also requires Φ < reciprocal − ε on the canonical pair).

## H1 — clean type→verdict map

On the canonical triple, pooled is dyadic, sequential sits strictly below
reciprocal in Φ (chain/low), and reciprocal is triadic. Null: at least one
leg fails.

## H2 — mismatches

At least one Thompson label maps to the wrong verdict under a defensible
encoding (alternate or canonical). Null: every encoding matches its type’s
predicted verdict.

## H3 — partial alignment only

Some legs hold (e.g. pooled_indep→dyadic and/or recip_cyclic→triadic) and
some fail; the typology tracks the verdict loosely, not as a clean map.
Null: full H1, or zero aligning legs.

## Reading keys

- **FULL_THOMPSON_MAP:** H1.
- **THOMPSON_MISMATCH:** H2 with no useful aligning legs.
- **PARTIAL_ALIGNMENT:** H3 (¬H1 ∧ some legs hold ∧ mismatches exist).
