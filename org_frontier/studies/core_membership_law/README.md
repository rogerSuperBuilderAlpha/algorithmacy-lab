# The core-membership law

The first paper-pipeline category (A) taken through the full protocol: a literature-grounded,
pre-registered study of which parties of a coordination form belong to its IIT-4.0 major complex.

**The law.** A party is in the irreducible core when it is **bidirectionally coupled** to the
determination (necessary — IIT 4.0's own substrate requirement, confirmed here: 0/660 non-bidirectional
nodes in the core in the strict-mediation family) and **causally pivotal** to it (membership rises
monotonically with the determination's sensitivity to it, 38.9% → 87.5% — novel relative to IIT's
binary exclusion, the cause-effect image of Shapley pivotality). Substitutability removes a party.
