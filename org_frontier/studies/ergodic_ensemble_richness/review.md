# Review — why B5 is still open

The designed panel in `studies/ergodic_components_vs_phi/` asked whether
triadic forms carry more ergodic components than dyadic forms. On nine
hand-built forms the answer was no: component counts differed by 0.1429,
under the margin 0.5, and basin entropy ran the other way. That cell's
findings name the limit in one line: the panel is designed, and the
population correlation on a random ensemble is still open. Agenda item
B5 is that item. It has a method line and a falsifier, and no status tag
of `run`.

The lab already owns the population those nine forms were drawn toward.
`corpus/population.py` enumerates all 256 strict-mediation wirings at
n=3 and reports a triadic rate of 9.4%. The census records the verdict.
It does not record attractors or basin entropy, so it cannot answer B5.

A later sample at n=4 or n=5 (`multiparty/scaling.py`) would reopen the
size question. B5 fixes n. The computable population at that n is the
256-form census. Using it, rather than a fresh draw from the same family,
is the choice locked in `hypotheses.md` before any coefficient is
computed.

The gap this cell closes is internal to the track: whether the designed
panel's null survives the family the structural findings already treat
as the n=3 population. It does not close the validation gap to a real
organization.
