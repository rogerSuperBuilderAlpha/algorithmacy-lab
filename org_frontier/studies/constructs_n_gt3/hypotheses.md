# constructs_n_gt3 — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (#42 construct arm).** Do the HMC / CMC / AI-MC discriminants
hold at n>3, or does scale change how these constructed forms read under
exact Φ (whole verdict, major complex, Φ)?

**Definitions (lab).** From `discriminant_boundaries` / probes 15, 19, 20:
- **HMC** — human communicates with a machine that is not a third person
  (two-party human–machine loop; no constituted counterpart).
- **CMC** — technology *conveys* (transmits) a human message; no joint commit.
- **AI-MC** — computational agent *transforms* a message on the sender’s
  behalf; recipient still coordinates with sender intent, not a third-party
  commit (Hancock et al. 2020 encoding used in the lab).
- **Algorithmacy (control)** — system commits a joint determination both
  outer parties must heed (W∧C into S; both read S).

**Already known at n=3.** Convey constructs → dyadic (HMC surprise: two-node
mutual is irreducible but is not a triad by party count). Commit → triadic.
AI-MC W→A→C loop can be structurally triadic (probe 20 boundary). Omit-atom
arc separate (`OMIT_ATOM_ARC.md`). Ternary / residual noted only.

**Universe.** Binary exact IIT-4.0. Conjunctive AND. Designed forms at
n∈{4,5} (honest small N; no random spray). Report **whole** structure/Φ_MIP
and **major complex** (core, Φ, n_core) — idle padding makes wholes factor
while cores persist.

## H1 — HMC at n>3 stays non-algorithmacy

Every HMC form’s major complex is a **two-party** (or smaller) human–machine
core; it does not become a three-party algorithmacy core. Null: some HMC
form has a ≥3-party algorithmacy-style core with joint-commit signature.

## H2 — CMC conveyors at n>3 stay non-commit

Every CMC form has whole Φ_MIP = 0 (factors) or a core that is not a
joint-commit triad (no S = W∧C style binding). Null: a conveyor reads as
commit/triadic algorithmacy.

## H3 — AI-MC remains the structural boundary case

At least one AI-MC form at n>3 has a major-complex Φ > 0 with ≥3 nodes in
core (transform loop irreducible), matching the n=3 boundary tension.
Null: all AI-MC forms have n_core ≤ 2.

## H4 — algorithmacy controls stay irreducible at n>3

Designed joint-commit controls at n∈{4,5} have major-complex Φ > 0 with the
committing mediator and both (or all) bound parties in core. Null: controls
collapse to Φ=0 cores.
