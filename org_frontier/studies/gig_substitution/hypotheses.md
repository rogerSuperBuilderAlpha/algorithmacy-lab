# Gig substitution — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (RESEARCH_AGENDA_50_V2 #35).** In a gig market with many
substitutable workers, at what substitution rate does the individual
worker drop from the core (#8, #22)?

**Universe.** Exact binary IIT-4.0 Φ. Nodes
`(W1..Wk, S, C)` — k gig workers, platform, counterpart. Designed
size/substitution sweep; candid N (k≤4, n≤6). In-silico Boolean
abstractions.

**Definitions (fixed).**
- **Substitution rate** `r = 1 − m/k` for an m-of-k match
  (`S' = C ∧ (at least m of the Wi)`). Full OR is `m=1` so
  `r = 1 − 1/k`; all-required is `m=k` so `r=0`.
- **Individual drop:** a labeled worker `Wi` is absent from the major
  complex.
- **Sharp threshold:** there is a smallest positive `r` (or smallest
  k under OR) at which workers drop, with the adjacent weaker cell
  still hosting workers in core.
- **Smooth dilution:** fraction of workers in the major complex
  declines gradually across ≥3 ordered `(k,m)` cells (not a single
  jump from all-in to all-out).
- **Solidarity encoding:** all-required (`m=k`) or worker-union peer
  coupling under all-required commit (#31-style).

## H1 — sharp drop threshold in substitution / k

Under OR (`m=1`), workers are in core at k=1 and drop for all k≥2; or
equivalently on the m-of-k grid, workers drop at every cell with
`r>0` while `r=0` keeps them. Null: no such adjacent threshold.

## H2 — smooth dilution

The fraction of workers in the major complex glides across ≥3 ordered
substitution cells (by r or k) without a single all-in→all-out jump.
Null: the sweep is discrete (workers all in at r=0; all out at r>0
under symmetric forms).

## H3 — never drops under designed solidarity encodings

On all-required (`m=k`) forms through the largest k tested, every
`Wi` remains in the major complex. Null: some all-required cell ejects
a worker.
