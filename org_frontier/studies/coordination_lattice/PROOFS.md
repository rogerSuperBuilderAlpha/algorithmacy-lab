## Primary order

$\kappa(f)=(v(f),\Phi(f))$ with $v=1$ iff triadic. Lex order on $\kappa$
is a total preorder on forms. Kinds = $\kappa$-classes. The quotient
is a **chain**, hence a **lattice** (join = max key, meet = min key).

This is the sense in which verdict + Φ induce a lattice of kinds: not
a free enrichment lattice on wirings, but the quotient of the
evaluation map $\kappa$.
