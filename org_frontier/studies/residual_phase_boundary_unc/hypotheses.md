# residual_phase_boundary_unc — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

**Question (RESEARCH_AGENDA_50_V2 F28, extended).** Do the cheap-feature residual misses from the
unconstrained k=2 size series sit on a genuine Φ verdict phase boundary — do one-bit truth-table
perturbations flip the exact dyadic/triadic verdict more than for matched non-miss controls?

**Primary universe.** The **75 RF misses** from `holistic_residual_n4_unconstrained/` (N=1000, seed
40, k=2). Forms are regenerated by replaying that sampler; miss indices come from the same Probe-131
RF protocol on the committed panel.

**Secondary universe.** The **45 RF misses** from `holistic_residual_n5_unconstrained/` (N=500,
seed 50) — run if exact-Φ runtime allows; otherwise defer with a documented obstruction.

**Perturbation family.** One-bit Hamming-1 flips in each node's 2-input truth table, holding the
chosen input pair fixed. At n=4 that is **16** neighbours per seed (4 nodes × 4 bits); at n=5,
**20** neighbours. Exact IIT-4.0 Φ_MIP via `classify_rules`. A neighbour *flips* if its structure
(triadic vs dyadic) differs from the seed's. Direction (tri→dya vs dya→tri) is recorded.

**Controls (seed 28 within each universe):**
- **Near-hit:** correctly classified forms with `|p − 0.5| < 0.25` (full pool if smaller than the
  residual set).
- **Far-hit:** sample of correctly classified forms with `|p − 0.5| ≥ 0.40`, size equal to the
  residual miss set.

**Primary rate.** Mean over seeds of (flipping neighbours / neighbourhood size).

Size series 4.8% → 7.5% → 9.0% and F27's affine refutation are cited, not reopened. The n=3 F28
study (`residual_phase_boundary/`) remains the Probe-131 baseline.

## H1 — residual misses are phase-unstable

Among n=4 unc residual misses, mean one-bit flip rate ≥ **0.25** and fraction with ≥1 flip ≥ **0.80**.

Null: mean < 0.25 or any-flip fraction < 0.80.

## H2 — residual exceeds confident forms

n=4 residual mean flip rate − far-hit mean ≥ **0.10**.

Null: difference < 0.10.

## H3 — residual exceeds near-hit (uncertainty-matched) controls

n=4 residual mean flip rate − near-hit mean ≥ **0.05**.

Null: difference < 0.05 (phase structure tracks classifier uncertainty, not the residual label).

n=5 results are reported under the same thresholds as a replication, not as a separate
pre-registered band.
