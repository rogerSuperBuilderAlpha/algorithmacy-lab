## 3. Line-item inventory

### Surgical — deletion, reordering, or minimal substitution; in bounds to apply

**P1 (§3, defect).** "The second matters because it matters here, because it locates the decision in
an officer." An editing artifact — the doubled "matters" reads as a stammer. → "The second matters
here, because it locates the decision in an officer."

**P2 (§4, dangling sentence = AR3).** "The film also ends after a fixed sequence." Repeats the
fixity point and connects to nothing downstream. Cut.

**P3 (§4, meta-residue).** "Her minimum for a system is where the answer starts:" narrates the
argument's plumbing ("where the answer starts" is the retired-metaphor family the bans lens flagged
as "Her own floor is the answer"). → the panel's adjudicated paste-ready: "Her own minimum for a
system answers the objection:".

**P4 (§5, unglossed jargon — bans B21).** "The median take rate per driver rose from 25 to 29
percent" — *take rate* is Binns's term, unglossed for a general collection reader. → "The median
share Uber kept per driver rose from 25 to 29 percent."

**P5 (§5, noun switch).** "Workers found choice inside those narrow openings, and that consent could
mask the structure." *Choice* becomes *consent* with no bridge; Cameron's own term is "choice-based
consent." → "and that choice-based consent could mask the structure."

**P6 (§7, self-narrating).** "though they are careful to add that…" → "though they add that…".

**P7 (§7, antecedent = AR1).** "a fourth thing the three questions do not reach" → "a fourth thing
the three powers do not include."

### Authorial — needs a sentence in the author's voice; flagged with proposals, not applied

**P8 (§5).** "Driver, trip, market, and customer-side fare are evidenced objects of reading."
Research-speak ("evidenced objects of reading") at the exact point the section states its evidence
boundary. Proposal: "The record shows the platform reading the driver, the trip, the market, and the
customer-side fare. It does not show identity read to choose the match."

**P9 (§2 = AR2).** "The narrower opening is visible across three canonical statements that do not
cite the other tradition." Opaque referent plus a count the next three sentences appear to satisfy
and do not. Proposal in `01_argument.md`.

**P10 (§5 = AR4).** "Three powers mark what that addition costs." Proposal: "Three powers come with
that sideways position."

**P11 (§7, optional — bans B25 residue).** "its middle tier refilled" borrows Tzioumakis's tiers
without definitions. One clause would gloss it; costs words against the ceiling. Author's call.

**P12 (§4, optional).** "cannot price, sanction, or remove the people it shows from Austin" — the
final phrase can parse as "the people it shows, from Austin." Proposal: "cannot price or sanction the
people it shows, and cannot remove them from Austin."

**P13 (§1, optional).** "Watching *Slacker* now feels like swiping through TikTok or Instagram, only
slower. Ann Althouse has also compared *Slacker* to scrolling TikTok." Two TikTok sentences in a row;
the second exists to credit the observation, which the brief requires. If the author wants one
sentence, the credit must survive the merge.

## 4. Protected and endorsed

The chapter's signature moves read as a writer, not a machine, and none should be touched: "Matricide,
arrest, grief."; "Nobody is owed a return."; "What it had was slack."; "He grants the selecting and
refuses the selector."; "Münsterberg, Benjamin, and Hansen between them measured nothing."; "Nobody
games a cut."; "Licensing decides what can be found. Prominence decides what is found."; "The sequence
is the relation, and it arrived from outside."; the paired "begins" sentences that close the chapter.
The concessions are pointed rather than uniformly gracious ("People walked out."; "He cannot mean
there are no cuts"), which is the emotional-flatness tell run in reverse — the register modulates.
First person, contractions, and questions answered in stride are the chapter's voice and were not
counted.
