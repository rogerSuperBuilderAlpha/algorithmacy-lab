## The one architectural decision — the author's, not mine (5 reviewers)

Film narrative, network, platform, critical theory, and Reviewer 2 all land on the same crack. §4 honestly
concedes the camera coordinates the **viewer** (viewer↔character), not the characters. But §2's own taxonomy
puts the **feed** in the reads-all/brokers-none corner, *opposite* the engineer, and the camera fails the
test's "reads the parties" axis. So the camera looks like the **feed's kin, not the engineer** the title and
chapter defend — and §5 masks this by reverting to the Uber/dispatch analogy in the very "both select, rank,
and filter" sentence that re-runs the equivocation §4 claimed to retire, giving the feed one sentence while
Uber gets the section. Critical theory frames the same doubling as worker-side dispatch + viewer-side feed
folded into one by "one composes, the other governs."

Three ways out, all the author's call (do not paper over with a sentence):
1. **Commit the camera to "engineer"** and defend viewer-coordination as genuinely pair-brokering and
   constitutive (argue the viewer-character dyad is the coordinated pair).
2. **Rebuild §5 around the feed** as the camera's true kin (Netflix/recommender), with Uber/dispatch demoted
   to the far, instructive-because-it-breaks case.
3. **Name the doubling as the thesis** — the engineering third has a consumption face (feed) and a production
   face (dispatch), and *Slacker*'s camera is the former.
Whichever is chosen, promote §4's self-correction into an explicit reconciling paragraph and flag at §2 that
the camera is a hybrid on axis 3.
