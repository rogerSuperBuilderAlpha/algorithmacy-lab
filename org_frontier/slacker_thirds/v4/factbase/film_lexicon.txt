# Film-referent lexicon for check_film.py
#
# Terms that count as "the film is present in this sentence." Built from
# research/scene_inventory.md (35 dialogue segments) plus the production proper
# nouns the chapter uses. One term per line. '#' starts a comment.
#
# A leading '~' marks a term that counts as a film referent but NOT as a distinct
# scene, so the film's title and the director's name cannot inflate scene coverage.
# Everything else is a scene marker: a distinctive string that appears when the
# chapter actually engages that segment rather than gesturing at the film.
#
# Quoted spans of eight words or more also count as film referents automatically,
# without appearing here -- the chapter quoting dialogue is the film being present.
#
# SEEDED 2026-08-10 from the scene inventory. Revise against v4/factbase/scene_ledger.md
# once that lands: a term that never fires is a scene the chapter cannot reach.
