- **"Presenter" as a neutral term standing between Chatman and Bordwell.** It is Chatman's own term of art (S4-040), introduced to argue that presenting entails an agent; using it as a neutral hinge concedes the argument it is meant to mediate.

- **"Linklater drew the platform analogy."** He names no platform; "what now would be exponentially more complex" names nothing (S4-090).
- **Criterion's "Shooting on 16 mm" synopsis line as a counter-indication to a Super-8 finale claim.** It is promotional copy about production, not a claim about the last shot (S4-072).
- **Rosenbaum's cards described as tarot cards.** They are Brian Eno's "oblique strategies" cards (S4-070).
- **Any *Slacker* line of dialogue printed as a direct quotation.** The published record splits on the film's two most-quoted lines (S4-065/S4-066, S4-067/S4-068/S4-069). Nothing goes in quotation marks without the disc or a physical copy of the 1992 St. Martin's book.
