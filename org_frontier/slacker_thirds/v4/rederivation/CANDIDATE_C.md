# CANDIDATE_C — the incumbent's defence

**What the chapter is for.** It makes the film's handoffs arguable: it takes the sentence every account
of *Slacker* already contains — the camera links the scenes — turns it into a countable claim about who
composes what a stranger meets next, and says what that has and has not got to do with the platforms
that compose ours.

**The frame.** A third party can put people in one another's way, or it can compose the order in which
they arrive. The film's Austin does the first: its people meet because a sidewalk or a bookstore holds
them and a code says who may speak to whom, so a man overhearing "You seen Gary around?" can walk in
with "Did I overhear you say you got a friend that's missing?" (segs 4–5, subtitle-derived). No place in
the film arranges a particular meeting. The film does the second: the order of the visits is fixed,
identical for every viewer, and mostly has no cause inside the story world. Call the first a
**setting**, the second a **selector**. One question sorts them — does the order of encounters arise
inside the field or outside it? — and it answers *outside* for the camera, *inside* for Austin,
*outside* for Uber and for a landing page. §3 checks it.

**The strongest objection, met.** Selection is one power. Pricing, rating, removal, and refusing a
reason are a **regime**, held through market position, contract and law, not through choosing. A setting
can hold them: the club in *Slacker* takes "Five-dollar cover," turns away a man promised the guest
list, and clears the room — "It's past 2:00. Everybody out… You can't be here" (segs 28–29, 31). That
concession is the frame's point. Strip the regime away and a residue remains that a camera and a
platform genuinely share: composing a serial order for a party who never negotiates with the composer.
The residue is the least contested thing platforms do, because it is the one thing a century of film
taught audiences to receive without noticing. That is the work the distinction does.

**The nine objections, one sentence each.**

1. *The test does not sort.* I cut the three questions and run the one above, which sorts the camera and
   the landing page correctly and is settled by evidence, not definition.
2. *Distinction or relabelling.* At the Austin end it is a relabelling — Feld named the mechanism,
   Lofland the code, and I say so — and it earns its place as the contrast term that makes the selecting
   visible and as the split that keeps the Uber end honest.
3. *The powers do not follow from selecting.* Conceded in the body, from the film: a venue prices a
   passage and empties a room, and nothing about selection entails either.
4. *Which Austin.* Conceded and named: the real 1990 city is the material base, the film's a stitched
   city — Gaughen's three-mile jump played as a walk, and Linklater's "if you didn't know Austin, the
   film makes sense… they're still walking right to left."
5. *Is §6 self-defeating.* No, once narrowed: cinema trained a competence at receiving an arrangement
   and none at answering back, and *Slacker* is where that training becomes visible because no plot is
   there to absorb the credit.
6. *"Host" is unfalsifiable.* Conceded — "a setting coordinates" is a placement, not a mechanism — and
   the selector half carries a falsifier the transcript can decide: show the order arising inside the
   story world and the claim dies.
7. *Suppressed prior art.* Cited and marked second-hand: Fuhse's *konkreter/abstrakter Dritter*
   anticipates the placement, and O'Reilly–Strauss–Mazzucato and Meyer et al. are right that matching
   and attention coexist on most platforms, which is why I split powers, not platforms.
8. *A fourth power.* Yes, and it belongs to the regime — withholding the reason; Amazon promises a
   verdict and, "unless otherwise indicated," no appeal, while the club door gives a reason you can
   dispute ("I'm supposed to be").
9. *Enclosure.* Dropped; the Smits fact that would earn it was never entered and Tzioumakis's refilled
   middle tier stands against it, so §7 argues replacement plus one documented closure.

**The abstract's commitments.** *Simmel's third* — kept, at the fourth kind he named on p. 145 and
deferred, with the deferral stated. *The Austin/camera contrast* — kept, renamed setting/selector,
narrowed to the film's Austin. *The Uber parallel* — kept, narrowed to one shared power, the rest moved
into the regime, which is the abstract's "political economies differ" made specific. *The training
claim* — kept in its darker form: a century of reading, no century of reply. *The closing questions* —
answered at the film's own doors, where a coordinating third is contestable because its rule is spoken,
general and gameable: a stated cover, a 2:00 announcement, a hand stamp anyone can transfer.

Twenty-four segments, all dialogue-level; roughly fourteen quoted lines, all marked subtitle-derived; no
paragraph without the film in it. Nothing from segment 35, which is wordless, and no format named for
any camera.

§3 is the section the frame stands on. From the transcript, fewer than a third of the handoffs are
produced by something a character says or does — "Why don't you come look?", "How about a lift back into
town?", "Just drop me off at this guy's house" — and the rest have no bridge in the dialogue at all, or
only a rhyme nobody in the film could hear: one man's "What a day, what a day" answered by a stranger's,
a cigarette asked for twice across a cut. The count is subtitle-derived and re-countable against the
disc, and it converts "the camera links the scenes" from figure into finding. The film says the form
itself, reading *Ulysses* aloud at the creek: "the last term of a preceding series… in a series
originating in and repeated to infinity."

**What I give up.** The clean equation. Camera-is-to-Uber stops being one move and becomes one shared
power out of five, less than the abstract's "formal and specific" promised, and a reader wanting a
theory of platforms will find four fifths of platform power filed under a term the chapter does not
develop. I give up any sociological contribution at the Austin end, and any claim to have named
something new, since Fuhse named the placement first. I give up enclosure and the streaming arc. What is
left is a film chapter with a borrowed vocabulary, one original count, and a concession on every page.
