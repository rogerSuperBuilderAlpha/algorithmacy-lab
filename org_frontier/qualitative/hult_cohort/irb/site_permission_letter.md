# Site permission letter — template for Hult signature

**For signature by a Hult programme officer senior to the investigator.** A principal investigator
who grants himself access to his own teaching site is a pattern review boards notice, and the
letter exists to remove that objection before it is raised. Roger Hunt does not sign this.

Send on Hult letterhead. Bracketed fields are for the signer.

## Notes for the author, not part of the letter

**Who signs.** Someone with oversight of the programme and senior to you. If the honest answer is
that nobody at Hult sits above you on this programme, say that to the analyst rather than finding a
signature — a board would rather hear the structure described accurately than see a letter that
implies an oversight relationship that does not exist. In that case the letter comes from whoever
holds the institutional relationship, and the protocol says plainly who decides what.

**Two bracketed conditionals need answers before this goes out**, and both are on the analyst-call
list: whether Hult runs its own review or requires a reliance agreement, and who owns the
repositories. If Hult owns them, participant consent may not be sufficient on its own for the
coordination record, and this letter is where that gets settled.
