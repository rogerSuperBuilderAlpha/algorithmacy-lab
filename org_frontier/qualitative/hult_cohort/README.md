# Hult cohort — qualitative study

The qualitative arm of the algorithmacy programme, and the empirical basis of **dissertation Paper
3**. Field site: the Hult Cohort Program, Fall 2026, sixteen weeks.

It runs beside the three-wave survey panel in
[`../../survey/cohort_algorithmacy/`](../../survey/cohort_algorithmacy/), which is a **separate
study under a separate protocol** (Bentley IRB 260511078, exempt 11 May 2026). The two share a
field site and nothing else. Neither depends on the other's approval.

## Why a qualitative arm at all

The panel measures three self-reported competences at three points and can show whether scores
move. It cannot show how anyone learns, what evidence they revise their working theories on, or
what a participant does when a gate decision strikes them as wrong. The last of those carries the
theoretical claim Paper 2 makes — that accountability requires a forum and cannot be supplied by
individual competence however skilled — and the panel has no accountability measure at all, by
design. Question 2 of the protocol is that claim's only empirical test, and it is written with a
stated falsification condition.

## The two things a reader should know first

**The investigator teaches the cohort he proposes to study.** That is the review's whole subject
and the protocol treats it as such. What it offers is structural rather than reassuring: the peer
gate decides advancement and the investigator casts no vote in it, a named third party outside the
programme holds the consent records and receives withdrawals, no interview touches a live outcome,
and the investigator is not told who declined until after the first gate cycle closes. The one
sentence the panel's consent could not carry forward — "Program staff who assess your work do not
see who chose to take part" — is replaced outright, because the PI conducts the interviews and will
know who sat for one.

**Timing is the binding constraint on the whole programme.** Approval has to be in hand before the
Fall cohort starts, which puts submission at roughly 12 August 2026 — ahead of the Lima paper due
10 September. Missing the cohort costs a full cycle. `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` shows the working.
