# Coding scheme — [study slug]

Commit this file before the fieldwork. It fixes the questions before the answers, and the git history is the
evidence that it did. Fill every section, then commit, then collect data.

## Interview guide

The backward-working questions for rule elicitation: from concrete recent instances to the conditions that
governed the action to a stated rule the party confirms or corrects. List the prompts. Mark the questions
that recover the system's rule, which the parties theorize from the outside.

## Bit calibration

For each node, the threshold that separates active from inactive, stated before any instance is coded. What
the bit collapses, and what a finer encoding would carry.

## Coding scheme and reliability

The categories applied to instances, the rule for assigning each, and the plan for a second independent
rater. The reliability bar (Krippendorff's α ≥ 0.80) and the member-checking step (showing the parties the
encoding and recording acceptance).

## Pre-registered verdict (model-bound only)

The expected verdict and the reason, from the anticipated structure of the rules, before computing. For a
stand-alone study, mark this not applicable.

## Falsification conditions

What observation would overturn each claim the study will make — a worker who is in fact substitutable, a
system that passed the determination through unchanged, a party who turns out idle. Written here, before the
field visit, and reported in `STUDY.md` whether or not they were met.
