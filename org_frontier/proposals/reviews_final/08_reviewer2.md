# Reviewer 2 — final pre-submission read

**Manuscript:** "When is a combination a configuration? Integrated information and the constitution of organizational wholes"
**Venue:** Organization Theory, special themed section on configurational phenomena (theory essays)

## 1. Verdict

**MAJOR REVISIONS.** The problem is real, two of the three contributions survive scrutiny, but the constitution criterion — the paper's foundation — rests on a stack of analyst choices the paper discloses without defusing, and the submission package does not yet back the numbers the text leans on.

## 2. What the paper claims

The essay claims configurational theorizing lacks a within-case test for constitution: given one arrangement modeled as interacting parts, is it a single joint determination or an aggregate? It imports IIT's partition calculus — stripped of the consciousness theory — and proposes: a combination is a configuration when every partition of its elements loses constraint (Φ > 0). From the criterion it derives membership (the complex, with a graded pivotality law over 660 enumerated forms), dissolution (three operators — substitution, bypass, constraint-lifting — none the inverse of formation), and a necessary/contingent distinction that gives power a formal seat: some core members do integrating work, others are held in place by a constraint, and lifting the constraint evicts only the second kind. Five exactly-computed toy results (interior quorums factor, rotations bind, lockstep factors, dispatch binds only if the commit reads the hidden party, maximal wiring factors) mark the criterion's distance from interdependence, fit, and density. QCA is positioned as complement, not rival.

## 3. Strongest attacks

### 3.1 The measure split guts the criterion's authority, and "a criterion needs zeros" begs the question

The paper's most striking results — the interior quorum, the synchronized veto, the maximal wiring — are the three places where surface interdependence maximally misleads, and by the paper's own admission (§2) all three flip sign under IIT 3.0: identical models, identical states, positive Φ. The supplement confirms it (E2 carries Φ = 1.2688 under 3.0 at every reachable state; E5 and E8 likewise positive). So the signature exhibits are not results about the models; they are results about IIT 4.0's partition family applied to the models. The paper's defense is that "a constitution criterion needs zeros" and that 4.0's partitions "include exactly the party-respecting cuts that detect substitutability." The first clause assumes what is at issue. Whether constitution is binary (zeros required) or graded (weak integration nearly everywhere, as 3.0 reports) is precisely the theoretical question, and the paper itself wants gradation whenever gradation flatters — Φ "prices the cheapest cut" for coupling strength in §3, membership is graded in §4 — and zeros whenever sharpness flatters. Choosing the measure version because it delivers the concept you intend to define is legitimate *stipulation*, but then say so: the real move is a definition of constitution as identity-sensitivity of the joint determination, with 4.0 as its formalization. Stated that way, honestly, the reader may ask what the Φ apparatus adds over the definition. The second clause (party-respecting cuts detect substitutability) is the paper's only substantive argument for 4.0 and it deserves a full paragraph, not a subordinate clause. As it stands: the essay's claim to be "the first [within-case ordering] with a decision procedure attached" is compromised by the fact that two versions of the decision procedure disagree on the paper's own showcase cases. Calling the binds-verdicts "family-robust" over a family of exactly two members does not repair this.

### 3.2 The state-dependence is disclosed and never theorized, and the verdict rule is asymmetric

The manuscript defines a verdict of *binds* as positive Φ **at some reachable state** (§2), while *factors* requires zero at every one. Look at what this policy does in the supplement. The dispatch triad (E6), the paper's flagship platform exhibit, has Φ = 0 at four of its five reachable states and binds only at 111 — driver, system, and rider all simultaneously on. The strict-mediation triad (CTRL+) binds only at 111. The any-one quorum (E1) binds only at 0000: the arrangement is a configuration precisely and only when *nobody is active*. Unanimity (E3) binds only after everyone has signed. What does it mean, organizationally, that the ride-hail configuration exists in the instant of a fulfilled match and is an aggregate the rest of the time — that the "everyone must sign off" whole exists only at the moment of unanimous assent? The manuscript never asks. And the celebrated "sharp boundary" of the quorum result (extremes bind, interior factors, "no gradient") is partly a policy artifact: extremes bind at exactly one state each under a max-over-states rule that a single positive state satisfies. A different, equally defensible policy — Φ at the typical operating state, or expected Φ over the reachable distribution — would soften or move the boundary. A criterion for *constitution*, of all things, owes the reader an account of why a whole that exists at one state out of sixteen counts as a whole.

### 3.3 The degrees of freedom compound, and the reproducibility package has holes

Take 3.1 and 3.2 together with the paper's own encoding result: four of ten stylized cases flip verdict under a defensible re-encoding (§2, §7). The verdict is thus a function of (a) the encoding, on which defensible alternatives disagree 40% of the time, (b) the measure version, on which the two available versions disagree on the three subtle cases, and (c) the state policy, which is asymmetric by fiat. The paper sells decidability — "decidable relative to a stated encoding" (§4) — and its own admissions dissolve the decidability into three analyst-chosen arguments. The reframing of (a) as "informative displacement" is elegant but does not answer the practical question: two honest analysts modeling the same platform will, on the paper's own numbers, reach opposite verdicts about who is bound roughly as often as not. Worse, for a manuscript whose banner promise is "every number cited as an illustration is exactly computed and reproduces": Part C of the supplement — the source of the four-in-ten figure cited twice in the main text — is a placeholder ("will be included in the submission package"). The §4 coalition case, the five design moves of §5, the 660-form enumeration, and the §7 ride-hail/marketplace models likewise have no supplement backing; Part A covers §3's exhibits only, and the quorum tables cover n = 3 although the text claims computation at four and five parties. A final-submission package that documents the easy half of its claims and IOUs the load-bearing half is not final.

### 3.4 The catalog does not sort; the coders do

Supplement Part B concedes, creditably, that computed class follows the structural template *by construction* — margin is constant within template — so the zero-mismatch tally "checks the pipeline's consistency, not a prediction." But §5 of the manuscript still says "the distinction sorts a wide range of cases" and reports quarter/half proportions as if the criterion produced them. It did not; the coders assigned templates and the templates entail the classes. The manuscript's hedge ("disciplines the distinction rather than testing it") is itself generous — a computation that cannot disagree with its input disciplines nothing. And the coding harbors an unresolved tension with the manuscript's own theory: §5 argues that once necessity is cost-relativized, "a middleman that only economizes a real cost is doing integrating work of Williamson's kind, not holding a gate" — yet the catalog codes the search-friction cases (arbitrageur, freight broker) as *contingent*, friction listed as the constraint. By the manuscript's sentence they are necessary-side; by the catalog's rule they are contingent. The paper's two halves classify the same case oppositely.

### 3.5 Cost-relativized necessity hands the distinction back to economics, and the best idea needs the least IIT

Press the cost-relativization further. Once "restore the forbidden tie" means "restore it at a stated cost," the necessary/contingent binary becomes a threshold on a continuum of bypass costs — which is the continuum platform economics already owns as disintermediation and bargaining theory owns as the outside option, both cited by the paper. What remains distinctively new is the whole-partition version of the test (the coalition exhibit), and the paper's own summary concedes the division: "the plain counterfactual signs the verdict; the calculus locates it." The verdict — the part that carries §5's theory of power and the historical narrative — needs no Φ at all. The paper's strongest contribution is thus nearly detachable from its imported machinery, and the machinery-dependent contributions (the §3 zeros) are the ones that fail measure-robustness. That inversion should worry the authors more than it seems to.

### 3.6 The gap, after the paper's own credits, is narrower than the abstract claims

The abstract says the within-case question "has no formal test." Section 3 then credits Thompson with a within-case ordering of joint determination and §2 credits Simon–Ando with "its own mathematics" for decomposability. The residual gap is real but narrower: no *counterfactual-partition* test with a decision procedure. Even there the paper undersells its ancestor — Simon–Ando aggregation conditions are conditions on the transition matrix, i.e., on the rules, not merely on "realized structure" as §2 has it; the honest contrast is severed-constraint counterfactuals versus weak-coupling asymptotics, and the paper should draw it rather than the realized/counterfactual strawman. Finally, §7 disclaims organizational measurement in one paragraph and issues organizational verdicts in the next ("strict-mediation ride-hailing holds the dispatcher in the core... a partial-mediation marketplace... leaves the platform outside"). Provisional models of documented forms are still toy models; the disclaimers do not license the declaratives.

## 4. What survives

I want to be honest about what this hostile read did not dislodge. The problem statement (§1) is the best I have seen on the within-case gap: the boundary-by-research-design point, the coupling-without-a-scale point, and the dissolution-as-reversal point are each genuine and well documented. The identity-sensitivity versus outcome-pivotality distinction (§3's voting-theorist paragraph) is sharp, correct, and citable independently of the measure dispute. The rotation result — full constitution with no reciprocal pair, a case Thompson's typology cannot slot — is a real finding about the verbal canon. The dissolution section is the paper's core contribution and mostly survives: three operators, none an inverse of formation, the organizing/disorganizing asymmetry derived rather than asserted, and the self-liquidating tertius iungens is a derivation the verbal literature could not produce. Contingent irreducibility as the formal shape of power-maintained configuration is a concept the field can use, precisely because (per 3.5) it survives without the machinery. And the paper's candor — the measure split, the encoding flips, the by-construction catalog are all disclosed by the authors — is unusual and welcome. But candor inventories the weaknesses; it does not repair them.

## 5. What would change my verdict

1. **Own the stipulation (3.1).** Argue for identity-sensitivity as the concept of constitution on independent theoretical grounds, present IIT 4.0 as its formalization, and report both measures for every exhibit, reframing the three subtle factorings as measure-conditional in the main text — or find a third formalism that breaks the tie.
2. **Theorize the state policy (3.2).** Justify max-over-reachable-states against alternatives, and confront the organizational meaning of one-state wholes (the idle any-one quorum, the instant-of-match dispatch) explicitly.
3. **Complete the package (3.3).** Ship Part C and the missing §4/§5/§7 computations, or cut every number they support, including the four-in-ten figure.
4. **Rewrite the catalog paragraph (3.4).** Present Part B in the main text as a coding exercise, full stop, and resolve the friction-coding contradiction between §5's cost-relativization and the catalog's rule.
5. **Downgrade the §7 platform verdicts (3.6)** to hypotheses, and give Simon–Ando the accurate contrast.

None of this requires new theory; it requires the paper to stop cashing checks its own boundary section has already voided.

## 6. Bottom line

A genuinely interesting essay whose best ideas (dissolution operators, contingent irreducibility) barely need its imported machinery, and whose machinery-dependent showpieces depend on one contested version of a contested measure, an untheorized state policy, and encodings that flip 40% of the time — with the supporting computations for the load-bearing numbers not yet in the package. The field should eventually have this paper. It should not have this draft.
