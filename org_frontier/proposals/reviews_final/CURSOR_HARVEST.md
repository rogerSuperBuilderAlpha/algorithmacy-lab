# Harvest — the Cursor parallel draft (branch deleted 2026-07-09)

The remote branch `cursor/egos-configurational-cfp-proposal-255d` held a parallel run of the same OT-CfP
brief: a Cursor Agent, working 2026-07-08 from a 2026-07-03 base, produced its own proposal, a 6,353-word
manuscript with the identical title, a four-member prose-focused committee review of itself, and a
de-slopped rewrite. It was never merged and duplicated the submission package's paths. Before deletion,
its committee review — the one artifact with a lens our four panels lacked (structural fluency-tells
rather than sentence tics) — was tested against the current manuscript. This memo records the verdicts.

## Harvested (applied to the manuscript, this commit)

1. **Section roadmap → compact first-person itinerary.** The Cursor committee wanted the §1
   section-by-section tour deleted outright; the house style keeps "one compact, first-person itinerary."
   The house style wins on form, the Cursor committee on diagnosis: the numbered tour is gone, replaced
   by a two-sentence first-person itinerary.
2. **Meta-scaffolding openers cut.** "Section 3's quorum result, read dynamically" / "Section 4's
   fringe, read dynamically" — the reader is already in section 5. Replaced with the image, not the
   section number.
3. **"This essay" self-reference reduced 11 → 3.** Convergent with the final panel's house-style seat
   (which counted the same tic); the survivors are the abstract, the §2 binds/factors flag, and the §6
   table's ladder-ownership note, each doing referential work.
4. **The five-piece primer's announcement sentence softened.** "The machinery … has five pieces" was the
   committee's exhibit for outline-prose; the header structure stays (see rejections), the announcement
   goes.

## Rejected (with reasons)

- **"Metronomic punchy openers."** The committee's lead exhibit — "Organization theory borrows formal
  models from other fields. It always has." — is the sentence the repo's own style guide presents as the
  *good* example. Style-guide conflict resolved in the guide's favor.
- **Unbold the primer / exhibits / operators.** Four independent panels engaged these blocks without
  objection; the final panel's general reader called the worked box the paper's best accessibility
  device. Register call, made against the Cursor committee.
- **Cut the microscope metaphor entirely.** Already trimmed 3 → 2 in round 2; two instances
  (abstract, §2) are within the style budget.
- **Cut the closing line ("told from the heaps").** The house style permits closing on a concept that
  re-lands; four panels let it stand.
- **Consciousness handled once, not three times.** The three current instances do different work
  (abstract scoping, §2 provenance, §7 boundary); collapsing them would re-open the reviewer-fit risk
  the layering exists to manage.

## Not at issue

The Cursor draft's substance was the same pitch at round-0 altitude (its abstract still carried the
"no criterion… no account… no theory" foundation overreach that three panels forced out of the lab's
version), so nothing substantive was harvestable: every theoretical difference between the two texts is
work the four-panel/three-round process already did. The value of the parallel run was its committee's
one distinct lens, banked above.
