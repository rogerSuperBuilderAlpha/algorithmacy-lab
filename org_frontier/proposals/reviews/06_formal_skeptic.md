## Part 1 — Theory and structure

**§2** Three modeling commitments are the essay's best methodological honesty. Davis et al. (2007) appears only in §7 as limitation; simulation-as-theory-building should frame §2 from the start: small Boolean models are theory probes, not population inference.

**§3–§5** Population statistics (660 forms, ~10% triadic rate, 51-intermediary catalog, 4/10→9/10 membership) do argumentative work without a methods appendix. Header footnote to `ci/reproduce.json` is insufficient for external readers. Demote to "in one enumerated model class" or add Methods appendix.

**§7** Four-of-ten encoding flip on stylized cases is honest and should move earlier — abstract or §2 — because it defines what "criterion" means (verdict on stated encoding, not oracle).

**Consciousness objection:** §7 analogy to ecology/Williamson is directionally right but thin. One paragraph engaging a formal Φ critique (proxy validity, partition dependence) would disarm hostile readers without importing phenomenology.

**Methods trap:** CfP excludes pure methods. Essay stays theory if §6 does not read as procedure pitch. Current altitude is acceptable; avoid "computable property" without "on a stated model" every time.
