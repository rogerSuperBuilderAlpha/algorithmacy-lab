# House-style and prose audit — `ot_configurational_nature_2027_manuscript.md`

Audited against `~/.claude/writing-style.md` (house style), the repo's antithesis-count target, and the
register of *Organization Theory* (Stark & Vanden Broeck 2024; Cornelissen, Höllerer & Seidl essays).
Line numbers refer to the manuscript as read. Body length excluding references: ~7,000 words.

## 1. Verdict

**Minor pass needed.** One focused editing pass — not a rewrite. The manuscript already does the hard
things right (named agents in the literature review, concrete anchors, claim-first sections, real rhythm
variation, zero filler). What remains is a passive cluster in section 2, a gap-sentence drumbeat in
section 1, one verbatim repeated sentence, a handful of doubled antitheses, and an epigram density that
reads as optimization.

## 3. The worst ten offenders, with paste-ready rewrites

**1. Line 95–96 — agentless passive, no imaginer.**
> "its coming-apart is imagined as the formation running backwards"

Rewrite: *"the tradition imagines its coming-apart as the formation running backwards"*

**2. Line 128 — agentless passive at the formalism's introduction, with the builder cited in the same sentence.**
> "Integrated information theory (IIT) was built to characterize the physical substrate of consciousness (Tononi, 2004; …)"

Rewrite: *"Tononi built integrated information theory (IIT) to characterize the physical substrate of consciousness (Tononi, 2004; Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi, 2014; Albantakis et al., 2023)."*

**3. Lines 171–177 — the three-commitments paragraph is a passive cluster ("is encoded… is read…"), exactly where the analyst's agency is the point being made.**
> "a candidate arrangement is encoded as a small transition system over its parties, and the encoding is the analyst's construction… irreducibility is read along the lines between the parties"

Rewrite: *"the analyst encodes a candidate arrangement as a small transition system over its parties — the encoding is the analyst's construction of the coordination, not the coordination itself… the analyst reads irreducibility along the lines between the parties whose relation is in question."* (The paragraph itself argues that the encoder's choices carry the verdict; the grammar should show the encoder.)

**4. Lines 238–239 — verbatim repetition of a landing line already used at line 183.**
> "Surface description misleads in both directions: the busy quorum factors, the idle-looking cycle binds."

Line 183 already opens a paragraph with "The commitments matter because surface description misleads in both directions." Keep the line-183 instance, which is doing structural work, and rewrite the rotation paragraph's close: *"The rotation is the quorum's mirror image: the busy quorum factors, the idle-looking cycle binds."*

**5. Section 1's gap-sentence drumbeat — the same "no within-case test" claim stated four times (lines 66–71, 86–87, 98–99, 101).**
> "What none supplies is a within-case criterion for constitution itself…" / "yet without a within-case test for when the joint apparatus is one configuration…" / "no within-case test decides, for a given arrangement, whether it is one whole." / "This essay supplies that within-case criterion…"

This is the style file's named offense ("the same gap sentence… restated across sections"). Keep the full statement at 66–71 and the payoff at 101. At 86–87, cut "yet without a within-case test for when the joint apparatus is one configuration rather than an aggregate of smaller ones" to *"yet with no test for when the joint apparatus is one whole"* (also removes one "rather than" and one "aggregate of smaller ones," a phrase already used twice). At 98–99, the sentence can lean on the earlier statement: *"One place the lag shows is here, at the missing test."*

**6. Line 352 — self-narrating rigor: the prose praises its own answer before giving it.**
> "The irreducibility account answers it with unusual directness: dissolution is not formation run backwards, because dissolution has its own operators…"

Rewrite: *"The irreducibility account answers it: dissolution has its own operators, and none of them is the inverse of building a joint determination."* (Also removes one "is not X" — the operators themselves make the contrast.)

**7. Lines 188–189 — two ", not X" antitheses in adjacent sentences.**
> "These are model outputs on stated encodings, not field measurements. They show that constitution is a property of joint determination, not of headcount or wiring density."

Rewrite: *"Both are model outputs on stated encodings. What they show is that joint determination decides constitution; headcount and wiring density decide nothing."*

**8. Lines 452–453 — a decorative antithesis bolted onto a sentence that already makes the point.**
> "This is a partnership, not a takeover: the criterion adds a within-case test to a tradition whose across-case test already works."

Rewrite: *"The criterion adds a within-case test to a tradition whose across-case test already works."* The colon clause is the claim; the flourish in front of it is cadence. (The paragraph's opening already ran "complements… labour"; and line 71 already said "complement, not its rival" — this is the same contrast a third time.)

**9. Lines 568–569 — the closer stacks an agentless passive under the final epigram.**
> "Constitution, membership, and dissolution are stated here as concepts configurational scholarship can use with or without the borrowed formalism. With them, the wholes of configurational theory can be told from the heaps."

Rewrite: *"The essay states constitution, membership, and dissolution as concepts configurational scholarship can use with or without the borrowed formalism. With them, configurational theory can tell its wholes from its heaps."* (Active closer; keeps the heap image, which has earned its one reprise of line 490.)

**10. Lines 25 / 197 / 505 — the microscope metaphor run three times.**
> "the essay is theory, and the models are its microscope" (abstract) / "the microscope slides of the argument" (197) / "illustrated on the slides the microscope can hold" (505)

Keep the abstract's instance — it is the strongest and the first. At 197: *"three- to six-element Boolean models, each solved exactly and reported together with the rules that produce it."* At 505: *"illustrated on models small enough to solve exactly."* Related fix at line 500: "no organization has been measured" → *"the essay measures no organization"* (the style file's own exemplar sentence-shape: "The paper builds no construct and measures no case").

## 4. Findings by category

**Antithesis machine.** 4.3 per 1,000 words against a target of well under 5 — under the line, and far
below the ~7 the old drafts ran, but the distribution matters more than the mean. Five paragraphs carry
two or more (abstract lines 22–24; lines 179–181; 188–189; 266–269; 452–453), and by the house rule one
of each pair is decoration. The good news: most of the survivors are contrasts a reader would actually
reach for ("keep the formal syntax, rework the semantics"; "the formal core, not the theory of
consciousness"), which is exactly the licensed use. Fix the five doubled paragraphs (offenders 7 and 8
above, plus trim one of the two "rather than"s in lines 179–181) and this category is clean.

**Agentless passives.** The literature handling is genuinely good — "Miller called," "Meyer, Tsui and
Hinings defined," "Doty and Glick showed," "Simon argued," "Thompson named the mediating technology,"
"Ragin's truth tables… say precisely when." Zero "It has been shown" anywhere. The passives that remain
are methods-recipe passives ("is modeled," "is encoded," "is read," lines 136, 172–176) plus four
scattered ones (95, 128, 337 "The convergence is developed in section 6" → *"Section 6 develops the
convergence"*, 568). The §2 cluster is the one that costs something, because that paragraph's argument
is that the analyst's choices carry the verdict — the grammar hides the very agent the content foregrounds.

**Self-narrating rigor.** Nearly clean. "With unusual directness" (352) is the one true offender.
"Five results on small models, each exactly computed, mark the distance" (215) and "The dependence is
real and measurable" (178) are borderline — they assert facts about the computation rather than narrate
virtue, but "exactly computed / exactly solved / exactly computable" appears five times across the
essay, and by the third occurrence it starts to perform. Keep the header note and line 191 (where
computability is the claim); let 215 and 499 drop the adverb.

**Em-dashes.** 38, mostly the licensed kind: coined-construct expansion ("the minimum-information
partition — the cut that does the least damage"), staged reveal ("a distinction — necessary against
contingent irreducibility — that answers the disorganizing question"), and list-expansion inside a
claim (line 553's "a data moat, an exclusive, a regulatory license"). These should stay. Four or five
are paired bolt-ins that interrupt rather than expand — lines 320–321 ("— the arrangement that invites a
two-dyads reading —") and 387–388 ("— same wiring, same verdict, same core —") are the clearest; each
would read better as a trailing clause or a colon. Do not zero this category; the corpus (Cameron,
Stark) runs dashes at comparable density.

**Uniformity of optimization.** The manuscript's biggest residual tell, and it is a distribution problem,
not a sentence problem. Six of seven sections close on an epigram ("A conjunctive account… is an account
whose model does not factor"; "become, on a stated model, decidable"; "which is what happens when the
constraint falls"; "share a criterion"; "told from the heaps"), and inside section 3 all five results
land on a quotable line ("Connection is not constitution… if it could have read anyone"; "the busy
quorum factors, the idle-looking cycle binds"; "the constraint structure has to be computed"; "Every
surrogate that counts parties or channels would miss it"). Each landing is good; all of them together
is the metronome. The cure the style file prescribes is plainness, not more polish: pick two or three
paragraph-closers per section and let them end on content ("…and the whole factors." full stop). The
section-3 results and section-5 operators are the places to flatten first, because the template already
gives them structure — they do not need the epigram too. Keep "Connection is not constitution" (the
essay's best line) and the final heap sentence; sacrifice some of the middle-rank landings around them.

**Verbatim repetition.** Four clusters: (a) the §1 gap sentence ×4 (offender 5); (b) "Surface
description misleads in both directions" ×2 verbatim (offender 4); (c) the microscope metaphor ×3
(offender 10); (d) "aggregate of smaller ones" ×3 in the first 90 lines (fixed inside offender 5).
"Writes X out of the core" recurs four times (375, 378, 414, 472) but functions as operator vocabulary —
technical reuse, not slop; leave it.

**Filler and empty closers.** Zero filler transitions, zero banned openers, no duplicate roadmap (the
one itinerary at lines 103–111 is compact and earns its place). The manuscript's closers err rich, not
empty — the problem above, not this one. One near-empty forward tag: "and section 5 builds on exactly
this" (329) could lose "exactly."

**Hedging.** Flat and specific throughout, and this must not be touched. "Its phenomenological ambition
is contested, and section 7 returns to what the controversy does and does not touch" (130); the four
boundary paragraphs of section 7; "Read as a property of the model class, not as a census of real
organizations" (273). This is the house style's own register ("The claim is contested"), executed well.

## 5. Register fit for *Organization Theory*

Strong on the dimensions that matter for a Catalysing & Crystallizing essay: it engages the call's three
themes explicitly and by name, argues conceptually with small formal illustrations rather than reporting
an empirical study, uses one summary table the way Stark & Vanden Broeck use theirs, and cites the
venue's own methodological charter (Cornelissen, Höllerer & Seidl 2021) as the rule the borrowing
follows — a well-judged move for this journal. Sentence texture (dense build punctured by short
declaratives: "It always has." "A rotation binds.") matches the OT essay register.

Two register notes. First, **person**: the manuscript's agent is uniformly "this essay" / "the present
account" — never "I" or "we." The OT exemplars all write in the first person ("we contend," "our aim"),
and the house style file calls impersonal third-person "the single biggest source of the robotic feel";
the repo's project style, however, bans first person, and the manuscript executes the impersonal
register consistently and without strain. This is a deliberate decision to make, not an error to fix:
if the project rule holds, keep it as is; if the venue's voice wins, add "we" at three or four analytic
hinges only (the section-1 contribution statement, the section-7 boundary paragraphs) and leave the rest.
Second, **length**: at ~7,000 words of body the essay sits below the 10–12k that OT theory pieces
typically run; that is a scope question for the authors, not a style defect — nothing here reads padded,
and nothing should be padded to fix it.

## 6. What is already good and must not be "fixed"

- **The named-agent literature review** (lines 42–53, 60–66): Miller called, Meyer/Tsui/Hinings defined,
  Doty and Glick showed, Simon gave it a name. Textbook execution of the style file's first rule.
- **Concrete anchors everywhere**: the majority gate, the four copyists, the dispatcher and the unseen
  rider, the escrow agent, the car dealer under franchise law, classified advertising's death. No
  mechanism is left abstract.
- **The five-result template in section 3 and the three-operator template in section 5.** These are
  sustained analytic categories, not decorative parallelism — precisely the pattern the style file
  protects. Do not break the template; only flatten some of its landings.
- **The rhythm.** Long citation-dense builds punctured by real short declaratives ("It always has,"
  "A rotation binds," "The mechanism is substitutability"). This is the corpus cadence, done well.
- **Flat hedging and the section-7 boundaries.** "Contested," stated once, returned to once, resolved
  at the right altitude.
- **Load-bearing em-dashes** that coin and expand constructs (necessary/contingent irreducibility,
  the minimum-information partition). Keep them.
- **"Connection is not constitution."** The essay's best sentence. It survives any antithesis purge.

## 7. Bottom line

The manuscript is close. Its mechanics are largely clean — zero filler, zero banned openers, named
agents where the literature is handled, hedging in the right register — and its antithesis rate is
under target. One pass fixes what remains: (1) activate the ~10 agentless passives, above all the
§2 encoding paragraph and the IIT introduction; (2) collapse the §1 gap-sentence to one statement
plus one payoff; (3) cut the second "surface description" sentence and two of the three microscope
reprises; (4) delete one antithesis in each of the five doubled paragraphs; (5) flatten roughly a
third of the paragraph landings so the epigrams that remain — "Connection is not constitution,"
"told from the heaps" — land against quiet, not against each other. Then decide the first-person
question as policy, not by default. After that pass, the read-aloud gate is the author's.
