## The spine fixes (structural; each flagged by 2+ Vega reviewers)

1. **Resize the foundation claim (08, 01, 02, 06).** Stop "no formal statement anywhere." Credit QCA
   *outcome*-constitution and rival formalizations (fit, complementarity, Simon near-decomposability, NK).
   Claim: a formal *within-case causal-unity* criterion for when a *modeled* arrangement is one joint
   determination — not *the* missing foundation of the whole tradition.

2. **Population / catalog frequencies → model-class properties (06, 08, 10, 07).** Fence 660 forms, ~10%
   triadic, 4/10→9/10 pivotality climb, and 51 intermediaries as properties of an enumerated Boolean class.
   Kill "configurational fact about the modern coordination landscape." Package for theory desk: Methods
   note or demote numbers to appendix.

3. **§6 consolidation altitude (08, 04, 06, 01).** Kill identity theater. Only Shapley has computation;
   other rows are structural correspondences. Put **QCA partnership at the center** of §6 (not a closing
   note). Soften Emirbayer/Kimsey "collapses into identity."

4. **Gestalt / level-of-analysis gap (02, 01).** Φ on mediated Boolean parties ≠ yet shown identical to
   Miller / Miles–Snow / Mintzberg / Doty–Glick gestalts. Supply encoding protocol or one worked typological
   sketch; do not leave "re-derive the canon" promissory.

5. **Classical lineage before novelty (03, 07).** Thompson mediating technology; Powell once; Williamson
   make-vs-buy + essential facilities / multihoming as siblings of constraint-lifting. Defend contribution as
   function-level, rule-based, model-stated — not as first discovery of bypass.

6. **Systems siblings, not captures (05, 02).** Simon near-decomposability ≠ Φ "made exact." Orton & Weick
   distinctiveness/responsiveness must not collapse to a Φ scalar. Re-engage NK as search sibling; mark
   emergence off from power-held (contingent) irreducibility.

7. **Encoding as contribution, early (06, 10, 08).** Relativize constitution to the three modeling
   commitments. Theorize the 4/10 flip. Front-load Davis et al. (2007) as simulation-as-theory standards,
   not only calibration hedge in §7.

8. **Platform citation depth (04).** Soften pairwise indictment of Kellogg-era work; add Cutolo & Kenney /
   Rahman where membership and power need them; treat Rochet–Tirole/Armstrong as price/bottleneck theory
   (not Φ in other clothes); bypass as multihoming continuum.

9. **External reader checkability (10, 07).** Remove lab-path/CI leaks from public prose; every number and
   "try this at home" claim evaluable without private repo.

10. **House style (09).** Antithesis already inside budget (~2.3/1k). Primary: cut ~74 em-dashes by ~half;
    purge self-narrating altitude phrases; trim stock refrains and self-congratulatory close.
