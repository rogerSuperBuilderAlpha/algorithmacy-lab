## Part 1 — Theory and structure, §1–§7

### §1 The unformalized foundation
The opening move is correctly configurational. The special section's three questions share one prior question — when is a combination a configuration at all — and that framing fits a *Catalysing & Crystallizing* submission. The lineage is complete: Miller's gestalts; Meyer, Tsui and Hinings's program definition; Miles and Snow / Mintzberg as canonical wholes; Doty and Glick's rehabilitation of typologies as theory; Ragin–Fiss set analysis and the neo-configurational consolidation (Misangyi et al.; Furnari et al.; Campbell & Fiss).

The three surrogates — interdependence, fit, co-occurrence — are §1's sharpest move: each is a symptom of wholeness, not constitution itself. Quattrone and Zilber's disorganizing question is correctly positioned; Furnari et al.'s observation that methods outran theorizing serves the right purpose — the shortage begins at the foundation.

Two cautions. The platform/contract paragraph is apt for the call but sits outside the configurational *canon* until the essay shows that "configuration" here is the same object Miller and Meyer et al. meant, earned on typological material and not only mediator models. And "a claim of constitution" recurs as a near-verbatim refrain (Part 2).

### §2 A borrowed formalism
The borrowing frame is disciplined and matches organization theory's precedents (ecology, TCE, NK landscapes). Cornelissen, Höllerer and Seidl on keeping formal syntax while reworking semantics is the right citation. The five pieces and the three modeling commitments are the right load-bearing disclosure for a reader who mistrusts black-box import.

The pressure point from the canon is **level of analysis**. Miles and Snow's defender and Mintzberg's machine bureaucracy are gestalts at the level of strategy–structure–process couplings. The microscopy here is three- to six-element Boolean party models of mediated coordination. Both can be "configurations" formally, but a Doty and Glick–trained referee will ask whether Φ on a dispatch triad is the *same theoretical object* as the typological configuration whose ideal-type distance those authors scored. The essay should say early that the formalism travels across levels — party, unit, or attribute package — provided the encoding states the couplings, and that the mediated-triad illustrations are microscope slides, not the full domain of the claim. Without that sentence, the borrowing looks like a platform paper in configurational clothing.

The QCA translation note closing §2 is excellent: within-case causal unity versus across-case condition-set membership is the division of labour a neo-configurational reader needs before §6.

### §3 Constitution: the criterion
This is the payoff of §1's surrogate critique, and it largely lands. Quorum (interdependence without constitution), rotation (constitution without the look of it), synchronization and maximal wiring (wiring without constitution), and dispatch (appearance without constitution) cut *inside* the space the surrogates would score as configured. "Connection is not constitution" is earned.

Three demands remain.

1. **Gestalt versus causal irreducibility.** Miller and Meyer et al. meant more than non-factorability of a transition system. A gestalt is coherent *as a type*: attributes reinforce one another, and deviation from the ideal type predicts poorer performance (Doty and Glick). Irreducibility formalizes joint determination; it does not by itself recover thematic coherence or equifinal attribute packages. A majority quorum that factors under Φ could still be coherent in Miller's sense. The essay should acknowledge that constitution-as-irreducibility covers the causal-conjunction strand and leaves the coherence/ideal-type strand partially open — or show, on one typological encoding, that thematic coherence and irreducibility travel together.

2. **Fit and complementarity are named then under-engaged.** §3's results mostly defeat interdependence and network density. A referee will want one worked contrast with fit: an internally consistent package that still factors, or a poorly fitting package that still binds. Without it, "what the tradition was reaching for" overreaches relative to the fit program's own formalizations.

3. **Population rarity.** That genuine triadic constitution is on the order of a tenth of mediated three-party forms is a serious configurational fact — keep it — with a one-line caveat that the population is a defined model catalog, not a field sample. Tsoukas's conjunctive theorizing is aptly placed; linking AND-structure to non-factorability is one of the cleanest crosswalks in the manuscript.

### §4 Membership: cores and peripheries
For the canon this section is highly valuable. The complex as *output* boundary answers Meyer et al.'s suspicion that the research-design element list and the real whole diverge. Bidirectional coupling as necessary, and pivotality-graded membership, give Miller's "central orchestrating themes" a computable sense: high average marginal contribution to irreducibility.

Two pressures. First, **Grandori and Furnari (2008)** already called for laws of combination rather than lists of types; the membership law and the holistic-ceiling example *are* combinatory chemistry results. Citing them only in §6 wastes a lineage claim that belongs here. Second, **QCA already uses "core" and "peripheral"** for across-case recipe roles (Fiss, 2011). One differentiation sentence prevents a terminology fight: QCA cores are across-case causal recipes; these cores are within-case constitutive centers. The Shapley bridge is intriguing and honestly scoped.

### §5 Dissolution: operators, and the necessary and the contingent
This section answers Quattrone and Zilber directly. Substitution, bypass, and constraint-lifting are operators that are *not* formation run backwards. The asymmetry claim — formation builds joint determination; dissolution needs one operator to fire — is the right payload for the dynamics theme. Necessary/contingent irreducibility seats power as a maintained constraint and sorts brokerage (Simmel / Burt / Obstfeld), including the claim that completed *tertius iungens* is self-liquidating. Keep both.

Two refinements. **Power as constraint is necessary but narrow** — agenda control inside an already-bound whole sits closer to §4's pivotality. **Siggelkow's (2002) formation sequences** belong in the dynamics payoff now: the same graded membership predicts formation order in reverse. The intermediary catalog is useful but pulls toward platform economics; one typological dissolution vignette would do more canon work than another franchise-dealer sentence.

### §6 One test in four vocabularies
The consolidation table is the right ambition, and the altitude split — Shapley as computed agreement; other rows as structural correspondences — is honest. The QCA division of labour is the paragraph a neo-configurational editor will keep: a QCA-configuration can be an aggregate, and a causal unity can be invisible to set analysis. Against Furnari et al. (2021) specifically: irreducibility answers "is this one whole?"; it does not replace their process heuristics for articulating and evaluating combinations over time. State the complementarity: constitution criterion underneath, Furnari-style heuristics above. Kimsey et al. and Emirbayer are apt.

### §7 Boundaries of the borrowing, and an agenda
The four boundaries hold the right altitude: models are models; encoding is a theoretical commitment (the ten-case re-encoding sensitivity is exactly the honesty wanted); consciousness is not at issue; interpretive configurations are out of scope.

The agenda's first item — *re-derive the canon* — is the make-or-break promise. Miles and Snow and Mintzberg "stated with enough structure to model" is asserted; *how* one encodes defender couplings as a transition system is unspecified. Either supply one worked encoding sketch of a canonical type, or soften the claim to a research program whose first deliverable is an encoding protocol for typological couplings. Without that, §7's bravest sentence is a promissory note on the objects that define the audience. "Theorize the fringe" and "Map the constraints" are well ordered; canon re-derivation should lead so the essay does not confirm the suspicion that the configurational frame is scaffolding for a mediation paper.

**2. Verbatim refrain — "parts take their meaning from the whole."** §1 Meyer gloss and §3 restatement. Keep the attribution in §1; §3 can invoke it by shorthand.

**3. Antithesis machine — moderate, mostly earned.** Surrogate-vs-criterion contrasts that defeat a wrong alternative a reader would reach for should stay. Trim restaging of lack-then-supply in the §7 close, and do not re-punch "not formation run backwards" when introducing each operator after the section's opening statement.

**4. Self-narrating rigor — light.** "inspectable rather than asserted" (§2) can become "Φ is a property of the model these three choices define." "Theoretical economy a pluralist field can actually use" (§6) — cut; let Cornelissen et al. carry the warrant.

**5. Opener uniformity — mild.** Parallel result and operator headers in §3–§5 aid enumeration; within tolerance.

Overall slop verdict: **clean, with refrain trimming.**

## Part 3 — Line-level revisions (paste-ready)

**R1 — §2, level-of-analysis declaration.** Add after the five pieces:
> The formalism is level-agnostic in principle: elements may be parties, units, or the coupled attributes of a typological gestalt, provided the encoding states who reads whom. The illustrations use small mediated-party models because those are computable under exact Φ. They are the microscope slides, not a restriction of the claim to platforms.

**R2 — §3, gestalt half-cash acknowledgment.** Add after the "reaching for" sentence:
> Irreducibility formalizes the causal-conjunction strand — joint determination that no partition preserves. It does not by itself exhaust Miller's thematic coherence or Doty and Glick's ideal-type distance. Whether a typological gestalt's claimed couplings are irreducible is a further check; the criterion covers constitution as non-factorability, which is the strand the surrogates left open.

**R3 — §3, one fit contrast.** Add after the maximal-wiring result:
> Fit can diverge from constitution. A package whose attributes match on a deviation score can still factor if its rules admit a cheap cut; an ill-fitting package can still bind if its rules jointly determine what no part determines alone. Complementarity raises joint returns and still leaves open whether those returns survive partition.

**R4 — §4, elevate Grandori and Furnari.** Add after the exclusion result:
> Grandori and Furnari (2008) called for a chemistry of organization — laws of which elements can co-constitute a whole rather than lists of named types. The complex answers that call for any stated model: membership is a finding about combination, not an input list.

**R5 — §4, QCA core/periphery disambiguation.** Add near the graded law:
> These cores and peripheries are within-case constitutive centers graded by pivotality. They are not Fiss's (2011) across-case core and peripheral conditions in a set-analytic recipe. The words overlap; the objects differ.

**R6 — §5, broaden the power seat.** Add in the agency/power payoff:
> Constraint-lifting captures power as a maintained forbidden tie. Power as agenda control inside an already-bound whole — whose state is pivotal, who authors the commit — is already graded by the membership law of section 4. Contingency is one seat of power, not the only seat.

**R7 — §5, Siggelkow link now.** Add in the dynamics payoff:
> The same fringe grades formation in reverse. Siggelkow's (2002) sequences of elements added toward fit acquire a predicted order if early pivotal members shape which later couplings can bind — a hypothesis section 7 flags and section 4's law already licenses.

**R8 — §6, Furnari heuristics complementarity.** Add after the QCA division-of-labour paragraph:
> The partition test does not replace Furnari et al.'s (2021) heuristics for articulating and evaluating configurations as theoretical arguments. It sits underneath them: a candidate combination can be checked for constitution before it is elaborated as a causal recipe across cases.

**R9 — §7, canon-rederivation deliverable.** Tighten the first agenda bullet:
> *Re-derive the canon.* The first deliverable is an encoding protocol that turns a typological claim — Miles and Snow's defender couplings, Mintzberg's machine-bureaucracy coordinating mechanisms — into a stated transition system and a partition family. Only then is "the gestalt is irreducible" a checkable claim rather than a metaphor. Failures under that protocol falsify either the encoding or the type's status as a configuration, and either result advances the theory of that type.

**R10 — Part 2 fixes:** apply refrain and self-narration trims in items 1 and 4 above.

## Verdict

**Major revisions.** The essay identifies a real foundation gap, states a criterion that cuts where interdependence, density, and co-occurrence do not, and gives dissolution operators that answer Quattrone and Zilber without reducing disorganizing to formation reversed. Membership via the complex is a genuine gift to a tradition that has long let research design set the whole's boundary. What stands between this manuscript and configurational-canon acceptance, in priority order: (1) the **level-of-analysis and gestalt gap** — Φ on mediated Boolean parties is not yet shown to be the same theoretical object as Miller / Miles and Snow / Mintzberg / Doty and Glick configurations, and §7's "re-derive the canon" remains promissory without an encoding protocol or worked typological sketch; (2) **under-engagement of fit and complementarity** as rival formalizations; (3) **Grandori and Furnari and QCA core-language** need earlier, sharper placement. None is fatal; R1–R9 strengthen the affirmative configurational claim rather than retreat from the formalism.

**Most important fix:** R1 + R9 together — declare level-generality early, and make canon re-derivation a concrete encoding protocol (or supply one typological sketch), so the special-section audience sees its own objects under the microscope rather than only the platform triad.

## Biggest strength

The **constitution criterion paired with dissolution operators that are not formation reversed**. Showing that densely wired and co-occurring arrangements can still factor — while idle-looking cycles can bind — forces the tradition past its surrogates. Adding substitution, bypass, and constraint-lifting, with the necessary/contingent split that seats power as a maintained forbidden tie, gives configurational theory a dynamics vocabulary Quattrone and Zilber called for. The within-case / across-case division of labour with QCA is the cleanest consolidation move in the paper.

## The one thing only the author can supply

A **worked encoding of at least one canonical typological configuration** (Miles and Snow defender, Mintzberg machine bureaucracy, or another Doty and Glick–grade type) into the transition-system / partition vocabulary, with a reported Φ or complex verdict — or an explicit statement that no such encoding has yet been computed and that the canon-rederivation agenda is therefore unpaid debt. From the text alone, a configurational referee can see the criterion work on mediator models; only the author can show whether the gestalts that define the audience survive, fail, or require restatement under the same microscope. That single exhibit decides whether the essay re-founds configurational theorizing or remains a powerful formalization of mediated coordination under a configurational banner.
