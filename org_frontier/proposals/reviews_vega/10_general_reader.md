## Part 1 — Readability and arc (§1–§7)

### Overall arc
The manuscript has a clean three-beat spine — constitution, membership, dissolution — and an outsider can hold it after one careful read. §1 names a real gap; §2 imports a formalism; §3–§5 deliver criterion, cores/peripheries, and operators; §6 consolidates neighbor tests; §7 bounds the claim and opens an agenda. That architecture is journal-ready. The trouble is not the destination. It is the on-ramp and the evidentiary fog around the microscope.

A general reader leaves partly convinced that irreducibility names the missing foundation, and partly unsure how much persuasion rests on a research program they cannot see. That split organizes this review.

### Abstract and §1
The abstract states the contribution in ordinary language before the apparatus: a constitution criterion, a membership account, dissolution operators that are not formation run backwards, and a necessary/contingent split. §1's literature arc is enough for an interdisciplinary OT reader. The surrogate paragraph (interdependence, fit, co-occurrence) teaches the gap without specialist literacy.

Two early frictions:

1. **Lab leak in the title block.** The manuscript points to a local planning file and claims numbers reproduce from `ci/reproduce.json`. A special-section reader meets a file path before a thesis. Replace with: illustrations come from small Boolean models archived in a supplementary appendix or public replication package.

2. **"Unexpected neighbour" without a motive clause.** Consciousness science is a leap. Before Tononi is named, one sentence should say the neighbour built partition mathematics of wholes that OT already gestures at with coupling and gestalt language.

### §2 — half translation, half glossary
Elements, cause-effect structure, partition, Φ, and the complex arrive in accessible order. Simon and modularity land the partition as a familiar idea made exact. The consciousness hedge is the right altitude.

Where outsiders still get lost:

- **Φ is branded before it is felt.** Non-computational readers hear "information" as Shannon. Add one plain sentence: the cheap cut removes joint constraint on pasts and futures that noised-apart parts can no longer impose; Φ records how much survives the gentlest cut.
- **Three modeling commitments arrive compressed.** One worked encoding sketch (driver, rider, dispatcher; states and rules) before the commitments would let an outsider feel the model.
- **"Several hundred forms" / continuous integration.** Population claims are unanchored without the repo. Demote to "in the stylized catalog" or attach a visible methods note. Interdisciplinary referees forgive borrowed math; they do not forgive unverifiable censuses about "the modern coordination landscape."

Elevate the QCA note (across-case recipes vs. within-case wholes). Many generalists otherwise hear Ragin throughout.

### §3 — exhibits carry the essay; density crowds the first pass
Quorum and rotation teach the criterion without computing Φ. Dispatch is the platform payoff and should stay. After those two, synchronization and maximal wiring refine the same lesson for specialists — three exhibits with a one-sentence take-home would be kinder; move the rest to a footnote or appendix.

"On the order of a tenth of the population" will get quoted, then challenged. Soften to "on the stylized population examined here" or supply a one-page supplementary frame.

### §4 — membership intelligible; Shapley needs a handshake
Spectator exclusion and bidirectional coupling are intuitive. Cores and peripheries as graded causal structure will land for anyone who teaches Miles & Snow. The Shapley bridge is correct and hard: "integration game" appears without rules. A three-sentence box — elements as players, coalition integration as value, Shapley as average marginal contribution — turns a name-drop into a crosswalk. Forward-point §4's "boundary as output" claim to §7's encoding sensitivity so it does not sound like automatic ontology extraction.

### §5 — highest payoff, densest stretch
Substitution without cutting wires, bypass that leaves a party busy but outside the complex, and constraint-lifting that splits necessary from contingent irreducibility are memorable. Escrow vs. franchise dealer teaches the counterfactual in one pass. Process asymmetry and power-as-maintained-constraint answer the special section's themes in seminar-portable language.

Overcrowd points: the five design moves on a strict-mediation triad are a catalog; keep open-channel and constraint-lifted conduit as worked cases, table the other three. The "fifty-one intermediary" quarter/half taxonomy asserts a field result without a field-visible source — call it illustrative pending published catalog, or attach the catalog. Simmel / Burt / Obstfeld consolidation should stay near the surface; it reconnects formality to teaching-canon names.

### §6–§7
The vocabulary table serves economists (Rochet & Tirole, outside options), sociologists (relation, commit), and methods readers (null player). Soften "One test" to a shared-verdict frame; the manuscript already wisely separates computed Shapley agreement from structural correspondence elsewhere. Keep the QCA division-of-labour paragraph prominent.

§7's four boundaries restore trust — models are models; encoding flips verdicts; consciousness is out; hermeneutic configurations stay hermeneutic. The agenda is actionable only if a follower can attempt it without the author's tree. State the starter kit: a handful-element transition system, the party-partition rule, and a Φ tool or published lookup for small forms.

## Part 2 — Style / slop audit

Register holds. Impersonal voice is consistent. Residual issues that bite a general reader:

**1. Antithesis cluster in §1 and closes.** "Stands in for constitution" thrice, then "None states the thing itself." Compress surrogates to one contrast plus a positive gap sentence. Keep quorum/rotation and necessary/contingent.

**2. Self-narrating rigor — sparse tags.** "Inspectable rather than asserted"; "altitude of the claim should respect"; "theoretical economy a pluralist field can actually use"; "with full rigor in a neighbouring science." Delete announcements; leave warrants.

**3. Mechanized openers — mild in §4.** Vary the "first result / second result / the law has" run.

**4. Verbatim refrain — configuration vs. aggregate / heap.** Full criterion once in §3; later "partition verdict" or "whether the whole factors."

**5. Insider deixis.** "The program," "microscope slides," "continuous integration," local pathnames. Replace with "the illustrations," "small models below," "replication materials."

Triads (constitution/membership/dissolution; substitution/bypass/constraint-lifting) are earned. Overall: **readable theory prose with local compression and de-insidering required.**

## Part 3 — Reader-facing revisions (paste-ready)

**R1 — Title block.** Replace path-and-CI language with: illustrations are small Boolean models archived with the submission or a public replication package; counts refer to that catalog, not field measurement.

**R2 — Early motive for the neighbour.** Add: the neighbour discipline already built partition mathematics of when interacting elements form one cause-effect whole rather than side-by-side parts; OT has long needed that test in its own semantics.

**R3 — §2, plain gloss of Φ.** Add: the cheap cut removes joint constraint — noised-apart parts no longer fix the same pasts and futures; Φ records how much of that constraint survives even the gentlest cut.

**R4 — §2, worked encoding before commitments.** Driver, rider, dispatcher on/off; dispatcher busy only when both request; that rule set binds; read-driver-only factors. Then name the three commitments as how such encodings stay inspectable.

**R5 — §3 resting take-home after quorum and rotation.** Busy connection can still factor; quiet circular dependence can still bind. Shorten or footnote the later wiring exhibits.

**R6 — §4 Shapley box.** Elements as players; coalition integration as value; Shapley as average marginal contribution; null players fall outside cores. Conceptual grading, not full transferable-utility identity with Φ.

**R7 — §5 compress design moves.** Worked cases: open channel; constraint-lifted conduit. Table owner-tilt, contestability, dashboard decoupling with one-line glosses.

**R8 — Census claims.** Wherever "a tenth," "660," or "fifty-one" lacks a visible source: "in the stylized catalog (see replication materials)" or move to a supplementary table the submission includes.

**R9 — §6 title.** Shared partition verdicts across four native tests — keep the altitude sentence separating Shapley computation from structural correspondence.

**R10 — §7 starter kit.** Transition system of a handful of elements, party-respecting partition, Φ tool or published lookup. Invitation to concepts, not to a private tree.

**R11 — Style pass.** Apply Part 2: compress §1 antitheses, cut self-narration, replace program/CI deixis, reduce configuration/aggregate refrain after the criterion is defined.

## Verdict

**Minor-to-major revisions for accessibility; the intellectual arc is already there.** A general OT / economics / sociology PhD can follow constitution → membership → dissolution and will remember necessary vs. contingent irreducibility and the dissolution operators. The essay loses that reader where lab infrastructure leaks into the public text, where §2 defines Φ without a plain "what is lost" sentence, and where §3–§5 stack more exhibits than a first pass can hold. That is packaging, not a theory veto.

**Most important fix:** R1 + R8 + R10 — make every number and every "try this at home" claim evaluable without the private dissertation or lab checkout.

**Biggest strength:** §5's necessary/contingent split under a bypass counterfactual, cashed out with escrow vs. franchise conduit and tied to power as maintained constraint. Portable seminar language; no need to believe IIT about minds; answers the special section's agency/power question cleanly.

**The one thing only the author can supply:** a journal-legible inventory of the model catalog (even a compact appendix: how forms were generated, what the censuses count, which encodings produced quorum/rotation/dispatch). Without it, an outsider must take the microscope on trust — and for a paper whose claim is formal decidability on stated models, trust is the wrong currency.
