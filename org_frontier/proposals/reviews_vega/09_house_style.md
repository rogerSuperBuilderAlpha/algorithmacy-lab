CLAUDE.md: if a paragraph has more than one, cut to one. Keep one earned contrast each for §5 ("dissolution is not formation run backwards"), §4 ("not a flat set"), and the §7 subhead "Consciousness is not at issue." Broader `is/are/does not` (15 hits, including ordinary negation) does not pad the priority tally.

### 5. Em-dashes

| Metric | Count |
|---|---:|
| Em-dashes in body | **74** (~11 per 1,000 words) |
| Paragraphs with two or more | **31 of ~73** (~42%) |
| Worst pockets | §7 Boundaries (~14), §2 (~12), Abstract / §1 |

House rule: at most one per paragraph, usually none. This is the dominant copyedit failure. Dashes are mostly paired appositions ("parties, units, roles, systems — each…"); commas, colons, or split sentences carry the same content. §2 Elements block and §7 Map-the-constraints each reach four dashes in one paragraph — cadence, not emphasis.

### 6. Refrains

1. **"stands in for constitution"** — three times in one §1 paragraph; keep the triad once or vary the verb on beats two and three.
2. **"without the label"** — ×3; keep once.
3. **"surface description misleads in both directions"** — §2 and §3; state once.
4. **"earns its keep"** — §3 and §5; cut both.
5. **"formation run backwards"** — abstract + §5; definitional once in §5.
6. **Closing triad encore** — §7 re-lists criterion / membership / dissolution already mapped in §1; refer back.

Vocabulary terms (`cause-effect structure`, `joint determination`, `formal core`) are not refrains — leave them.

## Verdict

**Minor revisions on style.** Publishable register, clean first person, antithesis already inside house budget, fragment openers sparse. Remaining work is copyedit concentration: **em-dash inflation** (primary), **self-narrating payoff and altitude phrases** (secondary), one **§2 antithesis cluster**, and **stock refrains** including a self-congratulatory close.

**Most important fix:** cut em-dashes by roughly half and enforce one-per-paragraph.

**Biggest stylistic strength:** §3's five computed contrasts and §5's operators mostly *state* results without announcing their own honesty — the model for cleaning §2's hedges and §7's close.
