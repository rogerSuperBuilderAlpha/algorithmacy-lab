## Part 1 — Theory and structure, §§1–7

### §1 The unformalized foundation
The opening move is correctly scoped: the gap is a *constitution* gap (when is a combination one causal whole?), not a methods gap. The surrogates — interdependence (Thompson 1967 → Milgrom & Roberts → NK), fit, co-occurrence — are the right family, and the claim that each captures a symptom of wholeness without stating constitution is fair.

Two cautions. First, Thompson (1967) appears only as the interdependence lineage. For a coordination theorist that is incomplete. *Organizations in Action* also supplies **mediating technology** — the classical name for linking clients who wish to be interdependent (bank, agency, exchange). The essay's illustrations lean on mediated triads; those are Thompson's mediating technology restated. Novelty sharpens if the lineage is named: mediating *technology* is old; the contribution is a criterion for when that mediation is one configuration rather than an aggregate of channels.

Second, the platform/contract paragraph never places the problem against Powell's (1990) three coordination logics or Williamson's governance forms. A referee will ask whether "configuration" here is a new object or a new *test* on familiar forms. The essay can answer — the criterion is level-agnostic — but §1 currently leaves the question to omission.

### §2 A borrowed formalism
The borrowing frame is disciplined (Cornelissen, Höllerer & Seidl, 2021): keep formal syntax, rework semantics. The five pieces are clear; the Simon/modularity link is apt; the three modeling commitments keep "property of the model" from becoming "property of the firm." One demand: **Powell is still absent.** The mediated-dispatch examples are network/hybrid coordination in Powell's sense. Saying so once would place the formalism inside the field's map of how activity is coordinated.

### §3 Constitution: the criterion
Strongest section for a coordination reader. The five model results (quorum, rotation, synchronization, dispatch, maximal wiring) earn the criterion. The quorum result travels; the dispatch contrast is the right platform exhibit.

Three presses. (1) Reciprocal interdependence is Thompson's strongest form; the essay shows reciprocal *wiring* can still factor. That separates technology of interdependence from constitution of a whole — state it explicitly. (2) Φ-as-coupling-scale and the rarity finding (~one tenth of mediated three-party forms bind) imply, for Powell's network form, that most mediated platforms as modeled are aggregates or conduits, not integrated wholes — a finding about the coordination landscape, not a computational footnote. (3) Soften "the property the tradition's language has been reaching for"; keep the contribution as a *decision procedure*.

### §4 Membership: cores and peripheries
Exclusion of spectators, bidirectional coupling as necessary, and pivotality as graded predictor are clean. The owner-outside-the-complex case is excellent: legal ownership ≠ causal membership. One ask: **Burt's structural holes are about network position, not pivotality in a cause-effect structure.** A one-sentence distinction — betweenness vs. contribution to joint determination — would prevent §6 from looking like a rebrand of structural holes.

### §5 Dissolution: operators, and the necessary and the contingent
Analytic core for this lens. The three operators (substitution, bypass, constraint-lifting) are a genuine coordination contribution: dissolution-as-not-formation-reversed answers Quattrone & Zilber with usable operators. Necessary vs. contingent irreducibility is the payoff, and the Simmel/Burt/Obstfeld recovery is correctly done: *tertius gaudens* and hole brokers as contingent; *tertius iungens* splitting into necessary integrator vs. self-liquidating joiner. That derivation is something the verbal literature could describe and this formalization yields.

Four gaps remain. (1) **Williamson / make-vs-buy is missing** beside the dealer example. Contingent ≈ held outside only by constraint; necessary ≈ genuine integration. Name TCE, then state the add: element-level counterfactual that relocates membership, not only a firm-level governance choice. (2) **Powell's hybrids.** Contingent conduits look like hybrid governance with a third-party enforcer; necessary integrators look like specialized joint work. Does the cut cross-cut market/hierarchy/network or collapse into it? Say which. (3) **Multihoming / essential facilities** already reach bypassability fairly formally; match §6's altitude and differentiate in one sentence. (4) Soften the dealer: dealers integrate inventory, financing, service — mark as limiting idealization, not "integrates nothing." **[VERIFY franchise-law framing against Lafontaine & Scott Morton.]**

Agency and power via contingent irreducibility is the right answer to the special section's agency theme once the TCE neighbor is named.

### §6 One test in four vocabularies
The table is useful; the altitude split (Shapley backed by computation; other rows as structural correspondences) is the right honesty. Missing, and felt from Part 1, are **Williamson** and **Burt** — not as fake computational rows, but as explicit neighbor sentences. The QCA division of labour is clean.

### §7 Boundaries and agenda
Four boundaries match the correct altitude. The ride-hailing vs. partial-mediation marketplace contrast is the best empirical hook: dispatcher in the core vs. platform as spectator. Elevate it earlier if word budget allows. Closing is competent; slightly ceremonial (see Part 2).

## Part 3 — Line-level revisions (paste-ready)

**R1 — §1, complete Thompson (after the interdependence lineage).** Add:
> Thompson (1967) also named the *mediating technology*: the bank, agency, or exchange that links parties who wish to be interdependent. The mediating technology is old. What the present criterion asks, and the interdependence typology did not, is when that mediation constitutes one causal whole rather than an aggregate of channels.

**R2 — §1 or §2, locate Powell once.** Add:
> Powell's (1990) market, hierarchy, and network remain the field's map of coordination logics. The criterion does not replace that map; it asks, of any arrangement drawn from it, whether the modeled joint determination factors.

Add Powell (1990) to the references.

**R3 — §3, reciprocal wiring ≠ constitution.** After synchronization or maximal wiring, add:
> Reciprocal interdependence, in Thompson's sense, is therefore not yet constitution. Parties can constrain each other, and the wiring can be complete, while the cause-effect structure still admits a partition that loses nothing.

**R4 — §5, name Williamson beside necessary/contingent.** Add:
> The distinction neighbors Williamson's make-or-buy problem and the asset-specificity intuition that genuine integration resists cheap externalization. What the bypass counterfactual adds is a reading off the coordination's causal rules, applied to an element inside a modeled arrangement, that relocates membership when the forbidden tie is restored.

**R5 — §5, soften the dealer.** Change "doing no integration franchise law does not force" to "doing little integrating work a restored direct tie would not reproduce under the same franchise constraint (Lafontaine & Scott Morton, 2010)."

**R6 — §5 or §6, honest neighbors.** Add:
> Brokerage theory reached related terrain through structural holes (Burt, 1992) and the *tertius iungens* orientation (Obstfeld, 2005); platform economics and antitrust reached bypassability through multihoming and essential-facility reasoning. The criterion's additions are a stated counterfactual on causal rules, a membership verdict, and the dissolution operators that follow.

**R7 — §4, Burt vs. pivotality.** Add:
> Pivotality here is contribution to joint determination, not network betweenness. A structurally central broker can still sit outside the complex if the determination does not read it, and a quiet escrow can sit inside it if the joint condition cannot be computed without it.

**R8 — Part 2 fixes.** Trim refrains; delete the two self-service sentences.

## Verdict

**Minor-to-moderate revisions.** The essay is conceptually ambitious, honestly bounded, and unusually useful to a coordination theorist: constitution-as-irreducibility separates interdependence from wholeness; membership computes cores against org charts; and necessary/contingent irreducibility plus the three dissolution operators give power and disorganizing formal seats. Relative to the special section's questions, this is already a serious contribution.

What stands between it and a clean accept, in priority order: (1) **complete the classical lineage** — Thompson's mediating technology, Powell once, Williamson beside necessary/contingent; (2) **defend against sibling distinctions** — structural holes, make-vs-buy, multihoming/essential facilities — with §6's altitude discipline; (3) **soften the dealer idealization.** None is fatal; R1–R6 mostly strengthen novelty by saying what is old and what is new.

**Most important fix:** R1 + R4 — name mediating technology and make-vs-buy so necessary/contingent reads as a refinement inside organization theory's vocabulary rather than an import that bypasses it.

## Biggest strength

The **necessary/contingent cut paired with dissolution operators that are not formation reversed**, cashed out on brokerage (Simmel / Burt / Obstfeld) and on mediated-platform models. Deriving that completed *tertius iungens* self-liquidates, while *tertius gaudens* persists only with the maintained gap, is a result coordination theory can use whether or not it adopts the full Φ apparatus.
