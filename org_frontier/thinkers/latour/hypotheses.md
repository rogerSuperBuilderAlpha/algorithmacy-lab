# Latour — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

An **intermediary** is a node whose rule reads exactly one other node and copies it: I' = X. A **mediator** is
a node whose rule reads more than its one input — another party (M' = X ∧ Y, joint) or its own state (M' = X
⊕ M, specificity). A node **makes a difference** to another when flipping its state changes the other's next
state at some reachable configuration (Boolean influence > 0). **Counting** is membership in the major
complex: a node counts for one when it is in the core, for nothing when it is not.

Latour's claims are read as claims about structure and tested as such. His vocabulary is anti-structural —
"attachments are first, actors are second" — and the rendering takes him at his word: a form is a set of
attachments, and which nodes count is what the instrument reads off.

## H1 — The intermediary counts for one (C1)

- **H1 (Latour).** Inserting a chain of k intermediaries into the tie of a mutual dyad (A ⇄ B becomes
  A → I₁ → … → I_k → B → A) leaves the whole's Φ unchanged and the core equal to {A, B}: the intermediaries
  are outside, however many there are.
- **H0.** Φ changes with k, or an intermediary is in the core.
- **Lab prior.** Against Latour. The chain is a copy ring of length k + 2, and copy cycles have read as
  irreducible wholes with every node in the core (Peirce H1, probe #374, on one-input wirings). An
  intermediary in a loop is expected to count for one *more*.

## H2 — The mediator transforms and therefore counts (C2)

- **H2 (Latour).** Between the same two parties, a mediator is in the core and an intermediary is not: the
  core of the joint-mediator form and of the specific-mediator form contains M; the core of the intermediary
  form does not.
- **H0.** Membership does not sort by intermediary/mediator.
- **Lab prior.** Half with Latour: the joint mediator is the lab's committing third (probe #19) and is
  expected in the core. The intermediary is expected in the core too (H1 prior), so the sort is expected to
  fail on the intermediary side.

## H3 — The actor is whatever makes a difference (C3)

- **H3 (Latour).** Every node that makes a difference to some other node's next state is in the major
  complex, and every node in the major complex makes a difference: membership = difference-making.
- **H0.** A node makes a difference and is not in the core.
- **Lab prior.** Against Latour on sufficiency. An exogenous source — a node that determines others and is
  determined by nothing but itself — makes a difference at every step and has read as outside the whole it
  drives (Peirce H4, probe #377: the exogenous object). Membership requires being read *and* returning
  (finding 3); Latour's trial tests only the first.

## H4 — The citizen-gun (C4)

- **H4 (Latour).** In the translation form (the act is C ∧ G; each of citizen and gun is reshaped by the
  act), the core is {C, G, X}: both participants and the act. In the neutral-tool form (G' = C, X' = G,
  C' = X) the gun is outside the core; in the autonomous-destiny form (G' = G, X' = G, C' = X) the citizen
  is outside. The two myths are structurally alike with roles swapped.
- **H0.** The translation form does not bind both, or a myth form binds the party it is supposed to reduce
  to an intermediary.
- **Lab prior.** With Latour on translation (a conjunctive triad) and on the autonomous form (the citizen
  is a reader only). Against him on the neutral tool: the gun there is an intermediary in a loop (H1 prior)
  and is expected in the core.

## H5 — The actor-network: attachments first (C5)

- **H5 (Latour).** A center attached to three mediators (M_i' = A ∧ M_{i+1}; A' = M₁ ∧ M₂ ∧ M₃) is a whole
  with them — core {A, M₁, M₂, M₃} — and has higher Φ than the same center attached to three intermediaries
  (M_i' = A; A' = M₁ ∧ M₂ ∧ M₃), whose core does not extend beyond A and whatever counts for one.
- **H0.** The mediator star does not bind all four, or the intermediary star binds as many.
- **Lab prior.** With Latour on the mediator star. Open on the intermediary star: three copies of A read
  back by A is A relayed to itself, and the relays may read as in the core (H1 prior) or not.
