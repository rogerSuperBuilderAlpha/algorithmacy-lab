# Hegel — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

A **term** is a Boolean node. **Mediation** is reading: X is mediated by M when X's next state depends on M's.
A **syllogism** with middle M and extremes A, B is the form in which the middle reads both extremes and
conjoins them (M' = A ∧ B) and each extreme reads the middle (A' = M, B' = M) — the lab's conjunctive triad,
which is also the instrument control. **Being one** is membership in the major complex; the whole's
integration is Φ. A term's **share** is what its removal costs: Φ(core of the whole) − Φ(core with the term
and its edges deleted).

Forms (rules in `methods.md`): **judgment** (A ⇄ B, no middle); **syllogism** (fixed middle M);
**communication** (relay: A holds, M copies A, B copies M) and **scent** (broadcast: A holds, M and B copy
A); **half-return** (M reads both; A reads M; B holds); **system** (every term reads the other two);
**rotating** (a three-phase clock names the current middle; that term conjoins the other two, which read it).

## H1 — The middle founds the connection (C1)

- **H1 (Hegel).** (a) In the syllogism the middle is in the core and necessary: delete M and Φ = 0.
  (b) The syllogism is more integrated than the judgment: Φ(syllogism) > Φ(judgment).
- **H0.** M dispensable, or Φ(syllogism) ≤ Φ(judgment).
- **Lab prior.** With on (a). Against on (b): the copy dyad and the conjunctive triad are both Φ = 2.

## H2 — The external middle connects without uniting (C2)

- **H2 (Hegel).** Communication and scent have Φ = 0 and no major complex containing the middle: the terms
  are connected (every extreme reachable from A) and not one.
- **H0.** Some relay form has a complex containing M.
- **Lab prior.** With Hegel; the conduit result (finding 3) restated.

## H3 — The real middle is one the extremes return through (C3)

- **H3 (Hegel).** In half-return, A (which reads M and is read by M) is in the core and B (read by M, not
  reading) is out; in the syllogism both are in.
- **H0.** B in, or A out.
- **Lab prior.** With Hegel.

## H4 — The system of three syllogisms has no distinguished binder (C4)

- **H4 (Hegel).** In the syllogism the middle's share is the whole (deleting M costs Φ = 2) and an extreme's
  share is nothing (deleting A leaves the dyad {M, B} at 2). In the system every term's share is equal and
  positive.
- **H0.** Shares unequal in the system, or an extreme's share positive in the syllogism.
- **Lab prior.** With Hegel on both.
- **Descriptive (not a verdict).** Whether Φ(system) equals the sum of the three syllogisms' Φ.

## H5 — The process of alternating middles is the totality (C5)

- **H5 (Hegel, Moss's reading).** The rotating form — each term middle in turn, by a clock — is a whole of
  three with the clock outside, and its Φ equals the system's (every term middle at once), not the single
  syllogism's.
- **H0.** Φ(rotating) equals the syllogism's, or the core is not the three terms.
- **Lab prior.** Against on Φ: Φ is a property of a state, and in any state only one term is the middle.
  With on the core.
