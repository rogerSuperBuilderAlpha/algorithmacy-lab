# Peirce — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

Peirce's "relation" among terms is rendered as an **irreducible distinction** in a form's cause-effect
structure (IIT 4.0, `pyphi.new_big_phi.phi_structure`): a mechanism in a state that irreducibly constrains a
purview, with φ > 0. Its **adicity** is the number of parties the fact is about, |mechanism ∪ purview|, taken
as the larger of the cause and effect sides. A form's **genuine adicity** is the maximum adicity over its
irreducible distinctions, maximized over reachable states. A form contains a *genuine triad* in Peirce's
sense when its genuine adicity is 3 or more; it is *dyadically degenerate* at genuine adicity 2 and
*monadically degenerate* at 1 or 0.

On the instrument control (A'=M, M'=A∧B, B'=M), M's cause purview {A, B} is an irreducible distinction of
adicity 3 with φ = 2.0, and A and B alone are adicity 2. That is the calibration: a two-input rule yields a
three-term fact.

Whole-system Φ and the major complex are reported alongside but are not the criterion for genuineness. A
form can be an irreducible whole (Φ > 0) while containing only two-term facts; whether Peirce's line and the
lab's coincide is itself a result.

## H1 — Triadic irreducibility (C1)

- **H1 (Peirce).** No form in which every element reads exactly one other element — every "complexus of
  dyadic relations" at n = 3 (all 8 wirings) and n = 4 (all 81 wirings) — has genuine adicity 3 or more. The
  control form, with one two-input rule, has genuine adicity 3.
- **H0.** Some one-input wiring reaches adicity 3.
- **Lab prior.** None on adicity. Copy rings are known to be whole-irreducible in some geometries
  (`ring_verdict_geometry`), so Φ > 0 is expected for some of these wirings; the question is whether they
  contain a three-term fact.

## H2 — Grades of degeneracy (C2)

- **H2 (Peirce).** Peirce's three exemplars order strictly by genuine adicity: the monadically degenerate
  triad ("A, B, C each red": three independent self-reading elements) at ≤ 1; the dyadically degenerate triad
  ("A father of B, B father of C": a chain B'=A, C'=B with A self-reading) at exactly 2; the genuine triad
  (the control) at 3. The degenerate forms have no three-member complex.
- **H0.** The ordering fails, or a degenerate form carries a three-member complex.
- **Lab prior.** Chains without return are feed-forward and factor (probe 5); agrees with Peirce.

## H3 — Giving (C3)

- **H3 (Peirce).** Genuine giving — the receiver's possession determined jointly by the giver's act and the
  thing under one rule, R'=G∧T — has genuine adicity 3 with G, T, and R all in one complex. The degenerate
  imitation — "A's putting B away" T'=G, "C's subsequently taking B up" R'=T — has genuine adicity 2, with
  the same giver rule in both.
- **H0.** The degenerate form reaches adicity 3, or the genuine form does not.
- **Lab prior.** The degenerate form is a ring of one-input rules and may be whole-irreducible (Φ > 0) while
  having adicity 2 — the case where Peirce's line and the lab's part. Peirce's text predicts this too:
  "a degenerate form of Thirdness in which the thirdness is externally appended."

## H4 — The sign relation (C4)

- **H4 (Peirce).** With the dynamical object exogenous (O'=O), the sign determined by the object (S'=O), and
  the interpretant determined by the sign with respect to the object (I'=S↔O, the interpretant registers that
  the sign stands to its object as it should), the form has genuine adicity 3 and all three of O, S, I are
  in one complex: "bound together … not resolvable into actions between pairs."
- **H0.** No adicity-3 distinction, or O outside the complex.
- **Lab prior.** Against Peirce on membership: an emit-only source is never in the core (probe 5), and a form
  driven entirely by a constant exogenous element is expected to factor. A second form with pragmatic
  feedback, O'=I (interpretation alters conduct toward the object), is run as the comparison; the lab expects
  it, and not the exogenous form, to bind all three.

## H5 — Polyadic reducibility (C5)

- **H5 (Peirce).** Among four-element forms in which every element reads exactly two others, with rules drawn
  from {AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR}, at least one has genuine adicity 4 — a four-term fact built from
  two-input (triadic) rules alone — while no four-element form of one-input rules reaches adicity 3 (from
  H1). Triads compose upward; dyads do not compose into triads.
- **H0.** No two-input four-element form in the sample reaches adicity 4.
- **Lab prior.** q211 built a four-member core (Φ = 3.0) from two-input rules; whether it contains an
  adicity-4 distinction has not been read.

## Not tested here

Peirce's categories as modes of being, and the claim that genuine thirdness "involves thought or meaning,"
are outside what a Boolean form carries (`exegesis.md`).
