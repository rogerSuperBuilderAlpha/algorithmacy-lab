# The genuine triad: Peirce's reduction thesis under exact Φ

## Abstract

Peirce's reduction thesis is the oldest general claim about which relations are irreducible: some triadic
relations cannot be built from dyads, and every relation of higher adicity can be built from triads. He
sorted triads into monadically degenerate, dyadically degenerate, and genuine; made giving the type case of
a genuine triad against "one dyadic relation followed by another"; and defined the sign as a genuine triad
whose three members are "bound together … in a way that does not consist in any complexus of dyadic
relations." The thesis has been formalized on extensional relations and never on a dynamical system. This
paper renders Peirce's relation as an irreducible distinction in a system's IIT-4.0 cause-effect structure
and its adicity as the number of parties that distinction spans, then tests five claims against hypotheses
fixed before computation. Three are recovered: his three grades order exactly (adicity 1, 2, 3); genuine
giving carries a three-term fact that the two-step imitation lacks, though both are Φ = 2.0 wholes; and
two-input rules compound into four-term facts in 58 of 60 random four-element forms. One is refuted as
registered: 78 of 89 wirings of one-input rules reach adicity 3 or more. Every one of them does so at a
branch point, on the effect side, and the maximum cause-side adicity in all 89 is 2 — the negative clause
holds exactly for "determined by," and the exceptions are the junctions Peirce told Kempe were triadic. One
is refined: the sign relation with an exogenous object contains a genuine triad and is not a whole, Φ = 0.
The instrument's addition is that Peirce's line and the lab's are distinct and cross both ways: a cycle of
dyads is an irreducible whole with no genuine triad in it, and a genuine triad can sit in a system that
factors.

## Introduction

The lab's criterion is a reduction test. A form is irreducible when Φ, integrated information over the
minimum-information partition, is positive: when no cut of the system into parts reproduces its cause-effect
structure [albantakis2023iit4; mayner2018pyphi]. The first person to claim, in general terms, that some
relations pass such a test and others fail it was Peirce. His reduction thesis, first stated in 1870 and
defended for the rest of his life, has two clauses: some triadic relations cannot be reduced to combinations
of monadic and dyadic ones, and all relations of adicity four and above can be reduced to combinations of
triadic ones [peirce1897relatives; peirce_cp]. The triad is the smallest thing that cannot be taken apart and
the largest thing that needs to be built. Every later thinker in this series who says a third party makes a
whole that two cannot is, whether or not they know it, taking a position on Peirce's first clause.

The thesis has been argued about as logic. Löwenheim and Quine showed that set-theoretic pairing reduces any
relation to dyads, and the thesis was widely dismissed. Skidmore (1971) named it and raised the worry that any
proof would restrict the allowed operations until the theorem came out. Herzberger (1981) proved a version in
a bonding algebra whose valency rule forbids the branching of lines; Burch (1991) built a Peircean algebraic
logic in which the branching junction is itself a triad — the teridentity predicate — and the thesis is a
theorem; Hereth and Pöschel (2011) gave the strongest proof. Koshkin's recent preprint argues that all of
these tie the thesis to privileged operations and sketches an informational reading instead: a relation is
degenerate when its places can be filled independently and genuine when fixing one place constrains the
others, and he points to "information integration" measures as the analogous quantity
[koshkin_gerrymandered]. That is the quantity this lab computes. None of this work has asked Peirce's question
of a system of elements that determine one another by rules — which arrangements contain a fact that is
irreducibly about three of them — because the question needs a way to read the relations a dynamical system
actually asserts.

IIT 4.0 reads them. A system's cause-effect structure is a set of *distinctions*, each a mechanism in a state
that irreducibly constrains a purview of other elements, with an integrated information φ > 0 that says no
partition of the mechanism-purview pair reproduces the constraint. A distinction is a relation among the
parties it spans, and the number of those parties is the adicity of the relation it asserts. On the lab's
control triad — A'=M, M'=A∧B, B'=M — the mediator's constraint over {A, B} is an irreducible distinction of
adicity 3 with φ = 2.0, and each outer party's constraint over the mediator is adicity 2. A two-input rule
yields a three-term fact. That calibration turns Peirce's genuine/degenerate line into a computation, and the
computation can be run on his own exemplars: chains of dyads, the branching of a line, giving, the sign, and
the compounding of triads into tetrads.

Peirce makes five claims about the triad that concern which terms are bound and not what binding is. Each is
quoted, rendered as a Boolean form, and tested against a prediction fixed before the computation, with the
lab's standing results stated beside it. Three claims are recovered, one is refuted as registered and holds
exactly under a split the results forced, and one is refined. The discussion draws them into one finding
about two lines that do not coincide. The sections that follow give the claims in Peirce's words, the
translation, the hypotheses, the methods, the results, and what they mean.

## Peirce's account

The exegesis is in `exegesis.md`; the five claims are summarized here with their labels.

**C1 — triadic irreducibility.** "The triadic relation is genuine, that is its three members are bound
together by it in a way that does not consist in any complexus of dyadic relations" (CP 2.274). The argument
is from valency: combining two dyads "by means of an X, we find we only have two blank places in the
combination" (CP 1.363), and "no branching of a line can result from putting one line on the end of another"
(CP 8.331). A relation is genuine when no assembly of two-term relations reproduces it, and dyads laid end to
end make chains and rings, never a three-term fact.

**C2 — grades of degeneracy.** "Every triad is either monadically degenerate, dyadically degenerate, or
genuine. A monadically degenerate triad is one which results from the essence of three monads, its subjects.
A dyadically degenerate triad is one which results from dyads. A genuine triad is one which cannot be
resolved in any such way" (CP 1.473). The exemplars are Peirce's: "A is father of B and B father of C is a
genuinely dyadic degenerate triad," and "A is mother of B and B is wife of C" is "a purer case" because the
component dyads are more independent. The three grades order by how many terms any single irreducible fact
binds: one, two, three.

**C3 — giving.** "A gives B to C. This does not consist in A's throwing B away and its accidentally hitting
C … If that were all, it would not be a genuine triadic relation, but merely one dyadic relation followed by
another" (CP 1.345). Giving "consists in A's making C the possessor according to Law," and the imitation in
which A lays B down and C picks it up "would be a degenerate form of Thirdness in which the thirdness is
externally appended" (CP 8.331). In genuine giving the receiver's possession is determined jointly by the
act and the thing under one rule; in the imitation, the thing is determined by the giver and the receiver by
the thing, two dyads in sequence.

**C4 — the sign relation.** "A Sign, or Representamen, is a First which stands in such a genuine triadic
relation to a Second, called its Object, as to be capable of determining a Third, called its Interpretant, to
assume the same triadic relation to its Object in which it stands itself to the same Object" (CP 2.274).
Semiosis is "a coöperation of three subjects, such as a sign, its object, and its interpretant, this
tri-relative influence not being in any way resolvable into actions between pairs" (CP 5.484). The object
determines the sign; the sign determines the interpretant with respect to the object. Peirce's dynamical
object determines and is not determined: it is the source. The claim is that the three are nonetheless bound.

**C5 — polyadic reducibility.** The tetrad "A sells C to B for the price D" resolves into "A makes with C a
certain transaction E" and "E is a sale of B for the price D" (CP 1.363): two triads sharing a term minted
by hypostatic abstraction. Three-term facts compound into four-term facts, and the triad is the smallest
building block from which every higher adicity can be reached.

What the secondary literature did with these claims is set out in `literature/deep_research_report.md`. The
formal tradition from Herzberger to Hereth and Pöschel treats relations extensionally — sets of tuples
composed by relative product, junction, and identity — and asks whether the thesis is a theorem under a
chosen set of operations. The semiotic tradition reads the genuine-triad clause of the sign definition as
logic and does not ask whether an object that "determines and is not determined" would be inside or outside
a causal whole. Neither computed irreducibility on a dynamical system, because until IIT there was no
instrument that returned the relations a system asserts with a measure of each one's irreducibility.

## From claims to forms

Every claim is rendered with one construction. A *relation* is an irreducible distinction in the form's
cause-effect structure, computed by `pyphi.new_big_phi.phi_structure`: a mechanism, its cause purview, its
effect purview, and φ > 0. Its *adicity* is the number of parties it spans, |mechanism ∪ purview|, taken as
the larger of the cause and effect sides. A form's *genuine adicity* is the largest adicity among its
distinctions, over all reachable states. A form contains a genuine triad in Peirce's sense at genuine
adicity 3 or more; it is dyadically degenerate at 2 and monadically degenerate at 1.

| claim | structural restatement | Boolean form | keeps / drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | a complexus of dyads never binds three terms at once | every wiring in which each node copies exactly one other: 8 at n = 3, 81 at n = 4 | keeps "dyadic relation" as one-input determination; drops the extensional composition operations |
| C2 | grades order by the largest fact any one term is party to | monads A'=A, B'=B, C'=C; chain A'=A, B'=A, C'=B; the control | keeps Peirce's own three exemplars; drops the "purer" mother/wife variant, which has the same wiring |
| C3 | possession jointly determined vs. two dyads in sequence | genuine G'=¬R, T'=¬R, R'=G∧T; degenerate G'=¬R, T'=G, R'=T | keeps the shared giver rule so only the transfer differs; drops "right" and "law" as content |
| C4 | object → sign → interpretant, the last with respect to the object | exogenous O'=O, S'=O, I'=(S↔O); pragmatic O'=I, S'=O, I'=(S↔O) | keeps the object as source in the first form; the second adds the pragmatist's feedback |
| C5 | triads compound into tetrads | 60 random four-node forms, each node reading two others through a gate in {AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR} | keeps "built from triads" as two-input rules; drops hypostatic abstraction as a named operation |

Two renderings were considered and rejected. For C1, whole-system Φ > 0 as the criterion of genuineness:
rejected because a mutual dyad A'=B, B'=A is a Φ = 2.0 whole and Peirce calls it a dyad, so Φ > 0 cannot
mark the triad. For C4, I'=S alone — the interpretant determined by the sign and nothing else: rejected
because it is the chain O→S→I, the "complexus of dyadic relations" Peirce explicitly denies, and it would
refute him by construction. The rendering chosen, I'=(S↔O), makes the interpretant register that the sign
stands to the object as it should; it is the smallest rule under which the interpretant's determination is
a fact about sign and object together. The main risk in the translation is the adicity measure: it treats a
mechanism that constrains a two-element purview and a two-element mechanism that constrains one element as
facts of the same adicity, and it does not distinguish the side on which the spread occurs. The results
turned on that distinction, and it is reported below as a post hoc split.

## Hypotheses

Fixed in `hypotheses.md` before any probe ran.

- **H1 (C1).** No one-input wiring at n = 3 or 4 has genuine adicity 3 or more; the control has 3. *H0:*
  some wiring reaches 3. *Lab prior:* copy rings are Φ = 2.0 wholes at n = 3–5 (probe #168), so whole
  irreducibility is expected for some wirings; nothing is on record about the adicity of their distinctions.
- **H2 (C2).** Peirce's three exemplars order strictly at adicity ≤ 1, 2, 3, and neither degenerate form has
  a three-member complex. *H0:* the ordering fails. *Lab prior:* chains without return factor (probe #5);
  agrees with Peirce.
- **H3 (C3).** Genuine giving has adicity 3 with G, T, R in one complex; the degenerate imitation has
  adicity ≤ 2. *H0:* the degenerate form reaches 3, or the genuine does not. *Lab prior:* the degenerate form
  is a ring of one-input rules and may be a whole at Φ > 0 while carrying only two-term facts.
- **H4 (C4).** The exogenous-object sign has adicity 3 and a complex containing O, S, and I. *H0:* no
  adicity-3 distinction, or O outside the complex. *Lab prior:* against Peirce on membership — an emit-only
  source is never in the core (probe #5) — with the pragmatic form expected to bind all three.
- **H5 (C5).** At least one sampled two-input four-node form has genuine adicity 4, while no one-input
  four-node form reaches 3 (from H1). *H0:* none in the sample reaches 4. *Lab prior:* q211 built a
  four-member core from two-input rules; adicity not read.

## Methods

**Instrument.** Exact IIT 4.0 via PyPhi 4.0. The whole-system verdict is Φ over the minimum-information
partition, maximized over reachable states; the major complex names the parties the irreducible whole
contains, robust to spectators. The cause-effect structure at each reachable state (and the all-ones state)
is read with `new_big_phi.phi_structure`; only distinctions with φ > 0 are returned. For each distinction the
adicity is `max(|mechanism ∪ cause purview|, |mechanism ∪ effect purview|)`; the form's genuine adicity is
the maximum over distinctions and states, and the distinction reported is the largest and, among those, the
one with the highest φ. The two sides are also recorded separately as cause-side and effect-side adicity;
they enter no pre-registered decision rule.

**Control.** The conjunctive triad A'=M, M'=A∧B, B'=M must read Φ = 2.000000, core {A, M, B}, genuine
adicity 3 with the maximal distinction M over {A, B}. Every probe runs it first and halts if it fails. It
passed in all five.

**Forms.** As in the translation table. For H1, `one_input_wirings(n)` enumerates every assignment of one
source j ≠ i to each node i with the copy rule, 8 wirings at n = 3 and 81 at n = 4. For H5,
`two_input_sample(60, seed=0)` draws, for each of four nodes, an unordered pair of the other three and a
gate uniformly from the six two-input gates. Full rules are in Appendix A and `forms.py`.

**Decision rules.** H1 CONFIRMED iff the maximum genuine adicity over the 89 wirings is ≤ 2 and the control's
is 3. H2 CONFIRMED iff adicity is ≤ 1, 2, 3 in order and neither degenerate form has a three-member complex.
H3 CONFIRMED iff the genuine form has adicity 3 with {G, T, R} in the core and the degenerate has ≤ 2;
PARTIAL if one holds. H4 CONFIRMED iff the exogenous form has adicity 3 and core {O, S, I}; PARTIAL if it has
adicity 3 without the full core, or the pragmatic form binds all three at adicity 3; REFUTED otherwise. H5
CONFIRMED iff at least one sampled form has adicity 4.

**Reproduction.** `python -m org_frontier.thinkers.peirce.probe_peirce_<claim>` from the repository root
on the 4.0 environment; `probe_peirce_polyads` takes the sample size as its first argument. The five checks
are registered in `ci/reproduce.json`; the polyad check runs the 12-form prefix of the seed-0 sample.

### H1 — triadic irreducibility: REFUTED

Of 89 one-input wirings, 11 have genuine adicity 2, 66 have 3, and 12 have 4. The decision rule required a
maximum of 2; the hypothesis is refuted as registered.

*Post hoc.* The 11 wirings at adicity 2 are exactly the wirings in which no node is read by more than one
other: the two 3-cycles at n = 3, and at n = 4 the six 4-cycles and the three products of two mutual dyads.
The 78 wirings in which some node is read by two or more all reach adicity 3, and the 12 in which one node
is read by all three others reach 4: the hub's constraint over its three readers, D → {A, B, C} at
φ = 1.000, is one irreducible distinction. Every adicity above 2 in this family is on the effect side. The
maximum cause-side adicity across all 89 wirings is 2: no complexus of one-input rules makes any element
jointly determined by two others. On whole-system Φ the family splits the other way. The eight pure cycles
(both 3-cycles and all six 4-cycles) are Φ = 2.000 wholes with every element in the core, matching probe
#168, and all eight are at adicity 2. All 78 branched wirings have Φ = 0.

### H2 — grades of degeneracy: CONFIRMED

Three monads have genuine adicity 1 (each element constrains only itself, φ = 1.000), Peirce's father-of chain
has adicity 2 (A's constraint over {A, B}), and the control has 3. Neither degenerate form is a whole (Φ = 0)
or has a complex larger than one element. The ordering 1 < 2 < 3 holds and the decision rule is met.

### H3 — giving: CONFIRMED

Genuine giving has genuine adicity 3: the receiver's possession R is irreducibly constrained by {G, T}
jointly at φ = 2.000, the same distinction as the control's mediator, and the whole binds at Φ = 2.000 with
core {G, T, R}. The degenerate imitation has genuine adicity 2 — its largest fact is the giver's constraint
by R and over T, φ = 1.000 — and is also a Φ = 2.000 whole with core {G, T, R}. The two forms are identical
on the whole-system reading and differ on Peirce's. The decision rule is met.

### H4 — the sign relation: PARTIAL

The exogenous-object form has an adicity-3 distinction — the joint mechanism {O, S} constrains {S, I} at
φ = 1.000 — and no whole: Φ = 0.000, and the major complex is the object alone. Its cause-side adicity is 2;
no element is jointly determined by two others, because the object is determined by nothing and the sign by
the object alone. The pragmatic form, O'=I, has adicity 3 on the cause side — the interpretant is jointly
determined by {O, S}, φ = 0.500 — and binds at Φ = 2.000 with core {O, S, I}. Peirce's adicity claim holds
and his membership claim does not; the second form meets both. PARTIAL by the decision rule.

### H5 — polyadic reducibility: CONFIRMED

Of 60 two-input four-node forms, 58 have genuine adicity 4 and 2 have 3; none is below 3. On the cause side
43 reach adicity 4 — a pair of elements jointly determined by the other pair, or a triple by the remaining
elements — so the four-term facts are not only branching. The first sampled form, A=AND(C,D), B=OR(C,D),
C=NOR(B,D), D=XNOR(A,C), has {B, D} irreducibly constrained by {A, C} at φ = 1.000. On the whole-system
reading 46 of 60 are Φ > 0, 7 have a four-member core, and 4 reach Φ = 3.000. The decision rule is met.

## Discussion

**C1, refuted as registered and recovered under a split.** The pre-registered rendering treated every
one-input wiring as a "complexus of dyadic relations" and predicted that none would carry a three-term fact.
Most do, and the instrument says where: at branch points, on the effect side. A node read by two others
constrains both at once, and IIT counts that constraint as one irreducible distinction because no cut of the
purview reproduces it. This is the Kempe problem. Kempe's 1886 memoir appeared to build triads from dyads by
junctions; Peirce's answer, in "The Critic of Arguments" and the letter to Lady Welby, was that the junction
is the triad — "no branching of a line can result from putting one line on the end of another" — and Burch's
teridentity predicate is the formal version of that answer [burch1991reduction]. Herzberger's bonding algebra
took the other route and forbade branching [herzberger1981remarkable]. The instrument reproduces both
positions and assigns each a side. On the cause side — how many parties jointly determine a given one —
Herzberger's valency argument holds without exception across all 89 wirings: dyads compose into chains and
cycles and never into joint determination. On the effect side, Burch is right: the branching of a line is a
three-term fact, and a hub read by three is a four-term one. The registered hypothesis conflated the two
sides and fell to the second. The claim Peirce most often states, "A is determined by B and C together,"
survives exactly.

**C2, recovered.** Peirce's three grades are three values of one number. The monads are adicity 1, the
father-of chain is 2, the joint rule is 3, and the two degenerate forms have no complex larger than a single
element. The "purer case" — mother and wife, whose dyads are "more independent" — has the same wiring as
the father-of chain and was not run; Peirce's distinction between them is about the kinds of the dyads, which
the rendering does not carry.

**C3, recovered, with the split the lab prior predicted.** Genuine giving and its imitation are the same on
the whole-system reading — both Φ = 2.000, both with all three in the core — and differ on Peirce's. The
joint rule R'=G∧T puts a three-term fact in the structure, φ = 2.000, and the two-step chain does not; its
largest fact is two-term. The imitation is a whole because it returns: the giver reads the receiver, the
receiver the thing, the thing the giver, and a cycle of one-input rules is a Φ = 2.0 whole (H1, probe #168).
Peirce's phrase for this case is exact — "a degenerate form of Thirdness in which the thirdness is externally
appended" — and the instrument gives it a location: the wholeness is in the return, not in any relation
among the three.

**C4, refined.** The sign relation with an exogenous object contains a genuine triad and is not a whole. The
three-term fact is on the effect side — the object and sign together constrain sign and interpretant — and
on the cause side the form never exceeds 2, because the dynamical object is determined by nothing. That is
probe #5 in Peirce's terms: an emit-only party is never in the core, and here the party is the object. The
form binds all three only when interpretation feeds back on the object, O'=I, at which point the interpretant
becomes jointly determined by object and sign (cause-side 3) and the core is {O, S, I}. Peirce's own account
supplies the feedback: the interpretant of a sign is a habit, and a habit changes conduct toward the object.
The pragmatist's sign is a whole and the logician's is a genuine triad in a system that factors.

**C5, recovered.** Two-input rules compound into four-term facts in 58 of 60 forms, and in 43 the fact is on
the cause side — a pair jointly determined by a pair. Peirce's "A sells C to B for the price D," two triads
sharing a hypostatic term, has that shape. The clause is an existence claim and the sample settles it.

**The through-line.** Peirce's line and the lab's do not coincide, and the results place forms in all four
cells. A pure cycle of dyads is a Φ = 2.0 whole with no genuine triad in it (H1, H3 degenerate). A branched
wiring, or a sign with an exogenous object, carries a genuine triad and factors (H1, H4). The control, genuine
giving, the pragmatic sign, and 46 of the 60 two-input forms are both. Three monads and two independent
mutual dyads are neither. Genuineness is a property of a *relation*: does one fact span three parties. Whole
irreducibility is a property of a *system*: does every part read and get read by the rest (finding 3 of
`STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md`; probe #274, where a feedback cycle is necessary and not sufficient). A system can
be a whole by return alone, and a three-term fact can sit in a system whose source is outside every whole.
Simmel's superindividual triad (probe #369) is a whole; whether it contains a genuine triad depends on whether
the third is jointly determined or merely returns.

**What the instrument added.** It located the Herzberger–Burch disagreement on a side of the mechanism. It
found that giving and its imitation, which Peirce could separate only by appeal to "law," differ by exactly
one distinction, and that the imitation's wholeness is elsewhere. It moved the object of the sign out of the
core and said what would move it back in.

## Limitations

Translation risk comes first. Adicity as |mechanism ∪ purview| is one rendering of "the number of terms a
relation binds," and it was pre-registered as the maximum over the two sides. The results forced a split
into cause-side and effect-side that was not pre-registered; the split is reported as post hoc and H1's
verdict stands as refuted. A reader who takes Peirce's "determined by" as the whole of the thesis will read
H1 as confirmed on the cause side, and a reader who takes his teridentity as the whole will read it as
refuted; the paper reports both and changes neither. For C4, I'=(S↔O) is one rule under which the
interpretant's determination is about sign and object together; I'=S∧O is another and was not run. Peirce's
"purer" dyadically degenerate case was not separately rendered.

The scope is in-silico. The forms have three and four binary elements; the H5 sample is 60 forms at one seed
and settles an existence claim, not a rate. Nothing here is about a sign, a gift, or a relation between
people; it is about which Boolean forms carry a three-term fact.

The rendering does not carry what Peirce most insisted on. "Every genuine triadic relation involves thought
or meaning" (CP 1.345); the instrument reads adicity and says nothing about meaning. Law, right, habit, and
the categories as modes of being are outside the form. Where that bit is C4: Peirce's reason for the sign's
genuineness is that the interpretant is a *thought*, and the rendering can only make it a joint
determination.

## Conclusion

Peirce's reduction thesis, rendered as a claim about the adicity of irreducible distinctions in a dynamical
system, holds exactly for joint determination and fails at branch points, which is where he said thirdness
lives when he answered Kempe. His three grades are three values of one number; giving differs from its
imitation by one three-term fact while matching it as a whole; the sign with an exogenous object is a genuine
triad that factors; and triads compound into tetrads in nearly every random form. The genuine triad and the
irreducible whole are different properties, and the lab's next thinkers will be read against both.

- **one_input_wirings(n)**: for each node i, a source j ≠ i and rule node_i'=node_j; named `copy_` followed
  by the sources in node order (e.g. `copy_BCA`: A'=B, B'=C, C'=A).
- **two_input_sample(60, seed=0)**: `random.Random(0)`; for each of four nodes in order, `rng.sample` two of
  the other three (sorted) then `rng.choice` over the sorted gate names AND, NAND, NOR, OR, XNOR, XOR; the
  spec string is stored with each result.
