# Girard — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

Three nodes. **S** is the subject's desire for the object; **M** the mediator's desire for it; **O** the
object's value. **Imitation** is reading: S' = M is the subject desiring what the mediator desires. The
object's value is conferred: O' = M ∧ S — valued when the mediator desires it and the subject pursues it.
**Distance** is how much the mediator reads the subject: M reads S with probability p per step and otherwise
holds his own desire (p = 0 external, p = 1 fully internal). The **structure** is the major complex; a
term's **share** is its deletion cost on the core's Φ, orphaned terms rendered as constants.

Forms (rules in `methods.md`): **spontaneous** (S ⇄ O, no mediator — the romantic lie); **external** (M
holds; S' = M; O' = M ∧ S); **internal** (M' = S ∧ O; S' = M; O' = M ∧ S); **double** (M' = S; S' = M;
O' = M ∧ S); **distance sweep** (M' = S w.p. p else hold, p ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}).

## H1 — Desire is triangular; the straight line is not essential (C1)

- **H1 (Girard).** In the internal form S and O are members of one whole although S does not read O; delete
  M and no complex containing both remains.
- **H0.** S and O not in one complex, or still one after M is deleted.
- **Lab prior.** With Girard: every term returns in the internal form.

## H2 — External and internal mediation (C2)

- **H2 (Girard).** In external mediation M is in no complex and S and O are not one ("no contact between
  the two spheres"); in internal mediation the major complex is {S, M, O}.
- **H0.** M in a complex when external, or the internal core smaller than three.
- **Lab prior.** With Girard: a held source is outside; the internal form returns everywhere.

## H3 — The object is a means of reaching the mediator (C3, C4)

- **H3 (Girard).** In double mediation the major complex is {S, M} and O is out; Φ(double) equals Φ of the
  bare S ⇄ M dyad.
- **H0.** O in the core, or the object's presence changes Φ.
- **Lab prior.** With Girard: O reads both and is read by neither.

## H4 — Distance (C2)

- **H4 (Girard).** As the mediator draws nearer (p from 0 to 1) the core's Φ rises monotonically, and O is
  in the core at no p > 0: the mediator's weight grows, the object's does not.
- **H0.** Φ non-monotone in p, or O in the core at some p.
- **Lab prior.** With on monotonicity (the Granovetter dyad graded with p); open on O at intermediate p.

## H5 — Doubles (C5)

- **H5 (Girard).** In double mediation the shares of S and M are equal and positive and O's is zero; in
  single internal mediation the shares of S and M differ.
- **H0.** Shares of S and M unequal in double mediation, or equal in internal mediation.
- **Lab prior.** With Girard on double mediation (the form is symmetric in S and M). Open on internal.

## Not tested here

Escalation ("with every cycle it increases in intensity") — Boolean states do not escalate; metaphysical
desire; conversion; the scapegoat.
