# Blau — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

A **superior** S has a service bit (1 = serves); a **subordinate** B has a compliance bit (1 = complies).
**Contingent service** (B1): S serves when complied with, S' = B. **Dependence**: B complies when served,
B' = S. **Independence** is a constant supplier, S' = 1 — the Serres paper's producer. An **alternative
supplier** (B2, second alternative) is a second S that B may be served by, B' = S1 ∨ S2. **Organization**
of the dependent (B2, third alternative): a subordinate complies only if the peer does, B_i' = S ∧ B_j.
**Legitimation** (B3): a subordinate complies when served or when the peer complies, B_i' = S ∨ B_j.
**Opposition** (B4): B_i now means opposes, and opposes when exploited or when the peer opposes,
B_i' = ¬S ∨ B_j; S yields to joint opposition, S' = B1 ∧ B2. The **structure** is the major complex; **value
added** V and **positional advantage** are the Coleman paper's quantities (a deleted variable reads 0).

Forms (rules in `methods.md`): **independent**, **contingent**; **single**, **alternatives**;
**unorganized**, **organized**; **power**, **authority**; **opposition_isolated**, **opposition_shared**.

## H1 — Power is a relation when service is contingent (B1; against Serres H1)

- **H1 (Blau).** contingent is a complex of both parties (Φ > 0, core {S, B}); independent is not (no
  complex containing B).
- **H0.** contingent binds nothing, or independent binds.
- **Lab prior.** With. Serres H1 found the arrow from a constant producer binds nothing; the copy dyad is
  the lab's Φ = 2 control.

## H2 — An alternative supplier removes the superior's power (B2, second alternative)

- **H2 (Blau).** In alternatives, S1's positional advantage over the others is lower than in single, and
  S1's value added is lower.
- **H0.** No fall in either.
- **Lab prior.** With. alternatives is the Burt paper's control_open form seen from the contacts' side.

## H3 — Organizing the dependent shifts power (B2, third alternative)

- **H3 (Blau).** From unorganized to organized, S's positional advantage falls and each subordinate's value
  added rises.
- **H0.** S's advantage does not fall, or the subordinates' V does not rise.
- **Lab prior.** Open.

## H4 — Legitimate authority is carried by the subordinates (B3)

- **H4 (Blau).** In authority, from the state (S = 0, B1 = 1, B2 = 1) both subordinates remain compliant at
  the next step, and in power they do not; and in authority B1 and B2 are members of one complex.
- **H0.** Compliance lapses without service in authority, or the subordinates are not co-members.
- **Lab prior.** With on the dynamics (worked by hand); open on the complex.

## H5 — Opposition mirrors legitimation (B4 against B3)

- **H5 (Blau).** opposition_shared and authority have the same core size and the same core Φ (to 1e-6); and
  opposition_shared has a fixed point in which S yields (S = 1 with B1 = B2 = 1) while opposition_isolated
  has none.
- **H0.** The mirror fails in size or Φ, or isolated subordinates also bring S to rest.
- **Lab prior.** With on the isolated clause (it is the Coleman norm_open cycle); open on the mirror.

## Not tested here
