# Bowen — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

Parties are Boolean nodes; a party's state is its emotional state this step. **Togetherness** is reading: A
is together with B when A's next state depends on B's. **Anxiety** is flip noise: with probability ε a
party's next state is the opposite of what its rule gives, applied to every party (uniform anxiety) or to one
(anxiety at a position). **Stability** is Φ retained under anxiety: Φ(ε) / Φ(0), and the persistence of the
same major complex. The instrument is exact IIT-4.0 Φ on the stochastic TPM, maximized over reachable
states.

Forms (rules in `methods.md`): **dyad** (A ⇄ B); **calm triangle** (A ⇄ B; the outsider C reads both, is read
by neither); **triangled** (the uncomfortable insider A also reads C); **interlocked** (A also involves D by
the same move); **neutral third** and **reactive third** (both insiders read C; C reads both and either holds
or reacts).

## H1 — The triangle is the smallest stable system (C1)

- **H1 (Bowen).** At every ε in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3} the triangled form retains a larger fraction of its
  Φ than the dyad, and its major complex stays the full triad while the dyad's Φ falls further.
- **H0.** The dyad retains as much or more at some ε.
- **Lab prior.** Open. The lab's noise sweeps (#235, #145–150) show every form's Φ sliding toward zero at
  ε = 0.5 with no interior collapse; whether a three-cycle slides slower than a two-cycle has not been run.

## H2 — Calm twosome and outsider; tension involves the third (C2)

- **H2 (Bowen).** In the calm triangle the major complex is {A, B} and C is outside; in the triangled form the
  major complex is {A, B, C}.
- **H0.** C is inside in calm, or outside when triangled.
- **Lab prior.** With Bowen on both: a party that reads and is not read is outside the core (finding 3); a
  party read by one insider and reading both returns and is inside.

## H3 — Under stress the outside position costs least (C3)

- **H3 (Bowen).** In the triangled form, anxiety at C alone (ε = 0.1, 0.2) costs the whole less Φ than the
  same anxiety at A alone or at B alone.
- **H0.** Anxiety at C costs as much or more than at some insider.
- **Lab prior.** Open. The lab found noise at a mediator and noise at a party act differently (#145) and that
  the ordering can reverse across ε (#147).

## H4 — Interlocking triangles (C4)

- **H4 (Bowen).** With D involved by the same move that involved C, (a) the major complex of the four is a
  triangle — three parties, not four — and (b) the four-party form retains a larger fraction of its Φ under
  uniform anxiety than the triangled form at every ε.
- **H0.** The four are one complex of four, or the four retain no more than the three.
- **Lab prior.** Against (a): conjunctive forms that grow by a returning party tend to bind as a whole. Open
  on (b).

## H5 — Detriangling (C5)

- **H5 (Bowen).** (a) A neutral third — read by both insiders, reading both, holding its own state — is
  outside the major complex where a reactive third is inside; (b) under uniform anxiety the insiders' complex
  retains a larger fraction of its Φ with a neutral third in contact than the bare dyad does.
- **H0.** The neutral third is inside, or the twosome with a neutral third retains no more than the dyad.
- **Lab prior.** With Bowen on (a). Against on (b): a party that is read and holds is, under noise, a noisy
  source, and noisy sources cost the parties that read them.

## Not tested here

Differentiation as a scale, projection, transmission, sibling position, societal regression, technique.
