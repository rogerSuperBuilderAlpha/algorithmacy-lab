# Caplow — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

Four nodes: the members A, B, C and the outcome O of the issue the triad decides. Each type is a weight
vector (wA, wB, wC) from the table in `exegesis.md`. A **coalition** {X, Y} against the isolate Z is rendered
by Caplow's own assumptions:

- A.1 (the stronger controls the weaker): the coalition's stance is the weighted majority of its members'
  stances over the coalition's own strength — the stronger partner's stance when the weights differ, the
  conjunction X∧Y when they are equal (`bloc`).
- A.3 (strength is additive): the outcome is the weighted majority over the whole triad with the coalition
  voting as a bloc: O'=1 if (wX+wY)·bloc + wZ·Z > (wA+wB+wC)/2, O'=0 if less, and O'=O at exact equality
  (a block, not a decision).
- A.4 (an existing triadic situation): every member reads the outcome, A'=B'=C'=O.

The **precoalition** form for each type has no bloc: O' is the weighted majority of A, B, C individually,
ties holding O.

A coalition **binds** when both partners are in the major complex. The lab's reading of "control" is
membership: a party is in the core when the whole's determination depends on it, and out when it is only a
reader (probe #5). Caplow's control motive is not rendered; his structural assumptions are.

## H1 — Power predicts the coalition (C1)

- **H1 (Caplow).** In each of the eight types, the coalitions that bind are exactly the coalitions Caplow
  predicts: all three in Type 1; BC in Type 2; AB and AC in Type 3; none in Types 4 and 6; AC and BC in Type
  5; AB and AC in Types 7 and 8.
- **H0.** The set of binding coalitions differs from Caplow's prediction in at least one type.
- **Lab prior.** Against Caplow where his prediction rests on the weaker member's wish to control the isolate
  (Types 3, 5, 7, 8): under A.1 the weaker partner's stance is not read, and an unread party is out of the
  core (probe #5). Expected agreement in Types 1, 2, 4, 6.

## H2 — Strength is weakness (C2)

- **H2 (Caplow).** Across the binding coalitions of all eight types, A is a member in fewer of them than B
  and fewer than C.
- **H0.** A is a member in at least as many binding coalitions as B or C.
- **Lab prior.** Consistent with Caplow: equal-weight pairs bind and A is the unequal one in Types 2 and 3.

## H3 — A ≥ B + C: no coalition against A (C3)

- **H3 (Caplow).** In Types 4, 6, 7, and 8, BC never binds; A is in the core of every coalition form and of
  the precoalition form.
- **H0.** BC binds in one of these types, or A leaves the core in one form.
- **Lab prior.** Agrees: when A's weight is at least half the total, O tracks A. Types 7 and 8 (exact
  equality) are the open case, since the block rule O'=O at ties has no precedent.

## H4 — Conservative, revolutionary, improper (C4)

- **H4 (Caplow).** In Type 5, each of the three coalitions gives both partners control over the isolate:
  both partners are in the core in AB, AC, and BC. The revolutionary coalition BC removes A from the core;
  the conservative AB and the improper AC keep A in it.
- **H0.** A partner is outside the core in some Type 5 coalition, or A is in the core under BC.
- **Lab prior.** Against Caplow on the partners: every Type 5 pair is unequal, so under A.1 the weaker partner
  is a reader only and is expected out of the core in all three. Expected: A out under BC, in under AB and AC.

## H5 — Strength is additive (C5)

- **H5 (Caplow).** Among winning coalitions (bloc strength > isolate), whether the coalition binds does not
  depend on how the strength is divided between the partners: all winning coalitions bind.
- **H0.** Some winning coalitions bind and others do not, and the difference tracks the division of strength.
- **Lab prior.** Against Caplow: binding is expected to follow equality within the pair, not the sum — the
  form's wholeness depends on whether the partners are mutually necessary (finding 3).

## Not tested here

Continuous / episodic / terminal situations and linked triads (`exegesis.md`).
