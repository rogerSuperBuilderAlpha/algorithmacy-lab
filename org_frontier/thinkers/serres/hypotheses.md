# Serres — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computing)

## The rendering that all five share

A **party** is a Boolean node. **Taking** is reading: the parasite's state is a function of the host's. A
**producer** who is the origin of a chain is a constant 1 (it always produces; it reads nothing) — a
constant rather than a held node, so the self-holding one-node complex met in the Hegel and Girard papers
does not enter. **Noise** on a channel is a node the receiver XORs with the sender: B' = A ⊕ N. Noise is
**exogenous** when N is a fair coin every step and **endogenous** when the system's own activity makes it
(N' = A ∧ B). The **structure** is the major complex; a term's **share** is its deletion cost on the core's
Φ, orphaned terms rendered as constants 0.

Forms (rules in `methods.md`): **arrow** (F = 1; P' = F — the one-way parasite); **exchange** (A ⇄ B);
**chain** (F = 1; T' = F; R' = T); **chain + noise** (adds N' = R; R' = T ∧ ¬N); **reactive cascade**
(F = 1; T' = F ∧ ¬R; R' = T ∧ ¬N; N' = R); **dyad + exogenous noise**, **dyad + endogenous noise**;
**passing** (token ring A' = C, B' = A, C' = B); **stopped** (token frozen: constants).

## H1 — The one-way arrow is the atom of relation (C1)

- **H1 (Serres).** The arrow — a parasite reading a producer who does not read back — is a relation: the
  pair is a complex with Φ > 0.
- **H0.** No complex containing both; Φ = 0.
- **Lab prior.** Against Serres: a reader that is not read back does not bind (Simmel, Latour, Peirce
  papers). Exchange binds at Φ = 2.

## H2 — Noise gives rise to a new system (C3)

- **H2 (Serres).** The chain F → T → R has no complex. Add the noise the feast provokes (N' = R) and that
  stops the feast (R' = T ∧ ¬N): a complex appears, it contains N, and the producer and tax farmer are
  outside it.
- **H0.** A complex in the bare chain, or none after adding the noise, or one that excludes N.
- **Lab prior.** With Serres: the noise creates the chain's only return.

## H3 — The excluded third, included (C4)

- **H3 (Serres).** Exogenous noise on the channel is outside the complex (the pair is the complex) and
  lowers its Φ below the clean dyad's 2; endogenous noise — the system's own — is a member of the complex.
- **H0.** Exogenous noise in the complex, or Φ not lowered, or endogenous noise outside.
- **Lab prior.** With on exogenous (a coin is a source); open on endogenous (N reads both and is read by
  one; exclusion may still prefer the pair).

## H4 — The last position (C2)

- **H4 (Serres).** In the reactive cascade — each parasite ejects the level above — the complex is at the
  last position: it contains N and R and excludes F and T; N's share is at least R's, and T's is 0.
- **H0.** The complex spans T as well, or N's share is below R's.
- **Lab prior.** Open: whether nested reactions integrate the levels or exclusion picks the last pair.

## H5 — The quasi-object weaves the "we" (C5)

- **H5 (Serres).** Passing — the token ring — makes the three players one complex of all three; stopped,
  no complex at all.
- **H0.** The ring not one complex of three, or a complex when stopped.
- **Lab prior.** With Serres: a ring of copies binds; constants bind nothing.
