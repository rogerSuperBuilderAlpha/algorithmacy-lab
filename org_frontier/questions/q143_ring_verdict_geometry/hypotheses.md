# q143 — hypotheses

The object is the conjunctive ring on n nodes: each node updates to the AND of its two
neighbours, wired in a cycle. The study reads the whole-system verdict and the geometry of the
minimum-information partition (MIP) as the ring grows from n=3 to n=7.

## H1

The ring is triadic at every n=3..7 with Φ bounded away from zero, and its MIP is a single
balanced two-arc cut whose Φ is constant in n. Rotational symmetry leaves one kind of
bottleneck: a cut that severs the cycle into two arcs, and every such cut is equivalent, so the
MIP-Φ should not move with n.

Null: the ring goes dyadic at some n, or its MIP-Φ varies with n.

## H2

The major complex of the ring is the full node set at every n. No node drops. This contrasts
with the m-hub, where intermediate sizes shed parties from the core.

Null: the ring sheds at least one node from the core at some n=4..7.

## Scope

The forms are synthetic Boolean coordination rules. No real coordination is measured. Φ is the
exact IIT-4.0 value over reachable states. The instrument is explored here; the study does not
claim Φ is the necessary test for irreducibility.
