## The formal test recovers the literature's central line

The brokerage literature's oldest distinction is between the third who joins two others and the third who
profits by keeping them apart. The bypass-counterfactual draws the same line as a computed verdict. Every
gaudens-family broker — Simmel's *tertius gaudens*, the *tertius separans*, *divide et impera*, Burt's
structural-hole broker, Granovetter's bridge — classifies contingent with margin 2.0 (H1). Their entire
irreducibility is the maintained gap: restore the direct tie and they leave the core. Every integrating
mediator — Simmel's non-partisan reconciler, the two-sided platform that internalizes the cross-side
externality — classifies necessary with margin 0 (H3). The verbal contrast of gaudens and iungens is the
formal contrast of contingent and necessary, and the contingency margin measures which.

## The test refines what the words conflate

The *tertius iungens*, the third who joins, does not have one classification. Modeled as integrating an ongoing
joint condition it is necessary; modeled as fully joining its two parties it is reducible (H2). The second case
is the literature's blind spot: a iungens that succeeds completely connects the parties directly, which is the
bypass, and writes itself out of the core. The broker who most fully embodies the joining orientation makes
itself dispensable. The verbal theory calls both "iungens"; the bypass-counterfactual separates the broker who
sustains a joint condition from the broker who dissolves into the tie it created.

## Group boundaries are bypass constraints

Gould and Fernandez partition brokers by the group membership of the three parties. Under the bypass-
counterfactual the five roles sort by one thing: whether a group boundary forbids the direct A–C tie (H4). The
coordinator, brokering within a single group, classifies reducible — the parties can already connect, so the
broker is not load-bearing. The gatekeeper, representative, and liaison each span a boundary that forbids the
direct tie, and classify contingent. The itinerant, an outsider brokering between two who could connect,
classifies partial. The membership partition that the literature reads as a typology of positions is, formally,
a typology of which boundary acts as a bypass-forbidding constraint.

## The boundary of the instrument

A Heider-balance triad does not classify, and the failure is informative (H5). It is a signed-sentiment triad,
mutual rather than mediated: every party reads both others, the whole system is symmetric and reads triadic at
Φ=6.0 with all three in the core, and there is no designated third on a forbidden edge. Forcing the bypass-
counterfactual removes an edge that is already present rather than restoring a forbidden one, and returns a
spurious label. The taxonomy applies to mediated flow triads — a third party relaying or integrating a
resource between two others — and not to triads of mutual sentiment. Integration without a mediator is outside
its scope.

## Caveats

Stylized n=3 Boolean models, one per triad type, classified by exact Φ; worked illustrations of the structure
each theory names, not fitted models of any organization. The constraint or joint condition enters as a node's
update rule, the modeling choice that turns a verbal role into a wiring; a different rendering of "platform" or
"market maker" could shift a borderline type between partial and necessary. The gaudens/iungens recovery is the
contribution; the balance-theory non-result is a scope boundary, not a failure of the form. In-silico
throughout.
