# Q214 — Stage 2 deep research: triadic relationships across the literature

<!-- run: 2026-06-30 | 19 sources, verified via Consensus / Scholar Gateway | focus: brokerage, intermediation, balance -->

The third party has a century of theory behind it, and the theory keeps drawing one line: between the third
who joins two others and the third who profits by keeping them apart. This report collects the canonical triad
types and shows the line is everywhere verbal. The gap is a computable criterion, which q213 supplies.

## Simmel and the positions of the third

The oldest treatment is Simmel's [simmel1950]. A relation among three differs in kind from a relation between
two, and Simmel named the positions the third can occupy: the *mediator* (the non-partisan who reconciles),
the *tertius gaudens* (the third who rejoices, profiting from the division of the other two), and *divide et
impera* (the third who actively sets the two against each other to rule them). The mediator binds; the gaudens
and the divider live off a gap they preserve.

## Brokerage: structure and process

Network sociology formalized the structural side. Gould and Fernandez [gould1989structures] partition any
brokered triad by the group membership of its three parties and derive five roles — coordinator (all one
group), gatekeeper and representative (the broker shares a group with one party, spans to the other), itinerant
or consultant (an outsider brokering within a group), and liaison (all three in different groups). Burt
[burt1992structural; burt2005brokerage] made the broker who spans a *structural hole* the engine of advantage:
the broker profits precisely because the two sides are not directly tied.

The process side reintroduced motive. Obstfeld [obstfeld2005tertius] named the *tertius iungens* orientation,
the third who joins, against the gaudens of structural-hole theory. Obstfeld, Borgatti and Davis
[obstfeld2014brokerage] then posed three orientations a broker may take — *conduit* (pass it through), *tertius
gaudens* (bridge while maintaining separation), and *tertius iungens* (connect the parties together). Quintane
and Carnabuci [quintane2016howbrokers] made the distinction behavioral and almost mechanical: a gaudens broker
*intermediates the flow* between the parties, a iungens broker *facilitates direct exchange* between them.
Grosser and colleagues [grosser2019measuring] built separate measures for mediation versus separation
brokerage, and Lee and colleagues [lee2023strain] added the *tertius separans* who keeps people apart at a
cost. Stovel and Shaw [stovel2012brokerage] and Marsden [marsden1982brokerage] survey the field; Granovetter
[granovetter1973weak] supplies the bridge, the single tie whose removal disconnects two clusters.

## Economic intermediation and two-sided platforms

Economics models the intermediary as a party that resolves a friction. Rubinstein and Wolinsky
[rubinstein1987middlemen] model middlemen who exist because search frictions keep buyers and sellers from
meeting directly. Two-sided market theory makes the platform a party that internalizes a cross-side
externality neither side prices: Rochet and Tirole [rochet2003platform], Armstrong [armstrong2006competition],
and Hagiu [hagiu2009platforms] show the platform setting a price structure that gets both sides on board. A
platform that internalizes the joint condition is doing integrating work; a platform that merely gatekeeps
access is a relay behind a barrier.

## Balance theory: a different triad

Heider [heider1946attitudes] and Cartwright and Harary [cartwright1956structural] study a different object: the
signed *sentiment* triad, balanced when the product of its three signs is positive. This triad has no third
party relaying a resource between two others and no forbidden edge; it is a mutual structure, not a mediated
one. It marks the boundary of any mediation-based instrument.

## The open gap

Every distinction above is drawn in words. The gaudens "maintains separation," the iungens "facilitates direct
exchange," the gatekeeper "controls access" — but no one computes whether a given third party is in the
irreducible core because it integrates or only because the direct tie is forbidden. q213's bypass-counterfactual
is that computation: restore the forbidden edge and read whether the party survives. The gap this study closes
is the absence of a formal membership criterion that sorts the literature's triad types, recovers the
gaudens/iungens line as necessary/contingent, and refines it where the words run together — a iungens that
fully joins its parties writes itself out of the core, which the verbal theory does not say. No prior work
applies an integrated-information criterion to the catalog of theoretical triad types; the gap is open.
