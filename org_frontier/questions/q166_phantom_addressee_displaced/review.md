# q166 review

## What the probe shows

The instrument control passes on two cases: the canonical faithful committing triad reads
"triadic" with max Φ 2.0, and the faithful phantom form reproduces the battery's T1 (major
complex {W, S}, core Φ 2.0, S reads W, W never reads C). With the control fixed, the interested
ladder is determinate. The major complex flips from {W, S} to {C} at a single k, and cm[0,1]
drops at that same step, so H1 and H2 read off the same transition rather than two independent
effects. Both are supported.

## Strengths

The result reuses the theory-of-mind battery's exact forms and the Q126 mediator without
reimplementing Φ. The two hypotheses are coupled to one structural event (the W-exit), which is
what makes the "addressee becomes the agenda" reading legible rather than a coincidence of two
thresholds. The order-averaged sweep shows the surviving bind is not an artifact of the
least-warrant override order: the major-complex Φ stays positive across every override set and
ends at 1.0.

## Threats and limits

The structure verdict is "dyadic" at every k because the self-looping counterpart makes the
whole three-node system factor; all the address content lives in the major complex over states,
which is the right object for this question but means the whole-system Φ is uninformative here.
The "bind survives" claim rests on the major-complex Φ, not on whole-system Φ, and the writeup
says so. The counterpart self-loop is a stylization of a referent; a counterpart driven by the
system would change which subsystem carries the surviving bind and is the obvious next model.
The AND baseline is a choice; an OR baseline relabels the override states.

## Validation gap

Exact Φ on a three-node Boolean model. The empirical reading is on synthetic data. No worker,
addressee, or agenda is measured; the labels name output values and connectivity. The
contribution is what the instrument lets the construct say about interested mediation, not a
claim that Φ is necessary to reach this verdict.

## Reproducibility

All readers are exact and the RNG is seeded; three runs are byte-identical. The captured run is
in [`results/output.txt`](results/output.txt).
