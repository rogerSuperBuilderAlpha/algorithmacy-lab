# Q141 findings — a lagging objective rescues the worker that immediate adaptation displaces

Both hypotheses hold. The timescale of the system's learning decides the worker's place in the coordination.
An objective that adapts to the parties instantly substitutes for the worker and pushes it out of the core; an
objective that adapts on a delay keeps the worker constitutive and binds the whole into one irreducible core
at higher Φ.

## Reading

Q129's displacement was a fact about immediate adaptation. When the system's objective reads the parties'
joint state directly and feeds the commit in the same step, the objective stands in for the worker's
contribution, and the worker drops out of the irreducible core — the core becomes the system, a counterpart,
and the objective. Inserting a memory that holds the joint state for a step changes this. The worker's signal
now flows through the memory to the objective with a delay, so the worker stays in the active loop rather than
being short-circuited by the objective, and the whole — worker, system, counterpart, objective, memory — forms
one irreducible core at Φ = 2.0, double the immediate form's.

The organizational reading is about the speed of algorithmic learning. A platform whose objective adapts to
the parties in real time substitutes its own model of them for their participation and displaces them; a
platform that learns slowly, carrying a memory of what the parties did rather than reacting to what they do
now, keeps them constitutive. The same self-interested, self-executing system either pushes the worker out or
holds it in, depending only on whether its learning lags. Slower learning is, structurally, more inclusive.

## Limitations

The lagged form has five nodes to the immediate form's four, so the comparison spans a size change; the memory
is the lag mechanism, and isolating the timescale from the node count would need a same-size delay
construction, the natural next step. A one-step lag is tested; longer lags and partial-memory objectives are
untested. Exact Φ on five nodes; the Φ-to-economic-value bridge is open (Q122).
