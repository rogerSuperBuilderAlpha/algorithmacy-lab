# q196 — Φ_coord predicts algorithmacy more where the system commits

A coordination form carries integrated information when the system jointly determines two parties
who cannot each fix it alone. The study-1 bridge (q193) turns each simulated worker's reported
coordination row into such a W-S-C form and reads its exact IIT-4.0 Φ_MIP as Φ_coord. q193 found
Φ_coord tracks the Algorithmacy Competence Scale across a simulated cohort. This study asks a
sharper question: where does that tracking live? The thesis names the system's commit-vs-convey
stance as the lever. A system that commits binds the parties; a system that conveys relays one
party's signal and the form factors. The prediction is that Φ_coord earns its association with
algorithmacy under commitment, and loses it under conveyance.

## Design

Two simulated cohorts of 600 workers each carry the structure. The commit cohort lets reported
commit vary over the full scale, and its ACS-total loads on Φ_coord only through an interaction with
standardized commit. A worker whose form is irreducible and who reports a committing system scores
higher on algorithmacy; the same irreducible form under low reported commit carries no lift. The
convey cohort floors commit below the threshold, so every form stays dyadic and Φ_coord is zero
throughout.

A moderated regression of ACS-total on Φ_coord, commit, and their product reads the interaction
(H1). The Φ-ACS slope in each cohort and their bootstrap-tested difference read the moderation a
second way (H2). The probe begins with an instrument control on the canonical faithful triad.

## Result

The interaction coefficient is +0.50 with a 95% CI of [+0.36, +0.64]. The standalone Φ_coord term
sits at +0.10 with a CI that includes zero, so the association is carried by the moderation, not a
main effect. The commit cohort's Φ-ACS slope is +0.91; the convey cohort's is flat at zero by
construction; the slope difference is +0.91 with a bootstrap CI of [+0.80, +1.02]. H1 is supported
and H2 is confirmed.

## Scope

The cohort is simulated and the moderation is built into the generator on purpose. The test shows
the bridge and the exact-Φ instrument recover a commit-gated association when one is present, which
is evidence about the measurement chain rather than a measured effect in a real panel. The validation
gap stands: no worker is measured, and the commit lever is demonstrated in silico. Real waves replace
the simulated cohort by supplying the response files in the cohort codebook, at which point the same
moderated regression runs unchanged.
