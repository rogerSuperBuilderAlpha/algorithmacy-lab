# q196 — hypotheses

Question: does the Φ_coord-to-ACS association strengthen under perceived system commitment
(commit vs convey)?

Φ_coord is the per-worker exact IIT-4.0 max Φ_MIP of a worker's W-S-C coordination form, derived
by the study-1 bridge (q193). SA-commit is the reported commit-vs-convey of the system the worker
coordinates through. ACS-total is the standardized Algorithmacy Competence Scale factor score.

Both hypotheses are fixed before computing.

H1: the interaction Φ_coord × SA-commit on ACS-total is positive, with a 95% CI that excludes 0.
Φ_coord predicts ACS more where workers report the system commits. Null: the interaction CI
includes 0.

H2: in a convey-only control cohort, SA-commit is floored so forms stay dyadic and Φ_coord is
near-constant. Its Φ-ACS slope is flat. The commit cohort shows a positive Φ-ACS slope. The slope
difference (commit minus convey) has a 95% CI that excludes 0. Null: commit and convey cohorts have
equal Φ-ACS slopes.

Scope: the cohort is SIMULATED. No worker is measured. The result is evidence about the bridge and
the exact-Φ instrument on synthetic data.
