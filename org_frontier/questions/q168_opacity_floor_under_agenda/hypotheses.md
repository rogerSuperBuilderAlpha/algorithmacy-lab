# q168 — hypotheses

Probe 322. Fixed before computing.

PP1 (predictive_processing.pp1_irreducible_surprise) derives a residual surprise H(out|W) = 0.50 bits for
the faithful hidden-counterpart gate out = W ∧ C, with C hidden and uniform. That half-bit is the floor a
merely hidden counterpart sets: surprise no model from W alone removes. PP2 adds that probing W (active
inference) closes the channel the worker controls and leaves the floor intact. Q126 made the mediator
interested: it imposes an agenda on the k states where the parties least warrant it. This study recomputes
H(out|W) and the W-probing limit with the output drawn from mediator(agenda, k), sweeping k.

## H1

The residual surprise H(out|W) for an interested mediator exceeds the 0.50-bit floor that PP1 derives for
the faithful hidden-counterpart gate, because the agenda adds output variance uncorrelated with anything
the worker can set or see.

NULL: H(out|W) under interest equals the 0.50-bit faithful floor, so an agenda adds no irreducible surprise.

## H2

The added surprise is unremovable by active inference: probing W (PP2) leaves H(out|W) under the interested
mediator unchanged, so the agenda contributes to the opacity floor and not to the channel the worker
controls.

NULL: probing W drives interested H(out|W) toward 0, so the agenda's contribution is epistemic and
self-resolvable.
