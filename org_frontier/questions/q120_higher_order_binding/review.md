# Q120 — review of prior work

The question sits on three earlier results. Finding 8 in
[`STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md`](../../STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md) grounds core membership in graded pivotality, and
the law states that substitutability of any party collapses a triadic form — both assert that parties
carry the bind individually. The influence-membership thread
([`threads/influence_membership`](../../threads/influence_membership/THREAD.md)) shows the converse can
fail: a party can move outcomes yet sit outside the irreducible core, so influence and constitutivity come
apart. Q75's spectator-robustness result (probe 239) established that adding non-participating parties
leaves the core unchanged, which fixes the knockout intervention used here: a knocked-out party is the
spectator construct already validated.

None of these settles whether a triadic form can be held together purely at the higher order, with no
single party pivotal. Q117's Φ-free predicate (probe 274) reads triadicity off the gate logic of the
256-form family but does not ask about single-party necessity. Q120 is the missing test: sweep the triadic
forms and check, for each, whether any single-party knockout preserves the verdict.
