# Q131 — review of prior work

The question is named future work in the interested-third-party synthesis
([`essays/the_interested_third_party.md`](../../essays/the_interested_third_party.md)): the Shapley
decomposition applied across the interestedness axis, to say not only whether the coordination survives but
who captures what as the system serves itself. It joins two earlier lines.

Q111 ([`q111_shapley_value`](../q111_shapley_value/paper.md)) established the value function — coalition value
as subsystem Φ, Shapley value as average marginal contribution — and found the faithful mediator captures
two-thirds. Q131 reuses that function unchanged and reads it along the interestedness ladder of Q126
([`q126_interested_mediator`](../q126_interested_mediator/paper.md)), where the total Φ falls as the mediator
imposes its agenda. The veto-power result (Q112, probe 269) found value concentrated at the mediator even
though every party can collapse the triad; Q131 shows that concentration is conditional — it holds for the
faithful mediator and dissolves as the mediator turns interested.

The standing caveat carried from Q111 and Q122 is the value function's background-state dependence and the
unproven Φ-to-economic-value bridge; Q131 inherits both and states them, claiming only the distribution of
integrated value, not of money.
