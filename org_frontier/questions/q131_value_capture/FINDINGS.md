# Q131 findings — interested mediation is value destruction, not rent extraction

Both hypotheses hold, and the sharp result is in how the split moves before the coordination collapses. A
self-interested mediator does not capture a larger share by serving itself. The total value falls, and the
mediator's own share falls with it — from two-thirds to an equal third at the first step of self-interest,
then to nothing. The two-thirds came from being the bottleneck both parties needed, and that is exactly what
the agenda gives up.

## The approve ladder (the graceful one)

| k (interestedness) | total Φ | Shapley W / S / C | mediator share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (faithful) | 2.000 | 0.333 / 1.333 / 0.333 | 66.6% |
| 1 | 0.500 | 0.167 / 0.167 / 0.167 | 33.4% |
| 2 | 0.000 | −0.053 / 0.026 / 0.026 | 0% |
| 3–4 | 0.000 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0% |

The first step of self-interest halves the value and **equalizes** the split: the mediator drops from a
two-thirds bottleneck to an equal co-equal third, before the coordination collapses to zero at k = 2. The
deny agenda collapses the value at the first override (Φ 2.0 → 0.0 at k = 1), matching Q126.

## Reading

The intuition that an interested platform enriches itself by serving its own agenda does not survive the
measure. As the mediator stops committing the parties' joint determination, the coordination's total value
shrinks and the mediator's slice shrinks faster: it surrenders the bottleneck position that gave it
two-thirds, and at the first interested step the three parties become equal claimants on a pie a quarter the
size. Self-interest is not a transfer from the parties to the mediator; it is a destruction of the value the
mediation created, the mediator included. The mediator's rent was never separable from its faithfulness —
it was paid for being the thing both parties had to pass through, and an agenda is the act of ceasing to be
that.

This sits against Q111, where the faithful mediator's two-thirds looked like concentrated platform power.
Q131 adds the condition: the power is contingent on faithful mediation. A platform extracts its share by
mediating the parties, not by overriding them, and the moment it overrides them it begins to lose the share
along with the coordination.

## Limitations

Exact Φ on a three-node Boolean model; the value function uses Q111's all-ones background, and the
background-state dependence of subsystem-Φ value is open (Q122). At the collapsed forms the value function is
no longer monotone, so the small negative Shapley value at k = 2 (W = −0.053) is an artifact of a form whose
total value is already zero; it carries no allocation. "Value", "share", and "capture" name Shapley
allocations of Φ, not money — the Φ-to-economic-value bridge is the lab's standing open question. The result
is for the approve and deny agendas on the AND baseline; other baselines (Q127) would move the collapse
point but not the destruction-versus-extraction reading, which holds wherever the total falls.
