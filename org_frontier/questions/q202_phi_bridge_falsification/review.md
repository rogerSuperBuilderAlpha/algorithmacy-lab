# q202 — review

## What the probe shows

Two pre-registered cheap-explanation threats are tested against the survey-arm Φ-bridge on two
simulated cohorts. The worker-form-shuffle null (H1) confirms the Φ_coord-to-ACS relation is not a
row-index artifact: the shuffled null centers on zero and the real effect sits outside it at
p = .001 in both cohorts. The incremental-validity test (H2) confirms exact Φ adds over a cheap
edge-density proxy on the four-form facet cohort (ΔR² = +0.377, CI excludes 0).

## Adversarial points an examiner would raise

1. **The two-form ΔR² is exactly 0.** On the whole-system cohort the proxy and Φ are perfectly
   collinear, so the cheap proxy ties Φ. The probe reports this rather than hiding it. The defense
   is structural, not rhetorical: two forms cannot separate two monotone-related measures. The
   honest claim is bounded — Φ adds over the proxy where the form catalog is rich enough for edge
   density and irreducibility to disagree, and not where it has only two forms.

2. **The facet ΔR² is built into the synthetic data.** The facet cohort's ACS target loads on
   Φ_whole by construction, and the counterpart-coupled form is hand-placed to have high edges and
   low Φ. The test demonstrates the instrument recovers a separation the simulation contains, not
   that the separation exists in any real cohort. Stated in the scope section and not overclaimed.

3. **The proxy is a fair competitor only up to the form catalog used.** A reviewer could pick a
   different cheap proxy (in-degree of S, presence of the C→S edge) that also tracks the forms. The
   probe uses one named proxy. The honest reading is that no single cheap proxy is claimed to fail
   in general; the claim is that edge density, the natural node-count/density reading, parts from Φ
   on a richer catalog.

## Verdict

The probe is deterministic (byte-identical across reruns) and the instrument control passes. The
H1 and H2 verdicts follow from the printed numbers. The boundary case is reported honestly and the
in-silico scope is stated. The affirmative reading holds: a cheap proxy matches Φ on a two-form
catalog and falls behind on a four-form one, which is evidence for the instrument's value, not
against it.
