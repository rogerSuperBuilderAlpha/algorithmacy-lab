# q145 — hypotheses

Question. When one mediator spans (reads and is read by) every node of an otherwise
unconnected party set, how do Φ and the core scale versus a mediator that spans only a
fraction of the parties?

## H1 — the spanning-mediator law

A fully spanning single mediator holds the entire node set in the major complex, and Φ grows
linearly in n (the conjunctive-hub law). A partially spanning mediator caps the core at its
span+1 (the hub plus the parties it covers) and leaves the unspanned parties out.

Null: core size is independent of the span fraction.

## H2 — fraction collapse

Φ scales with the spanned fraction f as a single monotone curve that collapses across n when
plotted in f. Span fraction, not raw n, is the controlling variable.

Null: Φ(f) curves for different n do not collapse.

## Scope

The forms are synthetic Boolean coordination structures. No organization is measured. The
study explores how the major-complex membership and Φ respond to one design parameter, the
span of a single mediator. It does not establish that any real coordination form has this
structure.
