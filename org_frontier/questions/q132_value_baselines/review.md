# Q132 — review of prior work

The question extends Q131 ([`q131_value_capture`](../q131_value_capture/paper.md)), which read value capture
along the interestedness axis on the AND baseline and found destruction not extraction, across the four
baselines of Q127 ([`q127_interest_baselines`](../q127_interest_baselines/paper.md)), where self-interest
collapses a sparse mediator and re-integrates a balanced one. It reuses the Shapley value function of Q111
([`q111_shapley_value`](../q111_shapley_value/paper.md)), but with the background state read at the verdict's
integrating state rather than fixed at all-ones.

That correction connects to the critical review of the value waves, which flagged the value function's
background-state dependence and the unproven Φ-to-economic-value bridge as open (Q122). Q132 shows the
background choice is load-bearing: the all-ones reading misses the OR mediator's value and the XOR mediator's
re-integration, so the verdict-aligned reading is needed even to reproduce what the verdict already reports.

What is new is the value-side statement of the Q127 flip: the mediator's two-thirds rent is not a fixed
platform power but a function of whether the form sits at full integration, and self-interest moves it there
on a balanced baseline and away from it on a sparse one.
