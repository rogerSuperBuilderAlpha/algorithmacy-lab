# Q134 — review of prior work

Q134 extends Q111 ([`q111_shapley_value`](../q111_shapley_value/paper.md)), which computed the mediator's
two-thirds on the three-party triad, to larger conjunctive coordinations, and bounds the political-economy
reading of the interested-mediation synthesis
([`essays/political_economy_of_interested_mediation.md`](../../essays/political_economy_of_interested_mediation.md)).
The conjunctive scaling Φ = n − 1 it relies on is the lab's established multiparty result
([`multiparty/`](../../multiparty/)); the new content is the Shapley split at each size, showing the
mediator's share is not scale-invariant but dilutes toward one-half. It connects to the veto-power result
(Q112), which found destruction democratic but value concentrated — Q134 shows the concentration itself
thins as parties are added.
