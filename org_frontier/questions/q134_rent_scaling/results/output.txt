PROBE 289 (Q134) — does the mediator's two-thirds rent scale with the number of parties?
====================================================================================
  CONTROL 3-party triad: total Φ=2.000, mediator 1.333 share 66.6%  PASS

  parties | total Φ | mediator Shapley | mediator share | outer-party share
  --------+---------+------------------+----------------+------------------
     3    |   2.000 |            1.333 |         66.6%  |            16.7%
     4    |   3.000 |            1.750 |         58.3%  |            13.9%
     5    |   4.000 |            2.200 |         55.0%  |            11.2%

====================================================================================
  H1 (mediator share is scale-invariant at two-thirds): NOT SUPPORTED
  H2 (the share declines as parties are added — the rent dilutes): SUPPORTED  (share 66.6% -> 58.3% -> 55.0%)
  Reading: the mediator's rent is not scale-invariant. As the coordination grows the total
  integrated value grows (Φ = n-1) but the mediator's slice falls toward one-half; the parties
  collectively keep more (33% -> 45%), though each individual party keeps less. The bottleneck's
  dominance is a small-coordination phenomenon — in a larger pool it still takes the plurality.
====================================================================================
