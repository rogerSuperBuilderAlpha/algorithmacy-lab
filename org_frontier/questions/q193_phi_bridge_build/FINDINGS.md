# q193 — findings

A per-worker Φ-based coordination measure can be derived from each simulated worker's coordination form,
and on the synthetic panel it tracks the latent algorithmacy construct. The bridge module maps a worker's
reported interdependence, commit authority, and substitutability to a W-S-C Boolean form, then reads the
form's exact max Φ_MIP. Workers whose conditions map to the irreducible commit form (Φ_coord = 2.0) score
higher on ACS-total than workers whose conditions map to the factorizable convey form (Φ_coord = 0.0).

Forced-dyadic rewiring sets Φ_coord to 0 for every worker and the correlation collapses, which places the
association in the form's irreducibility rather than in a scale shared between Φ_coord and ACS.

The instrument control passed: the faithful triad `[x1, x0&x2, x1]` reads triadic with max Φ_MIP = 2.0.

| cohort  | Φ_coord range | r(Φ_coord, ACS-total) | 95% CI             |
|---------|---------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| bridge  | [0.00, 2.00]  | +0.4234               | [+0.3257, +0.5121] |
| control | [0.00, 0.00]  | +0.0000               | [+0.0000, +0.0000] |

N = 300; 49 commit forms, 251 convey forms in the bridge cohort.

- **H1: SUPPORTED.** Bridge r = +0.4234, 95% CI [+0.3257, +0.5121]: above 0.20 and the interval excludes 0.
- **H2: CONFIRMED.** Control r = 0.0000 (|r| < 0.10) while the bridge association survives at +0.4234.

Scope: the cohort is simulated. No worker is measured. The result is evidence about the Φ bridge on
synthetic data, and is the shared module the later survey studies (invariance, growth, sub-competences)
build on.
