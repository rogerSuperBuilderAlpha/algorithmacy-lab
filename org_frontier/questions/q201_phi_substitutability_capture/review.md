## What the probe shows

Substitutability lowers Φ_coord and Φ_coord carries most of the substitutability-to-algorithmacy
association on the simulated cohort. The instrument control passes on the canonical faithful triad
(Φ = 2.0, triadic). The bridge map is auditable: the pivotal-W form reads Φ_coord = 2.0 (triadic) and
the substitutable form reads Φ_coord = 0.0 (dyadic). Both hypotheses hold: H1 SUPPORTED, H2 CONFIRMED.

## Adversarial reading

The capture path is built into the synthetic generator. The result is recovery of a designed
structure, so it speaks to the measurement chain, not to a worker population. The direct
substitutability-to-ACS leg is nonzero, so the claim is mediation-dominant, not full mediation: a thin
path reaches ACS without passing through Φ_coord, which the bootstrap reports honestly.

A reviewer may ask whether the binary form map (Φ_coord ∈ {0, 2}) overstates the structural channel.
Φ_coord here takes two values by the bridge's commit-vs-convey gate, so the structural leg is a
two-point contrast rather than a graded dose. The pivotal-W control answers the shared-scale worry:
when the form cannot factor, the substitutability slope on Φ_coord is flat, so the bridge-arm slope is
not a scale artifact.

## On the apparatus

The exact-Φ instrument does the structural reading: it is what turns a reported coordination row into a
Φ_coord and grades its irreducibility. The contribution is the principled bridge from a survey scale to
an irreducibility measure, not a claim that Φ beats a cheap factorization check on this two-form map.
