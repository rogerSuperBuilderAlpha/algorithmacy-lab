# q201 — hypotheses

Question: does worker substitutability degrade Φ_coord, and does that degraded Φ_coord track lower
reported algorithmacy (the displacement/capture prediction)?

Φ_coord is the per-worker exact IIT-4.0 max Φ_MIP of the worker's W-S-C coordination form (study-1
bridge, q193). SU is the reported substitutability of the worker's role. A worker whose role admits
an interchangeable copy sits in a more factorizable coordination form.

## H1

Higher perceived substitutability (SU) lowers Φ_coord: workers whose form admits a substitutable copy
sit in more factorizable structures, so β(SU → Φ_coord) < 0 with a 95% CI that excludes zero. The
lower Φ_coord then predicts lower ACS: β(Φ_coord → ACS) > 0 with a CI that excludes zero.

Null: SU is unrelated to Φ_coord.

## H2

Φ_coord mediates the SU-to-ACS association. The indirect path SU → Φ_coord → ACS is nonzero
(bootstrap CI excludes zero) and its magnitude exceeds the direct SU → ACS path.

Null: no indirect path through Φ_coord.

## Control

The pivotal-W cohort holds every worker non-substitutable, so SU cannot factor the form and Φ_coord
is constant. A flat SU → Φ_coord slope in this arm shows the degradation in the bridge arm rides on
substitutability factoring the coordination form, not on a shared-scale artifact between SU and any Φ
proxy.
