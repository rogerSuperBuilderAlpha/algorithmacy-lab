# q201 — substitutability degrades Φ_coord and captures algorithmacy

A coordination form carries integrated information when the system jointly determines two parties who
cannot each fix it alone. The study-1 bridge (q193) turns each simulated worker's reported
coordination row into such a W-S-C form and reads its exact IIT-4.0 Φ_MIP as Φ_coord. The
displacement thesis names substitutability as a force that drains it. A worker whose role admits an
interchangeable copy sits in a more factorizable structure: a substitutable copy relays the worker's
signal, the system no longer needs the worker as one of two irreducible parties, and the form factors.
The prediction is that perceived substitutability lowers Φ_coord, and the lower Φ_coord tracks lower
algorithmacy — that capture runs through the structure, not around it.

## Design

Two simulated cohorts of 600 workers each carry the structure. The bridge cohort lets substitutability
vary over the full scale and gates the form: a high-substitutability row maps to the convey
pass-through with Φ_coord = 0, and a low-substitutability, interdependent, committing row maps to the
irreducible AND(W,C) mediated triad with Φ_coord = 2.0. ACS-total rides predominantly on Φ_coord, with
a thin coordination-latent residual that leaves a minor direct substitutability path. The pivotal-W
control holds every worker non-substitutable, so substitutability cannot factor the form and Φ_coord
is constant.

A regression of Φ_coord on substitutability reads the structural degradation, and a regression of
ACS-total on Φ_coord reads the construct leg (H1). A single-mediator bootstrap of the path
substitutability → Φ_coord → ACS reads the capture (H2). The probe begins with an instrument control
on the canonical faithful triad.

## Result

In the bridge cohort the substitutability-to-Φ_coord slope is −0.67 with a 95% CI of [−0.72, −0.62],
and the Φ_coord-to-ACS slope is +0.66 with a CI of [+0.59, +0.73]. The pivotal-W control holds
Φ_coord constant at 2.0, so its substitutability slope is flat: the degradation rides on
substitutability factoring the form. The bootstrapped indirect effect is −0.36 with a CI of
[−0.43, −0.29], and the direct effect is −0.14 with a CI of [−0.23, −0.05]; the indirect magnitude
exceeds the direct. H1 is supported and H2 is confirmed.

## Scope

The cohort is simulated and the capture structure is built into the generator on purpose. The test
shows the bridge and the exact-Φ instrument recover a substitutability-driven, Φ-mediated path when
one is present, which is evidence about the measurement chain rather than a measured effect in a real
panel. The validation gap stands: no worker is measured, and the displacement/capture path is
demonstrated in silico. Real waves replace the simulated cohort by supplying the response files in the
cohort codebook, at which point the same regressions and bootstrap run unchanged.
