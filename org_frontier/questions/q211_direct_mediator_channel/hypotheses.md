# Q211 — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computation)

Five working hypotheses spanning the space, each with its structurally-expected null and the outcome it
predicts. Written and committed before any test runs.

Model: two complete conjunctive triads, triad 1 = {W1,S1,C1}, triad 2 = {W2,S2,C2}, n=6. Each triad on its
own reads triadic at Φ=2.0. A direct channel couples the two mediators: S1 reads S2 and S2 reads S1,
swept over three rules — none (control, no channel), AND, OR. The complement of q210, where the link ran
through a shared counterpart instead of directly between mediators.

## H1 — instrument control
- **Claim:** the single conjunctive triad reads triadic at Φ=2.0, and with no channel the two triads stay separate.
- **H0:** the control does not reproduce the triad's known value.
- **Predicted outcome:** single triad triadic Φ=2.000000; with the none channel the whole system factors (Φ_MIP=0) and the major complex is one triad at Φ=2.0.

## H2 — a direct channel merges the two cores
- **Claim:** under the AND channel the major complex spans both triads (contains mediators from both, or members of both triads).
- **H0:** the major complex stays inside one triad, as it did in q210.
- **Predicted outcome:** the AND-channel major complex contains nodes from triad 1 and from triad 2.

## H3 — the merged core is super-additive
- **Claim:** the merged core carries more integration than a single triad, Φ > 2.0.
- **H0:** the core Φ stays at the single-triad value of 2.0.
- **Predicted outcome:** AND-channel core Φ > 2.0.

## H4 — the direct channel succeeds where the shared counterpart failed
- **Claim:** the direct mediator-mediator channel merges the cores, unlike the shared counterpart of q210, so the obstacle there was the indirect path, not the principle.
- **H0:** the direct channel also fails to merge, matching q210.
- **Predicted outcome:** at least one channel rule produces a major complex spanning both triads, which no q210 bridge did.

## H5 — the combination rule matters
- **Claim:** the channel rule changes the outcome; AND and OR do not give the same core.
- **H0:** the channel rule makes no difference to the core.
- **Predicted outcome:** the AND-channel core and the OR-channel core differ in membership or Φ.
