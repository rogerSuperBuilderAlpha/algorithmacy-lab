# Q217 — Does a rotating mediator keep the coordination irreducible, and who holds the bottleneck? · Stage 1 review

**Timing.** This review was completed after probes 453–456 ran, during cleanup. The scaffolded file was empty
when the hypotheses were committed. It cites only the lab's own record (`probes/PROBES.md`, threads), which

**Question.** When the mediator role passes among the parties on a clock (the current chair aggregates the
others and the others copy the chair), does the coordination keep its irreducible core, and which parties
hold the veto?
