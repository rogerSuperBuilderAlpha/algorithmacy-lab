# Q137 findings — the worker cannot reclaim value by gaming; resistance breaks the coordination

Both hypotheses hold. The worker has no structural lever to capture value. Acting on its own agenda rather
than the system's signal collapses the irreducible bind, so gaming yields nothing; the worker's share rises
only when the system defects and the shrinking value equalizes, but its absolute take never exceeds the
faithful baseline.

## Reading

The worker's structural role in the triad is to read the system faithfully, and that role is a thin one: its
rule is a copy of the system's commit, so any agenda the worker injects makes it unresponsive, and an
unresponsive worker decouples from the loop and the triad factors. Gaming the system therefore destroys the
coordination rather than capturing a share of it — the worker that acts on its own theory rather than the
system's signal breaks the bind. The one place the worker's share rises is when the system defects: there the
total value falls and equalizes (Q131), lifting the worker's share from a sixth to a third, but the worker's
absolute value falls with the total, from 0.333 to 0.167. The worker is better off in share and worse off in
substance.

The political-economy reading is that resistance is not redistribution. A worker pressed by an extractive
system cannot reclaim value by withdrawing faithful coordination; withdrawing it collapses the coordination,
which leaves the worker with a larger share of nothing. The structural asymmetry is that the system, reading
two parties, has room to be selectively interested and survive at reduced value, while the worker, reading
one, has no such room — its only interested move is to break the coordination.

## Limitations

Exact Φ on the three-node triad where the worker reads only the system; a richer worker that also infers the
counterpart (Probe 9) would have more states and possibly more room, and is the natural next model. Value read
at the integrating state; the Φ-to-money bridge is open (Q122). Negative Shapley values at collapsed forms are
non-monotonicity artifacts.
