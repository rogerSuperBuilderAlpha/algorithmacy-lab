# Q137 — review of prior work

Q137 turns the interestedness lens from the system to the worker, extending Q126
([`q126_interested_mediator`](../q126_interested_mediator/paper.md)) and the value studies (Q131, Q132). The
result that the worker's share rises while its absolute value falls is the Q131 equalization seen from the
worker's side. The structural reason gaming collapses the coordination is the substitutability law
([`STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md`](../../STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md)) and Probe 3's finding that an exogenous,
self-driven rule decouples from the core. The connection to algorithmic resistance — workers acting on folk
theories of the system — is the cognition/qualitative reading the lab's watch tracks; Q137 gives it a
structural verdict: resistance modeled as the worker imposing its own determination breaks the bind rather
than redistributing it. The natural extension is Probe 9's inferential worker, which reads more than the
system and would have more room to act.
