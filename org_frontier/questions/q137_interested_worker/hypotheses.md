# Q137 — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

The interested-mediator studies put the agenda in the system. The worker is also described as interested —
algorithmic resistance is the worker acting on its own theory of the system rather than the system's signal.
Q137 makes the worker interested too and asks whether gaming reclaims value, or whether mutual self-interest
only destroys the coordination.

The triad W, S, C, faithful rules W' = S, S' = W ∧ C, C' = S. The system's interestedness is the Q126 ladder;
the worker's is the analogous override of its own rule (the worker reads only S, and an interested worker acts
regardless of S on some states). Both levels are swept.

- **H1.** The worker cannot reclaim absolute value by gaming: worker interestedness collapses the coordination
  (Φ → 0), so the worker captures nothing.
- **H2.** The worker's share rises only when the system defects (the Q131 equalization), and even then its
  absolute value falls. No cell lets the worker gain.
- **Null.** Worker interestedness raises the worker's value.

Method: sweep k_S, k_W in {0,1,2}; per cell the Φ and the worker's Shapley value (absolute and share).
