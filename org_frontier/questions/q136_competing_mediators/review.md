# Q136 — review of prior work

Q136 extends the value line (Q111, Q131–Q135) from one mediator to two, and connects to the substitutability
law in [`STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md`](../../STRUCTURAL_FINDINGS.md): a form demands algorithmacy only when no
party is substitutable, and two substitutable mediators are exactly the violation — the result that they
factor the coordination is that law read in the value setting. The multiparty arm ([`multiparty/`](../../multiparty/))
established that conjunctive coordinations scale as Φ = n − 1, which the complements form (Φ = 4.0 at four
nodes) instances. The new content is the distribution: substitutes leave nothing to distribute, complements
split evenly, and neither matches the market intuition that competition returns the platform's cut to the
parties.
