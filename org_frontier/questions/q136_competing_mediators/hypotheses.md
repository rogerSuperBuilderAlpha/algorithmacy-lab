# Q136 — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

A single faithful mediator captures two-thirds (Q111). Q136 asks what a second mediator does, in two
regimes. The form is four nodes: W, two mediators S1/S2, C, each mediator committing Si' = W ∧ C. In the
substitutes form the parties read either (W' = C' = S1 ∨ S2); in the complements form they read both
(W' = C' = S1 ∧ S2).

- **H1.** Substitutable mediators eliminate the irreducible coordination: with either sufficient, neither is
  necessary, the form factors (Φ → 0), and there is no rent for anyone.
- **H2.** Complementary mediators split the value: each captures less than two-thirds and the parties more,
  the total rising — a required second mediator dilutes the rent toward the parties.
- **Null.** A second mediator leaves the value and split unchanged.

Method: build both forms; whole-system verdict, major complex, total Φ, Shapley value at the integrating state.
