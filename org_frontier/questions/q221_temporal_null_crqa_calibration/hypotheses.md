# Q221 — Research questions and hypothesis status

**Retrospective exploratory record, not preregistration.** The previous scaffold contained unfilled H1–H5 placeholders; they were **not** valid precommitted hypotheses. The exact-Φ decision rules in `EXACT_PHI_DECISION_RULES.md` were fixed before the post-rules Φ run, but this does not make the broader study prospectively preregistered.

## E1 — Population summary sufficiency (exploratory)

Question: Are stationary categorical cross-channel match-rate profiles sufficient to identify q161 leave-one-out bottleneck sets within the selected three-node Boolean model families?

The bounded search found candidate pairs with equal cross-profiles and different eligible bottleneck sets. A stricter retrospective filter found three 48-group-unique classes matching channel marginals and all tested auto/cross profiles. This is an **existence result under the documented search and target definitions**, not a confirmatory population-frequency estimate.

## E2 — Finite-sample discrimination (exploratory)

Question: Can fixed classifiers distinguish selected pairs from finite trajectories using recurrence summaries, marginals, or complete transition information?

The recorded simple summary baselines were near chance on held-out trajectories; transition-aware classifiers achieved 100% in the tested sets. This does **not** establish equality of finite-sample summary distributions or generalization to unseen network forms.

## E3 — Temporal null calibration (separate exploratory pilot)

Question: Does independent time shuffling control false positives for zero-lag Pearson correlation between independent persistent binary series?

A small seeded pilot reports inflated false positives under high persistence. This is a known statistical failure mode, not a q220-specific or confirmatory result.

## Not yet specified prospectively

A future confirmatory H1–H5 suite, its null-generating mechanisms, form-level replication, primary estimand, uncertainty method, sample-size determination, and multiplicity correction **have not been frozen**. They must be preregistered separately before any confirmatory data are generated. Do not reinterpret E1–E3 as original precommitted H1–H5.
