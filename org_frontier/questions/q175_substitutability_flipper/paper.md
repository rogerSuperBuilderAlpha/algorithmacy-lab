# q175 — The substitutability flipper: coding a worker as interchangeable dissolves the triad

A coded account of a coordination names, for each party, how its next state follows from the
current states of the others. The worker can be named two ways. The specific reading makes the
worker one irreplaceable party: the system binds the worker to the counterpart, S = W & R. The
substitutable reading makes the worker a slot that any of several members can fill: S = (D1 | ... |
Dk) & R. Which reading a coder writes down is a coding choice. This study measures what that choice
does to the exact-Φ verdict, and whether the coder-disagreement interval reports the choice as such.

## The flip

The specific worker reads triadic at max Φ_MIP = 2.0. The system, the worker, and the counterpart
form an irreducible whole: cut any party from the rest and the structure loses information.

Re-encode the worker as a pool of k interchangeable members and the structure dissolves. At pool
sizes k = 2, 3, 4 the verdict reads dyadic at max_phi = 0. The OR over the pool is the mechanism:
any one member firing carries the slot, so removing a single member changes nothing the system
depends on. Substitutability is exactly redundancy, and redundancy is what Φ measures the absence
of. A worker who can be swapped out is, to the integrated-information reading, not part of an
irreducible triad at all.

The number reports what the coded account yields once the worker is written as substitutable. The
same coordination, coded specific, reads triadic; coded pooled, reads dyadic. The verdict moves with
the coding.

## The interval flags it

Two coders who read the same account differently produce two readings: one at Φ = 2.0, one at
Φ = 0. The bridge from q173 propagates that split into a Φ interval. When the panel is genuinely
contested, the reading interval runs from 0 to the specific Φ on 97.5% of contested forms. An
interval anchored at 0 with positive upper endpoint says the verdict is contested between dissolved
and irreducible, so the reader sees a coding split awaiting adjudication.

The control fixes the scale. A unanimous-pool panel, where every coder reads the worker as
substitutable, collapses the interval to [0, 0]: no disagreement, no spread, a settled dyadic
reading. Spread in the interval tracks disagreement among coders, and only that.

## What this shows and does not

The flip and the interval are properties of synthetic coded accounts with known ground truth. No
worker is measured. The study does not establish that any real coordination is dyadic or triadic;
it establishes that the substitutability coding is the lever that moves the verdict, and that the
disagreement interval surfaces the lever when coders disagree about pulling it. Whether a given
field worker is best read as specific or substitutable is an empirical coding question this study
does not answer. Results are on synthetic data.

The contribution is the diagnostic. A coordination whose verdict flips under the substitutability
reading is one where the coding decision carries the result, and the interval marks exactly those
cases for a coder to adjudicate.
