# Q203 hypotheses — fixed before computing

The recurrence line's synthetic studies (q153–162) found that behavioral coupling does not cleanly recover
the structural coordination verdict. The recurrence program's open gap names the missing test: a head-to-head
of CRQA, transfer entropy, Granger causality, and convergent cross mapping on the *same real* dyadic data.
This study runs that head-to-head on a real two-party coordination (the crqa-package `handmovement` dyad),
with each directed measure validated on a control in its own domain.

- **H1.** All three directed measures — transfer entropy, Granger causality, convergent cross mapping — reach
  significance against circular-shift surrogates (p < .05) on the real dyad.
- **H2.** The three directed measures agree on the direction of coupling.

A null for H1 would say that the directed measures do not individually establish coupling on this real dyad.
A null for H2 would say they contradict each other on direction. The instrument controls (a linear AR drive
for transfer entropy and Granger; a coupled chaotic logistic drive for CCM) must pass first, or no claim is
made.
