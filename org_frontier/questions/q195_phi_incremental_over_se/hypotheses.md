# q195 — hypotheses

Question: does Φ_coord carry incremental association with the Algorithmacy Competence Scale (ACS)
beyond general self-efficacy (SE) and belonging (BE)? This is the discriminant-validity test of the
Φ bridge: a measure that only restated generic competence would add nothing once self-efficacy is
controlled, and would track competence as well as it tracks algorithmacy.

Both hypotheses are fixed before computing.

**H1.** Φ_coord predicts ACS-total controlling for SE and BE. The partial correlation
r(Φ_coord, ACS | SE, BE) exceeds 0.15 and its 95% CI excludes 0.
Null: the partial-r CI includes 0 once SE and BE are partialled out.

**H2.** Φ_coord's correlation with the ACS factor exceeds its correlation with the SE factor. The
bootstrap CI on Δ = r(Φ, ACS) − r(Φ, SE) excludes 0, so Φ tracks algorithmacy specifically rather
than generic competence.
Null: r(Φ, ACS) and r(Φ, SE) are equal (the Δ CI includes 0).
