# q195 — Discriminant validity of the Φ bridge: incremental association over self-efficacy and belonging

A bridge measure earns its place only if it adds something the existing constructs miss. Self-efficacy
and belonging are standard covariates in survey work on competence and engagement, and either could
absorb a naive coordination measure. The question here is whether Φ_coord, the exact-Φ reading of a
worker's coordination form, still predicts the Algorithmacy Competence Scale once self-efficacy and
belonging are controlled, and whether it tracks algorithmacy more specifically than it tracks generic
competence.

The study-1 bridge module supplies Φ_coord. It maps a worker's reported interdependence, commit
authority, and substitutability to a W-S-C Boolean form and reads the form's max Φ_MIP: 2.0 for the
irreducible commit form `S' = W AND C`, 0.0 for the factorizable convey form. This study extends the
synthetic cohort with two nuisance constructs. A coordination latent drives the reported conditions and
Φ_coord. ACS-total loads on that latent and on a separate generic-competence latent. Self-efficacy loads
mostly on generic competence and only lightly on coordination. Belonging loads on its own latent with a
light competence tie. Self-efficacy ends up correlated with ACS at +0.45, which is what makes the
control informative.

H1 asks whether Φ_coord survives the control. A hierarchical regression enters self-efficacy and
belonging first, then Φ_coord. The nuisance block explains 21% of ACS variance; adding Φ_coord raises
that to 34%, a 13-point gain. The partial correlation r(Φ_coord, ACS | SE, BE) is +0.40 with a 95% CI of
[+0.32, +0.48]. The interval clears the 0.15 threshold and excludes 0. H1 is supported.

H2 asks whether Φ_coord is algorithmacy-specific. Φ_coord correlates with ACS at +0.40 and with
self-efficacy at +0.10. The bootstrap CI on the difference is [+0.20, +0.39], excluding 0. The Φ
instrument reads the coordination-specific signal that algorithmacy carries and self-efficacy does not.
H2 is supported.

The result is built on planted structure: algorithmacy and coordination share a latent that
self-efficacy reaches only weakly, and the bridge recovers that separation through Φ. The cohort is
simulated and no worker is measured, so the finding states what the instrument and the discriminant
design yield on synthetic data. Whether a real cohort separates algorithmacy from self-efficacy this way
is the open empirical question, and the validation gap is the unfielded survey. The contribution is a
discriminant-validity test the survey arm can run once waves exist, with the pre-specified thresholds and
the Φ bridge already in place.
