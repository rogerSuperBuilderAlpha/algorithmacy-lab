# Q212 — Stage 3 hypotheses (fixed before computation)

Five working hypotheses spanning the space, each with its structurally-expected null and the outcome it
predicts. Written and committed before any test runs.

Model: two complete conjunctive triads, triad 1 = {W1,S1,C1}, triad 2 = {W2,S2,C2}, n=6. A single AND
channel links one homologous node pair across the triads, swept over three locations: mediator (S1↔S2,
the q211 AND baseline), worker (W1↔W2), counterpart (C1↔C2). A core "spans both triads" when it contains a
member of {W1,S1,C1} and a member of {W2,S2,C2}. In the conjunctive triad the worker and counterpart are
symmetric leaves, so the worker and counterpart channels are predicted to agree.

## H1 — instrument control and q211 replication
- **Claim:** the single triad reads triadic at Φ=2.0, and the mediator channel reproduces q211 — triadic whole system, core spanning both triads at Φ=3.0.
- **H0:** the control or the q211 replication does not reproduce.
- **Predicted outcome:** single triad triadic Φ=2.000000; mediator-channel major complex spans both triads, Φ=3.0.

## H2 — a worker-worker channel merges
- **Claim:** an AND channel between the two workers produces a major complex spanning both triads.
- **H0:** the worker channel leaves the major complex inside one triad.
- **Predicted outcome:** worker-channel major complex contains members of both triads.

## H3 — a counterpart-counterpart channel merges
- **Claim:** an AND channel between the two counterparts produces a major complex spanning both triads, and matches the worker channel by leaf symmetry.
- **H0:** the counterpart channel leaves the major complex inside one triad.
- **Predicted outcome:** counterpart-channel major complex spans both triads, with the same Φ as the worker channel.

## H4 — the mediator channel is the strongest
- **Claim:** the mediator-mediator channel gives the highest core Φ of the three locations.
- **H0:** all three locations give the same core Φ.
- **Predicted outcome:** mediator-channel core Φ > worker-channel core Φ and > counterpart-channel core Φ.

## H5 — channel location matters
- **Claim:** the location of the channel changes the result; not all three placements give the same spanning core.
- **H0:** all three locations give a core of the same span and Φ.
- **Predicted outcome:** at least one location differs from the others in whether the core spans both triads or in its Φ.
