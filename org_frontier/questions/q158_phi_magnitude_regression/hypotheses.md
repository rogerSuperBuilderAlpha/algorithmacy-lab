# q158 — hypotheses

Fixed before computing.

## H1
Whole-system md_recurrence DET correlates positively with major-complex Φ across the corpus and
the random 3- and 4-node ensembles, with Spearman rho above 0.4.

Null: the DET-Φ correlation is not different from zero.

Verdict: REFUTED. Spearman rho(DET, Φ) = -0.19 (p = 9.9e-03), below the 0.4 threshold and
negative in sign. The whole-system recurrence rate runs more strongly the other way, rho = -0.40.

## H2
The DET-Φ relationship saturates. Above a DET ceiling, Φ keeps rising while DET does not, so a
linear fit underpredicts the high-Φ forms and leaves a positive high-Φ residual.

Null: the DET-Φ relation is linear, with no systematic high-Φ residual.

Verdict: REFUTED as stated. The high-Φ forms do carry a positive residual (+0.53 against -0.47
for the rest), but a monotone increasing fit does not beat the line (isotonic RMSE 0.536 vs linear
0.526). The residual pattern reflects the negative trend, not saturation of a rising curve: high-Φ
forms sit at low DET, which the downward line underpredicts.
