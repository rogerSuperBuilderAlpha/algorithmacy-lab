# q158 — Whole-system recurrence does not track the magnitude of major-complex Φ

Earlier studies in this line read a binary structural verdict off behavioral recurrence: a few CRQA
features separate triadic from dyadic forms. The binary success raises a sharper question. Φ is a
continuous magnitude, and a coordination form has more or less of it. If the behavioral recurrence
of a sampled run tracks that magnitude, then a CRQA reading would estimate how integrated a form is,
not just which side of a line it falls on.

The test pairs one whole-system behavioral measure with one structural magnitude. For each form,
md_recurrence DET reads the share of whole-system recurrence that sits on deterministic diagonal
lines, the part of the system's revisiting of global states that is sustained. Exact major-complex
Φ reads the structural magnitude from IIT-4.0. The pool is the 3-node forms_library plus random 3-
and 4-node ensembles, 188 forms once the no-complex forms are dropped.

The correlation is negative. Spearman rho between DET and Φ is -0.19 (p = 9.9e-03), and the
whole-system recurrence rate runs further the same way at -0.40. The forms with the highest DET are
not the high-Φ forms; they are often low-Φ forms. A tight limit cycle revisits a handful of global
states on long deterministic lines, so its DET is high, and it is weakly integrated, so its Φ is
low. The forms that carry the most Φ keep moving through new global states, which lowers their
recurrence.

The saturation hypothesis fails for the same reason. A linear fit of Φ on DET does leave a positive
residual on the top-Φ quartile (+0.53 against -0.47 for the rest), which by itself looks like
underprediction. A monotone increasing fit does not improve on the line (isotonic RMSE 0.536 versus
linear 0.526), so the residual is not the signature of a rising curve that flattens. It is the
signature of a falling line: the high-Φ forms sit at low DET, where a downward slope places its
lowest predictions.

The result narrows the bridge's claim. Behavioral recurrence carries enough signal to call the
binary verdict, and the magnitude of integration is a different quantity that whole-system DET and
RR do not estimate, and estimate with the wrong sign when forced. The contribution of the IIT lens
here is the exact magnitude that exposes the gap: without a principled continuous Φ to regress
against, the negative relation would be invisible.

Scope. Every number is exact IIT-4.0 Φ on synthetic Boolean coordination forms. No worker is
measured and no field coordination is sampled. The validation gap from these in-silico forms to
real coordination data is open, and the finding is a property of the corpus, not a measurement of an
organization.
