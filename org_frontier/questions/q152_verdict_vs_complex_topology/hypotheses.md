# q152 — Hypotheses

## Question

Across the topologies built in studies 1-9 at matched n=5,6, does the whole-system
verdict (triadic vs dyadic) ever disagree with major-complex membership — a triadic
verdict whose core excludes one or more of the parties it nominally coordinates? If it
does, the two readouts are not interchangeable diagnostics.

## H1

There exist studied topologies (e.g. chain, large-m symmetric multihub, hub-chain) where
the whole-system verdict is triadic yet the major complex excludes one or more parties.
The whole-system verdict and core membership are then not interchangeable diagnostics.

Null: a triadic verdict always implies a full-party core.

## H2

Verdict-complex disagreement appears exactly when a topology carries a node whose Shapley
value is zero, so a zero-value party is the marker of a coordinated-but-excluded member.
Operationally: the biconditional `disagree <=> has-zero-Shapley-node` holds for every
studied topology.

Null: zero-Shapley parties are always inside the core (disagreement and zero-value
membership are not the same event).

## Scope

Exact IIT-4.0 Φ on synthetic Boolean coordination forms. This is an in-silico study of
the two diagnostics on a pre-disclosed prior catalog of topologies, not a measurement of
any empirical organization. There is no claim of external validity for real groups; the
validation gap to field data is open.
