# q187 hypotheses

Setting: a takedown decision settled by three parties. The poster (P), the automated system
with memory (S), and the policy team (T). The poster's account and the system's account agree
on how the poster and the system behave and differ only on the policy team. The poster's
account places the policy team outside the coordination, a spectator that writes rules and
hears appeals but is not read at the moment of decision. The system's account folds the policy
team into the top of the integrated core, a principal coupled to the automated moderator.

## H1

The two accounts differ only at the policy-team node yet produce a nonzero core_jaccard gap
concentrated on that node, so a per-node core-divergence attribution names the policy team as
the disputed member.

H1-null: removing the policy-team rule difference (the matched control, with the policy team
external in both accounts) leaves the spread unchanged, so the disagreement is not attributable
to that node.

## H2

The oversight account (policy team in the core) has strictly higher Phi than the spectator
account (policy team out), giving a signed phi_gap above zero.

H2-null: the two accounts have equal Phi, so adding the policy team to the account changes core
membership without changing integration.

## Fixed before computing

Both hypotheses were stated before the probe ran. H1 requires the disputed set to be exactly
{T} in the treatment and empty in the matched control. H2 requires the signed phi_gap, oversight
minus spectator, to exceed zero.
