# Q133 findings — the destruction-vs-extraction split is symmetric in the agenda

Both hypotheses hold. Under the deny agenda the extracting baseline is XNOR, the mirror of XOR under approve.
A balanced mediator is re-integrated by the agenda whose minority output it overrides, and at full
re-integration (Φ = 2.0) the mediator captures two-thirds — extraction — exactly as in Q132 with the
baselines swapped.

| baseline | approve (Q132) | deny (Q133) |
|---|---|---|
| AND | destroy (2/3 → falls) | destroy (2/3 → falls) |
| OR | destroy | destroy |
| XNOR | destroy (0.5, flat) | **extract (0.5 → 2.0, 2/3)** |
| XOR | **extract (0.5 → 2.0, 2/3)** | destroy (0.5, flat) |

## Reading

Whether interested mediation destroys value or extracts it depends jointly on the faithful baseline and the
agenda, and the dependence is one relation seen from two sides: each agenda re-integrates the balanced
baseline whose rare output it removes, and re-integration is extraction. The political economy of interested
mediation is agenda-symmetric — the platform that extracts by approving on one mediation extracts by denying
on its mirror. This is the value image of Q127's collapse-versus-re-integrate symmetry.

## Limitations

Exact Φ on a three-node model; value read at the verdict's integrating state (Q132); Φ-to-money bridge open
(Q122). Small negative Shapley values at collapsed forms are non-monotonicity artifacts.
