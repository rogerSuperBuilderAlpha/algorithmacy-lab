# q194 — Hypotheses

## Question

The Algorithmacy Competence Scale scores three facets: counterpart inference (ACS-CI), signal
compression (ACS-SC), and rule-change tracking (ACS-RT). The survey_bridge map ties counterpart
inference to a hidden counterpart sitting behind a committing third party. The study asks whether an
exact-Φ coordination measure predicts the facets differentially, and whether a partition-restricted
component aimed at the worker-counterpart relation carries the counterpart facet specifically.

## H1 (fixed before computing)

Φ_coord predicts counterpart-inference (ACS-CI) more strongly than it predicts a non-targeted
covariate. The ACS-CI slope on Φ exceeds the general-self-efficacy (SE) slope, and the difference in
the Φ-to-construct correlation (ACS-CI vs SE) excludes 0.

Null: Φ_coord predicts ACS-CI no better than it predicts self-efficacy.

## H2 (fixed before computing)

A Φ component restricted to the W-C (counterpart) partition predicts ACS-CI above and beyond
whole-system Φ_coord. The incremental ΔR² exceeds 0 and its bootstrap CI excludes 0.

Null: the partition-restricted Φ adds no variance over whole-system Φ.

## Scope

The cohort is simulated. No worker is measured. The facets and the covariate are generated to the
survey_bridge facet-to-structure map, and the study tests whether the partition-restricted instrument
recovers the differential the map predicts. The result is evidence about the instrument and the
bridge on synthetic data, not a field finding.
