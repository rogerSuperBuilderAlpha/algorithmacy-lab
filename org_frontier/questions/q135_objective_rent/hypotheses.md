# Q135 — hypotheses (fixed before computing)

Q128 found a predatory mediator (S' = O) re-integrates the coordination when its objective O reads both
parties, the objective entering the core; Q129 found it displaces a party. Q135 asks the value question: when
the objective joins the core, does it capture value, and at whose expense?

- **H1.** When the objective adapts to both parties (joins the core), it captures positive value — a
  claimant, not a passive goal.
- **H2.** The objective captures as much as the system, and the human parties' shares are driven to near
  zero or below — the agenda displaces the parties in value as in the core.
- **Null.** The objective captures nothing; the value splits among the parties and the system.

Method: the Q128 forms (S' = O, objective frozen or adaptive: AND, OR, XOR); Shapley value of subsystem Φ at
the verdict's integrating state; the objective's, system's, and parties' shares. As recorded in
[`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md), H2 as stated was refuted — the objective always captures exactly what the
system does, but the parties' fate tracks displacement (negative under AND/OR, positive under XOR).
