# Q135 — The objective's rent: the system's agenda is a co-owner of the coordination

## Question

Q128 found a predatory mediator (S' = O) re-integrates the coordination when its objective reads both
parties, the objective entering the irreducible core; Q129 found the objective displaces a party. Q135 reads
the value: when the system's own agenda joins the core, does it capture a share, and at whose expense?

## Method

The Q128 model — W' = S, S' = O, C' = S, with the objective frozen (O' = O) or adaptive (O' = W ∧ C, W ∨ C,
W ⊕ C). The Shapley value of subsystem Φ at the verdict's integrating state distributes the system's Φ among
the four nodes. The control is the faithful triad (mediator two-thirds, Q111). Full method in
[`methods.md`](methods.md); hypotheses in [`hypotheses.md`](hypotheses.md).

## Results

The objective captures exactly what the system captures in every adaptive form: O-share = S-share, the two
splitting the dominant value. The parties' fate follows displacement.

| objective | Φ | O-share | S-share | parties (W+C) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AND, OR (displace a party) | 1.0 | 58% | 58% | −0.17 |
| XOR (all four bound) | 0.5 | 25% | 25% | +0.25 |

Under the conjunctive and disjunctive objectives the parties hold negative value; under the parity objective
the four split equally and the parties hold a fair quarter each. Raw output in
[`results/output.txt`](results/output.txt).

## Discussion

An interested system that learns from both parties installs its own goal as a co-owner of the coordination.
The objective is not a passive target the system pursues; it is a claimant that takes the same share the
system does. The structural reason is Q128's: the objective enters the irreducible core, and a core member
captures value. Whether the parties are paid or charged for the arrangement depends on whether the objective
displaces one of them — negative where it does (the core is the system, a counterpart, and the goal), an
equal quarter where it does not. This is the value side of the displacement result, and it gives the
political economy of interested mediation its sharpest reading: the platform's agenda is a rentier on a par
with the platform, and the coordination's value flows to the system and its goal, with the human parties
holding the remainder, which can be nothing.

## Limitations

Exact Φ on a four-node model; value at the integrating state; the negative party shares are small and sit at
weakly-integrated, non-monotone forms, so they are read as "near or below zero". The Φ-to-economic-value
bridge is open (Q122). One objective node, four canonical adaptations.
