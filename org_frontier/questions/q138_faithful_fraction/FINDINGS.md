# Q138 findings — reviving the coordination takes three-quarters faithfulness, and the rent is the last quarter

Both hypotheses hold. The coordination has a sharp faithfulness threshold: below three of four states
committed faithfully, it is dead. At the threshold the value splits evenly, the parties holding the majority;
the mediator's two-thirds returns only with the last faithful state.

| faithful states | Φ | mediator share | parties (collective) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0/4 – 2/4 | 0.000 | — | — |
| 3/4 | 0.500 | 33% | 67% |
| 4/4 | 2.000 | 67% | 33% |

| H | Result |
|---|---|
| H1 (minimum faithful fraction to revive = 3/4) | confirmed |
| H2 (threshold gives egalitarian split; mediator dominance only at full faith) | confirmed (3/4: mediator 33%; 4/4: mediator 67%) |

## Reading

Regulation that forces faithful commitment has a threshold and a distributional twist. The threshold is
three-quarters: a mediator committing the joint determination on fewer than three of its four states sustains
no irreducible coordination, and the value is zero. Crossing the threshold to three-quarters revives the
coordination, but at the weakly-integrated value (Φ = 0.5) where the Shapley split is even — each of the three
parties takes a third, so the two human parties hold two-thirds of the value and the mediator one-third. The
mediator's familiar two-thirds appears only at full faithfulness, when the last state is also forced to commit
and the value jumps to 2.0.

So the platform's rent is the fully-faithful commit, not faithfulness in general. Light-touch regulation that
forces just enough faithfulness to revive the coordination leaves the parties with the majority of the value;
it is the final increment, to complete faithfulness, that hands the mediator its dominant share. The
distributional reading inverts the naive one: the most faithful mediator is the most richly rewarded, and a
partially-regulated mediator that merely keeps the coordination alive is the one that takes the least.

## Limitations

Exact Φ on the three-node triad with the AND faithful commit and a constant predatory default; the threshold
and the egalitarian split at it depend on the forcing order (highest-warrant-first here) and on the baseline.
Value read at the integrating state; the egalitarian split at 3/4 is the Q131 equalization of a Φ = 0.5 form.
The Φ-to-money bridge is open (Q122).
