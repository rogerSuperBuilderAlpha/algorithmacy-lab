# Q138 — review of prior work

Q138 inverts the Q126 interestedness ladder ([`q126_interested_mediator`](../q126_interested_mediator/paper.md))
into a regulatory frame and reads the value with the Q131/Q132 Shapley function. The three-quarters threshold
is the Q126 collapse point seen from the other side (the mediator needs to commit faithfully on three of four
states), and the egalitarian split at the threshold is the Q131 equalization of a Φ = 0.5 form. The novel
content is the distributional reading: that the mediator's two-thirds rent is the fully-faithful commit, so
minimum-viable regulation revives the coordination without restoring the platform's dominance — full
faithfulness does. It connects to the political-economy synthesis
([`essays/political_economy_of_interested_mediation.md`](../../essays/political_economy_of_interested_mediation.md))
by adding the regulator as an actor that can sit the form at the egalitarian threshold.
