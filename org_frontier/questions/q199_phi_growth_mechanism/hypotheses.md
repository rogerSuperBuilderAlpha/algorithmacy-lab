# q199 — hypotheses

Question: across waves of the simulated panel, does within-person change in Φ_coord track within-person
change in algorithmacy? The baseline studies show Φ_coord correlates with ACS-total in a cross-section.
This study asks whether the bridge moves together with the construct within a person, not just at baseline.

H1: within-person wave-to-wave change in Φ_coord positively covaries with within-person change in
ACS-total. In a multilevel model with person-mean-centered Φ_coord, the within-person slope of ACS on
the within-person Φ deviation is greater than 0 with a 95% CI that excludes 0.
Null: within-person ΔΦ and ΔACS are uncorrelated (the within slope CI includes 0).

H2: the within-person Φ-ACS coupling exceeds the between-person coupling. The within-person slope is
larger than the between-person slope, and the 95% CI on Δ = within − between excludes 0, so the effect is
a person-level dynamic rather than stable selection.
Null: the within- and between-person Φ-ACS slopes are equal (Δ CI includes 0).

The between-person Φ term is the comparison slope for H2. A forced-dyadic control panel (Φ_coord ≡ 0 by
construction) is reported as a check that both slopes collapse when the form is rewired to factorize.
