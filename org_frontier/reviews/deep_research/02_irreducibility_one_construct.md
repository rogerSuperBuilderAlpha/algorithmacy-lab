# Irreducibility as one construct, measured several ways — deep-research dossier

*Deep-research pass (fan-out search → adversarial verification → synthesis) on concept 2 of [`../CONCEPTS_TO_RESEARCH.md`](../CONCEPTS_TO_RESEARCH.md). Question: is there a unifying formal account of irreducibility spanning integrated information, causal emergence, partial-information decomposition (synergy), and collective intelligence — and on what grounds prefer exact IIT-4.0 Φ for organizational coordination? 102 agents; claims carry a vote and sources.*

## Bottom line

Yes — a single formal account of irreducibility now spans these communities, and it runs through information decomposition. Partial Information Decomposition (Williams & Beer 2010) splits what several sources tell about a target into redundant, unique, and synergistic atoms, and its multi-target extension, Integrated Information Decomposition (ΦID, Mediano/Rosas), decomposes information over time. Three ostensibly separate ideas collapse into one condition inside this lattice: synergy ("the whole computes what no part holds"), causal emergence (Rosas et al. 2020: a system has causally emergent features of order k iff k-th-order dynamical synergy is strictly positive), and integrated information (empirical whole-minus-sum Φ is literally a fixed sum of ΦID atoms — seven synergy, two transfer, one negatively-signed redundancy). So synergy, causal emergence, and non-decomposability are provably the same mathematical object at the information-theoretic level. The principled grounds for preferring exact IIT-4.0 Φ do NOT come from this literature showing Φ is decorative; on the contrary, the decomposition family is itself fractured (Rauh et al.'s impossibility result: for >2 sources no redundancy measure keeps the identity axiom, the W&B axioms, and non-negative atoms; ~19 competing measures with "no principled guidance for preferring one over the others" because there is no agreed operational interpretation of synergy), and the ΦID results decompose the information-theoretic Φ (Balduzzi-Tononi/Barrett-Seth lineage), NOT the axiomatic cause-effect-structure Φ of exact IIT-4.0 — leaving exact Φ as the one member of the family with a unique, non-arbitrary decomposition procedure grounded in intervention rather than a contested choice of redundancy function.

## Verified findings

### 1. Partial Information Decomposition (Williams & Beer 2010) is the foundational formalism: it splits the mutual information that multiple sources carry about a target into non-negative, additively-summing atoms of redundant, unique, and synergistic information — for two sources exactly R + U(S1) + U(S2) + C — and thereby isolates synergy ('the whole computes what no part holds') as a distinct measurable quantity, explaining that classical interaction information goes negative because it conflates redundancy and synergy.
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0 (five merged claims all unanimous)*

Primary source arXiv:1004.2515 ('Nonnegative Decomposition of Multivariate Information'): atoms 'are never negative and always support a clear interpretation as informational quantities,' and 'the negativity of interaction information can be explained by its confounding of redundancy and synergy.' The bivariate four-term identity I({S1,S2};T)=R+U(S1∖S2)+U(S2∖S1)+C is verbatim in PMC7512824. Synergy defined operationally (PMC11276306) as 'information that the joint vector (X1,X2) has about X3 that cannot be obtained by observing X1 and X2 separately.' Caveat: non-negativity is proven for W&B's specific I_min measure, not for PID in general; for 2 sources there are four atoms grouped into three conceptual types.

- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7512824/
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S027826261530021X
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11276306/

### 2. PID is a single domain-general framework that unifies previously disconnected proposals into one comparison space — e.g. predictive coding, infomax, coherent infomax, and efficient coding are all expressible and comparable as PID goal functions.
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0*

Wibral, Priesemann, Kay, Lizier & Phillips 2017 (Brain and Cognition; preprint arXiv:1510.00831), 'Partial Information Decomposition as a Unified Approach to the Specification of Neural Goal Functions': 'PID allows to compare these goal functions in a common framework, and also provides a versatile approach to design new goal functions from first principles.' The paper's title and its 'coding with synergy' example back the unification claim. 'Domain-independent' is a mild extrapolation (the paper's application is neuroscience) but PID is genuinely domain-general mathematics.
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S027826261530021X

### 3. Causal emergence and synergy are the same mathematical condition: Rosas, Mediano et al. (2020) build an information-theoretic account on ΦID in which a macro/supervenient feature is causally emergent when it carries synergistic predictive information about the system's own future that no part holds alone, and prove (Theorem 1) that a system has causally emergent features of order k if and only if its k-th-order dynamical synergy is strictly positive.
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0 (four merged claims all unanimous)*

Rosas et al. 2020 'Reconciling emergences' (PLoS Comput Biol 16(12):e1008289): 'A macroscopic feature V ... is causally emergent if it contains some information about the future of X that no microscopic element of X has on its own,' and emergence capacity is 'determined by how synergistic its elements are with respect to their future evolution.' The 2022 review 'Greater than the parts' (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A; arXiv:2111.06518) states the iff verbatim: 'a system Xt possesses causally emergent features of order k if and only if Syn^(k)(Xt;Xt′) > 0 [Theorem 1].' SCOPING CAVEAT: this is causal emergence as defined WITHIN the ΦID framework; the iff holds under 'relatively general assumptions' and 'depends on a choice of redundancy function.' It is NOT asserted to be identical to Hoel's effective-information/coarse-graining causal emergence.
- https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pcbi.1008289
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356197938_Greater_than_the_parts_A_review_of_the_information_decomposition_approach_to_causal_emergence

### 4. There is a second, rival operationalization of causal emergence — Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi's effective information (EI) — and the synergy/ΦID camp explicitly positions itself as an alternative to it, on the ground that EI needs exact microscopic transition knowledge unavailable in most applications.
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0*

Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi 2013 (PNAS 1314922110): EI perturbs the system into all states with equal probability (maximum-entropy interventional distribution) and takes the KL divergence between the constrained (post-intervention) and unconstrained transition distributions; EI = log2(n)·(det − deg), capturing selectivity, determinism, and degeneracy relative to state-space size. Rosas et al. 2020 position their PID/ΦID account against it: EI 'relies on having exact knowledge about the microscopic transitions ... which is not possible to obtain in most applications,' and EI uses maximum-entropy distributions (what a system COULD do, including transitions 'never actually explored') versus their stationary distributions (what it actually does). This is the key rivalry between measure-families of irreducibility: interventional/max-entropy (EI, and by lineage exact IIT Φ) versus observational/stationary (ΦID synergy).
- https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1314922110
- https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pcbi.1008289

### 5. Integrated Information Decomposition (ΦID) is the formal bridge that places IIT's Φ inside the same lattice as synergy and causal emergence: it is a multi-target extension of PID that decomposes information over time into redundant/unique/synergistic atoms, and the empirical whole-minus-sum Φ is literally a fixed sum of 10 of ΦID's 16 atoms — seven synergy, two directional transfer, and one negatively-signed redundancy — which both formally links Φ to Williams-Beer PID and explains why Φ goes negative (it over-subtracts redundancy).
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0 (seven merged claims all unanimous)*

Mediano, Rosas et al. 2019 (arXiv:1909.02297): 'we combine concepts of partial information decomposition and integrated information, and obtain what we call Integrated Information Decomposition, or ΦID,' and 'ΦID reveals that what is typically referred to as integration is actually an aggregate of several heterogeneous phenomena.' Luppi et al. 2021 (Neuroscience of Consciousness niab027): 'ΦID decomposes the TDMI ... into 16 disjoint atoms ... 10 are represented in ΦWMS: seven ... synergistic ... two ... transferred ... and, importantly, one redundancy atom with a negative sign' (7+2+1=10). eLife 88173 (Luppi et al. 2024): Φ 'contains all the synergistic information ... the unique information transferred ... and, importantly, the subtraction of redundancy,' with corrected measure ΦR = Φ + Red(X,Y). CRITICAL SCOPING CAVEAT (do not overreach): the decomposed Φ is the information-theoretic whole-minus-sum Φ (Balduzzi-Tononi 2008 / Barrett-Seth 2011 lineage), NOT the axiomatic cause-effect-structure Φ of exact IIT-4.0. 'IIT's Phi' is accurate at the information-theoretic level the sources establish; it must NOT be silently upgraded to claim exact IIT-4.0 Φ decomposes into PID atoms.
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/335651481_Beyond_integrated_information_A_taxonomy_of_information_dynamics_phenomena
- https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2021/2/niab027/6429334
- https://elifesciences.org/articles/88173
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356197938_Greater_than_the_parts_A_review_of_the_information_decomposition_approach_to_causal_emergence

### 6. The synergy/decomposition measure-family is itself internally fractured, which is the affirmative ground for preferring a principled operationalization of irreducibility rather than an ad hoc synergy measure: Rauh et al. (2014) proved a formal impossibility (for >2 sources no redundancy measure jointly satisfies the identity property, the W&B axioms, and non-negativity), and the field carries ~19 competing decompositions with 'no precise guidance for preferring one over the others' because there is no agreed operational interpretation of synergy itself.
*confidence **high** · vote 3-0 (four merged claims all unanimous)*

PMC7512824 (Lizier/Bertschinger/Jost/Wibral 2018 Entropy editorial): information decomposition 'has proven far more difficult to solve than may have been expected'; and Rauh et al. (ISIT 2014, arXiv:1404.3146) 'demonstrated that no redundancy measure can satisfy the identity property along with the original axioms of Williams and Beer and still provide non-negative partial information atoms when we have more than two source variables.' Rosas 2020 (operational, arXiv:2001.10387, J. Phys. A 53): 'the adoption of these approaches has been hindered by there being multiple possible decompositions, and no precise guidance for preferring one over the others. At the heart of this disagreement lies the absence of a clear operational interpretation of what synergistic information is.' 2025-2026 reviews (arXiv:2508.05530, arXiv:2603.06678) document ~19 proposals, no consensus. This is the metascience 'six competing measure-families that don't cite each other' finding, corroborated: the fragmentation lives inside the synergy family, giving grounds to prefer an operationalization (exact IIT Φ) whose decomposition procedure is fixed by intervention rather than by a contested choice of redundancy function.
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7512824/
- https://pmediano.gitlab.io/publication/rosas-2020-operational/

### 7. Synergy is the operational signature of genuine irreducibility — information available only when parts are considered jointly and not driven by common input — which is the conceptual reason all these accounts converge, and which motivates (rather than demotes) a principled irreducibility lens for organizational coordination.
*confidence **medium** · vote 2-1 (one dissent; core qualitative claim well-supported)*

eLife 88173 (Luppi, Mediano, Rosas et al. 2024): 'synergy reflects the joint contribution of parts of the system to the whole, that is not driven by common input,' consistently distinguishing synergy (complementary, requires parts jointly) from redundancy (duplicated). Corroborated by arXiv:1311.7442 'Irreducibility is Minimum Synergy Among Parts.' The split vote and the source's own hedge ('can also be viewed as') reflect that synergy per PID is not identical to IIT-4.0 Φ and rests on a non-unique redundancy definition — this qualifies the operationalization but does not refute the qualitative convergence.
- https://elifesciences.org/articles/88173
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2515

## Caveats

Time-sensitivity: the field is active; 2024-2026 reviews (arXiv:2508.05530, arXiv:2603.06678, arXiv:2512.16662) keep documenting new PID measures and inconsistencies, so 'no consensus measure' is current as of 2026 but the specific count (~19) will drift. Source quality is strong throughout — nearly every claim rests on a primary, peer-reviewed source, most authored by the exact researchers named in the question (Williams & Beer, Rosas, Mediano, Hoel/Albantakis/Tononi, Luppi), with unanimous 3-0 verification on 24 of 25 claims. The single load-bearing limitation, flagged on multiple claims and essential to the dossier's honesty: every ΦID result that 'decomposes Φ into PID atoms' decomposes the INFORMATION-THEORETIC whole-minus-sum Φ (Balduzzi-Tononi 2008 / Barrett-Seth 2011 lineage), NOT the axiomatic cause-effect-structure Φ of exact IIT-4.0. The dossier must not silently equate them — doing so would both overstate the unification AND undercut the affirmative case, since exact IIT-4.0 Φ's distinctness from the decomposable empirical Φ is part of why it stands as a principled, non-arbitrary operationalization. Likewise, ΦID-based causal emergence (order-k synergy) is not shown identical to Hoel's effective-information causal emergence; they are two rival operationalizations, not one. Nothing in the verified evidence demotes Φ: the decomposition literature exposes fragmentation WITHIN the synergy family, which strengthens rather than weakens the case for a principled irreducibility lens.

## Open questions

- Does exact IIT-4.0 Φ (axiomatic cause-effect-structure, intrinsic-difference distance) admit any ΦID-style decomposition, or is its resistance to decomposition itself the principled ground for preferring it? The verified sources only decompose the empirical whole-minus-sum Φ, leaving the IIT-4.0 case open.
- Herbert Simon 1962 'The Architecture of Complexity' (near-decomposability) and Woolley et al. 2010 (the collective-intelligence c-factor) were named in the research question but produced NO surviving verified claims — the connection between organizational near-decomposability / collective intelligence and the information-decomposition account of irreducibility remains unestablished by this dossier and needs its own sourcing.
- On what independent criterion would one adjudicate between the interventional/max-entropy family (EI, exact IIT Φ — 'what the system could do') and the observational/stationary family (ΦID synergy — 'what the system actually does') for ORGANIZATIONAL coordination, where interventions on a real organization are typically infeasible? Rosas et al.'s critique that EI needs unavailable microscopic transitions cuts directly against applying interventional Φ to field organizations and must be answered.
- Given the proven multiplicity of redundancy measures (Rauh impossibility; ~19 proposals), which specific redundancy/synergy definition, if any, best matches the organizational-coordination construct — and does the affirmative case for exact Φ rest precisely on it NOT requiring such a contested choice?

## What this gives the dissertation

The affirmative case for exact Φ gets sharper, not weaker, from the family being fractured. Synergy, causal emergence, and integrated information are provably one object at the information-theoretic level (causal emergence of order *k* holds iff *k*-th-order dynamical synergy is positive; empirical whole-minus-sum Φ is a fixed sum of ΦID atoms). But the decomposition family cannot agree on a redundancy function — Rauh et al.'s impossibility result shows no measure keeps the core axioms with non-negative atoms beyond two sources, and roughly nineteen rival measures compete with no principled way to choose among them. The ΦID results moreover decompose the *information-theoretic* Φ (Balduzzi–Tononi, Barrett–Seth), not the axiomatic cause-effect-structure Φ of exact IIT-4.0. Exact Φ is therefore the one member of the irreducibility family whose decomposition is fixed by intervention and the minimum-information partition rather than by a contested choice of redundancy function. That is the principled ground Paper 2 needs: not that Φ is the only measure of irreducibility, but that among a genuinely unified yet unconverged family it is the member with a non-arbitrary operational procedure. Feeds Paper 2 (the affirmative case for the tool) and the `phi_measure_fragmentation` review (its six-family finding is this fracture seen from the literature side).
