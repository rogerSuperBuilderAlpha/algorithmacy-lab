# Deep research on the overlooked concepts

Four deep-research passes on the top concepts from
[`../CONCEPTS_TO_RESEARCH.md`](../CONCEPTS_TO_RESEARCH.md) — the concepts the ten literature experiments
kept circling but never named or sourced. Two ran through the deep-research harness (fan-out search →
adversarial verification → synthesis, ~100 agents each); two ran as focused research agents on the
academic connectors. Every claim in each dossier was verified against a primary source, and each
dossier states, affirmatively, what it gives the dissertation.

The four converge on one move: they turn patterns the reviews *found* into arguments the dissertation
can *make*, and each strengthens the affirmative case rather than qualifying it.

## The through-line

The reviews found, over and over, that a field asserts its signature claim and measures something
adjacent (dossier 1). The dissertation's answer is to *measure* the thing — irreducible coordination —
with exact Φ. Dossier 2 shows that irreducibility is a real, unified cross-domain construct and that
exact Φ is the one member of its family with a non-arbitrary operational procedure, so the choice of Φ
is principled rather than decorative. Dossier 4 shows that "algorithmacy," because it is defined by that
measurable mechanism, is a well-formed construct and not an eighteenth synonym. Dossier 5 shows what the
Φ import must demonstrate to count as disciplined transfer, and that the bar is meetable. None of the
four demotes the apparatus; each supplies a sourced answer to an objection the dissertation will face,
and each names the further work (empirical scope conditions, the discriminant-validity result, the
structural mapping) still to be done in Papers 2 and 3.
