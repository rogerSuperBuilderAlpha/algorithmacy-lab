# Hypotheses — integrated information beyond consciousness

*Committed before any corpus is harvested or coded. The question: has integrated information theory
(IIT / Φ) been applied beyond consciousness — to organizations, teams, economies, markets, and
collective/social systems — and what does that literature claim? Each hypothesis names the knowledge
claim it tests (in the knowledge-weaving typology), its operationalization, and the outcome that would
support versus challenge it.*

This review matters to the lab directly: the lab's program applies exact IIT-4.0 Φ to organizational
coordination. If that application is a small, disconnected, mostly-conceptual literature, the lab is
filling a real gap; if it is large and empirically mature, the lab is entering a crowded field. The
review measures which.

## H1 — Small and recent
- **Knowledge claim (stylized fact):** applying IIT beyond neural/consciousness substrates is a nascent
  activity, not an established literature.
- **Operationalization:** corpus size after boundary application; year distribution.
- **Predicts:** a corpus in the tens, not hundreds, concentrated after 2015 (IIT 3.0 was 2014).
- **Challenged if:** a large corpus (hundreds) or an even spread reaching well before 2010.

## H2 — Disconnected from organization theory
- **Knowledge claim (substantive omission):** the IIT-beyond-consciousness literature and organization
  / coordination theory do not cite each other; the bridge the lab builds is genuinely unbuilt.
- **Operationalization:** cross-citation between the IIT-applied corpus and a fixed coordination-canon
  reference set (Thompson 1967, Williamson 1975/1991, Powell 1990, Galbraith 1973, Malone & Crowston
  1994, Puranam et al. 2014), measured on the harvested citation graph.
- **Predicts:** near-zero cross-citation in both directions.
- **Challenged if:** a substantial share of the IIT-applied corpus cites the coordination canon (or is
  cited by it).

## H3 — Asserted, not measured
- **Knowledge claim (key assumption):** that Φ indexes organizational or collective integration is
  taken as motivating rather than demonstrated; most work is conceptual or analogical, not a
  computation of Φ on real social/organizational data.
- **Operationalization:** code each source `conceptual` (analogy / argument), `formal_model`
  (Φ computed on a toy or simulated system), or `empirical` (Φ, or a declared proxy, computed on real
  organizational / social / economic data).
- **Predicts:** `empirical` is a small minority; `conceptual` dominates.
- **Challenged if:** `empirical` is a plurality or more.

## H4 — Clustered by substrate
- **Knowledge claim (enduring critique):** applications fragment by substrate — neural-analogy,
  swarm/collective-behavior, socioeconomic/organizational, philosophical — with little cross-talk.
- **Operationalization:** cluster-to-cluster citation matrix over the substrate clusters, on the
  harvested graph.
- **Predicts:** a block-diagonal matrix (within-cluster ≫ cross-cluster citation).
- **Challenged if:** the matrix is well-connected across substrates.

## Method fixed in advance
- Corpus boundary and search: `methods.md` (substantive + procedural gates; seed + snowball harvest).
- Coders: three independent agents, blind to one another, on `coding_protocol.md`.
- Reliability reported (Fleiss' κ). Any hypothesis the data contradict is reported as challenged.
