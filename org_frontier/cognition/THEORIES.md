# Five theories, deepened: the experiment batteries

The single mappings in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md) showed that each cognitive theory's failure point has a
computable Φ counterpart. This takes each several experiments further and characterizes it. The numbers
reproduce from [`theory_batteries.py`](theory_batteries.py).

## 1. Computationalism — the channel and the actor

The information-processing view casts the algorithm as a channel. The battery shows the channel is one
corner of a space the apparatus reads in full.

A system that reads its own objective with strength a crosses from channel to actor continuously: Φ rises
from zero at a pure relay (a = 0) through 0.11, 0.28, 0.54 to 2.0 at full reading. There is no sharp line
between carrying and acting; the algorithm becomes a third party by degrees. No refinement of a channel
reaches it. Every system whose next state is a function of the worker alone, S = f(W), carries Φ of zero,
for all four such functions, so a channel model cannot be tuned into an actor by adjusting its function.
And reading is not yet acting: a system that reads both parties but stores their inputs for one to decide
carries Φ of zero, while one that reads both and commits carries 2.0. The actor is the system that reads
its objective and commits a determination, and that is the form the channel model has nowhere to put.

## 2. Direct perception — the rule is not in the light

Ecological perception has the affordance present in what the animal sees. The battery shows the rule the
worker needs is not there, and locates the ceiling of what she can infer.

The rule is recoverable from the outcomes a worker can observe at an AUC of 0.67, a little above chance,
which is the perceivable fraction; the rest she must reconstruct. The reconstruction works on what she can
see and stops where the hidden counterpart begins. A worker who could observe both inputs recovers the
determination exactly. A worker who sees only her own input and the outcome, with the counterpart hidden
behind the system, learns only the worker-marginal of the rule, 0.77 of it, and the part that turns on the
counterpart stays out of reach. The inferential core the paper names is real, the skill of guessing the
rule from outcomes is effective, and its ceiling is set by the signal asymmetry: what the system reads of
the other party, the worker never sees.

## 3. Embodiment — intent compression

Meaning that once rode on a body must compress into the system's input. The battery shows the curve and
what falls outside the boxes.

Co-monotone meaning degrades gracefully as the read fidelity drops, the binding falling from 2.0 at full
fidelity to 1.24 at 0.75 and 0.66 at 0.5: the system carries the worker's intent in proportion to how
faithfully it reads her. What it does not read is left behind entirely. Give the worker a nuance, a bit of
her intent beyond the fields the system takes, and the outcome depends on whether the system reads it. A
system that reads the nuance carries it into the bound whole; a system blind to it leaves the nuance
outside the core, decoupled from the coordination. Whatever the worker would have carried in her body has
to fit the boxes or drop out of the whole, which is intent compression read as membership.

## 4. Theory of mind — the phantom addressee

The worker addresses the interface as if a counterpart sat behind it. The battery characterizes the
phantom precisely.

The worker binds to the held position: she and the system sit in the core, and the real counterpart is
outside it. The address is one-way in a particular sense. The worker reads the position and the position
takes up her input, so something receives her social act, but the actual counterpart never reads the
worker, so the act never reaches the person it was for. The counterpart is causally read by the system and
is still not a member of the bound whole, the causal-versus-communicative gap: the system processes her,
the worker reaches her through nothing, and she cannot answer as a counterpart because she is not in the
coordination. The worker can reach her only through the system, and no direct channel between them binds.
The phantom solicits a full social act and routes it to a party with its own objectives, while the person
the act was meant for stays out of reach.

## 5. The extended mind — capture and governance

The platform is a part of the worker's cognition, and it works for someone else. The battery finds how
little capture it takes to displace her.

As the platform's interest supplants the worker's input to the system, she loses her place in the core at
a strikingly low threshold. The core holds worker, system, counterpart only while the worker's input is
the whole determination. By the time the platform supplies a tenth of it, the worker is already out and
the core has contracted to the system and the counterpart, and the coordination runs on irreducibly
without her. Governance is not a gradient she slides down; it is a seat she keeps only while her input is
the larger share of what the system commits, and loses early once the platform's objective enters the
determination. The worker can be reasoning all day through a system that no longer binds her into the whole
it runs.

## What the batteries establish

The cognitive theories fail because they model a channel, and the channel is the zero-Φ corner the
apparatus reads in full. Pushed past the single mappings, each theory's failure becomes a characterized
quantity: the channel-to-actor transition is continuous and a channel cannot be refined into an actor; the
rule is perceivable only in fraction and inference is capped by the hidden counterpart; intent degrades
with the fidelity of the read and the unread nuance is left out of the whole; the worker's social act binds
her to the held position while the counterpart stays a referent outside; and the worker's governance is a
seat she loses at a low threshold of platform capture. The phenomenal content of the paper's account stays
the paper's; the structure is here, measured.
