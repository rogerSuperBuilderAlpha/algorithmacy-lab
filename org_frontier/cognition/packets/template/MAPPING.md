# Mapping template — one theory of mind to the apparatus

Fill this in as you map a theory, and commit it beside `map_theory.py` once the models are written. Each
section of [`THEORIES.md`](../../THEORIES.md) is this template filled for one of the six worked theories.
Delete the guidance in brackets as you go.

## The theory

[Name the account of mind and its canonical source. State what it takes coordination to be: who the sender
and receiver are, what medium it places between them, and the work that medium is assumed to do. Keep it to
the structural commitments — the claims about what is in the system and what reads what — and leave the
phenomenal content to the theory's own literature.]

## The channel assumption

[Every two-party theory models the medium as a channel: a pipe indifferent to what it carries. State the
channel reading of this theory explicitly. What does it assume the third party does — relay a signal, hold a
symbol, carry meaning between the parties? This is the reading that will factor and give Φ = 0.]

## The failure point

[Name the structural fact about an opaque, interested third party the theory cannot represent. The six
worked mappings each found one: the system reads its own objective (computationalism), the rule is not in
the outcomes the worker sees (direct perception), the intent compresses into a narrow input (embodiment),
the counterpart is a referent the worker never addresses (theory of mind), the platform's interest supplants
the worker's input (the extended mind), the process retrains underneath the worker (predictive processing).
State this theory's.]

## The two models

[Write the channel reading and the committing reading as Boolean determination rules over the parties, the
form `map_theory.py` runs. State each rule and mark it as drawn from the theory's commitments versus a
modeling choice. Name the thread under [`../../../threads/`](../../../threads/) whose law the formalization
draws on.]

- Parties: [label each — worker, system, counterpart, and any added node]
- Channel model: [the rules under which the third party only carries a signal; predicted to factor, Φ = 0]
- Committing model: [the rules under which the third party reads the parties and commits a determination of
  its own; predicted irreducible, Φ > 0, the third party in the major complex]

## The predicted verdict

[State the verdict before running: the irreducible contribution the channel omits (committing Φ minus
channel Φ), and which parties the major complex names. What would refute the mapping — the channel model
giving Φ > 0, or the committing model factoring, or the third party falling outside the core.]

## Deepening into a battery

[The single verdict is the start. State which deepening this mapping calls for and where it runs: the binding

theory took one or more of these further in [`theory_batteries.py`](../../theory_batteries.py).]
