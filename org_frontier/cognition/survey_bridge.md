# The survey bridge: formal predictions for the Algorithmacy Competence Scale

The [survey arm](../survey/) measures algorithmacy as a lived competence with a three-facet scale:
counterpart inference, signal compression, and rule-change tracking. The cognition arm's experiments
characterize the structure a worker high in each facet is operating in. The two meet cleanly, because the
scale's three facets are the three places the cognitive theories break, and the batteries say what makes
each hard. This document maps facet to formal result and sharpens the survey's hypotheses into predictions
the formal work stands behind.

A note on what the bridge is. The formal results are about structure, and the scale measures self-reported
skill. The link is that the skill each facet names is the skill of operating in the structure a battery
characterizes, so the difficulty the battery measures is the difficulty the competent worker overcomes.
The bridge does not make the survey's findings follow from the models; it says which formal quantity each
facet is the skill against, and which survey associations the formal account predicts.

## Facet by facet

**Counterpart inference — reconstructing a hidden counterpart's wants from outcomes.** The formal
counterpart is the [direct-perception battery](THEORIES.md) and the
[opacity floor](PREDICTIVE_PROCESSING.md). The rule is recoverable from outcomes at only AUC 0.67, and a
worker who cannot see the counterpart learns only the worker-marginal of the rule, 0.77 of it, facing half
a bit of surprise no model removes. Counterpart inference is the skill against exactly this gap. The
prediction: the facet should matter most where the third party commits and the counterpart is hidden
behind it, and least where the system merely conveys, since a channel hides no counterpart to infer. This
is the formal reading of the survey's hypothesis that algorithmacy competence tracks perceived system
authority in the committing direction.

**Signal compression — pouring intent into the system's vocabulary.** The formal counterpart is the
[embodiment battery](THEORIES.md). The worker's intent is carried into the bound whole in proportion to
how faithfully the system reads her, and a nuance the system does not read is left outside the whole
entirely. Signal compression is the skill of choosing, among admissible inputs, the ones the system reads
co-monotonically into the determination she wants, and of keeping count of what spills. The prediction:
the facet should matter more the narrower the system's input, where more of the worker's meaning must
compress, and a worker high in it loses less of her intent to the boxes.

**Rule-change tracking — following rule changes the system makes without announcement.** The formal
counterpart is the [moving target](PREDICTIVE_PROCESSING.md). As the system retrains, the worker's model
lags, her prediction accuracy falling from ceiling to 0.65 as the rule drifts faster, and the drift thins
the binding as well. Rule-change tracking is the skill of re-fitting the model against the moving rule.
The prediction: the facet should matter most on platforms that retrain often, and a worker high in it
holds prediction accuracy that a worker low in it loses as the rule drifts.

## The cross-cutting prediction

Underneath the three facets is one formal claim the survey can test. Algorithmacy is the competence a
committing arrangement demands, where the third party is a constitutive member, and a conveying
arrangement, a channel, demands only the older literacy. So the whole scale should be associated with
perceived system authority in the committing direction and weak where workers experience the system as a
conduit. The survey's hypothesis to that effect is the empirical test of the
[mediated-or-irreducible](../essays/mediated_or_irreducible.md) thesis: that the line between literacy and
algorithmacy is the line between convey and commit. A null there, algorithmacy unrelated to perceived
commitment, would tell against the formal account, and the survey is where that check can be run on real
workers.

The governance result connects too. A worker displaced from the irreducible core, captured early as the
platform's interest enters the determination, is a worker whose autonomy and ownership over her own
coordination have been taken structurally. The survey's autonomy, psychological-ownership, and perceived-
substitutability measures are where that displacement would show as something a worker reports, and the
capture threshold predicts that the erosion sets in well before the platform's interest dominates.

## What this gives the survey

Three things. A reason each facet is a distinct skill rather than one undifferentiated competence: each
is the skill against a different structural difficulty, which is the formal case for the scale's
three-factor structure. A set of moderation predictions: counterpart inference should matter more under
commitment, signal compression under narrower input, rule-change tracking under faster retraining. And a
construct anchor: algorithmacy is not general self-efficacy because it is the competence against a
specific structure, the constitutive third party, that the formal apparatus can hold and a channel theory
cannot. The survey measures the competence; the cognition arm says what the competence is the competence
at; the field study where the two are read together is the standing open step the
[empirical ask](../../README.md) names.
