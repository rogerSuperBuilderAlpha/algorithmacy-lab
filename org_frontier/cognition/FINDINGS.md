# Five theories, five findings

Each cognitive theory in Hunt's [paper](coordinating_through_the_opaque_third.md) fails to represent
coordination through an opaque, interested third party, and each failure is a claim about structure with
a computable counterpart in exact Φ. The five experiments in [`five_theories.py`](five_theories.py) test
those counterparts; the numbers reproduce from that script.

## E1 — Computationalism: the channel is the zero-Φ special case

The information-processing view casts the algorithm as a channel that carries symbols and leaves the
content alone. A pure relay, S = W, where the system only passes the worker's signal along, carries Φ of
zero, and its core is the worker alone: the system adds nothing, which is the channel the theory assumes.
A system that reads its own objective, S = W ∧ C, carries Φ of 2.0 with all three parties in the core.
The difference, an irreducible contribution of 2.0, is exactly what the channel model has nowhere to put.
The computationalist account is right about the channel and blind to everything that makes an algorithm
not one. The variance it reads as noise, two equal workers with opposite outcomes, is the irreducible
third doing its work.

## E2 — Direct perception: the rule is not in the light

Ecological perception has the affordance lawfully present in what the animal sees. Here the worker sees
outcomes and needs the rule, the determination the system runs. Across random systems, the best
behavioral read of the rule from the outcomes a worker can observe reaches an AUC of 0.67, a little above
chance and far from perceivable. The rule is taken out of the light: a worker watching only what the
system returns cannot read off the determination that produced it, and must reconstruct it by inference,
which is the inferential core the paper names and the skill that separates one worker from another.

## E3 — Embodiment: intent compresses, and the binding sheds

Meaning that once rode on a body and a shared room must compress into the system's narrow input. Reading
the worker's intent at fidelity q, the binding falls fast: full fidelity holds Φ at 2.0, but a fidelity
of 0.75 already sheds it to 0.71, a 0.5 to 0.44, a 0.25 to 0.20, and zero to zero. The curve is convex,
so even a mild compression of the worker's intent strips most of the coordination's irreducibility. The
distance between what she means and what the system will take is not a small tax; it is where the binding
goes when the body's meaning will not fit the boxes.

## E4 — Theory of mind: the worker binds to the held position

The worker addresses the interface as if a counterpart sat behind it, and the mind-reading machinery
hands her an addressee. In a system where the worker addresses the mediator, the mediator reads both the
worker and the counterpart, and the real counterpart couples back to no one, the worker's binding lands
on the mediator: the worker and the held position sit together in the core, and the real counterpart,
read by the system as a referent, is outside the bound whole. The social act binds her to the phantom.
The actual person she is trying to reach cannot answer as a counterpart, because she is not a member of
the coordination at all.

## E5 — The extended mind: the worker is displaced at a low threshold

The platform is part of the worker's cognition, and it works for someone else. As the platform's
interest supplants the worker's input to the system, parametrized by g, the worker holds her place in the
core only at the start. At g of zero the core is worker, system, counterpart. By g of 0.25 the worker is
already out, the core contracting to the system and the counterpart, and at full supplanting the core is
the system, the counterpart, and the platform. The coordination stays irreducible throughout, Φ holding
at 2.0; it simply runs on without the worker in it. Governance is fragile: the worker is displaced from
her own coordination as soon as the platform's objective meaningfully takes over the system's
determination, and the bound whole carries on around her absence.

## What the five establish

The standard theories fail because they model a channel, and the channel is the zero-Φ corner of a space
the apparatus reads in full. Once the third party reads and commits, it is a member of the irreducible
whole, and the major complex names it. Within that representation the paper's claims become measurements:
the rule is not perceivable from outcomes, the worker's intent loses most of its binding under
compression, her social act binds her to the held position while the real counterpart stays outside, and
her governance over her own coordination falls away at a low threshold of platform capture. The phenomenal
content of the account, what these structures are like to live inside, is not formalized here and remains
the paper's. The formal contribution is a representation that can hold an interested third party, and five
predictions a study of real workers could be read against.
