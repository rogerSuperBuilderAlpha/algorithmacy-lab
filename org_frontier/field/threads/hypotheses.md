# Platform-position theory — hypotheses (pre-registered, confirmatory)

The two field threads were exploratory. After the Stage-2 literature pass
(`literature/deep_research_report.md`), which found the economic content already established, these
hypotheses are restated as a confirmatory test: do the lab's exact-Φ Boolean models reproduce the
established economics as a cause-effect membership law? They are fixed here; the deterministic thread
scripts (`mediator_in_core.py`, `enricher_regime.py`, both CI-gated) are the confirmatory run.

## H1 — The membership trichotomy
A mediating system in the major complex is a bottleneck (in core, indispensable), an enricher (in
core, dispensable), or bypassed (out of core), and which one is a property of the whole arrangement.
Decision rule: the trichotomy run reproduces all three regimes. **Relation:** the bottleneck is the
sole-route central node of network-exchange theory (Easley & Kleinberg).

## H2 — Disintermediation requires a substitute, not a complement
A platform is bypassed when the parties' direct tie is a substitute for it (either path works), not a
complement (both needed). Decision rule: the substitute configuration ejects the platform, the
complement keeps it. **Relation:** this is the fee-competition / diversion mechanism of two-sided
markets — a substitute route collapses platform power (Armstrong; Bakos & Halaburda).

## H3 — The outside-option core law
A platform's major complex is itself plus exactly the parties with no outside option. Decision rule:
the 4-node battery reproduces this (60/60 in the thread run). **Relation:** this is network-exchange
power (power = dependence + exclusion; Easley & Kleinberg) and the competitive bottleneck (Armstrong),
restated as Φ membership.

## H4 — Asymmetry gives capture, symmetry gives bypass
Asymmetric outside options produce capture (the dependent party locked into the core with the
platform); symmetric options produce bypass. Decision rule: the phase diagram reproduces this.
**Relation:** this is the outside-option principle / deal-me-out (Binmore, Shaked & Sutton 1989):
a binding option pins one party and makes the other the residual claimant; non-binding options give
the equal split.

## H5 — Enrichment is rare and fragile
Genuine enrichment (in core, dispensable, full triad) is a small minority of in-core forms and
degenerates into capture under perturbation. Decision rule: the regime-frequency run shows enrichment
rare and the perturbation run shows it collapsing to capture. **Relation:** this is the only framing
not directly stated in the economics, and the report notes it is a re-description of the knife-edge
between the competitive-bottleneck and both-multi-home regimes.

## What this paper does not claim
It does not claim to discover that outside options determine platform power, capture, or
disintermediation. Those are established (Armstrong; Binmore–Shaked–Sutton; Easley & Kleinberg). The
only claim is the cross-method restatement: the exact-Φ instrument reproduces them as a cause-effect
membership law. Refutation would be the Φ models failing to reproduce the economics — which would make
even the restatement claim false.
