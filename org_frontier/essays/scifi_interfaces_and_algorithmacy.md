# Speculative interfaces as a laboratory for algorithmacy

Speculative fiction stages triadic coordination under interface regimes that field studies cannot yet invent. The living cultural object is the per-work catalog under [`submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/): one file per franchise or paradigm (*The Matrix*, *The Feed*, *Upload*, embodied co-pilots, recommender oracles, algospeak), each with interface paradigm, triad mapping, cognitive integration, historical analogy, resistance modes, open questions, and a first-class **Alternative interpretations** section. The imported continuous manuscript [`essay.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/essay.md) is the seed narrative that filled the catalog; new shows and competing takes extend `works/`, not a rewrite of the essay. Contribution rules: [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Sibling cultural work is the *Slacker* chapter ([`submissions/slacker_thirds/`](../../submissions/slacker_thirds/)); the construct essays are [`literacy_or_algorithmacy.md`](literacy_or_algorithmacy.md) and [`the_interested_third_party.md`](the_interested_third_party.md).

This note does not import Φ numbers from those stories. It names follow-on questions the lab can take into probes or protocol studies, each still subject to the standing rules: hypotheses before compute, nulls reported, validation gap stated. Alternative interpretations in the catalog are first-class inputs to that agenda: a contrasting reading can motivate a different encoding or a sharper null.

## What the seed essay locks

Interface paradigm stratifies algorithmacy. Raw syntax, neural-spatial feed, immersive GUI, embodied human-agent teaming, and invisible curation demand different cognitive integration and admit different resistance modes. Literacy-era scaffolding (word separation, codex, punctuation) is analogy for how abstraction lowers load while also hiding the mediator's agenda. Resistance — bypass, extraction, subversive affordance, folk theory, linguistic camouflage — is a move against the third party's co-optive work, not proof of measured irreducibility in any real organization. Each of those moves is recorded per work in `works/`; disagreement with the seed belongs under Alternative interpretations on that file.

## Research questions for the lab

1. **Encoding ladders for interface opacity.** Can a Boolean ladder of mediator visibility (full syntax exposed → abstracted GUI → black-box curation) change MIP membership or Φ while holding the commit rule fixed? Prediction to pre-register: opacity alone does not flip the literacy/algorithmacy verdict if commit-and-read structure is unchanged; opacity may still change which variables enter the major complex when the mediator's private state is the only readable channel. Catalog anchors: [`the_matrix.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/the_matrix.md), [`upload.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/upload.md), [`omniscient_curator.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/omniscient_curator.md).

2. **Folk theory versus exact-Φ core membership.** Recommender-oracle and AV users build folk theories of punishment, reward, training, and anthropomorphism. On small models, do those encodings match, mis-name, or displace the parties that exact Φ places in the irreducible core? Null of interest: folk theory predicts core membership no better than chance once surface labels are stripped. Catalog anchors: [`omniscient_curator.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/omniscient_curator.md), [`embodied_co_pilot_avs.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/embodied_co_pilot_avs.md).

3. **Resistance and subversive affordance as core displacement.** *Upload*-style environmental hacks and algospeak-style channel evasion look like workers restoring a dyadic path around the mediator. Which design operations (omit mediator read, freeze mediator objective, open a direct worker–counterpart wire) reproduce that displacement in exact Φ, and which leave the triad intact while only changing payoffs? Catalog anchors: [`upload.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/upload.md), [`algospeak_resistance.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/algospeak_resistance.md).

4. **System-0 scaffolding versus committed determination.** *The Feed*'s pre-attentive neural preprocessor is a claim that the third party acts before deliberation. Does a mediator that commits *before* either party's deliberative bit updates factor differently from a mediator that commits only after both warrant? Link to the interested-third-party and committed-determination lines without treating fiction as measurement. Catalog anchor: [`the_feed.md`](../../submissions/dial_response_algorithmacy/corpus/works/the_feed.md).
