# Committee panel review — "Integrated information as a cooperative game"

A four-member committee reviewed the synthesis essay
(`phi_as_a_cooperative_game.md`) and the ten threads it leans on. Each member read from a distinct lens
and returned an independent verdict. Three returned **major revisions**, one **minor-to-moderate**. This
document records the panel, the chair's synthesis, and the consolidated revision list. The synthesis
responds point by point in its own revision note.

## The verdicts

| Member | Lens | Verdict | The one objection that mattered most |
|--------|------|---------|--------------------------------------|
| 1 | Cooperative game theory | major revisions | the named theorems (veto⟹max-Shapley, etc.) are invoked outside their hypotheses; the game is non-monotone and non-simple, so the 100% veto–Shapley result is empirical, not a theorem consequence |
| 2 | IIT / formal methods | minor-to-moderate | the essay is less honest than the threads it summarizes: it drops their "Limits, honestly" disclaimers from the body, especially the 91% size-≥2 normalization control |

| 4 | Adversarial skeptic | major revisions | the "mediator" is discovered in random noise (`m = next(iter(vset))`) and then narrated as a designed platform; the organizational nouns are attached to structureless forms |

## Chair's synthesis

The panel was consistent, and its objections cluster into six, in order of severity. The numerical integrity
of the essay was not in question — member 3 cross-checked seven headline figures against the threads' own
`run.txt` files and all matched. The problems are of altitude, population, and licensing; the arithmetic is sound.

**1. The percentages are frequencies over random Boolean forms, and the essay reports them as facts about
coordination.** Every headline rate — 96% empty core, 94% normalization collapse, 99% subadditive, 86%
concentration — is a frequency over forms generated by independent uniform coin-flips per truth-table cell.
That distribution is not a model of any organizational population; a uniform-random Boolean network is
generically near-chaotic or near-constant, and coordination forms are a structured corner of it. The threads
hedge this ("over a sampled population," "will move with the population"); the essay strips the hedge and
elevates the number to a law. Two members raised this independently, and it is the single most important
revision.

**2. The essay does not reconcile with the program's own prior audit, q122.** q122 asked exactly whether
the value function is a valid cooperative game and returned a **split verdict**: monotone and superadditive
with zero violations on the *structured* coordination forms the value-wave used, non-monotone only on a
degenerate form. The essay asserts the opposite as near-universal (99% subadditive, 96% empty core) without
citing q122 or noting that the contradiction is a difference of population — random forms versus the
structured forms the dissertation actually theorizes. The essay must cite q122 and state plainly that the
two populations behave oppositely, and that the dissertation's claims rest on the structured one.

**3. The "mediator" is discovered in noise and then narrated as designed; the dissertation mappings are
relabelings, not earned bridges.** In the generative model there is no mediator, no platform, no party with
an outside option — there are random truth tables, and one node is labelled "the mediator" post hoc because
it came out indispensable (`m = next(iter(vset))`). The prose then writes "the committing system is the veto
player that captures the credit and has no outside option." The organizational nouns are attached to a
structureless population. This does not require new computation to fix in prose, but the honest fix is
flagged as future work: build forms with a designated mediator architecture and show the veto player
coincides with it above chance. Until then the dissertation section must demote identity ("is the veto
player") to analogy with an explicit gap statement, consistent with CLAUDE.md's "irreducibility is explored,
not established."

**4. Named theorems are invoked outside their hypotheses.** The veto⟹maximal-Shapley theorem is a fact about
simple or monotone games and a veto player in every *winning* coalition. The essay's game is neither simple
nor monotone, and it redefines "veto player" as a party in every *integrating* coalition. So the clean
115/115 veto–Shapley result is **empirical**, not a realization of the cited theorem — the theorem motivates
the question but does not apply, and presenting an empirical regularity as a deductive consequence is the
game theorist's central objection. The essay already handles the empty-core theorem correctly (it notes the
veto⟹non-empty-core guarantee *fails* because the game is non-monotone); it must handle the veto–Shapley and
symmetry results the same way.

**5. The body presents near-definitional and recovered results as findings; the disclaimers arrive too
late.** The major-complex-is-argmax identity is true by IIT's definition of the major complex, so 100% is
the only non-buggy result — a consistency check, which the coalition-structure thread states but the essay
quarantines in a late section. Move the "theorem that had to hold / consistency check" qualifier into each
body section at first mention. Two members noted the body-versus-recovery-section
contradiction as the recurrence of the pattern the prior committee caught.

**6. Two specific over-readings, and a double-count.** (a) Subadditivity is partly an artifact of scoring
singletons at intrinsic φ and of comparing φ across system sizes, which IIT itself treats as not meaningful;
"the slogan is false" refutes a reading of "more than the sum of parts" no IIT theorist holds (IIT cashes
out the slogan as irreducibility, never as φ_whole > Σφ_parts). (b) The normalization section omits the
decisive control: forbid singletons and the per-element and absolute rules agree in 91% of forms, so the 94%
collapse is the singleton winning on density, not a re-ranking of coordinations — and the real
normalization critique targets large systems these 3–4 node models do not reach. (c) The empty core,
credit concentration, and subadditivity are one mechanism — φ not aggregating across a merge — viewed three
times; the essay should say so once and refer back, then stop presenting three independent confirmations.

## What the panel credited

The construction (φ_s as a characteristic function, the major complex as the argmax coalition) is genuinely
clean and the recovered-versus-added discipline in the essay's penultimate section is its strongest move and
the model for the rest. The state-aggregation diagnosis — that the prior 0.87 membership ceiling was an
artifact of scoring coalitions across all states instead of at the complex's forming state — is a real,
reproducible self-correction and the strongest single result. The commit-versus-convey distinction (a
quarter of single-bottleneck forms are dyadic hubs, so structural indispensability is not committed
determination) is the one place an organizational construct earns contact with the math. And the essay does
not demote Φ: it keeps integrated information as the object and the game theory as a language, satisfying the
dissertation spine.

## The consolidated required revisions

1. State once, up front, that every percentage is a frequency over a uniform-random Boolean sampling
   distribution chosen for coverage, not a model of organizations; demote specific rates to "generic in this
   sample" language and carry the threads' population hedges into the body.
2. Cite q122 and state that the structured coordination forms behave oppositely (monotone, superadditive,
   zero violations), and that the dissertation's claims rest on that population.
3. Demote the dissertation section from identity to analogy with an explicit gap statement; name the missing
   experiment (a designed-mediator population) as the bridge not yet built.
4. Stop claiming the veto⟹max-Shapley and symmetry theorems *explain* the data; state they motivate the
   question but do not apply on a non-monotone, non-simple game, so the regularities are empirical.
5. Move the "consistency check / theorem that had to hold" qualifiers into each body section at first
   mention; label the 100% argmax identity as near-definitional where it appears.
6. Add the size-≥2 normalization control (91% agreement) and narrow the normalization claim; note the real
   critique targets a regime these models do not reach.
7. Name the singleton convention (zero in the membership game, intrinsic φ in the allocation games) and note
   that subadditivity is partly a convention-and-cross-size artifact; reframe "the slogan is false" as "the
   slogan, read as superadditivity, is the wrong formalization — irreducibility is the right one."
8. State once that the empty core, credit concentration, and subadditivity are one mechanism viewed three
   ways; demote "solution concept" and "winner-take-all coalition formation" to "the argmax of the
   characteristic function" and "argmax selection."
