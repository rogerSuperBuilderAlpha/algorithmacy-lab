**Flag (MOST DAMAGING, the objective-half equivocation):** l.293 — "He has **Hegel's objective condition** and lacks the subjective one," and l.322–324 — "His Hegel certifies an institution as free only when it furnishes the objective preconditions of freedom *and* its members can recognize themselves in it. - **Exact rewrite (l.322–324):** *"This is where Neuhouser's two-condition standard comes back to collect. His Hegel certifies an institution as free only when it furnishes the objective preconditions of freedom and its members can recognize themselves in it. The bypass test reads neither. What it reads sits beneath both: that the exit is shut, that the member is in for good. A warehouse shuts the exit and furnishes almost none of the preconditions; a marriage shuts it and furnishes many. ## The sharpest Hegelian objection, pressed (teleology / Ng–Kreines)

For the metaphysical-revival wing — Kreines (*Reason in the World*), Ng (*Hegel's Concept of Life*), and the Roy the post cites — *Beisichselbstsein im Anderen* is first a Logic-level, teleological structure: the Concept "at home in its other," freedom as the truth of necessity, self-determining purposiveness. On that reading, mapping the *objective* condition of being-at-home onto a **causal-partition** structure proxies the freedom-relation with precisely the *Mechanism*-level relation Hegel ranks *below* the Concept (objects externally determined by others — the antithesis of being-at-home). A teleological reader will say the post's whole "objective half = causal wiring" gambit is category-crossed.

**Does the draft survive? Yes, at homology level** — and this is to its credit. It never identifies causal structure with being-at-home; it reads only the threshold and disclaims the rest; and the warehouse/co-op case concedes the causal structure is silent on rational content, which is the teleological reader's *own* point. But the post never *names* the teleological/metaphysical reading it is bracketing, and l.91's "not metaphysics" leaves a thread for that reader to pull. One sentence acknowledging that even the objective side, for a teleological Hegel, is already purposive (not mechanistic), and that the causal reading proxies only its threshold, would inoculate the post against its single most sophisticated Hegelian critic.
