## Round 2 (v4 → v5)

- [`reviewer2_hostile.md`](reviewer2_hostile.md) — the decisive finding, reached independently of the Hegel
  specialist: the paper contradicts itself at the hinge (the rung is "a causal middle and nothing more…
  without translation loss" vs. "already the Concept showing through… not neutral machinery"); "the same
  boundary" is asserted as identity where the caveats say the structures differ in kind; the bridge answers
  only the formalist skeptic, never the causal-realist one; and a new topology catch — Hegel's genuine
  middle *rotates* in an all-to-all triad while the instrument's integrator is a fixed-mediator star, closer
  to Hegel's *defective* middle. Verdict: major revision.
- [`hegel_specialist.md`](hegel_specialist.md) — converged on the same hinge contradiction, verified against
  di Giovanni p. 641, and supplied the graded-fit rewrite (tightest at the conduit pole, loosest at the
  integrator pole). Plus: "communication has no middle" (the arc is *no* real middle → a real one, a tighter
  match); the fifth Hegel-within-Sans misattribution (line 248); Stein's Concept is the *modeler*, not the
  update rule; name a second ontological reader.
- [`formal_specialist.md`](formal_specialist.md) — re-ran the repo census; all fifteen receipts match. Top
  fix: the Φ verdict/scale tension is concrete — the family attains three bands (232 at 0.0, **eight at
  0.5**, sixteen at 2.0), so "the largest value any form attains" both does scale-work the verdict reading
  disavows and hides eight integrators. Cut it. Plus the flagship-term split (integrator vs. necessary
  middle).
- [`copyeditor.md`](copyeditor.md) — citation resolution clean both ways; the one live error is the line-248
  misattribution (Hegel's words credited to Sans); a Stein italic inconsistency; the stale Christensen entry
  (now vol. 90, pp. 3447–3482); the cross-post EL DOI split (keep post3's DOIs, add to post6/post9).
- [`voice_reader.md`](voice_reader.md) — the architecture problem from round 1 survived: ~13/13 sections
  still land on a polished epigram, and v4 re-introduced six performed-honesty phrases. Flatten four closes;
  cut the six phrases; name one real middle in the opener.

**Convergent core:** the hinge self-contradiction (2 reviewers, independently); "same boundary" overclaimed
as identity (2); the bridge never faces the causal-realist skeptic (2); the overclaims all ride in
section-closing epigrams (3). v5 fixes the hinge (graded fit), downgrades to "a boundary of the same
shape," faces the second skeptic with Sans's own words ("objectivity… do[es] not refer directly either to
mind or to nature"), concedes the topology gap as a division of labor across receipts, cuts the "largest
value" clause, downgrades Stein from confirmation to rhyme, and makes the register/citation fixes. Both
series guards hold (no anticipation, no demotion of Φ).
