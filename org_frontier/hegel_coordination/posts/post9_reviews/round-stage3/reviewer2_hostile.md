**Single most important fix:** In "The one computed thing," the sentence *"It builds a larger irreducible thing worth more than the sum of the parts it is built from"* is arithmetically false and it sits on the essay's one affirmative exhibit. Two triads at Φ = 2.0 each sum to 4.0; the merged core reads 3.0. Three is *less* than the sum — it is above a *single* triad, which is all "super-additive" means in the FINDINGS ("H3 the merged core is super-additive (Φ > 2.0)"). The draft even states it correctly one paragraph earlier ("above the 2.0 either triad carried alone"). A hostile reader does the sum in his head, gets 3 < 4, and the whole foothold looks oversold. Fix the phrase and the exhibit is airtight.

## Findings, most-damaging first

### 1. "worth more than the sum of the parts" — false arithmetic on the load-bearing exhibit (CONFIRMED)
Exact text: *"Binding two mediators directly does not lay two coordinations side by side. It builds a larger irreducible thing worth more than the sum of the parts it is built from."*

The FINDINGS defines super-additivity as Φ > 2.0 — the merged core (3.0) exceeds a *single* triad (2.0). It does not exceed the two triads summed (4.0); 3.0 is in fact *below* 4.0, so relative to the arithmetic sum the merger is sub-additive. The FINDINGS' own loose "worth more than its parts" the draft has amplified into "the sum of the parts," which is the one reading that is demonstrably wrong. And it contradicts the draft's own correct comparison in the Lift clause ("above the 2.0 either triad carried alone"). This is precisely the overclaim-on-the-exhibit a hostile panel exists to catch.

Exact rewrite (author voice):
> Binding two mediators directly does not lay two coordinations side by side. It builds one irreducible thing that reads higher than either coordination it was built from — 3.0 where each triad alone read 2.0.

### 2. Owl close contradicts its own next sentence (CONFIRMED)
Exact text: *"…and the two drawings never came to the same line. On the coarse cases they fall together: a market is an aggregate whether you grade it by recognition or by a cut, and both hands draw that line in the same place."*

"Never came to the same line" is flatly denied by "both hands draw that line in the same place" two clauses later, and again by the closing "two lines that meet on the easy cases and part on the hard ones." The v3 correction fixed the *thesis* of the owl close but left this absolute still standing at the head of it. An epigram that its own next sentence refutes is exactly the joint this lens is told to attack.

Exact rewrite:
> …and the two drawings never resolved into one line. On the coarse cases they fall together: a market is an aggregate whether you grade it by recognition or by a cut, and both hands draw it in the same place.

### 3. "the resemblance stops being a rhyme" tips against the essay's own altitude (PLAUSIBLE)
Exact text: *"it is the one place the resemblance stops being a rhyme and becomes a computed structure a reader can point at."*

The series' spine — and the closing image — is homology: two lines that *part on the hard ones*. Sublation is the hardest case (immanence, life). Saying the resemblance there "stops being a rhyme" reads, on a hostile pass, as the rhyme becoming coincidence exactly where the essay insists the instruments diverge. The paragraph is saved by its own hedges ("sublation with the immanence surgically removed," "the *what* … without the *because*"), so this is not identity-smuggling — but the phrasing overshoots the hedge. Keep "rhyme" alive; claim only that it now has something computed under it.

Exact rewrite:
> it is the one place where the rhyme has a computed structure under it — something a reader can point at, the *what* of cancel-preserve-lift, still without the *because*.

### 4. AUC 1.0 vs accuracy 0.250 — name the two metrics (PLAUSIBLE)
Exact text: *"a learned surrogate hits 1.0 — but neither generalizes past it: the same surrogate, tested on triads and dyads a size larger or smaller than the ones it trained on, collapses to 0.250 accuracy (q81, q82)."*

Both numbers are correct against the receipt (in-distribution CV AUC 1.000; cross-size accuracy 0.250), but the draft compares an AUC to an accuracy as if one number fell to another. A reviewer holding q81 will note 1.0 and 0.250 are different scales. The collapse is real; just label it so the drop cannot be read as a single metric cratering.

Exact rewrite:
> a learned surrogate hits AUC 1.0 in its own size class — but neither generalizes: the same surrogate, tested on triads and dyads one size up or down, drops to 0.250 accuracy, chance on four classes (q81, q82).
