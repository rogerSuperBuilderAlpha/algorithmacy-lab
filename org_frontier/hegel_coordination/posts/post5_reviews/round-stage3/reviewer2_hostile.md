What does not survive is a single recurring move at the load-bearing joint: the paper hedges anticipation on the *time* axis ("not its ancestor," "before there was a number") while quietly asserting identity on the *content* axis ("the verbal form of my result," "are exactly parts a partition can hold apart"). ## Section by section

### Opening (paragraphs 1–5)

**Flag 1 (medium) — the number "attaches" to Hegel's own conclusion.** Paragraph 2:

> "That is the verdict my two-rooms cut reaches too — the same conclusion by a different road, a century and a half before there was a number to attach to it, in a register that had no number and wanted none."

"a number to attach to *it*" — *it* is Hegel's conclusion. This says the Φ number would attach to the very thing Hegel found, i.e. one referent, later measured. That is the identity-of-referent the series forbids and paragraph 3 explicitly disavows ("my instrument is not cashing out something he already meant"). The guard now sits at paragraph 3, but this sentence lands first and pre-loads the wrong reading.

Rewrite (author voice): *"That is the verdict my two-rooms cut reaches too — the same conclusion by a different road, in a register that had no number and did not miss one."* Drop "before there was a number to attach to it"; it is the phrase doing the smuggling.

**Survives:** Paragraph 3 ("this is an agreement of verdicts, not an identity of method… two different questions land on the same case and vote the same way; that is worth a paper, and it is not worth more than that") is exactly the guard the digest asked to be pulled forward, and it is well placed and well phrased. Keep it verbatim.

### The Verdict: External Unity

**Flag 2 (high, LEAD) — "the verbal form of my result" is identity of referent, and it contradicts paragraph 3.** In the *Understanding* paragraph:

> "Hegel filed the market under the faculty that fixes and relates without constituting; that filing is the verbal form of my result, not its ancestor."

"the verbal form of my result" makes Hegel's Verstand-filing *the same content as the partition verdict, expressed in words instead of a number*. That is identity of referent — precisely what Guard 1 bars ("same boundary, different instrument, never identity of referents") and precisely what paragraph 3 denies ("my instrument is not cashing out something he already meant"). The trailing "not its ancestor" hedges *temporal precedence* only; it leaves the *sameness-of-content* claim standing. Epigram over-asserts on one axis, hedge covers a different axis.

Rewrite (author voice): *"Hegel filed the market under the faculty that fixes and relates without constituting; that filing lands where my test lands, in words rather than in a number — a resonance across two registers, not one claim wearing two costumes."*

**Flag 3 (high) — "are exactly parts a partition can hold apart without loss" is a general biconditional the paper elsewhere denies.** Same paragraph:

> "parts that subsist on their own account, related without being constituted, are exactly parts a partition can hold apart without loss."

Present tense, no scope marker: this reads as a law — Verstand-self-subsistence *is* partition-survivability. But the paper's whole thesis (and Post 1's receipt: "a whole in the register of norms can be, in the register of cause, a very well-organized heap") is that the two registers are independent and merely *coincide on the market*. "exactly… without loss" claims co-extension, not coincidence. The following hedge ("an analogue to my result, not an anticipation") again guards the *upgrade/Vernunft* axis, not this bridge — so the overclaim goes out unretracted. This is the second head of the same beast as Flag 2.

Rewrite: *"parts that subsist on their own account, related without being constituted, are — on this case — just what a partition can hold apart without loss. That the two descriptions land together here is the finding; it is not a law that Verstand and my test must always agree."*

**Survives (and note it):** The *zunächst* / "in the first instance" hedge on the external state is correctly added and correctly deployed ("'external' is his verdict on civil society considered under its first moment, not his last word on it"). The three-word gloss (External / Necessity / Understanding) is disciplined. The register-relocation, whole. The paper's real intellectual risk was letting the causal-partition verdict annex Hegel's ethical complaint (the digest's named refutation condition). Instead it holds the two apart correctly at their true hinge — the rabble as a *recognitive* failure, recognition as Hegel's own upgrade condition, so the registers meet *in Hegel* and go orthogonal only *under the criterion that brackets recognition* — and it pays the honest cost out loud in the temper paragraph without once demoting Φ.
