## Part 2 — Itemized issues

### A. APA 7 in-text citation compliance

**Issue 1 (required). "as quoted in" → "as cited in" — line 181.**
Current: `(Stern, 2012, pp. 357–358, as quoted in Saunders, 2026, p. 43)`.
APA 7 §8.6 prescribes "as cited in" for secondary sources. Correct to: `(Stern, 2012, pp. 357–358, as cited in Saunders, 2026, p. 44)` — page per Issue 13. This is the only instance in the manuscript.

**Issue 2 (required — pick one path). Stern secondary-source vs. reference list — line 181 and lines 422–423.**
APA 7: when a work is cited only through a secondary source, the reference list carries the *secondary* source only (Saunders); the primary (Stern) is named in text with its year but gets no entry. The manuscript currently does both halves of both options: secondary-style citation in text *and* a full primary entry in the list.
- Path (a): obtain Stern (2012), verify the quotation at pp. 357–358 directly, cite it directly as `(Stern, 2012, pp. 357–358)`, drop the "as cited in" clause, keep the reference entry. (The DOI resolves and BJHP 20(2), 333–361 checks out, so this is feasible and is the better path for a scholarly venue.)
- Path (b): keep the secondary citation (with "as cited in" per Issue 1) and **delete the Stern entry** from the references.

**Issue 3 (required). Orphan reference entry — Hegel (1977), Miller translation, lines 388–389.**
Never cited in text. Every entry must be cited. Either delete it, or earn it with a note (e.g., a first-citation footnote or parenthetical stating that quotations follow Pinkard and giving Miller as the alternative standard translation).

**Issue 4 (required). Parenthetical citations missing author and year.**
APA 7 requires author + year in every *parenthetical* citation (the year-omission allowance applies to narrative citations only, within a paragraph). Offending parentheticals:
- Line 108: `(¶191, pp. 113–114)`
- Line 113: `(¶191, p. 114)`
- Lines 122, 124: `(¶192, p. 114)` twice
- Lines 126, 127: `(¶193, p. 114)` twice
- Line 149: `(p. 20)` (Kojève)
Least-intrusive compliant fix: make the source narrative once per paragraph — e.g., "Hegel (1807/2018) …" — after which page/paragraph-only parentheticals following each quotation are standard practice; or add `Hegel, 1807/2018,` inside the first parenthetical of each paragraph. The paragraph at lines 102–108 already opens with a full parenthetical at line 104, so line 108 minimally needs `(Hegel, 1807/2018, ¶191, pp. 113–114)`. Same repair for the Kojève `(p. 20)`: either "Kojève (1947/1980) …" narratively, or `(Kojève, 1947/1980, p. 20)`.

**Issue 5 (required). Hybrid narrative citations — year detached from the author's name.**
- Lines 87–88: "Hegel says it outright: death is '…' (1807/2018, ¶188, p. 112)." → "Hegel (1807/2018) says it outright: death is '…' (¶188, p. 112)."
- Line 157: "Williams himself writes that 'Hegel considers mastery to be self-subverting' (1997, p. 10)." → "Williams (1997) himself writes … (p. 10)"; or, since Williams (1997) is already cited in this paragraph (line 152), simply "(p. 10)".

**Issue 6 (recommended). Redundant author in parenthetical after narrative naming.**
- Line 322: "Brandom reads the standing … (Brandom, 2019)" → "Brandom (2019) reads …".
- Line 323: "Honneth built a whole theory … (Honneth, 1992/1995)" → "Honneth (1992/1995) built …".

**Issue 7 (required if kept). Simmel named with no citation and no entry — line 207.**
"…since Simmel's *tertius* and Burt's (1992) structural holes." Attributing the *tertius* to Simmel is a citable claim. Either add a citation and entry (e.g., Simmel, G. (1950). *The sociology of Georg Simmel* (K. H. Wolff, Ed. & Trans.). Free Press. (Original work published 1908) — cited as Simmel, 1908/1950) or rephrase so the sentence rides on Burt alone.

**Issue 8 (journal-dependent — flag, no change demanded). ¶ symbol for the Phenomenology.**
APA 7 (§8.13) uses "para." for paragraph locators; ¶/¶¶ is the discipline convention in Hegel scholarship and the manuscript uses it consistently (single ¶ for one paragraph, ¶¶ for ranges — correct form). Defensible for a Substack post and most philosophy venues; convert to "para./paras." only if the target venue enforces strict APA.

**Issue 10 (verified clean). Multi-source parentheticals and et al.**
