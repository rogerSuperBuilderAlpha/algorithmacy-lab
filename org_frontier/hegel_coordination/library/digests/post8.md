# Post 8 — At Home in the Other (Freedom): grounding digest

*Stage-1 research digest (Fable grounding + Opus adversarial verification, run wf_ad0ba178). The rewrite draws from this file plus the source cards in `../cards/`. Verification verdict: **needs-correction**.*

## Claim (correct altitude)
Beisichselbstsein im Anderen — freedom as being at home with oneself in the other — is the problem coordinative sovereignty answers for a platform-mediated world, and the lab's bypass (necessary/contingent) test locates, in causal structure, the members for whom exit has stopped being a freedom and only the Hegelian question of voice-within-the-whole remains — the same boundary Hegel drew, read with a different instrument, which reaches only the objective-institutional half of his freedom test and goes silent at the subjective (recognitive) half where freedom actually lives.

## Guard check
Clean. No anticipation-of-Φ: the one time-depth sentence ("the problem my lab has been circling... the better part of two centuries before we had the grammar to restate even its institutional half") runs the safe direction — Hegel owns the problem, the lab restates only its institutional half; no claim that Hegel foresaw the instrument. No demotion of Φ: the co-op-and-cage concessions are scope statements ("its competence runs out exactly at the border of freedom"), not calculator/decorative/hollow language, and the close re-affirms the instrument ("it is the doorway, and until now no instrument had found it"). The v3 sourcing note itself frames the re-routing fix as "Φ mis-location corrected, not Φ demoted," which is right. One watch-item, not a violation: "A test that cannot tell a co-op from a warehouse is not measuring freedom" is fine as long as the following sentence (it reads the closing of the exit, which is freedom's precondition) stays attached — cutting that pair apart in a future edit would tip it toward demotion.

## Refutation condition
Hegel's institutions earn the "at home" claim through their rational content, not their irreducibility; a platform can be irreducible and estranging, so if the post lets irreducibility stand in for being-at-home it becomes apologetics for the wholes the lab studies (a certificate of contentment for every whole strong enough to trap its members). v3 holds the line explicitly and structurally: the three-rung separation (coupling → voice as objective condition → being-at-home as achievement), the warehouse/co-op twin-verdict case, and the closing "diagnosis vs apology" paragraph. The condition it must keep surviving: the instrument returns the same verdict for the marriage and the sweatshop, and the post must keep saying that this is where its competence ends — which it now does.
