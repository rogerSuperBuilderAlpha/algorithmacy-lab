## Claim (correct altitude)
Hegel's master and slave is his first proof that a whole bound by domination fails on its own terms — a mainstream result, not the program's discovery; what the coordination program adds is a general class (contingent irreducibility) and a decision procedure (the bypass/constraint-removal counterfactual with its computed margin) that give that result a different, partition-sensitive truthmaker, drawing the same boundary Hegel drew with a different instrument, and inheriting the desire-relativity objection along with the result.

## Guard check
Substantially clean. No demotion of Φ anywhere: the scope statements ("the structural signature is all the test can read," "the program carries it too, and repairs nothing") are honest limits paired with the affirmative case — the decision-procedure paragraph explicitly claims what the recognition literature lacks and Φ supplies, and "what the program adds is small" is modesty about the addition to Hegel scholarship, immediately followed by the class/margin/operation claims, not a demotion of the tool. On anticipation: the bridge is held at homology throughout ("Hegel reached the verdict… in a different vocabulary, with a different proof. The class and the test came later, and they are the program's, not his"). One borderline sentence to watch at the next panel: the closing "a binding held by an external constraint leaves the same mark on a cause-and-effect structure wherever it appears, and that Hegel drew that mark first and hardest" — since "that mark" is defined as a mark on a cause-and-effect structure, "Hegel drew that mark" drifts a half-step toward crediting Hegel with the causal signature itself. The surrounding hedges (his proof is "in the register of what an acknowledgment is worth") hold it, and prior panels kept the sentence; if tightened, credit Hegel with drawing the boundary, not the mark.

## Refutation condition
The structural reading is not a discovery — Kojève through Brandom already hold it, and Saunders/Cunniff have now put even the result in dispute. So the post stands only if the causal truthmaker earns its keep: it must offer the general class (contingent irreducibility) and the decision procedure (constraint-off recomputation, margin) as genuine additions, concede analogy wherever nothing is computed (no model of the passage exists; the n=3 master–slave–world run named in the sourcing note remains the open endgame), and carry the desire-relativity objection (Wood's community of masters, Fanon's master-who-wants-work, Cunniff's obedient \"Yes, sir\") rather than claim to escape it. If Cunniff is right there is no dissolution in the passage at all, only stable extraction — the draft makes its title explicitly conditional on the tradition's reading and rests the residual mixed verdict on the labor channel (¶190) and the Reconstruction record, which do not depend on what the master wants. A fair Hegel reader sinks the post by showing either that the two-channel sort quietly re-imports the recognition premise it claims independence from, or that the taxonomy's silence on the wrongness of domination (Saunders) makes the filing itself illegitimate rather than merely incomplete.
