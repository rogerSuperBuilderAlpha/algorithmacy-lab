## Claim (correct altitude)
Civil society in PR §§182–189 is Hegel drawing, in 1821 and in his own register, the same boundary the partition test draws on the market — universal interdependence whose unity is external, connection without constitution — as a coincidence of verdict between two different senses of "external" (unwilled/Verstand-self-subsistent vs. partition-survivable), not an anticipation of the causal criterion and not the same ground.

## Refutation condition
Hegel's deficiency claim about civil society is normative-economic — a failure of freedom and distribution (Kain, García Mills, the rabble sections) — not a claim of structural non-wholeness; and in Hegel the two registers meet at recognition, his own upgrade condition. The post stands only if it keeps the registers distinct while showing they co-sort the market case, without letting the structural reading annex the ethical complaint (v3's relocation of the register-split from Hegel to the criterion, plus the temper paragraph, is the current answer; Lazarus 2025 is the published version of the recognitive counter-reading a referee would wield).
