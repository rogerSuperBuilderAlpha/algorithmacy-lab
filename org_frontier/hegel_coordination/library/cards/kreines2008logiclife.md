## Summary
Kreines's chapter is Post 4's (and Post 6's) realist-teleology anchor: Hegel holds, against Kant, that living beings do manifest true internal purposiveness, explicable in teleological terms, of which we can have objective knowledge. It grounds "Hegel wins the classification" as a REPLACEMENT of the criterion with inner teleology, not an anticipation of a causal one — and doubles as the threat that a Φ measure neither entails nor requires the teleological ground.
