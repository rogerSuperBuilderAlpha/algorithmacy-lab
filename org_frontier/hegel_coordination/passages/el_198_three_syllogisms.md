# EL §198 — the state as a system of three syllogisms

**Locus.** *Encyclopaedia Logic* §198 (Remark): the state as three mutually mediating syllogisms —
individuals, their needs (civil society), and government each taking a turn as the middle term.
**Status.** **Verified 2026-07-10**: §198 is the right section. Quote from Brinkmann/Dahlstrom at
drafting.
**Series use.** Post 6's toy-encoding target (HC5): a triad Hegel himself presents as mutual mediation.
**Secondary.** There is a dedicated scholarly treatment ("The State as a System of Three Syllogisms," in
Vieweg's *Hegel's Political Philosophy*, OUP) — read before encoding, both for the structure and to
avoid rediscovering its analysis.
