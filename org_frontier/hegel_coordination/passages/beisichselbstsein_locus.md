# "Bei sich selbst im Anderen" — locus OPEN

**The phrase.** Freedom as being at home with oneself in one's other — post 8's anchor and the bridge to
coordinative sovereignty.
**Status.** **Locus not yet pinned.** Candidates: *EL* §24 Addition 2; *PR* §7 Addition; the formulation
recurs in the *Lectures* corpus. The scholarship (e.g., Roy, "In seinem Anderen bei sich selbst zu
sein," *Epoché* 2006) confirms centrality without settling a single canonical locus — there may not be
one, in which case the post cites the family and says so.
**Rule.** Post 8 does not draft until this card carries a verified quotation with edition and page.
**Companions (loci standard).** *PR* §5+R (the freedom of the void), §260 (concrete freedom).
