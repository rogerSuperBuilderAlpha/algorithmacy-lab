O03 · PROPOSED RESEARCH LENS

Does meaning survive the gate?

A possible test beside the lab map. System acceptance and counterpart understanding are separate observations.

ROWS: SYSTEM ACCEPTS / REJECTS · COLUMNS: UNDERSTOOD / NOT
Accepted + understoodThe gate passes it; meaning arrives.
Accepted + not understoodThe gate passes it; meaning is lost.
Rejected + understoodThe gate blocks it; meaning may travel another way.
Rejected + not understoodNeither condition holds.

Accepted means accepted by the system. Understanding does not prove agreement or action. Measure coordinated action separately.
