# A car dealer and a clearinghouse: held in place by law, or by the work it does?

A stipulated description of two intermediaries. It is not field data. Paragraph numbers are locators.

**¶1.** A car maker sells to a buyer through a franchised dealer. Franchise law forbids the maker
from selling to the buyer directly. "The maker ships to the dealer; the dealer delivers to the buyer;
the maker builds to the dealer's orders, which follow the buyers'."

**¶2.** A clearinghouse settles trades between two counterparties. "A trade settles only when both
legs are confirmed; each party acts on the clearinghouse's settlement notice."

**¶3.** The counterfactual for both: lift the constraint and let the downstream party read the
upstream party directly. "If the law changed, buyers could order from the maker."
